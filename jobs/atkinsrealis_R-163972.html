Job Description

Overview

Shape the Future of our cities and environments.

Are you a Civil Engineer who is passionate about making the world a better place?

Are you ready to take up a vital role in some of the UK’s most exciting and innovative projects?

Join AtkinsRéalis and take a leading role in shaping the future of our cities and environments.

We are looking for several motivated and energetic Senior Civil Engineers to join our Land Development team within the Design & Advanced Technology (D&AT) Practice of AtkinsRéalis in the Manchester Office and other northern UK offices.  You’ll be part of a talented, multi-disciplinary team delivering cutting edge, iconic projects to a wide variety of Land Development clients – projects like Sizewell C, Ministry of Defence Frameworks, Net Zero and Renewable Energy Projects, Homes England, Revantage UK & MADE Partnership among others.

This role would suit existing experienced Senior Civil Engineers through to Chartered / Incorporated Engineers looking to step into a Senior Civil Engineer position. As a key member of our growing team, you’ll play a vital role in delivering transformational programmes for critical UK infrastructure, with opportunities to work on landmark engineering projects both domestically and internationally. We’re committed to providing industry-leading solutions throughout the project lifecycle, adding value through client-side and construction-phase support.

Become part of a team where every voice matters, and collaboratively together we can tackle our clients’ toughest challenges. We foster a diverse and inclusive environment where world-class talent thrives without boundaries.  We collaborate and value the different perspectives our people bring recognising everyone’s contributions equally.

We also know that different people have different priorities, which is why we’re here to support you with whatever you need.

We offer hybrid working which involves 3 days per week being office/site based.  These positions are based in our Manchester, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds, or Newcastle offices.

Your role

The role will offer a challenging and rewarding opportunity to apply your knowledge, experience, and problem-solving skills whilst providing good opportunities for your career development. Ideally the successful candidate(s) would meet the responsibilities, skills and attributes identified below, however we will be pleased to consider candidate(s) that meet most of the criteria with demonstrable technical skills and capabilities.

Technical Responsibilities

As a Senior Civil Engineer in the D&AT practice, the successful candidate(s) would have the following responsibilities:

  • Coordinate and manage a team of civil engineers for delivery of technical design work on complex multi-disciplinary projects following relevant national and project specific design codes and standards, and to high-quality levels in areas such as utilities, drainage, paved and unpaved surfaces, access and accessibility, earthworks and construction logistics.
  • Responsible for producing, checking and reviewing technical deliverables such as design reports, specifications, drawings, calculations etc
  • Provide technical input into project design deliverables for external works aspects of complex multi-disciplinary projects and work with the project management team to successfully manage time and budgets to key project milestones and programmes.
  • Collaborate with client, operational, project, or service activities within your immediate team and other related AtkinsRéalis teams.
  • Provide mentorship and help to develop the capabilities and skills of other less experienced colleagues.
  • Work independently with minimal technical guidance whilst drawing on the technical network within AtkinsRéalis for more challenging technical problems.  Exercise personal judgement to solve technical problems where appropriate and provide guidance to more junior team members.
  • Provide technical support for bid proposals.
  • Ensure compliance with CDM Regulations, Safety Legislation and AtkinsRéalis Company Safety Procedures.
  • Work closely with multi-disciplinary teams in the UK and other teams based overseas to deliver integrated design solutions.
  • Adhere to quality assurance standards and procedures for deliverables, including check and review of your own work as well as other’s work.

About you

The ideal candidate would be a well-rounded and versatile individual who exemplifies the following key skills and attributes:

  • A degree in Civil Engineering or equivalent
  • Chartered or Incorporated Civil Engineer, or actively in the final stages of working towards chartered professional accreditation.
  • Proven experience in coordinating or managing a civil engineering design delivery team for a design consultancy included within a multi-disciplinary environment.
  • Takes a broad perspective to identify innovative solutions to design problems.
  • Can demonstrate strong technical knowledge and practical experience in external works such as drainage, utilities, pavements, and earthworks.
  • Practical knowledge of current design codes and standards as well as industry best practices, including CDM regulations.
  • Ability to work independently with minimal technical guidance at Senior Civil Engineer level.
  • Preferably proficient in applicable analytical software and related tools such as Causeway Flow, InfoDrainage and Civil3D.
  • Experience in the Energy or Defence markets is beneficial but not required.
  • Produces safe designs and promotes the safety and welfare of others.
  • Demonstrates strong communication and technical briefing skills, with the ability to convey technical concepts clearly and effectively within diverse multi-disciplinary teams.
  • A proactive and highly motivated mindset with a collaborative approach ensuring successful teamwork to enable excellent client outcomes on all projects.
  • Full Driving License is desirable
