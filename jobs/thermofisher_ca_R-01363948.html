Job Summary

We are seeking a 5+ years’ experienced Senior Salesforce Developer with strong technical expertise and a proven ability to design, build, and deliver scalable Salesforce solutions. The ideal candidate will have deep hands-on experience across Salesforce Lightning, Apex, integrations, and cloud solutions, with the ability to lead development efforts, support production environments, and collaborate closely with business stakeholders to deliver high-quality outcomes aligned with organizational goals.

Salesforce.com developer will be responsible for working with the business analyst teams to convert the given business requirements into technical solutions and implement them in the salesforce platform. Is also responsible for documenting and implementing the best practices in the system to ensure the stability of the system.

Salesforce Development & Solution Delivery

  • Build and enhance user experiences using Lightning Web Components (LWC) and Aura Components .

  • Experience in Sales cloud, Service cloud , Custom Cloud, Chatter and App Exchange applications.
  • Having administration Config experience on CRM applications like data exports & imports, application support, security administration, CMC maintenance, and release management.
