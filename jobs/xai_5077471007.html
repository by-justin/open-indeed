Job Description

SpaceXAI’s mission is to create AI systems that can accurately understand the universe and aid humanity in its pursuit of knowledge. Our team is small, highly motivated, and focused on engineering excellence. This organization is for individuals who appreciate challenging themselves and thrive on curiosity. We operate with a flat organizational structure. All employees are expected to be hands-on and to contribute directly to the company’s mission. Leadership is given to those who show initiative and consistently deliver excellence. Work ethic and strong prioritization skills are important. All employees are expected to have strong communication skills. They should be able to concisely and accurately share knowledge with their teammates.

ABOUT THE ROLE:

We’re looking for a detail-oriented and proactive recruiting coordinator to support our fast-paced JAPAC Talent Acquisition team. This is a contract role ideal for someone who thrives in a dynamic environment and enjoys coordinating processes, managing systems, and creating a great candidate experience.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Schedule interviews and coordinate logistics across multiple time zones

  • Maintain accurate data in our Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

  • Communicate with candidates throughout the interview process to ensure a smooth and positive experience

  • Partner with recruiters and hiring managers to support end-to-end recruitment processes

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

  • 1–3 years of experience in a coordination, recruiting, or people ops/HR support role

  • Strong organizational and time management skills; ability to manage multiple tasks with high attention to detail

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Japanese and English will be a plus

  • Comfortable working with different time zones and managing scheduling complexity

  • Familiarity with ATS (Greenhouse or Workday) and Google Suite products is a plus

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
