Why join us?

The Networks Rotational Program at Bell offers a unique launchpad to shape the future of Canada's most advanced technologies while fast-tracking your leadership career. By joining, you will deliver on high-impact, transformational projects, learn directly from supportive executive mentors, and expand your professional network through industry experts and a thriving peer-and-alumni community - all while gaining the hands-on technical and strategic experience needed to excel in your career.

What you’ll do

Through this accelerated and immersive 36-month experience, you will rotate through different areas within the Networks organization, collaborating with cross-functional teams, using cutting-edge tools, and working with vast amounts of data, you will deliver actionable insights, manage critical network assets, and drive end-to-end solutions that impact key business decisions across Bell.

Our Networks program features key rotations in:

  • Core Technical
  • Network Operations
  • Leadership and Strategy

Education & Eligibility

  • Completed or completing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree (January 2025 - May 2027) in Engineering, Computer Science, Analytics, Business Technology, Math, Statistics, or a related field.
  • Under three years of full-time professional experience (excluding internships and part-time work).
  • A proven track record of taking initiative and leading others through student societies, case competitions, volunteer work, or academic projects.
  • A valid driver's license (or equivalent) may be needed for specific field-based or operational roles.

Core Capabilities & Technical Skills

  • Advanced proficiency in MS Office Suite (specifically MS Excel for data analysis and reporting).
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a talent for synthesizing data and identifying trends.
  • Familiarity with project and program management methodologies, frameworks, and tools (e.g., Agile, PMP, Scrum, Kanban, Jira).
  • Technologically savvy, with a strong interest in staying up to date with emerging IT, cloud, and digital transformation technologies (e.g., RPA, AI tools).

Specialized Focus Skills (Depending on Rotational Placement)

  • For Core Engineering Rotations: Knowledge of network architecture (wireless, wireline, TV), routing protocols, broadcast, data center and/or cloud technologies (containers); familiarity with construction drawings, structural analysis, building materials, and quality standards; proficiency in Teldig and Cognibox (for Access Network Engineering).
  • For Operations & Strategy Rotations: Solid understanding of core principles for working with large, complex datasets; introductory experience—or a strong desire to learn—data tools and programming languages such as SQL, Python (object-oriented programming), SAS, MicroStrategy, Java, Kotlin, or Objective-C; working knowledge of Lean, Six Sigma, or process improvement methodologies.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
