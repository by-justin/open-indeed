About us:

Join the Wing Architecture and Integration team, where we are at the forefront of Airbus wing concept design. Our work spans the full spectrum of Airbus commercial wing designs, from supporting current solutions to developing improvements through process digitalization. This is an important part of shaping the future of environmentally sustainable aircraft.

We are looking for a forward-thinking individual who is not afraid to be constructively disruptive and help us redefine the way we work. As we continue our digital journey, we need a unique skill set to guide us and show us a different way of doing things.

The Airbus Engineering Structures Wing organization is a transnational team with sites in the UK, France, and Germany. This position is primarily based in Filton, UK, where a community of over 400 engineers collaborates on the design and manufacture of wings for all Airbus commercial aircraft.

Our work covers all major aircraft programs—A220, Single Aisle, Long Range, A350, A380, and A400m—and we are also actively engaged in research and development for new aircraft configurations to enable more efficient and environmentally sustainable aviation.

What you will be doing:

As a student engineer you will develop the digital solutions needed across a wide range of more traditional disciplines within the wing business. During this placement, the activities you may undertake will include:

  • Develop your learning journey towards Configuration management and Digital Mock up and processes;
  • Applying Python, Java, GScript, C++ based Automation in order to improve design and stress processes;
  • Influence Digital Transformation within the wing and airframe organisations;
  • Perform Data Analytics on Skywise, Google Studio and internal platforms developed by Airbus;
  • Develop machine learning algorithms on various platforms;
  • Use of Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality (VR/AR) techniques;
  • Support the move from Server Based Microsoft Applications to Cloud Based Google Applications.
