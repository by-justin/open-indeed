Your Role and Responsibilities

In this role, you'll help drive ABB's commitment to health, safety, and environmental excellence by supporting continuous improvement and fostering a positive safety culture across the site.

  • Support the HSE Manager in delivering the site HSE strategy in line with the ABB Way.
  • Assist line managers and supervisors in understanding their HSE responsibilities, legal obligations, and requirements for effective role fulfilment in accordance with local legislation and ABB standards.
  • Ensure HSE capabilities are maintained and aligned with company standards, processes, guidelines, and best practices.
  • Support the provision and effective use of HSE-related equipment, tools, and resources across the site.
  • Oversee HSE risk assessments conducted by line management and ensure appropriate preventive and corrective measures are implemented.
  • Ensure HSE incidents, near misses, and observations are reported to management and relevant stakeholders, with appropriate actions followed through to completion.
  • Drive positive behavioural change through the implementation of ABB group-wide and business-specific HSE improvement programmes and initiatives.
  • Promote and embed a strong health, safety, and environmental culture throughout the organisation.
  • Lead and support HSE-related learning, awareness, and development activities to drive continuous improvement.
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to strengthen HSE performance and share best practice.

Work model: onsite from our Stonehouse location (#LI-onsite)

Qualifications for the Role

  • Experience managing, conducting, and controlling HSE audits is essential.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to influence and build commitment at all levels.
  • Experience coaching, supporting, and advising leaders and line managers on HSE matters.
  • Proven ability to guide, motivate, and develop stakeholders, including direct and indirect reports, to support the successful implementation of HSE policies, standards, and programmes.
  • Collaborative and proactive approach, with a passion for driving continuous improvement and a positive safety culture.
  • Ambition to develop professionally and progress into broader HSE leadership responsibilities over time.

More about us

The Measurement & Analytics Division is among the world’s leading manufacturers and suppliers of smart instrumentation and analyzers, working at the heart of industrial digital transformation. The Measurement & Analytics Division’s portfolio consists of analyzers measuring compositions of gases and liquids; instrumentation measuring process variables such as temperature, pressure, flow, and level; force measurement solutions measuring parameters such as flatness, thickness, and tension; and advanced digital solutions for device management, device health check and predictive maintenance. The Measurement & Analytics Division serves key industries such as oil and gas, chemical, water and wastewater, power, hydrogen, batteries, as well as the marine industry. The Division enables the optimization of industrial processes by providing and analyzing data collected from sensing and smart measurement devices. Parameters such as emission levels and production inputs are measured by providing ‘before’ and ‘after’ values, enabling efficient operations and environmental sustainability through measurement.
