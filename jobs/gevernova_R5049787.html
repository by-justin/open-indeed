Job Description

Job Description Summary

GE Vernova is seeking a seasoned Cyber Security Engineer capable of developing cutting-edge cyber security detection solutions. This role will also be a driving force behind the adoption of new detection technologies based on behavioral analytics and machine learning. This position is responsible for leading the development of enterprise platforms that enable the monitoring, automation, and orchestration of GE's network security platforms, including both physical and virtual IDS, and big-data analytics. The Senior Cyber Security Engineer will be expected to leverage open-source technology and industry standard programming languages to enhance cyber security operations. Success in this role will require delivery of engineering, software development, and build-automation projects in an agile environment.

Job Description

Roles and Responsibilities:

In this role, you will:

  • Shape the technology strategy and approach to securing GE's assets in cloud and on-premise environments
  • Implement detection and monitoring rules to meet security and compliance requirements for GE's IaaS, Paas, and SaaS , OT environments
  • Apply hands-on approach to driving the development, improvement and enhancement of detection technologies in support of GE Vernova's transformation strategy.
  • Provides high-quality cost-effective engineering support to key GE leadership teams to protect the companies brand.
  • Works with cross-functional teams and cross-business teams to drive broad-reaching technology initiatives
  • Promotes best practices, design patterns, standards through workshops, knowledge sharing, and code walk-throughs
  • Builds automation around operations & software development processes (CI/CD)
  • Provides mentorship, advice, and skill enhancement to junior team members.
  • Participates in on-call afterhours support rotation to ensure that critical performance issues are resolved in a timely manner, in accordance with established customer expectations and SLAs.
  • Design, build, and continuously improve detection coverage using AI/ML capabilities, behavioral analytics, and big-data analytics across enterprise environments
  • Build intelligent automation to streamline alert triage, threat prioritization, and response execution — reducing alert fatigue and accelerating mean time to respond (MTTR)
  • Leverage AI/ML to improve detection accuracy and integrate automated decision-making into security workflows
  • Evaluate and drive adoption of emerging security technologies with an AI/ML focus, including proof-of-concept delivery and technical assessments

Monitoring, Investigation & Incident Response

Automation & Software Development
