Company Description

Empower the World to Design!

Canva는 디자인, 프레젠테이션, 문서, 영상, 웹사이트, AI 기반 콘텐츠 제작까지 하나의 플랫폼에서 제공하며 누구나 아이디어를 현실로 만들 수 있도록 돕고 있습니다.

Canva Korea는 한국 사용자들이 더욱 쉽고 효과적으로 Canva를 활용할 수 있도록 현지 시장에 최적화된 콘텐츠와 경험을 만들어가고 있습니다.

팀 소개

International SEO Team은 Canva 웹사이트의 비로그인 영역에 대한 검색 성과를 향상시키고 전 세계 사용자가 Canva를 발견할 수 있도록 돕고 있습니다.

우리는 키워드 리서치, 테크니컬 SEO, 콘텐츠 전략, 아웃리치, 엔지니어링, 데이터 분석, 디자인, 운영 등 다양한 전문성을 가진 글로벌 팀으로 구성되어 있습니다. 검증된 글로벌 SEO 프레임워크와 각 시장의 로컬 인사이트를 결합하여 Canva의 글로벌 성장을 이끌고 있습니다.

포지션 소개

Canva의 SEO는 세계 최고 수준입니다. 우리는 한국 시장에서 콘텐츠를 통한 성장을 직접 만들어갈 SEO Content Specialist를 찾고 있습니다. 이 포지션은 정규직으로, 네이버 생태계를 깊이 이해하고 한국 사용자에게 진정으로 도움이 되는 콘텐츠를 전략적으로 만들어갈 수 있는 분이라면 잘 맞을 것입니다.

네이버 블로그를 직접 운영하며 콘텐츠의 퀄리티와 성과 모두를 책임지고, 프리랜서 카피라이터 팀을 이끌며 한국 시장만의 콘텐츠 전략을 주도해나가게 됩니다. 단순히 콘텐츠를 '만드는' 것이 아니라, 콘텐츠가 실제 비즈니스 성장으로 이어지는 연결고리를 직접 만들어가는 역할입니다.

한국어 원어민으로서의 언어 감각, 네이버에 대한 깊은 이해, 그리고 콘텐츠 팀을 이끈 경험이 있다면 더욱 좋습니다.

About the role

As Canva scales, change continues to be part of our DNA. But we like to think that's all part of the fun. So this will give you the flavour of the type of things you'll be working on when you start, but this will likely evolve.

Canva's SEO is world-class, and we're looking for a passionate SEO Content Specialist to join the International SEO team to help scale our next phase of growth in South Korea! This is a permanent contract, working [full-time - 40 hours per week].

The SEO Content Specialist will work closely with the Korea SEO Lead and local team. The role of an SEO Content Specialist is to create and manage engaging, SEO-optimized copy and content that informs, inspires, and converts local audiences.

As an SEO Content Specialist, you will provide initiative, direction, and support to create helpful and culturally relevant content for users in the South Korean market. Ideally, you are a native speaker with proficiency in Korean & English.

Canva is a fast-growing, mission-driven company, and we're looking for talented people who want to contribute to something meaningful. If you're excited about the opportunity to make an impact, show us what you've got—strong performers in this role have significant opportunities to grow with us.

About the team

The International SEO team is responsible for improving SEO performance of logged-out pages across the Canva website. Our mission in SEO is to empower every user in the world to discover Canva.

We're a global team of specialists spanning keyword research, technical SEO, content strategy, outreach, engineering, data analytics, design, and operations. We work across many regions and languages, combining proven frameworks with deep local market expertise.

Job Description

  • Take full ownership of Canva's Naver Blog as a core organic content channel, with end-to-end responsibility for editorial quality, consistency, and performance

  • Actively manage and engage with comments and community interactions on Naver to strengthen credibility and user trust
  • Track Naver content performance and visibility, using insights to continuously refine editorial strategy and planning

Website Content & Optimization

  • Champion content requirements for landing pages and blog articles, supporting the development of long-term content strategy by maintaining an up-to-date editorial calendar and publishing plan
  • Identify and prioritise high-opportunity keywords and content gaps to grow Canva's traffic share in the Korean market
  • Create and edit SEO-optimized, engaging content about various design topics that align with the SEO team's content strategy and resonate with local audiences
  • Upload and publish content that follows the latest SEO best practices and fits our local tone of voice (as defined by our language managers)
  • Regularly track and optimize landing pages to maintain freshness, improve content credibility, and enhance user engagement
  • Maintain a high level of quality and consistency in alignment with Canva's brand guidelines
  • Provide feedback on content management systems, translation quality, and page layout to continuously improve efficiency and quality

Collaboration & Strategic Partnership

  • Work closely with the wider Korea SEO team to align on goals, priorities, and SEO best practices that drive growth
  • Support broader SEO activities such as keyword research, competitor analysis, and collaboration with SEO Generalist for content production
  • Collaborate with the country SEO team and broader marketing/community teams to ensure content strategy reflects local market dynamics and user needs
  • Contribute local market intelligence and user insights that inform content priorities and opportunity identification
  • Build relationships across functions to understand how your content work supports broader country initiatives

Navigating the Evolution of Search & Discovery

  • Stay up to date on GEO/AEO evolutions and next-generation optimization techniques
