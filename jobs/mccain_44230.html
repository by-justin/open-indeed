Position Title: Health & Safety Manager

Position Type: Regular - Full-Time ​

Requisition ID:  44230

In every role, McCainers are ambitious, curious, and passionate about creating exceptional work experiences - together. With a customer-first mindset, we make doing business with McCain easy.

About the role.

At Scarborough, we’re looking for someone who goes beyond compliance - someone who can shape culture, influence leaders, and inspire teams they don’t directly manage. Of course, safety is non-negotiable.

We are always Safety First. For Us, For Family.

So much so, we’ve built our own internal safety brand - because making sure every colleague goes home safely to their family and friends isn’t just a priority, it’s part of who we are. But this role? It’s bigger than that. This is about setting the bar - not just for Scarborough, but for the wider business. It’s about embedding a safety mindset so deeply that it becomes instinctive. Lived. Breathed. Owned by everyone.

What you’ll be doing.

You’ll sit at the heart of the site leadership team, influencing thinking, challenging norms, and driving a culture where safety isn’t driven by process… but by people. Because here, HSE isn’t a function. It’s a culture. And for the right person, this is more than a role - it’s a genuine opportunity to grow your career, with a pathway towards future Director-level appointments.

You will be able to.

  • Influence and coach leaders to take true ownership of safety
  • Build belief and accountability across teams you don’t directly lead
  • Turn strategy into everyday behaviours on the ground
  • Create momentum — where safety is continuously improving, not standing still
  • Flex your leadership style to get the best out of our teams and processes

You will be accountable for.

  • Driving safe working practices and ensuring full legal compliance
  • Shaping HSE strategy and influencing policy development
  • Leading risk assessments and safe systems of work
  • Embedding HSE standards through daily site presence and coaching
  • Delivering impactful training and inductions
  • Investigating incidents to identify root causes and prevent recurrence
  • Leading audits, inspections, and compliance reporting
  • Owning ISO 45001 & 14001 systems and audit plans
  • Partnering with SLT, HR and Occupational Health on key issues
  • Driving continuous improvement through data, insights and scorecards
  • Leading emergency preparedness and environmental compliance

What you’ll need to be successful.

  • NEBOSH General Certificate & National Diploma (essential)
  • Environmental qualification (essential)
  • Experience within a senior HSE role in manufacturing (7–10 years)
  • Knowledge of ISO 45001 (essential) and ISO 14001 (desirable)
  • An engineering background and familiarity working within manufacturing environments
  • Strong influencing and stakeholder management skills
  • An adaptable mindset, an appreciation of the need to adjust your style when the need arises
  • Experience in food manufacturing, HACCP or DuPont methodologies (desirable)

About the team.

You'll be based at our Whittlesey plant in Peterborough. If you’ve spotted our three wind turbines, then you’ll know where we are! With about 350 employees on site, we’re one of our largest plants across the GB business. Our Whittlesey plant is where we make our famous French Fries as well as our chilled products, such as our Shake Shake Fries.

Don't live near our plant location? We will pay for you to relocate!

This is a real manufacturing career based in Peterborough, with the Fens on your doorstep and a cathedral city five minutes from the factory floor. If relocating is the only thing stopping you applying, talk to us. We can help make the move financially viable.
