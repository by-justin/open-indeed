Job Purpose职位宗旨

The "Future of Sales" project aims to continuously enhance efficiency and effectiveness by optimizing organizational structure and business processes, leveraging the latest technological tools, and based on the SLNB concept, in order to meet evolving customer needs and market competition.

Global has outlined a roadmap for the years 2025 to 2030, with a plan to gradually realize the "Future of Sales" over a span of five years.

This position is responsible for the specific planning and implementation of Global "Future of Sales" and other related projects of Global Sales Development in China. It involves designing and optimizing systems for channel sales processes, sales quality, and sales process management, as well as setting execution and evaluation standards,

And promote the implementation of projects. Assist the Sales Development Director in connecting with the Global Sales Development Team, participate in promoting the design, execution, and advancement of various projects, and achieve the strategic goals of process optimization and efficiency improvement.

Global"Future of Sales"项目旨在基于SLNB理念，通过优化组织架构和业务流程，借助最新的技术工具支持，持续提升效率效能，以满足不断发展的客户需求和市场竞争。

Global规划了从2025-2030年的路线图，计划使用5年时间逐步实现"Future of Sales"。

该岗位负责Global"Future of Sales"及Global Sales Development其他相关项目在中国的具体规划和实施。设计优化相关渠道销售流程、销售质量和销售过程管理的体系，设定执行和考核标准，
