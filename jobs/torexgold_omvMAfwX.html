About the RoleReporting to the Vice President, Mine Technical Services, the Director, Mineral Resources provides corporate technical leadership and governance for Mineral Resource estimation, reconciliation, and related public disclosures across Torex Gold. As the Company's designated Qualified Person for Mineral Resources, the Director is accountable for the accuracy, timeliness, and regulatory compliance of Mineral Resource estimates and applicable exploration disclosures. This role requires the technical depth to independently evaluate complex geological models and estimates, together with the leadership maturity to guide, challenge, and mentor site teams without taking over their work. The Director will work with stakeholders ranging from site geologists and technical specialists to executives and the Board and will support technical due diligence for potential acquisitions. The position is based in Canada and requires travel to site approximately every four to six weeks. About Torex Gold“Our purpose is to transform finite mineral resources into lasting prosperityby positively impacting all the lives we touch.” We are a Canadian mining company engaged in the exploration, development, and production of gold, copper, and silver from our flagship Morelos Complex in Guerrero, which is currently Mexico’s largest single gold producer. The Company also owns the advanced-stage Los Reyes gold-silver project in Sinaloa, Mexico, and a portfolio of early-stage exploration properties, including the Batopilas and Guigui projects in Chihuahua, Mexico, and the Medicine Springs project in Nevada, USA as well as an option to acquire the Gryphon project in Nevada, USA. Torex consistently delivers on our commitments guided by our key strategic objectives: optimize Morelos production and costs; disciplined growth and capital allocation; grow reserves and resources; project delivery excellence; retain and attract best industry talent; and be an industry leader in responsible mining. In addition to realizing the full potential of the Morelos Property, the Company continues to seek opportunities to acquire assets that enable diversification and deliver value to shareholders. We have proven over the past decade that we can make a positive impact on people’s lives and the environment around us, all while improving safety, achieving operational efficiencies, realizing cost reductions, and generating positive return on investment. This is what makes us strong. This is the Torex way.  What We're Looking for:Qualifications:

  • Post-secondary degree in Geology or a closely related geoscience discipline.
  • Current registration as a Professional Geoscientist (P.Geo.) with a recognized professional association.
  • Currently qualified to act as a Qualified Person for Mineral Resources under NI 43-101, with previous experience signing public Mineral Resource disclosures or NI 43-101 technical reports.
  • Minimum of 15 years of progressive mining and/or mining consulting experience.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Spanish is an asset.Experience & Skills:
  • Extensive experience in geological modelling, geostatistics, Mineral Resource estimation, resource classification, and technical documentation in accordance with CIM Best Practice Guidelines.
  • Demonstrated experience across the field-to-model process, including drilling, sampling, chain of custody, QA/QC, database management, and reconciliation.
  • Experience in an owner-operator mining environment with accountability for technical work through its full lifecycle; consulting experience is an asset.
  • Experience with underground and open-pit operations. Precious metals and complex-deposit experience is preferred.
  • Proficiency with major geological modelling and resource-estimation software. Leapfrog and experience with another major platform are preferred.
  • Strong leadership and influencing skills, including the ability to mentor technical teams, work across functions and cultures, and communicate complex matters to executives and the Board. Key Accountabilities Mineral Resource Governance and Estimation
  • Develop and maintain corporate systems, standards, processes, and a clear calendar of expectations for Mineral Resource and Mineral Reserve governance.
  • Oversee and critically review Mineral Resource estimates to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, CIM Best Practice Guidelines, and accepted industry practices.
  • Act as Torex’s designated Qualified Person for Mineral Resources, providing final approval and accountability for applicable public disclosures.Reconciliation
  • Develop and oversee consistent reconciliation standards and processes across Torex operations.
  • Review reconciliation results, investigate material variances, verify consolidated data, and approve final reporting provided to auditors.Leadership and Technical Collaboration
