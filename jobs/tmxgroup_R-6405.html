We are seeking a highly skilled and strategic Solution Architect, Google Workspace & Gemini AI to lead the design, security, and operational enablement of our enterprise Google ecosystem. Reporting directly to the Director of Enterprise Platforms, you will bridge the gap between high-level architectural strategy and hands-on operational delivery. As our definitive subject matter expert (SME), you will dictate the tenant roadmap, establish robust security postures, and empower our IT Operations and L2/L3 Admin teams to safely adopt cutting-edge features. In this role, you will be instrumental in driving secure, compliant, and high-impact enterprise generative AI adoption through Gemini.

Job Location: Hybrid (2–3 days in office) – based in Toronto, ON.

Key Accountabilities

Architecture & Strategic Design

  • Act as the principal designer and engineer for the Google Workspace tenant, ensuring organizational units (OUs), groups, and access controls are optimally structured for scale and security.
  • Continuously evaluate the Google Workspace and Gemini product roadmaps, translating upcoming features into actionable adoption strategies for the enterprise.
  • Design hybrid and cloud-native collaboration workflows that enhance productivity while minimizing administrative overhead.
  • Define and oversee solution execution of workflows required across Okta, ServiceNow, etc.and other identity mgmt solutions in working with Security team and platform managers

Gemini AI Integration & Governance

  • Lead the enterprise rollout of Gemini for Google Workspace, dictating which AI features should be enabled across different user cohorts and OUs.
  • Establish the governance model for generative AI usage within the tenant, ensuring enterprise data protection is actively maintained and AI models do not train on internal proprietary data.
  • Design and document AI use cases, prompt engineering best practices, and integration workflows to maximize ROI on Gemini licensing.

Security, Compliance & Data Protection

  • Define, design, and oversee the implementation of comprehensive Google Workspace security policies, including Context-Aware Access, Data Loss Prevention (DLP) rules, and advanced phishing/malware protections.
  • Establish strict security guidelines for Gemini integrations, ensuring all data flows comply with corporate security standards, privacy policies, and regulatory requirements.
  • Configure and oversee eDiscovery, retention policies, and legal holds utilizing Google Vault.
  • Conduct regular security audits and posture assessments of the Workspace environment, remediating vulnerabilities in partnership with the Cybersecurity team.

Operations Guidance & Admin Enablement

  • Serve as the highest point of technical escalation (Tier 4) and the primary mentor for the IT Operations team and Workspace Administrators.
  • Create highly detailed, prescriptive playbooks and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) detailing exactly how to configure, turn on, and manage Workspace and Gemini features.
  • Lead monthly operational syncs to review tenant health, train admins on new admin console updates, and align the ops team on upcoming architectural changes.
  • Automate routine administrative tasks and bulk operations using tools like GAM (Google Apps Manager) and Google Apps Script, handing off polished scripts to the operations team for daily use.
  • Embed Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs), including availability, reliability, maintainability, and supportability into all high-level SaaS and Google ecosystem designs to guarantee seamless lifecycle management.
  • Ensure all new cloud workflows and tenant modifications seamlessly integrate with established ITIL processes, specifically Change Management, Incident Management, and Problem Management.

Must Have(s)

  • 10+ years of progressive engineering and architectural experience managing large-scale, enterprise-level Google Workspace environments.
  • Deep, hands-on expertise with the Google Workspace Admin Console, Google Cloud Identity, and Google Vault.
  • Strong understanding of generative AI mechanics and specific experience configuring and deploying Gemini for Google Workspace (or equivalent enterprise AI assistants like Copilot).
  • Proven mastery of Google Workspace security constructs: SAML/SSO integration, 2-Step Verification, DLP, Context-Aware Access, OAuth app whitelisting/blacklisting, and API controls and Mailflow.
  • High proficiency in automation and scripting specifically for Google environments (GAM, GAMADV-XTD3, Python, and Google Apps Script).
  • Professional Google Workspace Administrator.
  • Professional Cloud Security Engineer (Google Cloud).
  • Exceptional ability to translate complex architectural and security concepts into easy-to-follow, step-by-step guidance for operations teams.
  • Strong documentation skills, with a track record of creating robust technical runbooks and architectural diagrams.
  • Ability to effectively influence and collaborate with cross-functional leaders, specifically across Enterprise IT, Cybersecurity, and Procurement.

Nice to Have(s)

  • ITIL Foundation Certification (highly preferred) or a proven, robust understanding of the ITIL service lifecycle framework (Service Design, Service Transition, Service Operation).
  • Any certifications or formal training related to AI integration, AI governance, or Prompt Engineering.
