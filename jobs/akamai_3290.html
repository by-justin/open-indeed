Description

Do you have a passion for network engineering technologies?

Are you looking for the freedom to innovate and help shape the next generation of the network?

Join our Network Operations Engineering team!

Our operations team is responsible for maintaining the reliability, performance, and availability of large-scale network infrastructure. Work night shift 18:00 - 2:00.

Partner with the best

A Senior Network Operations Engineer joins a skilled 24x7 team ensuring reliability, performance, and availability of large-scale network infrastructure. Collaboration with cross-functional teams supports CDN and cloud compute platforms while driving improvements in network observability and operational excellence.

As a Senior Network Operations Engineer you will be responsible for:

  • Monitoring and maintaining network infrastructure to ensure optimal performance, reliability, and security across all systems and components.
  • Troubleshooting complex network issues and coordinating resolution efforts to minimize downtime and service disruptions.
  • Leading and supporting the resolution of critical technical incidents effectively and efficiently to ensure optimal outcomes.
  • Providing expertise during incident response and conducting operational reviews to ensure thorough analysis and effective resolution.
  • Designing, developing, and maintaining alerts, runbooks, dashboards, and monitoring solutions to enhance fault detection and operational visibility effectively.
  • Developing and maintaining operational tools, automation, and technical documentation to support extensive network infrastructure effectively and efficiently.

Do what you love

To be successful in this role you will:

  • Have a bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent.
  • Have relevant hands-on experience operating and supporting large-scale network environments.
  • Have excellent communication, stakeholder management, and customer service skills.
  • Possess strong expertise in IP networking, routing, and switching; CCNA, JNCIA, or equivalent certifications are desirable.
  • Have experience operating and troubleshooting service provider networks and Internet routing protocols, including BGP, IS-IS, OSPF, and MPLS.
  • Have experience writing, modifying, and troubleshooting SQL queries.
