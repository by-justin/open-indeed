Description

Solution Architect – Secure Networking – India

Location: Gurgaon / NCR Department: SME Engineering – Secure Networking

About Fortinet

Fortinet has been founded on the principle of converging networking and security into a unified solution. Over the past 12 years, Fortinet has significantly expanded its Wired and Wireless networking portfolio through organic innovation and strategic acquisitions.

This growth has been recognized by Gartner®, which positioned Fortinet as a Leader in the 2024 and 2025 Magic Quadrant™ for Enterprise Wired and Wireless LAN Infrastructure.

If you have 12+ years of hands-on experience designing, implementing, and troubleshooting enterprise Wired and Wireless networking solutions, this is an opportunity to join Fortinet's Subject Matter Expert (SME) team and take your networking expertise into the next level of Network Security.

Role Overview

As a Solution Architect – Secure Networking, you will be part of the SME Engineering team supporting the India & SAARC region.

You will work closely with Account Teams and Field Systems Engineers to position Fortinet's Secure Networking portfolio, including:

  • Network Access Control (NAC)
  • Wireless
  • Enterprise Switching
  • Identity & Access Management (AAA)
  • Voice Solutions
  • ZTNA
  • SD-Branch

The role involves providing second-level pre-sales technical support, leading complex customer engagements, conducting POCs and demonstrations, and acting as a technical bridge between the field teams and Fortinet's Consulting Systems Engineering (CSE) organization.

Key Responsibilities

Pre-Sales & Technical Support

  • Provide second-level pre-sales support to the Field SE organization.
  • Support RFPs/RFIs with solution design, specifications, and product information.
  • Support strategic and complex customer projects to ensure successful Fortinet engagements.
  • Lead technical discussions and articulate technology capabilities and differentiated value.
  • Deliver technical presentations, demonstrations, and Proofs of Concept (POCs).
  • Provide hands-on technical assistance during customer engagements.
  • Conduct external workshops and POCs for prospects and customers in collaboration with Account SEs.
  • Perform hands-on testing to validate network designs, POC environments, and network deployments.

Technical Content & Enablement

  • Develop technical materials to support sales activities, including:Presentations / slidewareSolution briefsWhite papersSuccess storiesTechnical notesCompetitive comparisons
  • Design and conduct internal technical workshops.
  • Enable and disseminate product knowledge across Sales and Channel teams.

SME & Product Collaboration

  • Act as a technical point of contact for the CSE team.
  • Consolidate customer requirements and technical feedback across the India region.
  • Validate business cases and create/escalate product feature requests.
  • Report project activities and analyze project trends.
  • Provide market and competitive insights.
  • Participate in the development of commercial strategies.
  • Collect competitive intelligence on networking and security solutions and share insights with Account and Channel teams.

Travel

  • Expected to work from the office four days per week to facilitate collaboration with extended teams and build strong working relationships.
