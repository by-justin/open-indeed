The Studio has a mission to create top quality, innovative games for players of all ages on PlayStation. Want to learn more? Head over to the PlayStation Blog: PlayStation Blog .

Role overviewTeam ASOBI is looking for a talented 3D Environment Artist. The ideal candidate will be responsible for creating playful and stunning looking environment art for our game.

Responsibilities

  • You create high quality 3D models and textures both organic and architectural.

  • Layout Levels using our inhouse editors.

  • Troubleshoot Levels and maintain their design integrity, visual quality, and performance.

  • Close collaboration with the Environment Art Team and Level Designers.

Required Skill and Experience

  • An experience as an Environment Artist in Game Development, Feature Film or VFX.

  • Excellent modeling and UV skills in Maya / Zbrush or other equivalent software to create high quality environment assets at a fast pace.

  • Strong skill at creating beautiful environment compositions that elevate the intended gameplay.

  • Strong ability to envision entire levels with minimal concept art and direction.

  • Strong texturing skills using substance painter and designer.

  • An emphasis on realistic texturing techniques applied to a playful art style.

  • Being able to closely adapt a specific art style

  • Being used to work on tasks autonomously and to deliver results on time

  • Team player with positive attitude and great communication skills

  • Task driven, highly motivated and proactive.

  • Passion for Art, game design, and gaming

Language proficiency

  • Basic Japanese language skill is required alongside desire to improve (lessons will be provided and paid for).

Note: You will be required to pass a practical test using your own time and resources. Please refrain from applying if you cannot meet these criteria.
