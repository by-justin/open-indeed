Position Overview:

The  Senior Ops Staff  is responsible for executing and supporting day-to-day operational activities across Blue Dart's Service Centres, Hubs, Warehouses and Processing Units while ensuring high standards of shipment accuracy, operational productivity and service quality.

In addition to performing operational processing activities, the role supports Shift In-charges by assisting in work allocation, coaching newly inducted employees, handling operational exceptions and ensuring adherence to Blue Dart's Standard Operating Procedures. The incumbent plays an important role in maintaining operational discipline, supporting service commitments and driving consistent execution across operational workstations.

Job Purpose:

To ensure efficient shipment processing and operational execution by maintaining high levels of productivity, quality, safety and operational compliance while supporting the Shift In-charge in achieving daily operational objectives and mentoring junior operations team members.

Operations Execution

• Perform receiving, segregation, sorting, scanning, bagging, tagging and shipment processing activities across operational workstations.

• Support loading and unloading of shipments while ensuring safe material handling practices.
