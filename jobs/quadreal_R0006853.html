Role Overview:

QuadReal Property Group is seeking a detail-oriented Leasing Coordinator to cover an 18-month maternity leave. This is a fast-paced, deadline-driven role at the centre of the leasing team's deal execution — you will own the end-to-end process of getting transactions closed, recorded, and published, while keeping the team organized and on track.

Responsibilities:

Deal Execution & Documentation

  • Prepare and manage all deal package documentation including generating Trade Record Sheets
  • Upload completed deal packages into Deal Manager and maintain accurate deal inputs
  • Update all deals done trackers upon execution, including the consolidated lease expiry roll, deals done tracker, leasing commission fees log, team deals done dashboard, FindSpace, and MLS

Listings & Online Presence

  • Prepare MLS agreements, circulate for signatures and upload to MLS
  • Ensure listings are current across FindSpace, MLS, Altus, Arcestra, and the QuadReal website
  • Create feature sheets for new listings using the Marq platform
  • Track and manage external listings
  • Maintain Expiry Rent Roll Sheet

Finance & Administration

  • Process monthly expense reports for all leasing team members in Workday
  • Submit broker commission payments to accounting and process invoices in Yardi, including wire transfer coordination
  • Code and manage all departmental invoices; coordinate with Accounting and request new vendor setups as required
  • Update the Marketing Property Level Budgets spreadsheet monthly

Marketing & Events

  • Prepare and coordinate annual MASTERS GOLF distribution list and deliverables
  • Assist with creation of RFPs and marketing materials
  • Arrange building photography as required
  • Maintain and update the broker contact database (February and September)

Experience and Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of administrative experience, with commercial real estate experience strongly preferred
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint); familiarity with Yardi, Deal Manager, FindSpace, Marq, or similar platforms is an asset
  • Exceptional organizational skills with the ability to manage competing priorities and tight deadlines
  • Strong attention to detail — accuracy in deal documentation and data entry is critical
  • Superior written and verbal communication skills; comfortable liaising with internal departments and external brokers
  • Self-directed and proactive — able to work independently and anticipate team needs without being prompted
