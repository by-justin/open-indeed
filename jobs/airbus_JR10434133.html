Job Description

Job Description:

Background:

Fuel Systems team is a part of the ‘Internal Flow & Data Analytics’ group under the Structures department and plays a key role providing solutions through competencies in the area of Thermal and Internal Flow analysis.

The Job Holder will act as a System Simulation Engineer for the Fuel Systems (ATA28) perimeter with the task to perform numerical simulations (0D/1D) to ensure the certification of the aircraft fuel system.

The job holder will be responsible for understanding, development, maintenance and enhancement of the 0D/1D numerical models pertaining to Aircraft Fuel System.

Role:  Support all Fuel Systems Engineering Simulation activities

Key Words: Programming Language (Python, MATLAB), Thermofluidic 0D/1D System Simulation (Amesim,MATLAB/Simulink,Flomaster, Python), Single Phase/Two Phase Flow Simulation & Analysis, Mathematical/Numerical Modelling, System understanding (Fuel system preferred)

Technical Skills

As the successful candidate, you will be able to demonstrate the following skills and competencies:

Essential skills

  • Strong experience in numerical, physics based 0D/1D modelling, simulation and analysis (Matlab/Simulink/Flomaster/Amesim/Python).
  • Profound Thermodynamics, Fluid Dynamics, Heat Transfer, Refrigerants, Two Phase Flow and Cooling Systems understanding.
  • Strong experience in applying first-principles engineering (physics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics) to validate and challenge simulation results.
  • Experience in developing numerical models in Python to solve heat transfer and fluid flow problems.
  • Experience in analyzing test data and extracting system-level insights to drive simulation.
  • Ability to learn new tools and processes to ensure the project deliveries.
  • Ability to build the understanding of fuel systems of various aircraft to build Matlab/Simulink/ Flomaster/Python models is essential.
  • Ability to work in collaboration with a team to ensure the quality of the deliverable is must, as the project deliveries may directly contribute towards the certification of the aircraft.

Desired skills

  • Knowledge of FORTRAN, JAVA would also be beneficial.

  • Broad understanding/knowledge of System Design/Development processes.

  • Ability to establish strong working relationships with other areas of the organization.

Soft Skills:

  • Teamwork and excellent communication skills
  • Willingness to learn new skills, Self-motivation and Self-initiative
  • Fail-fast mindset and Solutions approach
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to integrate a team which values respect, integrity, teamwork, reliability, customer focus and creativity.
  • Strong Safety and Quality Mindset: Consistently upholds a culture of safety and quality. Demonstrates responsibility and a proactive attitude in preventing issues before they arise.
  • Attention to Detail: Delivers work with accuracy and thoroughness. Carefully reviews data, designs and documentation to ensure compliance with engineering and quality standards.
  • Rigor and Discipline: Applies a structured and methodical approach to problem-solving and daily tasks. Maintains traceability, consistency, and alignment with defined processes.
  • Speak-Up Culture: Openly shares concerns, identifies potential risks, and constructively challenges when needed.
  • Effective Communication: Clearly conveys information to both internal teams and external stakeholders, including suppliers, customers and cross-functional partners.

Qualification: Completed Bachelors or Masters degree in Aerospace, Mechanical, Thermal or other related streams with minimum of 5+ years of relevant experience.
