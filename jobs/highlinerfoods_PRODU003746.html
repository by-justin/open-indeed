Job Description

Job Overview:

This 2nd Shift Production Superintendent oversees efficient production of quality products through effective communication with managers, team members, and all other departments within the organization.

Essential Duties and responsibilities

  • Responsible to ensure the attainment of Facility Food Safety Standards, Production of Products that meet or exceed customer and product specifications.  Food Safety- Compliance to specs, HACCP and GMP's.

  • Attainment of high scores on Quality audits and reduction of customer complaints.

  • Assurance of up-to-date, cost effective manufacture of products through increased efficiency, quality standards, and company objectives.

  • Support the timely and cost effective manufacture of products through increased efficiency, quality standards, and company objectives.

  • Keep current with all new labor laws including HR, as well as, all governmental regulations, etc. to ensure a safe & healthy working environment for themselves and those they supervise.

  • Implement Lean Manufacturing Principles or ‘Problem Solving & Lessons Learned’ procedures to ensure continual growth within the company.

  • Provide assistance to Research & Development (R&D) in the processing of new products.  A basic knowledge of cuttings and processing specifications is required in this area.

  • Managers are responsible for developing the proper attitudes toward safety and health in themselves and in those they supervise, and for promoting that all operations are performed with the utmost regard for the safety and health of all personnel involved including themselves.

  • Ensure the facility and equipment are properly maintained, cleaned, and sanitized on a daily basis to produce products that meet HLF and all governmentally established Food Safety standards.

  • Responsible for the continual training of their production employees in cost effective and safe production methods and performance.

  • Responsible for, and has the authority to, resolve production problems and complexities and acts as an intermediary for the Manufacturing

  • Manager on those issues that need not go to that level, in compliance with established policies and objectives.

  • Closely review of performance, analysis of reports, downtime, labor and material usage, etc., with the Manager, Manufacturing

Qualifications

  • BS or BA degree in a related technical field.

  • 3 to 5 years of leadership experience with a background in consumable production procedures and supervision.

  • A basic knowledge of cuttings and processing specifications is required.

  • Basic knowledge and understanding of both Scheduling procedures and costing of products is required to ensure the production of products in an efficient & cost effective manner.

  • Must have the ability to effectively delegate responsibilities and follow up with projects to completion.

  • Good communication and mathematical skills and a good rapport with coworkers and subordinates.

  • Self-motivated and capable of sound decisions and viable solutions to problems.

  • Highly collaborative, engages other leaders and team members to resolve challenges together.

  • Demonstrates a passion for product quality, food safety and standards

  • Demonstrates a commitment to driving a focus on health and safety practices everyday

About High Liner Foods

Founded in 1899 in Lunenburg, Nova Scotia , High Liner Foods has grown from a small salt fish company into a leading North American processor and marketer of value-added frozen seafood . Our trusted brands — including High Liner, Fisher Boy, Mirabel, Sea Cuisine, C.Wirthy & Co, Mrs. Paul’s, Van de Kamp’s, and Icelandic Seafood — are enjoyed by families, restaurants, and institutions across Canada and the United States.

Guided by our purpose of Reimagining Seafood to Nourish Life , we combine over a century of expertise with innovation, consumer insight, and responsible practices to deliver high-quality seafood people can trust.

At High Liner Foods, we are a people-first organization . We invest in our employees through development opportunities, collaborative teamwork, and a culture where safety, well-being, and growth are priorities. Whether in our offices, plants, or distribution teams, our people work together to deliver excellence and drive the future of seafood.
