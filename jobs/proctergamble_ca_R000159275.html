Job Description

You are a student looking for a meaningful internship? Come and live a P&G business experience as a Digital trader Intern! Did you know P&G invented the concept of brands and brand management? Over the years, we have remained consistently on the cutting edge of marketing & media, always finding new and exciting ways to reach consumers, from the classic store to the most sophisticated digital technology.

Our Digital trader internship offer is a unique opportunity to obtain insights into the world of communications through a fast-moving consumer goods company.

Start Date: January 2027

YOUR CONTRIBUTION TO P&G’S SUCCESS

Join P&G’s Media Team and play a key role in shaping the media strategy and performance of some of the most iconic brands in France: Ariel, Pampers, Gillette, Oral-B, Always, Head & Shoulders, Mr. Proper , and more.

As a Digital Trader Intern , you will be fully immersed in the execution and optimization of high-impact digital media campaigns, working closely with senior media experts and agency partners.

In this role, you will:

  • Manage and operate digital media platforms across Programmatic, Search and Social , ensuring smooth campaign execution from launch to delivery
  • Contribute to the design of digital media strategies , based on business objectives and brand priorities
  • Ensure excellence in campaign execution across both brand-building and performance-driven initiatives
  • Monitor, analyze and optimize campaign performance on an ongoing basis, turning data into actionable insights
  • Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to drive continuous improvement and innovation
  • Bring fresh, innovative ideas to media strategies and stay ahead of the market by actively tracking, analyzing and documenting digital and media industry trends

YOU ARE THE RIGHT FIT IF YOU ARE/HAVE:

  • Student currently enrolled in digital marketing program of a Business / Media specialized school
  • Proficiency in French (oral and written) is a plus and a good level of English
  • Accountable, Autonomous, Organized, Curious, Dedicated, Analytical, Agile.

YOUR ADVANTAGES :

  • Monthly allowance: 1 600 EUR/month
  • 5 days of paid leave for a 6-month internship
  • Bridge days offered by the company (up to 3 days, depending on the internship period)
  • Company support for 55% on public transports costs in Paris area or eco mobility support (eg. bike)
  • Tickets Restaurant card by Edenred (optional)
  • Company restaurant
  • Work from Home (1 day per week)
  • Xmas hamper
  • Conciergerie services on site
  • Gym room on site for free
  • Coupons on our P&G products

Rejoins l’équipe Média de P&G et joue un rôle clé dans la définition de la stratégie et de la performance des certaines des marques les plus emblématiques en France telles que : Ariel, Pampers, Gillette, Oral-B, Always, Head & Shoulders, Mr. Propre etc.

Dans ce poste, tu dois :

  • Gérer et opérer les différentes plateformes de médias numériques à travers de la Programmatique ainsi que la Recherche et les Réseaux Sociaux, en assurant une exécution fluide des campagnes depuis le lancement jusqu'à la fin.
