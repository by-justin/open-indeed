公司描述

We are seeking a Strategic AI Industrial Solution Architector as JMP with deep expertise in AI full stack technologies, industrial operations and enterprise digital transformation. This role requires not only strong technical capabilities, but also strategic thinking and business transformation leadership.

我们正在寻找一位技术型管理培训生，具备战略视野的AI全栈工业解决方案架构师。候选人不仅需要拥有扎实的AI与工业数字化技术能力，还需具备较强的业务战略理解与企业转型推动能力。

The ideal candidate must be capable of aligning technology strategy with business objectives, embedding AI capabilities into operational value chains, and designing scalable, reusable, and modular AI platform architectures that accelerate industrial innovation and business outcomes.

该岗位需要能够将技术战略与业务目标深度融合，将AI能力嵌入业务价值实现过程，并构建具备“最小可复用柔性开发单元（Minimum Reusable Flexible Development Unit）”能力的AI平台架构，以支撑企业持续创新与规模化推广。

职位描述

Job Responsibilities / 工作职责

  1. Business-Driven AI Strategy & Architecture业务驱动的AI战略与架构设计

Define AI and industrial digitalization strategies aligned with enterprise business objectives. Translate operational challenges and business opportunities into scalable AI solution roadmaps. Ensure technology investments directly support measurable business value realization. Build modular, reusable, and service-oriented AI platform capabilities. Design “Minimum Reusable Flexible Development Units” to accelerate rapid deployment and scalable replication across business scenarios.

制定与企业业务目标一致的AI及工业数字化战略。将运营挑战与业务机会转化为可落地的AI解决方案路线图。确保技术投入能够直接支撑业务价值实现。构建模块化、可复用、服务化的AI平台能力。设计“最小可复用柔性开发单元”，实现业务场景快速部署与规模化复制。

  1. Industrial AI Transformation Leadership工业AI转型领导能力

Lead enterprise-level AI transformation initiatives across manufacturing and operations. Drive AI adoption by embedding intelligent capabilities into operational workflows and decision-making processes. Collaborate with business leaders to identify high-value industrial AI use cases. Balance strategic architecture planning with practical execution and delivery.

主导制造与运营领域企业级AI转型项目。将智能能力嵌入业务流程与运营决策体系，推动AI真正落地。与业务管理层协同识别高价值AI应用场景。平衡战略规划与实际落地执行能力。

  1. AI Platform & Data Ecosystem Engineering AI平台与数据生态建设

Architect enterprise AI platforms integrating data, analytics, AI models, workflows, and industrial systems. Enable cloud-edge-device collaborative architecture. Standardize reusable AI services, APIs, data models, and workflow components. Establish scalable data governance and industrial knowledge frameworks.

构建融合数据、分析、AI模型、工作流及工业系统的企业AI平台。建设云-边-端协同架构。标准化AI服务、API、数据模型及流程组件。建立可扩展的数据治理与工业知识体系。

  1. AI Productization & Continuous Innovation AI产品化与持续创新

Drive AI capability productization for sustainable enterprise reuse. Promote agile and iterative AI solution delivery. Introduce Generative AI, AI Agents, and Industrial Copilot capabilities into industrial operations. Continuously optimize AI architecture for scalability, flexibility, and business agility.

推动AI能力产品化，实现企业级持续复用。推进敏捷化与迭代式AI交付模式。推动生成式AI、AI Agent及工业Copilot在业务中的应用。持续优化AI架构的扩展性、柔性与业务敏捷性。

  1. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Executive Communication 跨部门协同与高层沟通

Act as the bridge between business stakeholders and technology teams. Communicate strategic AI vision and transformation roadmap to executives and customers. Support enterprise consulting, solution workshops, and strategic planning sessions.

作为业务与技术团队之间的桥梁。向管理层及客户清晰传达AI战略与转型路线。支持企业咨询、方案共创及战略规划工作。
