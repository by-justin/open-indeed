Purpose The Water Quality Technologist is the primary service provider to ensure that Guardian treatment programs perform at the least cost operating scenario within customer sites. The Water Quality Technologist works closely with Account Managers, Business Development Representatives, and the Water Treatment Technical Director to maintain a high level of customer satisfaction through achieving a high-performance standard. This role includes potential travel of up to 25%, with the possibility of 3-4 consecutive nights away.

What you will do

  • Visit client sites to conduct water quality testing on Boiler, Cooling Tower, and Closed systems.
  • Write Technical Service Reports for clients which document successful application of chemical treatment programs within the DATAGUARD software application.
  • Achieve target water quality through adjustment of chemical dosages and optimization of water treatment equipment including programmable controllers, probes, meters, and chemical pumping systems.
  • Assist in account management through ensuring inventories are maintained at proper levels and that all preventive maintenance is performed on equipment to ensure reliable operation and optimum performance.
  • Communicate with customers to ensure that the treatment program is a strong collaborative effort where Guardian oversight assures success.
  • Work safely and with minimal supervision in all aspects of the role including vehicle travel to various sites.
  • Ensure all hazard assessments are completed when necessary and provide training for customers on safe handling and procedures related to chemicals.
  • Support the sales team through participation in new account set up and in training and mentoring new staff members.
  • Support the Mission, Vision and Core Values of Guardian through your proactive nature and positive attitude.
  • Comply with and support our Quality and Environmental Health and Safety Policies and Procedures.
  • Continuously learn through extensive training and hands on experiences to become a heating, cooling, and HVAC water treatment specialist.

The ideal candidate is a life-long learner and strives for career advancement through a progressive career strategy of becoming a Team Leader or Sales Account Manager. The candidate prefers an environment of accountability where rewards are aligned with results and customer success.
