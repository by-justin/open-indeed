Description

As a Senior Advanced Project Engineer in Honeywell’s Projects & Automation Solution (PAS) group, you will be supporting the Sheet Manufacturing market playing a critical role in the delivery of our advanced engineering projects. You will be responsible for developing and aligning project technical scope, objectives, and deliverables in collaboration with cross-functional teams. By developing project work plans, including schedules, resource fulfillment, and managing individual cost controls, you will ensure efficient project execution and successful delivery of our solutions. Your industry experience and expertise in project management and technical engineering will contribute to the overall success of our projects.

In this role, you will lead the success of our advanced engineering projects. You will be responsible for coordinating and communicating with stakeholders, identifying and eliminating project risks and issues, and driving continuous improvement initiatives to enhance project management processes and practices.

The markets you will be serving include Pulp & Paper, Lithium-ion Battery (LIB), aluminum, rubber, plastic films, as well as OEMs and other processes where a continuous sheet is produced. The Honeywell solutions delivered for these projects include Quality Control Systems (QCS), Cross Direction (CD) Control Systems, Machine Direction (MD) Control Systems, Distributed Control Systems (DCS), Web inspection, and other Continuous Web Solutions (CWS).

You will report directly to a Project Engineering Manager and work remotely or from a dedicated Honeywell location when not deployed for project activities at one of our many customer locations across the U.S. and Canada.  You will also work closely with our aftermarket service branch, Lifecycle Services and Solutions (LSS), with their Regional Operations Collaboration Center (ROCC) located in Duluth GA.

At Honeywell, our people leaders play a critical role in developing and supporting our employees to help them perform at their best and drive change across the company. They are there to build a strong team by recruiting high performing talent, identifying and developing successors, driving retention and engagement, and fostering a collaborative work culture.
