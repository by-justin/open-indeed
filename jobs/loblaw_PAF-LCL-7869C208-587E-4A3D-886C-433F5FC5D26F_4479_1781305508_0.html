As a Technical Lead, WMS – Manhattan Active (Supply Chain) , you are responsible for the design, configuration, integration, testing, implementation, and stabilization of our Manhattan Active Warehouse, Labour and Yard Management Systems. You will be part of a diverse, collaborative and agile team that is constantly pushing what is possible with our processes, skills and systems driving the strategic delivery of our Supply Chain Systems roadmap. What You’ll Do:

  • Lead end-to-end solution design and delivery for Manhattan Active WM implementations, enhancements, and site conversions.
  • Plan and lead technical and business cutover activities for WMS site implementations.
  • Partner with Distribution Centre Operations, business teams, product owners, vendors, and technology partners to understand requirements, assess fit/gap, and drive consensus on scalable solutions.
  • Translate business requirements into detailed functional and technical designs, including process flows, use cases, configuration requirements, interface impacts, test scenarios, and operational support needs.
  • Configure and guide Manhattan Active WM solutions, including data elements, strategies, extended attributes, facility setup, master data, and environment configuration.
  • Use Manhattan and technical toolsets such as ActiveGo, Config Director, ProActive, Protec, Postman & REST APIs, SQL, MIF, and SCI reporting tools.
  • Lead testing activities across SIT, UAT, regression, smoke testing, performance testing, and defect triage.
  • Maintain clear, concise documentation for functional designs, technical designs, configuration decisions, test scenarios, support models, and operational processes.
  • Coach and mentor team members on Manhattan Active WM design, configuration, testing, troubleshooting, and implementation best practices.
  • Drive adoption of new Manhattan Active capabilities and help influence the product roadmap by identifying opportunities to reduce customization and improve DC operations. What You Bring:
  • 5+ years of hands-on experience with Manhattan WMS.
  • 2+ years of Manhattan Active WM experience preferred.
  • Deep hands-on experience designing, configuring, testing, and supporting Manhattan WMS or Manhattan Active WM solutions.
  • Strong fit/gap analysis skills, with the ability to assess business requirements against standard Manhattan Active functionality and recommend practical, scalable solutions.
  • Experience creating detailed functional designs, process flows, use cases, test scenarios, and support documentation.
  • Strong technical troubleshooting skills across configuration, interfaces, data, APIs, environments, and production issues.
  • Experience with relational databases and SQL, preferably MySQL and/or Oracle.
  • Strong REST API knowledge, including experience using tools such as Postman to view, extract, update, and validate system data.
  • Experience working with integration technologies and message flows, ideally including MIF, MHE interfaces, host systems, and master data feeds.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, evolving environment while managing multiple priorities and driving high-quality delivery.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence business and technical teams. What You’ve Done:
  • Led or played a senior role in at least one full Manhattan WMS or Manhattan Active WM site implementation, conversion, or go-live; multiple implementations strongly preferred.
  • Delivered WMS solutions from requirements through design, configuration, testing, cutover, go-live, and hypercare.
  • Worked with SQL, APIs, interface messages, configuration tools, data conversion, and environment troubleshooting.
  • Completed an undergraduate degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, or a related field, or have equivalent practical experience. Nice to Have:
  • Experience with IBM Cognos SCI reporting.
  • Experience with grocery, retail, food distribution, high-volume DCs, or complex multi-site supply chain environments.
  • Knowledge of ELS Labour Management, Yard Management.
