Job Description

The PrIME KU provides daily support to the overall operation via troubleshooting and maintenance of the PrIME warehouse management system which enables site throughput flowing and meeting operational performance goals. The PrIME KU also owns standardized reporting & project testing to support ongoing maintenance and all changes. This person is the site technical expert on PrIME and, as such, needs to understand the front end of the system as a key user as well as the back end with deep knowledge of warehousing operation processes.

P&G is home to many of the world's most trusted brands, including Tide, Dawn, Pampers, Charmin, Bounty, Crest, Gillette, and Swiffer. At the Dayton Fulfillment Center, our team uses advanced automation and robotics technology to move these products to customers efficiently, safely, and reliably.
