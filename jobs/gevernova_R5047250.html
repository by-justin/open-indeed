Job Description

Nous sommes à la recherche d’un(e) Ingénieur(e) en mécanique pour diriger la conception et le soutien au cycle de vie des unités de puissance hydraulique et des systèmes mécaniques essentiels au fonctionnement des centrales hydroélectriques. Ce poste est axé sur la conception et la coordination des auxiliaires mécaniques, notamment les pompes, les vannes et les actionneurs utilisés dans les vannes principales d’admission et les régulateurs de vitesse. Une solide expérience dans le dimensionnement et la spécification d’équipements mécaniques tels que les pompes, accumulateurs, vannes, actionneurs et tuyauterie est essentielle. Le rôle comprend également un soutien technique tout au long du cycle de vie des équipements, incluant la chaîne d’approvisionnement, l’assurance qualité, ainsi que les activités de construction et de mise en service des générateurs hydroélectriques.

Responsabilités et activités :

About the role:

We are seeking a Lead Mechanical Engineer to drive the design and lifecycle support of hydraulic power units and mechanical systems essential to the operation of hydropower plants. This role focuses on the design and coordination of mechanical auxiliaries; particularly pumps, valves, and actuators, used in main inlet valves and speed governors. A strong background in sizing and specifying mechanical equipment such as pumps, accumulators, valves, actuators, and piping is essential. The role also includes providing technical support across the equipment lifecycle, including supply chain, quality assurance, and Construction & Commissioning activities related to hydro generators.

About Us:

We are a purpose-built company leading a new era of energy – electrifying the world while simultaneously working to help decarbonize it! Our culture is built on inclusivity, diversity, and equality, creating a dynamic environment where every team member can thrive. We are united by our mission to lead the energy transition and make a tangible impact on the future of energy.

GE Vernova’s Hydro Power business designs, develops, manufactures, and services advanced technologies that harness the power of water to deliver reliable and sustainable power. For more than a century, hydropower has played an essential role in the North American and global electricity systems. Today, hydropower is the world’s largest source of renewable energy, and its pumped storage technology is the world’s largest source of energy storage. But to reach net-zero globally by 2050, we need to double the amount of hydro currently installed. Whether it’s through pumped storage, run-of-river, or by upgrading the existing installed base, hydropower will continue to be a cornerstone of North America’s energy future. Join us at GE Vernova and be part of a team that is not only shaping the future of energy but also fostering a culture of excellence and innovation. Together, we have the energy to change the world!

  • Provide support on technical tendering activities (site survey included)
