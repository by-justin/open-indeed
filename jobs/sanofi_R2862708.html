About the job

Ready to push the limits of what’s possible? Join Sanofi Digital Data & AI and you can play a vital part in the performance of our entire business while helping to make an impact on millions around the world.

The Head of Data Governance Product Line is a strategic Digital leadership role responsible for driving the development, implementation, and evolution of data governance products and platforms across Sanofi's global operations. We are looking for a senior Digital Product leader, who will play a critical role in continuing evolution of our enterprise-wide AI-ready data capabilities, ensuring data trustworthiness, and establishing data governance standards that support Sanofi's mission to chase the miracles of science.

As a senior member of the Digital Data & AI team, he/she will report directly to the Global Head of Data, AI, Governance Platforms. This role will closely work with multiple transversal Digital teams across Sanofi's value chain, as well as key business stakeholders (including, but not limited to, legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and various business data office teams), and will have exposure to Sanofi executive committee leadership in accountability for enterprise-wide Data Governance.

About Sanofi

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

***Hybrid Schedule - 3 days per week onsite - required. ***

Main Responsibilities:

Product Ownership

  • Bring a product-first mindset to Data Governance ecosystem within Sanofi
  • Own the design, development, and deployment of innovative Digital products that will enable and accelerate AI-Readiness of data within Sanofi
  • Manage the lifecycle for data governance products and platforms, ensuring they meet business requirements and regulatory compliance standards
  • Drive innovation in data governance technologies, including metadata management, data lineage, data quality frameworks, and automated governance solutions
  • Collaborate with DevOps, Platform, Software and Data Engineering team members, and business stakeholders to build and deliver fit-for-purpose solutions

Strategic Leadership & Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Define and execute the strategic roadmap for data governance products and platforms across all business units (R&D, Clinical, M&S, Commercial, Corporate)
  • Lead the Data Governance transformation from traditional compliance-focused approaches to product-centric, AI-Ready data capabilities
  • Drive change management with senior Digital and Business leaders to align data governance initiatives with business objectives and digital transformation goals
  • Champion the adoption of AI-Ready Data Charter principles and standards across the organization
  • Work closely with Product Owners, Technical Data Stewards, and Business Data Stewards to ensure effective governance implementation

Governance & Compliance

  • Ensure all data governance products comply with regulatory requirements, data privacy laws, and Sanofi's internal policies
  • Establish data quality frameworks and monitoring capabilities across priority datasets
  • Implement enterprise data governance with clear ownership, roles, and responsibilities per domain
  • Manage enterprise-wide data readiness compliance risks through collaboration with various business teams and risk and compliance groups

Team Leadership & Development

  • Build and lead a high-performing team of product owners, and technical data governance specialists
  • Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement within the AI-Ready Data Product Line
  • Provide mentorship and career development opportunities for team members

About You

  • 5+ years of leadership experience managing cross-functional teams and complex technology initiatives

  • Knowledge of data quality tools, metadata management systems, and data lineage technologies
  • Understanding of regulatory compliance requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, FDA, GxP, etc.)
