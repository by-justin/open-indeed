Automation Technician

About Us

Linamar Corporation is a Canadian-founded global manufacturer, renowned for its advanced engineering and innovative product development across diverse industries and markets. Our journey started in 1966 under the visionary leadership of our founder, and today, we remain committed to cultivating a culture of innovation and collaboration.

With access to state-of-the-art tools and resources, you'll have the opportunity to make a meaningful impact alongside a team of driven and passionate professionals. Join us and be part of a company where innovation, collaboration, and growth are at the heart of everything we do.

Qualifications:

  • Completion of a minimum 3-year diploma or degree program in a Technologist or Engineering program from Electrical/Mechatronics/Electronics or equivalent.
  • Minimum of 3 years experience in PLC programming, troubleshooting and modifications across platforms that could include: Allen Bradley, SLC500, MicroLogix, CompactLogix and ControlLogix using Rslinx, RsLogix 500, Rslogix 5000, Connected Component WorkBench and Safety PLC’s
  • Minimum of 3 years experience in troubleshooting and making HMI modifications using various software which could include: Rockwell Automation software (Rslinx, FactoryTalkView, Rsview 32, RsNetworx, PanelBuilder 32)
  • Knowledge of industrial automation networks Like Ethernet, DevicNet, ControlNet, DH+, DH-485, RS-232, Profibus & Asi
  • Experience in CNC and robotic repair/ programming would be an asset.
  • Understanding of electrical / electronic schematic diagrams, blueprints, and pneumatic/hydraulic systems.
  • Siemens S7 would be an asset.
  • SolidWorks or AutoCAD would be an asset.
  • Knowledge of robot systems (ABB, Motoman, Fanuc, Kuka).
  • Knowledge of Powerflex, Danfoss and Rexroth drives and Kinetix Servo drives, sensors, smart actuators would be an asset

Responsibilities:

  • Program, debug and commission automation system, during the system integration stage and then perform a runoff of automated equipment.
  • Test and integrate hardware and software components such as robots, analog devices, motion controllers, sensors, vision and laser system.
  • Act as a plant resource for line improvements, line automation, realignment of product flow equipment and machinery in conjunction with Engineering and Maintenance.
  • Complete design parameter of the automation to the level of resources able at the plant level.
  • Work with Engineers for all phases of design, maintenance, and inspection to meet the time requirements; provide technical support and services and plan upcoming activities and to update projects.
  • Work with program managers and project engineers at the APQP stage of a program start-up.
  • Work with engineering following PPAP approval in resolving non-value-added processes.
  • Evaluate the condition of new and damaged mechanical equipment and make recommendations for repair or replacement.
  • Plan assigned work in an efficient method to minimize machine downtime while ensuring line productively, and employee safety is maintained.
  • Troubleshoot process automation problems.
  • Perform regular housekeeping duties. Updated and controlled PLC program data base.
  • Perform periodically PLC and program backup.
  • Maintain equipment documentation updates.
  • Have knowledge of CSA, OSHA, and Electrical Safety Code.
  • Work within Quality standards, procedures, and requirements.
  • Attend formal professional development seminars and conferences as needed.
  • Must be willing to work all shifts.
