Job Description

At Trustmotion, part of NXP since June of 2025, we’re accelerating the shift to safe, software-defined vehicles. With proven expertise from millions of cars on the road and cutting-edge technology, we empower OEMs to innovate faster and smarter.

Ready to drive the future with us? Keep reading!

We are looking for a   Project Manager to join the mission of accelerating the transformation of the automotive industry toward safe software-defined.

As a technically savvy Project Manager, you will play a key role in supporting our customer-facing activities in Korea. With your strong software background and excellent organizational skills, in close collaboration with a renowned OEM and our internal expert teams, you will be well-equipped to manage strategically important projects.

Your Tasks:

  • Manage several customer projects in parallel, ensuring delivery within scope, time, and budget
  • Serve as the main point of contact for project-related topics, ensuring professional and transparent communication with customers
  • Translate customer requirements into internal actions and align closely with engineering teams on technical feasibility
  • Oversee both technical and organizational aspects of project management: milestone planning, risk and claim handling
  • Set up and maintain effective project steering and reporting structures across internal and external stakeholders
  • Lead technical discussions with customers and internal experts, ensuring alignment throughout the project lifecycle
  • Identify and resolve issues proactively, escalate when needed, and maintain clear, up-to-date documentation and reporting
  • Leverage your hands-on engineering experience to support technical understanding and project alignment where needed
  • Support engineering teams with coordination or mentoring based on previous team leadership experience

Your Profile:

  • At least 10 years of overall experience in the automotive software domain
  • Several years of experience managing software development projects in the automotive industry (Tier 1, Tier 2, or OEM)
  • Hands-on engineering experience is required
  • Previous experience as an engineering team lead is a strong advantage
  • Interest and potential to grow into a Customer Center Leadership role is a plus
  • Solid understanding of software development processes, embedded architectures, and integration workflows
  • Familiarity with automotive standards such as ASPICE and ISO 26262
  • Technical degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, including experience interacting with technical level
  • High level of customer orientation, with the ability to manage expectations and build long-term relationships
  • Structured, proactive, and able to prioritize and stay composed in a dynamic environment
  • Business fluency in both Korean and English

Our Offer:

  • Exciting work environment – be part of future high-tech innovations and cutting-edge technologies.
  • We are a team – flat hierarchies, open door policy, communication at eye level and hands-on mentality.
  • Technical Expertise – work among fellow technical experts and minds that will inspire you
  • Trust and responsibility – turn your ideas into reality from day one and have a real impact on megatrends shaping the future.
