Job Description

P&G is looking for a dynamic and experienced HR Manager to join our growing team!

Are you a passionate HR professional (or soon to be) with a knack for building strong relationships and fostering a positive work environment? Do you thrive on developing and implementing HR strategies that align with business goals? If so, we want to hear from you!

At P&G, we believe our people are our greatest asset. We're committed to creating a culture where employees feel valued, supported, and empowered to do their best work. As our HR Manager, you will play a pivotal role in shaping this culture and ensuring our HR practices are best-in-class.

What You'll Do:

As our HR Manager, you will be a key leader in creating a positive and productive environment for all employees. Your main responsibilities will include:

  • Building Our Team:  You'll oversee all aspects of hiring, from attracting great talent to ensuring new employees feel welcome and get off to a strong start.
  • Supporting Our People:  You'll be the go-to person for employee questions, concerns, and conflict resolution, making sure everyone feels heard and supported. You'll also lead efforts to keep our team engaged and happy.
  • Developing Our Talent:  You'll manage our performance review processes and help identify opportunities for employees to grow and develop their skills.
  • Managing Compensation & Benefits:  You'll oversee our pay structures and employee benefits programs, ensuring they are competitive and well-managed.
  • Ensuring Compliance & Best Practices:  You'll make sure our HR policies and procedures are fair, up-to-date, and follow all legal requirements.
  • Strategic Partnership:  You'll work closely with company leadership to align our HR efforts with our business goals and help us build a strong, effective organization.

What We Offer:

  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
  • Opportunity to make a significant impact on our company culture and growth.
  • A collaborative and supportive work environment.
  • Professional development and growth opportunities.

Job Qualifications

What You'll Bring (Required Qualifications):

  • Bachelor's degree. While a degree in Human Resources or Psychology is an asset, candidates with degrees in any other fields and relevant HR experience are strongly encouraged to apply.
  • Has been exposed to all aspects of Organization management, including recruitment, employee relations, performance management, compensation & benefits, and compliance.
  • Excellent communication (written and verbal), interpersonal, and negotiation skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to build trust and credibility with employees at all levels.
  • Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to handle sensitive and confidential information with discretion.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
