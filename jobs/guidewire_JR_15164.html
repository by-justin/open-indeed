Job Description

Summary

Guidewire is looking for a Technical Program Manager within its Security organization to lead execution of critical security programs aligned with Guidewire’s security maturity strategy. In this role you will drive complex, high impact security programs, partnering with Product, Engineering, and Enterprise teams/stakeholders. This role will report to the Director of Security Customer Success.

At Guidewire we use AI to work smarter and treat securing AI as part of our mission. The ideal candidate understands security risks, product and platform context and process efficiency. They naturally view every program, process and tool through an AI lens; asking where AI can help and where it creates new risks to manage. They communicate well with both technical and non-technical audiences.

Job Description

Responsibilities

  • Manage complex and cloud-scale technical security programs to meet Guidewire’s business objectives
  • Define and lead roadmap execution for critical security functions and programs
  • Evaluate program design, tooling and workflows through an AI lens, identifying where AI and automation can reduce manual effort and improve outcomes across the security organization
  • Champion responsible adoption of AI within the Security organization including AI assisted program management, reporting, and analysis in your own work
  • Develop strong partnerships and feedback loops with leaders and stakeholders across the organization
  • Lead with data and promote a culture of metrics driven decision-making, while holding stakeholders accountable
  • Be a thought leader to drive strategic change and help advance our security maturity, including how AI is reshaping the security landscape
  • Communicate effectively verbally and in written form, both internal and external stakeholders including execs, engineering leads, consultants, and vendors

Requirements

  • You are a pragmatic security expert and a hands-on technical program manager
  • You are genuinely curious about AI and actively use AI tools in your daily work, stay current on AI capabilities and default to asking “how can AI improve this?” when evaluating any program, process or problem
  • You understand emerging and tried and tested security technology including AI enabled security technology and how it applies to both cloud products and an enterprise environment
  • You are open minded and comfortable with ambiguity as AI rapidly changes the security landscape, and you can distinguish genuine AI value from hype
  • You evangelize proactive security within the organization and educate and influence stakeholders in building a secure environment, including secure and responsible AI adoption
  • You enjoy working on hard problems with no clear answers and can work with SMEs to design, scope, and iterate to create prototypes to demonstrate value
  • You have experience driving security programs for a cloud company and its practical applications
  • You understand the value of efficient processes and constantly look to improve them for productive scaling, leveraging automation and AI where it drives measurable benefit
  • You are data driven, analytical, and can turn information into action

Qualifications

  • B.S. degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or other relevant technical fields
  • 5+ years of experience documenting processes, security requirements, and managing complex cloud-scale technical security programs for an enterprise company
  • 3+ years of experience with managing IGA, privilege assessment management, Zero Trust, and single sign-on solutions programs
  • Experience with SOC 1, SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI, or equivalent preferred
  • Familiarity with application of the NIST Cyber Security Framework towards security program maturity
  • Experience evaluating of governing enterprise AI adoption (AI usage policies, AI tool assessments, model/data risk considerations) preferred
  • Demonstrate end-to-end ownership of security and across multiple teams, influence product security program direction and plans
  • Strong business acumen with excellent organizational, problem-solving, and negotiating skills The Canadian CAD base salary range for this full-time position is 120,000.00 - 150,000.00.
