Learning Experience Design Specialist  (Open)

Full time / Hybrid

Cracow, Poland

Why join Rolls-Royce?

At Rolls-Royce we are proud to be a business that has truly helped to shape the modern world and are committed to always being a force for progress; powering, protecting and connecting people everywhere.

By joining Rolls-Royce, you'll have the opportunity to work on world-class solutions, supported by a culture that believes individuality is our greatest strength, and all perspectives, experiences and backgrounds help us innovate and enable our high-performance culture.

Launched in March 2025, the Kraków R-R Global Capability Centre represents an exciting new chapter in the growth of Rolls-Royce Group Business Services. Designed to support global operations and employee services, the Centre is rapidly evolving into a key hub for enabling operational efficiency and strengthening global business processes. With a fast-growing team, it delivers exceptional services across finance, procurement, HR, Data Analytics and Service Management. This is a place where your expertise can drive real impact - enhancing operational excellence, shaping streamlined global processes and contributing directly to the long-term success of one of the world’s most respected engineering companies. Join us and be part of building something exceptional.

An exciting opportunity is available for a  Learning Experience Design Specialist  to join our global learning services delivery team and play a key role in shaping how colleagues build skills and capabilities for the future.  You’ll design and develop engaging digital learning experiences using AI-powered tools and modern authoring platforms, help maintain our learning ecosystem and support our growing community of “do-it-yourself” content creators and editors across the business.

What you will be doing

  • Design and develop engaging digital learning experiences (e-learning, microlearning, video-based learning, toolkits and more) that help colleagues perform and grow.
  • Own small to medium learning solutions end-to-end – from early ideas and storyboards through to build, testing, launch and continuous improvement.
  • Use AI-powered content tools (especially Sana Learn) and modern authoring platforms (e.g. Articulate Storyline, Rise 360, Elucidat, iSpring Suite) to create high-quality learning at speed.
  • Apply instructional design and adult learning principles so solutions are practical, just-in-time and embedded in real work.
  • Ensure all outputs – including those created by other content creators – meet agreed design, brand, accessibility and technical standards through quality checks and reviews.
  • Shape the learner experience on our corporate intranet site (Leatro Engine Room) and in Workday Learning, keeping structures, pages and catalogues clear, on-brand and easy to navigate.
  • Support and enable our Sana creator and Leatro Engine Room editor communities by onboarding and coaching new creators/editors, answering questions and sharing good practice.
  • Use feedback and data to refine learning experiences and contribute ideas to improve our tools, templates and ways of working.

What you will need:

  • A strong eye for design and user experience – able to make learning look and feel modern, clear and engaging (e.g. through layout, typography, imagery, interaction).
  • Some relevant experience creating digital experiences or content (through a first role, internship, placement, freelance work or substantial personal projects) in areas such as:  Digital learning / e-learning Web design / web content management Graphic design, multimedia, video or motion design UX / UI or interactive media
  • Genuine enthusiasm for AI and its potential in creative and learning work, with examples of how you currently use (or are experimenting with) AI tools.
  • Basic understanding of how adults learn and what makes learning effective (a formal L&D background is not essential).
  • Comfort working end-to-end on small to medium digital products, from early concept sketches through to build, testing and release.
  • Strong digital literacy and the ability to learn new tools quickly.
  • Good written and visual communication skills, with strong attention to detail and quality.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities, delivering to agreed timelines.
  • Fluent in English ( spoken and written) able to collaborate and create learning content in a global environment.
  • Fluent in German (min C1) to support German-speaking stakeholders and learners.
  • Degree, vocational qualification or equivalent experience in a relevant area (e.g. design, media, communication, digital, education, psychology, technology, or similar).

Preferred

You don’t need all of these to apply, but it’s great if you have some:

  • Exposure to modern learning authoring tools, for example:  Sana Learn Articulate Storyline / Rise 360 Elucidat iSpring Suite
  • Experience with SharePoint or intranet site/page design (beyond basic content editing), including structure, navigation and page layout.
  • Experience using or administering Workday Learning or another enterprise LMS/LXP.
  • Some familiarity with HTML/CSS or web design principles.
  • Experience producing or editing video, animations or motion graphics.
  • Understanding of digital learning standards (e.g. SCORM, xAPI) and accessibility best practices.
  • A portfolio or examples of digital work (learning, web, media or UX) that show your design skills, creativity and thinking.

Behaviours and Mindset

  • Curious and experimental – eager to try new tools and approaches, especially AI, and to share learning with others.
  • Learner-centric – always thinking about what will make sense and add value for colleagues in real work situations.
  • Quality-focused – cares about craft, detail and maintaining the reputation of the Rolls-Royce and Leatro learning brand.
  • Collaborative and supportive – enjoys working with others, coaching and enabling communities of creators and editors.
  • Outcome-oriented – thinks beyond “deliverables” to the difference learning makes for people and the organisation.
  • Organised and reliable – able to juggle multiple projects and keep promises to stakeholders.
