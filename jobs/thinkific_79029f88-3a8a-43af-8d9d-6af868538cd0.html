About the Role🚀

Are you energized by complex, cross-functional challenges — the kind where you’re the person who brings clarity to ambiguity, momentum to high-stakes initiatives, and trust to every room you walk into? We’re looking for a Senior Technical Project Manager to join us at Thinkific.

This role sits within our Program Management team inside Research & Development — a small but mighty team that operates at the heart of how Thinkific delivers. This team is a strategic force multiplier, owning complex end-to-end initiatives operating at the intersection of people, product, and AI-augmented delivery.

A note from the Hiring Manager

Hi! I am Afro, Manager of Program Management. I’m passionate about tackling complex cross-functional problems and excited for this person to join our team. This role is not just about organizing meetings and doing follow-ups. You will be highly engaged in owning projects end-to-end, solving interesting problems, and making meaningful decisions that contribute to Thinkific’s goals and our customers’ success. If this and a fast, AI-driven, and highly collaborative environment excites you, I can’t wait to meet you.

Your goal will be to drive end-to-end delivery of complex, cross-functional initiatives and raise the bar on how we ship. Here’s how you’ll accomplish this:

  • Ownend-to-end delivery of high-stakes initiatives, leading programs that cut across engineering, product, data, and go-to-market teams from scoping through launch. You’ll define what “done” looks like and hold the organization accountable to it
  • Build and maintainstrong relationships with stakeholders at every level — engineers, product managers, executives, and external partners. You’ll surface trade-offs clearly, manage competing priorities, and keep everyone rowing in the same direction
  • LeverageAI as a force multiplier. You already integrate AI tools into how you work — drafting status updates, synthesizing risk signals, generating meeting summaries and documentation, and accelerating decision-making
  • Translatebusiness objectives into executable project plans by identifying dependencies, sequencing work intelligently, and proactively flagging when scope, timeline, or resources need to shift
  • Anticipateproblems before they become blockers. You’ll build contingency plans, facilitate hard conversations early, and keep stakeholders informed
  • Define and reinforce standards for how we ship — quality gates, rollout plans, retrospectives, and post-mortems that actually lead to change
  • Own executive-level AI-assisted reporting with clear, opinionated status updates, risk dashboards, and decision-ready briefings

The person we have in mind likely:

  • Has 5+ years of experience in technical project management or program management, with a track record of delivering complex, cross-functional initiatives in a software or SaaS environment
  • Is genuinely technical. They don’t need to write code, but understand how software is built and what makes an architecture decision hard
  • Is AI-fluent. They actively use AI tools (Claude, Copilot, Cursor, Notion AI, or similar) in their day-to-day work. They’re comfortable helping teams define guardrails for responsible AI use in workflows
  • Is a systems thinker who holds the whole picture — dependencies, risks, resource constraints, business context — while staying sharp on the details that matter most
  • Communicates with clarity and confidence. They write concisely, adapt their message to their audience, and can explain technical blockers in plain language
  • Is experienced with modern delivery frameworks (Agile, Scrum, Kanban)
  • Is data-informed and uses metrics to track progress and make decisions
  • Loves to learn and grow. They’ve found (and keep looking for) ways to level up their skills in this field, whether that’s through formal education, gaining professional experience, or maybe even building their own business

These things would also be nice, but we think you could learn them on the job:

  • Experience in a B2B SaaS or learning product company
  • Familiarity with evaluating and rolling out AI tooling within engineering or product teams
  • PMP, PgMP, or equivalent certification
  • Experience managing vendor relationships or third-party integrations at scale

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
