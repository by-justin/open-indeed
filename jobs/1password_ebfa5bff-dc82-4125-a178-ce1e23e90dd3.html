Job Description

1Password is growing. We’ve surpassed $400M in ARR and we’re continuing to accelerate, earning a spot on the Forbes Cloud 100 for four years in a row and teaming up with iconic partners like Oracle Red Bull Racing.

About 1Password

At 1Password, we’re building the foundation for a safe, productive digital future. Our mission is to unleash employee productivity without compromising security by ensuring every identity is authentic, every application sign-in is secure, and every device is trusted. We innovated the market-leading enterprise password manager and pioneered Unified Access Management, a new cybersecurity category built for the way people and AI agents work today. As one of the most loved brands in cybersecurity, we take a human-centric approach in everything from product strategy to user experience. Over 180,000 businesses, from Fortune 100 leaders to the world’s most innovative AI companies, trust 1Password to help their teams securely adopt the SaaS and AI tools they need to do their best work.

If you're excited about the opportunity to contribute to the digital safety of millions, to work alongside a team of curious, driven individuals, and to solve hard problems in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, then we want to hear from you. Come join us and help shape a safer, simpler digital future.

Technical authority and commercial judgement create the bridge between architecture and business outcomes. As a 1Password Field Chief Technology Officer (Field CTO), you’ll help the world’s largest organizations navigate that bridge while shaping the future of enterprise identity security.

The Field CTO is the most senior customer-facing technical advisor within the 1Password Solutions Engineering organization. You’ll influence enterprise technology leaders, accelerate adoption of the 1Password platform and help define how modern organizations secure every sign-in to every application from every device, including AI agents.

You’ll partner with executive buyers, enterprise architects, developers and security practitioners to solve some of the industry’s most complex identity and access challenges. You’ll engage in our most strategic customer opportunities, represent 1Password in executive briefings, customer advisory boards, industry events and executive forums, and serve as a trusted advisor to Product, Engineering and GTM leadership. You’ll focus your efforts where executive technical leadership can materially influence customer outcomes, while transforming those experiences into repeatable technical motions that scale across the broader Solutions Engineering organization.

Beyond customer engagement, you’ll help scale the technical capabilities of the entire Solutions Engineering organization. You’ll transform customer insights into repeatable playbooks, mentor senior Solutions Engineers, influence product direction and establish technical strategies that improve how we engage enterprise customers around the world.

How we’re using AI today

Across GTM, we’re transforming the way we work with AI. We expect every seller, CSM, and team member to become a power user, using it daily to work smarter and move faster. AI is already embedded in how we operate – from real-time coaching that sharpens how we sell and interact with customers, to surfacing insights that help us anticipate needs – creating more time for meaningful customer engagement. If you’re excited to reshape how you think, make decisions, and drive growth with AI, you’ll feel right at home here.

This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US.

What we're looking for:

  • 12+ years of experience in enterprise technology, cybersecurity, consulting, solutions engineering, enterprise architecture or technical leadership roles.
  • Demonstrated success influencing executive technology leaders within Fortune 500 organizations.
  • Deep technical expertise across identity, access management, privileged access, cloud security, developer platforms, SaaS security or AI security.
  • Exceptional executive communication skills with the ability to translate complex technical concepts into business outcomes.
  • Demonstrated ability to simplify highly complex technical concepts into compelling narratives for executive audiences.
  • Proven ability to influence enterprise architecture decisions across large, complex organizations.
  • Experience supporting strategic enterprise sales opportunities and partnering closely with executive sales teams.
  • Strong understanding of enterprise buying processes and modern sales methodologies such as MEDDPICC.
  • Experience building technical strategies, reusable methodologies or customer engagement frameworks that scale beyond individual contributions.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence product strategy through customer engagement and market insights.
  • Proven ability to build trusted relationships across Product, Engineering, Sales and executive leadership while influencing without direct authority.
  • Experience speaking to diverse audiences at industry conferences, executive forums and analyst events.
  • Willingness to travel up to 50%.

  • Influence the successful outcome of 1Password’s most strategic customer opportunities and executive relationships.
  • Build trusted partnerships with Product, Engineering and GTM leadership that shape future platform investments.
  • Create repeatable technical plays, executive messaging and reference architectures adopted across the global Solutions Engineering organization.
  • Elevate the technical capabilities of the field through mentorship, thought leadership and executive engagement.
  • Expand 1Password’s influence across the identity security community through conference speaking, customer advisory boards and industry events.
