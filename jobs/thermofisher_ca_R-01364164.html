Job Description

Job Title: System Analyst III Job Location: India Bangalore

Who are we: Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. is the world leader in serving science, with revenues of more than $40 billion and approximately 130,000 employees globally. Our M​ission is to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. We help our customers accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver medicines to market and increase laboratory productivity. Through our premier brands – Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific and Unity Lab Services – we offer an unmatched combination of innovative technologies, purchasing convenience and comprehensive services. ​

About Team:  We are a strong and competent extension of global IT in India which is committed to serve the organization by providing the best services, implement innovative solutions and provide stability to our enterprise applications which will enable our customers to accelerate growth and increase productivity.

How will you make an impact? As Oracle Cloud PLM System Analyst , you will provide technical expertise to build effective and scalable PLM solutions, aligning with Thermo Fisher Scientifics standards & strategic objectives.

What will you do? As a Oracle Cloud PLM System Analyst, you would be responsible to support the validated and non-validated production systems by resolving access, incident and change requests in timely way and enhance user experience.

How Will You Get Here?

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related discipline.
  • Oracle Cloud PLM Developer, Administrator, or Fundamentals certification is preferred.
  • 8+ years of hands-on experience in the development, implementation, and support of Oracle Cloud PLM and /or Agile PLM solutions.
  • Strong expertise in Oracle Cloud PLM modules, including Product Development, Product Data Hub (PDH), and Innovation Management.
  • Experience participating in at least one end-to-end Oracle Cloud PLM implementation project.
  • Hands-on experience with Oracle Application Composer, Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence (OTBI), and Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC).
  • Working knowledge of validated systems and regulatory compliance requirements, including 21 CFR Part 11.
  • Proven experience providing L2/L3 production support and troubleshooting for Agile PLM and Oracle Cloud PLM applications.
  • Extensive experience designing, developing, and implementing PLM and Master Data Management (MDM) solutions.
  • Functional understanding of PLM business processes, including Part Management, Document Management, CAD Management, and Change Management.
  • Knowledge of life sciences regulations, including Design History File (DHF), Device Master Record (DMR), and global regulatory compliance, is an advantage.
  • Experience integrating Oracle Cloud PLM with enterprise applications such as JD Edwards (JDE), SAP ERP, Business Intelligence (BI) platforms, Hybris, and other downstream systems.

Knowledge, Skills & Abilities

  • Strong collaboration and interpersonal skills with the ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams and independently.
  • Hands-on experience delivering PLM solutions within the life sciences industry.
  • Experience gathering business requirements, performing solution analysis, and participating in solution design, development, testing, and deployment.
  • Familiarity with software build and deployment processes, version control systems, and release management practices.
  • Experience supporting, monitoring, and troubleshooting production and non-production environments to ensure system stability and performance.
  • Ability to promote support best practices, drive continuous improvement, and enable support teams to resolve issues efficiently.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a proactive approach to issue resolution.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to effectively engage both technical and business stakeholders.
