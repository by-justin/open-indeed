Description

Precision Analog-to-Digital Converter (ASC-DCC-PADC) team is seeking an experienced Systems Engineer. In this role, you will work in a vibrant, team-oriented environment with exposure to a broad range of state-of-the-art signal chain applications.

TI offers the industry's largest portfolio of ADCs — spanning 8 to 32 bits in resolution — with best-in-class performance, compact footprint, and high signal-chain integration. Our ADC solutions power a wide range of fast-evolving applications includingdatacenters, test & measurement, factory and building automation, test & measurement, automotive, medical instrumentation, and more.

We are seeking a candidate with deep analog signal chain expertise and a strong ability to translate customer system-level needs into next-generation ADC product definitions. This engineer will be expected to develop detailed product specifications, define system-level architectures, and provide cross-functional guidance to design, test, applications, and marketing teams to ensure flawless execution.

Responsibilities May Include

  • Deeply understand customer systems and end-equipment to identify technical challenges across the full analog signal chain (sensors, amplifiers, references, ADCs, and digital interfaces)
  • Analyze key end-equipment in datacenter, automotive and industrial markets to identify future trends and opportunities for performance improvement and differentiation
  • Develop compelling short- and long-term product roadmaps grounded in system-level insights, market trends, and customer engagement
  • Author detailed product specifications (target specs, system block diagrams, competitive benchmarking) for next-generation precision ADC solutions
  • Assess and negotiate IC development tradeoffs with design teams and customers to balance performance, power, area, and cost
  • Drive execution from initial concept through RTM , ensuring critical performance metrics and customer timelines are met
  • Serve as the technical expert for product ramp-up, supporting customers through design-in and volume production phases
  • Collaborate with marketing and applications teams to develop high-quality technical collateral (application notes, reference designs, white papers) that accelerate product adoption
  • Relentlessly pursue new design wins for both new and existing products through deep technical engagement with customers and field teams
  • Create and review technical documentation including datasheets, application notes, and system design guides
  • Build strong relationships with customers and field teams by identifying and solving real-world signal chain problems with TI silicon

Provide strategic input on business direction and revenue planning to sustain and grow market share

Qualifications

Minimum Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering
  • 5+ years of experience in systems engineering, applications engineering, or analog/mixed-signal IC design
  • Hands-on experience with analog signal chains , including ADCs, amplifiers, voltage references, or related components
  • Experience developing differentiated product roadmaps tied to market and customer insights
  • Strong understanding of analog and mixed-signal IC specifications and their system-level implications
  • Experience promoting/marketing ICs and driving design wins through system-level solutions
  • Proven involvement in the product development lifecycle , from concept through volume production
  • Comfortable engaging in deep, in-depth technical discussions with systems engineers and customers

Preferred Qualifications

  • Demonstrated expertise in precision ADC architectures (SAR, Delta-Sigma, Pipeline) and their application in real-world signal chains
  • Experience with signal chain system design — noise budgeting, dynamic range analysis, anti-aliasing filters, grounding/layout best practices
  • Familiarity with key application domains such as control systems, power measurement, data acquisition, or ADAS
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to model and simulate signal chain performance
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills — able to present complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Proven ability to collaborate effectively across design, applications, test, marketing, and sales functions
  • Strong time management and project execution skills in a fast-paced, rapidly evolving environment
  • Self-starter with the ability to take initiative and drive results with minimal supervision
