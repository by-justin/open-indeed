Job Description

Job Description Summary

The Automation & Process Controls Technician is responsible for supporting the installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement of automation and process control systems used in a manufacturing environment, with an initial focus on ECM equipment and associated manufacturing processes.

This role supports safe and reliable production by maintaining PLCs, HMIs, instrumentation, electrical controls, process equipment, and industrial communication systems. The technician works closely with Automation Engineering, Engineering, Maintenance, EHS, Operations, and equipment vendors to improve equipment reliability, process control, safety, and maintainability.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

Automation & Process Controls Support

  • Install, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot PLC, HMI, instrumentation, and industrial control systems.
  • Perform routine inspections on automation and process control equipment.
  • Support ECM equipment and associated process systems, including pumps, valves, motors, sensors, instrumentation, chemical handling systems, ventilation, and process controls.
  • Troubleshoot electrical, instrumentation, process control, and automation system issues.

Programming & Controls

  • Assist with PLC and HMI programming, modifications, testing, commissioning, and implementation under engineering direction.
  • Perform PLC troubleshooting, I/O diagnostics, and control system verification.
  • Work with HMI interfaces to monitor equipment status, alarms, process conditions, and system operation.
  • Assist with machine and process safety functions, including alarms, permissives, interlocks, and shutdown sequences.

Instrumentation & Industrial Networks

  • Install, calibrate, test, troubleshoot, and validate process instrumentation, including:Temperature, Pressure, Flow, Level, Gas detection, Other process sensors and instrumentation
  • Support industrial communication networks and connectivity between PLCs, HMIs, drives, remote I/O, instrumentation, and other automation equipment.
  • Support industrial Ethernet and communication protocols used within manufacturing control systems.

System Integration & Improvements

  • Support Automation Engineers with new equipment installations, system upgrades, and retrofits.
  • Participate in commissioning, startup, FAT, and SAT activities for new and modified equipment.
  • Support equipment vendors during troubleshooting, commissioning, and system modifications.
  • Identify recurring equipment and process issues and work with Engineering and Maintenance to develop sustainable corrective actions.
  • Recommend improvements to increase equipment reliability, process stability, maintainability, efficiency, and safety.

Production & Reliability Support

  • Respond to automation and process-control-related downtime events quickly and effectively.
  • Work with Production, Engineering, and Maintenance teams to diagnose and resolve equipment and process issues.
  • Support machine and process data collection for troubleshooting, reliability analysis, and continuous improvement.
  • Maintain and improve electrical drawings, I/O lists, network information, control narratives, equipment documentation, and troubleshooting procedures.
  • Support preventive maintenance and lifecycle management of automation, instrumentation, and control equipment.
  • Engage in reliability engineering efforts, including FMEA, spare-parts strategy, TPM, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement activities.

Safety & Compliance

  • Follow all industry, regional, and GE Vernova safety standards.
  • Follow applicable electrical safety practices and lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures.
  • Assist with machine safety verification and testing of alarms, permissives, interlocks, and shutdown functions.
  • Ensure automation and process control systems are maintained and operated in accordance with company and applicable regulatory standards.
