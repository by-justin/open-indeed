Job Description

Summer Sales Intern - Grow Your Future with BASF

As a Summer Sales Intern, you'll be part of a team that's shaping the future of agriculture while building your own career foundation.

Internship Details:

  • Duration: 4 months (Summer 2027)
  • Location: Medicine Hat and Calgary, AB
  • Compensation: $25 - $32 / Hour

Welcome to BASF

We are BASF in Agriculture and everything we do, we do for the love of farming, and the biggest job on Earth. We are an internationally successful company of the BASF Group. Our solutions are purpose-designed for different crop systems. Connecting seeds and traits, crop protection products, digital tools and sustainability approaches, to help deliver the best possible outcomes for farmers, growers and our other stakeholders along the value chain. With teams in the lab, field, office and in production, we do everything in our power to build a sustainable future for agriculture.

#ForTheLoveOfFarming #TheBiggestJobOnEarth #AgEmployerOfChoice

Here you can gain more insight into our Vision at BASF Agricultural Solutions!

Why Join Us?

This isn't your average internship. At BASF, you'll:

  • Make an Impact : Contribute to meaningful projects that support farmers, strengthen relationships in our communities and drive sustainability.
  • Build Your Network : Develop lasting relationships with mentors, managers, and peers who will guide and support your career journey.
  • Learn by Doing : Gain practical, hands-on experience in sales, agronomy, and customer engagement that translates into real-world skills.
  • Grow Your Career : Explore career paths and gain visibility into potential full-time opportunities, including our Professional Development Program (PDP).
  • Have Fun : Participate in field tours, networking events, and team activities that make your summer both rewarding as it is memorable.

Students will be hired and employed through a third-party contracting agency - Raise Recruiting. Opportunities are based on business needs at our sites.

Candidates resumes will be presented to BASF based on requirements of the assignments being a match and fit based on experience. Applications can be considered for multiple opportunities now through March 31, 2027. If you are selected, you will be contacted about the summer employment opportunity directly.

What You'll Learn:

  • How to manage a customer portfolio and drive innovation in the field.
  • The ins and outs of BASF's product line and how it supports sustainable agriculture.
  • Communication, leadership, and decision-making skills that will serve you in any career path.

What You'll Do:

  • Build relationships with growers and promote BASF solutions.
  • Support field tours, events, and product demonstrations.
  • Lead a field-scale project and scout fields to gain technical insights.
  • Collaborate with BASF teams to grow the business and your skills.
  • Travel within your assigned territory.

What We're Looking For:

  • You're currently enrolled in an agriculture or related degree program.
  • You're passionate about ag, sustainability, and making a difference.
  • You're a strong communicator, organized, and ready to learn.
  • You have a valid driver's license and are open to travel.

To be eligible for this internship, you must be currently enrolled in a post-secondary program and returning to studies following the internship. Applications from individuals who have already graduated will not be considered.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
