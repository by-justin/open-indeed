Job Description

Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for modern marketing teams, helping organizations build, manage, and optimize high-performing web experiences that drive predictable growth and strengthen brand trust. Building at Webflow will need grit, because we move fast, without ever sacrificing craft or quality.

We’re looking for a VP of Revenue Marketing to lead Webflow’s Revenue Marketing org and be the marketing DRI for the revenue engine: pipeline creation, conversion, expansion marketing, and lifecycle. You’ll be accountable for marketing-sourced and influenced pipeline and iARR across sales-led and partner-led motions, with visibility into self-serve iARR performance in lockstep with Sales, Partnerships, RevOps, and Product. This role owns the full line from data diagnosis to strategy to campaign, not just execution against a plan someone else set.

As a VP, Revenue Marketing you’ll …

  • Revenue & funnel ownership

Own marketing-sourced and influenced pipeline and iARR targets (new business + expansion), including forecasting, budget allocation, and channel ROI Balance Demand Creation (category and awareness plays that create future pipeline) with Demand Capture (high-intent programs that efficiently convert in-market demand) with a clear view of CAC, payback, and Cost to Book by channel. Build and optimize the full funnel: lead → MQL/PQL → SAO → closed/won, plus upsell/cross-sell motions Own webflow.com performance as our highest leverage pipeline channel: conversion, channel optimization, and demand capture, in partnership with Product and Brand on experience and design.

  • Team leadership & org design

Lead a multi-disciplinary Revenue Marketing org (demand gen, digital channel optimization, field marketing, partner marketing, lifecycle) Build and scale a high-performing team aligned to our sales led and partner led motions. Partner with peer Marketing Leaders to ensure integrated, end-to-end campaigns rather than siloed programs

  • Sales-led growth

Work with Sales leadership to prioritize segments and plays, and ensure marketing programs, SDR/BDR sequencing, and AE outreach are orchestrated around those plays Build ABM/account-based plays for mid-market + enterprise in close partnership with SDR/BDR and AE teams Build the Revenue ‘feedback loops’ from sales calls into messaging, offers, campaign strategy, and competitive positioning.

  • Partner-led growth

Partner with VP of Partnership to define partner-sourced pipeline targets by tier and region, and build the co-marketing and enablement programs to hit them Build a partner tiering and activation model: recruit → enable → co-market → measure Develop scalable plays for different partner types, including tech/integration partners, Agencies, and platforms.

  • Lifecycle, expansion, and retention

Own lifecycle strategy across onboarding, activation to paid, re-engagement, expansion, and advocacy Partner closely with CS and Product to design and test lifecycle journeys that improve time-to-value, expansion iARR, and multi-product attach Operationalize customer proof as part of core sales plays, measured by win rate, attach, and cycle time

  • Customer Zero

“Act as ‘Customer Zero’ for Product: use Webflow internally at a high bar, surface friction and unmet needs, and translate real internal workflows into prioritized, testable product and GTM requirements.” Lead in the AI transformation of our Revenue Marketing team.

About you:

Requirements:

  • 12-15+ years in B2B SaaS revenue marketing, including senior leadership ownership of pipeline and ARR

  • Demonstrated ability to synthesize a data diagnosis into a stated strategic thread, and defend that thread.

  • Track record of building trust and results with a new team fast, specifically in the first 30 to 60 days. Clear, sized instructions to directs rather than high volume, ambiguous asks.

  • Proven ability to operate in a sales-led and partner-led model with visibility into self serve iARR performance; knows how these motions feed each other instead of competing

  • Experience building integrated revenue plays (not just channels); can design and run cross-functional plays from concept through enablement to measurement

  • Deep analytics and operating rigor: attribution approach, forecasting, budget optimization, experimentation discipline

You’ll thrive as a VP, Revenue Marketing if you:

  • Have credibility with Sales, Partnership, and Product: Known for creating alignment, clarity, and trust

  • AI-native working style: AI-native working style that produces judgement, not just volume. Uses AI to accelerate research and execution, and can articulate where AI output stops and their own synthesis starts.

  • Stay curious and open to growth — demonstrating a proactive embrace of AI, and actively building and applying fluency in emerging technologies to elevate how we work, drive faster outcomes, and expand collective impact.
