Job Description:

Airbus Americas, Inc is looking for a Aircraft Certifying Manager (2nd Shift) to join our team based in Mobile, AL.

The A220 Aircraft Certifying Manager (2nd Shift) is responsible to collect, review, and sign aircraft documentation to ensure the "As-Built" status is in line with and finally equal to the "Target Configuration". This position is also responsible for the issuance of the Aircraft Statement of Conformity and/or Transport Canada Export Airworthiness Certificate.

Meet the Team:

Our Engineers at the Airbus U.S. Manufacturing facility take our aircraft from concept to customer delivery – working with production teams on every aspect of assembly from managing technical information, quality control, maintenance tasks and health & safety – along flowlines and flightlines. Join our team and watch our aircraft come to life.

Your Working Environment:

On ‘Airbus Way’ you will find the Airbus U.S. Manufacturing Facility for commercial aircraft. Opened at the Mobile Aeroplex at Brookley in 2015, the facility produces A320 Family aircraft and added a second product line for the A220 in 2019. The facility, which strengthens the U.S. aerospace industry, is part of Airbus’ strategy to enhance its global competitiveness by meeting the growing needs of its customers in the United States and beyond.

Responsibilities:

Conformity Documentation:

  • Ensure that aircraft documentation is up to date and the "As-Built" status is in line with the “As Designed” target definition. This documentation includes (but is not limited to): As-Built Component Listing (ABCL), Functional Test Plans (FTP's), Certificates of Conformance (C of C's), Engineering Configuration Statement (ECS), Technical Logbook (TLB), Quality Logbook (QLB).
  • Establish, analyze and validate ABCL under responsibility of Final Assembly Line (FAL), Plant, or Supplier as assigned.
  • Issue the Aircraft Statement of Conformity and/or Transport Canada Export Airworthiness Certificate.
  • Transmit files pertaining to Aircraft Certification activities to Transport Canada.
  • Assist FAA Designated Airworthiness Representative (DAR) for the issuance of FAA Certificate of Airworthiness.
  • Report to Transport Canada of Export activities and attend all required TCCA training.
  • Maintain Transport Canada embossing seal and stamps per TCCA regulation.
  • Transmit files pertaining to Aircraft activities to Transport Canada.

Problem Solving:

  • Investigate, solve, or justify conformity discrepancies.
  • Manage the resolution of all discrepancies at aircraft level in strong coordination with internal / external contacts (Design Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Production, Quality, I.T. etc.).
  • Check the conformity status of all Constituent Assemblies (CA) integrated in the aircraft.

Administrative Duties:

  • Attend status meetings and be able and willing to represent the Conformity Department.
  • Collect, review and file documentation relevant to support the build and sell of aircraft
  • Other duties as assigned.
