Job Description

Procore is growing, and our SMB team in Dublin is the driving force behind that success. While we aren't hiring for an immediate start this second, we know the best talent doesn't always wait for a job board. We’re looking to connect with "Construction-Curious" sales pros now so that when our next hiring sprint begins, you’re already at the front of the line.

This isn't a "sit back and wait for inbound" role. We’re looking for SMB Account Executives who have a true "Hunter" mindset balanced with a consultative approach. You’ll be the face of Procore to SMB construction firms across the region, helping them trade in their messy spreadsheets for the world’s leading construction platform.

What you'll actually do:

  • Lead your own prospecting strategy: You’ll have the autonomy to identify and open your own accounts. We have a brilliant SDR team to support you, but we value AEs who take ownership of their own pipeline.
  • Manage the Full Cycle: You’ll own the journey from that first outreach to the final "closed-won" celebration.
  • Consult, don’t just sell: You’ll become a trusted advisor in the construction world, helping our customers build safer and more efficiently.
  • Master the Stack: You’ll use tools like Salesforce and Gong to sharpen your approach. We’re big on continuous learning and peer-to-peer coaching here.

What we're looking for:

  • The SaaS "It" Factor: You have experience in B2B tech sales and the resilience to thrive in a fast-paced environment.
  • Growth & Pipeline Ownership: You take pride in your numbers and are always looking for ways to exceed your goals.
  • Curiosity & Impact: You’re the type of person who wants to understand how things are built and the positive impact technology can have on a traditional industry.
  • Dublin Based: You’re ready to join us in our Dublin office to soak up the culture, collaborate with the team, and enjoy our famous free lunches.

The Process: If your profile looks like a fit, our recruiting team will reach out for a "Coffee Chat" screen. No pressure, no formal case studies yet - just a chance for us to get to know you and for you to see if Procore is your next home.

Please Note: This is a "Pipeline" posting intended to identify potential candidates for future openings. By applying, you are registering your interest for upcoming roles and not a currently active vacancy. We will review all applications and contact relevant candidates as soon as a new seat launches.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
