Robotics Manufacturing Engineer, Line Design, Geartrain

Description

In this&nbsp;role&nbsp;you’ll&nbsp;support&nbsp;the design,&nbsp;construction, launch,&nbsp;and&nbsp;transfer of&nbsp;geartrain&nbsp;manufacturing&nbsp;lines and equipment used&nbsp;in&nbsp;Optimus Actuators.&nbsp;This role&nbsp;will&nbsp;prioritize&nbsp;successful&nbsp;design and launch&nbsp;of&nbsp;production&nbsp;line while&nbsp;maintaining&nbsp;maximum&nbsp;efficiency,&nbsp;production&nbsp;volume,&nbsp;and safety while&nbsp;validating&nbsp;performance and&nbsp;fully&nbsp;documenting&nbsp;all aspects&nbsp;of the project for handover to sustaining&nbsp;operations.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;<br>The ideal&nbsp;candidate will&nbsp;bring deep&nbsp;technical&nbsp;expertise&nbsp;in high-volume&nbsp;geartrain&nbsp;manufacturing lines and&nbsp;the successful&nbsp;introduction&nbsp;of&nbsp;new technologies&nbsp;to mass production&nbsp;line.&nbsp;They&nbsp;must&nbsp;demonstrate&nbsp;experience&nbsp;translating&nbsp;manufacturing requirements into efficient,&nbsp;detailed&nbsp;production line&nbsp;designs&nbsp;that are robust and&nbsp;practical to&nbsp;implement&nbsp;.&nbsp;In this position, you&nbsp;will&nbsp;take&nbsp;ownership&nbsp;of&nbsp;production line&nbsp;creation&nbsp;of&nbsp;specialized&nbsp;equipment&nbsp;from&nbsp;concept through&nbsp;line&nbsp;launch&nbsp;completion—defining performance requirements,&nbsp;running&nbsp;process flow&nbsp;simulations&nbsp;as needed to&nbsp;validate&nbsp;solutions grounded in&nbsp;first principles, and leading&nbsp;commissioning&nbsp;through&nbsp;final validation. Travel expected 25%.
