Role Description

DescriptionInvent the future with us.

Ampere is a semiconductor design company for a new era, leading the future of computing with an innovative approach to CPU design focused on high-performance, energy efficient AI compute.

As a pioneer in the new frontier of energy efficient high-performance computing, Ampere is part of the Softbank Group of companies driving sustainable computing for AI, Cloud, and edge applications.

Join us at Ampere and work alongside a passionate and growing team - we’d love to have you apply!

Ampere is seeking to fill a part-time (~20 hrs/week) temporary staff augmentation Technical Program Manager role supporting our IT Department for an anticipated period of 6 months.Scope of Work:

Ampere is seeking an experienced Technical Program Manager to lead complex, cross-functional programs across engineering, IT, EDA, infrastructure, security, and cloud operations. This role requires both strategic program leadership and sufficient technical depth to understand chip-design workflows, evaluate system and infrastructure risks, challenge engineering assumptions, and drive execution across globally distributed teams.

The Technical Program Manager will own programs throughout their entire lifecycle, from requirements definition and roadmap development through implementation, operational readiness, and continuous improvement. The successful candidate will maintain a clear view of business and engineering objectives while engaging deeply in the technical challenges associated with semiconductor design environments and cloud-scale infrastructure.

Deliverables:

As a core member of our IT team, you will:

  • Lead complex, multi-disciplinary programs supporting semiconductor design, EDA infrastructure, engineering productivity, IT, and cloud platforms.
  • Define program objectives, scope, milestones, dependencies, resource requirements, and success metrics in partnership with technical and business stakeholders.
  • Develop and maintain integrated project plans, technical roadmaps, schedules, risk registers, dependency maps, and communication plans.
  • Partner with engineering, IT, infrastructure, security, operations, finance, and executive stakeholders to align priorities and drive timely decisions.
  • Manage programs across the full lifecycle, including requirements definition, planning, execution, validation, deployment, operational readiness, and post-implementation review.
  • Coordinate globally distributed teams across multiple time zones, disciplines, and organizational boundaries.
  • Identify technical, operational, organizational, and security risks; establish mitigation plans; and escalate issues with clear recommendations.
  • Drive resolution of cross-functional dependencies, resource constraints, design conflicts, and execution blockers.
  • Establish program governance, operating mechanisms, review cadences, decision records, and reporting standards.
  • Publish accurate weekly status reports covering progress, milestones, risks, issues, dependencies, decisions, and required actions.
  • Translate complex technical issues into concise, actionable communication for engineering leaders, executives, and nontechnical stakeholders.
  • Evaluate EDA and cloud infrastructure decisions with sufficient technical depth to identify scalability, reliability, performance, security, and operational risks.
  • Work with engineering leaders to define requirements for highly available, secure, and scalable design environments.
  • Ensure program deliverables align with information security, compliance, infrastructure, and operational requirements.
  • Challenge existing processes and technical assumptions constructively when necessary to support broader business and engineering objectives.
  • Establish mechanisms to measure program health, delivery quality, stakeholder alignment, operational performance, and realized business value.
  • Lead post-program reviews to capture lessons learned and improve future planning and execution.

**Education, Skills, & Experience Desired: **

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline.
  • At least 8 years of experience in technical program management, engineering program management, or a related leadership role.
  • At least 5 years of experience leading technical teams or cross-functional programs, with or without direct reports.
  • Professional experience with semiconductor design, chip development, EDA environments, VLSI design flows, or related engineering infrastructure.
  • Working knowledge of public cloud services and cloud infrastructure concepts.
  • Demonstrated experience managing complex programs involving multiple technical disciplines and organizational stakeholders.
  • Experience developing project plans, schedules, milestones, risk registers, and executive-level status reporting.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with engineers, executives, and global stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline.
  • 15 or more years of experience managing complex, cross-functional technical programs.
  • Experience leading global programs across IT, semiconductor engineering, EDA, infrastructure, security, and cloud operations.
  • Experience managing programs that span multiple engineering disciplines, including hardware design, software, infrastructure, platform engineering, and operations.
  • Working knowledge of information security principles, controls, risk management, and compliance requirements in enterprise or cloud environments.
  • Expertise in developing long-term technical roadmaps, execution strategies, operating models, success metrics, and investment plans.
  • Extensive experience in hardware engineering, chip design, VLSI CAD environments, EDA platforms, cloud computing, or large-scale engineering infrastructure.
  • Ability to evaluate technical architectures, identify system design and operational risks, and credibly challenge engineering decisions.
  • Experience delivering highly available, scalable, secure, and performance-sensitive platforms.
  • Experience managing vendor relationships, third-party dependencies, budgets, and procurement activities.
  • Experience establishing governance and operating mechanisms for long-running, high-visibility programs.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence senior technical leaders and experienced engineers without relying on direct authority.
  • Ability to challenge the status quo, resolve ambiguity, and maintain focus on broader engineering and business objectives.
  • Experience working in a fast-paced environment where priorities, dependencies, and technical requirements evolve over time.

Engagement Arrangements:

As a Contingent Worker, you will work as an employee of a Staffing Vendor firm, who provides staff augmentation and payroll services for Ampere Computing. The staffing firm offers weekly pay based on approved timecard submittal, medical benefits, and other employment benefits, based on eligibility. The hourly pay range for this role is between $93.75 and $140.39 per hour.

This role is a part-time and onsite at Ampere’s Santa Clara, CA office.
