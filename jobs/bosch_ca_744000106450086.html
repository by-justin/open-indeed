JMP_Technical Project Manager（XC）

Company Description

Do you want beneficial technologies being shaped by your ideas? Whether in the areas of mobility solutions, consumer goods, industrial technology or energy and building technology - with us, you will have the chance to improve quality of life all across the globe. Welcome to Bosch.

Job Description

Responsibilities：

  1. Technical Strategy Support:

-Track global & marketing autonomous driving trends and assess their impact on L2++ Cross-Trim product roadmaps.

-Assist in decomposing the L2++ Cross-Trim Platform’s 3–5-year autonomous driving strategy into actionable annual milestones.

  1. Cross-Departmental Collaboration Strategy Definition & Implementation:

-Establish cross-departmental collaboration mechanisms, design joint KPIs, and eliminate technical silos.

-Coordinate resources between stakeholders and teams to align technical solutions with project team needs.

  1. Full Project Lifecycle Support:

-Assist the project lead in developing L2++ Cross-Trim feature development plans, breaking down technical milestones, and tracking progress.

-Support coordinating cross-departmental technical meetings, follow up meeting outcomes, and drive issue resolution to ensure alignment and execution.

  1. Technical Decision Support:

-Assist in evaluating supplier solutions, participate in technical reviews, and conduct cost-performance trade-off analyses.

-Monitor development risks, proactively flag issues, and collaborate on mitigation strategies.

  1. Resource Coordination:

-Coordinate internal/external resources to optimize task prioritization.

-Regularly update project leads with progress reports, and support executive decision-making.

岗位职责：

1.技术战略支持：

-追踪全球及市场自动驾驶行业趋势，评估其对 L2++ 跨产品线产品路线图的影响。

-协助将 L2++ 跨产品线平台的 3-5 年自动驾驶战略，拆解为可落地的年度里程碑任务。

2.跨部门协作策略制定与执行：

-建立跨部门协作机制，设计联合关键绩效指标（KPI），打破技术壁垒。

-协调各利益相关方与团队的资源，推动技术方案与项目团队需求相匹配。

3.项目全生命周期支持：

-协助项目负责人制定 L2++ 跨产品线功能开发计划，拆解技术里程碑，并跟进项目进度。

-协助组织协调跨部门技术会议，跟踪会议决议事项，推动问题解决，保障各方协同与项目执行。

4.技术决策支持：

-协助评估供应商解决方案，参与技术评审工作，并开展性价比权衡分析。

-监控研发风险，主动预警潜在问题，协作制定风险缓解策略。

5.资源协调：

-协调内外部资源，优化工作优先级排序。

-定期向项目负责人汇报项目进展，为管理层决策提供支持。

Qualifications

Qualifications:

  1. Technical Acumen:

-Preferred education background in engineering fields.

-Deep understanding of L2++ functional challenges and solution frameworks.

-Familiarity with autonomous driving development workflows (V-Model) and ability to rapidly absorb technical documentation.

  1. Project Management Skills:

-Proficiency in Jira, DOORS, and relevant tools for task breakdown and critical path planning.

-Data-driven mindset to extract key metrics from logs and test data.

  1. Strategic and Systems Thinking:

-Ability to design transitions from incremental improvements to systemic breakthroughs.

-Skilled in Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) analysis to balance innovation and feasibility.

  1. Communication & Execution:

-Skilled at translating complex technical issues into actionable tasks and driving cross-team collaboration.

-Strong upward/downward communication skills to align with project lead priorities and team execution.

任职要求：

1.技术素养：

-优先考虑工程类专业出身。

-深刻理解 L2++ 功能开发的难点与解决方案架构。

-熟悉自动驾驶开发流程（V 模型），能够快速研读并消化技术文档。

2.项目管理能力：

-熟练使用 Jira、DOORS 等工具，开展任务拆解与关键路径规划工作。

-具备数据驱动的思维模式，能够从日志及测试数据中提炼核心指标。

3.战略与系统思维：

-能够设计从渐进式优化到体系性突破的转型路径。

-精通总拥有成本（TCO）分析，平衡技术创新与落地可行性。

4.沟通与执行能力：

-善于将复杂技术问题转化为可落地的任务，推动跨团队协作。

-具备出色的向上 / 向下沟通能力，匹配项目负责人的优先级要求，保障团队执行效率。
