Job Description

Job Purpose:

  • Lead and drives functional excellence in engineering solution and provide knowledge base, services and technical understanding for the "Process Industries" applications as per the industry standards and its regulatory requirements.
  • Manage all aspect of project life cycle from pre-sale proposal support, Engineering and site support as per the scope.
  • Will be reporting to the Engineering Manager.
  • Must be able to work independent and handle technical tasks and activity on HW/SW/HMI as well as any 3rd party interfaces as applicable in the projects.
  • This may include travel for short or long term work assignments within India or overseas for customer meetings, project training and site support.
  • Organizes and schedules job tasks with your team.
  • Provides leadership to the assigned team & Drive Productivity.
  • Manage team in terms of people development and issue resolution
  • Manage Global stakeholder including the regional solution teams and Global Program Managers
  • You will report to the Engineering Manager

Key Responsibilities:

  • Deliver & execute the assigned projects on time with good quality/ cost effectiveness and customer satisfaction
  • Take on challenging tasks and be a solution provider to internal & external stakeholders & industry team on solutions
  • Keep improving self-engineering capabilities and exchange best practice with other team members to improve team's capabilities
  • Lead technical implementation in whole life-cycle of project delivery
  • Collaborate & work with Technical Lead / SMEs to clarify technical issues and share workloads
  • Ability to present solution to the stakeholders and coordinate development tasks with team members
  • Work with PM for scope management, deliver execution plans & effectively monitor the execution with good quality, cost effectiveness, customer acceptance and customer satisfaction
  • Work closely with Global Program Managers and PM for WBS development
  • Develop robust quality process & ensure strict adherence
  • Work closely & guide the proposal team for timely support on estimation and proposal delivery
  • Ensure project implementation follows the procedure of SSB standard.
  • Drive project cost reduction through continuous process improvement, by applying and maximizing reusable engineering standards in project delivery
  • Drive business growth & strengthen foot print of GEC as technology CoE
  • Work for the optimization of procurement processes and identify process improvement opportunities
  • Support in AOP planning
  • Ensure that all audits (internal/external) are adequately documented and significant risk are dealt in a timely manner
  • Engage with various stakeholders to identify opportunities for increased engagement, seek feedback on deliverables and to improve overall engagement
  • Ensures deliverables provided by the GEC are delivered within exceptional good quality on time and on budget with high levels of Internal Customer Satisfaction.
  • Manage utilization of all resources and ensure accurate capacity planning.
  • Responsible to manage resources appropriately and to assign right skills to the work.

Job Related Competencies:

  • Have 10+ years of experience with min 5 years' experience on Sr. Engineer position.
  • Should have exposure on applications in Process Applications (consumer goods/life sciences).
  • High level of expertise in RA products such as Control Logix, FTView SE/ ME, ACM or GCM tool, GEMS or Process Objects.
  • Good understanding of advanced solutions like FT Batch, Line performance, LBSM, Sequence Manager, etc.
  • Good experience on PlantPAx systems and functionalities including PlantPAx 5.0. Awareness of NextGen libraries would be an added advantage.
  • Good understanding of Server Client architecture, Thin client and RDP
  • Experience with at least one third party DCS, PLC, HMI, SCADA systems each
  • Knowledge of IT software platforms, prior hands on experience with migrations, awareness of latest updates on upcoming automation technologies (Cloud solutions, IIoT etc.) would be an added advantage either
  • Conduct research into systems issues and products as required
  • Proven record of successfully managing multiple & complex customer projects and a good understanding about Project Management.
  • Major Commissioning Know-How experience and troubleshooting methods
  • Is flexible / responsive to manage global needs & expectations about GEC engineering deliveries
  • Recognized as a subject matter expert in Consumer Goods Industries Applications and lead the development of process improvements.
  • Manages team performance to develop team capability and motivate the team. Create developmental plans for any identified gaps.
  • Supports best practice sharing and knowledge transfer among teams to improve competency of whole team.
  • Assesses and develops competency and capabilities among the team. Encourages multi-skilling within the team to ensure better utilization.
