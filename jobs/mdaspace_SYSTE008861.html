Job Description

Building the space between proven and possible, MDA Space (TSX:MDA) is a trusted mission partner to the global space industry. A robotics, satellite systems and geointelligence pioneer with a 55-year+ story of world firsts and more than 450 missions, MDA Space is a global leader in communications satellites, Earth and space observation, and space exploration and infrastructure. The MDA Space team of more than 3,400 space experts in Canada, the US and the UK has the knowledge and know-how to turn an audacious customer vision into an achievable mission – bringing to bear a one-of-a-kind mix of experience, engineering excellence and wide-eyed wonder that’s been in our DNA since day one. For those who dream big and push boundaries on the ground and in the stars to change the world for the better, we’ll take you there.

The Role:

MDA is seeking a Systems Analyst to be part of the Business Systems Team; provides support to the MDA community in the design, development, implementation, and evaluation of new and existing systems hosted on premises and third party IaaS/PaaS/SaaS clouds. Provides analyst support for all phases of assigned systems development and implementation projects including hands-on application support.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Act as a link between IT and non-IT stakeholders to design/implement the appropriate on premises systems or cloud services to solve business problems

  • Work closely with managers and end-users to define systems requirements and business goals

  • Provide on-call support in support of production systems and support upgrades, Hardware/Software upgrade projects with impact analysis, fixes and end-to-end testing.

  • Provide operational support on failures and outages

  • Research and implement new technologies by participating in the assessment of tools, software, and technological practices

  • Design/implement customized technology solutions and enhancements to existing systems

  • Perform cost-benefit analyses for potential computer system upgrades to support purchase decisions

  • Assist in the deployment, customization and post-implementation troubleshooting of new systems

  • Collaborate and work effectively with other IT support teams to deliver required solution and a positive client experience

  • Follow the release cycles and implement on-time delivery of bug fixes, change requests and enhancements

  • Troubleshoot issues related to software, compute, storage and networking

  • Create and maintain requirements specifications

  • Design training programs, create documentation and user guides, and deliver training to other Team Members

Job Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer/Data Science or related discipline

  • 5+ years of experience in IT Systems Administration in an enterprise environment and cloud-based technologies

  • Strong communication, interpersonal, presentation and technical skills

  • Superior time management skills, multitasking skills, and the ability to prioritize tasks with minimal supervision

  • Critical Thinking, Strong problem-solving and analytical skills

  • Highly motivated, enthusiastic, and a positive thinker

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience with Azure platform administration

  • Experience with ERP, UKG, HR Systems, Time & Attendance Systems. Project Online, SharePoint Online, Salesforce, Banking Systems

  • Hands on experience in Soap/Rest API/web service

  • Relational Database Experience (SQL Server)

  • Programming languages: C# and VB.Net

  • Reporting and Business Intelligence platform: Power BI, IBM Cognos, Crystal reports, Microsoft BI (SSRS, SSIS, SSAS) are a plus or equivalent

Special Considerations:

Successful candidates must obtain and hold security clearance at the Reliability status level, and pass security assessment for the Controlled Goods Program (CGP), and be able to obtain higher clearances.
