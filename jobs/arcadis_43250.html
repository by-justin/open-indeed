Responsabilités:

  • Dans ce rôle, vous serez responsable du développement et de la gestion d'une petite équipe en pleine expansion à Vancouver.

  • Vous assurerez la production, la supervision et la gestion de la création de documents d'ingénierie pour une grande variété de projets, des plus petits aux plus grands, dans les secteurs routier, portuaire, ferroviaire et du transport en commun.

  • Vous dirigerez des équipes de projet afin de définir le problème, d'établir le périmètre des travaux, de préparer les budgets et les échéanciers, de planifier le travail et d'assurer la direction et la supervision techniques.

  • Vous effectuerez également des tâches d'ingénierie technique complexes avec efficacité et précision, et encadrerez les membres moins expérimentés de l'équipe.

  • Vous préparerez et vérifierez des calculs et des modèles d'ingénierie détaillés, des plans, des spécifications, des estimations de quantités et de coûts, des rapports techniques détaillés, des documents d'approvisionnement, des dossiers de conception, des propositions et des analyses détaillées.

  • Vous fournirez des services d'ingénierie technique pendant la construction, notamment l'examen des soumissions des entrepreneurs, la coordination et la collaboration avec les autres disciplines et départements d'ingénierie, et vous établirez et maintiendrez des relations de travail efficaces avec les clients.

  • Vous serez également responsable de la conduite réussie des projets, notamment de l'établissement des estimations budgétaires et des besoins en personnel, ainsi que de la rédaction de propositions convaincantes.

  • La personne titulaire du poste sera responsable d’assumera également des fonctions de gestion de projet, incluant la constitution et la gestion des équipes, le contrôle des budgets et des échéanciers, le contrôle de la qualité des livrables, la relation avec la clientèle et la gestion de ces relations, ainsi que le reporting financier.

  • Vous devrez respecter des échéanciers serrés tout en garantissant la qualité des livrables, apporter votre expertise et votre soutien technique aux dessinateurs-projeteurs CAO, aux concepteurs et aux ingénieurs moins expérimentés travaillant sur les mêmes projets et d'autres, tout en veillant au respect de toutes les normes et réglementations applicables.

  • Connaissances techniques des principes de conception et d'analyse des structures civiles et des ponts.

  • Excellentes compétences et expérience en rédaction de rapports techniques.

Role description:

Arcadis is a global leader in design and engineering for a sustainable future. Our Design & Engineering team is a dynamic group of professionals dedicated to delivering exceptional civil structural and geotechnical solutions across Canada. We are committed to fostering a collaborative, innovative, and inclusive work environment where our employees can thrive and contribute to the success of our clients and communities.

Arcadis is looking for a Discipline Team Leader with a strong civil structural background to develop and grow our design team in East Canada to lead and support road, rail, and port projects, including heavy civil structures, foundations, and bridges, in QC., Alberta and across Canada.

Arcadis provides flexible and remote work arrangements, career development opportunities and a supportive and collaborative team environment. We encourage involvement in professional associations, conferences, and support our staff to grow through life-long learning.

As a part of Mobility Advisory Canada, we partner with our clients across the globe to design thriving and connected cities and communities that enable opportunities for everyone. Climate change, urbanization and digitization trends are requiring today’s mobility projects and systems to address an evolving set of demands from the world’s growing population. We design connected, sustainable solutions that integrate existing infrastructure with new technologies, and optimize the mobility of people and goods.

Role accountabilities:

  • In this role you will be responsible for developing and managing a small and growing team in Vancouver, producing, supervising, and managing the creation of engineering products on a wide variety of small to large projects in the road, ports, rail and transit sectors.

  • You will lead project teams to define the problem, establish the work scope, prepare budgets and schedules, plan the work, and provide technical direction and oversight while also performing complex technical engineering tasks efficiently and accurately and lead and mentor less experienced team members.

  • You will prepare and check detailed engineering calculations and models, drawings, specifications, quantities and cost estimates, detailed technical reports, procurement documents, design packages, proposals, and detailed analyses.

  • You will provide technical engineering services during construction, including the review of contractor submissions, coordinate and collaborate with other Engineering disciplines and departments, and build and maintain effective working relationships with clients.

  • You will also be responsible for leading successful project pursuits, including determining budget estimates and staffing requirements and writing winning proposals.

  • The successful candidate will also perform Project Management duties including assembling and managing project teams, controlling project budgets and schedules, quality control of deliverables, client interaction and management, and financial reporting.

  • You will be expected to work to tight project schedules while ensuring quality of project deliverables, provide input and technical guidance to CAD Designer/Drafters, Designers, and less experienced Engineers working on the same and other projects, while ensuring compliance with all applicable codes and regulations.

Qualifications & Experience:

  • 4-year degree in Civil or Structural Engineering from a recognized post-secondary institution

  • Minimum 10 years experience in civil structure design on small, medium, and large civil engineering projects, ideally in the roads, rail, and ports sectors including deep excavations, retaining walls, foundations, and bridges

  • Strong communication, interpersonal, problem-solving, and collaborative skills

  • Ability to provide effective technical mentoring to less experienced engineers

  • Must be a licensed Professional Engineer in Quebec, and Atlantic Canada and Ontario

  • Technical knowledge of civil structural and bridge engineering design and analysis principles

  • Excellent technical report writing experience and skills
