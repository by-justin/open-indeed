Job Description

Position Summary:

As a creative problem-solving leader on the Core Solutions Consulting team, the candidate will have the chance to -create solutions for the most valuable client -problems. As the Core (account) Solution Consultant, you will be aligned to 2-3 Account Executives (AE/NAM) as technical lead and coordinate with a team of technical experts on DMe & DX to craft solutions, and vision, that pursue prospects' most meaningful business problems. Also, partnering with cross functional resources such as Product Marketing, Product Management, and Engineering will enable you to be the voice of the field and drive innovation for Adobe. As part of this, you will tailor sales plays, use-cases, and technical solutions that clearly articulate the context and technical value of Digital Media solutions including ongoing advancements in GenAI. You will work collaborative with DX counterparts to showcase the end-to-end value of the Adobe Experience Platform. Depth of technical knowledge of Adobe solutions will be key to your success and value to the team.

Solid experience in partnering with sales to drive customer change and innovation is a requirement. 4+ years’ experience in Digital Media solutions is key. Additionally, “customer side” experience is an asset that would bring credibility to our sales process and most importantly our customers.

Prioritization is a key skill. You must be able to strategize with your account team (and management) to manage your schedule to drive revenue impact and help achieve quarterly/annual quota. This can be one of the most difficult aspects, choosing the right activities to helping drive sales and when to say no with kindness and respect. We want you to maintain a healthy work/life balance and will support you in doing so.

Job Description:

The Core Solution Consultant primarily leads a pre-sales technical team that is focused on the development and implementation of complex products/applications/solutions with Digital Media (creative) technologies. Core SCs use in-depth product knowledge to provide technical expertise to sales staff and the customer through discovery, sales presentations, and product demonstrations. Core SCs assist the Sales in assessing solutions’ ability to meet the customer needs, including the handoff of detailed product specifications to post-sales teams for the development and implementation of solution. This is a dynamic role that will allow the individual the freedom to tackle something new and exciting at each turn. Core SC activities span prioritized sales stages, across key opportunities, and engage with various account teams and surrounding resources. The Core SC must be able to easily integrate into many different environments and team cultures, and, ultimately, play the role that is best for the customer.This will vary across engagements based on customer journey, personas, relationships, etc.

· The Core SC will partner with the Adobe Account team aligned directly with the AE/NAM, to set the strategy, vision, and technical solution for Digital Media customer opportunities to maximize the customer benefits and establish a baseline for value realization.

· A Core SC drives the technical ecosystem to understand the customer’s needs, match that to Adobe technology and communicate the value this solution will deliver to the customer.

· The Core SC will coordinate key deliverables and programmatic artifacts to ensure that the account reaches the sales goals and beyond, in coordination with the account team and specialist SCs (DX, DC, FF, Substance, Frame.io) when engaged by their Product Sales Specialists.

· The Core SC will provide tech risk analysis, updates, and consult on other structural updates to the larger team/ecosystem.
