Job Description

Job Description

Overview

You’ll play a key role in delivering projects within our Building Structures team in Manchester, taking responsibility for both design and project delivery across a varied client base. Our work is underpinned by a strong commitment to innovation, sustainability, ethics, and safety, and you’ll be central to embedding these principles in everything you do. You will contribute to the design and delivery of projects while developing your technical expertise and supporting collaborative team outcomes.

Join our well-established Manchester team and be part of a dynamic, collaborative environment delivering a diverse and exciting portfolio of projects across the UK and beyond.

Our Manchester office is involved in a vast array of work, including Industrial and Commercial developments, Education schemes, Asset Inspections, Refurbishments, Civil Infrastructure and Rail projects.

You’ll have the opportunity to contribute to technically challenging and varied schemes, working alongside experienced engineers and multi-disciplinary teams to deliver innovative, sustainable solutions. From detailed design on new build developments to the assessment and strengthening of existing structures, no two projects are the same – offering excellent scope to broaden your experience and develop your expertise.

The Manchester team combines a strong local presence with the backing of a global organisation, providing the best of both worlds: meaningful regional work with national and international support, knowledge sharing, and career development opportunities.

Your role

As a Structural Engineer, you will:

  • Apply engineering knowledge across a range of building structures projects.
  • Support project delivery from concept through to detailed design.
  • Contribute to innovative and sustainable design solutions.
  • Work collaboratively within multi-disciplinary teams.
  • Engage with clients and stakeholders on live projects.
  • Apply technical skills to a wide range of building structures and design scenarios.
  • Develop conceptual solutions through to detailed design stages.
  • Assist in delivering projects within defined programme and cost parameters.
  • Contribute to the multi-disciplinary team approach on projects.
  • Undertake research, data collection, and presentation of findings.
  • Present results to clients under supervision of a lead designer.
  • Present findings and solutions through excellent written communication and report writing.
  • Represent AtkinsRéalis at site visits and design team meetings.
  • Ensure compliance with Quality Assurance procedures.
  • Work in accordance with BIM standards, Digital Plan of Work, and BIM Execution Plans.
  • Support and mentor junior or less experienced team members.
  • Maintain and develop a broad technical skillset.
  • Demonstrate proactive commitment to self-development.

About you

You’ll bring a strong technical foundation along with a proactive and collaborative approach:

  • Degree in Civil or Structural Engineering.
  • Graduate Member of a relevant professional body (IStructE or ICE).
  • Working towards, and be close to achieving Chartered status, or has recently achieved Chartership status.
  • Experience designing traditional materials (steel, concrete, timber, masonry) to Eurocodes.
  • Familiarity with structural analysis software (e.g. Tekla Structural Designer, Robot, Revit – desirable).
  • Willingness to learn and adopt new tools and technologies.
  • Interest in digital engineering, automation, and parametric design.
  • Strong communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and take initiative.
  • Flexible and versatile in approach, undertaking design, inspection and technical advisory work, with responsibility for project aspects commensurate with proven capability and experience.
  • Curious mindset and enthusiasm for collaboration and idea-sharing.
  • Willingness to travel and work across different offices when required.
