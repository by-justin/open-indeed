Job Description

Company Secretary (Manufacturing Listed Company, India)

Experience: 15+ years Designation: Company Secretary & Compliance Officer (KMP) Reporting to: Managing Director & CFO Location: Gurugram

Role Summary

The Company Secretary (CS) will act as a Key Managerial Personnel and Compliance Officer responsible for ensuring compliance with Companies Act, SEBI LODR, and sector-specific manufacturing regulations, while providing strategic governance advisory to the Board.

Key Responsibilities

1. Corporate Governance & Board Advisory

  • Advise the Board on corporate governance, compliance risks, and regulatory developments

  • Manage Board, Audit Committee, NRC, CSR and other Committee meetings

  • Drive governance frameworks across multiple manufacturing units

  • Ensure implementation of Secretarial Standards and Board processes

  • Support Board in risk oversight

2. SEBI LODR & Listed Entity Compliance

  • Act as Compliance Officer under SEBI LODR

  • Ensure:

  • Timely stock exchange disclosures (financial & operational)

  • Compliance with related party transactions norms

  • Disclosure of material events (including plant shutdowns, accidents, expansions)

  • Manage investor communications and earnings disclosures

  • Handle insider trading compliance and structured digital database (SDD)

3. EHS, ESG & Sustainability Governance

  • Support governance and reporting on:

  • Environmental, Health & Safety (EHS) frameworks

  • ESG disclosures and sustainability reporting

  • Ensure accurate reporting of:

  • Safety incidents

  • Environmental compliance breaches (if any)

  • Work with internal teams on BRSR / ESG reporting (for listed entities)

4. Statutory & Secretarial Compliance

  • Ensure compliance with:

  • Companies Act, 2013

  • SEBI Regulations (LODR, PIT, ICDR)

  • FEMA, RBI norms (for import/export exposure)

  • Maintain statutory registers and filings with ROC/MCA

  • Manage secretarial audit, annual compliance certifications, and board disclosures

5. Capital Markets & Corporate Actions

  • Manage Dividend declarations, bonus/rights issue, ESOPs

  • Support M&A, JV, and restructuring transactions involving plants/business units

  • Liaise with merchant bankers, legal advisors, and regulators

6. Stakeholder & Regulatory Interface

  • Act as key liaison with SEBI, Stock Exchanges, ROC, MCA

  • Manage shareholder relations and grievance redressal

  • Support audit interactions (internal, statutory, secretarial)

Qualification & Experience

  • Member of ICSI (qualified CS)

  • 15+ years experience, preferably in:

  • Listed manufacturing companies or industrial groups

  • Strong exposure to:

  • SEBI LODR & Companies Act

  • Multi-location regulatory compliance

  • EHS / ESG frameworks (preferred)
