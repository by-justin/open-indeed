ポジション概要

Autodesk Customer Success では、お客様が製品への投資価値を最大化し、具体的なビジネス成果を実現できるよう支援する、意欲の高いテクニカルアドバイザーを募集しています。

本ポジションでは、Architecture, Engineering and Construction（AEC）業界における高い技術経験、Autodesk ソリューションに関する深い専門知識、そして AI を活用したワークフローへの強い関心と理解を持つプロフェッショナルを求めています。理想的な候補者は、AEC 領域において AI 技術の探索・検証・活用、または導入支援・助言を行った実践的な経験を有しています。

大規模な顧客環境で幅広い技術を支援した経験を持ち、戦略顧客に対して Autodesk ソリューション、特に AI 活用型ワークフローの導入・定着を支援できる方を求めています。本ポジションでは、実務に基づく高い技術的信頼性に加え、生産性、品質、コラボレーション、ビジネス成果の向上につながる実用的なワークフローを、お客様に分かりやすく伝え、活用を支援する力が求められます。

本ポジションは、Customer Success Management グループ内の Technical Adoption チームに所属します。このチームは、戦略顧客が Autodesk への投資からより大きな成果を得られるよう支援するとともに、社員の専門性開発、成長支援、高いパフォーマンスへの評価を重視しています。

Position Overview

Autodesk Customer Success is looking for highly motivated technical advisors to help our customers maximize their product investment and realize measurable business outcomes.

We are looking for a professional with strong technical experience in the Architecture, Engineering and Construction (AEC) industry, with deep technical expertise in Autodesk solutions and a strong focus on AI-enabled workflows. The ideal candidate has hands-on experience exploring, testing, using, or advising on AI technologies in an AEC context.

You should have experience supporting a broad range of technologies in large-scale customer environments and be comfortable helping large strategic customers improve the way they adopt Autodesk solutions, particularly for AI-enabled workflows. This role requires both hands-on technical credibility and the ability to coach customers on practical workflows that improve productivity, quality, collaboration, and business outcomes.

You will join the Technical Adoption team within the Customer Success Management group, a team that helps strategic customers maximize success and achieve stronger returns on their investment while valuing professional development, supporting growth, and rewarding high performance.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver product and workflow coaching sessions that help strategic customers adopt Autodesk solutions and activate key workflows.
  • Conduct coaching discovery calls to understand customer workflows, adoption barriers, learning needs, and desired outcomes.
  • Lead live coaching and scalable group enablement sessions (1: many) for end users, including workflow-specific guidance and best-practice sharing.
  • Act as a trusted technical advisor to customers, demonstrating thought leadership and product expertise to help customers adopt and achieve business outcomes using Autodesk technology
  • Develop, update, and maintain enablement content, learning resources, and digital assets that support coaching delivery and address specific customer workflow challenges, in line with Autodesk quality standards.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience
  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in the AEC industry or AEC technology industry
  • Expert knowledge of Autodesk Revit and/or Forma, formerly Autodesk Construction Cloud
  • Interest in AI technologies and hands-on experience exploring or testing how AI can be applied to AEC workflows.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and prioritize effectively
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to clearly explain complex technical concepts
  • Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills
  • Ability to converse fluently in Japanese

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience applying AI technologies to real-world AEC workflows, such as design exploration, model checking, documentation, coordination, analysis, knowledge management, or process automation.
  • Additional knowledge of AutoCAD and/or other Autodesk AEC solutions, and AI
  • Experience with automation, scripting, computational design, low-code or no-code environments, or data-driven workflow optimization is a plus
  • Experience working as a BIM manager or Automation lead in a architecture/construction firm beneficial
  • Experience delivering technical training to large, demanding customers
  • Demonstrated ability to build and maintain strong relationships at all levels
  • Excellent team player, enjoying supporting and interacting with other members of a global team.
  • Ability to converse in English.
