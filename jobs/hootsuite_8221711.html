Job Description

Please submit your resume in English

We’re looking for an Account Executive who will help us drive net-new opportunities with mid sized companies and meaningfully impact customers' business goals. You will work in a fast paced sales environment selling Hootsuite and our partner ecosystem solutions to prospective customers. You will be responsible for sourcing your own pipeline through business development and prospecting as well as partnering with SDRs. You will use your sales skills to educate prospective customers on the benefits and value of Hootsuite solutions and manage the end to end sales process, helping our customers choose Hootsuite as their Social Media Management partner. While based in Hootsuite’s Mexico City office, this is a hybrid work from home/in an office environment.

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

  • Prospect and generate your own leads in order to achieve individual quota targets

  • Identify and develop new business opportunities with mid size companies. You will partner with our pre & post sales teams to ensure customer success and long term value

  • Collaborate with sales leaders and relevant stakeholders to drive the entire sales process, including the negotiation of scope and business terms, ensuring customer value is prioritized

  • Create and execute on the quarterly sales and account plans that drive high volume activities for your assigned territory

  • Effectively navigate fast sales cycles and present the value of our solutions to all levels of prospective customers, including C-Level executives

  • Recommend and sell relevant solutions by offering related product mix from the range of Hootsuite products to solve clients business challenges

  • Serve as voice of the customer by sharing external market insights and feedback from prospects and customers to the Product and Marketing teams in order to drive improvements and product/feature requests

  • Perform other related duties as required

WHAT YOU’LL NEED:

  • Demonstrated applicable B2B outbound sales experience (experience in software considered an asset)

  • Proven ability to prospect with a thorough process, assess customer challenges, align solutions and read prospective buyers

  • Demonstrated ability to predictably forecast, leverage data driven insights in  decision making within the sales process and simultaneously manage a high volume of opportunities and transactions

  • Proven ability to successfully collaborate and build trust based relationships with teams such as Presales, Customer Success, Executive Leadership, etc throughout the sales process

  • Demonstrated experience working with senior executives and their teams, and building consensus at speed among multiple, cross-functional decision makers and influencers in a company

  • Experience using sales tools such as SFDC, Sales Navigator, 6Sense

  • Exposure to a line of business such as Marketing, Sales, Finance where you have learned and applied direct knowledge of business challenges issues to your solution.

  • Proven record of consistent quota attainment and can share experiences of developing business plans with a mix of tactics.

  • Excellent communication and presentation skills (phone, video, email)

  • Adaptable and able to manage through a fast paced, constantly changing environment

  • Commitment to Results: consistently achieving results, demonstrating high performance, and challenging self and others to deliver results

  • Business Acumen: Focus on business value vs. features/products selling

  • Customer Focus: demonstrates a desire to proactively help and serve internal/external customers meet their needs

  • Negotiation: successfully obtains commitment to a solution or idea, while maintaining integrity and relationships

  • Perseverance: pursues everything with energy, drive, and a need to finish—doesn’t give up

WHO YOU ARE:

  • Tenacious. You are determined to succeed and motivated by the success of customers, colleagues and the community.

  • Curious. You are always learning and seeking ways to make things better.

  • Conscientious. You keep your promises, taking your commitments to others seriously, and you have strong integrity.

  • Humble. You lead with humility and empathy, respecting and learning from the perspectives of others.

  • You have decided on a career path in sales for the long term. You are focused on building your skills and expertise and committing to a journey of self development

  • Crear y ejecutar planes de ventas y cuentas trimestrales que impulsen actividades de gran volumen para tu territorio asignado.

  • Avanzar eficazmente por ciclos de ventas rápidos y presentar el valor de nuestras soluciones a todos los niveles de clientes potenciales, incluidos altos directivos.

  • Recomendar y vender soluciones relevantes ofreciendo una combinación de productos relacionados de la gama de productos de Hootsuite para resolver los desafíos comerciales de los clientes.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
