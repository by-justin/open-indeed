Job Description

Job Location: Brucejack Workplace Type:   Onsite Pay Grade: 002   Closing Date:

Brucejack is one of the highest-grade gold mines in the world – a remote underground operation famously located on top of a glacier in northwestern British Columbia. With a fly-in, fly-out roster and on-site camp, our team works in a truly unique environment, using modern mining methods to safely extract gold and silver from deep beneath the ice. The challenges of Brucejack’s location create a tight-knit team spirit: safety, adaptability and camaraderie are paramount in everything we do. We are quietly proud of our strong partnerships with local First Nations and nearby communities, which help us operate responsibly and share benefits in the region. Brucejack offers the rare opportunity to build a career on the cutting edge of mining, delivering world-class results in an environment like no other.

Role Purpose

Join Newmont’s Brucejack Mine Winter Snow Removal Team and help keep surface operations safe, accessible, and efficient throughout the winter season. As a Surface Labourer, you’ll perform snow removal, site clean-up, material handling, and general labour duties in a remote camp environment while maintaining Newmont’s high safety standards. This is a temporary role .

Key Roles and Responsibilities

  • Assist with snow stacking, shoveling duties and winter site maintenance activities
  • Perform general housekeeping, site clean-up, and material handling
  • Support construction projects and tradespeople with labour tasks
  • Use hand tools and equipment to move materials and level ground
  • Follow all safety procedures, policies, and training requirements
  • Work safely in challenging weather conditions and outdoor environments

Required Skills, Knowledge, and Experience

  • High school diploma or GED
  • Physically fit and able to lift and move up to 50 lbs
  • Experience in mining, industrial, construction, or labour roles is an asset
  • Valid Class 5 Driver’s License preferred
  • WHMIS and TDG certifications are considered assets
  • Strong communication skills and commitment to workplace safety
  • Ability to work 12-hour shifts in remote winter conditions
  • Must successfully pass pre-employment drug and alcohol testing
