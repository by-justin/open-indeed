The Platforms TPM organization partners with Experimentation, ML, LLM/GenAI, and Data Infrastructure teams to shape how Pinterest measures everything that matters — from product experiments to AI systems to the platforms everyone builds on.

What you'll do:

  • As a Staff Technical Program Manager for Measurement, you'll have the rare opportunity to shape how a company the size of Pinterest measures itself — turning a portfolio spanning experimentation, machine learning, generative AI, and data infrastructure into one coherent, high-impact program.

  • Drive Pinterest's experimentation roadmap — accelerating how confidently and quickly teams can test, learn, and ship new ideas at scale.

  • Own the program driving cost and compute efficiency across our ML systems, and help scale data science workflows into production-grade tooling.

  • Lead cost optimization and evaluation for our LLM and GenAI systems — building the measurement backbone that keeps them fast, affordable, and trustworthy.

  • Own Presto query optimization and support for our analytics agent, keeping the data infrastructure that powers company-wide decisions fast and reliable.

  • Own measurement of GenAI-driven productivity and evaluation frameworks for coding models — helping quantify the impact of AI on how we build.

  • Own cost attribution and usage understanding across shared infrastructure, bringing clarity to how the company invests in its platforms.

  • Own measurement and understanding of Shopping infrastructure, helping the team see clearly where to invest next.

  • Build repeatable prioritization frameworks that let the team move fast and confidently across a wide, fast-changing portfolio.

  • Build transparent communication channels that keep leadership and partner teams aligned on status, risk, and impact.

  • Mentor others and help set the standard for what great measurement program management looks like at Pinterest.

What we're looking for:

  • 5+ years of experience as a technical program manager, ideally driving broad, high-visibility portfolios.

  • A track record of building order out of ambiguity — leading complex, multi-team initiatives from the ground up.

  • Strong instincts for, or hunger to quickly learn, experimentation platforms, ML systems, LLM infrastructure, or data platforms.

  • A sharp sense for prioritization — comfortable making confident calls across competing initiatives with incomplete information.

  • Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with a bias toward pragmatic, creative solutions.

  • A track record of earning trust with executive leadership and representing your program's interests at the highest levels.

  • Excellent written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills — you know how to bring people along.

  • A genuine commitment to inclusion, sound judgment under pressure, and courageous, resilient leadership.

  • Bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science, or equivalent experience.
