About the role.

Sustainability isn't just a priority at McCain - it's a responsibility we take seriously across every site, every shift, and every decision. As our REO (Resource Efficient / Environment Operations) Manager for GB&I, you'll be at the heart of that effort, leading the deployment of our sustainability strategy across the GB&I manufacturing network and turning ambition into measurable action.

From reducing CO₂ emissions and cutting energy consumption to driving progress on water stewardship, renewable electricity and waste reduction, you'll own the environmental performance agenda across the network. Working alongside plant, engineering and regional teams, you'll bring structure where it's needed, cut through complexity, and champion consistency - all while helping to build a culture where sustainability and continuous improvement go hand in hand.

If you're ready to make a genuine environmental impact at scale, this is the role to do it in.

What you'll be doing.

  • Drive deployment of the REO roadmap across all sites
  • Ensure compliance with environmental regulations and internal policies
  • Track and analyse performance indicators (CO₂, water, energy, waste)
  • Develop and coordinate sustainability action plans
  • Lead the community of plant REO managers
  • Harmonise processes and governance at regional level
  • Conduct internal audits and manage relationships with authorities
  • Promote best practices and a culture of continuous improvement
  • Support initiatives to reduce environmental impact
  • Collaborate with global, regional and local teams on key projects

What you'll need to be successful.

  • Extensive leadership experience in industry, engineering or R&D
  • Strong understanding of industrial environments and associated KPIs
  • Proven project management experience
  • Knowledge of environmental management systems (ISO 14001 / 50001)
  • Excellent communication and influencing skills
  • Analytical, problem-solving and continuous-improvement mindset
  • Ability to support change and engage stakeholders

About the team.

You'll be joining our Operations team, which spans both GB&I and EMENA - a broad, experienced and closely connected manufacturing community. Reporting into our Director, REO (who in turn reports into our VP, Operations EMENA), you'll also enjoy a strong dotted-line relationship and active partnership with our VP, Operations GB&I, meaning you'll be well-connected at a senior level on both sides of the network.

On the ground, you'll work alongside a high-performing manufacturing team across four plants here in GB&I - one of which sits within our global snacking business - and you'll partner closely with your counterpart leading the same agenda across Europe. While your primary focus will be GB&I, the nature of this role means you'll occasionally pick up projects, conversations and priorities in other markets too, so a flexible, collaborative mindset will go a long way.

It's a role that sits at the intersection of local delivery and regional influence - and the team around you reflects that ambition
