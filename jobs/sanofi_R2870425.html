JOB PURPOSE:

The Order to Cash (O2C) – Customer Contact Management (CCM) Senior Analyst is responsible for managing customer inquiries, requests, claims, returns, and disputes across the Order-to-Cash process.

The role serves as a key point of contact between customers and internal stakeholders, with the objective to:

  • Deliver timely and accurate resolution of customer cases within defined service level targets.
  • Maximize First Contact Resolution (FCR), particularly for standard customer requests and inquiries.
  • Ensure all customer cases are properly registered, classified, investigated, resolved, and documented in Salesforce.
  • Provide a consistent and positive customer experience through clear communication, effective case ownership, and timely follow-up.
  • Ensure customer claims, returns, and related financial transactions are managed in accordance with applicable policies, approval requirements, and internal controls.
  • Support continuous improvement through accurate case classification, root cause identification, and analysis of recurring customer issues.

This role requires a detailed understanding of Order to Cash activities passionate by customer satisfaction and problem resolution

ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT:

SBO O2C

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES:

Customer Case Management & Resolution

  • Manage customer inquiries, requests, claims, returns, and disputes from receipt through resolution, ensuring adherence to agreed service-level targets.
  • Accurately register, classify, prioritize, and maintain cases in Salesforce in accordance with the established case classification framework.
  • Conduct initial case assessment and drive First Contact Resolution (FCR) wherever feasible.
  • Ensure all case activities, communications, decisions, and resolution outcomes are documented accurately and in a timely manner.

Claims, Returns & Transaction Management

  • Review claims and returns against applicable policies, procedures, and business rules, and escalate exceptions or non-compliant cases for appropriate business decision.
  • Coordinate investigations involving refusals, damages, shortages, short shelf-life, distribution center errors, and other delivery or service discrepancies.
  • Determine and execute the appropriate resolution process in line with claims and returns management guidelines.
  • Manage related transactions and supporting documentation, including credit/debit memos, return orders, refusal orders, and other corrective actions, where applicable.

Stakeholder Coordination & Customer Communication

  • Collaborate with relevant internal and external stakeholders, including Supply Chain, Commercial, Quality, Finance, distributors, logistics providers, and internal sites, to facilitate timely case resolution.
  • Gather relevant information and supporting documentation, establish clear ownership, and follow up on outstanding actions through to closure.
  • Escalate critical, complex, or overdue cases through the appropriate escalation and governance channels.
  • Ensure clear, timely, and professional communication of case progress, decisions, and resolution outcomes to customers and relevant stakeholders.

Governance, Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement:

  • Ensure required approval workflows, internal controls, policies, and supporting documentation are consistently applied.
  • Monitor operational reports and Salesforce dashboards to manage open, aging, overdue, and high-priority cases.
  • Ensure consistent case classification and data quality to support meaningful reporting and root cause analysis.
  • Identify recurring issues and process gaps and support corrective actions and continuous improvement to enhance FCR, resolution lead time, SLA compliance, and overall customer experience.

Continuous Improvement

  • Analyze process metrics and identify optimization opportunities
  • Lead small-scale improvement projects
  • Collaborate with IT teams on system enhancements
  • Document and share best practices
  • Measure and track adoption success metrics
  • Support team leads on performance reporting

Process & System Management

  • Monitor and maintain O2C processes and system performance for a given area
  • Provide first-level support for process and system-related issues to end-users
  • Manage master data readiness & quality when relevant
  • Create, document & monitor incidents, change requests, tickets in ServiceNow, Jira, OneSupport
  • Represent the hub team for a given area in the corresponding governances (AMS calls, monthly Key Users community)
  • Contribute to business requirements gathering for Core model evolution/continuous improvement initiatives
  • Update CI tracker for owned initiatives
  • Update documentation (DTPs, BOP) per RACI

Training & Support

  • Create and maintain process documentation and work instructions
  • Coach and mentor team members on system functionality
