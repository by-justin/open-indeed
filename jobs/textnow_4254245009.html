Job Description

We believe that communication belongs to everyone. TextNow is redefining how people connect by combining simplicity, intelligence, and accessibility. We’re a team of curious builders using technology to make communication more affordable and powerful for millions of users every day.

Free has to keep getting better. Monetization has to feel fair. And there’s a much larger TextNow ahead of us than the one we’ve built so far.

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager to help shape that future.

What You'll Do

You’ll lead a customer-facing product area at TextNow. Scope will be defined at offer and calibrated to your strengths alongside the company’s highest-impact opportunities. We craft roles around leaders — not the other way around.

You’ll own strategy and outcomes for your area, partner directly with company leadership, and ship products that move us closer to our mission of democratizing phone service.

You’ll be expected to:

  • Own product strategy, roadmap, execution, and outcomes for a key product area

  • Identify meaningful customer problems and translate them into impactful solutions

  • Partner closely with Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, and Leadership teams

  • Make high-quality decisions in ambiguous, fast-moving environments

  • Drive experimentation, prioritization, and iteration using both qualitative and quantitative inputs

  • Balance customer experience, business impact, and technical realities

  • Build systems and workflows that leverage AI as part of how products and teams operate

  • Ship quickly, learn continuously, and raise the bar for product thinking across the company

You Will Be a Match If

  • You build for users, not at them, and consistently identify meaningful customer problems

  • You thrive in ambiguous environments and can make thoughtful decisions before consensus forms

  • You have strong product instincts and high standards for quality and execution

  • You’ve shipped products scrappily and understand the measurable impact of your work

  • You know how to prioritize effectively and communicate tradeoffs clearly

  • You care deeply about outcomes, learning, and continuous improvement

  • You elevate the people around you and work collaboratively across functions

How the Team Operates

  • Outcomes matter more than output

  • Customer insight guides decisions, while metrics validate direction

  • AI is integrated into how we work, not just the tools we use

  • Teams operate with transparency and close partnership

  • We value speed, ownership, and building over bureaucracy

You Might Be a Good Fit If

  • 4+ years of product management experience owning a defined product area end-to-end

  • Proven track record delivering large-scale initiatives impacting millions of consumers

  • Experience building B2C mobile products, ideally involving subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or trust & safety

  • Strong analytical and experimentation skills, with experience making decisions in evolving environments

  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills

  • Fluency with AI — from building workflows to creating scalable systems and operating models

  • Strong product instincts paired with operational rigor and execution speed

Your First 90 Days

First 30 Days

Build context. Develop a deep understanding of your product area, customers, business model, and opportunities. Form a strong point of view on what matters most.

By 90 Days

Ship something meaningful that drives measurable impact and generates learnings for the business and team.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
