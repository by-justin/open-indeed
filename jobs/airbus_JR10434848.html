Job Description

Job Description:

Accountability:

This position aims to provide the sufficient engineering service for preparing, scheduling and following up various maintenance, storage, engine and/or dismantling work. He/she prepares the work, ensures the follow-up and provides mechanics and technicians with technical support. He/she is required to determine solutions especially in the structure domain in accordance with the manufacturer's guidelines.

Operational activities:

  • Prepare and set up the items of the maintenance, storage, engine and/or dismantling work site (Work Package, scheduling) in accordance with:the protocol provided by the customer customised manufacturer documentation (AMM, IPC, etc.).
  • Establish work orders in accordance with the latest updates of the manufacturer's documentation.
  • Provide approved data for all work to be performed, maintenance, modifications or repairs, in accordance with the organisation's procedures and CAAC/EASA/NAA rules and requirements
  • State work requirements in accordance with the technical documentation and the work protocol/programme (WP) provided by the customer.
  • Produce all documents required to ensure the completion of all contracted work and to meet allocated deadlines.
  • Assist in the follow-up and management of schedules (start of workshop, end of TA, TC workshop).
  • Ensure that all work carried out on aircraft has a work order (WO) saved in the existing system.
  • Ensure that the aircraft's technical documentation is available to work staff, in coordination with the Project Manager.
  • Distribute the required documentation sufficiently in advance to allow the organisations involved (Production/Procurement/GSE & Tooling) to deliver their associated product (Qualified staff/Equipment/GSE/ Tooling) on time
  • Manage the follow-up and tracing of removed parts including equipment in coordination with the ordering officers.
  • View, during site inspections, the technical problems encountered on the aircraft.
  • Propose technical support to teams operating on site.
  • Provide operating experience.
  • Participate in the continuous improvement of assistance documents and tools and procedures in coordination with the Quality Department and the technical teams
  • Assist the Project Manager on request
  • Ensure the technical follow-up of maintenance, storage and dismantling work
  • Be the technical focal point for managing the Work Package in conjunction with the Project Manager
  • Prepare and manage additional work related to: Non Routine Card, Customer Work Order initiated by the Project Manager, Deferred work initiated by the Project Manager, etc.
  • Follow up and update work documents
  • Issue the CAAC/EASA F1.
  • Manage all technical records involved to ensure that an appropriate CRS can be produced
  • Prepare the items of the release file with the assistance of "C" staff of the work site by approving the "Work Package"
  • Produce RTS supporting documentation in accordance with the customer contract and CAA/EASA/NAA rules and requirements.
  • Check the Work Orders
  • Update the work orders in accordance with the aircraft schedule
  • Ensure the follow-up of the airworthiness directives (AD) applicable to the related aircraft and equipment.
  • Participate in customer reporting as required, in support of the Project Manager
  • Carry out all the checks and tests required to ensure the technical compliance and traceability of the services provided.
  • Check and/or draft analysis reports
  • Communicate with manufacturers in technical English
  • Read drawings
  • Ensure the summary of maintenance, storage, engine and/or dismantling work in order to capitalise on the experience acquired
  • Issue the end of work file (CRS, RCT)
  • Ensure that the Work Pack is closed with release documents and finalise the aircraft file
  • Assess the project's positive or negative aspects
  • Update the tech log book in order to "release" the aircraft:Answer the tech log pointsManage deferred work
  • Release customer orders. Update the database and ensure that it is kept up to date in our ERP (work order, supplies, tools, etc.) in accordance with the manufacturer's technical documentation.
  • Library and Publication Management: Ensure that aircraft documents are kept current and available for all ALS operational activities and staff. Manage and dispatch documentation to all concerned ALS organisation and people.
  • Technical Records management: Manage all the Technical Records during and after maintenance check embodiment.
  • Other tasks that assigned by manager.
