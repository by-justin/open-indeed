Job Description

Clio is the global leader in legal AI technology, empowering legal professionals and law firms of every size to work smarter, faster, and more securely.

We are transforming the legal experience for all by bettering the lives of legal professionals while increasing access to justice.

Summary:

What You'll Do

As a Customer Training Specialist , you will play a key role in helping customers successfully adopt Vincent & vLex solutions. You'll design and deliver engaging training programs, support onboarding, improve product adoption, and partner closely with Customer Success, Sales, and Product teams to create an outstanding customer experience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver onboarding and advanced training sessions for customers in virtual and in-person formats.
  • Customize training content to meet the needs of the Mexican legal market and corporate customers.
  • Create learning materials, including guides, videos, webinars, and other educational resources.
  • Partner with Customer Success, Account Management, Renewals, and Sales teams to improve customer adoption and engagement.
  • Monitor training effectiveness and customer adoption using HubSpot and other internal tools.
  • Gather customer feedback and share insights to improve training programs and product adoption.
  • Support product launches by delivering webinars and enablement sessions.
  • Help ensure a smooth transition from implementation through long-term customer success.
  • As the business grows, expand training support to Academic and Government customers and other LATAM markets.

What You Bring

  • 2+ years of experience in Customer Training, Customer Success, Enablement, or Corporate Training, preferably in SaaS or LegalTech.
  • Native or fluent Spanish and intermediate to advanced English (B2+).
  • Excellent presentation, communication, and facilitation skills.
  • Experience using CRM platforms (HubSpot preferred) and e-learning/LMS tools.
  • Strong customer focus with the ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Based in Mexico.

Nice to Have

  • Background in Law, Education, or a related field.
  • Experience with legal research or legal knowledge management solutions.
  • Instructional design experience.
  • Experience working with Academic or Government customers.

Why Join Clio?

  • Hybrid work environment.

  • Opportunity to work with one of LATAM's fastest-growing LegalTech organizations.

  • Exposure to a diverse customer portfolio across Corporate, Law Firms, Academic, and Government sectors.

  • Collaborative, inclusive culture with opportunities for professional growth.

  • Ongoing learning and development in Customer Success and LegalTech.

If you're passionate about customer education, technology, and creating exceptional customer experiences, we'd love to hear from you.
