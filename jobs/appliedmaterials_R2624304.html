  As a Buyer, you’ll be responsible for purchasing the materials, supplies, machinery, and services that equip our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities. You’ll collaborate across teams and showcase your organization and strategic planning skills to monitor inventory, manage delivery scheduling, negotiate with suppliers, and procure essential items that transform possibilities into realities for our customers and teams.

Applied Materials is seeking highly motivated procurement professionals to support both production purchasing and New Product Introduction (NPI) initiatives. These roles are responsible for managing supplier relationships, sourcing materials, driving procurement execution, and ensuring uninterrupted supply to support manufacturing and product development activities.

The Buyer owns an assigned Purchasing Group (P-Group) and manages a high volume of parts and suppliers, while the NPI Buyer II & III focuses on full lifecycle management of complex custom components supporting new product development programs.

Successful candidates will be highly organized, analytical, proactive problem-solvers who thrive in fast-paced environments and are eager to continuously grow their expertise in supply chain, manufacturing, and product development.

Buyer - Core Function

  • Manages a large volume of parts and suppliers.
  • Owns and manages an assigned Purchasing Group (P-Group).
  • Ensures material availability to support production requirements.
  • Serves as the primary procurement contact for assigned commodities and suppliers.

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Receive demand requirements from assigned P-Group.
  • Issue Requests for Quotations (RFQs) and bids to suppliers.
  • Evaluate supplier quotations based on price, lead time, availability, quality, and overall value.
  • Select suppliers and negotiate pricing and delivery requirements when appropriate.
  • Create, release, and manage purchase orders.
  • Monitor open orders and supplier commitments.
  • Coordinate with planning, manufacturing, engineering, and operations teams regarding supply requirements.
  • Ensure timely delivery of materials to support business needs.
  • Maintain accurate purchasing records within ERP systems.

Supplier Management & Issue Resolution

  • Develop and maintain strong supplier relationships.
  • Monitor supplier performance including quality, cost, delivery, and responsiveness.
  • Track and drive resolution of Supplier Problem Sheets (SPS).
  • Ensure supplier-related issues continue progressing toward closure.
  • Escalate critical issues when necessary to minimize operational impact.
  • Support supplier development and performance improvement initiatives.

Continuous Improvement

  • Identify opportunities for procurement process improvements.
  • Support cost reduction initiatives and sourcing strategies.
  • Participate in supplier business reviews.
  • Analyze procurement metrics and supplier performance data.
  • Support inventory optimization and supply continuity efforts.

NPI Buyer II & III

Position Overview

The NPI Buyer II & III is responsible for sourcing, purchasing, and managing materials required for New Product Introduction (NPI) programs. This role focuses on highly engineered and technically complex components, requiring full lifecycle ownership from initial release through production readiness.

The ideal candidate thrives on challenges, takes ownership of critical projects, and is committed to continuous growth and development. This position serves as a key interface between engineering, manufacturing, sourcing, suppliers, program management, and leadership teams.

Candidates must be hungry for growth, eager to take on challenges, and capable of managing the complete lifecycle of highly complex parts and supplier relationships.

Key Responsibilities -New Product Introduction

  • Responsible for sourcing, purchasing, and managing materials required for new product development and introduction.
  • Lead supply chain management activities involving identification, development, qualification, and management of existing and new suppliers.
  • Partner with Manufacturing and Engineering to maintain inventory at planned levels and support production readiness.
  • Lead resolution of material-related design, accounting, supplier, and receiving issues.

Procurement & Supply Chain Leadership

  • Develop and implement business process simplification initiatives within procurement.
  • Participate in the creation and maintenance of ISO documentation related to purchasing processes.
  • Drive supplier accountability regarding delivery, quality, responsiveness, and program commitments.

Parts Lifecycle Management

  • Manage 200-250 custom NPI parts simultaneously.
  • Own full lifecycle management from engineering release through production readiness.
  • Coordinate daily and weekly parts reviews with suppliers, engineers, managers, directors, and program teams.
  • Track component readiness and drive issue resolution to support program milestones.
  • Manage long lead-time and highly complex custom components.
  • Identify supply chain risks and develop mitigation strategies.
  • Act as the primary point of contact between engineering, sourcing, suppliers, manufacturing, and leadership teams.

Cross-Functional Leadership

  • Direct end-to-end parts management activities including: Supplier communication Scheduling Escalations Risk management Issue resolution Delivery tracking Program execution
  • Work with technically complex components requiring proactive planning and detailed coordination rather than simple transactional purchasing.
  • Maintain alignment across all stakeholders throughout the product development process.
  • Continuously expand knowledge of supply chain, manufacturing, engineering, and product development operations.

Functional Knowledge

Problem Solving
