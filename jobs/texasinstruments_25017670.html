Analog Design Engineer Success Profile: Success Factors & Descriptions

Circuit Design, Analysis & Execution
Designs, and implements analog, analog-mixed signal & integrated circuits (ICs) that enable new TI products and customer solutions.
Leads IP and chip architecture, schematic design & simulation (pre- and post-layout) basis the specification requirements.
Applies deep understanding of analog fundamentals, process technologies, and circuit design techniques.
Delivers robust, innovative, and efficient designs aligning with customer needs.
Meets project milestones while adapting to changing requirements.
Develops and executes experiments to debug and improve designs.
Able to understand & use relevant software tools (MATLAB, Cadence, etc.) for drawing schematics, simulations, data processing, Modeling etc.
Understands and applies the concepts of feedback loops, design reliability, stability analysis, and debug analysis
Demonstrates a high sense of ownership on design decisions & accountability while executing technical developments.
Understands and applies TI’s NPDE Methodology; Proactively identifies and addresses risks in design & execution
Strives to improve execution efficiency using automation

Innovation, Problem Solving & Continuous learning
Stays abreast of industry trends and emerging Circuit topologies & architectures. Proactively proposes and implements innovative solutions
Demonstrates curiosity to go beyond assigned tasks to learn and understands the entire product development
Applies classroom/theoretical knowledge to real-world problems and expands expertise across application areas.
Engages in deep technical discussions and “what if” scenarios
Keeps the end-user in mind, anticipates market/application needs
Able to explain complex concepts and innovations to technical and non-technical stakeholders
Actively pursues professional development, continuous learning and shares knowledge with peers.
Seeks feedback; Responds constructively to failures or missed goals

Cross-functional Collaboration
Works with cross-disciplinary teams (Systems, layout, test, validation, design verification, digital design) across the full product lifecycle—front end to back end.
Participates & contributes to design reviews, product definition, requirements capture, bench characterization etc.
Builds trust and rapport across teams; listens to and incorporates diverse perspectives
Brings together input from different functions to optimize the design for layout ease, test, manufacturability and reliability
Demonstrates accountability and professionalism in team settings; proactively resolves technical and interpersonal obstacles.
Documents and Communicates results clearly through presentations and technical reports to diverse stakeholders.
