Purpose of the SE Role

As the technical counterpart to the VSAM (Virtual Sales Account Manager), you will provide high-quality remote pre-sales technical support. You will conduct solution demonstrations that encompass Cisco's overall architecture, including Network (NW), Security, and Observability, rather than focusing on specific products. Your goal is to guide customers in resolving their unique technical challenges. You will specifically drive the adoption and deployment of Cisco solutions by providing advanced technical enablement to partners serving public and social infrastructure sectors such as education committees, local governments, and medical institutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Scalable Technical Support: Collaborate closely with VSAM, leveraging cloud demo environments like web conferencing systems and Cisco dCloud. This enables you to conduct effective technical presentations, solution demonstrations, and Q&A sessions for multiple customers and partners daily.
  • Standardize Architecture Proposals: Develop reference architectures (standard proposal templates) tailored for modern use cases, such as the next phase of the GIGA School Initiative (GIGA School Renewal) and the relaxation of three-tier separation requirements in local governments (e.g., LGSS). These templates will be disseminated across the internal organization and partner ecosystem, contributing to standardized proposal quality and accelerated proposal speed.
  • Partner SE Enablement: Support the skill development of technical staff at local partners covering broad regions through webinars and remote hands-on workshops. Focus on Cisco's latest technologies, particularly security solutions (e.g., XDR, SASE, Cloud Security), to enhance the competitiveness of Cisco solutions in the market.

Key KPIs

Revenue Contribution from Supported Deals: Contribute to VSAM's quota achievement, aiming for $20M - $30M in Incremental Bookings annually.
