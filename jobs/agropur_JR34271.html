Job Description

Job Type:

RegularThe Laboratory Technician I is responsible for supporting production by analyzing a variety of samples using different test methods; performing microbiological and/or physical tests on raw material, in-process products and finished products. Daily tasks include the receipt of samples, determining methods by reviewing procedures and specifications, testing samples (reporting any abnormal reactions to Laboratory leadership), documenting, and reporting of results, and cleaning and stocking the work area. The Laboratory Technician I is also responsible for daily maintaining laboratory equipment and performing routine quality control checks.

Your role in achieving results:

  • Perform chemistry and/or physical sample preparation and testing.
  • Responsible for equipment operation, calibration, cleaning and minor maintenance.
  • Analyze and report laboratory data, verifying interpretation with more senior Technicians or leadership.
  • Accurately enter all required data into log books, spreadsheets and the reporting system as appropriate.
  • Perform general lab cleanup and organization; restocking supplies as needed.
  • Follow work instructions set forth by Test Procedures (TPs), Standard Operation Procedures (SOPs), Good Manufacturing Practices (GMPs) and Master Sanitation Schedules (MSS) and Good Laboratory Practices (GLP).
  • Maintain a clean work place that is free of safety hazards.
