Job Description

The Video and Digital Evidence Systems Unit (VDESU) requires a self-motivated individual to ensure the efficient operation, maintenance, and administration of the Edmonton Police Service’s physical and virtual infrastructure.  Roles and responsibilities include:

  • Provide day-to-day operational support and maintenance of assigned applications and infrastructure.

  • Monitor system health, logs, batch jobs, and incident queues. Troubleshoot incidents and service requests escalated from the Service Desk and implement remedial actions.

  • Maintain existing systems through routine checks, maintenance activities, operational testing, unit testing, procedural testing, pre-user acceptance testing, and patching.

  • Participate in assigned phases of solution delivery, including research, design, development, implementation, and maintenance.

  • Install, configure, and maintain application and server components within established standards.

  • Assist with planned enhancements and updates and complete low-risk, well-defined assignments using existing technologies.

  • Maintain technical documentation, system procedures, and operational records.

  • Communicate system status and issues to internal stakeholders and collaborate primarily with internal teams.

  • Resolve operational and technical issues using established procedures and standards and escalate complex, high-risk, or cross-platform issues to senior analysts or project managers.

  • Perform related duties as required. Qualifications

  • General knowledge of multiple technology areas and system integration.

  • Strong troubleshooting, analytical, problem-solving, organizational, communication, and collaboration skills.

  • Ability to work independently and in a team environment.

  • Experience with the Axon suite of products, Genetec Clearance, Genetec Security Center, Microsoft Esri, SaaS video solutions, video analysis or analytics, facial recognition, or end-user training would be considered an asset.

  • Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), Microsoft Certified Solutions Associate (MCSA), Systems Security Certified Practitioner (SSCP), or Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate would be considered an asset.

  • A valid Class 5 Alberta Driver's License is required. A City Driver's License application, access to a personal vehicle, and written proof of current public liability and property damage insurance to a minimum of $1,000,000 are required. Mileage for private vehicle use during assigned duties will be reimbursed. Client transportation is not required.

Hours of Work : 40 hours per week. Hours of work may be subject to the terms and conditions of a variable hours of work program. Incumbents must be available for call-out outside of these hours on a rotational basis.
