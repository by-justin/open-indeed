Job Description

In support of the North America growth agenda, the Program Manager. Manages the implementation of large, complex new business start-up projects. Supports Business Development by de-risking new business opportunities through execution. Manages a portfolio of projects with a specific set of business objectives not achievable through management as individual projects. Ensures Ops Excellence and Project Delivery strategies are incorporated to all North America startups for large, high risk and complexity projects/programs that they lead. Deploy the strategic objectives set by the Project Delivery/Ops Excellence Leadership. Provide coaching and guidance to less experienced Project Managers in the execution of sales to delivery and DePICT® tools and processes and quality standards of Project Delivery.
