JOB SUMMARY:

The employee in this classification, on assigned shifts, performs a variety of tasks associated with the steam plant operation, fuel supply, emission control systems and other tasks associated with a thermal power station.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES:

• Operate, monitor, troubleshoot, clean and perform routine servicing and maintenance of assigned plant equipment, including the water treatment plant and ash removal system • Perform routine procedures to clean and ignite burners of gas, oil or pulverized coal-fired boilers where required • Receive and document fuel supply shipments • Arrange for appropriate permits and perform switching, as authorized, in accordance with the Permitting System • Operate, monitor and troubleshoot potable water and sewage systems where required to meet provincial and federal regulations • Provide assistance to shift personnel in the operation of boilers and associated equipment • Conduct inspections, analyze and diagnose equipment malfunctions and take corrective action, as required • Participate in preventative and corrective maintenance programs • Compile and maintain records detailing work performed, problems encountered, resources used, equipment malfunctions and/or corrective action taken and prepare various reports of information related to plant operation • Inform incoming shift personnel and other persons concerned of matters requiring attention and the operating status of plant equipment • Adhere to applicable safety and environmental standards, regulations and corporate commitments • Participate in internal and external continuous educational and professional development, including driver’s licensing upgrades as required by the position • Maintain a clean and orderly work area • Utilize SaskPower’s computer operating environment as required by the position • Direct, coach, train, orient and work effectively with other staff • Other related duties, as assigned

KNOWLEDGE/SKILLS/ABILITIES:

• Fourth Class Power Engineer’s Certificate and valid Fourth-Class License, or equivalent

• Possess the ability to make and implement sound decisions under emergent conditions

• Must be able to perform the physical requirements of the position

• Ability to cooperate and deal tactfully with others

• Ability to communicate effectively, both verbally and in writing

• Ability to update and maintain applicable licences, as required

• Valid Class 5 driver’s licence

This position falls within the scope of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW), Local 2067.
