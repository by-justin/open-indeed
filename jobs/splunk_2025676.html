Job Description

Meet the Team

We’re a team in Developer Observability, focused on giving engineers the tools and insights they need to ship faster and smarter. Our work supports teams across the organization by improving decision-making and development workflows. We operate as a distributed team across the US and Poland.

Your Impact

We are looking for an Software Engineer to join our engineering team and help design, build, and maintain high-quality, scalable systems and data pipelines. You will play a key role in setting engineering standards and driving best practices across the team.

What You’ll Get to Do

  • Design, build, and maintain Python applications, services, engineering tools, and data pipelines .
  • Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested Python code , following software engineering best practices.
  • Work with APIs, data processing, automation, and integrations to solve real engineering problems.
  • Debug and troubleshoot issues, improve existing code, and participate in code reviews and technical discussions .
  • Work with technologies such as Airflow, Snowflake, dbt, AWS, CI/CD, and containerized environments ( with opportunities to learn them on the job)
  • Use AI-assisted development and testing tools to improve coding, debugging, automation, and engineering productivity.

What You’ll Bring

  • 2+ years of hands-on software development experience , with Python as one of your primary programming languages.
  • Good knowledge of Python fundamentals , including data structures, functions, modules, classes, OOP, exceptions, and package management.
  • Ability to write readable, maintainable, and reusable code , rather than only scripts that solve a single problem.
  • Experience working with REST APIs, JSON, and external integrations.
  • Understanding of testing and debugging, with experience writing automated tests.
  • Good understanding of Git and modern software development workflows.
  • Good understanding of SQL and databases.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to break larger problems into smaller, manageable pieces.
  • A strong learning mindset and willingness to work with technologies you may not have used before.
  • Ability to ask questions, seek feedback, learn from code reviews, and apply that feedback to future work.

Nice to Have

  • Experience building dashboards or data visualizations using tools such as Splunk, Grafana, Tableau, or similar.
  • Experience with Airflow, Snowflake, dbt, or other data engineering technologies.
  • Experience with AWS or another cloud platform.
  • Experience building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
  • Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Familiarity with Terraform or infrastructure as code.
  • Familiarity with observability tools such as Splunk, Prometheus.
  • Experience in developer productivity engineering, including improving engineering tooling and workflows and supporting the full software development lifecycle (SDLC).
