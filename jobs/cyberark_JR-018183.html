Job Summary

As a Senior DevOps Engineer within the Saturn group, you will manage our cloud security platform's infrastructure through advanced automation, robust CI/CD pipelines, and proactive security-as-code. This is a highly integrated role where you will balance core infrastructure management with active cross-functional collaboration alongside software engineering, product, and security peers. You will drive high availability through sophisticated monitoring, optimize cloud spend via FinOps tracking, and act as a first responder to systemic environment issues. Beyond traditional operations, you will directly contribute to our product’s capabilities, partnering with your team to ensure secure, high-scale environments and maintain shared platform stability.

Key Responsibilities

  • CI/CD Pipeline Ownership: Develop, optimize, and maintain our end-to-end CI/CD processes to ensure seamless, secure, and rapid code deployment.
  • Production Monitoring & Reliability: Design, implement, and maintain comprehensive production monitoring tools and observability processes to guarantee service uptime and peak system performance.
  • FinOps & Cost Optimization: Develop our FinOps tracking processes, actively monitoring, analyzing, and optimizing cloud infrastructure expenditures.
  • Incident Response: Serve as a foundational first responder to systemic environment and infrastructure issues, ensuring swift mitigation and root-cause resolution.
  • Product Capability Contribution: Collaborate directly with engineering teams to contribute to the product's actual capabilities, leveraging your infrastructure expertise to enhance the end-user experience.
  • Infrastructure Lifecycle: Lead infrastructure initiatives from conceptualization to integration, ensuring technical decisions align with long-term organizational goals.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Experience: Bachelor’s degree with 5+ years of experience, or a Master’s degree with 3+ years of experience in a DevOps/Site Reliability Engineering role.
  • Cloud Expertise: Solid, hands-on experience building and managing cloud infrastructure within AWS.
  • Software Engineering: Strong programming proficiency in Python for automation, tooling, and infrastructure scripting.
  • Agentic Coding: Experience with agentic coding methodologies or leveraging AI-driven development workflows and tools to accelerate automation and engineering tasks.
  • CI/CD Mastery: Proven expertise designing and maintaining CI/CD workflows using GitHub and Jenkins.
  • Core Fundamentals: Solid proficiency in Linux administration, internals, and complex network troubleshooting.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Advanced Observability: Deep experience implementing and scaling DataDog for granular system performance, logging, and alerting.
  • Container Management: Practical experience building, troubleshooting, and optimizing containerized applications (Docker) within production environments.
  • FinOps Experience: Demonstrated tracking and optimization of large-scale AWS environments using cost-management tools like Umbrella.
  • High-Scale Architectures: Proven experience building high-scale, multi-service production architectures and managing database infrastructure (e.g., PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or ElasticSearch).
  • Mindset: An exceptionally collaborative approach, a strong commitment to task execution, and the ability to adapt quickly as a self-learner in a fast-paced environment.
