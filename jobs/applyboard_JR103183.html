Job Description

ApplyBoard simplifies the study abroad application process by connecting international students, international student advisors, and educational institutions on one personalized platform. For more than a decade, ApplyBoard's team members have been dedicated to breaking down barriers to international education, while thriving at a company that invests in their career growth. Our six core values guide us in our mission to educate the world, and are the foundation of our company culture. As an organization built on diversity, our team members are representative of the students we support.

The Opportunity:

The Business Development Manager will provide independent contractor services to support ApplyBoard’s B2B recruitment partner development, market engagement, partner enablement, and related commercial operations in the applicable territory. The Contractor will support ApplyBoard’s mission by helping recruitment partners better understand, promote, and use ApplyBoard’s platform, services, processes, and partner standards. The engagement is intended to support recruitment partner growth and operational effectiveness, and does not create an employment relationship, agency relationship, or authority to bind ApplyBoard.

The Contractor will provide the services described below, together with any incidental, ancillary, or reasonably related services that are necessary to support the effective performance of the engagement, as may be mutually discussed with or reasonably communicated by ApplyBoard from time to time.

What you’ll be doing every day:

The Contractor will be responsible for providing business development, partner management, and operational support services, including the following:

  • Represent ApplyBoard’s mission, services, platform capabilities, partner standards, and commercial objectives to recruitment partners, education agents, institutional contacts, and other relevant B2B stakeholders in a professional and accurate manner.
  • Develop and maintain strong working relationships with existing and prospective recruitment partners, with a focus on improving partner engagement, platform adoption, application quality, and commercial performance.
  • Support the identification, onboarding, activation, training, and ongoing enablement of recruitment partners, including by explaining ApplyBoard’s processes, platform usage, documentation expectations, quality standards, and compliance requirements.
  • Provide guidance and training to recruitment partners to improve the quality, completeness, and volume of student applications submitted through ApplyBoard’s platform, while ensuring that such guidance remains aligned with ApplyBoard’s approved processes, standards, and B2B operating model.
  • Support market development activities, including partner outreach, market research, territory analysis, competitor awareness, event participation, and preparation of market insights that may assist ApplyBoard in strengthening its local presence and recruitment partner network.
  • Develop and implement practical business development initiatives to increase recruitment partner engagement, improve conversion opportunities, and support ApplyBoard’s broader international education objectives.
  • Use ApplyBoard-approved systems, CRM tools, communication channels, content, scripts, and materials to manage partner interactions, maintain accurate records, share relevant updates, and support business development activities.
  • Coordinate with ApplyBoard teams on partner-related matters, including commercial updates, operational issues, application quality concerns, compliance escalations, partner training needs, and market feedback.
  • Support compliance-related activities connected to recruitment partner management, including assisting with partner due diligence, documentation follow-ups, code of conduct awareness, training completion, issue escalation, and monitoring of partner conduct where relevant.
  • Promptly escalate to ApplyBoard any suspected misconduct, misrepresentation, mis-selling, unauthorized counselling, improper partner conduct, data protection concern, regulatory inquiry, complaint, or any issue that may create legal, compliance, operational, financial, or reputational risk for ApplyBoard.
  • Attend meetings, events, partner visits, institutional discussions, regulatory or authority-facing meetings, and other business engagements where reasonably required for the performance of the services and where authorized by ApplyBoard.
  • Support ApplyBoard in preparing partner-facing updates, presentations, training materials, market notes, reports, and other business documents reasonably connected to the services.
  • Ensure that all external communications are accurate, professional, consistent with ApplyBoard-approved messaging, and do not include any unauthorized commitments, guarantees, representations, or statements on behalf of ApplyBoard.
  • Provide periodic updates, reports, pipeline information, market feedback, and performance-related information as reasonably required by ApplyBoard for business planning, partner management, compliance, and operational purposes.
  • Perform any other services that are reasonably incidental or related to the above business development, recruitment partner management, compliance support, and market engagement activities, provided such services are within the scope of the independent contractor engagement and are not inconsistent with the contractor’s status.

What You Bring to the Table:

  • 2-3 years managing recruitment or enterprise accounts, ideally within the international education sector.
  • Previous B2B experience preferred
  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., Business, Marketing, Education) is preferred.
  • Fluent in English; additional language skills are an asset.
  • Ability to engage and motivate diverse stakeholders, from students to educational agents, with clear and persuasive communication.
  • A self-starter who thrives in a collaborative, team-based environment and works effectively independently.
  • Excellent organizational skills with a talent for managing priorities and ensuring accuracy in information and communications.
  • Resourceful problem-solver capable of navigating complex situations to deliver positive outcomes for students and partners.
  • Proficient in CRM systems; experience with social media and other digital engagement tools is an advantage.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
