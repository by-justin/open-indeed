职位概述

我们正在寻找一位积极热情、充满动力的初级SAP EWM（公有云）顾问加入我们的团队。在这个职位上，您将学习如何优化并支持在SAP S/4HANA公有云环境中实施SAP扩展仓库管理（EWM）解决方案。在经验丰富的团队成员指导下，您将参与部署博世标准化云EWM模板的项目，助力提升仓库运营效率，并确保SAP系统间的无缝集成。

Role Overview

We are looking for a motivated and enthusiastic Junior SAP EWM Consultant (Public Cloud) to join our team. In this role, you will learn to optimize and support the implementation of SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) solutions within the SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud environment. With guidance from experienced team members, you will contribute to projects aimed at deploying Bosch's standardized Cloud EWM template, helping to enhance warehouse operations and ensure seamless integration across SAP systems.

主要职责

协作： 在指导下参与研讨会，协助收集业务需求，并参与将需求转化为技术解决方案的过程。

配置、测试与支持： 遵循既定指南，协助完成SAP公有云EWM模板的配置、定制和部署工作；参与测试活动（单元测试、集成测试、用户验收测试），帮助识别和解决问题，并在上线后的强化支持及培训期间提供协助。

流程优化： 学习并运用SAP EWM最佳实践，支持仓库流程的改进；积极与全球团队协作，协助维护和完善云EWM全球模板。

Key Responsibilities

  • Collaboration: Participate in workshops to assist in gathering business needs and contribute to the translation of requirements into technical solutions under guidance.
  • Configration & Testing & Support: Assist in the configuration, customization, and deployment of the SAP Public Cloud EWM template for internal clients, following established guidelines. Participate in testing activities (unit, integration, UAT), help identify and resolve defects, and provide support during post-go-live hypercare and training.
  • Process Optimization: Learn and support the enhancement of warehouse processes by applying SAP EWM best practices. Actively collaborate within our global teams to help maintain and improve the Cloud EWM global template.

Qualifications

任职要求

学历： 计算机科学、供应链管理或相关专业本科学历。

经验： 相关专业的应届本科或硕士毕业生，或参加过公认的SAP培训/认证项目。对SAP S/4HANA公有云有浓厚兴趣；有与SAP或物流相关的学术项目或实习经验者优先，但非必需。

软技能： 良好的分析和解决问题能力，较强的沟通技巧，渴望在协作的团队环境中学习。英语口语流利者优先。

Required Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Supply Chain, or related field.
  • Experience: Recent graduate (Bachelor’s or Master’s degree) in a relevant field, or participation in a recognized SAP training/certification program. A strong interest in SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud; prior academic projects or internship experience related to SAP or logistics is beneficial but not mandatory.
  • Soft Skills: Good analytical and problem-solving abilities. Strong communication skills and eagerness to learn in a collaborative team setting. Fluent English speaking is a plus.
