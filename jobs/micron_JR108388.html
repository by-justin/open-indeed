Engineer for Developing Metrology Processes

Position Summary

Engineer for Metrology Process Development

The Metrology Process Development Engineer is responsible for developing, evaluating, and deploying innovative semiconductor metrology solutions to support next-generation DRAM and HBM technologies. This role collaborates closely with Process Integration, Manufacturing, Yield Improvement, and equipment suppliers to establish reliable metrology strategies, enhance process control, and speed up technology development for Micron's groundbreaking memory products.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and qualify new metrology methodologies for advanced DRAM/HBM technology nodes.
  • Define process control strategies and establish monitoring plans for critical process modules.
  • Evaluate and introduce new metrology tools and measurement capabilities.
  • Analyze experimental and production data to improve measurement accuracy, matching performance, and manufacturing efficiency.
  • Work together with cross-functional groups such as Process Integration, Manufacturing, Yield, and Equipment Engineering.
  • Drive supplier engagement and technical reviews with metrology vendors.
  • Support capital planning, tool justification, installation, qualification, and ramp activities.
  • Lead problem-solving activities related to process variation and metrology performance.
  • AI-enabled ways of working

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in cross-functional and global teams.
  • Good communication skills in English.
  • AI proficiency

Preferred Qualifications

  • Semiconductor process or metrology experience.
  • Knowledge of XRD, SIMS, OCD, Scatterometry, CD-SEM, Film Metrology, or related techniques.
  • Experience with statistical data analysis and process control methodologies.
  • Experience working with advanced memory technologies (DRAM, HBM, NAND).

Reasons to Join Micron

  • Opportunity to contribute to groundbreaking memory technologies.
  • Collaboration with global technology teams.
  • Exposure to advanced semiconductor process development and metrology innovation.
  • Career growth in a highly technical and dynamic environment.
