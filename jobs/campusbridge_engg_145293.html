Who we are

Clean water and reliable energy are at the heart of what we do. We proudly keep the lights on and the water flowing in over 100 communities across North America. With a rich 125-year history, EPCOR has ambitions to be one of North America's premier providers of essential services, and we have the top employer and sustainability awards to prove it. If you're looking for new opportunities, people who care and work that matters, apply today to find a career where you matter more. Check out epcor.com/careers to find out more.

Electricity Operations is responsible for the electrical transmission and distribution systems in the City of Edmonton. Our employees look after electrical infrastructure and the underground and aerial distribution networks.

The Grid Transformation team helps prepare for the future demands of the electric grid. As electrification increases and technologies such as electric vehicles, battery energy storage, solar generation, and Virtual Power Plants (VPPs) become more common, the team evaluates emerging technologies, develops innovative solutions, and supports strategic investment decisions for short-, medium-, and long-term solutions to grid transformation challenges.

As a co-op student, you will contribute to research, analysis, and pilot projects that support EPCOR's distribution and transmission decision-making. You will gain exposure to technology evaluation, investment planning, project execution, scheduling, and insights into the utility regulatory landscape. Working alongside engineers, system planners, researchers, technology vendors, and utility partners, you will help assess new opportunities, support decision-making, and participate in collaborative projects that advance grid modernization. This role offers hands-on experience with industry-leading tools and technologies, including ADMS (Advanced Distribution Management System), DERMS (Distributed Energy Resouce Management System), data analytics, and power system modeling, while providing valuable insight into the future of the electric utility industry.

What you'd be responsible for

The Engineering Co-op Student is accountable to the Manager, Grid Transformation, and operates within the context and prescribed limits established by the Manager, Grid Transformation. The main accountabilities of the role this term include, but are not limited to:

  • Researching battery energy storage, EV chargers, demand-side management, and virtual power plant (VPP) management technologies, techniques, and optimization approaches;
  • Conducting data analysis, and developing studies, project proposals and business cases to address challenges related to:Distributed Energy Resources (DER) uptake and management; Electric vehicle penetration and its impact on various levels of the power grid;Energy storage;
  • Applying and developing project management principles through planning, coordination, documentation, and execution of grid transformation initiatives
  • Supporting the engineering and deployment of pilot projects related to grid transformation.

To be successful in this competition, the student must submit a resume and demonstrate the following:

• Currently enrolled in an Electrical or Computer Engineering co-op degree program.

• Possess a keen attention to detail, and are able to conduct research and analysis with a high degree of accuracy.

• Effective verbal and written communication skills and the ability to maintain strong working relationships and collaborate with others.

• Proficient in the use of Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook), PC operating system, computer programming and data base query tools.

• Ability to take direction from and provide technical and project support to various EPCOR teams.

The following will be considered an asset (or a learning opportunity):

• Familiarity with electric vehicle and solar PV technology;

• Azure cloud tools and services, e.g., Azure Databricks;

• Python;
