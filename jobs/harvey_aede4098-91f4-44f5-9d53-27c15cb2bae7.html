Role Overview

Harvey designs and builds for a legal industry being reshaped by AI, moving with the urgency and ambition the moment demands, not just adapting to change but architecting it. As a Design Systems Designer at Harvey, you will define the foundations that make our product coherent, scalable, and trustworthy as it grows in complexity. The design system is not just a library of components. It is the shared language between design, engineering, and AI that ensures consistency, quality, and confidence across every interaction.

This is a design role with unusual technical depth. You lead with design: craft, systems thinking, and a strong point of view on product experience. But you're equally at home in architecture and implementation. You understand how tokens are structured, how components are composed, and how systems are built to scale, and you use that fluency to push the system forward rather than hand decisions over the wall.

We're looking for someone who has lived through the evolution of the discipline, someone who understands not just how modern design systems work, but why they're built the way they are, and where the field is heading, especially as AI-native products reshape what a design system needs to be.

This role will report to the Design org, and will be based in SF, NYC, or Toronto.

  • Own AI design and implementation workflows for the design team, establishing best practices for prototyping and shipping with AIEstablish in-code prototyping patterns that make it easier to be innovative and consistent as designers increasingly leverage IDEs to experiment and ship.Define AI-assisted design and implementation workflows, establishing best practices for prototyping and shipping with AIDocument system usage and best practices in a way that is clear, practical, and trusted by product teams

What you have

  • 5+ years (Senior) / 8+ years (Staff) of product design experience, with significant ownership of design systems in complex product environments
  • A design portfolio that demonstrates high design craft in system-level thinking, but a strong point of view on quality, and visual cohesion
  • Deep design systems expertise, including an understanding of the history and evolution of the discipline. You know why the field moved from style guides to tokens to composable architectures, and you have a point of view on what comes next
  • Excellent design engineering communication: you articulate system decisions and tradeoffs fluently to designers, engineers, and cross-functional partners
  • Ability to clearly articulate system decisions and tradeoffs to designers, engineers, and cross-functional partners
  • Strong knowledge of scalable system architecture and component implementation: component API design, design tokens, theming strategies, and how systems hold up across teams and codebases
  • Design Engineer experience preferred, but not required in an AI-first world where the boundary between design and engineering is shifting
  • High ownership: you treat the system as a product, not a ticket queue
  • Strong opinions, loosely held, being comfortable challenging conventions and evolving best practices as the discipline changes
  • Comfort operating in ambiguous problem spaces and bringing structure where it doesn't yet exist

Why design systems matter at Harvey

Harvey is an AI-native product. As the system reasons, responds, and adapts in real time, the interface must remain consistent and legible. A strong design system ensures that complexity does not leak into the experience. It enables teams to move faster, reduces cognitive load for users, and builds trust in moments where clarity matters most.
