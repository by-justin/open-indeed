Industry Liaison and Industrial & Technological Benefits (ITB) Manager

The Industry Liaison and ITB Manager is responsible for developing, implementing and managing Top Aces' Industrial and Technological Benefits (ITB) strategy and industrial participation activities in support of major Canadian defense and security programs. The position serves as the primary interface between Top Aces, Canadian industry partners, Indigenous organizations, academia, government stakeholders, and key subcontractors to maximize Canadian industrial participation and ensure compliance with contractual ITB obligations.

Working closely with Business Development, Program Management, Legal, Supply Chain, Finance and external partners, the Industry Liaison and ITB Manager will identify, develop and manage strategic industrial partnerships that strengthen Top Aces' competitive position while supporting Canada's defense industrial priorities.

Key Responsibilities

Industrial and Technological Benefits (ITB)

  • Develop and execute ITB strategies for major defense procurements.

  • Support the development of ITB Value Propositions during capture and proposal phases.

  • Identify, qualify and manage ITB-eligible transactions and investments.

  • Develop and maintain ITB credit forecasts, achievement projections and risk assessments, including identification of potential shortfalls and corrective actions.

  • Establish internal processes, controls and governance practices to track, validate and document ITB activities.

  • Coordinate preparation and submission of ITB plans, reports, compliance documentation and supporting records.

  • Maintain auditable records supporting all ITB claims and credits.

  • Liaise with government regarding ITB requirements and act as the focal point for ITB audits, verification activities and compliance reviews.

  • Monitor evolving Canadian industrial policy, defense industrial priorities and ITB program guidance.

Industry Engagement and Partnership Development

  • Serve as Top Aces' primary industry liaison for strategic Canadian industrial partnerships.

  • Develop relationships with SMEs, Indigenous businesses, defense contractors, technology firms, academic institutions and research organizations.

  • Identify opportunities for collaborative development, workshare arrangements and technology partnerships.

  • Facilitate industrial engagement activities, industry days and partner workshops.

  • Negotiate and support teaming agreements, MOUs and partnership frameworks.

  • Maintain a database of qualified Canadian suppliers and strategic industry partners.

Indigenous Engagement

  • Support development and implementation of Indigenous participation strategies.

  • Engage Indigenous businesses and organizations to identify partnership opportunities.

  • Work closely with Indigenous Partner Managers and program teams to maximize Indigenous economic participation.

  • Assist in tracking and reporting Indigenous participation commitments, outcomes and supporting documentation.

Capture and Proposal Support

  • Contribute directly to bid strategies for major Canadian defense programs.

  • Lead industry participation and ITB content development for proposals.

  • Support competitive assessments of industrial participation approaches.

  • Coordinate partner commitments, workshare agreements and letters of support.

  • Develop industrial participation roadmaps and execution plans.

Program Execution

  • Ensure industrial participation commitments are incorporated into program execution plans.

  • Coordinate across functional teams to align subcontracting, supplier development and industrial participation activities.

  • Support development and negotiation of contractual provisions necessary to capture, document and validate ITB-eligible activities.

  • Track, report and present achievement of contractual obligations and key performance indicators.

  • Develop and maintain performance metrics related to ITB commitments and industrial participation objectives.

  • Identify risks to ITB performance and develop mitigation strategies.

Education and/or Work Experience

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, Economics, Public Policy, Procurement, International Business or a related discipline.

  • Master's degree or MBA considered an asset.

Experience

  • Direct experience with Canada's Industrial and Technological Benefits Policy.

  • Experience developing or managing industrial participation, offsets, supply chain or partnership programs.

  • Experience supporting major government procurements and proposal efforts.

  • Knowledge of Canadian defense procurement processes and stakeholders.

  • Experience with ITB credit modelling, reporting, audit support or compliance documentation considered an asset.

  • Ability to perform all work in support of our Corporate Values of Excellence, Service, Integrity … Together.

  • Excellent relationship-building and stakeholder engagement skills.

Assets
