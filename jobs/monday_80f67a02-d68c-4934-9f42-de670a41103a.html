About the role:

We are looking for a highly technical R&D Technical Support (RTS) professional to join the team and act as a technical bridge between Customer Experience (CX), Technical Support, Product, and R&D.

The RTS role is essentially Tier 3 technical support, but with a strong focus on deep investigation, troubleshooting, product understanding, and driving issues toward long-term solutions.

This is not a role focused simply on communicating customer issues to R&D.

We are looking for someone who can investigate problems independently, identify root causes, influence prioritization, and increasingly resolve issues themselves through AI-assisted investigation and code contributions.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Own complex technical escalations end-to-end, from Zendesk through investigation to R&D/Development.
  • Investigate complex product issues using logs, metrics, system data, and other technical tools to find root causes.
  • Use AI Agents and AI-powered tools to accelerate investigations, identify bugs, analyze code, and increasingly create PRs to resolve issues directly.
  • Take ownership of bugs rather than just reporting them — investigate root cause and drive resolution.
  • Partner with Product Managers to understand product behavior, requirements, and new features.
  • Help prioritize bugs by customer impact, technical severity, and business considerations.
  • Identify recurring issues and drive long-term product or technical improvements, not just individual fixes.
  • Feed technical and product insights back into CX and Technical Support, and educate them on new product behavior and investigation techniques so issues get resolved earlier.
  • Help improve the escalation and troubleshooting process between Support and R&D.
  • Act as a technical stakeholder and partner to engineering/product leadership on complex issues.

Your Experience & Skills:

  • +5 years of experience in technical support or similar roles (SaaS/B2B preferred).
  • Strong technical background with excellent troubleshooting and debugging skills; comfortable investigating complex systems via logs, metrics, data, and code.
  • Ability to break down ambiguous technical problems systematically and learn new technologies quickly.
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools/Agents for technical investigation, debugging, or development — not just for information retrieval.
  • Product mindset: goes beyond "what's the bug" to "why is this happening and how do we prevent it," with strong judgment on prioritization and trade-offs.
  • Experience in a leadership, technical leadership, or highly cross-functional role; comfortable working with senior stakeholders (Group Leads, PMs, R&D leadership) and influencing without direct authority.
  • Strong communicator, able to translate between technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • End-to-end ownership mentality — proactively drives investigations and solutions rather than stopping at identifying the issue, comfortable in ambiguity.
