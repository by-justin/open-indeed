At Airbus in Belfast, we are pioneering the future of sustainable aerospace. We are looking for an experienced Lead Design Engineer to provide technical guidance and leadership across a broad spectrum of design engineering activities and ongoing design support of the manufacturing build.

In this role, you will lead specialist technical packages within our Structures and/or Systems Installation teams, ensuring full compliance with Design Authority requirements and airworthiness standards. You will bridge the gap between initial design, feasibility, and shop-floor manufacturing to deliver world-class aerospace components

HOW YOU WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THE TEAM:

  • Provide technical leadership, mentorship, and advice to an engineering team, developing innovative approaches to complex design problems and fostering continuous development.
  • Take full accountability for assigned projects or programmes, ensuring all technical commitments are delivered strictly within agreed schedules and budgets.
  • Maintain deep design engineering expertise to advise user departments across the design-to-manufacturing lifecycle, ensuring all components satisfy customer specifications and airworthiness regulatory standards.
  • Work closely with cross-functional specialists, customers, and suppliers to effectively coordinate complex engineering packages and conduct full project feasibility studies.

ABOUT YOU:

To be considered for shortlisting, candidates must clearly demonstrate the following requirements in their application:

Essential criteria:

  • Degree (BSc/BEng or equivalent) in Mechanical, Aerospace, or Manufacturing Engineering with 3+ years’ design engineering experience, OR an HNC/HND in a relevant discipline with 5+ years’ design engineering experience.
  • Comprehensive theoretical and applied knowledge of aircraft structures and/or systems installation, alongside thorough working knowledge of applicable airworthiness regulations (e.g., CAA, EASA, FAA), analysis methods, testing procedures, and engineering processes. Proven track record of managing, guiding, and advising an engineering team on specialist technical packages, with the demonstrated ability to lead, motivate, and mentor staff through project updates and organizational change. Proven experience liaising directly with senior management, external customers, key suppliers, and Certification
  • Authorities, supported by strong organizational and presentation skills for cross-functional project reporting.
  • While applicants only need to meet the essential criteria, relevant desirable experience within your specific discipline may be used to shortlist candidates.

Desirable criteria:

ABOUT US

Join Airbus Belfast - Pioneering Sustainable Aerospace for a Safe and United World

As a premier centre of excellence for advanced aerostructures, our Belfast team drives the complete wing lifecycle - from concept design to in-service support - for the world’s leading aerospace manufacturer.  To support major multi-million-pound site investments and expanding operations, we are recruiting talented engineers to scale world-class aerospace innovation into the future.

What We Do

  • Global A220 Wing Nerve Center: Our specialist engineering teams drive the continuous design and technical development of the advanced composite A220 wing. With over 1,100 firm orders from 30+ global customers and 500+ aircraft flying across 25 international operators, Belfast is accelerating production ramp-ups to meet record global demand. By leveraging cutting-edge digital tools, advanced composite materials and automated manufacturing processes, our engineers are actively advancing performance, passenger experience and structural capabilities for the world's most popular single-aisle aircraft in its class.
  • Iconic Commercial Programmes: Our expertise spans the entire Airbus commercial family, delivering critical innovations from the record-breaking A350-1000 fuel tank integration that enabled the longest flight in history, to the primary structural backbone for the A350F- the world’s most eco-efficient cargo aircraft.

Shape the Future of Flight To fuel our long-term growth, we are expanding our engineering team in Belfast and seeking specialists across key disciplines, including structural design, stress analysis, aircraft integration and materials & processes. If you are ready to work with cutting-edge technologies and define the next generation of aerospace, apply today . In Belfast, we make it fly!

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
