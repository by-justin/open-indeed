Your future duties and responsibilities:

Guide and coach teams in Agile principles to achieve high performance

FacilitateAgile ceremonies (Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Planning, Reviews, Retrospectives) and ensure effective communication and collaboration in the team, with other teams as well as customers and stakeholders

Foster Continuous Team Improvement by identifying inefficiencies and drive process optimization

Keep an eye on the psychological safety of your teams, manage team dependencies and risks, ensuring timely mitigation and clear communication

Improve team processes and ensure alignment with organizational and Agile guidelines

Collaborate and exchange with other Scrum Masters to work on a continuous improvement of the whole project

Coaching POs in product backlog management and prioritization techniques

Required qualifications to be successful in this role:

At least 3 years of recent experience in agile development projects within a larger multi-team ecosystem and complex stakeholder environments

Proven track record (with references) of leading and coaching Agile delivery teams in complex environments, applying Scrum and/or Kanban

Experienced in facilitating workshops and team sessions (Sprint events, retrospectives, cross-team alignment), using tools such as Miro or Mural to support collaboration

Ability to foster collaboration and maintain transparency across and between teams

Strong problem-solving and communication skills

Pragmatist with a strong focus on everyday impact

Can give support during change, uncertainty and transitions

Solid Agile fundamentals with a hands-on mentality rather than a dogmatic approach

Scrum Master certification (e.g., CSM, PSM) is highly desirable.

Teams Facilitation and Coaching skills to monitor team maturity and drive continuous improvement

Knowledge of scaled agility (SAFe, LeSS)

Fluency in English

Nice to have

Expertise in Jira or Azure DevOps

Knowledge of Project Management Tools, Data Analysis, Metrics Tracking and Reporting

Stakeholder Management

Knowledge of DevOps practices

User Experience (UX) Design knowledge

Experience in dependency & risk management and stakeholder alignment

Cloud technologies and platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
