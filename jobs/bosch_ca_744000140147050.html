Job Description

職務概要

ブレーキシステム開発プロジェクトにおいて、技術面のプロジェクトマネジメントを担当するポジションです。

顧客要求の理解・分析から、設計、ソフトウェア開発、テスト、品質、開発プロセスまでを見渡し、プロジェクトマネージャー、アプリケーションチーム、ソフトウェア開発チームなどと連携しながら、開発を推進します。

また、顧客との技術的な調整や、社内外の関係者とのコミュニケーションを通じて、顧客の要望を具体的な開発成果物に落とし込む役割を担います。

このポジションで得られる価値（魅力）

  • 海外拠点を含む社内外の関係者と連携しながら、ブレーキシステム開発を推進します。複数の拠点・専門領域をつなぎながら、プロジェクトを前に進める経験を積むことができます。
  • 要求解析、設計、ソフトウェア開発、テスト、品質、プロセスなど、開発全体を見渡しながら、裁量を持って技術面のプロジェクト推進にかかわることができます。
  • 車両の安全性に直結するブレーキシステムに関わり、システム・ソフトウェアの役割を車両レベルで理解しながら専門性を高めることができます。
  • 顧客要求を正しく理解し、社内外の開発チームと連携して成果物に反映していく、プロジェクト上重要な役割を担います。

Position Overview

This position is responsible for technical project management in brake system development projects.

The role covers a broad range of technical areas, from understanding and analyzing customer requirements to design, software development, testing, quality, and development processes. You will work closely with project managers, application teams, software development teams, and other stakeholders to drive development activities.

You will also play an important role in translating customer needs into concrete development deliverables through technical coordination with customers and communication with internal and external stakeholders.

What You Can Gain from This Position

  • You will gain experience in driving brake system development while collaborating with internal and external stakeholders, including overseas locations. This role provides the opportunity to move projects forward by connecting multiple locations and areas of expertise.
  • You will be involved in technical project execution with a high level of autonomy, while overseeing the overall development process, including requirements analysis, design, software development, testing, quality, and processes.
  • You will deepen your expertise in brake systems, which are directly linked to vehicle safety, while understanding the role of systems and software at the vehicle level.
  • You will play an important role in correctly understanding customer requirements and reflecting them in development deliverables in collaboration with internal and external development teams.

Main Responsibilities

Brake System Development Project Management

  • Manage the technical aspects of brake system development projects
  • Understand and analyze customer requirements, and manage progress and issues across requirements analysis, design, software development, testing, quality, and development processes
  • Identify and organize technical risks, changes, and issues, and coordinate with relevant stakeholders
  • Manage and coordinate development schedules
  • Drive development activities in collaboration with project managers, application teams/vehicle application engineers, and software development teams

Technical Coordination with Customers

  • Clarify technical topics related to brake system development and coordinate with customers
  • Understand customer requirements and requests, and communicate them to internal stakeholders
  • Coordinate internally and externally to reflect customer requirements in development plans and deliverables
  • Share progress, provide technical explanations, and follow up on issues with customers as needed

Component Development Coordination and Internal/External Collaboration

  • Drive development activities in collaboration with component development teams
  • Coordinate and align with internal and external stakeholders, including overseas locations
  • Promote development processes in cooperation with quality and process-related stakeholders
  • Organize issues, priorities, and action plans among stakeholders

Vehicle-Level System Understanding and Development Execution

  • Understand the role of the system and software being developed at the vehicle level
  • Understand the functions, performance, and quality required for brake systems
  • Ensure alignment between customer requirements and development deliverables
  • Drive the project from a technical perspective and ensure the quality of deliverables
