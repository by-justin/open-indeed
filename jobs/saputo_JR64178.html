Overview of The Role

The Power BI Developer is a business‑focused analytics role responsible for partnering closely with business teams to design, deliver and sustain insightful Power BI dashboards and reports. This role acts as a bridge between business users and the enterprise analytics platform, ensuring business questions are translated into clear, accurate, and performant analytical solutions.

The Power BI Developer plays a critical role in driving data‑driven decision‑making, enabling self‑service analytics, and providing hands‑on guidance and support to business users working with Power BI in a governed enterprise environment.

How You Will Make Contributions That Matter:

  • Partner directly with business stakeholders to understand business processes, KPIs, and decision needs, translating them into effective Power BI dashboards and reports
  • Design, build, and deliver Power BI reports and dashboards that translate business requirements into actionable insights
  • Engineer high‑quality semantic models (datasets) optimized for performance, reusability, and enterprise reporting
  • Own Power BI development standards, including data modeling, DAX patterns, visual design, and usability best practices
  • Partner with Data Engineering teams to consume curated Lakehouse and Fabric data assets
  • Develop optimized DAX measures and calculations to support complex analytical requirements
  • Enforce dimensional modeling best practices within Power BI semantic models
  • Own report performance tuning, including model optimization, query reduction, and visual efficiency
  • Validate data accuracy and consistency across reports, dashboards, and semantic models
  • Support and resolve production issues, including data discrepancies, refresh failures, and performance degradation
  • Drive user adoption by delivering intuitive, well‑designed analytical experiences
  • Document report logic, data definitions, and business rules to ensure transparency and reuse
  • Coach and support business users on Power BI usage, self‑service analytics, and best practices
  • Document data definitions, assumptions, and report logic in business‑friendly language

Supervision

  • Operates independently while aligning to enterprise BI strategy and standards
  • Works closely with Data Engineers, architects, platform admin, governance and business SMEs throughout the analytics lifecycle
  • Supports multiple concurrent reporting initiatives in a fast‑paced enterprise environment
  • Communicates clearly with technical and non‑technical stakeholders

You Are Best Suited for The Role If You Have the Following Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Analytics, or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years of hands‑on experience developing enterprise Power BI solutions
  • Strong experience with Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, with a solid understanding of modern Azure analytics services and exposure to other enterprise analytics tools in the market
  • Strong foundation in:Data modeling and semantic layer designAnalytical reporting and dashboardingBusiness intelligence and data visualization
  • Expertise in advance modeling techniques using tabular editor and DAX studio
  • Experience in maintaining language translations – Meta data translation and data translation
  • Experience working in governed enterprise BI environments
  • Experience working with SAP data and business processes (nice to have)
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work across multiple business domains and stakeholders
  • Up‑to‑date knowledge of Power BI features, performance optimization techniques, and industry best practices

Technical Requirements

  • Advanced Power BI development experience, including:Semantic model (dataset) engineeringAdvanced DAX development and optimizationM Query for data transformationReport and dashboard design following UX best practices
  • Strong understanding of dimensional data modeling (Kimball / star schema)
  • Strong SQL skills for data validation and analysis
  • Experience working with Microsoft Fabric components:Fabric Lakehouse (as a consumer)Fabric SQL endpointsFabric Dataflows / Data Factory (consumption and understanding)
  • Experience with Power BI Service administration:Workspace managementSecurity (RLS/CLS, object‑level security)Capacity awareness and optimization
  • Familiarity with DevOps / GitHub for version control and deployment
  • Understanding of data governance, security, and lifecycle management in Power BI
