Job Description

Generic:

  • The Digital Shopfloor Expert serves as the on-site Digital representative for the factory, responsible for supporting and driving all Digital activities with a strong focus on manufacturing operations, shopfloor systems, and OT/IT convergence . As the site's Digital Security Expert, they ensure the security of OT/IT systems, identify and resolve potential cybersecurity risks in the manufacturing environment. They also act as a comprehensive Digital risk expert, evaluating risks related to GxP-regulated computerized systems and providing risk management recommendations.
  • The Digital Shopfloor Expert leads the implementation of MES, IIoT, automation, and connectivity solutions on the shopfloor, supporting Hangzhou's ambition as a Connectivity & Lighthouse pilot site . They collaborate closely with Engineering, Automation, Quality, and Production teams, providing training and guidance to ensure technical capabilities align with business needs. By driving Digital innovation on the shopfloor, they contribute to smart, green, and agile manufacturing operations.
