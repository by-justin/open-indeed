Purpose of this staffing process :

The pool that will be created will be used to staff full-time SP-04 Call Centre customer service positions at the Quebec Regional Contact Centre (QRCC) in Montreal. Hiring is anticipated for late 2026; however, the exact timing may vary depending on operational requirements.

What to expect when applying for this position?

Among other things, you will be called upon to work together with taxpayers, your colleagues and partners, in an innovative and stimulating climate, within a dynamic team.

These tasks require several skills:

  • Excellent customer service, oral and written communication skills
  • Judgment
  • Strong organizational and priority management skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Results-oriented attitude

You think you are the right candidate? Then join us!

Why work at the CRA? In addition to competitive salaries starting at $33.41 per hour for SP-04, we offer some of the most sought-after benefits:

  • Flexible leave for work-life balance.
  • A health care system that covers multiple life events such as medical expenses, prescription drugs, vision care, dental care, out-of-province medical expenses and hospitalizations, etc.
  • An advantageous pension, employee assistance program (EAP) and more.
  • Ongoing training to reach your full potential.
  • Career opportunities supported by a competent management team.
  • A workplace that supports a diverse workforce and talent development.

Before submitting your application, please ensure that the following conditions apply to you:

  • Mandatory full-time daytime training for several weeks , provided upon hiring (paid and supervised training);
  • Required on-site presence at a CRA-approved location, in accordance with current workplace arrangements;
  • Variable shift schedules , primarily Monday to Friday between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m ., based on operational requirements. Please consider these hours before applying, as your preferred shift may not be available;
  • Required to wear a headset, answer calls, and work at a computer for extended periods during each shift;
  • Temporary term position with the possibility of extension;
  • Access to a high-speed Internet connection is required to support telework arrangements.

Work arrangements, including remote work, may change following the training period, depending on operational requirements.

  • Respond to a variety of enquiries or requests related to tax matters, CRA administered programs and related accounts.
  • Perform various calculations to determine tax or CRA administered program requirements, elections, entitlements, or administrative relief.
  • Accept payment arrangements where applicable and authorized, within established parameters.
  • Educate taxpayers by providing information and promoting available services.

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.
