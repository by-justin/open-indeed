ポジション概要

Autodeskでは、建設・建築業界におけるデジタルトランスフォーメーションの推進に情熱を持つプロフェッショナルを募集しています。テクニカルサクセスマネージャー（Technical Success Manager：TSM）として、戦略的顧客がForma（旧 Autodesk Construction Cloud）から測定可能なビジネス価値を実現できるよう、スケーラブルなワークフロー、システム連携（インテグレーション）、データ活用の設計・導入を支援していただきます。

APAC カスタマーサクセス シニアマネージャーの直属として、Autodeskの主要な建築・エンジニアリング・建設（AEC）顧客に対する技術的な顧客支援をリードしていただきます。Customer Success Manager（CSM）と密接に連携しながら、顧客のビジネス戦略や目標を実践的なFormaソリューションへと落とし込み、プロジェクト遂行、コラボレーション、業務パフォーマンスの向上を支援します。

信頼できる技術アドバイザーとして、ヘルスチェック、ワークフロー設計、トレーニング、ダッシュボード構築、システム連携などを提供し、顧客がFormaを効果的に活用できるよう支援するとともに、Autodeskへの投資対効果（ROI）を最大化する役割を担います。

主な業務内容

  • Formaの機能、制約事項、およびシステム連携の可能性について深い知識を持つ専門家（Subject Matter Expert）として活動する

  • ドキュメント管理、RFI（情報照会）、サブミッタル、現場業務フロー、モデルコーディネーションなどのFormaワークフローを構築・最適化する
  • 顧客が利用状況、プロジェクトパフォーマンス、業務成果を可視化できるダッシュボード、レポート、分析基盤を設計する
  • 利用可能なAPIやデータコネクタを活用し、Formaとビジネスインテリジェンス（BI）ツール、およびその他の建設関連テクノロジーとの連携を支援する。
  • 技術ヘルスチェック、ワークショップ、トレーニングを実施し、プラットフォームの継続的かつスケーラブルな活用を促進する
  • Customer Success Managerと連携し、Customer Success Planで定義された施策を実行しながら、技術的成果物が顧客のビジネス目標達成に貢献することを確実にする
  • 顧客のビジネス課題や戦略をAutodeskのソリューションおよび業界のベストプラクティスに結び付け、定量的なROI創出を推進する
  • ベストプラクティス、競合情報、顧客成功事例をCustomer Success組織全体へ共有する
  • 顧客対応、社内ナレッジ共有、部門横断的な協業を通じて、業界知識やソートリーダーシップの発展に貢献する

Position Overview

Autodesk is seeking construction industry professionals who are passionate about advancing digital transformation across the built environment. As a Technical Success Manager (TSM), you will help strategic customers realize measurable business value from Forma (formerly Autodesk Construction Cloud) by designing and implementing scalable workflows, integrations, and data insights.

Reporting to the APAC Senior Manager, Customer Success, you will lead the technical components of customer engagements for some of Autodesk’s largest architecture, engineering and Construction (AEC) customers. Partnering closely with Customer Success Managers, you will translate customer business initiatives into practical Forma solutions that improve project delivery, collaboration, and operational performance.

As a trusted technical advisor, you will deliver health checks, workflow design, training, dashboards, and integrations that enable customers to adopt Forma effectively and maximize return on their Autodesk investment.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as a subject matter expert in Forma, including platform capabilities, limitations, and integration possibilities
  • Lead technical engagements with customers, analysing workflows and designing solutions that support improved project and construction processes
  • Configure and optimize Forma workflows including document management, RFIs, submittals, field workflows, and model coordination
  • Design dashboards, reports, and analytics that help customers track adoption, project performance, and operational outcomes
  • Support integrations between Forma and business intelligence tools, and other construction technologies using available APIs and data connectorsConduct technical health checks, workshops, and training sessions that enable scalable platform adoption
  • Partner with Customer Success Managers to execute initiatives defined in the Customer Success Plan, ensuring technical deliverables support customer business goals
  • Map customer business initiatives to Autodesk capabilities and industry best practices to drive measurable ROI
  • Share best practices, competitive insights, and customer success stories with the broader Customer Success organization
  • Contribute to industry knowledge and thought leadership through collaboration, customer engagements, and internal knowledge sharing

Minimum Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in the AEC industry or AEC technology industry
  • Experience supporting or implementing construction technology solutions across project teams or organizations
  • Strong understanding of construction workflows and project delivery processes
  • Familiarity with SaaS platforms and technology adoption in enterprise environments
  • Experience creating data dashboards, reporting, or analytics using construction project data
  • Experience supporting digital transformation or technology change initiatives within construction organizations
  • Ability to travel as needed to support customer engagements and internal collaboration

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with construction contractors, engineering firms, or BIM/VDC consulting organizations
  • Familiarity with construction technology ecosystems, including ERP systems, BIM tools, and project management platforms
  • Experience working with data integrations, APIs, or business intelligence tools such as Power BI

The Ideal Candidate

  • Passionate about improving the construction industry through technology and better workflows
  • Curious and analytical, with a strong interest in understanding customer challenges and solving complex problems
  • Comfortable collaborating with stakeholders ranging from project teams to executive leadership
  • Motivated by helping customers achieve measurable outcomes and realize value from technology investments
  • Effective in cross-functional environments and committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences
