Job Description

Job Description Summary

Are you passionate about innovation? Are you excited at the opportunity to electrify and decarbonize the world?

We operate with a founder’s mindset. We deliver innovation with passion, speed, and courage. We continuously challenge our thinking and are empowered to dream big and take smart risks.

What impact you’ll make: If you have a passion for technology and want a career in engineering, the Edison Engineering Development Program (EEDP) is for you! It is an entry-level engineering program consisting of rotational assignments, technical training, and endless opportunities to accelerate your professional development. GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy (GVH) is committed to the development of tomorrow’s engineers and graduates of EEDP have the technical foundation to innovate and shape the future of nuclear with GE Vernova!

Job Description

What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)

As a Nuclear EEDP, you will:

  • Work on impactful, technically challenging projects in the Nuclear power generation industry.
  • Participate in work rotations in a variety of disciplines (i.e., Design, Analysis, Systems, Integration, Controls, etc.) over the span of approximately 2 years.
  • Enhance core engineering skills through technical, business, and general development training.
  • Receive technical training, mentorship, and coaching from world-class technologists and experienced technical managers.
  • Collaborate in general development and technical training activities with other program members.
  • Grow technical expertise in your chosen field and expand skills in new areas.

What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)

  • Bachelor's OR Master's Degree in one of the Engineering majors stated from an ABET accredited university: Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, Controls Engineering, Civil/Structural Engineering, Materials Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering or other Energy Engineering related majors will be considered.
  • Must have the ability to work in the US for an unlimited amount of time without sponsorship.
  • Ability and willingness to work full-time and in-person in Wilmington, NC.

What will make you stand out (Desired Qualifications)

  • Overall GPA greater than or equal to 3.0 (on a 4.0 scale) without rounding.
  • Passion/desire to work in the Nuclear industry.
  • Prior intern, co-op, or research experience in the power industry, design engineering or nuclear engineering.
  • Highly motivated, self-starter, energetic, confident, flexible in changing environments.
  • Strong dedication to a career in technology and a real passion for engineering.
  • Ability to demonstrate an analytical approach to problem solving, learning agility.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to articulate technical problems in clear and simple terms.
  • Demonstrated and enthusiastic leadership and team building skills.
  • Strong team and interpersonal skills; ability to work in project teams.
  • Proven scholastic achievements and proactive leadership skills.

Where you will land in 2 years

After the program, you will be a well-trained Engineer applying your technical expertise to drive innovation in the nuclear power industry. You might be working on cutting-edge projects in Design, Systems, Integration, or Controls, playing a crucial role in our mission to deliver reliable, affordable, sustainable solutions.  You will offboard into an Engineering role within the Nuclear Organization.
