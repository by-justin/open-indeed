Your role and responsibilities

Responsible for mechanical design of MV switchgear.

负责中压开关设备的机械设计开发

1、  Have the ability to select standard scheme and design non-standard scheme according to the specific engineering requirements.

根据工程具体要求进行标准结构方案选型及非标准方案的设计。

2、  Cooperate in designing the project schedule to make sure the project on time.

协助制定项目计划并保证相关环节的准时完成。

3、  Cooperate in the production issue.

协调处理生产中产生的问题。

4、  Design new structure scheme according to the market requirements.

对市场要求设计新的结构方案。

5、  Support on design scheme confirmation before contract receiving.

支持售前的设计方案确认。

6、  To write the related technical documentations.

编写技术文件。

Requirements:

1、Bachelor degree or above, major in mechanical engineering or mechanism related.

本科及以上学历，机械工程或机械相关专业。

2、Have a comprehensive and systematic knowledge of Mechanical design, mechanical drawing, machining and application processing with more than 2 years working experience in this field.

2年以上中压成套产品设计开发经验，熟悉中压成套产品标准和设计规范，熟悉中压成套产品结构、一次原理，熟悉中压成套产品生产制造、工艺流程。

3、Good command of power system knowledge and MV switchgear technology on manufacture process and standards. Be familiar with MS word, Excel, AutoCAD, Solidworks etc.

较全面、系统地掌握电力系统知识，了解中压开关柜的相关技术，流程及制造标准。熟练使用微软办公软件，AUTOCAD、SolidWorks等相关绘图软件

4、Ability to communicate in English, both written and oral.

英语流利。

More about us ABB's Distribution Solutions Division facilitates the efficient and reliable distribution, protection and control of power by improving electric power quality while strengthening the resilience of the grid. The Division offers segment-specific products and solutions that largely serve utilities, industry and infrastructure segments, often providing the requisite medium-voltage link between high-voltage transmission systems and low-voltage users. With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions at its core, the offering includes medium-voltage air- and gas-insulated switchgear (1 to 66 kilovolts), indoor and outdoor circuit breakers, reclosers, fuses, contactors, relays, instrument transformers, sensors, motor control centers, as well as low-voltage switchgear for the ANSI standard markets.
