Job Description

📍  Location:  Aspropyrgos, Athens 📅  Type:  Full-Time 🎓  Ideal for:  Candidates with a background in logistics/operations, ideally with SAP or Warehousing Management System experience.

🌍 Traveling : Up to 30% International traveling required

Do you want to join Supply Chain Network in a world's leading FMCG company?

The Warehouse Orchestration Center team, part of Product Supply in P&G, focuses on the innovative operational improvement of our European Distribution Centers, centralizing core supply chain processes in a digital, innovative and effective way. Our goal is to build a connected network of intelligent DCs through centralization, standardization and digitalization of Europe Warehousing tasks.

💰Competitive compensation details: for this role the  monthly starting salary is 2.470   EUR Gross (yearly starting salary 34.580 EUR).  We consistently benchmark our salaries against top competitors and every P&G employee’s salary is reviewed at least annually so there is ample room for growth.

We are looking for motivated individuals to join our W.O.C. organization in Aspropyrgos, Athens.

🔧  What You’ll Do

As a Europe Warehousing Business Expert, you will join the Systems & Innovation team and be instrumental in executing digitalization & automation transformative projects assigned by the team manager, across the warehouses in Europe. If you thrive in a fast-paced, complex environment and enjoy solving problems, we want to hear from you!

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in the implementation of warehouse system solution projects, including software version upgrades (e.g., SAP, WMS), by participating in Site Integration Testing (SIT) and User Acceptance Testing (UAT), as well as supporting trial cutovers and post-cutover support.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to identify and define new requirements for necessary changes at the site, such as process modifications or enhancements (e.g., Requests for Change - RFC).
  • Act as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for the site regarding any new technology implementations, ensuring smooth integration and user adoption..
  • Identify and implement process improvements by analyzing current workflows, proposing changes, and collaborating with management and IT to test and roll out enhancements.
  • Work with other departments (such as IT, logistics, and supply chain management) to ensure that warehouse operations align with broader organizational goals and strategies.
  • Facilitate training sessions for End Users, ensuring they are proficient in new and existing processes and systems. For instance, conducting workshops on SAP and WMS functionalities & create relevant documentation.
