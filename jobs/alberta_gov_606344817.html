Job Information Job Title: Public Land Policy Analyst Req Id: 87676 Ministry: Forestry and Parks Location: Edmonton Full or Part-Time: Full Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent Scope: Open Closing Date: October 6, 2026 Classification: Program Services 3 Salary: $2,935.39 to $3,850.29 bi-weekly ($76,613 - $100,492/year) Forestry and Parks are responsible for growing our manufactured wood products and forestry sector, preserving, and managing our public lands and protected areas. Protected areas are essential to the quality of life that Albertans enjoy. They conserve our natural landscapes and wildlife habitat and offer a broad range of nature-based outdoor recreational opportunities. Alberta’s protected areas inspire people to discover, value, protect, and enjoy the natural world and the benefits it provides for current and future generations. Role Responsibilities As a Public Lands Policy Analyst, you will contribute to the aligned and integrated delivery of public land management and administration in support of regulatory transformation and departmental priorities. Working collaboratively with policy specialists, regional staff, subject matter experts, and other government business areas, you will support the development, review, implementation, and communication of public land policies, directives, disturbance standards, guidelines, and related materials. Roles and Responsibilities: Develop and review public land policies, directives, disturbance standards, guidelines, and procedures to support regulatory transformation, legislative requirements, and departmental priorities. Analyze legislation, regulations, policies, and program requirements to identify gaps, risks, emerging issues, and opportunities, and develop recommendations to ensure alignment with public land management objectives. Lead and facilitate policy development initiatives by engaging subject matter experts, regional staff, and stakeholders, coordinating working sessions, and integrating diverse technical, operational, and policy perspectives. Conduct environmental scans, jurisdictional reviews, and evidence-based research to identify trends, leading practices, and developments that may affect public land management and regulatory initiatives. Prepare briefing materials, analyses, recommendations, decision documents, and responses to support managers and senior managers in addressing public land policy, legislative, regulatory, and implementation issues. Develop and coordinate communication, training, and awareness materials for new or revised policies, directives, procedures, information letters, and guidelines, and support the maintenance of accurate public-facing information. Build and maintain collaborative relationships across the department and Government of Alberta, participate in teams and committees, and support effective implementation of policies and regulatory transformation initiatives.
