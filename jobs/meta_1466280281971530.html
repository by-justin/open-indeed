Description

Meta is seeking an experienced Building Management System (BMS) Quality Manager to join our Data Center Engineering & Construction (DEC) Quality team. Our team’s mission is to optimize the delivery of our Building Management Systems to ensure they are completed on time, are safe, robust, reliable, and conform to our design and quality requirements. This role will support internal cross-functional teams, lead installation and commissioning efforts, oversee quality, and quickly adapt in an evolving space.

Our data centers are the foundation upon which our rapidly growing infrastructure efficiently operates, and our innovative services are delivered. Building and operating data centers the "right" way is synonymous with ensuring high uptime, capacity availability and capital conservation. The data center engineering team thinks from chip to chiller (or electrical substation), determining configurations and ensuring maximum efficiency of our compute infrastructure.

Responsibilities

  • Manage, onboard, and lead the General Contractor BMS Lead(s), the Control Systems Integrator (CSI) and Electrical and Installation (E&I) controls contractors throughout all phases of the project, while developing efficiency improvements and incorporating lessons learned to ensure a safe, robust, reliable and functional controls system that adheres to Meta’s design and quality requirements
  • Establish a clear vision and foster cross-functional collaboration, coordination, and support among on-site project teams, including the Meta BMS team, general contractors, Control System Integrators, and installation contractors
  • Drive consistency and standardization across all buildings and Control System Integrators and installation contractors
  • Review, understand and ensure project level adherence to the issued revision of the BMS PLC Playbook
  • Perform BMS inspections (wiring, installation, and commissioning) and validate the status/progress of the projects including supporting the project teams, both internal and external, starting from the early stages of Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) construction by working with project level Construction Managers (both internal and external) to check the health of the project (including schedule), working with the project teams and supporting Regional Quality Managers (RQMs). Align internally on program/project related communications to ensure a clear, consistent message to trade partners
  • Assess team performance, build relationships, and support the project benchmark and mock-up process of the BMS installations to assure consistency across the project while coordinating these efforts across multiple cross-functional partners, within project-defined timelines, to minimize re-work
  • Work with Construction Managers, Regional Quality Managers and General Contractors to ensure creation, accuracy and maintenance of logical schedule(s) for construction, commissioning, and packaged equipment BMS activities while leveraging this data to identify early finish dates for detailed design delivery
  • Coordinate and manage both packaged equipment factory installed controls audits and BMS commissioning audits to ensure specification/requirement adherence and track identified deficiencies through completion
  • Coordinate and lead large multi-stakeholder meetings on a regular basis and communicate discussions to all key stakeholders
  • Support custom tooling and their integration into the construction processes as well as supporting other cross-functional program/project initiatives that directly impact the delivery of the BMS while simultaneously working to identify and mitigate risk associated with these efforts
  • Travel domestically as needed (anticipated 50% travel)

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree (Engineering, Construction Management, or equivalent degree) or relevant work experience
  • 10+ years of experience with a combination of instrumentation and controls programming/installation, general contractor MEP coordination/project management, commissioning (both mechanical and electrical) of data centers, or other large-scale mission-critical buildings
  • Experience with Industrial Automation and Building Management Control Systems (i.e., PLC/DDC)
  • 3+ years of experience with programming and designing of HVAC control systems and Electrical Power Monitoring

Preferred Qualifications

  • Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
  • Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
  • Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
  • Certified in the following: cGMP, CAP (Certified Automation Professional), and/or PMP (Project Management Professional)
  • Experience with Autodesk ACC Build and Procore Platforms
  • Experience in adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
  • Experience with PLC, SCADA, and OPC UA systems
  • Experience with Schneider Modicon and Rockwell Allen-Bradley PLC platforms
  • Experience with Schneider Electric Unity Pro, AVEVA, and Ignition platforms
