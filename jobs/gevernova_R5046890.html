Activities, Responsibilities and Missions

  • Identify BOP requirements during the project execution phase (OTR) in collaboration with Lead Engineers, and during the pre-project phase (ITO) with Application Engineers.
  • Launch BOP OTR sourcing/consultation processes using the existing supplier panel.
  • Monitor ITO/OTR quotation requests and provide standardized reporting.
  • Conduct negotiations, mainly bundling negotiations, for standard equipment.
  • Define a purchasing strategy for Class C components.
  • Establish Class C price lists and framework agreements.
  • Participate in OTR project reviews to communicate the progress of supplier consultations and purchase order placement, and manage quotation follow-ups.
  • Conduct technical evaluation phases with Lead Engineers and lead negotiations through to finalization before placing orders.
  • Manage commercial relationships with suppliers, monitor proper contract execution, and handle disputes or non-compliance with contractual terms.
  • Monitor project dashboards (Project Trackers) and ensure alignment with the OTR Tracker.
  • Conduct technological and economic market intelligence.
  • Evaluate suppliers by conducting supplier performance reviews based on criteria (ratings) defined with the Purchasing Management team.
  • Support commodity buyers with quotation follow-up, reporting, and supplier onboarding.
