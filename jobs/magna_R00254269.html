Job Responsibilities:

MISSION: As part of the Hardware team, the Hardware Engineer participates in the development of ADAS products along with other members of the R&D department, considering the respect of customer requirements, project schedule, quality and manufacturing objectives. 作为硬件团队的一员，硬件工程师与研发部门的其他成员一起参与ADAS产品的开发，并综合考虑客户需求、项目进度、质量和制造目标。

POSITION RESPONSIBLITY:     Manage analysis of the customer requirements relating with HW and Identify the risks to meet the customers' requirements in cost, timing and quality. 管理与硬件相关的客户需求分析，识别风险，以满足客户在成本，时间和质量方面的要求     Manage technical solutions, in collaboration with System Engineer. 管理技术解决方案，与系统工程师合作     Capture the circuit schematic and pilot the board layout and routing. 负责完成电路原理图，并引导电路板的布局和布线     Perform design analysis (including Spice simulations). 执行设计分析(包括Spice仿真)     Write Technical Notes and Test Reports. 撰写技术笔记和测试报告     Manage and maintain a complete design document set for the project. 管理和维护项目的完整设计文档集     Pilot HW HSIS (Hardware Software Interface Specification), PoD (Proof of Design) including WCCA (Worst Case Circuit Analysis). 引导文档撰写，包括HSIS(硬件软件接口规范)，PoD(设计证明)，WCCA(最坏情况电路分析)等     Initiate & Pilot design FMEA, Reliability Study, Failure Tree Analysis, Safety Hazard Analysis. 启动和引导FMEA设计，可靠性研究，故障树分析，安全隐患分析     Write Technical Specifications. 编写技术规格书     Manage the verification of the design concept. 管理硬件设计的验证     Perform tests of PoD verification and DFMEA verification. 执行PoD验证和DFMEA验证的测试     Analyze and solve issues in design verification phase. 分析和解决设计验证阶段的问题     Perform tests with support of product verification team. 在产品验证团队的支持下执行测试     Perform design improvement and validate based on verification result. 根据验证结果进行设计改进和验证     Support the industrialization of the product or cross team related HW knowledge. 支持产品量产或跨团队相关的硬件技术支持     To support FUSA development based on ISO26262-5支持基于ISO26262-5的FUSA开发     To support PV testing (test scope, test setup, issue debug) 支持PV测试(测试范围，测试设置，问题调试)     To support the quality team for the PPAP delivery协助质量团队完成PPAP的交付     To support the cross teams for product knowledge 为跨团队提供产品知识支持     Support Industrialization, Manufacturing and Purchasing team regarding hardware. 支持量产，制造和采购团队的硬件工作
