Job Description

Are you an experienced HR, HC or MC driver looking for your next long‑term opportunity in Sydney?

DHL Supply Chain is seeking expressions of interest from safety‑focused, professional drivers who want to join a global leader in logistics, servicing a range of dedicated contracts across the Sydney region.

We are building a talent pipeline for upcoming packaged gas , bulk , and general freight driving roles across multiple sites including Fairfield, Horsley Park, Yennora, Eastern Creek and nearby locations .

About the Roles

Depending on licence class and operational requirements, duties may include:

  • Local metro pick‑ups and deliveries

  • Operating HR, HC or MC vehicles (whichever applies to you)

  • Safe loading, securing and unloading of freight or packaged product

  • Manual handling as required

  • Completing daily paperwork, run sheets and safety checks

  • Providing excellent customer service to our contracted partners

  • Following all DHL and customer-specific safety procedures

Shift patterns may vary, with opportunities for day, afternoon and night shifts , depending on contract needs.

What We Offer

  • Permanent, stable work with a global industry leader

  • Competitive hourly rates + penalties + allowances

  • Opportunities for overtime

  • Modern, well‑maintained fleet

  • Full uniform and PPE provided

  • Strong commitment to safety, compliance and driver wellbeing

  • Ongoing training and career progression including upskilling pathways (e.g., HR → HC → MC)

  • Supportive team environment and onsite leadership

What you need

  • A valid HR, HC or MC licence

  • Recent driving experience in your licence class

  • A strong commitment to safety and compliance

  • Good communication and customer service skills

  • Ability to complete daily pre‑start checks and paperwork

  • A clean driving history (traffic history check required)

  • Ability to pass pre‑employment medical, D&A screening and background checks

How To Apply

If you are interested in joining DHL Supply Chain as a driver in Sydney, please submit your Expression of Interest by applying through this advert.

A member of our Transport Talent Acquisition team will contact shortlisted drivers to discuss current and upcoming opportunities aligned to your licence, experience and shift availability.
