Job Description

About the Team

DoorDash Labs is an independent team within DoorDash. We explore robotics and autonomy to transform last-mile logistics through drone delivery in the long term. If you have a passion for applying autonomous aviation technologies in a service used by millions of people, then we want to talk to you!

About the Role

This role offers a unique opportunity to participate and build regulated drone delivery operations by:

  • Working cross functionally with Flight Operationsand being a part of the Ground Operations team

  • Identifying operational risk and escalating safety-critical matters to the Ground Operations Lead

  • Assisting in all operational tasks outside of flight - ex. Drone Drop Spots, manual data collection, merchant launch readiness, etc.

  • Working on other Commercial Operations associated tasks assigned by mgmt

You’re excited about this opportunity because you will…

  • Support the Commercial Ground Operations team in assigned tasks

  • Collaborate with software and hardware teams to provide structured operational feedback on production systems and tools

  • Work closely with Flight Operations& support on the ground

We’re excited about you because…

  • You have strong operational judgment and risk-based decision-making capability

  • You communicate clearly with executive leadership, maintenance, and operations crew

  • You maintain uncompromising standards for safety, compliance, documentation, and accountability

  • You thrive in environments where precision, operational discipline, and scalability are critical

About DoorDash
