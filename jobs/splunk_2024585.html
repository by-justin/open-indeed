Job Description

Meet the Team

The  Splunk Metadata Catalog (MDC)  team is part of the core Splunk Platform organization. We are building a high-scale  Knowledge Graph  that empowers Splunk users to discover data assets effortlessly, map complex relationships, and trace data lineage across diverse ecosystems.

You will join a fast-paced, high-impact team pioneering foundational technology central to the  Cisco Data Fabric  strategy. As a Senior Engineer, you will shape architectural decisions and build systems that scale across enterprise-grade data platforms.

Your impact

As a  Senior Software Engineer on the team, you will:

  • End-to-End Feature Delivery:  own development, and implementation of complex, high-impact features and microservices within the Metadata Catalog platform.
  • Collaborate on System Architecture:  Partner with Technical Leaders and Architects to translate product requirements into modular, scalable, and resilient distributed components.
  • Build for Scale & High Performance:  optimize robust, low-latency applications capable of managing large-scale graph relationships and data lineage queries.
  • Drive Quality & Engineering Excellence:  Champion a  Developer Owned Quality  mindset within the team by authoring comprehensive unit, integration, and performance tests to ensure high product reliability.
  • Operational & Production Ownership:  Take active ownership of services in production-monitoring service health, debugging complex distributed issues, participating in on-call rotations, and driving post-incident reviews.
  • Technical Mentorship & Collaboration:  Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through detailed code reviews and design discussions, fostering continuous learning and engineering rigor across the team.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Education & experience: Bachelor's in computer science / related field + 6 years of related experience, or Masters + 3 years of related experience
  • Software Development & Debugging:  Strong hands-on proficiency in an object-oriented or systems language (such as  Go, Java, or C++ ), with proven experience designing, developing, and debugging production-grade services.
  • Cloud & Container Technologies:  Demonstrated experience building and operating cloud-native microservices using  AWS, Azure, or GCP , alongside containerization and orchestration tools ( Docker, Kubernetes ).
  • Distributed Systems Foundations:  Solid grasp of algorithms, data structures, concurrency, distributed systems architecture, and RESTful / gRPC API design.
  • CI/CD & Agile Rigor:  Proven background delivering software in fast-paced Agile environments with automated testing and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines.
  • Reliability & Performance Focus:  Track record of diagnosing and solving performance bottlenecks and ensuring high availability in distributed environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Graph Databases & Technologies:  Hands-on experience with graph databases (e.g.,  Neo4j, AWS Neptune, Apache AGE ) and query languages/frameworks (e.g.,  Cypher, Gremlin, GraphQL ).
  • Data Lineage & Metadata:  Experience with metadata catalogs, data governance frameworks, schema registries, or knowledge graph platforms.
  • E vent Streaming:  Familiarity with distributed message brokers and streaming platforms (e.g.,  Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ ).
  • Observability:  Experience instrumenting services with observability metrics, distributed tracing, and logging frameworks (e.g., OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Splunk).
  • Collaboration & Adaptability:  Strong communication skills with a demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across distributed global engineering teams in an evolving technical landscape.
