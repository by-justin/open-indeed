Role: Senior Front-End Developer (UI Components & Design Tokens) – Quantum

If you enjoy bridging design and development and turning Figma logic into robust, accessible component libraries, this role is for you! 🫵

Help us mature Quantum: components, patterns, tokens, theming, and premium UI at scale.

About the Design System and the Team:

Quantum, the Thales digital Design System, is a strategic pillar adopted by more than 300 designers and 2,000 developers across the Group. It enhances our brand image and enables the creation of premium user experiences, while supporting the company’s overall performance.

By joining the product team responsible for Quantum, you will participate in the design, evolution, and maintenance of the Design System. As the true common foundation for all our digital products, our Design System ensures brand and design coherence and the operational efficiency of Thales interfaces at scale.

Quantum Design System Overview:

Design: design libraries (on Figma and other design tools), covering color palettes, typographies, layout structures, elevation levels, icon systems, components, patterns, templates, and guidelines. Each component has been meticulously designed in various states, resulting in hundreds of variants.

Dev: The system includes comprehensive implementation packages and documentation, based on a single source of truth via design tokens. It supports all major web technologies, as well as extensions for desktop applications.

Quantum is built on a hybrid contribution model: a central team (2 designers, 2 front-end developers) operates in Agile-Kanban and collaborates with a guild of 30 contributors across Thales entities.

Our Target:

We are looking for an experienced developer , passionate about design, capable of bridging design and development, and contributing to a robust, scalable, and inspiring system for teams.

Your Skills and Experience:

  • Strong expertise in modern UI development, with a sharp eye for detail and a solid understanding of UI principles: accessibility, code quality, and design rigor.
  • Advanced knowledge of web development fundamentals: HTML, CSS (pre/post processors a plus), JavaScript/TypeScript, and experience building and maintaining component libraries in a collaborative IDE environment.
  • Proficient in translating design assets from Figma to code (experience with Penpot is a plus).
  • Demonstrated experience integrating and developing accessible components and interfaces to meet at least AA accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1).

  • Awareness of UX design methodologies (user research, usability testing, etc.) and comfort participating in, or facilitating, workshops with designers and stakeholders.

  • Strong, stimulating, and flexible leadership
  • Excellent command of English (C1 level minimum) and professional proficiency in French is desirable but not mandatory

🚀 Does this sound like you? Let’s talk missions!

Your Mission:

Under the operational responsibility of the Tech Lead of the Design System, you will play a key role in advancing Quantum, our Design System, to a new level of maturity. Quantum is currently undergoing an ambitious transformation: implementation of an advanced Design Token system, creation of a brand-new visual language, while consolidating the development libraries. As such, you will be at the heart of strategic developments, take part in scaling to the next version, and support the smooth adoption of these innovations within the organization. By joining the Thales Quantum Design System team, you will play a key role in evolving our Design System.

Your missions will include:

  • Designing and developing design system components using a code-first approach and best development practices, ensuring quality, scalability, and maintainability.
  • Collaborating closely with the Design Lead and Tech Lead to tackle large-scale design system challenges, and contribute to technical decisions and architectural choices.
  • Gathering and understanding user needs and behaviors in collaboration with UX specialists, and participating in UX activities such as interviews, workshops, and user testing to inform component development.
  • Contributing to and implementing a complete visual review of the design system, ensuring developed components elevate the brand and align with premium design standards.
  • Structuring, implementing, and maintaining design tokens, including the modeling, management, and automation required for scalability and multi-theme support.
  • Translating design specifications and prototypes into robust, reusable code.
  • Contributing to comprehensive documentation, formalization, and support of the design system, explaining usage to the internal developer and designer community and supporting adoption.
  • Acting as a UI and frontend reference within the team and broader community: participating in code/design reviews and sharing best coding and UI practices.
  • Staying informed about advancements and best practices in design systems, frontend development, and related technologies.
