À propos de Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (P&WC) est un leader mondial de l’industrie aérospatiale établi à Longueuil, au Québec. Nous fabriquons des moteurs de nouvelle génération qui équipent la plus grande flotte d’avions et d’hélicoptères au monde dans les segments de l’aviation d’affaires, de l’aviation générale et de l’aviation régionale.

En près de 100 ans, nous avons réalisé de nombreuses percées dans le développement de moteurs qui assurent le transport de marchandises et de matériel, luttent contre les feux de forêt et effectuent le transport de passagers.

Un employeur de choix

Pratt & Whitney Canada a été reconnue comme l'un des 10 meilleurs employeurs au Canada en 2025 selon Forbes, en plus d’être classé au premier rang des employeurs dans l'industrie de l'aérospatiale et de la défense au pays! Notre siège social figure sur le palmarès des meilleurs employeurs dans la région de Montréal. Ensemble, ces distinctions confirment notre réputation d'employeur de choix dans la région de Montréal, au Canada et dans le monde.

Description de la fonction

En tant que membre de l’équipe d’ingénierie – service à la clientèle – faisant partie de l’équipe du programme de l’Aviation Régional, vous vous rapporterez au gestionnaire du programme après marché. Vous serez responsable de gérer la performance du produit en service, la relation avec les clients / manufacturier avionneur et s’assurer que les équipes de service rencontrent les besoins stratégiques et spécifique à notre programme.  Vous faites partie d’une équipe de 14 experts responsable de diriger différentes équipes intégrées ayant pour but d’influencer les améliorations du produit qui peuvent avoir une incidence sur la sécurité, la durabilité, la fiabilité ou les coûts engendrés par la maintenance en service.

À titre d’ingénieur de programme de service à la clientèle vous serez également responsable du produit et du service rencontrant les plus hautes exigences de nos clients. Nous bâtissons une relation solide avec nos clients en gardant le cap sur les besoins de ceux-ci et ce en développant de nouvelles solutions spécifiques à leurs besoins.

Nous facilitons cette tâche en s’assurant d’utiliser les nouveaux outils disponibles, nos actions sont basées sur la proactivité, nous sommes commit à travailler ensemble avec intégrité et respect.  Le programme de l’Aviation Général aide nos clients à voyager afin de tisser des liens ensemble et à générer des économies à travers le monde.

Le poste est à temps plein du lundi au vendredi selon des horaires flexibles à Longueuil et s’effectue exclusivement en présentiel.

Description de la fonction

About Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (P&WC) is a global leader in the aerospace industry, headquartered in Longueuil, Quebec. We manufacture next-generation engines that power the world’s largest fleet of business, general aviation, and regional aircraft and helicopters.

For nearly 100 years, we have pioneered advancements in engine development, supporting cargo and equipment transportation, wildfire suppression, and passenger travel.

An Employer of Choice

Pratt & Whitney Canada was recognized as one of Canada’s Top 10 Employers in 2025 by Forbes and ranked #1 in the aerospace and defense industry in the country. Our headquarters is also listed among the top employers in the Montreal region. These accolades reinforce our reputation as an employer of choice in Montreal, Canada, and worldwide.

What our expectations are

As a member of the Customer Engineering team for the Regional program, reporting to the Regional program manager of aftermarket, you will be responsible of managing in-service product performance, the relationship with customers / airframe OEM & aligning service team to meet our specific program strategies. Part of a team of 14 experts, you will have the opportunity to lead an integrate product team (IPT) and influence the redesign of a part which could affect the safety, durability, reliability of a product or the cost of operation.

As a Customer Service Program Engineer, you will also oversee our product and service by delivering world-class product & service dependability. We enable customer relationships through intense market focus and we develop customized solutions. We augment all of this by the use of technology, we strive for proactivity, commit collectively and we always act with integrity and respect. The Regional Aviation program is powering aircraft which help connecting people and economies around the world.

This is a full-time position from Monday to Friday with flexible hours, based in Longueuil, and performed exclusively on-site.

Function Description

As a NPI Customer Engineer in the Regional Customer Program team, you will be responsible of managing in-service product performance, relationship with customers / airframe OEM & aligning service team to meet our specific program strategies.  Part of a team of experts, you will have the opportunity to influence business models and to lead an integrate product team (IPT) to define and influence the redesign of a part which could affect the safety, durability, reliability of a product and the cost of operation of a regional airline.

What your day to day will look like

  • Lead Customer Engineering activities for the Regional Aviation team
  • Act as technical liaison between Engineering and Customer Service, ensuring aftermarket requirements are integrated into the development of new engines.
  • Lead the definition and implementation of aftermarket technical requirements within Product Design Requirements Specifications (PDRS) and monitor compliance throughout the engine development lifecycle.
  • Review and approval of maintenance documentation, including Maintenance Manuals, Overhaul Manuals, and MSG-3 maintenance program development
  • Leverage field experience and lessons learned to influence design modifications and ensure long-term product supportability and customer value.
  • Collaborate closely with engineering, marketing, business development and customer service subject matter experts and strategy owners to define, monitor, track, and influence new engine programs deliverables
  • Interface directly with OEMs, partner and suppliers.
  • Ownership of Product Performance - including safety, reliability, durability and all services in relation with maintenance of the product in service
  • Comply with Airframe OEM, Operators and Airworthiness authorities’ obligations
  • Align organizations with a vertical view of the market – Cross Value Stream
  • Provide business forecast & manage in-service product campaigns
  • Accountable for engine operating cost optimization
  • Develop strategies and ensure readiness of new program & customer entry into service
  • Lead customer relationship value stream
  • Commercial and warranty programs management
  • Subject matter expert for the front-line team & provide product knowledge to field communities throughout conferences and customers dedicated technical reviews
  • Support development of new business models and strategies

What you need to be successful:

  • Minimum of bachelor’s degree in engineering or equivalent
  • Looking for 10-15 years progressive experience in Aerospace, Aftermarket, Operations, or similar functional expertise
  • Proficient business communications in English
  • Demonstrated ability to manage and drive customer satisfaction at all levels (internal and/or external)
  • Demonstrated ability to lead in a matrixed organization
  • Demonstrated ability to manage product performance and delivery to drive customer satisfaction
  • Ability to translate technical data into action plans and strategy
  • Experience with Maintenance Program Development/MSG-3
  • AME license a plus
  • This position is part of a French-speaking work environment, it requires a very good knowledge of English to read and understand, on a daily basis, documentation and technical terms that may emanate from international organizations, as well as to write and communicate in English with customers, suppliers or international colleagues.
