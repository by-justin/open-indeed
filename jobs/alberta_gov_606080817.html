La Direction des services en français (DSF) appuie le ministère de l’Éducation et de la Garde d’enfants en favorisant l’accès aux services, aux ressources et au soutien en français pour le système d’éducation de l’Alberta. La DSF travaille en collaboration à l’échelle du ministère et avec ses partenaires en éducation pour renforcer la prestation des services, pour soutenir les communautés francophones et d’expression française et pour contribuer à des résultats inclusifs et axés sur les élèves partout en Alberta. Pour de plus amples renseignements, consultez https://www.alberta.ca/fr/education-and-childcare Role Responsibilities Reporting to the Director, French Language Services, the French Language Services Manager provides leadership, direction and oversight for branch operations, service delivery and strategic initiatives that support French-language services within the Ministry of Education and Childcare. This role leads a team and works collaboratively with ministry partners and education stakeholders to support effective planning, coordination and delivery of programs, resources and services that meet the needs of Alberta’s Francophone and French-speaking communities. The responsibilities of this position include, but are not limited to, the following key areas: Strategic Leadership and Planning Provide strategic direction for branch priorities, business planning and service delivery objectives aligned with ministry goals and government commitments. Branch Operations and Service Delivery Oversee day-to-day operations, coordinate work across the branch and ensure services, resources and supports are delivered effectively and consistently. Program and Policy Implementation Lead and support the implementation of programs, policies and initiatives that advance French-language services within Alberta’s education system. Team Leadership and Performance Management Lead, coach and support staff by setting expectations, assigning work, monitoring performance and fostering a collaborative and accountable team environment. Stakeholder and Partner Collaboration Build and maintain effective working relationships with ministry areas, education partners and community stakeholders to support coordinated service delivery. Francophone and French-Language Services Support Support initiatives that respond to the needs of Francophone and French-speaking communities and promote access to French-language services and resources. Issue Management and Advice Identify emerging issues, assess impacts and provide timely advice, options and recommendations to support informed decision-making. Resource, Budget and Contract Management Manage assigned resources, budgets, contracts and administrative requirements to support effective stewardship and operational accountability. Reporting, Evaluation and Continuous Improvement Monitor results, prepare reports and identify opportunities to improve processes, services and outcomes for the branch and its partners. Compliance, Accountability and Risk Management Ensure work is completed in accordance with applicable legislation, policies, standards and accountability requirements while identifying and managing risks. Rôle et responsabilités Relevant de la direction de la DSF, le ou la gestionnaire des services en français assure le leadeurship, la direction et la surveillance des activités de la DSF, de la prestation des services et des initiatives stratégiques qui appuient les services en français au sein du ministère de l’Éducation et de la Garde d’enfants. La personne titulaire de ce poste dirige une équipe et travaille en collaboration avec des partenaires du ministère et des parties prenantes en éducation pour appuyer la planification, la coordination et la prestation efficaces de programmes, de ressources et de services qui répondent aux besoins des communautés francophones et d’expression française de l’Alberta.
