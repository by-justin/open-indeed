Optronics & Missile Electronics (OME) Sales Manager - Sea Sector

Location: Glasgow (preferred) or Reading. While this is a hybrid role, successful candidates will spend regular time on-site to build strong relationships and collaborate with colleagues and stakeholders. We will also consider candidates based elsewhere in the UK who can commit to regular travel and maintaining a strong presence within the team. UK and international travel (approximately 50-60%) is required.

Shape the Future of Naval Defence

We're looking for a driven and commercially minded Sales Manager to help grow our Optronics & Missile Electronics (OME) business within the Sea Sector.

This is an exciting opportunity for a defence sales, business development, commercial or programme professional who thrives on building relationships, identifying opportunities and turning them into long-term business success.

You'll join an experienced and ambitious team, working with cutting-edge technologies and a globally recognised portfolio of naval defence solutions. Our heritage includes delivering every sighting solution (Periscope and Optronics Mast) to the Royal Navy, alongside supporting customers around the world with advanced maritime capabilities.

The Opportunity

As Sales Manager, you'll be responsible for developing and converting a strong pipeline of opportunities across our Sea Sector portfolio. You'll work closely with customers, industry partners, major prime contractors and internal stakeholders to identify opportunities, shape winning strategies and secure profitable growth.

A key focus of the role will be building and strengthening industry relationships, expanding our market presence and helping position the business for future success in both UK and international markets.

We're looking for someone who can quickly establish credibility, build networks and bring energy, drive and commercial focus to a growing business.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Developing and delivering a robust pipeline of new business opportunities.
  • Building relationships with customers, major prime contractors and key stakeholders across the defence community.
  • Identifying, qualifying and pursuing opportunities that support strategic growth objectives.
  • Developing compelling capture and win strategies to secure new business.
  • Leading and influencing cross-functional teams throughout the sales and bid process.
  • Working closely with Product Line, Strategy, Marketing and Operational teams to develop customer-focused solutions.
  • Managing key customer accounts and supporting long-term relationship growth.
  • Representing the business at customer meetings, industry events, exhibitions and conferences.
  • Contributing to strategic planning, market analysis and future growth initiatives.

About You

You'll be a motivated, relationship-focused professional with a strong commercial mindset and a track record of delivering results.

You enjoy working at pace, building networks, influencing stakeholders and creating opportunities. You're equally comfortable developing strategy, engaging senior customers and rolling up your sleeves to support the wider team when needed.

We're particularly interested in candidates who have:

  • Experience in sales, business development, capture management or commercial leadership within the defence sector.
  • A proven track record of winning business and delivering sustainable growth.
  • Strong relationship-building and networking skills, with the ability to quickly establish credibility and identify opportunities.
  • Knowledge of, or experience working with, major defence prime contractors and defence supply chains.
  • Strong commercial and business acumen, with the ability to balance customer needs and business objectives.
  • The ability to lead, motivate and influence cross-functional teams.
  • Excellent communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills.
  • A proactive, resilient and positive attitude, with a willingness to take ownership and make things happen.
  • The drive to continuously learn, develop and stay connected to market trends.

It would be beneficial if you also have:

  • Knowledge of UK MOD procurement and commercial processes.
  • Experience of complex defence sales, capture management or bid leadership.
  • Understanding of export or international defence programmes.
  • Experience of structured bid methodologies such as Shipley.

Why Join Us?

This is an opportunity to join a growing business at the forefront of defence technology, where you'll have the autonomy to build relationships, shape opportunities and directly influence future growth. You'll work alongside highly experienced colleagues, engage with leading defence organisations and play a key role in delivering capabilities that matter.
