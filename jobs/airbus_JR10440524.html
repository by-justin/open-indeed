Job Description

Job Description:

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

Assembly Inspection/Paint Inspection

  • Visual Inspection
  • Verify potting material shelf life
  • Verify that inserts, components, etc. are installed as per drawing.
  • Visual Inspection for improper or insufficient coverage, rough surfaces, pitting.
  • Dust inclusions, orange peel, tears, sagging or blistering.
  • Inspecting parts  to planned MIDs and Inspection documents , drawings etc.
  • Recording of nonconformities and quarantining non conforming parts with red tags.
  • Create and maintain inspection plans and procedures.
  • Participate and lead  root cause and corrective action process using Rapid 5 Whys, 8D tools etc..
  • Perform First Article Inspections to AS9102 requirements.
  • Execute inspections to planned weekly and daily KPIs to Assembly Inspection Plans.
  • Serve as a guide to Assembly technicians by sharing requirements and acceptance criteria for Inspection.
  • Participate in all initial and recurrent training to maintain certifying staff authorities.

Trim Inspection

  • Verification of tool validity
  • Visual Inspection
  • Inspection of parts  to planned MIDs and Inspection documents , drawings etc.
  • Participate and lead  root cause and corrective action process using Rapid 5 Whys, 8D tools etc.
  • Recording of nonconformities & quarantining non conforming parts with red tags.

Shipping Inspection

  • Verify that all inspection stages have been completed.
  • Inspecting parts to planned MIDs and Inspection documents , drawings etc.
  • Recording of nonconformities & quarantining non conforming parts with red tags.
  • Preparation of shipping documentation including Form Ones and C of Cs.
  • Execute inspections to planned weekly and daily KPIs to Assembly Inspection Plans.
  • Participate in all initial and recurrent training to maintain certifying staff authorities.
  • Participate and lead  root cause and corrective action process using Rapid 5 Whys, 8D tools etc.

Lay-up Inspection

  • Review all materials in kits for shelf life & out life remaining
  • Verification that all materials have been cut & identified with ply number & orientation
  • Verification that Material Identification Sheets are complete.
  • Verification validity of tooling.
  • Visual Inspection to planned MIDs and Inspection documents , drawings etc.
  • Execute inspections to planned weekly and daily KPIs to Assembly Inspection Plans.
  • Participate and lead  root cause and corrective action process using Rapid 5 Whys, 8D tools etc.
  • Serve as a guide to Layup  technicians by sharing requirements and acceptance criteria for Inspection.
  • Recording of nonconformities &  quarantining non conforming parts with red tags.

Core Inspection

  • Verification validity of tooling
  • Verification of conformity to templates and drawings
  • Visual Inspection for damage, cell ruptures, contamination dust /oil
  • Inspection of parts  to planned MIDs and Inspection documents , drawings etc.
  • Recording of nonconformities & quarantining non conforming parts with red tags.
  • Execute inspections to planned weekly and daily KPIs to Assembly Inspection Plans.
  • Participate and lead  root cause and corrective action process using Rapid 5 Whys, 8D tools etc.

MINIMUM JOB REQUIREMENTS

Education:

  • College Diploma (Mechanical Engineering Technician or Technologist) or relevant experience

Experience:

  • 5 years of composite experience (Manufacturing/Assembly Inspection), specifically with a Quality Assurance System (ISO 9001, AS 9100)
  • NDT/Ultrasonic Inspection experience an asset

Knowledge, Skills, Demonstrated Capabilities:

  • Ability to work under broad supervision
  • Independent and able to work with a team
  • Attention to detail
  • Able to manage time effectively and prioritize work
  • Professional working relationships with internal and external sources, including regulatory and quality certified agencies
  • Able to work under pressure to meet demanding work schedules
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Excellent problem solving skills
  • Able to write accurate technical reports
  • Ability to read various Blueprints
  • Able to use measurement tools
