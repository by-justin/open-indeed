Job Description

Requisition ID: 269197

Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture.

Team

The Data team within Global Finance & Risk Technology (GFRT) provides enterprise-level analytics, data services, governance support, and operational data management for Global Finance initiatives. The team plays a critical role in supporting financial close processes, financial reporting, data migration initiatives, system implementations, and ongoing operational activities while ensuring the delivery of high-quality Financial Data Management, Data Analysis, Data Quality, and Data Governance services across the organization.

Role

  • We are seeking a Data Analyst to support business operations through data analysis, reporting, process improvement, system implementation, and operational support activities.

  • The role is responsible for delivering analytical insights, managing data migration initiatives, supporting financial and operational processes, and developing solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy, and control effectiveness.

  • Working closely with business stakeholders, operations teams, finance partners, and technology teams, the position plays a key role in maintaining data integrity, supporting system transitions, and ensuring successful business outcomes.

  • The successful candidate will combine strong technical expertise in data engineering, programming, data analysis, data modeling, and database design with a solid understanding of finance, accounting, and operational processes.

  • This individual must be highly analytical, proactive, detail-oriented, and capable of working effectively with stakeholders across Finance, Operations, Technology, and Vendor teams. Experience supporting system implementations, data migrations, financial reconciliations, and process automation initiatives is highly desirable.

What will we expect from you?

Data Analysis & Reporting

  • Collect, validate, analyze, and interpret data from multiple business systems.

  • Develop and maintain reports, dashboards, and performance metrics to support decision-making.

  • Conduct ad hoc analysis and provide actionable recommendations to stakeholders.

  • Monitor data quality, identify anomalies, and investigate discrepancies.

Process Improvement & Automation

  • Identify opportunities to streamline and automate operational processes.

  • Develop and maintain reporting, reconciliation, and analytical solutions.

  • Support the implementation of process enhancements, system upgrades, and operational efficiencies.

  • Create and maintain process documentation, user guides, and operational procedures.

Data Migration & System Implementation

  • Support data migration initiatives involving the transition from legacy systems and local ledgers to enterprise platforms, including PeopleSoft 9.2 (PS92).

  • Coordinate data extraction, validation, transformation, and loading activities.

  • Establish and validate historical balances and reference data within the new system environment.

  • Perform reconciliation activities to ensure completeness and accuracy between source and target systems.

  • Collaborate with business and technology stakeholders to resolve migration and conversion issues.

  • Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT), parallel runs, production readiness, and go-live activities.

  • Provide post-implementation support, including issue investigation, defect resolution, process stabilization, and end-user assistance following deployment.

Stakeholder Management & Governance

  • Partner with business, operations, finance, and technology teams to gather requirements and deliver solutions.

  • Communicate findings, risks, and recommendations to stakeholders and management.

  • Support audit requests, control testing, and regulatory compliance requirements.

  • Maintain strong governance and control practices over data, reporting, and operational processes.

Operational Support

  • Support day-to-day operational and financial processes.

  • Prepare, review, and coordinate special entries, adjustments, corrections, and related operational transactions.

  • Investigate, resolve, and escalate operational issues as required.

  • Perform reconciliations and exception analysis to ensure data accuracy and completeness.

  • Support month-end, quarter-end, and year-end operational activities.

Data analysis and reporting Large datasets and data-driven decision-making Process improvement or automation initiatives
