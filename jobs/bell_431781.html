Job Description

Dome Productions is North America’s leading production and transmission facilities provider, Dome is unique in its business approach and sets itself apart from others with its integration of primary business units of Mobile Production, Host Broadcaster and Media Services, and Transmission services all under its corporate umbrella. Dome Productions’ resume includes work with major worldwide broadcasters, leagues, production companies, government parties and other content creators, with a major emphasis on sports, eSports, and entertainment.

We provide a supportive and inclusive community where all team members can succeed. And through our commitment to environmental, social and governance initiatives, you will feel good about the greater impact you will have – making every day better for people as they connect, work, learn and play.

Join us. You belong at Dome Productions’

Responsibilities

  • Provide engineering support in the operation and maintenance of mobile production facilities.  Act as lead engineering technician onsite.
  • Set-up mobile production facilities including power connections and proper personnel access that adheres to established safety practices
  • Identify, propose and complete technical solutions in support of event production
  • Maintain established level of facility performance and inventory on mobile units
  • Assist operators and production in usage of facilities with a focus on due care for equipment
  • Maintain current knowledge of television broadcast equipment, including switchers, cameras, audio consoles, and media recorders
  • Provide complete and thorough detail for event reports and equipment status information according to established practices
  • Act as client facing liaison in the field; working closely with team in the office to ensure issues and opportunities are addressed.

Qualifications

  • Recognized post-secondary degree/diploma in Electronic Technology or equivalent
  • Minimum of 5 years technical television experience.  Experience as a mobile EIC preferred
  • Thorough knowledge of all types of broadcast equipment and expected performance
  • Effective communication skills and ability to work through difficulties
  • Understanding of remote sports and entertainment broadcasting
  • Knowledge of Networking Fundamentals, including LAN/WAN, VPN, Routing, Firewall and remote communication. Experience with AWS implementation and a working knowledge of core AWS products
  • Certification, experience and / or training related to Cloud & Service Delivery Frameworks (TOGAF, Microsoft, Amazon, eTOM, ITIL, etc.)
  • Must be detailed-orientated, multi-tasking, self-directed with proven ability to prioritize tasks and work well under pressure and deadlines

Requirements

  • Willing and able to travel throughout North America
  • Must hold a valid Canadian G-Class driver’s license
  • Required to work weekdays, evenings and/or weekends as assigned;  specifically evenings/overnight shifts
  • Ability to work in a dynamic and changing environment and deal with emergencies as they arise

Adequate knowledge of French is required for positions in Quebec.
