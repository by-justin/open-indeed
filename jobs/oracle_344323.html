Description

We are seeking a Product Manager to own the architecture, engineering, automation, and operational excellence of our hands-on labs program. This role builds the cloud environments and developer platforms that enable learners, customers, partners, and field teams to use complex technologies reliably at scale. You will lead the technical strategy for lab provisioning, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, source control, security, testing, release management, and cost efficiency. You will work across product, engineering, enablement, marketing, and field organizations to translate business and learning objectives into scalable technical solutions.

What You’ll Do

  • Own the technical strategy, architecture, and operational excellence of a high-impact hands-on labs platform.
  • Build and scale secure, reliable, and cost-efficient cloud lab environments using OCI, Terraform, Infrastructure as Code, and automation.
  • Establish engineering standards and delivery practices across source control, CI/CD, testing, security, observability, and release management.
  • Lead the development and operation of tools, services, APIs, and user-facing lab components.
  • Diagnose and resolve complex platform issues; lead incident response, root-cause analysis, and lasting improvements.
  • Partner across product, engineering, enablement, marketing, and field teams to translate learning and business needs into scalable solutions.
  • Influence technical direction across teams and mentor lab developers through hands-on guidance and standards.
