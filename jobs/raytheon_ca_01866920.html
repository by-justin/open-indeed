Launch Your Career with Raytheon ELCAN – Build the Future with US!

Now Hiring 15 Afternoon Shift Manufacturing Associates

Are you ready to make an impact in the defense and manufacturing industry? At Raytheon ELCAN, we offer more than just a job; we offer the opportunity to be part of a global team that is shaping the future of high-precision optical systems.

Our commitment to innovation, quality, and excellence spans over 70 years has earned us a reputation for delivering cutting-edge solutions for the most demanding challenges in the world. Join our dynamic team and contribute to projects that have a meaningful impact across the globe.

We are seeking 1 5 motivated individuals to join our afternoon shift team as Manufacturing Associates in our Assembly & Production departments . Help us create critical technologies that protect lives and strengthen national security. This is your chance to be part of something bigger while enjoying a role that fits your schedule.

Why Work with US?

  • Signing Bonus: Receive a signing bonus as a welcome to the Raytheon ELCAN team!
  • Shift Premium: Competitive pay rates with added $2.00/hour premium for afternoon shift hours.
  • Afternoon Shift Opportunity:  Enjoy a 4-day work week (Monday-Thursday, 10 hour shifts), Paid lunch and 2 breaks.
  • Work-Life Balance: Stable hours that allow you to balance work and personal time.
  • Comprehensive Benefits: Enjoy health, dental, vision, retirement plans and more.
  • Training & Growth : Build on your existing manufacturing experience with specialized on-the-job training to enhance your skills and advance your career.
  • Purpose-Driven Work: Be part of a collaborative team culture where your contributions make meaningful impact by protecting and connecting the world.

What You Will Do:

  • Assemble products with precision by interpreting blueprints, specifications, and workflow instructions.
  • Handle precision components, including optics, and operate calibrated tools, measuring equipment, and machinery.
  • Inspect parts and assemblies for quality, using strong attention to detail and visual acuity to identify imperfections.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work environment while following all safety protocols.
  • Perform basic computer tasks and data entry to support production tracking and ensure efficient processes.

What You Will Learn:

  • Advanced handling and assembly techniques for precision components, including optics.
  • How to interpret and utilize blueprints, technical drawings, and detailed specifications.
  • Proper operation of calibrated tools, measuring equipment, and manufacturing machinery.
  • Quality control processes to identify and address product imperfections effectively.
  • Best practices for maintaining a safe, organized, and efficient manufacturing workspace.

Qualifications You Must Have:

  • Ontario Secondary School Diploma or equivalent.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Basic computer literacy and ability to use technology effectively.
  • Understanding of technical issues and make sound decisions.
  • Comfortable and familiar working with the metric and imperial system.

Qualifications We Prefer:

  • Previous experience working in a manufacturing environment.
  • Demonstrated mechanical aptitude and hands-on skills.
  • Self-motivated with a consistently positive attitude.
  • Flexibility to work the afternoon shift and adapt to extended periods of standing or sitting.
  • Familiarity with maintaining safety and quality standards in a manufacturing setting.
