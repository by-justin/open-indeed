Job Title

MR Systems Architect

Job Description

Job Description

As a MR Systems Architect/Engineer, you will play a key role in driving system definition, design integration, risk management, and technical problem solving for Philips MR products. You will work across global and local R&D teams to translate system-level requirements into subsystem and component requirements, ensuring product quality, reliability, regulatory compliance, and successful product delivery.

You will serve as a critical technical interface between Global System Engineering, System Architects, and component development teams, contributing to the development of next-generation MR systems.

Key Responsibilities

System Engineering & Architecture

  • Analyze, define, and manage system and subsystem requirements.
  • Drive requirement decomposition from system level to subsystem and component level.
  • Support system architecture design and overall system integration activities.
  • Ensure component designs comply with system requirements and product objectives.
  • Evaluate system performance and reliability through data analysis and engineering methodologies.

Technical Leadership

  • Lead technical reviews, design reviews, and feasibility studies.
  • Provide technical guidance throughout the product development lifecycle.
  • Make design recommendations that improve product reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • Support concept exploration, system definition, design verification, issue investigation, and continuous improvement activities.

Risk & Quality Management

  • Lead FMEA and risk management activities.
  • Drive defect management and root cause analysis.
  • Investigate technical issues and coordinate corrective and preventive actions.
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and quality standards.

Cross-functional Collaboration

  • Collaborate closely with Global System Engineering teams, System Architects, PMO, subsystem teams, and component development teams.
  • Influence stakeholders and drive alignment across multiple functions and locations.
  • Facilitate effective communication and decision making throughout development programs.

Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in:Electrical EngineeringElectronic EngineeringMechanical EngineeringComputer ScienceBiomedical EngineeringOr related engineering disciplines.

Experience

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in MR Systems Engineering or related medical imaging product development.
  • Solid experience in system design, system integration, and product development.
  • Hands-on experience in risk management, technical problem solving, and reliability engineering.
  • Experience working in a global matrix organization is highly desirable.

Required Skills

  • Systems Engineering Methodology
  • System Design & Integration
  • Requirements Engineering
  • FMEA & Risk Management
  • Root Cause Analysis
  • Technical Leadership
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Cross-functional Collaboration
  • Strong Analytical & Problem-solving Skills
  • Ability to Influence and Drive Decisions Across Teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • Strong understanding of MR product architecture and technologies.
  • Experience conducting feasibility studies and system-level trade-off analysis.
  • DFSS (Design for Six Sigma) certification.
  • INCOSE CSEP certification or equivalent Systems Engineering qualification.
  • Knowledge of medical device regulations and quality processes.

Success Profile

The ideal candidate demonstrates:

  • Deep understanding of MR systems and system design methodology.
  • Strong capability in driving technical issues to resolution.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills.
  • Ability to balance technical depth with system-level thinking.
  • Passion for product quality, innovation, and continuous improvement.
