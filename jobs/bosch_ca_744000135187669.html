预防质量工程师Preventive quality engineer(太仓）

Company: Bosch Canada Location: Taicang, Jiangsu, China URL: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/BoschGroup/744000135187669--preventive-quality-engineer-

Company Description

Do you want beneficial technologies being shaped by your ideas? Whether in the areas of mobility solutions, consumer goods, industrial technology or energy and building technology - with us, you will have the chance to improve quality of life all across the globe. Welcome to Bosch.

Job Description

Plan and follow-up quality preventive plan and

implementation in the phase of new project development and SOP production, as

well as via product audit planning, ECR involvement, concession handling and

etc. to solidly prevent the quality problem occurred.

Attend the internal quality problem solving w/ 8D

Qualifications

1. Quality assurance during the new project developing.

新项目开发期间的质量保证

Attend the project meetings and activities actively according to PEP process requirements. Identify

the quality risk and support to set up the counter-measures w/ follow-up.

积极参加PEP（项目执行计划）项目会议和活动做好质量风险识别，并参与和支持应对措施建立和跟踪。

Clear understanding and passing the customer

quality requirements within the organization.

清晰理解客户质量要求，并将其传达给组织内部。

·

Handle new project process audit w/ OPL follow-up, and release for mass

production.

新项目过程审核，问题处理跟踪，达到批量生产要求。

·

Clear understanding PPAP submission requirement, arrange the ISIR sample

inspection. Collect required PPAP documents and submit to customer. Forward the

PSW release status to team.

清晰理解PPAP（生产件批准程序）的提交要求，安排ISIR（初次样品检查报告）样品检测。收集检查和提交客户要求的PPAP文件。并告知团队产品提交保证书的客户批准状态。

·

Ensure new project is derived from output and measurements based on

customer complaint 8D and internal problem solving result.

确保在新项目中导入已发生客户抱怨和内部质量问题的措施。

·

RBCC in house and Bosch plant parts PSW review and approval.

对长沙博世自制件及博世内部工厂的零件PSW的审查和批准。

2. Customer audit planning and coordination

计划和协调客户审核

· In time communicate either

internally and with customer to fix the audit date, to understand the audit purpose

and audit scope, which are acceptable by both side.

及时与客户（或内部）进行沟通，确定审核日期、理解审核目的和审核范围，并确保双方均能接受。

· Distribute detail requirements

from customer to the relevant departments. Support other department to prepare

before/during the audit.

发布详细的客户要求给相关部门。在审核之前和审核期间，支持其他部门进行审核准备。

· Organize the necessary relevant activities

with respect to the audit.

组织必要的关于审核的相应活动。

· Distribute the audit result

within the organization, follow up and summary corrective actions.

在组织内部发布审核结果，跟进和总结改进措施。

3. Product quality issue involvement

处理产品质量问题 ·

Work with team for internal deviation product handling w/ 8D approach.

和团队一起按照8D方法处理内部偏差产品。

·

Manage deviation from Bosch plant part, provide failure information and

necessary sample to respond plant, coordinate with the containment actions for

production, create QC in IQIS system to tracing the problem solving. Charge the

related cost to respond plant. Pushing plant to achieve the plant-plant quality

target.

管理博世工厂产品的偏差，给相关工厂提供失效信息和必要的样品，采取围堵措施调整生产，在IQIS系统创建QC对问题解决

进行跟踪。向相应工厂收取费用。敦促工厂达到工厂与工厂之间的质量指标。

·

Follow-up the blocked product status, push the problem solved according

to target setting (block pallet, block cycle time).

跟进封存产品的状态，推进问题解决，达到设定的封存托数和封存周期要求。

·

Support to in time solve the quality issues which impacted delivery.

对解决影响交货的质量问题给予及时支持。

4. Product Audit planning

计划产品审核

·

Verify and plan for test equipment and tools/gauge availability and

capacity during the phase of new project developing, Define the test equipment

specification and requirement. Support the new equipment acquisition process.

And BP yearly.

在新项目开发阶段，检验测试设备及测量仪器的适用性及性能，定义测试设备规格和要求。支持新设备购买流程。

每年制定BP（预算计划）。

·

Deal with audit plan, check list and inspection instruction for A, Q and

Z test, periodically evaluate the necessity of A test, increase or reduce the

test items and frequency accordingly.

安排A/Q/Z检验的审核计划、检查表和检查操作指南，定期评估A检的必要性，相应地增加或减少测试项目和频率。

·

Follow-up nonconformity report found in product audit, and closure in

time.

跟进产品审核中发现的问题报告，并及时关闭。

approach.

在新项目开发、批量生产阶段，计划并跟进质量预防计划和实施；同时也通过规划产品审核、工程变更管理、让步管控等，有效预防问题的发生。

使用8D的方法参与内部质量问题的解决。
