Flexitank General Worker

Would you like to be part of Trans Ocean Liquid Technologies (TOLT), previously Trans Ocean Distribution (TOD). A company focused on in-house Flexitank manufacturing known for its RhinoBulk® and VinBulk® products as well as Vinliner manufacturing known for its 20ft and 40ft dry bulk solutions, ensuring quality control from raw materials to final product. As a global producer, TOLT emphasizes rigorous design, manufacturing, and quality standards. The company aims to expand its engineering and manufacturing capabilities into other related product areas. Would you like to be part of a business that connects people across the globe and want to make a difference? Then waste no time and join our amazing team at Trans Ocean Liquid Technologies South Africa.

Trans Ocean Liquid Technologies has an opening for a Flexitank General Worker in Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa. Join us in connecting people and improving lives

Job Purpose: Support in manufacturing of Flexitanks. Produce each article of the right quality, material handling, ground activities including movement, storage and packaging of materials. Adhere corporate guidelines and policies.

In this   Flexitank General Worker position

  • Achieve hourly targets
  • Report all material defects to QCS and Senior Supervisor
  • Ensure that each product manufactured are of correct quality
  • Complete cutting and roll sheets in department
  • Ensure that any foreign objects that may enter the production floor are reported.
  • Ensure housekeeping is conducted in his department
  • To verify tank number according to job sheet
  • Conduct visual inspections for material quality, correct numbers and alignment
  • Ensuring to report malfunction machinery to station or maintenance supervisor
  • Ensuring product are manufactured in excellence
  • Ensure that I report any hazard from a health safety perspective
  • To report to superior on all issue related to repair works
  • To update job sheet with respect to individual job done
  • Comply with procedures of ISO9001, FSSC22000, OSHAS 18001 and ISO14001
  • To take all necessary action to achieve company’s objective
  • Ensuring tanks are cut/trimmed as per quality specification
  • Competent in the following processes cutting, wedge welding, gland welding, sleeving, and folding

Now, here is what we need from you!

  • Outstanding time-management abilities
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Strong ethics and reliability
  • Education: Vocational/Specialized/Technical/Certification
  • Must have matric
  • Experience: Less than 2 year of experience in working in a warehouse environment

Why join Trans Ocean Liquid Technologies South Africa

We are part of Deutsche Post DHL, the world’s leading logistics provider with operations in over 220 countries. Visit our career site on the web at  https://www.logistics.dhl/us-en/home/careers.html

Key Aspects of TOLT's Flexitank & Vinliner Manufacturing

  • In-House Expertise: TOLT designs and builds its Flexitank and Vinliner equipment in-house, maintaining control over the entire manufacturing process.
  • Rigorous Standards: The manufacturing process adheres to strict quality standards, covering everything from raw material sourcing to product completion and delivery.
  • Market Leadership: TOLT aims at being the world's largest producer of Flexitanks and Vinliners with its products approved by bodies like the American FDA.
  • Brand Focus: The company primarily focuses on its proven, RhinoBulk®, VinBulk®, 20ft and 40ft equipment.
  • Strategic Expansion: TOLT is looking to leverage its engineering and manufacturing expertise to expand into other relevant product areas.
  • Manufacturing and Quality Control.
  • Self-Sufficiency: TOLT's strategy includes being self-sufficient in its supply of Flexitanks and Vinliners, a trademark characteristic that sets it apart in the market.
