ポジション概要

Autodesk Consultingでは、 Design for Manufacturing and Assembly（DfMA） および Industrialized Construction（IC） 戦略の実行・推進において確かな実績を持つ、ダイナミックで先進的な思考を持つ ビジネスストラテジーコンサルタント を募集しています。 理想的な候補者は、DfMA／ICにおけるグローバルなトレンドへの深い理解、戦略的思考力、そしてビジョンを具体的な実行計画に落とし込む経験をお持ちの方です。

このポジションでは、業界全体および新興テクノロジー分野における幅広い専門知識を活かし、エグゼクティブ層のクライアントと密接に連携しながら、デジタルトランスフォーメーション戦略の共同策定、価値指標の定義、現状評価、そして実行可能なソリューションの立案を行います。 本ポジションは東京拠点で、Autodeskが建設・製造業のイノベーションを牽引する取り組みの中核を担う貴重な機会です。

Position Overview

Autodesk Consulting is seeking a dynamic, forward-thinking Business Strategy Consultant with a proven track record in executing and enabling Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DfMA) and Industrialized Construction (IC) strategies. The ideal candidate will bring deep insight into global trends in DfMA/IC, a strategic mindset, and experience in turning vision into execution.

You are a seasoned strategic thinker with cross-functional expertise in industry domains and emerging technologies. You possess the ability to work closely with executive-level clients to co-develop digital transformation strategies, define value metrics, assess current performance, and shape implementable solutions that drive measurable business outcomes. This is a Tokyo-based role that offers the opportunity to be a key part of Autodesk’s efforts to lead innovation in construction and manufacturing.

Responsibilities

  • Advise clients on strategic transformation initiatives, including supply chain integration, digitalization, Industry 4.0, enterprise asset management, BIM, cloud platforms, AI/ML, and related innovation programs
  • Translate visionary ideas and market trends into measurable business outcomes
  • Collaborate closely with executive and senior business leaders to clarify and prioritize strategic goals
  • Work cross-functionally with solution architects, consultants, and Autodesk stakeholders to propose optimal approaches and deliver customer-ready solutions
  • Contribute to the creation, review, and management of high-quality deliverables such as proposals, business assessments, recommendations, workflow documentation, and process analysis
  • Support project scoping, estimation, work breakdown structuring, and delivery methodology selection
  • Facilitate effective decision-making and resolution of complex business/technical issues
  • Support sales pursuits and identify additional product/service opportunities within client organizations
  • Help establish Autodesk as a thought leader by developing and promoting compelling insights in the DfMA/IC space
  • Willingness to travel up to 50% as required

Minimum Qualifications

  • Business-level proficiency in both Japanese and English, with excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Master’s degree or equivalent in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or Process Engineering
  • Minimum of 5 years of industry experience, preferably in construction, manufacturing, or industrial consulting
  • Deep process knowledge of Autodesk and/or competitor products such as ACC, Inventor, Factory Design Suite, Fusion 360, Dynamo, BIM 360, and Revit etc
  • Strong facilitation skills for both in-person and remote sessions, including conflict resolution and stakeholder alignment
  • Ability to clearly articulate complex technical concepts and build influence with stakeholders
  • Skilled in defining practical, value-driven solutions grounded in both product and domain knowledge
  • Proven collaborator and team player, able to support and work effectively across shared responsibility teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • In-depth knowledge and practical experience with BIM 360
  • Demonstrated experience and leadership in DfMA and IC implementation
  • Familiarity with global DfMA/IC trends and innovations, and how they apply in the Japanese market
  • Strong vision development and strategic planning experience
  • A mindset focused on “how we can make it work,” with a bias for execution
  • A leadership attitude aligned with Autodesk’s mission to lead the industry transformation in IC and digital construction
