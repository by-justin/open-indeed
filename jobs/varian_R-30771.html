Job Title: Technical Product Owner – Healthcare IoT & Connected Solutions

Role Overview

We are seeking a Technical Product Owner to drive the development and delivery of connected healthcare solutions, working closely with Solution Architects, engineering, and customer service teams. This role bridges business, clinical operations, and engineering—owning product direction while translating real-world healthcare service needs into scalable, secure, and usable solutions.

Although this position is listed as a UK-based role, we welcome applications from candidates located elsewhere in Europe and will consider suitably qualified applicants across the region.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own the product roadmap and backlog for healthcare IoT and connected device solutions
  • Translate customer service workflows and field operations needs into product features and technical requirements
  • Work closely with Solution Architects to align product vision with system architecture
  • Define user stories, acceptance criteria, and technical requirements for engineering teams
  • Prioritize features based on business value, operational impact, and customer experience
  • Drive development of solutions supporting device monitoring, diagnostics, remote support, and service automation
  • Collaborate with customer service, support, and field teams to improve issue resolution and service efficiency
  • Ensure compliance with HIPAA and HITECH Act in product design
  • Participate in Agile ceremonies (sprint planning, backlog grooming, retrospectives)
  • Validate delivered features through UAT, field feedback, and operational metrics
  • Drive integration with EHR/EMR systems, service platforms, and IoT ecosystems
  • Partner with DevOps teams to ensure smooth releases, monitoring, and continuous improvement
  • Track and improve KPIs such as uptime, response time, MTTR (mean time to resolution), and customer satisfaction

Required Skills & Experience

  • 5–10 years of experience in product ownership, product management, or technical program delivery
  • Strong understanding of healthcare customer service operations (support workflows, ticketing systems, field service)
  • Hands-on experience or strong understanding of IoT / connected devices and edge-to-cloud architectures
  • Technical background in software development or engineering (ability to work closely with developers and understand trade-offs)
  • Experience working with APIs, microservices, and distributed systems
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies and backlog management tools (Jira, Azure DevOps)
  • Understanding of NoSQL data stores (e.g., Elasticsearch) and data-driven applications
  • Strong ability to translate business needs into technical requirements

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in healthcare environments (hospital systems, medical devices, digital health platforms)
  • Familiarity with healthcare interoperability standards (HL7, FHIR, DICOM)
  • Experience with service management platforms (ServiceNow, Salesforce Health Cloud, etc.)
  • Exposure to DevOps practices (CI/CD, monitoring, release management)
  • Experience with remote device management, telemetry, and diagnostics systems
  • Background in AI/analytics use cases (predictive maintenance, support automation, knowledge systems)

Key Competencies

  • Strong understanding of customer service workflows in healthcare environments
  • Ability to balance technical depth with product and user experience focus
  • Comfortable working in ambiguous environments and driving clarity
  • Excellent stakeholder management across engineering, operations, and clinical teams
  • Data-driven decision-making with focus on operational efficiency and customer outcomes
  • Ownership mindset with ability to drive product from concept to delivery

Why join us?

This is an opportunity to work on high-impact data initiatives that support a global healthcare business. You'll play a key role in building the foundations for advanced analytics, self-service reporting and AI-driven decision-making while working with modern cloud technologies and a highly collaborative team.

If you're passionate Technical Product Owner who thrives when driving the development and delivery of connected healthcare solutions, we'd love to hear from you.
