Job Description

Standard Job Description

The successful candidate will be called to work at Lockheed Martin Canada Inc. (Lockheed Martin) in Esquimalt and ships within His Majesty Canadian (HMC) Dockyards once the necessary security clearances have been granted by the Government of Canada (CANADA).  The candidate will work on site at the Lockheed Martin, Victoria, BC.

The candidate will be involved in various In-Service Support (ISS) Programs, including installation, integration, planned and preventive maintenance and field troubleshooting for systems and capabilities of a highly complex naval combat system.

The candidate will function in a multi-disciplined team environment, participate in the installation and maintenance of the Halifax class common shipboard equipment, software, and participate in all appropriate engineering activities including but not limited to:

  • Performing preventive and corrective maintenance, troubleshooting, and repairing commercial and military equipment
  • Provide technical support for equipment and software installations
  • Providing multilevel technical support for integration of cable and equipment configuration
  • Provide user support to development, training, and test teams
  • Provide technical support to our customer
  • Coordinate work and provide support to subcontractors
  • Liaise with Fleet Maintenance Facility Cape Breton (FMFCB) and Subcontractors for the coordination of Lockheed Martin scope of work
  • Recording and tracking issues (equipment failure trends, etc.) that may affect operational performance and efficiency
  • Assisting members of the engineering staff in maintaining detailed configuration documentation applicable to equipment and cable configurations, firmware settings, equipment utilization metrics, equipment status, etc.
  • Assisting members of the engineering staff in conducting Technical Investigation Reports and Engineering Change (EC) documentation.
  • Assisting in the procurement, delivery, installation, and testing of new equipment configurations
  • Provide technical support for life cycle initiatives.

Basic Qualifications

  • Completion of a 2-year college diploma in Electronic Technology or Electronic Technician (ET)
  • 5 + years of experience working in a related field
  • Experience with MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Strong working knowledge of electronic and electrical test equipment
  • Strong troubleshooting abilities
  • Experience in handling technical aspects of maintenance repairs
  • Aptitude for providing technical guidance and solving challenging problems
  • Works well individually or in a team environment
  • Experience in leading a multi-disciplinary team through technically complex projects
  • Experience in project planning
  • Willing to support after hour work when required
  • Able to communicate effectively with technical and non-technical members of the engineering/management staff and personnel assigned to related work
  • Technical Knowledge of Halifax class Combat Systems and Harry DeWolf class Navigation/Combat Systems.

Desired Skills

  • Experience with Military Naval domain
  • Experience with Sensors, Weapons or Ship integration
  • Experience with Linux Programming
  • Experience with Computer Networking
  • Experience with Fiber Optics
  • Familiarity with Dynamic Object-Oriented Requirements System (DOORS) or Windchill.
