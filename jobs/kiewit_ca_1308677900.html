Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with project managers, engineers, designers, procurement professionals, construction managers, subcontractors, and project services personnel to develop and maintain comprehensive project schedules from conceptual planning through design, construction, commissioning, and operational startup.

  • Utilize Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) scheduling principles, terminology, and methodologies to develop critical path method (CPM) schedules and logic networks that support project planning, execution, and forecasting.

  • Apply historical performance data and industry best practices to develop realistic schedules, resource plans, and progress forecasts.

  • Ensure all scheduling activities comply with site-specific Earned Value Management System (EVMS) requirements and certified project controls processes.

  • Analyze schedule data to identify trends, risks, constraints, and potential impacts to project milestones, and develop recommendations for corrective actions.

  • Collect, validate, and integrate schedule inputs from engineering, procurement, construction, startup, and project controls teams to ensure accuracy and alignment across the project.

  • Facilitate coordination meetings with project stakeholders to communicate schedule status, resolve conflicts, and ensure understanding of project schedule requirements and objectives.

  • Develop and maintain integrated master schedules that incorporate all project activities into a single, cohesive timeline.

  • Monitor project progress, evaluate schedule performance, and provide timely reporting and analysis to project and leadership teams.

  • Manage and implement schedule change control processes, ensuring all revisions are properly documented, evaluated, and incorporated while maintaining schedule integrity.

  • Support project risk management efforts by identifying schedule-related risks and opportunities and assisting in the development of mitigation and recovery plans.

  • Prepare and present schedule updates, recovery plans, progress reports, and schedule performance metrics to project teams, clients, and management.
