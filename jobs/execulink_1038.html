Job Description

About this Opportunity: If you thrive on solving complex problems and delivering a great customer experience, make a real impact as a Technical Specialist. You’ll troubleshoot and resolve escalated issues across internet, mobility, and advanced systems, while supporting customers through phone, callback, email, and chat channels. You’ll work in a fast-paced environment where accuracy, urgency, and clear communication matter, and where your ability to diagnose root causes and guide customers through solutions will be valued every day.

This role also offers strong exposure to a wide range of technologies and cross-functional work. You’ll collaborate with internal teams, outside technicians, and last mile providers to drive resolution, monitor network and abuse-related alerts, support high-priority requests, and contribute to continuous service improvement. It’s a great opportunity for someone who wants to deepen their technical expertise, build confidence in complex troubleshooting, and grow in an environment that values learning, ownership, and customer care.
