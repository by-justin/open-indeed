General Role Purpose (brief description of this job)

Senior Metallurgist Accounting & Strategic Planning provides valuable information about plant operation, recovery rates, mass and energy balances, and areas of material gains and losses.  It is a continuous process required to make necessary operational and metallurgical-based decisions as well as to effectively communicate Key Performance Indicators.  This role requires the development and training of the national direct reports and technical leadership to provide consistent, accurate (short and medium term) reports, forecasts, and associated reconciled to key stakeholders.

As a key member of the process team, the Senior Metallurgist – Accounting and Strategic Planning is responsible for the effective and efficient generation of all short & medium term production planning and forecasting works, including associated reconciliations.   These reports relate to the process operation and involve the balancing of critical process streams from crusher feed to concentrate shipments.  Duties also include (but are not limited to): power consumption and forecasting, integration of other key variables or parameters as required into the metallurgical accounting system and database.  In addition, manage the required critical plant sampling for monthly reconciliations as well as support internal QA/QC, as well as communicating/present performance reports to senior management and other duties as required and specified by their Manager.

Qualifications (required for the job) & Knowledge & Experience

  • A Tertiary Qualification in Extractive Metallurgy, Mineral Processing, Chemical Engineering or related field.
  • A minimum of 8 years of progressively relevant work experience in base metal processing facilities
  • A strong commitment to safety leadership and the ability to follow relevant policies and procedures
  • Strong metallurgical experience with the ability to effectively resolve complex technical problems, and utilize and create specialized computer applications (multi-element mass balancing, modelling and simulation, statistical processing, etc.)
  • Strong time-management, organizational, reporting, and presentation skills with the ability to multitask and prioritize tasks effectively.  Ability to set schedules, milestones, deadlines, and achieve them
  • Previous experience and highly skilled in metallurgical accounting, material balancing software, and database management systems
  • Excellent analytical skills for identifying process shortfalls, bottlenecks, and implementing systematic solutions.
  • Understanding of complex metallurgical information, process improvement, and control strategies for large-scale mineral processing plants.
  • Ability to provide clear and accurate operational performance reports in both an upward and downward direction.
  • English/Spanish verbal and written communication skills
  • Ability to assemble data and provide clear and accurate operational performance reports in both an upward and downward direction.
  • Computer fluency is essential, especially with Microsoft Office-based programs and data analysis software (MS Excel, Power BI, Minitab, etc.
  • Ability to work in a multi-cultural environment, including collaboration with other technical departments to ensure that Cobre Panama is prepared for future processing requirements

Core Specific Responsibilities & Expected Deliverables

  • Operate within and observe all relevant and applicable laws of Panama
  • Maintain HSE standards and ensure strict compliance within the Process Plant & Metallurgy Department
  • Deliver accurate and representative production planning:  Annual Budget, 5-year & LOM plan, and any other medium to long-term forecasts as required (i.e. 2+10, 5+7, 7+5 and 10+2)
  • Integration of new equipment (screening plant, Moly Circuit) into all necessary short and medium-term material balancing & associated production reports
  • Liaise with and provide technical support to other operational departments (mining, geology, finance) to generate accurate and representative production plans
  • Train and develop colleagues in short-term and long-term accounting and production planning
  • Develop SOP’s for all necessary production planning for effective knowledge transfer
  • Execute plant surveys, material balancing campaigns to evaluate circuit performance, and recommend ways of improving existing balancing and accounting systems.
  • Conduct troubleshooting and analysis to identify root causes and corrective actions to improve production planning.
  • Perform other related duties as required.
