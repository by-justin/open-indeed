Job Description:

Airbus Commercial Aircraft is looking for a Veteran Outreach Manager  to join our Talent Acquisition team.

This position is open to the following locations: Mobile, Alabama or Kinston, NC.

The Veteran Outreach Manager will manage and execute initiatives to source, recruit, and support transitioning service members, veterans, and military spouses, with the primary objective of creating and managing sustainable talent pipelines. This role will serve as a key point of contact for military transition programs, including DoD SkillBridge, and will coordinate efforts at military job fairs and career events. The Manager will be the designated company representative for select military base offices which support the transition of military members to civilian employment. The ideal candidate will have strong knowledge of military culture and transition processes and a proven ability to build partnerships with military installations and veteran organizations.

Meet the Team:

Working in our Human Resources department, it’s our mission to help support Airbus’ greatest assets: our team members. Our team works cross-departmentally as well as with our global counterparts to ensure we are making a positive impact on all aspects of our employee experience.

Your Working Environment:

On ‘Airbus Way’ you will find the Airbus U.S. Manufacturing Facility for commercial aircraft. Opened at the Mobile Aeroplex at Brookley in 2015, the facility produces A320 Family aircraft and added a second product line for the A220 in 2019. The facility, which strengthens the U.S. aerospace industry, is part of Airbus’ strategy to enhance its global competitiveness by meeting the growing needs of its customers in the United States and beyond.

Your Challenges:

Military WFD Strategy Leadership:

  • Work with the Director of WFD to design and lead the implementation of military and veteran talent strategies to enable regional growth, grow service member and veteran talent pipelines, and drive alignment with business objectives and long-term workforce planning needs.
  • Oversee the lifecycle of military and veteran-focused programs (including SkillBridge, mentorship, and veteran internship programs) from concept development to execution and performance review.
  • Lead the identification and development of Military Outreach initiatives in new regions and sites, including the establishment of new military base partnerships and talent pipeline strategies.
  • Work closely with HR, Talent Acquisition, Employee Resource Groups, and Business Leadership teams to ensure military talent programs align with overall business goals, growth projections, and regional expansion efforts.

Strategic Partnership Development:

  • Develop and expand military talent and transition programs (e.g., DoD SkillBridge) and partnerships across U.S. sites, focusing on building robust career pathways for service members that support critical roles, including technical, skilled trades, and leadership positions.
  • Partner with the HR and Business leaders across North America to advise on best practices for military and veteran talent acquisition and retention, ensuring programs align with regional needs.
  • Build and strengthen relationships with key military and veteran channels, including military transition offices, veteran service organizations (VSOs), base education centers, and military credentialing programs.
  • Direct, guide, and manage a network of local and regional associates who provide coordination and support for military recruitment and veteran-affiliated programs.

Veteran Talent Pipeline Development

  • Lead community outreach efforts within the military ecosystem (e.g., base events, family support groups) to promote career pathways and inspire future veteran talent into technical and professional careers at Airbus.
  • Create and manage military talent pipelines at all levels—from SkillBridge internships and entry-level roles through to experienced professionals and leadership positions.
  • Represent the company at military job fairs, transition summits, and career expos across the US Region.
  • Coordinate logistics and staffing for recruiting events targeting military talent.

Program Management:

  • Analyze military hiring data, veteran retention trends, and program outcomes (e.g., SkillBridge completion rates) to identify skills gaps and future talent needs, utilizing insights to inform program development and drive continuous improvement.
  • Ensure all military and veteran talent programs, particularly the DoD SkillBridge Program, comply with all applicable Department of Defense guidelines and veteran employment laws and regulations in North America.
  • Act as the primary liaison for the DoD SkillBridge Program, ensuring compliance with Department of Defense guidelines.
  • Develop internal processes for onboarding SkillBridge candidates and tracking their progress.
  • Other duties as assigned
