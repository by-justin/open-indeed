Job Description

At Saputo, we bring good to the table by making high-quality products, investing in our people, and supporting communities around the world . As a top 10 global dairy processor, we value contributions that matter and strive to foster an inclusive, growth-driven work environment. Ready to bring your best?

Job Description Summary: The Production Clerk actively supports manufacturing and/or production process by coordinating tasks, tracking materials, and maintaining records to facilitate efficient production flow.

How you will make contributions that matter … •Perform accurate data entry and reporting of ingredients, raw material usage, and inventories into the ERP system to support usage and inventory integrity on a daily basis •Support inventory control activities - frequency can include daily weekly, monthly, fiscal quarter-end and fiscal year-end. •Review, analyze and resolve material inventory variances in the Enterprise Control Component (ECC) in support of receiving, mix/blend and filling operations. •Investigate and communicate material usage inventory discrepancies with leadership team. •Monitor system functionality, resolve transactions and data entry errors or incidents to prevent production delays. •By production line, record daily production volume and product activity and validate OEE in support of both daily and weekly production plan •Help to identify projects based on data collected from shift notes and downtime logs •Assist production leadership in developing training materials, coaching, updating, implementing, and maintaining procedures •Use a variety of data analysis and SAP tools to discover the most impactful insights •Perform typical administrative duties: filing, faxing, scanning, emailing, report writing, and other clerical duties. •Support internal/external audits. •Perform good housekeeping within established housekeeping guidelines. •Adhere to the Saputo code of ethics and actively apply the Saputo values. •Work together professionally in a team environment with coworkers and other department personnel. •Monitor deliverables and ensure timely completion of projects •Other duties as required by management

You are best suited for the role if you have the following experience, skills, and qualifications … •Ability to prioritize tasks, manage deadlines, and maintain organized records •Accurate data entry and record-keeping are crucial •Effective written and verbal communication skills for interacting with team members and other departments •Proficiency in using computer software for data entry, record-keeping, and communication (SAP/DMS) •Ability to identify and resolve issues related to production, inventory, or scheduling •Ability to obtain and maintain a forklift certification, if applicable •Demonstrated work history •A working knowledge of food manufacturing acronyms (SQF, HACCP, SOP, 5S, GMP, etc.) •Good attendance is a must

We support and care for our employees by providing them with … •Development opportunities that enhance your career fulfillment •Meaning compensation & benefits that help you care for your family •Opportunities to contribute to your community and enhance the lives of others through Saputo products

Physical Demands • Periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer • Ability to work for periods of time in a manufacturing environment which includes but not limited to noise, changing temperatures (hot, wet, cold, humid), standing/walking/bending/stooping/climbing stairs, maneuvering around manufacturing equipment consistently throughout the shift • Physical lifting up to 40lbs unassisted

In the USA, Saputo ranks among the top three cheese producers and is one of the top producers of extended shelf-life and cultured dairy products, with a portfolio of well-loved brands such as Stella, Frigo Cheese, Montchevre, Stella, and Treasure Cave. We also boast a strong foodservice presence, partnering with reputable chain restaurants and broadline distributors.

Whether your expertise lies in manufacturing, operations, supply chain, sales, quality assurance, or in any other function, your role is integral to our success. You will make contributions that matter, all while working alongside colleagues who genuinely care about your success and who will roll up their sleeves to help.
