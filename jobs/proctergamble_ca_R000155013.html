Job Description

Are you a final-year student or fresh graduate in a STEM field and ready to explore a management career at an international manufacturing company? P&G’s 12-month traineeship program is designed for talents from all STEM backgrounds who want to build their future in management. During the program, you can work on real manufacturing projects, gain hands-on experience in a world-class production environment, and lay the foundation for your future engineering or production management career. 🚀

🌍 ABOUT US: At Procter & Gamble, we have been working for more than 180 years to make everyday life easier for millions of people around the world through our trusted and well-known consumer brands. As a global company, we are present in around 70 countries, and our portfolio includes internationally recognized brands such as Always®, Ariel®, Gillette®, Head & Shoulders®, Pampers®, Pantene®, Tampax®, Naturella® and Discreet®. Our Budapest Plant in Csömör has been part of P&G’s international manufacturing network since 1991. In our facility, we produce feminine hygiene products on modern, automated production lines, including products related to the Always®, Naturella®, Discreet® and Tampax® brands. In our work, we place strong emphasis on safety, quality, collaboration and continuous improvement. Our manufacturing processes are supported by automated technologies, technical systems and development projects. Our warehouse and material handling teams ensure that the necessary raw materials reach the right areas on time and safely. We work in an environment where safety, quality and continuous improvement are at the center of everything we do.

📋 WHAT YOU WILL DO:

  • Supporting, simplifying, and developing manufacturing processes
  • Understanding LEAN-based operational processes
  • Supporting waste elimination projects
  • Coordinating business and savings projects
  • Supporting the development of technical solutions
  • Collaborating with experts, partners, and cross-functional teams
  • Developing engineering and leadership skills

🔑 WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

  • Final year of Engineering, Technical Manager, or STEM BSc/MSc studies, or a degree obtained in the last 12 months
  • Availability for 24–40 hours per week
  • Proficient in Hungarian and English
  • Leadership potential and good communication skills

➕ NICE TO HAVE:

  • Flexibility
  • Teamwork and autonomy
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office

About Open Indeed
