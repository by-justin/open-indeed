Job title : internship 6 months – data science

●         Location: Paris 16e

●         Starting : January 2027

About the job

As an intern within our digital & data department, you will help develop marketing mix modeling and marketing budget allocation optimization tools, which will help us make data-driven decisions about our marketing spend across multiple channels. Ready to get started?

Ready to grow your skills while helping shape the future of healthcare? At Sanofi, you’ll be empowered to learn, ask questions, and bring your ideas to life – all while supported by inspiring mentors and collaborative teams.

About Sanofi:

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main responsibilities:

  • Participate in applying statistical methods and machine learning techniques to extract insights from datasets
  • Take part in implementing and fine-tuning AI and ML models, using especially Bayesian inference algorithms
  • Help present findings through clear and informative charts, graphs, and dashboards
  • Collaborate with data engineers, software engineers, product owners and marketing stakeholders
  • Contribute to creative approaches, developing innovative data-driven solutions, exploring new methodologies and thinking outside the box to tackle complex challenges

About you

  • Soft and technical skills : curiosity, communication, analytical mindset. Knowledge in supervised and unsupervised learning , in deep learning, and in LLMs,  experience with Python programming for data science and with big data concepts, basic understanding of database systems
  • Education : Master’s degree in mathematics, computer science, engineering, physics or statistics
  • Languages : english fluent
