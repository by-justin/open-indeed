Job Description

Overview

Due to an expanding project portfolio, we are seeking an exceptional Chief Geotechnical Engineer to lead our technical capability in dams, reservoirs and embankment engineering. This is a high-profile leadership role for a recognised industry expert who can provide strategic direction, technical assurance and thought leadership across a diverse portfolio of complex dam and reservoir projects. Some of our flagship projects include Fens, Lincs, Broad Oak and Havant Thicket reservoirs.

As the organisation's technical authority for dam and reservoir engineering, you will support major infrastructure programmes throughout the UK and internationally. Working closely with multidisciplinary teams, clients, regulators and industry stakeholders, you will play a pivotal role in advancing dam safety, resilience and sustainable water infrastructure.

This role offers a unique opportunity to shape technical standards, influence industry best practice, drive business growth and mentor the next generation of dam and geotechnical engineering professionals.

Success in this role will be measured through:

  • Maintaining the highest standards of technical quality and dam safety.
  • Strengthening technical governance and reducing technical risk.
  • Delivering business growth, profitability and market expansion.
  • Building strong relationships with clients, regulators and industry stakeholders.
  • Developing high-performing engineering teams and future technical leaders.
  • Enhancing the organisation's reputation as a trusted industry leader in dam safety and reservoir resilience.

Your role

  • As Chief Geotechnical Engineer, you will provide leadership across technical excellence, business growth, project delivery, client engagement and capability development.
  • Technical Leadership & Governance
  • Act as the technical authority for dam and reservoir geotechnical engineering across the business.
  • Provide expert oversight and technical assurance for major dam safety, rehabilitation and new-build projects.
  • Lead the development and implementation of technical standards, guidance and engineering best practice.
  • Act as Independent Technical Reviewer and Approver for complex geotechnical and dam engineering solutions.
  • Provide specialist advice on: Dam safety and risk managementSeepage control and seepage analysisInternal erosion and pipingStability and deformation assessmentSeismic performanceReservoir asset managementChampion innovation, digital engineering and advanced analytical techniques.Promote collaboration across geotechnical, civil, hydraulic, environmental and asset management disciplines.

  • Identify emerging market trends, regulatory developments and growth opportunities.

  • Establish business objectives, performance metrics and growth targets.
  • Lead strategic engagement with clients, regulators, asset owners and industry stakeholders.

Project Delivery & Client Leadership

Dam Engineering Scope

You will provide leadership in the design, assessment and rehabilitation of:

  • Earth embankment dams.
  • Reservoir embankments.
  • Flood defence embankments and levees.
  • Dam raising and modification schemes.
  • Seepage control measures.
  • Filters and drainage systems.
  • Foundation treatment and improvement works.
  • Instrumentation, monitoring and performance assessment systems.
