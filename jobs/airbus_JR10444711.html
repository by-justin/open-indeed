About AHUK...

Airbus Helicopters is the world’s largest helicopter manufacturer and the company’s site at Oxford Airport is Britain’s civil helicopter hub.

AHUK offers considerable experience in helicopter sales, bespoke completions, maintenance and spares support.  Its in-house engineering capabilities include design, customisation, installation, flight-testing and certification, as well as technical support, maintenance, training and the supply of spares for civil and military applications.

AHUK is the MoD’s chosen provider of helicopters and maintenance for its No.1 Helicopter Flying school (referred to as MFTS) located in RAF Shawbury (Shropshire) and RAF Valley (Anglesey).

The Opportunity

  • UK CAA B2 Licensed Aircraft Engineer at RAF Shawbury, Shropshire.
  • Reporting to the Line Maintenance Manager, to support the MFTS operation.
  • Responsible for the delivery of serviceable aircraft to meet the requirements of the daily flying programme.
  • Ensuring compliance with Aviation regulations and Company procedures.
  • Accomplishment of Maintenance inspections and repairs as directed by Maintenance Supervisors.
  • Complete maintenance to a high standard to ensure continued safe operation of the aircraft.
  • Completion of maintenance, including electrical systems and avionics systems.
  • Supervision of maintenance fitters and the certification of their work.
  • Assist in day to day running of the Hangar, including aircraft movement and general housekeeping responsibilities.

Knowledge and Skills

  • Working knowledge of UK CAA regulations including, but not limited to Part 145, Part CAMO and UK ANO
  • Thorough knowledge of electrical and avionics systems
  • Experience in an Aviation engineering environment
  • Ability to work as part of a team
  • Self-motivated and ability to work under own initiative
  • Strong communication, organisational and leadership skills
  • Flexible to the requirements of the company and needs of the customer
  • Operational resilience and collaborative problem-solving

Fitter-to-LAE Pathway

Aircraft Avionics Technicians who are studying towards their UK CAA Part 66 B2 Licence who have completed at least 6 modules and have more than 3 years’ experience documented in their experience logbooks are also encouraged to apply.

Successful candidates will be enrolled onto the AHUK Fitter to LAE Development scheme.
