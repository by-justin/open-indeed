About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Agentforce Technical Manager to join our Global Delivery Centre (GDC) This role blends people leadership, technical architecture, and delivery ownership to scale Agentforce capabilities across global customer implementations. You will lead teams building intelligent AI agents, copilots, and autonomous workflows while defining architecture standards, reusable frameworks, and enterprise deployment patterns.

Your Impact

Technical Architecture Leadership

  • Architect scalable, enterprise-grade Agentforce solutions.
  • Design intelligent agents leveraging LLMs, prompt engineering, and orchestration frameworks.
  • Define reusable patterns for agent skills, guardrails, and multi-agent collaboration.
  • Lead integrations across Salesforce Clouds, Data Cloud, MuleSoft, and external systems.
  • Establish RAG, knowledge grounding, and document ingestion architectures.
  • Drive performance optimization, monitoring, and telemetry frameworks.

Agentforce Capability Development

  • Lead end-to-end lifecycle of Agentforce implementations.
  • Drive agent design, prompt strategies, and skill orchestration.
  • Build reusable accelerators and deployment frameworks.
  • Partner with Product and COE teams on pilots and innovation programs.

People Management & Team Leadership

  • Manage and mentor a team of Agentforce engineers and architects.
  • Drive hiring, onboarding, and capability development.
  • Conduct performance reviews and career coaching.
  • Build high-performing pods aligned to delivery programs.
  • Foster a culture of innovation, learning, and technical excellence.

Delivery & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Own end-to-end delivery for Agentforce initiatives.
  • Partner with Product Owners, Architects, and Customer Success teams.
  • Manage risks, dependencies, and execution roadmaps.
  • Support solutioning, estimations, and POD planning.

Governance & Best Practices

  • Define AI governance and guardrail frameworks.
  • Establish DevOps, CI/CD, and release standards.
  • Ensure compliance with Responsible AI and data security policies.
