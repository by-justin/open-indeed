About the Role :

The Platform API team owns the sensor infrastructure that every CrowdStrike endpoint depends on: secure network communications, cryptographic operations, content delivery, and secure local data storage. These are foundational runtime components that execute on every protected endpoint across Windows, Linux, and macOS.

We're looking for a Senior Engineering Manager who brings the technical depth this domain demands and the organizational instincts to lead a team at the center of the sensor's dependency graph. You'll set technical direction, drive the roadmap, make high-stakes architectural decisions, and advise senior leadership, while staying close enough to the work to ensure every feature meets a high quality bar before it ships.

This is a cross-functional role. Mac, Linux, Windows, and many other teams inside and outside your core organization depend on what your team builds. You'll need to build trust with those teams, navigate competing priorities, and hold the reliability bar under delivery pressure. You will work cross-functionally with peers across engineering management, product management, and program management in our varied environment.

Engineering Managers at CrowdStrike are hands-on technical leaders. We don't expect you to write code on the critical path, but you'll need to deeply understand the architecture, data flows, and systems your team owns — well enough to troubleshoot, review PRs with substance, and push back when a design is heading somewhere it shouldn't. Your technical depth is also what makes you an effective advisor to leadership: you'll be expected to represent your team's position with confidence in cross-org discussions and to translate complex technical tradeoffs into clear guidance for leadership.

This role inherits a team with clear technical ownership and an expanded charter. The work is foundational — the services this team owns must be correct, fast, and reliable on every endpoint, every release. Operational risk is real and well-understood here; the right hire will bring the engineering rigor and management discipline to hold that bar while continuing to evolve the team's architecture.

Team leadership

  • Hire, coach, and grow engineers through honest, frequent feedback and genuine investment in their careers; lead the full recruitment process from initial contact through successful onboarding
  • Build team cohesion, resolve interpersonal friction, and create an environment where engineers can do their best work
  • Support your team through resourcing conversations, career development, and the harder conversations when they're neededDelivery and operations
  • Own project resourcing and workload which includes knowing when to say "no" and "not yet"; keep the team on track and adjust when surprises happen
