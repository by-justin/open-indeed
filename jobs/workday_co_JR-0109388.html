About the Team

The Advisory Services Project Manager will simultaneously handle several projects, coordinating the delivery of packaged services to customers of all sizes and industries around the world. Project Managers drive the execution of projects, ensuring success, efficiency and quality via project management practices (e.g., develop project staffing plan, coordinate system access, conducting meetings, etc.) and by handling the customer relationship. Within these projects, Project Managers serve as an advocate for Workday, our employees and our customers in equal measure, to complete projects under budget and on time per the definition of each service.

About the Role

Are you a dynamic Project Manager ready to drive impactful transformations for our 10 000+ customers across various industries and sizes? We're seeking a skilled Project manager to spearhead customer-facing advisory projects in EMEA, bringing Workday Success Plans to life and ensuring our customers not only adopt, but truly thrive with Workday. You'll be the master conductor, ensuring every project hits its mark—on time, on budget, and exceeding expectations. You'll equally champion Workday, our people, and our customers, making sure everyone wins. Ready to be the driving force behind successful customer engagements?

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead Advisory Projects : Drive the delivery of customer-facing Workday Success Plans services across EMEA, ensuring measurable impact and value.
  • Own the Engagement : Clearly define project goals, scope, and delivery plans to keep stakeholders aligned and informed throughout.
  • Engage with Senior Stakeholders : Build strong relationships and influence decision-makers across top EMEA customers to support their strategic goals.
  • Be a Trusted Advisor : Guide customers through the Optimization Packages portfolio, helping them unlock the full potential of Workday Success Plans.
  • Manage Multiple Projects : Oversee a dynamic portfolio of projects simultaneously, maintaining quality and meeting delivery timelines.
  • Coach and Support : Share knowledge and provide guidance to project team members on WSP methodology and best practices.
  • Collaborate with Clients : Work closely with customer project managers to anticipate risks, remove blockers, and ensure smooth execution.
  • Lead with Confidence : Facilitate impactful customer sessions and internal discussions, driving clarity and momentum.
  • Ensure Operational Excellence : Oversee project logistics including forecasting, scheduling, and administrative tasks from start to finish.

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together. Those in our remote "home office" roles also have the opportunity to come together in our offices for important moments that matter.
