Software Development Manager, MedTech

Analog Devices, Inc. (ADI)

Role Overview

Location: Wilmington, MA (Hybrid/In-office)

Analog Devices is seeking a Software Development Manager, MedTech to lead a high-performing, geographically distributed team of 12–15 software full-stack engineers building enterprise SaaS software for medical devices. This role is based in Wilmington, MA (with hybrid flexibility) and reports to the Engineering Director.

In this role, you will oversee the development and delivery of commercially deployed, cutting-edge medical devices and cloud-based enterprise software solutions focused on cardiopulmonary care. You will ensure these solutions meet the highest standards for quality, performance, security, reliability, and regulatory compliance.

The ideal candidate is a technically strong and hands-on leader, an experienced software engineer and product builder who has successfully transitioned into a management role while maintaining a passion for technology. You are comfortable engaging at all levels, including diving into code when needed, and thrive in environments that demand both technical depth and leadership excellence.

This position requires recent experience leading engineering teams within an enterprise software organization that operates under formal Quality Management Systems (QMS) and Design Controls . While MedTech experience is preferred, candidates from other regulated industries such as pharmaceuticals, automotive, or aerospace will also be considered.

You will work cross-functionally with Architecture, Product Management, Cybersecurity, Quality, and Regulatory teams to drive predictable delivery outcomes, enhance engineering maturity, and ensure continuous audit readiness—balancing compliance rigor with speed, innovation, and scalability.

What You Will Do

Team Building and Engineering Management

  • Serve as the development manager to a geographically distributed team of full-stack developers.
  • Build, mentor and coach high-performance engineering team
  • Foster a culture of AI-powered innovation and technical excellence
  • Align the skills to the solution roadmap and make decisions to strengthen the engineering capabilities.
  • Foster a collaborative environment that promotes professional development and team cohesion
  • Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies. Recommend and implement improvements to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of cloud operations

Delivery Enablement & Execution

  • Enable visibility across objectives, milestones, capacity planning, and dependencies.
  • Ensure timely delivery of the product roadmap.
  • Sustain strong execution cadence in an agile environment: iteration planning, backlog refinement, daily execution, reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Improve delivery performance across flow metrics, predictability, and release readiness.
  • Proactively identify and remove impediments; manage cross‑team dependencies and escalate risks appropriately.
  • Partner with Architecture, and Product leadership to define measurable delivery outcomes tied to business value.
  • Contribute technically and architecturally to the solution
  • Innovation and Improvement: Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies, especially in Agentic AI. Recommend and implement improvements to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of cloud solutions.

Regulated SDLC, Security, Privacy & Compliance Alignment (MedTech / FDA 510(k))

  • Act as an engineering subject‑matter expert supporting internal and external audits.
  • Support and implement FDA‑regulated SDLC expectations (requirements, design inputs/outputs, verification, validation).
  • Promote documentation practices that are disciplined and compliant.
  • Collaborate with Quality and Regulatory partners to align Agile execution with QMS, change control, and release governance.
  • Support leadership in transparent reporting
  • Security and Compliance: Implement comprehensive security measures to protect products and solutions. Ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements.
  • Performance Optimization: Continuously optimize cloud infrastructure performance. Implement best practices for cloud-based (AWS) resource utilization,
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with cross-functional leaders and stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver cloud solutions that meet their needs.

What You Bring (Required Qualifications)

  • Technical leader and manager for geographically distributed software teams of full-stack developers.
  • Hands‑on software development/commercial product building background in an enterprise software vendor setting.
  • Technical SME with full-stack cloud-based technologies (AWS preferred), working knowledge of enterprise software platforms, modern architecture, frameworks and AWS services
  • Demonstrated experience working in a regulated environment with Quality Management System and Design Controls
  • Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills
  • Bachelor’s / master’s degree in software engineering, Computer Science, or a related engineering field.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Demonstrated knowledge/track record of working with agentic architectures
  • Demonstrated experience working in a MedTech environment with FDA 510(k) Class II exposure building Software as a Medical Device solution.
