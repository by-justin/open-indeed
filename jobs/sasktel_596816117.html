JOB SUMMARY

Responsible to utilize SaskTel sales methodologies to proactively identify, research, and contact existing or potential new customers to gain an understanding of their business needs and initiatives. This includes the identification of potential opportunities from individual research and prospecting, attendance at company/industry events or referrals from other SaskTel sales channels. Develops customer relationships by conducting needs analysis, creating solutions, proposals, and presentations to secure sales opportunities. Partners closely with other internal sales roles and Account Executives/Strategic Accounts Managers, internal partner organizations including marketing, finance and operations personnel, for proposals and delivery and also external/third party resources. Establishes and meets personal, strategic and/or customer-based revenue results for SaskTel business products and services.

TYPICAL DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES

(Not all inclusive or applicable to all assignments)

  1. Accountable for maintaining and growing SaskTel ICT and/or digital media revenues, customer base and market share.

  2. Seek out, qualify and act on market/customer ICT and/or digital media opportunities. Actively work to understand customer business requirements, motivators and close sales.

  3. Analyze customer needs and deliver ICT and/or digital media solutions that solve identified business problems and quantify the expected return on investment.

  4. Develop and maintain a strong working relationship with key decision makers, owners, department heads or communication contacts to ensure acceptance of solution, successful sales negotiations and long-term customer relations.

  5. Responsible for generating market awareness of SaskTel’s ICT and/or digital media product capabilities through such activities as customer events, customer information sessions, etc.

  6. Recommend solutions, present proposals and deliver sales presentations.

  7. Work with customer post sale to validate the value of SaskTel solutions.

  8. Lead and/or team with Business Sales account teams and assign work to pro-actively protect network revenues, maintain working relationships and ensure strategic and/or individual sales targets are met.

  9. Develop and evolve customer account plans, ensuring they are in sync with sales and industry plans. Assist in the preparation, evolution and delivery of Business Sales plans and objectives.

10.Obtain customer commitment; negotiate conditions of sale and present documentation for authorization.

11.Ensure coordination of implementation is successful and follow up to ensure overall customer satisfaction.

12.Proactively research emerging technologies and competitive threats.

About SaskTel

.

SaskTel is the leading Information and Communications Technology (ICT) provider in Saskatchewan, with approximately $1.3 billion in annual revenue and nearly 1.4 million customer connections including more than 654,000 wireless accesses, 257,000 wireline network accesses, 295,000 internet and data accesses, 111,000 maxTV service subscribers, and 77,000 security monitoring customers. SaskTel and its wholly-owned subsidiaries offer a wide range of ICT products and services including competitive voice, data and Internet services, wireless data services, maxTV services, data centre services, cloud-based services, security monitoring services, advertising services, and international software and consulting services. SaskTel and its wholly-owned subsidiaries have a workforce of approximately 3,300 full-time equivalent employees (FTEs). Visit SaskTel at www.sasktel.com.
