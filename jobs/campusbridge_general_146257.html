FABER INC.

ANALYST, CORPORATE RESTRUCTURING

WHO WE ARE

Faber Inc. is a professional services firm specializing in consumer and corporate insolvency services. Our dynamic, multidisciplinary team includes Chartered Professional Accountants, Chartered Insolvency and Restructuring Professionals, Licensed Insolvency Trustees, Consumer Proposal Administrators, and Certified Insolvency Counsellors. Together, we deliver practical, forward-thinking debt solutions to a diverse client base.

Founded in 1997, Faber Inc. has grown from a single office to multiple locations strategically positioned throughout Alberta. We attribute our growth to a team of knowledgeable professionals who take pride in delivering clear, concise, and timely debt restructuring solutions with a strong commitment to client service excellence.

Our Corporate Restructuring & Turnaround Advisory professionals provide decisive solutions to small and mid-sized businesses facing financial distress. We act as trusted advisors and independent Court officers, delivering critical information, tactical advice, and targeted action in both in-court and out-of-court insolvency and restructuring matters where business continuity and stakeholder outcomes are at stake.

Our continued growth has created an exciting opportunity for an Analyst, Corporate Restructuring to join our Edmonton team. This role will appeal to a motivated professional with strong analytical and problem-solving skills, sound judgment, an entrepreneurial mindset, and the ability to build trusted relationships with clients and stakeholders across a range of industries.

THE ROLE WE OFFER

  • Support the VP, Corporate Restructuring in helping small and mid-sized businesses navigate financial distress, from early-stage cash flow and working capital challenges to formal insolvency and restructuring proceedings.
  • Work with clients to develop timely and relevant liquidity projections, viability assessments, recovery analyses, and other financial reports that support time-sensitive decision-making, negotiations, and credibility with stakeholders.
  • Attend client sites to perform possession taking, inventory procedures, and protective measures for bankruptcy and receivership engagements.
  • Evaluate business assets and contribute to realization strategies that consider relevant business, industry, stakeholder, and environmental risks.
  • Prepare detailed, comprehensive, and accurate financial reviews and written reports for clients and stakeholders, including creditors, the Court, and the Office of the Superintendent of Bankruptcy.
  • Proactively manage a portfolio of small business engagements in collaboration with other team members.
  • Collaborate with colleagues to strengthen internal systems, processes, and best practices.
  • Represent yourself and Faber Inc. through business development and marketing initiatives, community involvement, and volunteer activities.

THE CANDIDATE WE SEEK

  • A dynamic professional seeking a challenging and rewarding multidisciplinary career at the intersection of finance, accounting, business, and law.
  • A critical thinker who can quickly synthesize incomplete or unstructured information to identify gaps, risks, success factors, and solutions.
  • A positive and confident communicator with the tenacity to earn stakeholder trust in complex and often contentious situations.
  • Flexible, equally comfortable with conceptualizing sophisticated ideas and with rolling up their sleeves to perform elementary field work.
  • Intellectually curious and eager to develop expertise across diverse businesses, industries, and relevant legislation, including the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act, the Alberta Personal Property Security Act, and other laws governing insolvency, commercial contracts, and property rights.
  • An exceptional verbal and written communicator.
  • Highly organized, with strong time management skills and the ability to meet strict deadlines.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook
  • Comfortable learning and using emerging technology, including AI-enabled productivity tools
  • A collaborative team member who listens to and respects diverse perspectives and values individual contributions.
  • The desire and passion to pursue a career with a firm where honesty, integrity, respect and open communication forms part of its core values and rewards personal commitment while encouraging a healthy life and work balance
  • A strong desire to grow personally and professionally within the firm.

THE EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE WE REQUIRE

  • An undergraduate university degree with strong academic credentials.
  • Public practice experience at a reputable accounting or advisory firm is considered an asset.
  • Financial modelling experience will be viewed favourably.
  • Experience in corporate finance, financial due diligence, forensic accounting, consulting, or advisory services will be viewed favourably.

THE COMPENSATION WE PROVIDE

Faber Inc. offers a competitive compensation package, paid benefits, a comprehensive health and wellness spending account, paid professional courses, ongoing development and training, and annual bonuses for exceptional performance.
