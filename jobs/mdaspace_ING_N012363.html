Role:

The successful candidate will support the design and manufacturing engineering in the specification, selection and application of Electrical, Electronic, Electromechanical - EEE parts for space systems. Knowledge of military standards related to parts specification, selection and qualification is an asset.

Tasks:

  • Evaluation of components design and workmanship for high-reliability applications;

  • Evaluation of components discrepancies impact on design and reliability;

  • Preparation of Source Control Drawings (procurement specs);

  • Providing advice to design engineers on the component selection (based on heritage, quality and reliability requirements of programs/customers);

  • Identification of alternate parts for obsolescence or where lead times or component costs are unsuitable;

  • Approval of parts lists;

  • Preparation of declared components lists & non-standard parts approvals requests & participate in Parts Control Boards (PCBs) with customers;

  • Audit of component suppliers to assure their processes & products are under control and meet the relevant standards;

  • Review supplier surveys to determine if they are eligible for Approved Suppliers List;

  • Act as technical interface and coordinator with suppliers for meetings, documentation exchanges, NDAs, Export controls, etc (Qualification, etc);

  • Approval of Purchase orders for related component quality clauses;

  • Authorize the purchase of EEE items and ensure they fulfill program/customer requirements prior to reception/stocking;

  • Review/approval of data for incoming components (Incoming inspection reviews) including DPA results from external DPA houses;

  • Assign Value Added Services to EEEs to ensure full compliance to Product Assurance requirements;

  • Participate in internal and customer reviews of Non-conformances/discrepancies relating to EEEs (Engineering Review Boards – ERBs, Material Review Boards - MRBs);

  • Maintain EEE items database (item creation/review, verify/update documentation, etc);

  • Assess the impact of Alerts/Product Change Notices from suppliers/industry;

  • Participate in Product Assurance efforts/initiatives (Continuous improvement, Corrective Actions, 8D approach, etc.);

  • Review customer specifications to produce internal Statement of Compliance;

  • Participate in internal EEE related processes reviews & provide guidance/training to internal employees and customers about EEE requirements;

  • Participate in internal Qualification testing and Failure analysis of EEEs.

  • Identify and report lessons learned during the design or manufacturing phases to prevent recurring issues.

  • Assess the technical feasibility and integration risks of COTS (Commercial Off‑The‑Shelf) components according to the New‑Space approach.

  • Ensure that EEE and COTS components comply with radiation requirements for space applications.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical or physics engineering or a related field.

  • Minimum 2 years in component engineering (Electrical, Electronic, Electromechanical - EEE specialist) preferably with an aerospace company.

  • Familiarity with MIL, NASA and ESA standards for components and materials such as:

  • NASA GSFC EEE-INST-002 Instructions for EEE Parts Selection, Screening, Qualification, and Derating, ECSS-Q-ST-60 (Product Assurance Requirements of Space EEE Components) and their subsidiary specifications

  • MIL-STD-883/750 (Test Methods and Procedures for Microelectronics)

  • Good team player.

  • Good verbal and written communication skills ideally both in English and French (Contact with customers outside Quebec).

  • A EEE components Quality Controls and Reliability.

  • Experience with SEM, EDX, X-ray and other destructive and non-destructive techniques used for EEE inspection.

  • Knowledge of radiation effects on electronic components (TID, SEE, etc.) is a major asset for this position.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Familiarity with space radiation effects/testing on EEEs an asset

  • Ability to perform Constructional Analysis or Destructive Physical Analysis (DPA, MIL-STD-1580) on EEEs an asset

  • Familiar with Failure Analysis of EEE components an asset
