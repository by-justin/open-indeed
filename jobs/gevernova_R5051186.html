Job Description

Job Description Summary

We are seeking a hands-on Manufacturing Specialist – Machining with a deep background in shop-floor operations to join our team. This role is pivotal in driving operational excellence by creating robust manufacturing standards, including detailed Work Instructions and Job Safety Analyses (JSA). The ideal candidate will be a technical leader on the floor—standardizing tooling, driving complex troubleshooting, and providing active support to production teams. You will be responsible for leading continuous improvement initiatives, leveraging process innovation to optimize Safety, Quality, Delivery, and Cost (SQDC) in the manufacturing of high-precision gas turbine and aeroderivative components.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Standardization: Develop, maintain, and update Work Instructions and Job Safety Analyses (JSA) to ensure safe, repeatable, and compliant manufacturing processes.
  • Process Engineering: Design, program, and implement machining processes for complex components.
  • Programming & Optimization: Develop and refine CNC programs using CAD/CAM software (NX preferred) and manual ISO/G-code editing.
  • Tooling Strategy: Standardize tooling and lead the design of specialized jigs and fixtures required for specific machining operations.
  • Shop Floor Support: Provide active, hands-on technical support to production, including real-time troubleshooting, machine sub-routine optimization, and process adjustment.
  • Continuous Improvement: Lead process innovation projects to drive improvements in Safety, Quality, Delivery, and Cost (SQDC).

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • Practical Experience: Must have a proven history as a CNC Operator or CNC Shop Floor Supervisor/Manufacturing Support
  • CNC Programming: At least 2+ years of experience in CNC programming (ISO/G-code and CAD/CAM).
  • Siemens Controls: At least 2+ years of hands-on experience working with Siemens CNC controllers.
  • Process Knowledge: Comprehensive mastery of CNC machining processes, including lathe, milling, grinding, and drilling.
  • Technical Proficiency:
  • Excellent knowledge of mechanical drawings and blueprints.
  • Deep understanding of machine tool capabilities and process parameters.
  • Proven experience in developing and troubleshooting CNC machine sub-routines.
  • Software Skills: Previous experience in tooling design for machining processes using NX.
  • Mindset: Strong commitment to safety and quality standards.
  • Language skills: proficient in Italian and good English skills (reading & writing)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Specific experience in the repair and refurbishment of gas turbine and aeroderivative parts.
  • Advanced technical certifications related to CNC machining or mechanical engineering technology.
