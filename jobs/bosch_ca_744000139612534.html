Descrição da vaga

The role is a key member of the Global Bosch Home Comfort SAP ERP IT team , responsible for delivering standardized, scalable, and high-quality SAP solutions across a complex global ERP landscape.

The SAP environment under this role includes multiple SAP systems (ECC and S/4HANA), which will progressively be consolidated into a unified global SAP S/4HANA platform. This position plays a critical role in enabling and supporting this transformation.

The role works across the full SAP application lifecycle - from strategy and design through implementation, optimization, and decommissioning - collaborating with global IT teams, business stakeholders, and external partners.

Acting as an SAP MM & Ariba subject matter expert, the role drives solution design, process standardization, and business–IT alignment, supporting ongoing digital transformation initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

Strategy & Conceptualization:

  • Align SAP solutions with business and IT strategy
  • Identify business opportunities, challenges, and improvement areas
  • Develop business cases and conduct feasibility studies
  • Manage demand intake, portfolio prioritization, and roadmap planning
  • Identify and engage key stakeholders across business and IT
  • Support budgeting, funding, and cost planning activities

Design & Planning:

  • Lead requirements gathering, analysis, and solution evaluation
  • Define end-to-end SAP solution architecture aligned with global templates
  • Drive UX/UI design to enhance user experience and adoption
  • Ensure compliance with security, governance, and regulatory requirements
  • Support project planning, scoping, and execution
  • Participate in vendor selection and procurement processes

Build, Deploy & Transition:

  • Provide architectural oversight for development, testing, and deployment activities
  • Ensure alignment with SAP best practices and enterprise standards
  • Support quality assurance, User Acceptance testing and successful release to production
  • Drive knowledge transfer and documentation, aligned to governance standards

Run & Operate:

  • Drive Service Request Management and delivery
  • Support root cause analysis and continuous stabilization efforts
  • Ensure alignment with operational support models and SLAs

Optimization & Continuous Improvement:

  • Monitor system & solution performance and capacity reviews
  • Gather user feedback and drive feature enhancements
  • Identify and execute cost optimization opportunities

Phase-Out & Decommissioning:

  • Develop and execute phase-out strategies for legacy SAP systems
  • Plan and manage system decommissioning activities
  • Ensure effective communication with stakeholders and users
  • Oversee data migration, retention, and archiving
  • Conduct post go-live and post-decommissioning reviews

Qualificações

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related discipline
  • SAP experience, including SAP S/4HANA full lifecycle implementation and/or rollouts
  • Strong expertise in SAP Materials Management (MM) covering end-to-end Procure-to-Pay (P2P) processes including purchase requisitions, purchase orders, vendor management, pricing, and inventory management
  • Experience with SAP Ariba modules including Buying & Invoicing, Sourcing, Contracts, and Supplier Lifecycle Management , with strong understanding of indirect procurement and supplier collaboration
  • Proven experience integrating SAP MM with SAP Ariba using CIG, APIs, IDocs, and middleware to enable seamless procurement and supplier processes
  • Strong understanding of procurement execution including goods receipt, invoice verification, stock transfers, and alignment with inventory and warehouse processes
  • Integration expertise across SAP modules (MM, SD, FI, PP, WM/QM), including end-to-end supply chain and financial integration for procurement processes
  • Experience designing scalable procurement solutions aligned with enterprise architecture, including process standardization, automation, compliance, and supplier management strategies

Preferred

  • Experience in global template design and multi-country rollouts
  • Business engagement and stakeholder management
  • Ability to drive cost-benefit analysis and develop solution deployment roadmaps
  • Experience working in Agile / DevOps delivery frameworks
