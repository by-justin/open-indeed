Position Overview

The Manager – E-Tail Hub & CPU Operations is responsible for leading one of Blue Dart's largest integrated E-Tail processing facilities, overseeing end-to-end Hub and CPU operations to ensure operational excellence, service reliability, network efficiency, and customer satisfaction. Reporting to the Regional Manager – E-Tail CPU Operations (North), the role provides operational leadership across inbound and outbound processing, sorter operations, middle-mile connectivity, fleet planning, manpower productivity, safety, compliance, and continuous improvement initiatives.

As the National Hub Centre (NHC) continues to expand its processing capacity and network footprint, this role will play a critical role in ensuring scalable operations, operational resilience, cost optimization, and seamless execution of Blue Dart's service commitments while driving operational excellence across Hub and CPU operations.

Job Purpose

To lead and manage the end-to-end operations of the National Hub Centre (NHC) by delivering industry-leading service quality, ensuring efficient Hub and CPU operations, optimizing operational productivity, strengthening network connectivity, driving continuous improvement initiatives, and maintaining the highest standards of safety, compliance, and customer experience .
