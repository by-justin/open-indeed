🧠 Key Responsibilities

1. Requirements and Test Design

  • Analyze requirements, designs, user stories, and acceptance criteria
  • Identify ambiguity, risks, dependencies, edge cases, and negative scenarios early
  • Design and review test scenarios, test cases, and automation scripts
  • Apply risk-based and shift-left testing to critical business journeys
  • Ensure traceability between requirements, tests, defects, and releases

2. Automation Engineering

  • Design and maintain scalable frameworks using Playwright with Python
  • Automate web applications, APIs, integrations, workflows, permissions, and approvals
  • Build reusable components and effective test-data solutions
  • Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines
  • Improve test stability, maintainability, reporting, and execution speed
  • Eliminate flaky tests and automation bottlenecks
  • Perform exploratory and manual testing where it provides greater value

3. Integration and Performance Quality

  • Validate complex end-to-end workflows across multiple systems
  • Execute API, integration, performance, load, scalability, and resilience testing
  • Collaborate with Architecture, DevOps, Security, and Operations to validate non-functional requirements
  • Ensure platform reliability for mission-critical global operations

4. AI-Driven Quality Engineering

  • Apply AI to requirement analysis, test generation, test data, automation, defect analysis, and regression optimization
  • Explore Agentic AI for intelligent test execution, monitoring, and continuous improvement
  • Ensure human review, traceability, security, and governance for AI-generated assets
  • Measure AI impact through coverage, defect detection, productivity, and cycle-time improvements

5. Quality Reporting and Collaboration

  • Monitor test execution, defects, automation health, and release risks
  • Create clear dashboards and evidence-based quality reports
  • Convert findings into concrete recommendations and improvement actions
  • Support root-cause analysis and preventive measures
  • Share automation best practices and mentor other QA Engineers
  • Promote quality as a shared product responsibility
