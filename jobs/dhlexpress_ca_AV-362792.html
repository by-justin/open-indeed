Overall Role Purpose

The Operations Team Manager is  fully accountable for end‑to‑end customer service and operational performance across the EMEA region , ensuring service excellence, reliability, and continuous improvement. The role leads and develops regional operations and customer service teams, drives operational KPIs, manages critical customer relationships, and acts as the  final escalation point for service failures . The position plays a key role in translating global and regional strategies into effective local execution while working within a  matrix organization  across Commercial, Product, Finance, and Global Operations.
