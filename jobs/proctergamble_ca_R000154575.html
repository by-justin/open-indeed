PST - Packing Technician - Band BTA Job Description

Job Family Summary:

The Packing Technician belongs to the Production, Service & Transportation (PST) job family. This job family is responsible for the physical creation and transportation of our products, ensuring they reach our consumers in perfect condition.

Job Description:

As a Packing Technician in the Band BTA, you will play a crucial role in our production process. You will be responsible for operating and maintaining packing equipment, ensuring the quality of our products, and contributing to a safe and efficient working environment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Operate packing machinery in accordance with established procedures and guidelines.
  • Maintain equipment to ensure optimal performance and output.
  • Monitor product quality and report any issues or inconsistencies.
  • Adhere to all safety protocols and contribute to a safe working environment.
  • Participate in team meetings and continuous improvement initiatives.

We offer you:

  • Ownership of your work from your first day
  • Maintain world-renowned production technologies in some interesting ways
  • The chance to influence the production and quality of our products
  • Mentorship, coaching, training, and guidance
  • Known for providing a safe work environment
  • Onboarding and development training programs to build a strong foundation in manufacturing
  • Opportunity for Advancement and Skill Development
  • Competitive Compensation and Benefits Package

Our Work Schedule:

The Dover Procter & Gamble Plant operates 7-days per week on 12 hour rotating shifts. We operate on a 2-2-3 work schedule rotating between days (6:30 am-7:00 pm) and nights (6:30 pm-7:00 am).

Job Qualifications

  • 18 years of age or older
  • Have a minimum of a high school diploma, GED or equivalent education
  • Wear required safety personal protective equipment (eye protection, ear protection, safety shoes)
  • Experience in a manufacturing or production environment is beneficial.
  • Technical aptitude and ability to operate machinery.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to product quality.
  • Awareness of safety protocols and procedures.
  • Able to work collaboratively in a team environment.
  • Willing to work 12 hour rotating shifts
