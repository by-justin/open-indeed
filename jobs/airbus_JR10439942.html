Senior  Cybersecurity Architect

Airbus Defence and Space is one of the three Divisions of the Airbus Group and Europe’s Number 1 defence and space company. Interest group: Cybersecurity

Contract type/working time: Unlimited

Work experience: min 10 years

Airbus Defence and Space Romania is seeking a Senior Cybersecurity Architect to serve as a  strategic technical authority  for our critical systems. In this role, you will act as the definitive cybersecurity voice, shaping the resilient cyber architectures that support  missions critical systems  definition.Areas of impact:  Cybersecurity

Responsibilities:

  • Design, build, and oversee the implementation of system-wide  security software  systems. This includes creating the blueprints for IT Systems, applications, and data protection across on-premise and hybrid environments
  • Lead and facilitate threat modeling sessions during the design phase to identify, categorize, and prioritize threats to the software system.
  • Makes key decisions on technology and integration for the cybersecurity area
  • Architect and champion, the implementation of Zero Trust frameworks, focusing on advanced Identity and Access Management (IAM), and micro-segmentation.
  • Ensure all architectural designs meet or exceed stringent regulatory requirements and industry-specific mandates
  • Act as the primary Subject Matter Expert (SME) for the security organization, providing high-level technical guidance to Security Engineers and mentoring junior architectural talent.
  • Ensure all designs are compliant and aligned with industry standards (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001, DCS)
  • Govern technical and system solution designs with other stakeholders and partners

Requirements :

  • A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Information Technology, or related fields.
  • Minimum of 10 years Experience in Information Technology, with at least 7 years specifically in Cybersecurity and security architecture
  • Professional certifications such as Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP), Certified Information Security Manager (CISM), Information Systems Security Architecture Professional (CISSP-ISSAP) or similar would be a plus
  • Extensive experience in IT, particularly in information security  and software security
  • Strong understanding of security protocols, cryptography, application security and systems integration
  • Demonstrated expertise in modern security concepts such as Zero Trust Architecture, Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management
  • Interactions with SOC/Cyberdefence
  • Knowledge of the defense domain cybersecurity constraints (NATO or national) including specific regulations and accreditation processes
  • Knowledge of key security solutions: hardening of OS ,DB, IAM solutions, PKI, network security solutions (firewalls, proxies, WAF), virtualization security solutions (hypervisors, containers), DCS and Information Exchange Gateway

Soft Skills

  • Logical reasoning and problem-solving skills
  • Proven ability to work independently through the entire writing process
  • Good multi-tasking abilities and no problem in managing multiple topics at once
  • Strong ability to think analytically plus a high level of flexibility to adapt to the different priorities
  • Ability to work independently and in a team
  • Quick learning of new topics and general sense of curiosity
  • Very good English skills
  • Excellent research and analysis capabilities
  • Writing skills with a structured and synthetic approach

Competencies:

  • Solid communication and interpersonal skills are a must
  • Working in a multicultural environment / as part of a team
