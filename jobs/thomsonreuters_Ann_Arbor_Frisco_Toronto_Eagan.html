Job Description

Job Description

Thomson Reuters is looking for a Staff Software Engineer / Architect – AI to join our CoCounsel Forward Deployed Engineering team.

This role sits at the intersection of AI engineering, architecture, product development, and customer delivery . You will work directly with customers to understand complex business and technical problems, design the right AI-powered solution, and help build and deploy that solution into production.

The ideal candidate is a highly hands-on AI engineer who can move comfortably between customer discovery, technical architecture, software development, and production delivery. You will contribute to reusable AI capabilities while also solving customer-specific challenges in complex enterprise environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner directly with customers and internal stakeholders to understand business and technical problems and translate them into scalable AI solutions.
  • Architect, build, and deliver production-grade AI applications and services.
  • Develop solutions leveraging LLMs, RAG, agentic workflows, evaluation frameworks, and enterprise integrations .
  • Design secure, scalable architectures that integrate with customer-owned and Thomson Reuters infrastructure.
  • Contribute hands-on to software development, APIs, services, deployment, and production operations.
  • Build reusable technical components, patterns, and frameworks that can support multiple customer engagements.
  • Establish appropriate observability, monitoring, evaluation, MLOps/LLMOps, security, and governance practices.
  • Participate in technical design reviews and mentor engineers on architecture and implementation.
  • Work across engineering, product, and customer-facing teams to drive solutions from problem discovery through production deployment.

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of software engineering experience , including significant experience designing and delivering production systems.
  • Strong hands-on software engineering and architecture experience.
  • Demonstrated experience building and deploying production AI/ML or LLM-powered systems .
  • Experience with technologies and patterns such as LLMs, RAG, agents, evaluation, MLOps/LLMOps, observability, and enterprise integrations .
  • Experience working directly with customers or business stakeholders to identify technical problems and develop solutions.
  • Strong understanding of scalable APIs, services, distributed systems, cloud platforms, and production software engineering practices.
  • Ability to operate across the full solution lifecycle: discovery → architecture → build → deployment → production support .
  • Strong communication skills and ability to explain complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in Forward Deployed Engineering, Solutions Engineering, or customer-facing AI engineering .
  • Experience working in tax, audit, insurance, financial services, legal technology, or other regulated industries .
  • Experience working in enterprise SaaS, AI startups, or highly technical customer-facing engineering environments.
  • Experience with security, compliance, governance, and auditability requirements for enterprise AI systems.
  • Experience designing reusable platforms or frameworks across multiple customer implementations.
  • Experience mentoring senior engineers or leading complex technical initiatives.
