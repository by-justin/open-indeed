Job Responsibilities:

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS OF THE POSITIONS:

  • Follow and enforce all plant safety, environmental, and housekeeping (5S) policies and procedures, including strict compliance with Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) requirements and safe material handling practices.
  • Understand, support, and promote Magna's philosophies, including the Magna Operational Principles, Corporate Constitution, and Employee's Charter.
  • Perform preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance on dies, molds, trim dies, fixtures, and auxiliary tooling to ensure maximum equipment reliability, product quality, and production efficiency.
  • Troubleshoot, diagnose, repair, and optimize tooling issues during production and scheduled maintenance, both in and out of the die casting machines, minimizing downtime and supporting production requirements.
  • Perform die changes, startups, shutdowns, and new tooling tryouts while ensuring proper setup, alignment, and functionality up to technical specifications (Technical Manual).
  • Modify dies, tooling, fixtures, and related equipment to incorporate engineering changes, improve manufacturability, increase tool life, reduce scrap, and improve Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).
  • Interpret engineering drawings, CAD models, blueprints, GD&T, sketches, specifications, and technical manuals to perform tooling repairs, fabrication, and precision machining.
  • Set up and operate tool room equipment including manual lathes, milling machines, drill presses, surface grinders, band saws, pedestal grinders, and precision measuring equipment.
  • Perform TIG, MIG, and Arc welding, heat treatment, machining, polishing, fitting, grinding, and bench work required for tooling repair and fabrication.
  • Inspect and verify repaired or newly manufactured tooling using precision measuring instruments to ensure compliance with engineering specifications, tolerances, clearances, alignments, and quality standards.
  • Install, troubleshoot, repair, and maintain tooling systems including hydraulic, pneumatic, cooling, lubrication, and mechanical components associated with die casting tooling.
  • Safely operate forklifts, overhead cranes, hoists, and rigging equipment during die handling, installation, maintenance, and transportation.
  • Support Die Casting Operations by responding quickly to production issues, identifying root causes of tooling failures, and implementing permanent corrective actions to prevent recurrence.
  • Participate in continuous improvement, Lean Manufacturing, Kaizen, TPM, SMED, and waste reduction initiatives to improve safety, quality, productivity, equipment reliability, and tooling performance.
  • Analyze tooling wear patterns and recommend preventive actions, design improvements, replacement schedules, and spare parts inventory requirements to maximize tooling life.
  • Maintain accurate preventive maintenance records, tooling history, repair documentation, engineering changes, and maintenance reports within the company's maintenance management system.
  • Ensure compliance with quality standards, control plans, PFMEA requirements, work instructions, and customer-specific requirements while producing high-quality tooling and components.
  • Train, mentor, and support operators, apprentices, and new employees on tooling operation, troubleshooting, safe work practices, quality requirements, and preventive maintenance procedures.
  • Collaborate effectively with Production, Engineering, Quality, Maintenance, Process Engineering, and suppliers to resolve tooling issues, support new product launches, and improve manufacturing performance.
  • Recommend tooling design improvements, equipment modifications, cost reduction opportunities, and process enhancements to improve safety, quality, maintainability, and operational efficiency.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and audit-ready tool room through effective 5S practices and proper care of company tools, equipment, and measuring devices.
  • Perform all other duties and responsibilities as assigned in support of departmental and organizational objectives.

POSITION REQUIREMENTS

SKILL (Ability to do the job)

  • Strong knowledge of aluminum die casting processes, trim die operations, and tooling functions.
  • Ability to set up, maintain, troubleshoot, repair, and restore tooling to original specifications.
  • Proficient in operating manual tool room equipment, precision measuring instruments, and machine shop equipment.
  • Ability to interpret engineering drawings, blueprints, CAD data, and technical documentation.
  • Experience performing TIG and Arc welding for tooling repair and modification.
  • Skilled in sanding, polishing, stoning, highlighting, and correcting tooling surface conditions to meet customer quality requirements.
  • Strong troubleshooting skills for tooling and press-related issues, including identifying root causes and implementing effective corrective actions.
  • Knowledge of preventive maintenance practices and tooling restoration techniques.
  • Forklift certification and overhead crane experience preferred.
  • Basic computer skills, including Microsoft Office applications.

Core Competencies

  • Strong mechanical aptitude with excellent analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision while making sound technical decisions in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
  • Excellent organizational, planning, and time management skills with strong attention to detail.
  • Effective verbal and written communication skills; bilingual English/Spanish is a plus.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with Production, Engineering, Quality, and Maintenance teams.
  • Willingness to adapt to changing priorities, learn new technologies, and participate in continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Ability to perform manual work, including lifting and handling heavy tooling components safely.
