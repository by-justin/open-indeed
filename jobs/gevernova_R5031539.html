GE Vernova est une société mondiale dans le secteur de l’énergie qui comprend les segments de l'énergie éolienne, de l'électrification et qui est soutenue par ses activités d'accélérateurs de recherche avancée, de services de conseil et de services financiers. Chez GE Vernova, notre division Hydro Power est à l'avant-garde de l'industrie de la production d'hydroélectricité. Nous nous engageons à concevoir, fabriquer et installer des équipements de pointe pour les centrales hydroélectriques, ainsi qu'à entretenir et à remettre à neuf les installations existantes pour garantir des performances et une longévité optimales. Nos turbines et générateurs représentent plus de 25 % de la capacité hydroélectrique totale installée dans le monde. Avec une équipe dynamique de 3 600 professionnels répartis dans plus de 30 pays, nous avons l’énergie nécessaire pour changer le monde. Responsabilités et activités :

  • Superviser les dessinateurs pour la création des dessins du projet.
  • Superviser et accompagner les fournisseurs/sous-traitants.
  • Préparer les calculs de conception, les spécifications techniques, les dessins (supportés par les dessinateurs) et les nomenclatures sans supervision.
  • Collaborer et assister l’équipe de gestion de projet lorsque nécessaire.

  • Évaluer et agir sur les rapports de non-conformités.
  • Assurer le support sur site pour la mise en service et le montage.
  • Organiser et/ou participer aux revues de conception.
  • Fournir un soutien aux activités d’appel d’offres.
  • Effectuer une étude de site.
  • Collaborer avec des ingénieurs-conseils et des experts pour le FOE, la validation de la conception et l'analyse des causes profondes (RCA).
  • Participer aux réunions avec les clients et les fournisseurs pour l'approbation des documents et/ou la discussion NCR.

The employee will be responsible for the design and coordination of electrical systems for hydro speed governor. He / She will also be responsible to provide technical support for supply chain and Construction & Commissioning activities related to hydro generators.

GE Vernova is a planned, purpose-built global energy company that includes Power, Wind, and Electrification segments and is supported by its accelerator businesses of Advanced Research, Consulting Services, and Financial Services. Building on over 130 years of experience tackling the world’s challenges, GE Vernova is uniquely positioned to help lead the energy transition by continuing to electrify the world while simultaneously working to decarbonize it.

At GE Vernova, our Hydro Power division stands at the forefront of the hydroelectricity generation industry. We are dedicated to designing, manufacturing, and installing cutting-edge equipment for hydroelectric power plants, as well as servicing and refurbishing existing facilities to ensure optimal performance and longevity. Our turbines and generators account for over 25% of the total installed hydropower capacity worldwide. With a dynamic team of 3,600 professionals spread across more than 30 countries, we have the energy to change the world. Be part of a team that is not only shaping the energy landscape but also contributing to a more sustainable planet. Join us and make a difference with GE Vernova’s Hydro Power business division! Read more on www.gevernova.com

Responsibilities and activities:

  • Read and understand customer specifications to generate inputs for design team and deliverables for customer.
  • Deliver work packages for projects or programs using technical guidelines, leading design reviews and implementing feedback of experience (FOE).
  • Produce schematics, technical specifications, testing procedures and all documentation required for scope according to customer´s requirements and GE practices.
  • Supervise draftsmen for the creation of project drawings.
  • Supervise and support suppliers/sub-contractors.
  • Prepare design calculations, technical specifications, drawings (supported by draftsmen) and BOM’s without supervision.
  • Collaborate with and assist project management team when required.
  • Support supply chain for project purchase and new supplier development, when required.
  • Evaluate and act on non-conformities reports.
  • Provide site support for commissioning and erection.
  • Organize and/or participate on design reviews.
  • Provide support on tendering activities.
  • Perform Site Survey.
  • Collaborate with consulting engineers and experts for FOE, design validation and root cause analysis (RCA).
  • Participate, on meetings with customers and suppliers for document approval and or NCR discussion.

Qualifications / Requirements:

  • Fluent in written and spoken English. GE provides high-quality services to customers/clients located across the globe and the employee must be able to provide services in an efficient manner aligned with the standards of excellence of the company.

Required knowledge and/or experience:

  • Knowledge on programable logic controllers (control loops, actuators, sensors, signals).

  • Pro-activeness, sense of urgency, autonomy
