Role Purpose:

The purpose of this role is to:

• To support Strategy 2030 Digital Partner Program and Enterprise API Customers. • Ensure Express MyDHLAPI and developer portal enhanced documentation required for the Partner Program is developed and enhanced at pace of the DPP MVP and subsequent phase deliverables. • Ensure proactive identification of enhancements required by our Partners and formulate them into technical requirements for the DHL Express flagship API platform • Ensure projects are delivered to agreed scope, time, cost, quality, governance and satisfaction, as per agreed roadmaps. • Manage any enhancements towards applications/products/services as part of the DCS Programs domain’s portfolio relative to API or EDI Platforms, including build and run elements to support optimum functioning of the business functions. • Drive delivery of concurrent programmes/projects as needed to support DCS Programs across the API and EDI landscape. • Collaborate closely with the CSI X-BU Group API Centre of Excellence and relevant stakeholders to influence API roadmap developments, represent Express business requirements, and support the delivery of solutions that create value across DHL Group while meeting Express customer and partner needs.

Candidate may be based in Prague, Belgium, or Singapore.
