Job SummaryAs a Site Inventory Control (SIC) Planning Engineer, you will play a critical role in maximizing Fab 15 manufacturing capacity and ensuring on-time delivery to customers.

SIC Planning serves as a key control tower connecting Fab 15, the Supply Chain organization, and the global DRAM network. In this role, you will integrate information related to demand, supply, manufacturing capacity, inventory, and technology transitions, and provide clear direction on short- and mid-term production plans as a representative of Fab 15.

As manufacturing requirements continue to evolve through increased max-out expectations, HBM product growth, and increasingly complex offload operations involving Metallization, EUV, Wafer-to-Wafer (W2W), and cross-fab processes, the strategic importance of SIC continues to grow. You will evaluate constraints and opportunities across Fab 15 and other sites, develop scenario analyses, optimize Die Mix, Wafer Starts, Output Profiles, and Offload Loops, and provide decision-making frameworks that drive network-wide optimization.

This role goes beyond generating production plans. You will proactively identify emerging issues and opportunities, align stakeholders across Supply Chain, MCT, Manufacturing, Engineering, NPI, and other fabs, and translate plans into operational execution. Through data-driven decision making, AI-enabled solutions, rapid risk identification, standardization, automation, and continuous improvement, you will contribute to customer commitments and stable Fab 15 operations.

As a representative of Fab 15, you will have significant influence and visibility across the global DRAM network. This position is ideal for individuals who are passionate about driving business impact beyond a single manufacturing site and want to contribute to the optimization of a global manufacturing network.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as the control tower connecting Fab15, Supply Chain, and the DRAM network, and drive short- and mid-term production plans and execution direction.
  • Integrate demand, supply, inventory, capacity, cycle time, yield, and customer delivery data to provide Fab15 decision frameworks and response options.
  • Optimize Max-out, pull-in, die mix, NPI samples, TD lots, wafer starts, and output profiles to balance Fab output and customer commitments.
  • Manage increasingly complex offload operations across Metallization, EUV, W2W, and Probe, including send/receive plans, capacity, loop cycle time, priorities, performance, and risks.
  • Visualize constraints and available capacity across fabs and propose network-optimized loading and offload strategies through scenario analysis.
  • Monitor KPIs including pace, cycle time, wafer out, delivery adherence, and inventory; lead early deviation detection, impact assessment, and recovery direction.
  • Align planning and execution with Supply Chain, MCT, Manufacturing, Engineering, NPI, IE, and partner fabs.
  • Anticipate changes, proactively raise key issues, and support timely escalation and decision-making as a representative of Fab15.
  • Standardize and automate reports, dashboards, planning tools, and tracking solutions to improve accuracy, speed, and repeatability.
  • Lead continuous improvement and cross-functional projects that strengthen stable Fab15 operations and DRAM network competitiveness.
  • Utilize AI-enabled tools and advanced analytics to improve planning accuracy, optimize capacity utilization, support risk assessment, and drive data-driven operational decision making.
