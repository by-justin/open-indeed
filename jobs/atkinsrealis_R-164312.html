Overview

As a leading infrastructure consultancy, we strive to incorporate innovation and best practices into every aspect of the asset management life cycle. The digital transformation of infrastructure is gaining momentum, and we are actively seeking individuals who can assist our clients in making informed decisions and enhancing the visibility of their assets.

With our global experts and asset management centre of excellence, we offer cutting-edge solutions, ranging from core digital asset management systems (EAM, AIP, Document and Information Management, Reporting and Analytics) to visualisation Hubs and Tools (GIS, BIM, Digital Twins).

We love different – be it your experience in asset management, operational management, client advisory or management, civil engineers, and everything in between, we encourage applications from individuals who feel passionate about the future of our sector.

We are looking for diverse individuals who understand the complex, interlinking elements and strategy required that will enable you to assess and deliver digital solutions across the whole asset management lifecycle but are also willing to adapt and learn on the job with opportunities for professional development.

Your role

We are specifically looking for exceptional individuals who excel in delivery, innovation, and possess a deep understanding of both physical and digital technology. As an EAM Business Analyst, you will play a pivotal role in bridging the gap between business needs and technical solutions. Responsibilities span across data management, dashboard development, business process analysis and requirements gathering to support strategic decision-making and operational efficiency.

Delivery focused

  • Work as part of a cross-functional teams to deliver digital asset management solutions, fostering collaboration, accountability, and high performance across diverse stakeholder groups.
  • Proven delivery experience working closely with client stakeholders to understand digital asset management needs and in developing requirements to deliver tangible benefits.
  • Ability to identify opportunities to streamline information and data workflows alongside reporting processes.
  • Ability to work across multiple projects/programmes of work that have delivery value to your organisation or clients.
  • Experience or a willingness to learn how to apply Agile delivery methodologies to manage and maintain delivery strategies for project teams and clients.

Technical

  • A good understanding of the principles of Whole life Asset management as outlined in international standards (ISO 55000 Series), and you will be familiar with the Institute of Asset management Anatomy, standards, and principles.
  • Advisory and delivery capabilities in asset management, design & build engineering, Operational and maintenance management, that will deliver novel solutions for our clients.
  • Understanding of investment management practices and how to apply investment requirements to client priorities to support decision making across the asset life cycle.

About you

  • You will be motivated, excited and a team player.
  • Demonstrable experience in the Engineering, Transportation, Defence, Energy & Utilities sectors.
  • Working knowledge of Enterprise Asset Management applications.
  • Working knowledge of one or more of the following digital capabilities would be desirable; Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Project Programme and Portfolio Management (3PM), Asset Investment Planning (AIP), Geospatial and Visualisation (G&V), Document & Content Management (DCM), Asset Health & Performance (AHP), Reporting & Analytics (R&A).
  • Good knowledge of the Institute of Asset Management Anatomy, ISO55000 and either completed or working towards your IAM certificate.
  • Good knowledge of applying Asset Management standards.
  • Strong verbal and numerical reasoning skills, with practical examples of application through identification of solutions, articulation and buy-in from stakeholders.
  • Strong written communication skills with the ability to convert client needs into strong business cases and solutions.
  • A working knowledge of Power Platform and related applications is desirable.
  • Willing to stay informed of digital innovative systems, tools and technology developments within the wider business.
