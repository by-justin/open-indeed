Company Description

The Bosch Group is a leading global supplier of technology and services, in the areas of Automotive Technology, Industrial Technology, Consumer Goods, Energy and Building Technology.

In India, the Group operates through nine companies with a combined strength of over 30,000 associates which includes around 14,000 research and development associates.

Bosch Automotive Electronics India Pvt. Ltd. (RBAI) is a 100% subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH. RBAI was established at the right time to cater to the demands of future Indian market. Established in 2009, started out with manufacturing Electronic Control Units. RBAI has consistently added an average of one product every year resulting in a wide range of products comprising – Engine Control Units (ECU) for both 4W and 2W, Body Control Modules (BCM), ECUs for ABS, DC-DC Converters, iTraMS (Intelligent communication control unit) & Display Audio units to name a few. RBAI also produces products for eMobility like MCU (Motor control Unit), C-Box (Communication Box), VCUs (Vehicle Control Unit), Central gateway modules and is geared up for other futuristic products like Charcon. RBAI with its diverse portfolio, not only caters to local markets, but also exports products to all major global customers. With our first product having been produced in 2009, RBAI completed more than a decade of serving the Mobility Electronics (ME) Industry. RBAI successfully achieved a key milestone in 2016, completing manufacturing of 10 million Engine Control Units and 5 million Body Control Modules, which soon followed by 20 million and 10 million respectively by 2019. In the year 2023, RBAI celebrated achieving a significant milestone of 10 million 2W ABS PCBA’s and 5 million 2W ECU.

RBAI exports products to all major global customers spread across destinations. RBAI towards becoming a global benchmark in operational excellence, consistently produces single digit ppm quality products and maintains benchmark inventory levels. RBAI by inheriting the Bosch Production System (BPS) at all levels and practices, is consistently at the top of the ME BPS ranking since December 2018.

RBAI has been recognized as a Great Place to Work! This prestigious designation is a testament to RBAI’s unwavering commitment to fostering a positive and inclusive workplace culture. RBAI greatest asset is its talented and dedicated team, and this recognition reflects its ongoing efforts to create an environment where every member feels valued, supported, and inspired.
