Role Description:

Based out of Oakridge Park, the Property Manager (9-Month Contract) provides support to the retail and commercial office functions.  Reporting directly to the General Manager, Property Manager is responsible for assisting with day-to-day activities for the portfolio and providing best-in-class customer service.

Responsibilities:

  • Support the General Manager with managing the building portfolio and assist with completing annual budgeted projects
  • Complete the analysis of financial statements for preparation and posting of monthly reports
  • Assist with the annual budget process for buildings within the portfolio
  • Liaise with tenants for escalated collection of accounts receivable matters and reconcile accounts
  • Administer lease related payments, such as tenant allowances etc.
  • Supervise contractors and ensure compliance for major service contracts; maintain/update contract schedule and databases
  • Process all cheques, invoices and related reports within JD Edwards
  • Process and match invoices, code invoices to comply with budget schedules, and compile/verify for submission to Regional Accounting
  • Perform administrative functions related to property management and analysis of leases
  • Tender operational contracts (landscaping/snow removal, HVAC, etc.)
  • Monitor and follow up on QuadReal Connect/Tenant maintenance requests to ensure overall service delivery is consistent with QuadReal standards
  • Maintain and update tenant information for timely collection and recording of tenant insurance certificates
  • Maintain tenant manuals and assist with coordination of tenant appreciation function for the buildings in the portfolio
  • Oversee periodic tenant surveys, ensuring distribution of survey on a timely basis and encouraging maximum participation of all tenants.  Review all survey responses, analyze trends, and provide feedback on overall ratings, ensuring all tenants issues have been dealt accordingly
  • Type and distribute correspondence to tenants and prepare rental letters
  • Responsible for rent collection, cheque posting, application of money to tenant accounts, and reviewing monthly recurring billings
  • Liaise with tenants and provide prompt follow-up and communication for dealing with tenant matters
  • Monitor and track vacant unit utility accounts
  • Read and understand leases to ensure accurate information is received and maintained.
  • Read and review Lease Review Checklists and reports (LRC’S)
  • Maintain and update security deposit reports
  • Other related duties as directed by the Property Manager
  • At QuadReal, we value performance.  This may mean occasional requirements to work extended hours during evenings or weekends to meet critical business deadlines.

Experience and Qualifications

  • 3+ years of related property management experience
  • Post-secondary degree in Business Administration or Property Management is considered an asset
  • Ability to communicate effectively and professionally, both oral and written with owners, tenants and team members
  • Strong time management skills with the ability to effectively manage tight deadlines and work under pressure to achieve quality results
  • Ability to resolve problems using facts and sound reasoning
  • Innovative approach with a willingness to manage and adapt to change
  • Intermediate skills with MS Office. Knowledge of Yardi is considered an asset
  • It is a requirement of this position to hold a property management real estate license in good standing. Those who are in the process or have not yet obtained their license will be granted a 1 year period from the start date of employment to complete the process. Successful completion will be an added condition of employment.
