Job Description

About League

League is one of the fastest-growing technology companies in Canada and the leading healthcare experience platform. Getting healthcare is often the easy part — finishing it is where things fall apart: people book the appointment and skip the follow-up, fill the prescription and stop taking it, get the referral and never make the call. That gap costs health plans and health systems money, and it costs people their health. League closes that gap — identifying what each person needs to do next, clearing what’s in their way, and getting it done, for the 70 million+ people whose care already runs through our platform. Health plans and health systems trust us to do this at scale. Organizations like Manulife, SCAN, Geisinger, and Medibank on the payer side, and Baptist and Shoppers Drug Mart on the provider side.

Position Summary:

The Manager, Marketing Programs & Events owns two connected things: League's flagship event, League CONNECT, and the year-round programs that engage the community of leaders who convene around it. Reporting to the Director, Brand Experience & Communications, this role goes beyond running a great event — it's about designing and stewarding an ongoing membership-style experience for a global community of healthcare leaders, of which CONNECT is the annual centrepiece.

This person co-designs the community's full calendar: exclusive programming like thought leadership content, regional roundtables, and other member benefits that keep this group engaged with League and each other between CONNECT events. They combine event operations rigor with the relationship-building instincts of someone who has run or worked closely with a membership organization (e.g., YPO or similar) — comfortable with executive-level stakeholders, and able to think in terms of a member journey rather than a single event lifecycle.

League's events and community programs are its most direct signal of market credibility, and the quality of this experience directly reflects our positioning as the world's leading platform for AI-driven healthcare CX.

What you'll own:

League CONNECT

  • Own end-to-end project management of League CONNECT alongside the Director, Brand Experience & Communications — League's primary brand moment and the community's flagship annual gathering.

  • Own the attendee/member experience from registration through post-event, ensuring every touchpoint reflects the elevated, exclusive nature of the community.

  • Own digital strategy and demand gen for the event, partnering with internal teams on promotional tactics (email, social, SMS) that drive registration, attendance, and engagement.

  • Apply strong digital marketing instincts across the full event lifecycle so the digital experience is as considered and on-brand as the in-person one.

  • Collaborate with the internal Events & Design team and external agency on event operations — budget tracking, timeline management, cross-functional coordination.

  • Manage hotel, venue, and event agency relationships — room blocks, F&B, on-site logistics.

  • Own on-site execution alongside the event agency — run-of-show, real-time troubleshooting, vendor coordination.

Community Program Design & Ownership

  • Co-design and own the annual calendar of programming for League's community of leaders — thought leadership content, regional roundtables, member-exclusive convenings, and other benefits that extend the community's engagement beyond CONNECT itself.

  • Define and evolve what "membership" in this community means — value proposition, benefits tiering, cadence of touchpoints, and criteria for participation — in partnership with the Director, Brand Experience & Communications.

  • Build relationships with community members between events; act as a consistent, recognizable point of contact who understands their priorities and can represent League with executive presence.

  • Identify and program regional roundtables and smaller-format gatherings that deepen relationships with members in priority markets.

  • Curate and manage thought leadership opportunities for members — speaking slots, content contributions, peer introductions — that reinforce their reason to stay engaged.

Event & Field Marketing Support

  • Support production and execution of Ignite, Power Up, and other owned events with the same operational rigor as CONNECT.

  • Maintain consistent standards for vendor management, budget tracking, and on-site execution across all owned events.

  • Support trade show and field marketing events where capacity allows — digital promotion, vendor coordination, on-site support, logistics.

What You'll Collaborate On

  • Partner closely with the Director, Brand Experience & Communications on community strategy, CONNECT planning, and program design — a highly collaborative relationship built on clear communication and tight handoffs.

  • Partner with Brand Design on creative execution across owned events and community touchpoints — activations, signage, digital assets, branded collateral.

  • Partner with sales and growth teams on enablement to drive registration, event education, and tracking against event and pipeline goals.

  • Coordinate with the Content Team on programming-to-logistics handoffs, and on thought leadership content produced for/with community members.

  • Work with the ABX team to align invitation strategy and on-site activation to account priorities.

You should definitely apply if…

  • You've owned complex, multi-stakeholder events end-to-end — and you're excited to extend that ownership into building an ongoing community, not just a single moment a year.

  • You have program management experience, ideally from a membership-based organization (e.g., YPO or similar) — you know how to design a calendar of benefits and touchpoints that keep senior members engaged year-round.

  • You bring executive presence — you're comfortable being a trusted point of contact for senior healthcare leaders and can represent League credibly in those relationships.

  • You bring extensive digital marketing experience alongside large-scale event expertise, and understand how digital and in-person experiences reinforce one another.

  • You're highly organized, deadline-driven, and able to run multiple workstreams — event logistics and community programming — at once without sacrificing quality.

  • You've managed vendors, agencies, and hotel/venue relationships, and you brief partners clearly and hold them accountable.

  • You're comfortable managing budgets — tracking spend, flagging variances, keeping programs on track.

  • You love agile, fast-paced environments and move easily across a wide variety of stakeholder groups, from internal teams to C-suite community members.

  • You're fluent with AI tools and believe the best outcomes come from humans and technology working together.

  • Bonus points for B2B or enterprise SaaS marketing experience, and experience in healthcare, health tech, or a regulated industry is an asset.

This role may not be for you if…

  • You prefer staying at the strategic altitude and find operational details draining — this role lives in both worlds, and both event execution and member relationship-building require sweating the details.

  • You like long lead times and predictable plans — we move fast, priorities shift, and you'll need to stay calm and organized when they do.

  • You'd rather own a single workstream deeply than juggle many at once — this role spans event operations and year-round community programming simultaneously.

  • You're looking for a heads-down individual contributor role with minimal cross-functional or external stakeholder interaction — this job is highly collaborative and relationship-driven by design.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
