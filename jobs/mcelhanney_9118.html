Job Description

What is an Expression of Interest?  This posting allows us to connect with students and graduates interested in upcoming internship or new graduate opportunities for the following intake periods: Winter 2027, Summer 2027, Fall 2027.

By applying, you'll be considered for upcoming opportunities that align with your background, interests, and preferred locations. Note: Apply to the "Expression of Interest" form only if there are no current opportunities posted that match area of interest / study.

Before submitting your application, please review our currently open internships (click to view postings) and new graduate postings (click to view postings) . If you find a position that aligns with your area of interest, we encourage you to apply directly to that posting.The Opportunity

Are you looking for an internship or new graduate opportunity with a company that helps shape communities across Western Canada?   At McElhanney, you’ll have the opportunity to:

Learn from experienced professionals and technical experts. Work on real life projects. Build your skills through hands-on experience. Develop your network through mentorship and collaboration. Explore a variety of career paths across our business.

Many of our early talent opportunities involve a mix of office-based work, field work, and travel to project sites, depending on the role. We are looking for adaptable interns and new graduates who are eager to learn, apply their academic knowledge, and support multidisciplinary teams with project delivery from design through to construction. Whether you are developing drawings, supporting field work, or conducting site inspections, you’ll gain the skills, training, and experience needed to solve real-world problems.

Our teams foster a welcoming environment that encourages learning, growth, and meaningful relationships. We have cultivated a fun and inclusive culture where many students choose to build long-term careers after graduation. With programs designed to support future leaders, McElhanney offers big company opportunities with a small company feel.

What Can We Offer You?

As this is an Expression of Interest posting, salary details will be confirmed within each job-specific post as opportunities become available. Final compensation will be reflective of the actual role, education, skills, and qualifications required. As a general guide, McElhanney offers hourly ranges of $23-$32 for civil-related internships, $28-$40 for civil-related new graduate full-time roles, $22-$28 for geo-related internships, and $24-$37 for geo-related new graduate full-time roles.

Work-Life Harmony: Balance your personal and professional commitments. Flexible hours to fit your lifestyle. Comprehensive Benefits for Full-time roles: Maternity and parental leave top up program, health and dental coverage, RRSP matching, and Employee & Family Assistance Program. Education Support & Scholarship Program: Available for Eligible Interns. Safe & Inclusive Workplace: A culture of respect and inclusion for all. Professional Development: Reimbursement for dues, educational assistance, mentorship program, workshops, and committee involvement. Career Progression: Support and mentorship to shape a career path that fits your goals. Relocation Support: Financial assistance if applicable.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
