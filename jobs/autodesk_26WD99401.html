Software Java Developer – Platform & Emerging Technologies

Position Overview

Autodesk is looking for a Software Engineer to help build the next generation of cloud services, data platforms, and AI-powered capabilities that support Autodesk products and customers worldwide.

In this role, you will contribute to the design, development, and evolution of scalable services and platform capabilities that power Autodesk's growing SaaS ecosystem. You will work alongside experienced engineers, architects, product managers, and UX designers to develop high-quality solutions that enable innovation across Autodesk's products and services.

You will have the opportunity to contribute to cloud-native services, APIs, data platforms, and emerging AI-powered experiences while gaining hands-on experience with modern software engineering practices and large-scale distributed systems.

This position is ideal for engineers who are passionate about technology, eager to learn, and excited to solve complex problems in a collaborative environment.

Responsibilities:

Software Development

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain high-quality software solutions.
  • Contribute to the development of cloud services, APIs, and platform capabilities.
  • Participate in the design and implementation of new features and enhancements.
  • Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code.
  • Develop automated tests to ensure software quality and reliability.
  • Participate in code reviews and apply software engineering best practices.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve software defects and production issues.

Platform & Cloud Development

  • Contribute to the development of scalable SaaS and cloud-based solutions.
  • Help build and maintain APIs and backend services.
  • Participate in improving platform reliability, performance, and operational excellence.
  • Support the evolution of data platforms and intelligent platform capabilities.
  • Learn and apply cloud-native development principles and modern engineering practices.

Collaboration & Learning

  • Collaborate with Product Managers, UX Designers, Architects, and fellow engineers to deliver customer value.
  • Participate in Agile planning, estimation, and delivery activities.
  • Work closely with team members to solve technical challenges and share knowledge.
  • Continuously learn new technologies, tools, and engineering practices.
  • Contribute ideas that improve products, processes, and developer experiences.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 2–5 years of software development experience.
  • Good knowledge of Java and object-oriented programming principles.
  • Familiarity with Spring Boot or interest in learning modern backend development technologies.
  • Understanding of software development fundamentals, data structures, and algorithms.
  • Basic understanding of APIs and web services.
  • Familiarity with relational databases or NoSQL databases.
  • Interest in cloud computing and SaaS technologies.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to learn quickly and adapt in a fast-paced environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Java and Spring Boot through academic, personal, internship, or professional projects.
  • Familiarity with AWS or other cloud platforms.
  • Knowledge of REST APIs and distributed systems concepts.
  • Experience with Git and source control systems.
  • Familiarity with automated testing practices.
  • Interest in data platforms, artificial intelligence, or cloud-native technologies.
  • Participation in open-source, academic, or personal software projects.

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on experience building SaaS and cloud-based platforms at scale.
  • Exposure to modern software architecture and distributed systems.
  • Opportunities to work with data platforms, APIs, and emerging AI technologies.
  • Mentorship from experienced engineers and technical leaders.
  • Experience working in a global engineering organization focused on innovation and continuous learning.

Key Success Factors

  • Curiosity and willingness to learn.
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills.
  • Commitment to software quality and continuous improvement.
  • Ability to solve problems effectively and take ownership of assigned work.
  • Passion for technology and innovation.
  • Desire to help build solutions that enable Autodesk customers to imagine, design, and make a better world.

Développeur(e) logiciel – Plateformes et technologies émergentes

Présentation du poste

Autodesk est à la recherche d’un(e) Ingénieur(e) logiciel passionné(e) pour contribuer au développement de la prochaine génération de services infonuagiques, de plateformes de données et de solutions alimentées par l’intelligence artificielle.

Dans ce rôle, vous participerez à la conception, au développement et à l’évolution de services et de capacités de plateforme évolutifs qui soutiennent l’écosystème SaaS d’Autodesk. Vous travaillerez aux côtés d’ingénieurs expérimentés, d’architectes, de gestionnaires de produit et de concepteurs UX afin de développer des solutions de grande qualité favorisant l’innovation à travers les produits et services Autodesk.

Vous aurez l’occasion de contribuer à des services cloud-native, à des API, à des plateformes de données ainsi qu’à des expériences émergentes propulsées par l’intelligence artificielle, tout en développant votre expertise dans les pratiques modernes de développement logiciel et les systèmes distribués à grande échelle.

Ce poste est idéal pour les ingénieurs passionnés par la technologie, motivés à apprendre et enthousiastes à l’idée de résoudre des problèmes complexes dans un environnement collaboratif.
