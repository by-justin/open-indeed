Job summary

We are seeking an experienced Senior IT Business Analyst to lead medium- to large-scale ITC and WSIB initiatives. In this role, you will provide strategic business analysis expertise to support transformation, process improvement, and technology-enabled change. You will work closely with business partners, project teams, and leadership to identify opportunities, define solutions, and drive successful outcomes.

What you’ll do

  • Lead complex business analysis for enterprise-wide projects and initiatives
  • Facilitate workshops to gather, validate, and refine business requirements, processes, workflows, and business rules
  • Translate business needs into clear functional and information requirements
  • Identify options, assess impacts, and recommend solutions that meet business objectives
  • Support business transformation, process redesign, and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Develop project proposals, business cases, cost-benefit analyses, and briefing materials
  • Partner with Project Managers, Solutions Architects, and Enterprise Architects on solution design and implementation
  • Provide planning input, functional leadership, and guidance throughout development, testing, and implementation
  • Lead discussions and build consensus with stakeholders at all levels of the organization
  • Mentor Business Analysts and provide advice on best practices, methodology, and project deliverables
  • Contribute subject matter expertise to procurement activities, including bid evaluation and vendor selection
  • Research and apply innovative tools, technologies, and methods to improve systems and services

What you bring:

Education

  • University degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Administration, Commerce, Science, or Social Science

Experience

  • Seven years of experience in business analysis, business process re-engineering, process remodelling, and business transformation

Disclosing conflicts of interest

As public servants, employees at the WSIB have a responsibility to act in an ethical way at all times to create a respectful workplace and maintain public trust. Job applicants are required to disclose any circumstance that could result in a real, potential or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where your private interests may impair or be perceived to impair the decisions you make in your official capacity. This may include: political activity, directorship, other outside employment and certain personal relationships (e.g. with current WSIB employees, customers and/or stakeholders). If you have any questions about conflict of interest obligations and/or how to make a disclosure, please contact the Talent Acquisition Centre at talentacquisitioncentre@wsib.on.ca.
