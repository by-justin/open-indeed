Role Responsibilities Are you passionate about sustainable forest management and ensuring Alberta's forests thrive for future generations? Join the Genetics and Tree Improvement Unit in a leadership role where you'll oversee critical reforestation seed programs, provincial genetics assets, and operational initiatives that directly support Alberta's reforestation and forest conservation objectives. This is an exciting opportunity to combine technical expertise, operational leadership, and strategic problem-solving while working at the forefront of forest genetic resource management. Reporting to the Provincial Seed Officer, the Genetics & Tree Improvement Technologist is responsible for the operational implementation and delivery of provincial programs related to reforestation seed management, tree improvement assets, genetic trials, and site operations at the Alberta Tree Improvement & Seed Centre and sites across Alberta. The position provides leadership to a team of staff, coordinates annual operational workflows, ensures compliance with provincial standards, and supports the long-term sustainability of Alberta's forest genetic resources. The role also serves as the backup to the Provincial Seed Officer during periods of absence. Your key responsibilities include, but are not limited to : Managing the provincial tree and shrub seed inventory, including storage, security, facility improvements, and distribution to forest nurseries. Ensuring the accurate and timely delivery of reforestation seed to support annual seedling production programs. Maintaining and enhancing the Nursery Seed Information Management System (NSIMS) and associated operational seed databases and records. Applying and supporting the implementation of Alberta’s Forest Genetic Resource Management and Conservation Standards (FGRMS) in operational activities. Reviewing, coordinating, and supporting responses to industry submissions related to forest genetic resource management requirements. Facilitating seed transfer variance requests and addressing self-reported planting compliance issues. Coordinating provincial genetics and tree improvement operational activities, including seed orchards, clone banks, provenance trials, and progeny trials. Assessing operational requirements and developing recommendations regarding priorities, resource allocation, maintenance strategies, and work plans. Identifying, evaluating, and resolving operational issues affecting provincial genetics and tree improvement assets. Conducting research and analysis to support long-term program sustainability and effectiveness. Representing operational interests on internal committees, working groups, and inter-jurisdictional initiatives. Managing site operations, including warehouse and cold storage facilities, asset management, equipment maintenance, and tenant coordination. Supervising staff, organizing work schedules, and ensuring annual operational requirements are completed effectively. Participating in training, technology transfer, outreach, and education initiatives related to reforestation seed and tree improvement. Supporting organizational health and safety initiatives and wildfire operations based on certification and operational requirements. Collaborating with internal partners to reconcile and maintain accurate provincial seed and regeneration information. Join a team dedicated to advancing Alberta's reforestation and forest genetic resource programs while helping ensure the long-term sustainability and resilience of our forests.
