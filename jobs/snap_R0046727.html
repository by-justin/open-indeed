Job Description

Snap Inc is a technology company. We believe the camera presents the greatest opportunity to improve the way people live and communicate. Snap contributes to human progress by empowering people to express themselves, live in the moment, learn about the world, and have fun together.

The Company operates Snapchat, a visual messaging app that enhances your relationships with friends, family, and the world, and Specs Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary dedicated to making computing more human, in addition to Bitmoji, Saturn, and other digital services.

Snap’s SPECS Studio is a full-service go-to-market strategy and production studio that creates marketing experiences and materials that shape how SPECS is positioned and brought to life for the world. Powered by a team of world-class experts at the forefront of Augmented Reality, we translate emerging technology into innovative, impactful, and effective strategies for Snap and our partners. Our mission is simple: use AR to transform the way people see and experience the world.

SPECS Studio is looking for an experienced Senior Creative Producer to help drive complex workstreams from ideation through to execution. The role will require oversight of multiple projects and cross-functional teams. The producer will manage the day-to-day needs of creative, design, social, strategy and marketing teams, and will ultimately be responsible for delivering unique and innovative campaigns that span across SPECS. This role will require a candidate to flex between traditional producer and project management responsibilities.

What you’ll do:

  • Oversee the full lifecycle of multiple complex campaign projects from pitch through delivery.
  • Ensure successful delivery of a full package of project deliverables inclusive of presentation decks, marketing materials, social content, short form video content, still photography, keynote presentations, long-form branded films, and more.
  • Create and maintain project timelines, deliverable trackers, resourcing plans, and budgets.
  • Lead internal/external reviews, prep client-ready presentations, and track feedback.
  • Manage key client communication throughout the development process, and partner with cross functional teams at Snap.
  • Identify risks and roadblocks and work with teams to resolve them quickly.
  • Work closely with the Director of SPECS Studio and the SPECS Studio Creative Director to manage the workload of designers, creatives, marketers, and strategists.

Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities:

  • Deep understanding of the creative production lifecycle from concept development through delivery.
  • Understanding of Augmented Reality/tech adjacent production workstreams.
  • Candidates should possess exceptional ability to manage multiple projects with varying deadlines and complexities. Attention to detail is crucial.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, including ability to be client facing.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • 5-8 years in video production or project management at a creative agency or in-house creative environment
  • Proven ability to manage a high volume of creative and campaign projects in parallel
  • Experience working with global brands in the advertising or marketing space
  • Comfortable working in fast-paced, deadline driven environments
  • Deep understanding of video, print, and digital production processes
  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in a related field

Preferred qualifications:

  • Passion for Snap’s products
  • Experience producing video shoots, ranging in budget
  • Experience in Agile project management
  • Familiarity with Adobe software, Google Suite, and PM tools
