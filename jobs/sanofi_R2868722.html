Job Title : Head of Platform Technologies, MSAT Recombinant Mammalian Drug Substance CoE

  • Location: Framingham, MA

About the Job

Sanofi Global MSAT (Manufacturing Sciences, Analytics, and Technology) acts as a crucial link between our R&D and Manufacturing facilities, playing a vital role in securing the present portfolio and delivering future launches of high-quality and innovative drugs and vaccines. By driving its own transformation, Global MSAT fulfils an important function by providing day-to-day Manufacturing Support, focusing on technical and process aspects, effective Life Cycle Management, process robustness enhancement, and yield improvement to optimize performance.

Join our global network and contribute to the launch of 3 to 5 new products annually across various modalities, enabling us to reach and serve more patients and communities. Collaborate with a team of talented and passionate international experts, scientists, and project leaders who are dedicated to inventing, designing, and implementing cutting-edge industrial chemical and biological processes, analytical methods, and process modelling, all in support of Sanofi’s best-in-class ambition.

The Head of Platform Technologies will lead a dynamic team of Subject Matter Experts within the MSAT Recombinant Mammalian Center of Excellence (CoE), located at Sanofi’s Massachusetts Framingham Bio-Campus. This team is responsible for establishing state‑of‑the‑art innovation laboratory infrastructures and enabling next‑generation platform technologies, including perfusion cell culture and continuous purification processes.

This senior leadership role will support implementation strategies for commercial programs by collaborating closely with R&D, Manufacturing, and external partners. The team will also spearhead global MSAT innovation initiatives across platforms in partnership with other MSAT groups, while representing MSAT’s scientific leadership and excellence in both internal and external forums.

We deliver 4.3 billion healthcare solutions to people every year, thanks to the flawless planning and meticulous eye for detail of our Manufacturing & Supply teams. With your talent and ambition, we can do even more to protect people from infectious diseases and bring hope to patients and their families.

Main Responsibilities

The Head of Platform Technologies will lead a Subject Matter Experts (SME) team to deliver the following core objectives:

  • Lead the evaluation of Sanofi’s next‑generation platform technologies including perfusion cell culture, continuous purification, advanced Process Analytical Technology (PAT), smart manufacturing, and emerging novel process chemistries. Develop the MSAT position on development data package requirements, at‑scale operational readiness, transferability, lab‑scale model expectations, and MSAT training and expertise needs. Ensure alignment of platform‑technology initiatives with broader organizational objectives such as 2nd‑generation program development, process robustness, and yield improvement.
  • Establish and maintain cutting‑edge innovation laboratory infrastructures within the COE. Partner with upstream, downstream, and analytical teams to advance lab digitization and automation. Build additional laboratory and pilot‑scale capabilities that embody Sanofi’s smart‑lab future concept.
  • Build and maintain strategic relationships with external partners and vendors. Oversee multiple collaboration projects with external suppliers to support manufacturing investigations, process development, raw material characterization, novel equipment and capability assessment, and co‑development of new technologies. Coordinate these activities seamlessly with MSAT, R&D, and Global Procurement functions.
  • Drive global MSAT innovation initiatives in collaboration with other technology platforms (Vaccines, Drug Product, Microbial). Promote knowledge sharing and best‑practice adoption across teams. Represent MSAT’s scientific excellence in internal forums (smart factory, digital transformation, eco-design, efficiency) and external forums (Bio-Industry forums, biomanufacturing conferences). Strengthening Sanofi’s reputation as a leader in biopharmaceutical manufacturing innovation.
  • Contribute to CoE activities by providing SME input and technical guidance for laboratory work, commercial program life cycle management, and technology transfers. Collaborate effectively with both local and network cross-function teams (e.g. lab, manufacturing, quality, regulatory, business etc.) as needed.
  • Mentor and develop team members, fostering their professional growth and technical expertise, while ensuring a diverse technical background and comprehensive skillset coverage for drug substance processes as part of CoE strategic workforce planning.

About You

Basic Qualifications

  • PhD degree in Chemical/Biological/ Sciences/Engineering - preferred. Master's degree - required.
  • 10+ years of experience related to biopharmaceutical industry - required.
  • 5+ years of hands-on experience in biologics drug substance process development, technology development or manufacturing support - required.
  • Demonstrated experience leading bioprocess and biomanufacturing innovation initiatives from concept through implementation. Deep knowledge of bioprocess development and operations (e.g., upstream, downstream, drug‑substance stability). Familiarity with laboratory, pilot‑scale, and large‑scale manufacturing operations.
  • Process knowledge across multiple modalities (e.g. mAbs, small protein and peptide, bioconjugates, nucleotides, Gene/cell therapy, organic chemistry etc.)
  • Prior experience of managing suppliers, contractor manufacturing or contract research partners
  • Excellent scientific background and effective communication skills. Demonstrated ability to manage and prioritize multiple projects and consistently meet deadlines. Highly motivated individual, with the ability to work independently as well as within cross-functional and cross-site teams
  • Oral and written fluency in English
