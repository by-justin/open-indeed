General Description:

The Senior Product Manager will be responsible for the success of their product features, from overall product alignment to specific product requirements through to delivery to the market and end-of-life. The selected candidate will have a strong background in software product management and be a cross-functional leader, strategic thinker, continuous learner, and active contributor with the proven ability to deliver high-quality software solutions to the market.

This role will be instrumental in executing on an existing roadmap as well as contributing to future strategic planning.

Responsibilities:

  • Exercise curiosity and empathy in order to build products that our customers love. Initiate and involve both internal and external stakeholders in user empathy and discovery sessions.

  • Develop and maintain the product and feature roadmap for Creator products that consider business viability, value to the customer, feasibility and usability in collaboration with engineering and design

  • Solicit inputs for your roadmap by collaborating with engineering, marketing, sales, customer engagement and other stakeholders that addresses pressing customer requirements;

  • Work closely with the leadership and strategy teams to translate the organizational strategy, business/market requirements, product backlog into a product roadmap;

  • Effectively translate the product vision and roadmap into product requirements for each initiative, including functional needs, supported use cases/workflows constraints, and success criteria;

  • Lead the complete product development lifecycle in an agile environment from developing use cases to product delivery in market, to post release and end of life;

  • Collaborate with engineering, design, functional areas with senior management or executives to ensure unit objectives are met;

  • Demonstrate the ability to drive product innovation, advocate for product direction and build new industry-leadingcreation products or services

  • Ensure all the portfolio’s release candidates meet product requirements;

  • Collaborate with customers, Market Strategy, Architecture, Design, Engineering, PMO, Implementation, Support and Marketing teams throughout the product life cycle to ensure new solutions meet our business and market objectives;

  • Develop and regularly maintain a data-driven product backlog to deliver on the product vision and roadmap. Regularly collect market input (customers, sales, service, competitive intelligence, etc.) and update the backlog accordingly;

  • Conduct feasibility analysis (ROI, etc.), business case development and build/buy/partner evaluations;

  • Represent D2L thought leadership at industry events.

Competencies:

  • Proven progress towards data-backed and measured business outcomes

  • Enables, Informs, and Communicates with various teams across the organization

  • Collaborates with development and design on features, prioritization, and defects

Skills

  • Creates a strategy & vision for larger initiatives, smaller experiences, or smaller cross-cutting initiatives

  • Creates roadmaps for tools or smaller experiences aligned to strategy

  • Plans, prioritizes, and visualizes thin slices

  • Go-To-Market planning

  • Acumen with Artificial Intelligence Tools

You should have demonstrated expertise in Product Management including product planning, market research, competitive analysis, product positioning, enablement, evangelism and product marketing. Typically candidates will have 8+ years’ experience in software product management to meet these requirements.
