Hook-up Senior Engineer / Technical Lead

Position Summary

Serve as the technical lead for Hook-up projects, integrating design, construction, quality, and operational requirements to ensure the timely delivery of utility systems required for tool installation and volume manufacturing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the planning, execution, and schedule management of semiconductor fab Hook-up (secondary distribution) projects.
  • Coordinate all phases of tool installation, including Move-in, Pre-Hook-up, Hook-up, and Turn-on activities.
  • Collaborate closely with Facilities (FAC), Manufacturing Engineering (ME), Construction, Industrial Engineering (IE), Environmental Health & Safety (EHS), and contractors to ensure seamless project execution.
  • Review Hook-up engineering designs, BIM models, and construction plans to ensure compliance with safety, quality, and production requirements.
  • Manage project schedule, construction quality, cost, and risk issues to achieve key project milestones.
  • Lead on-site issue resolution, interface management, and resource coordination across multiple stakeholders.
  • Drive QA/QC verification, testing, commissioning, acceptance, and system handover activities.
  • Develop, standardize, and continuously improve Hook-up procedures, construction standards, and training programs.
  • AI & Digital Competency Demonstrated ability to leverage AI-powered tools, data analytics, and digital technologies to enhance project planning, engineering execution, risk management, and operational efficiency.Experience in applying AI-assisted solutions for design review, project controls, workflow automation, knowledge management, and continuous improvement initiatives.Strong commitment to driving digital transformation and fostering the adoption of innovative technologies across engineering and construction teams.
