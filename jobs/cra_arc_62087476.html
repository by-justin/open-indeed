INTENT OF THE PROCESS

Short term contract positions located at 1050 Notre Dame Avenue, Sudbury, Ontario will primarily be staffed with a duration up to 6 months. Shifts for these opportunities may vary between the hours of 7:00 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. in the Eastern Standard Time Zone.   No permanent offers will be made from this staffing process.

The pool established from this staffing process may be used to staff similar positions.

As there is an immediate need to staff these positions, applications will be processed when they are received.  For this reason, we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible.  Note that the closing date and time on this notice of job opportunity may change.

We may give preference to the first 200 applicants who meet the staffing requirements. Candidates will be screened against the prerequisite staffing requirements on an on-going basis. If you are considered further, we will notify you in writing of the next steps in this staffing process.

Assessments will take place in waves, and you may be placed in a pool and appointed accordingly. Additional candidates may be considered as needed. If you are considered further, we will notify you in writing of the next step in this staffing process.

Once you accept an offer of employment, you may not be eligible for other opportunities from this staffing process until your current contract ends, to minimize business disruption. You will, however, remain in the pool(s) until they expire.

Types of staffing requirements

Minimum : the minimum staffing requirements indicated in this notice of job opportunity are mandatory for all positions to be staffed. The minimum staffing requirements are: education, language, performance level, security status and/or clearance and suitability.

Essential: essential staffing requirements indicated in this notice of job opportunity will be assessed and may be used to identify candidates for positions to be staffed.

Asset: asset staffing requirements indicated in this notice of job opportunity may be assessed and may be used to identify candidates for positions to be staffed. You will be notified in advance of any asset staffing requirements being assessed, at the evaluation stage, that are not listed on this notice.

To apply

Who can apply:

Persons residing within reasonable commuting distance (under 125 kilometres) of the Sudbury Tax Centre located at 1050 Notre Dame Avenue, Sudbury Ontario.

Preference for appointment will be given to veterans, Canadian citizens and permanent residents.

If you are a candidate from the general public, you will have to pay for any expenses related to your participation in this staffing process, as well as any relocation costs if you receive an offer of employment from this process.

Qualified members and former members of the Canadian Armed Forces who receive an offer of employment are not entitled to relocation costs from the CRA. They may, however, be entitled to a final relocation from the Department of National Defence.

Please note that this process will be used to staff positions at the Sudbury Tax Centre located at 1050 Notre Dame Avenue . Many of the job opportunities will require an on-site presence 5 days a week.  Further information will be provided if you are selected for the position.

Candidates may only submit one application.  Subsequent applications will not be considered.
