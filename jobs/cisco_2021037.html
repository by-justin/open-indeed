Role Overview

We are seeking an Account Executive to manage the Rakuten account. In this role, you will build strong, long-term relationships with key decision-makers and leverage Cisco’s broad portfolio—including secure networking (switching, wireless, routing, firewall), AI, data center, and collaboration—to drive sustainable business growth and contribute to our customer's success.

You will work closely with internal SE teams and orchestrate experts from Services, Technical Support, US Headquarters Development, Delivery, Finance, and Legal departments to propose optimal solutions and resolve complex challenges.

Key Responsibilities

As the core of the account team, you will lead sales activities to support the digital transformation of our service provider customer:

  • Execute high-touch sales and develop/implement strategic account plans for Rakuten Group companies.
  • Maximize influence on business decisions by acting as a strategic advisor to IT leaders and CXOs, coordinating with internal experts when necessary.
  • Manage the sales pipeline accurately using CRM tools (e.g., Salesforce) and formulate weekly, monthly, and quarterly sales forecasts.
  • Collaborate with internal departments (Finance, Procurement, IT, etc.) to improve sales workflows and resolve bottlenecks.
  • Listen to and organize customer challenges and technical requirements, and lead proposal activities.
  • Maintain close coordination with US Headquarters and domestic departments to advance business negotiations.
  • Ensure alignment across the team by sharing customer status and plans with other members.
  • Perform daily tasks using Microsoft Office applications and other web-based tools.

Requirements

  • 10+ years of experience in IT infrastructure (office network, data center, security) or SP infrastructure business.
  • 5+ years of experience in business development or pre-sales activities.
  • 3+ years of experience in SaaS or software sales.
  • Experience driving business in large, cross-functional organizations as a high-touch account manager.
  • Language: Native-level Japanese; Business-level English (communication and negotiation).
  • Ability to execute sales methodologies based on strategic thinking and strong negotiation skills.
  • Team-oriented: Ability to share information, seek advice from others, and support/mentor team members.
  • Respect for business partners, both internal and external.
  • High coachability: A proactive attitude toward accepting advice/feedback and driving self-growth.
  • GRIT: Ability to take action to persevere through challenging situations.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Expertise in data center infrastructure (including networking, computing, AI accelerators) or public cloud.
  • Pre-sales experience for service providers or IT service companies.
  • Proven track record of expanding business using cutting-edge technology.
  • Experience using various business support tools and applications.
  • Project leadership experience.
  • Completed SPIN, MEDDPICC, and/or ITIL training/certifications.
