Job Description

Job Title:   Solutions Design Manager Job Location: Hebron, Kentucky

We have an exciting opportunity for a Solutions Design Manager for our AFR Team. This role is responsible for leading the strategic design, governance, and execution of end-to-end logistics solutions for DHL's global and regional Life Sciences & Healthcare customers. This role provides executive leadership for solution design, pricing strategy, operational excellence, and innovation while ensuring compliance with global regulatory standards, including GxP and GDP.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop and execute the regional Life Sciences & Healthcare Solution Design strategy aligned with DHL's overall business objectives and growth plans.
  • Establish long-term solution design capabilities that differentiate DHL in the pharmaceutical and healthcare logistics market.
  • Serve as the senior subject matter expert for Life Sciences & Healthcare solution design, cold chain logistics, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence.
  • Lead strategic initiatives that improve profitability, customer experience, scalability, and operational efficiency.
  • Drive innovation by identifying emerging technologies, digital capabilities, automation opportunities, and new service offerings within Life Sciences logistics.

Commercial Leadership

  • Partner with Commercial Leadership to develop growth strategies for strategic and multinational Life Sciences customers.
  • Lead complex customer pursuits by developing innovative and differentiated logistics solutions that maximize customer value and financial performance.
  • Provide executive leadership during customer presentations, solution workshops, and strategic business reviews.
  • Support contract negotiations by balancing commercial competitiveness with operational feasibility and profitability.
  • Drive pricing governance and ensure consistency in financial assumptions, investment decisions, and solution profitability.

Solution Design & Governance

  • Provide executive oversight of end-to-end solution design activities across Air Freight, Ocean Freight, Customs, Warehousing, and Integrated Logistics services.
  • Establish governance, methodologies, standards, and best practices for solution design across the organization.
  • Ensure all solution designs incorporate operational excellence, quality requirements, regulatory compliance, systems integration, risk mitigation, and financial performance.
  • Lead investment evaluations and business case development for new customer opportunities and strategic initiatives.
  • Oversee development of operating models, implementation strategies, validation plans, and transition frameworks for complex customer implementations.

Financial & Business Management

  • Own solution profitability by overseeing pricing strategy, cost modeling, financial analysis, and investment recommendations.
  • Lead development of business cases, customer P&L models, capital investment requests, and return-on-investment analyses.
  • Monitor solution performance against financial and operational KPIs while driving continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Identify opportunities to optimize costs while maintaining service excellence and regulatory compliance.
  • Support annual planning, forecasting, and strategic investment decisions for the LSHC portfolio.

Operational Excellence & Compliance

  • Ensure all solutions meet applicable regulatory requirements including GxP, GDP, IATA, and customer-specific quality standards.
  • Partner with Quality and Operations leadership to develop validation strategies, quality frameworks, and compliance governance.
  • Sponsor continuous improvement initiatives that enhance operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and quality performance.
  • Drive standardization of processes, documentation, implementation methodologies, and operational toolkits across the LSHC business.

Leadership & Organizational Development

  • Lead, mentor, and develop Solution Design Managers and Solution Engineers while building a high-performing organization.
  • Establish performance objectives, succession planning, and talent development strategies for the Solution Design function.
  • Foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, accountability, and continuous improvement.
  • Lead cross-functional teams supporting strategic customer implementations and business transformation initiatives.

Stakeholder Management

  • Build strong relationships with regional and global business leaders, customers, strategic partners, and regulatory stakeholders.
  • Represent DHL at executive customer meetings, industry conferences, and Life Sciences forums.
  • Influence senior leadership regarding investment priorities, market opportunities, organizational capabilities, and strategic initiatives.
  • Collaborate with Global Product Management to ensure regional alignment with global standards and product development.

Skills / Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Life Sciences, Business Administration, Engineering, or related discipline.
  • Master's degree (MBA or equivalent) preferred.
  • 5+ years of progressive experience in Life Sciences & Healthcare logistics, supply chain management, or solution design.
  • Minimum 5 years of leadership experience managing teams within a matrix organization.
  • Extensive experience designing complex global logistics solutions for pharmaceutical and healthcare customers.
  • Strong knowledge of GDP, GxP, IATA, regulatory compliance, cold chain logistics, and quality management systems.
  • Demonstrated success leading strategic customer pursuits and complex solution implementations.
  • Proven experience managing executive-level customer relationships.
  • Strong commercial acumen with extensive experience in pricing strategy, financial modeling, investment analysis, and P&L management.

Knowledge & Skills

  • Executive leadership and strategic planning
  • Life Sciences & Healthcare supply chain expertise
  • Enterprise solution architecture and design
  • Financial planning, business case development, and investment analysis
  • Pricing governance and profitability management
  • Executive stakeholder engagement
  • Organizational leadership and talent development
  • Cross-functional leadership within global matrix organizations
  • Change management and business transformation
  • Contract negotiations and commercial strategy
  • Risk management and regulatory compliance
  • Project portfolio governance
  • Strong executive presentation and communication skills
  • Advanced analytical and decision-making capabilities
