Move the World as an Electrical Design Engineer with ABB

Would you like to be part of digital development and contribute to innovations in engineering documentation? Are you interested in working with databases and CAD tools, and looking for a meaningful role within a supportive and highly skilled team? We are looking for a new colleague to join us. We will provide comprehensive onboarding, continuous support, and the opportunity to work on projects with an international reach.

What you can expect from us:

  • Working with ePlan and SAP systems
  • Preparing master data and developing logical rules for generating engineering drawings
  • Collaborating with colleagues across multiple departments
  • Supporting users of EPLAN and its extensions, both locally and within international teams
  • Participating in innovative projects and developing new solutions

What we are looking for:

  • Secondary school or university degree in Electrical Engineering (also open to graduates with experience in C++ or C# programming), Industrial Automation, or Measurement and Control Engineering
  • Ability to read and create technical documentation
  • Experience with EPLAN Electric P8 is an advantage
  • Knowledge of scripting or logical expressions is an advantage
  • English language skills at least at B1 level
  • Willingness to learn and continuously develop
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Independent and analytical approach to tasks
  • Team spirit, communication skills, and enthusiasm for learning new things
  • Category B driving license is an advantage

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.
