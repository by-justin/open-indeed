【業務内容】 弊社のフィールドセールス部門において、重要な役割を担うのがアカウントエグゼクティブです。新規顧客の獲得に注力しながら、顧客開拓の推進力となっております。勤勉で堅実なプロフェッショナルで構成される当チームは、レガシープラットフォームの壁を乗り越え、先進的なエンタープライズマネジメントクラウドを導入を検討している新規顧客を導く鍵となる存在です。チームとして、私たちは顧客とパートナーシップを組み、最適なソリューションを生み出すことで長期的な価値提供や、顧客満足度の向上にも努めています。

  • 担当エリアにおける重要な商談機会を選別し、対象を絞り込み、成約に導くための戦略を立てます。

  • 既存顧客 / 見込み顧客、パイプライン、サービス予測データを正確かつ速やかに更新します。

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together. Those in our remote "home office" roles also have the opportunity to come together in our offices for important moments that matter.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
