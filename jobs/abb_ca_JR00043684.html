Position Overview & Responsibilities

This position is responsible for supplier quality control, incoming inspection, process quality supervision, and issue closure for primary switchgear products. It ensures stable and reliable quality of purchased / outsourced parts, compliance with internal standards, customer requirements and industry regulations, and supports production delivery and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform incoming inspection of supplier parts, verify and judge quality of key components, complete inspections per standards and issue inspection records and reports.
  • Track and resolve incoming quality anomalies and supplier part quality issues on site, drive root cause analysis, corrective actions and closed-loop verification.
  • Participate in supplier qualification, process audits and annual performance evaluation; assist in establishing and updating supplier quality files.
  • Participate in First Article Inspection (FAI), test and validate supplier samples to confirm compliance with technical and quality requirements.
  • Drive supplier quality improvement projects, implement corrective and preventive actions, and enhance supplier quality stability and delivery capability.
  • Assist in training on quality tools, inspection standards and incoming material control; promote quality awareness and inspection specifications.
  • Properly maintain inspection equipment and measuring tools, conduct daily checks and calibration to ensure compliant inspection conditions.
  • Support customer audits, internal audits and FAT-related supplier quality activities.
  • Complete other quality control and external support tasks assigned by supervisors.

Qualifications

Education: Bachelor’s degree or above in Electrical Engineering, Automation, Mechanical Engineering or related fields.

Work Experience: 2+ years of relevant experience in supplier quality, incoming inspection (IQC), or SQE in electrical industry; outstanding fresh graduates may be considered.

Professional Knowledge:

  • Familiar with primary switchgear product structure, component characteristics and production processes.
  • Proficient in national / international standards and quality inspection specifications for electrical industry.
  • Understand supplier management, incoming inspection, first article inspection and non-conforming material control processes.
  • Be familiar with the basic properties, compositions, processing characteristics of materials such as metals and insulating parts, as well as industry standards. Skills:
  • Skilled in office software; able to independently complete inspection reports, data statistics and improvement reports such as 8D.
  • Master basic quality management tools (5Why, Fishbone, CPK, SPC, etc.) and able to apply them in practice.
  • are preferred.
  • High & low voltage electrician certificate or quality-related certificates are preferred.

Language: Professional English reading skills; basic English listening, speaking and writing skills; able to read English standards and technical documents.

Competencies: Result-oriented, responsible, with good communication, coordination and cross-departmental collaboration skills; adhere to the principle of “Safety First, Quality Priority”.
