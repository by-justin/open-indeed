Senior Pricing & Packaging Manager

We are seeking a detailed-oriented and motivated individual to join the Pricing & Packaging team. You are a strategic problem solver, thrive at building monetization strategies and enjoy a fast-paced & high-growth environment. Your role will be pivotal in driving long-term growth by executing pricing, packaging, and monetization initiatives across Fastly’s product portfolio.

What You'll Do:

  • Partner closely with the Product team build and define strategies and product roadmap

  • Lead pricing & packaging programs increase revenue and growth, capitalize on strengths and mitigate challenges for products

  • End-to-end commercialization and operationalization delivering programs from concept to launch partnering with Sales and Marketing, Legal, Finance, Business systems and others, owning every step along the way driving consensus cross-functionally

  • Conduct qualitative and quantitative research to test strategy hypotheses, review and analyze pricing trends and discounting, validate customer's willingness to pay and product positioning

  • Research buyer motivations, analyze performance data and market trends to drive frequent iteration of pricing for Fastly's products

  • Provide expertise to teams onpricing metrics, pricing & packaging strategies and frameworks.

  • Ensure consistency across the entire product portfolio

What We're Looking For:

  • 7-10 total years’ work experience in product management, pricing & packaging, management consulting or strategic finance field

  • Strong experience executing on monetization strategies for B2B, SaaS and usage-based companies in related industries

  • Excellent quantitative and data analytical skills. High proficiency in Excel, Looker, Salesforce or other business applications to gather data, structure and analyze into actionable pricing strategies and business insights

  • Ability to thrive and form well-reasoned opinions from multiple perspectives and limited information

  • Customer focused, seeking a deep understanding of target users and are adept at understanding their problems and arriving at elegant solutions to those problems

  • Self directed, highly motivated and intellectually curious to tackle complex challenges and deliver discernible impact on the business

  • Great presentation, communication and influence skills that tell a cohesive story and drive consensus to decision making

  • Foundational understanding of accounting and revenue recognition principles

We’ll be super impressed if you have experience in any of these:

  • Experience at infrastructure, cloud networking or security companies

  • Experience in driving requirements in CPQ or other quote-to-cash systems
