Job description :

Do you have solid experience in procurement or supplier management, have you led complex cross-functional projects, and are you looking to further your career in customer service? This position is for you!

The A220 commercial aircraft program is looking for an A ftermarket Supplier Account Manager - Equipment & Systems to join our A220 Customer Service team based in Mirabel (Quebec, Canada), where you will work jointly with the entire Customer Service and Procurement organization to support A220 operators.

You will be part of the Fleet Efficiency team , which is responsible for ensuring the safe and economical operation of the A220 fleet in service.

Reporting to the Head of Supplier Support Management and Continuous Improvement of the A220 Fleet Efficiency team, you will work directly with the aftermarket supplier management team based in Toulouse, France, to support A220 operators.

As an Aftermarket Supplier Account Manager - Equipment & Systems , you will serve as the primary interface for Airbus to drive the performance of suppliers and service providers within the support and services ecosystem.

Your working environment:

The A220 commercial aircraft Programme is headquartered in Mirabel, in the greater Montreal area, where the vibrant city of Montreal is just a stone’s throw away. Known for its peaceful and safe environment, the region offers a high quality of life for younger or experienced professionals and families, perfectly balancing career opportunities and outdoor activities that makes it a truly special place to call home.

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, annual bonus, group insurance plan, pension plan and share purchase plan.
  • Work / Life Balance:  A hybrid working policy, on-site cafeterias and a highly competitive holiday policy.
  • Wellbeing/Health: Employee Assistance Program (EAP), Discount Program and on-site Medical Service.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects  with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

Your challenges:

  • Airlines and Customers Support Negotiate supplier support conditions in order to protect Airlines and Airbus interest for support activities (such as but not limited to Spares price, GMTBUR, GDMC..)Act in escalation on customer issues and contribute to communication and events with customersControl and run the annual spares price variation
  • Be the S focal point for Equipment & Systems Be the primary S focal point for PE MFTs and contribute to MFT strategyIntegrate S (on the overall support & services perimeter) view in Call for Tender selection reportsSupport procurement projects
  • Manage suppliers Contribute to Procurement Senior Management Meeting and Organize S Senior Management MeetingDrive Supplier Support Rating and associated improvement plan for Airlines and AirbusLead escalations to suppliers on specific topics
  • Integrate overall Support & Services view Contribute to Service Forum to deploy new business modelsAct in escalation on supplier support & services related issuesOrganize periodic aftermarket review with key suppliersSupport customer services projects with impact or contribution from suppliers

Your boarding pass:

  • Education & Experience: Holder of a bachelor's degree in a relevant field (e.g., Aeronautical Engineering, Project or Supplier Management) or equivalent experience, you have at least 10 years of experience in Supplier, program, or project management in a technical or aeronautical environment;
  • Proactive & dynamic mindset with a strong sense of drive and initiative;
  • Ability to work effectively in a remote, multi-time-zone environment as the sole team member in Montreal (with the entire team based in Toulouse);
  • Proven leadership and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to drive cross-functional collaboration;
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, with a strong team-player and positive attitude;
