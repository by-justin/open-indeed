About the job

This is a rare and exciting opportunity to build something from the ground up. As Data Migration Lead , you will be the single global control point for all Workday data migrations, bulk loads, and mass data movements at Sanofi. This is a newly created, critical leadership role within the Data Governance and Master Data Management (MDM) team — and you will have the mandate, authority, and resources to establish the governance framework, templates, and processes that will define how Sanofi manages data migration globally.

You will lead a team of four Data Migration and Mapping Analysts — all new hires — and work at the intersection of technology, data governance, and business transformation. From M&A integrations to global reorganizations, your work will directly enable Sanofi's operational excellence, AI readiness, and strategic growth agenda.

If you thrive on building new capabilities, driving organizational change, and making a measurable impact on a global scale, this role is for you.

Key Responsibilities

Data Migration Leadership & Governance

  • Own all Workday data mapping, migration, and bulk data load execution globally
  • Act as the single control point for all mass data movement in Workday
  • Ensure all data loaded into or extracted from Workday adheres to global data standards, governance policies, and quality rules
  • Control and govern who can execute mass loads globally, preventing uncontrolled or local loads that bypass governance frameworks

Workday Data Mapping & Integration

  • Own source-to-Workday field mapping for all inbound and outbound data flows
  • Define and document transformation logic, validation rules, and value conversions
  • Maintain a central, controlled mapping repository aligned to the global data model
  • Ensure all mapping follows Workday configuration standards and global data governance requirements

Bulk Load Management & Execution

  • Lead all Workday bulk loads using Enterprise Interface Builder (EiB) and other approved tools
  • Design templates, sequencing, dependencies, and validation checks for all load activities
  • Manage test loads, reconciliations, and production loads end-to-end
  • Create and maintain migration playbooks, mock loads, and cutover plans to ensure a repeatable, audit-safe migration framework

M&A and Divestiture Excellence

  • Lead Workday data migration for all acquisitions, mergers, and divestitures globally
  • Assess incoming HR data from acquired entities and map to Sanofi's Workday global structure
  • Define the cleansing, transformation, and loading approach for acquired employee populations
  • Manage Workday data extraction and separation activities for divestitures
  • Ensure Day 1 readiness for all employee data in Workday during transactions
  • Coordinate with HRIS, IT, Legal, and integration teams throughout the transaction lifecycle

Organizational Change Support

  • Lead mass structural and organizational data loads in Workday
  • Execute reorganizations, cost center changes, and hierarchy restructuring
  • Drive global standardization and harmonization loads across the organization
  • Support reporting accuracy and AI readiness through correct and consistent data structures

Data Quality & Compliance

  • Enforce data quality standards before any Workday load is executed
  • Partner with Data Governance and Data Quality teams on remediation act
  • vities
  • Reject or escalate non-compliant data to protect system integrity and reporting accuracy
  • Maintain a full audit trail of all Workday mass loads and migrations
  • Ensure compliance with data security and privacy regulations
  • Support internal and external audits with complete documentation

Stakeholder Collaboration

  • Serve as the single global entry point for all Workday migration and bulk load requests
  • Partner with HRIS/Workday technology teams and transformation programmes
  • Advise projects on feasibility, sequencing, and risk management
  • Collaborate with P&C, Org Design, Tech Strategy, GPO, and People Services teams
  • Maintain a controlled library of mapping files, EiB templates, migration playbooks, load runbooks, reconciliation reports, and rollback procedures

Team Leadership

  • Build and lead a team of 4 Data Migration and Mapping Analysts from the ground up
  • Establish team processes, standards, and best practices for data migration governance
  • Provide mentoring, performance management, and professional development for team members
  • Foster a culture of quality, accountability, and continuous improvement

What Success Looks Like

  • Workday data is fit for global reporting, payroll, and AI use
  • Clean M&A and divestiture data transitions with Day 1 readiness achieved
  • No uncontrolled mass updates occurring in Workday
  • Full mapping transparency and reuse across the organization
  • Improved data quality post-load with reduced remediation needs
  • Audit-ready Workday data load environment at all times
  • All mass loads following approved templates and governance processes
  • Teams across the organization adopting centralized templates and playbooks
  • Reduced external consulting spend for data migration work
