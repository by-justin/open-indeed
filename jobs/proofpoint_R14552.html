First-Line Sales Leader (Regional Sales Manager)

About the Role

Proofpoint is seeking a high-impact First-Line Sales Leader to lead, coach, and develop a team of Account Executives responsible for acquiring new customers and expanding strategic relationships within a defined territory. This leader will be accountable for delivering predictable revenue growth while fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous development.

The ideal candidate is a modern sales leader who combines exceptional coaching skills, operational rigor, and data-driven decision making with a passion for developing talent. They embrace AI-enabled selling, leverage inspection to improve execution, and consistently raise the performance of their team through disciplined coaching and customer-centric leadership.

Key Responsibilities

Revenue Leadership

  • Lead a team of Account Executives to consistently achieve and exceed quarterly and annual revenue targets in the Healthcare vertical.
  • Drive predictable revenue through rigorous pipeline management, forecast accuracy, and disciplined business reviews.
  • Develop and execute territory and account strategies that maximize new logo acquisition and expansion opportunities.
  • Inspect leading performance indicators to proactively identify risks, accelerate deal progression, and improve conversion rates.

MEDDICC Inspection Excellence

  • Establish a culture of inspection over inspection for compliance, ensuring MEDDICC is used as a strategic coaching framework rather than a qualification checklist.
  • Conduct regular opportunity inspections focused on Metrics, Economic Buyer engagement, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion strength, and Competition.
  • Coach sellers to improve deal quality, eliminate blind spots, and increase forecast confidence through disciplined MEDDICC execution.
  • Drive consistent opportunity reviews that improve win rates, shorten sales cycles, and increase average deal value.
  • Partner with Sales Engineering and executive leadership on strategic deal coaching for the organization’s most critical opportunities.

AI-Enabled Sales Coaching & Productivity

  • Champion the adoption of AI-powered sales tools to improve seller productivity, coaching effectiveness, and customer engagement.
  • Leverage AI-generated insights to identify coaching opportunities, analyze customer interactions, improve pipeline health, and accelerate seller development.
  • Utilize conversational intelligence, account insights, and productivity platforms to deliver personalized coaching at scale.
  • Encourage responsible experimentation with AI to automate administrative work, improve account planning, enhance executive communications, and maximize customer-facing time.
  • Foster a culture of continuous learning where technology amplifies human coaching rather than replaces it.

Coaching & Talent Development

  • Recruit, onboard, develop, and retain top-performing sales talent.
  • Conduct structured weekly coaching focused on pipeline creation, opportunity progression, MEDDICC execution, executive engagement, negotiation strategy, and account planning.
  • Build individualized development plans aligned with career growth and performance objectives.
  • Develop future leaders by providing stretch opportunities, mentorship, and regular performance feedback.
  • Create an environment of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Sales Execution

  • Partner with Sales Engineering, Customer Success, Marketing, Channel, SDR, and Customer Advocacy teams to deliver exceptional customer outcomes.
  • Participate in executive customer meetings and strategic deal reviews.
  • Guide complex enterprise sales cycles involving multiple stakeholders and executive sponsors.
  • Ensure consistent execution of Proofpoint’s sales methodology, sales process, and operating cadence.

Business Planning & Operational Excellence

  • Develop quarterly business plans aligned with corporate objectives.
  • Maintain high forecast accuracy through disciplined inspection and data-driven decision making.
  • Analyze territory performance, competitive dynamics, whitespace opportunities, and pipeline health.
  • Use dashboards, analytics, and AI-driven insights to improve operational efficiency and sales execution.
  • Establish an operating rhythm that balances accountability, coaching, and execution excellence.

Leadership & Culture

  • Lead by example with integrity, humility, customer obsession, and operational excellence.
  • Foster an inclusive, high-performance culture where accountability and collaboration thrive.
  • Champion innovation by encouraging adoption of modern selling techniques, digital engagement, and AI-enabled workflows.
  • Reinforce Proofpoint’s values through consistent leadership behaviors and decision making.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of frontline sales leadership experience in enterprise SaaS or cybersecurity.
  • Demonstrated success leading teams that consistently exceed revenue targets.
  • Proven experience hiring, coaching, and developing high-performing Account Executives.
  • Deep expertise in MEDDICC (or MEDDPICC) opportunity qualification and inspection.
  • Experience leading complex enterprise sales cycles involving C-level stakeholders.
  • Strong forecasting, pipeline inspection, and business planning capabilities.
  • Experience leveraging CRM analytics, sales technology, and AI-powered productivity tools to improve sales execution.
  • Exceptional communication, executive presence, and coaching skills.
  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience selling cybersecurity, cloud security, email security, identity security, or data protection solutions.
  • Experience leading geographically distributed enterprise sales teams.
  • Familiarity with AI-powered sales platforms such as Gong, Salesforce Einstein, Microsoft Copilot, Clari, Outreach, or similar technologies.
  • Experience working with channel partners and strategic alliances.
  • MBA or advanced business education is a plus.

Success Measures

Within the first 12 months, successful leaders will:

  • Consistently achieve or exceed team quota.
  • Improve forecast accuracy and pipeline quality.
  • Increase MEDDICC adoption and opportunity quality across the team.
  • Demonstrate measurable improvements in win rates, sales cycle efficiency, and average deal size.
  • Increase seller productivity through effective AI adoption and coaching.
  • Recruit, develop, and retain top-performing talent.
  • Build a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
