Acting as a critical control point within operations, this role ensures the timely and efficient movement of time-sensitive medical specimens while maintaining high service standards and regulatory compliance.Operating in a fast-paced, high-volume environment, the Dispatcher is accountable for dynamically managing routes, prioritizing STAT and scheduled requests, and proactively mitigating service risks that may impact client commitments or laboratory operations. The role requires strong decision-making, continuous situational awareness, and the ability to adapt quickly to changing operational demands.This position plays a key role in supporting service excellence by monitoring on-time performance, optimizing route execution in real time, and serving as the primary point of contact for drivers and internal stakeholders across a multi-region and evolving national logistics network. Status: 1-year full-time contract Benefits eligible (37.5 hours)Vacancy type: ExistingDiscipline: LogisticsShift: This role is responsible for covering all shifts (Days, Afternoons, Evenings) in dispatch when team members are off.Work Style: On-siteInternal Posting Deadline: August 29, 2026, at 5 PM EST Why Dynacare is an amazing place for YOU:
