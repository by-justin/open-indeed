YOUR IMPACT

Cisco is seeking a Solutions Engineer based in the National Capital Region to work with customers across the Canadian Federal public sector.

As a Solutions Engineer, you will combine technical expertise, consultative engagement, and business acumen to help customers address complex operational and technology challenges. Working closely with Account Executives, specialists, partners, and customers, you will shape secure and scalable solutions and develop technology roadmaps that support customer mission, business, and operational outcomes.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Strategic Consultative Partnership:  Act as a trusted technical advisor to Canadian public sector customers, applying architecture, delivery, and implementation expertise to align solutions with their mission, business, and operational objectives.
  • Technical Sales Leadership:  Partner with Account Executives and cross-functional teams throughout the pre-sales cycle. Serve as the technical lead for discovery, solution design, customer presentations, proposal development, demonstrations, and high-impact proof-of-concept activities.
  • Discovery and Solution Alignment:  Lead comprehensive discovery sessions to uncover mission-critical challenges, technical requirements, constraints, and desired outcomes. Translate these insights into architectures that address applicable regulatory, security, and operational requirements in Canadian Federal environments.
  • Strategic Roadmap Development:  Collaborate across Cisco, customer, and partner teams to develop, document, and present clear technology roadmaps that guide customers from their current environments toward their desired future state.
  • Technology Leadership and Advocacy:  Maintain current knowledge of Cisco technologies, industry trends, and emerging capabilities—including AI infrastructure—and communicate their relevance and business value to technical teams, business stakeholders, and executive leaders.
  • Complex Problem-Solving:  Draw on Cisco’s broad portfolio and partner ecosystem to architect secure, resilient, scalable, and operationally practical solutions for complex customer environments.
  • Customer Influence and Business Value:  Translate complex technical considerations into clear recommendations, architectural trade-offs, and business value. Help stakeholders make informed technology decisions and build confidence in the proposed approach.

You are an enthusiastic, collaborative, and high-performing professional who enjoys working with colleagues, partners, and customers. You are curious, committed to continuous learning, and passionate about technology and its ability to solve meaningful customer challenges.

You may bring experience from pre-sales, architecture, consulting, delivery, or implementation. Regardless of your career path, you can apply your technical credibility and customer engagement skills to help Canadian Federal customers achieve their goals.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

• Minimum of 5+ years of professional experience in systems architecture, solution design, systems engineering, technical consulting, delivery, or implementation within enterprise environments.

• Completion of at least 3 large-scale, end-to-end solution architectures, implementations, or delivery leadership projects within enterprise, critical infrastructure, public sector, or regulated environments.

• Documented technical competency in at least two of the following domains: networking, security, data centre, collaboration, or observability, evidenced by past project work or technical portfolios.

•  Experience delivering a minimum of 5 technical presentations or architecture reviews to audiences including technical teams, business stakeholders, and executive leadership.

• Experience producing written technical documentation, including customer requirements analyses, solution architecture diagrams, and actionable implementation roadmaps.

• A bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline, or a minimum of 14 years of combined education, formal certifications, technical training, and practical work experience.

• Current residency in the National Capital Region availability to fulfill hybrid office, customer-site, and travel requirements.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

• Minimum of 4 years of professional experience providing technical services, solution architecture, or sales engineering for Canadian Public Sector accounts, including Federal, Provincial, Defense, or National Security organizations.

• Possession of at least one active industry-recognized certification, such as Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert (CCIE), Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP), or Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP).

• Minimum of 3 years of hands-on configuration or architecture experience utilizing Cisco enterprise networking, security, or data centre portfolios, or equivalent enterprise-grade vendor technologies.

• Completion of at least 1 project involving the design or implementation of AI infrastructure, AI workloads, or solutions across secure on-premises, hybrid, or cloud-connected environments.

• Minimum of 2 years of direct pre-sales or technical advocacy experience resulting in approved technical architectures or technology adoption strategies.

• Experience authoring at least 2 formal technology roadmaps or strategic architectural decision documents submitted to senior organizational stakeholders.

• Active Government of Canada Level II (Secret) or Level III (Top Secret) security clearance.

• Verified bilingual proficiency in English and French (reading, writing, and speaking).
