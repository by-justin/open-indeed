Job Description

About P&G and Our Engineering Sites:

P&G is a global leader in consumer goods, committed to innovation impacting 5 billion consumers daily. Our Product Supply (PS) team - of which includes our Engineering (ENG) experts - drives our creation process, offering a rewarding career path to build expertise and grow as a leader. These ENG roles are primarily located at our Business & Technical Centers, with some opportunities also available at our Manufacturing Sites across the United States. Explore our US ENG locations here:  Business & Technical Centers  and Manufacturing Sites .

The Opportunity: Lead the Future of Engineering at P&G – From Day 1

Are you a highly motivated and innovative engineer eager to apply your technical leadership to groundbreaking challenges? Do you possess a strong technical curiosity and a passion for inventing, developing, and delivering advanced engineering solutions? This is your chance to lead P&G's global success, whether you're driving new product innovations and capabilities in a technical center or implementing cutting-edge automation and systems in one of our advanced manufacturing plants.

As an Engineering Manager at P&G, you will be a critical leader, building technical mastery and guiding teams to deliver new product and process innovations. You will be responsible for tackling the most difficult engineering challenges, pioneering advanced manufacturing capabilities, and keeping P&G as the top producer of consumer goods in the world. This role offers unparalleled exposure to advanced engineering disciplines, where you will be at the forefront of technology development, capability building, and the successful execution of strategic initiatives.

What You Will Do: As an Engineering Manager, you will drive technical excellence and strategic projects within our technical centers and advanced manufacturing plants. Your role blends deep technical problem-solving with leadership and project execution, with a strong emphasis on innovation and capability building. Key responsibilities include:

  • Intelligent Controls & Automation (ICA):  Lead design, development, and implementation of complex control systems, robotics, Industry 4.0 technologies, and AI/ML solutions. Build digital landscapes for production equipment and lead data analytics for control system improvements.
  • Mechanical Design & Development (APS):  Lead all phases of mechanical process/equipment development and prototyping. Deliver disruptive solutions using modeling, analysis, and hands-on prototyping, and identify emerging technologies.
  • Manufacturing Process Development (MPD):  Lead the translation of R&D innovations into scalable manufacturing processes. Drive packaging material validation, create technical specifications, optimize production lines, and manage supplier qualifications.
  • Converting & Packing Engineering:  Lead invention and refinement of material conversion and high-speed packaging technologies, driving advanced mechanical design and process optimization.
  • Instrumentation & Metrology:  Lead design, implementation, and maintenance of precision instrumentation and metrology systems for quality control and performance optimization.
  • Robotics Engineering:  Lead conceptualization, development, and integration of cutting-edge robotic solutions for manufacturing tasks.
  • Environmental & Civil Engineering:  Lead sustainable engineering solutions (waste, energy, water) and manage site infrastructure projects.
  • Technical Leadership:  Build long-term technical capability, lead adoption of advanced digital solutions, and ensure HS&E/Quality standards.

Job Qualifications

Required:

  • Education:  Bachelor's degree in an Engineering or related discipline (e.g., Mechanical, Electrical, Mechatronics, Chemical, Materials Science, Packaging Science, Environmental, Civil).
  • Experience:  0-5 years of relevant work experience (internships, co-ops, research experience, academic count!).
  • Technical Curiosity & Acumen:  Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify root causes, develop comprehensive technical solutions, and interpret data to drive decisions. A strong technical curiosity and understanding to proactively develop new approaches to complex problems.
  • Project Mindset:  A strong desire and demonstrated capability to lead projects, manage timelines/budgets, and drive results in a dynamic manufacturing environment.
  • Leadership & Collaboration:  Demonstrated leadership potential and experience, strong interpersonal skills, and the ability to effectively communicate and collaborate with diverse teams and stakeholders (from operators to senior management).
  • Agility & Drive:  Ability to quickly embrace and adapt to changes in project scope, schedule and cost. Proactive, self-driven, and eager to make a tangible impact.
  • Mobility:  Willingness to relocate throughout your career. At P&G, we believe that diverse experiences help build strong leaders. Mobility is a key component of many management careers, providing opportunities to grow through different assignments, locations, and business challenges. Candidates should be prepared to consider relocation opportunities throughout their career as business needs and development opportunities arise. Relocation support available to those who qualify.
  • Travel:  Ability to travel 10-25% as business needs dictate.

Preferred:

  • Proven experience with industrial PLCs (Rockwell), process/machine control, sensors, motors, industrial automation, or computer/industrial networks.
  • Experience with Robotics, Machine Vision, or programming (Python, LabVIEW, Java, C#).
  • Proven application of mechanical systems engineering (3D CAD, CAE, machine design, mechatronics).
  • Experience in R&D, advanced manufacturing, or technical centers.
  • Specific expertise in packaging materials/processes and/or precision instrumentation/metrology.
