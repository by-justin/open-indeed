Job Description

Procter & Gamble (P&G) is the largest consumer packaged goods company in the world. We have operations in over 75 countries, with 65 trusted brands that improve lives for 5 billion consumers worldwide. Our portfolio includes Tide, Bounty, Charmin, Gillette, Pampers, Crest, Old Spice, Pantene, and many more.  This brings many advantages, including the opportunity for our employees to enjoy a diverse and rewarding lifelong career filled with new and exciting challenges.

What will you do?

The AGV technicians develop and demonstrate skills in the automated distribution operational processes, including operating equipment, troubleshooting, stop management, system maintenance, training, qualification, performing progressive maintenance, controlling processes from a computer, and maintaining accurate records and data. Along with this, the technician will be assigned an area of equipment ownership. The equipment owner is the technical expert of that specific equipment.

We are currently looking for entry-level warehouse technicians to join our automated warehouse distribution and customer fulfillment center team in Jackson, GA. If you would like to be a part of our team, then we want you to apply!

Key Responsibilities:

  • Leverages various systems to monitor and maintain an efficient AGV operation, with applicable SEFC standard operating procedures (SOP) meeting the daily business demands of the Site while meeting or exceeding the Site’s target AGV Throughput goals.
  • Actively identifies, reports, and works to eliminate defects within AGV operations and connected systems.
  • Develops technical mastery within AGV, as well as the ability to effectively train others.
  • Demonstrate active participation in Daily Direction Setting (DDS) and Shift Change meetings, communicate observed inefficiencies and outages as a part of ongoing process improvement.
  • Owns an equipment, system, area, or team program ownership that is maintained and supports the immediate AGV area.
  • Is initiative-taking to cross train and flow to work across technology systems at the site.
  • Works with the site Operations teams to maintain cohesive, responsive, and flexible operation.
  • Understands, follows, and ensures compliance with Quality Assurance and Site Safety requirements.
  • Acts as a positive influence on the culture of the Organization by role modeling The Company’s Principles, Values and Purpose.
  • Comply with all SEFC and applicable OSHA guidelines.
  • Comply with all corporate and local quality guidelines.
  • Daily equipment health check execution.
  • A Functional leader/DMS Owner (AMFL-CL/CIL/DH, PMFL, SFL, QFL, Training, Auditing, Admin, ERT)
  • Equipment/Area Ownership
  • CIL execution
  • Develop standards and documentation to maintain and improve the operation of the line (OPLs, SIMPTWWs, SOPs, etc.)
  • Flow to the work as need
