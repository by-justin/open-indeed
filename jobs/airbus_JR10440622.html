Job Description:

Are you an Engineering Project Manager who excels at turning complex software development into smooth, predictable delivery across distributed teams? Airbus is looking for a technical Engineering Project Manager-Software to lead planning, resource allocation, and execution for our software organization, anchored out of Bingen, WA with teams spanning multiple continents.

Whether you're a former software engineer who transitioned into program management or an experienced technical PM who speaks fluent developer, this role lets you own the project rhythm—bridging day-to-day engineering workflows in GitLab with corporate-level roadmaps in Smartsheet and JIRA.

Meet the Team:

The Airbus Helicopters Flexrotor Program offers one of the most sophisticated small tactical unmanned aerial systems (STUAS) with vertical take-off and landing (VTOL). It can be used day or night by US and allied forces for a diverse range of intelligence, surveillance, target acquisition and reconnaissance (ISTAR) on land and at sea.

Your Working Environment: Nestled in the heart of the spectacular Columbia River Gorge in Bingen, WA, our workplace offers innovation and the ability to contribute to game-changing solutions while enjoying the flexibility to spread your wings. Whether it's solving complex challenges, or celebrating victories, you’ll be part of a cohesive crew that thrives on innovation and the beauty that the Northwest has to offer!

  • Financial Rewards: Competitive base salary, incentive compensation which may include profit sharing schemes, retirement savings plan and the ability to participate in an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (“ESOP”)
  • Work/Life Balance: Paid time off including personal time, holidays and a generous paid parental leave program.
  • Health & Welfare: Comprehensive insurance coverage including medical (traditional and high-deductible health plans), prescription, dental, vision, life, disability, Employee Assistance Plan (“EAP”) and other supplemental benefit coverages.

Your Challenges:

Software Project Planning & Delivery Management — 40%

  • Serve as the primary Project Manager for the Software Engineering team, owning planning, execution, tracking, and delivery across all active software projects.
  • Develop, maintain, and continuously update integrated software project plans in Smartsheet, ensuring scope, milestones, dependencies, timelines, and overall status remain visible to engineering leadership and stakeholders.
  • Track day-to-day engineering activities, issues, and deliverables in GitLab, and translate relevant project-level status into JIRA for corporate visibility and cross-functional alignment.
  • Partner with Software Engineering leads to establish project execution priorities and continuously reassess plans based on technical progress, business needs, dependencies, and available resources.
  • Facilitate appropriate software development ceremonies and project reviews, including standups, sprint planning, backlog reviews, retrospectives, and milestone reviews, while maintaining alignment with the team's existing GitLab-based workflow.
  • Monitor project performance against commitments and drive corrective actions when schedule, scope, dependencies, or execution performance begins to deviate from plan.

Resource Allocation, Prioritization & Work Intake — 35%

  • Manage software engineering resource allocation across projects, ensuring engineers are appropriately assigned based on skills, priorities, workload, and project needs.
  • Establish and maintain a clear work-intake and prioritization process so that new software requests are deliberately evaluated before being assigned to the team.
  • Work with engineering leads to determine the highest-value activities for the team and ensure resources remain focused on agreed priorities.
  • Proactively identify when new projects or requests should be queued rather than immediately started, clearly communicating capacity limitations, priorities, and associated tradeoffs to leadership and stakeholders.
  • Support forward-looking capacity planning by identifying upcoming resource gaps, competing project demands, and potential staffing needs.

Cross-Functional Coordination, Risk & Stakeholder Communication — 25%

  • Coordinate software project activities across geographically distributed teams spanning multiple continents and time zones, maintaining alignment despite limited overlapping working hours.
  • Identify and surface project risks, technical blockers, dependencies, and resource conflicts early, and drive the appropriate owners toward resolution before delivery commitments are affected.
  • Maintain clear and consistent communication with Software Engineering leadership, Program Management, Systems Engineering, and other cross-functional stakeholders regarding project status, priorities, risks, and resource decisions.
  • Establish appropriate escalation paths for issues that cannot be resolved within the Software Engineering team.
  • Ensure project decisions, priorities, commitments, and changes are clearly documented and communicated across affected teams.
  • Provide concise project-level reporting to management, highlighting progress, upcoming milestones, major risks, resource constraints, decisions required, and recovery actions.

Your Boarding Pass: (Required for consideration)

  • Must possess a Bachelor's degree in a technical field (Computer Science, Engineering, or Information Systems).
  • Must have a minimum of five (5) years of experience in project or program management, with a significant portion of that spent managing software development projects specifically.
  • Proven ability to manage resource allocation and prioritization across teams supporting multiple concurrent projects and competing demands.
  • Demonstrated proficiency in tracking and communicating project status using structured planning and reporting tools such as Smartsheet, JIRA, or equivalent platforms.
  • Strong background partnering with software engineering teams and translating technical progress, risks, and schedule impacts into clear, actionable communication for leadership and non-technical stakeholders.

Preferred Skills:

  • Background as a software engineer or technical contributor prior to moving into project/program management.
  • Direct experience with GitLab (issues, boards, milestones) as a day-to-day engineering tracking tool.
  • Experience managing or coordinating distributed/global teams across significant time zone differences.
  • Experience operating in a company where team-level tooling (e.g., GitLab) differs from corporate-level tooling (e.g., JIRA), and bridging visibility between the two.
  • Experience in aerospace, defense, robotics, or other hardware-adjacent software environments.
  • Experience supporting software development programs subject to DO-178C or similar safety-critical software standards, with an understanding of how certification requirements influence project planning, deliverables, schedule, and verification activities.
  • PMP (Project Management Professional) or equivalent certification.
  • Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), SAFe, or similar Agile/Scrum certification.
