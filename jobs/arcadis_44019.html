Description

Arcadis is the world's leading company delivering sustainable design, engineering, and consultancy solutions for natural and built assets.

We are more than 34,000 people, in over 30 countries, dedicated to improving quality of life. Everyone has an important role to play. With the power of many curious minds, together we can solve the world’s most complex challenges and deliver more impact together.

This role will form an integral part of our Global Excellence Centre (GEC) – Arcadis’ centralized and fully integrated project delivery group. Our GEC is made up of more than 3,000 talented individuals working in offices across Philippines, Romania and India, providing Capability, Capacity and a Competitive Advantage across a wide array of unique projects and services, in collaboration with our global businesses.

Arcadis’ Philippines office is seeking an experienced Architect to join our dynamic team and contribute to industrial large-scale projects. The ideal candidate has strong technical design expertise, hands-on experience in industrial facilities, and a proven ability to collaborate effectively in fast-paced project environments.

Role accountabilities:

  • Promote Arcadis Health and Safety Principles during the planning and delivery of work

  • Responsible for managing technical aspects of smaller and/or complex tasks or components of larger tasks, meeting financial, quality, time frame.

  • Work to ensure maximum internal stakeholder satisfaction, championing care and proactively working with internal stakeholders (GEC Team Lead/Task Manager, GEC Project Leads from other disciplines (MEP & Structures) and US & Canada Project Leads/Reviewers/Approvers

  • Can execute or assists in Design tasks independently from Lead (US/ Canada) Office

  • Can initiate concept ideas and later produce technically feasible general arrangement layouts and detailed drawings as per codes and design requirements, using BIM (SD to CD)

  • Can also execute Design Management tasks during Construction phase up to Building Use Stages (Concept to CD)

  • Develop and maintain a good understanding of the services delivered by Arcadis and the industry sector to facilitate personal networking and to consolidate personal and company credibility in the industry

Qualifications & Experience:

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in an architecture related discipline

  • Achieved Licensed status or equivalent

  • Minimum 7 years of experience in architecture design works

  • Revit project experience is a must

  • Experience in large-scale industrial projects (e.g., Manufacturing, Data Center, Warehouse, Power & Energy Infrastructure, Oil & Gas refineries etc.)

  • Experience working with an international team is an advantage

  • Proficient or have knowledge in Design, Autocad, Sketchup, Revit, Adobe Suite, Lumion, 3D Max

  • Excellent in interpersonal skills and problem solving

  • Excellent in communication skills

  • Excellent time management skills
