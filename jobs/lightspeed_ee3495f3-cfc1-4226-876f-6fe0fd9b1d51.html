Job Description

Hi there! Thanks for stopping by 👋

Are you actively looking for a new opportunity? Or just checking the market? Well… you might just be in the right place!

We are looking for aSenior iOS Developer to join one of our squads where you will be supporting Lightspeed’s Hospitality product group on the mission to expand our reach into new markets and countries while enhancing our advanced hospitality commerce platform.

For this role specifically, you will be joining the team focused on the Lightspeed POS that enables customers to optimize their business workflows. Our mission is to make sure that our flagship product is perfect for our target audience in EMEA and NOAM markets and provides a variety of integrations with our partners.

You will join a healthy and collaborative team, continuously improving our engineering practices, and delivering state-of-the-art technology, all while keeping it fun. Above all, your work will change the way restaurateurs across the world experience hospitality.

Please note this role is based in Canada.

What you’ll be doing:

  • Collaborate with some of the industry's top talents to enhance our iOS app by developing innovative features and user-centric workflows.
  • Design and implement elegant, efficient, and functional mobile applications tailored for iOS devices using SwiftUI.
  • Prioritize daily activities between the development of new features and the maintenance of existing integrations, with a strong focus on expanding our presence in new markets through novel iOS capabilities.
  • Report to the squad’s Team Lead and maintain active engagement with Product Managers, Designers, and cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration and performance within the POS application.
  • Participate in daily Agile ceremonies, engage in grooming sessions, and execute cutting-edge solutions that enhance user engagement.
  • Champion high standards in code quality and system reliability, take ownership of the codebase, and contribute to the robustness of our infrastructure.
  • Elevate the team's performance by sharing insights, adopting best practices in iOS development, and continually seeking ways to innovate and improve our app.

Feel free to take a look at our Tech Stack here.

What you need to bring:

  • Advanced programming skills in SwiftUI
  • Good knowledge of Structured Concurrency, Blockchain, UIKit, Swift, SQLite, Core Data, REST APIs
  • Good knowledge of Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines
  • Experience in writing automated unit and functional tests
  • Good conceptual understanding of software design and code quality
  • Experience with local network communication, external hardware accessories (e.g. payment terminals, printers) is a plus
  • Experience with use of AI tools and implementation of agentic workflows

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
