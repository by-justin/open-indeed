Job Description

À propos du poste

Notre entreprise connaît une croissance rapide, et nous sommes à la recherche d’un adjoint de bureau dévoué pour centraliser toutes les tâches liées à l’accueil, aux installations et aux services administratifs. Vous serez le premier point de contact pour les visiteurs, la délivrance des badges, la commande de matériel et la gestion des services quotidiens.

Principales responsabilités

Zone

Tâches (heures hebdomadaires)

Réception et sécurité

Accueillir les visiteurs, délivrer des badges temporaires, tenir à jour le registre des visiteurs, réceptionner les colis et aller chercher le courrier.

Surveiller les lecteurs de badges, examiner les incidents de sécurité

Assister l’agent de sécurité des locaux dans les tâches suivantes :

•              Coordonner la sécurité physique des installations;

•             Gérer le système d’alarme et de surveillance;

•             Gérer le personnel de sécurité et d’entretien;

•             Le cas échéant, assurer la liaison avec les locataires du bâtiment afin de garantir leur respect des exigences de sécurité de MDA Space; et

•              Assurer la liaison, si nécessaire, avec les services de police locaux.

Installations et équipements

Recharge des machines à café, robinets d’eau gazeuse, approvisionnement des sanitaires, vérification du niveau de carburant des générateurs ; programmation et mise en marche des lave‑vaisselle; supervision du nettoyage et du réapprovisionnement des réfrigérateurs; planification des commandes de réapprovisionnement auprès des fournisseurs (café, fournitures de bureau, etc.); coordination des expéditions.

Office Assistant – Full‑Time (40 hrs / week)

Location: St-Bruno-de-Montarville, MDA Space office

Reports to: Operations Manager – works closely with Building Manager

About the Role

We are expanding rapidly and need a dedicated Office Assistant to centralise all front‑desk, facilities, and office‑service functions. You will be the first point of contact for visitors, badge issuance, equipment ordering, and daily amenities.

Key Responsibilities

Area

Duties (weekly hrs)

Reception & Security

Greet visitors, issue temporary badges, maintain visitor log, receive packages, pick up the mail.

Monitor badge readers, review security incident

Back up Facility security Officer to:

•              Co-ordinates physical security of the facility;

•              Manages the alarm/monitoring system;

•              Manages security and cleaning personnel;

•              If applicable, liaises with any building tenants to ensure their adherence to MDA Space security requirements; and

•              Liaison, as necessary, with Local Police Departments.

Facilities & Amenities

Refill coffee machines, sparkling‑water taps, restroom supplies, generator fuel level checks; schedule/run dishwashers; oversee refrigerator cleaning/stocking; schedule supply replenishment orders with vendors (coffee supplies, office supplies, etc.); coordinate shipping.

Equipment Procurement (LN)

Prepare and support

·         ~12 weekly orders: Purchase requests, Material Service Requisition (MSR) forms.

·         expense claims,

·         track status,

·         maintain dashboard

Printer / Shredder Management

Refill toner/paper, log service tickets

Meeting‑Room & Lunch‑Area Coordination

AV setup, reservation management, event support

Health & Safety

Primary First aid contact, Daily walkthroughs, incident logging, escalation

Microsoft Shifts Roster

Maintain roster, generate weekly “who is onsite” reports, train supervisors

IT Front‑Desk Triage

Answer basic “where is the printer?” queries, forward technical tickets

Administrative Support

Assist onboarding/off‑boarding, maintain contact directory, provide on‑call rotation, maintain vacation schedule

Backup / Escalation

Act as on‑call backup for badge/visitor emergencies; coordinate with Building Manager for high‑risk cases

Delegation when absent: Supervisors act as rotating on‑call backups; the Building Manager steps in only for high‑risk security incidents.

Required Qualifications & Skills

  • High‑school diploma + college/technical training (preferred).

  • ≥ 2 years experience in office administration, reception, or facilities coordination.

  • Health & Safety qualification:

Understand provincial rules like those from CNESST for Quebec workplaces. Basic H&S certification: Recent standard first aid and Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Response (CPR) course accreditation. Hazard Spotting: Review, find and fix common office risks. Emergency Plans: Help run fire drills and evacuation steps.

  • Strong customer‑service orientation and professional demeanor.

  • Excellent organizational skills; ability to manage multiple concurrent tasks.

  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office suite

  • Familiarity with security and badge‑access systems

  • Ability to follow detailed Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and maintain accurate logs.

  • Strong written and verbal communication in both French and English.

  • The ability to get SECRET level status.

Preferred Experience

  • Prior exposure to a secure or mission‑critical environment.

  • Experience maintaining live desk‑maps or hot‑desking systems.

  • Knowledge of health‑ and‑safety practices in an office setting.

  • Experience with Microsoft Shifts or similar scheduling tools is a plus.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Visitor badge issuance ≤ 5 min from arrival.

  • LN order processing ≤ 24 hrs.

  • Desk‑map accuracy ≥ 98 % (quarterly spot‑check).

  • After‑hours badge response ≤ 15 min.

  • Facilities supply availability 100 % of workdays.

  • Staff‑presence reporting (Shifts) ≥ 90 % daily entry.

Join us and be the front‑line hub that keeps our mission‑critical centre running smoothly as we scale to thousands of staff!
