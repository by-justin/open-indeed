Job Description

Global Head Asset Management (AM) GTM Lead

Overview

As the Global Head Asset Management (AM) GTM Lead, you will play a pivotal role in driving the execution of go-to-market (GTM) strategies for the AM product portfolio. Reporting directly to the Global Head Autonomous IT GTM, you will act as a key contributor to business growth, customer engagement, and operational excellence within our Autonomous IT growth motion – helping customers reduce cost, risk, and complexity across their software, hardware, and cloud assets. This role is ideal for a results-oriented professional with a passion for customer-centric innovation and cross-functional collaboration.

Key responsibilities

  • Engage directly with strategic customers to understand needs, drive adoption, and ensure satisfaction. Serve as a practitioner and advocate for customer-centricity, maintaining feedback loops to inform GTM decisions.
  • Design and activate prioritized sales plays that maximize pipeline generation, progression, and new net annual contract value (NNACV). Work closely with sales, product, and marketing teams to ensure cohesive execution.
  • Drive CIO GTM plays, repeatable motions, and readiness programs across geographies (AMS, EMEA, APAC, Japan), ensuring consistent field enablement and performance tracking.
  • Support the Autonomous IT motion by increasing AM attach rate and expansion across CIO/IT deals, in alignment with GTM priorities around selling the NOW platform.
  • Elevate, simplify, and transform solution messaging in partnership with Product Solution Marketing (PSM), ensuring clarity and impact across all channels.
  • Collaborate with the AM BU to develop and refine GTM strategies, including insights into pricing, packaging, and market positioning, as needed.
  • Drive integration of GTM initiatives across Global Partnerships & Channels (GPC), Customer Excellence Group (CEG), PSM, and Industry Solutions to ensure scalability and performance tracking.
  • Support partner co-sell strategy and solution partner enablement to unlock joint growth and market reach.
  • Stay ahead of market trends, competitive shifts, and emerging technologies to inform GTM strategy and positioning.
  • Represent the company at industry events, panels, and executive forums.
  • Support deal execution across stages, including but not limited to planning, discovery calls, engagement plans, workshops etc.
  • Support high-impact deals, advising field and geo leadership on strategic execution and customer unlock.
  • Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Qualifications

  • 7+ years of experience in GTM strategy, solution sales, business operations, or customer success, with at least 3 years in a senior role.
  • Proven track record of driving strategic initiatives and operational execution in complex, matrixed environments.
  • Deep experience engaging with senior stakeholders (e.g. CIOs) and influencing executive decision-making.
  • Strong analytical, communication, and storytelling skills.
  • Experience in SaaS, cloud, or enterprise technology preferred.
  • Familiarity with asset management – software asset management (SAM), hardware asset management (HAM), and cloud/SaaS spend and FinOps – or adjacent domains is a plus.
  • MBA or equivalent advanced degree is a plus.

What we offer

  • Senior leader level visibility and impact.
  • A dynamic, collaborative environment with top-tier talent.
  • Competitive compensation and benefits.
