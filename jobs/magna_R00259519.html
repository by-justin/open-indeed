Job Responsibilities:

MISSION:

Support the BaseTech system engineering activities for Active Safety products (ADAS) in projects, with focus on BSW, Linux platform, requirements management and system integration activities.

支持项目中主动安全产品（ADAS）的基础技术系统工程工作，重点参与BSW、Linux平台、需求管理及系统集成相关活动。

POSITION RESPONSIBILITIES:

• Requirements Management Support:

Assist in requirements review, classification, screening and traceability management. Support requirements reporting, metrics collection and data maintenance.

协助开展需求评审、需求分类、需求筛查及需求追踪管理工作；支持需求报表编制、统计数据收集及维护。

• Documentation Support:

Support the preparation, organization and maintenance of system engineering documents, meeting minutes and project-related records.

协助系统工程相关文档、会议纪要及项目资料的编制、整理和维护。

• Project Coordination Support:

Support project coordination activities, collect and consolidate information from different stakeholders, and assist in tracking open actions and project status.

支持项目协调工作，收集整理各相关方信息，协助跟踪待办事项及项目状态。

• Other Tasks:

Complete other tasks assigned by the team leader.

完成团队负责人安排的其他工作。
