Job Description:

The Software Engineer III (Salesforce Loyalty Management Technical Lead) leads the creation of comprehensive end-to-end detailed solutions for projects and platforms with overall accountability for the technical design and delivery of a specific technical solution based on requirements from stakeholders.

Develops and maintains technical solutions, ensuring adherence to engineering and architectural design principles while meeting business requirements. Provides technical expertise with a focus on efficiency, reliability, scalability, and security; includes planning, evaluating, recommending, designing, operationalizing, and supporting solutions in compliance with enterprise and industry standards.

Ultimately, providing expert advice and lead in system / application administration, installation, configuration / provisioning, platform planning and design, integration, and release / deployment management.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the end-to-end technical design and delivery of Salesforce Loyalty Management capabilities, including member management, loyalty programs, rewards, promotions, partner management, and transaction processing.
  • Design and implement scalable solutions leveraging Salesforce Loyalty Management, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Data Cloud, Marketing Cloud, and related Salesforce platform technologies.
  • Provide technical leadership for integrations with enterprise systems using APIs, middleware, ETL tools, and event-driven architectures.
  • Collaborate with business and product stakeholders to define requirements, evaluate solution options, and recommend optimal technical approaches.
  • Develop and maintain technical architecture artifacts, solution designs, integration specifications, deployment strategies, and operational support documentation.
  • Lead development teams through design, build, testing, deployment, and production support activities.
  • Ensure adherence to enterprise architecture, security, governance, and engineering standards.
  • Drive platform performance, scalability, reliability, resiliency, and operational excellence.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical and integration challenges across Salesforce and connected platforms.
  • Mentor engineers and provide subject matter expertise for Salesforce Loyalty Management best practices and solution patterns.
  • Support release management, environment strategy, DevOps automation, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) processes.

Depth & Scope:

  • Expert knowledge of the business, the broader organization, technical environment, processes, procedures, tools and other relevant technology areas (e.g. Architecture, Infrastructure, Security etc.) from a design / support / solutions perspective
