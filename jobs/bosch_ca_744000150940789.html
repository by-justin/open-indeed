Job Description

As an SAP Senior Technical Consultant for Manufacture to Supply , you will design, develop, and integrate technical solutions supporting end-to-end manufacturing processes in SAP S/4HANA . You will connect business requirements with technical architecture, ensuring scalable, reliable, and standard-aligned solutions.

Responsibilities:

  • Design and implement technical solutions for SAP M2S processes.
  • Develop enhancements using ABAP for SAP HANA, CDS Views, OData, Fiori/UI5, APIs, and SAP BTP .
  • Build integrations using IDoc, RFC, REST/OData, and Web Services .
  • Support data migration and ensure compliance with SAP and global architecture standards.
  • Work across Make-to-Stock, Make-to-Order, and Configure-to-Order scenarios.
  • Collaborate with international teams and provide technical consulting, training, and solution support.

Qualifications

Profile:

  • 6+ years of experience in SAP implementation and technical consulting, with a strong focus on SAP PP and/or PP-DS in S/4HANA .
  • Advanced knowledge of ABAP, SAP Fiori/UI5, BTP, integration technologies, and SAP data migration tools .
  • Strong understanding of M2S processes and their integration with supply chain, quality, warehouse, and logistics solutions, including SAP EWM .
  • Experience in international projects and customer-facing roles.
  • Manufacturing or automotive industry experience.
  • Fluent English and strong communication skills.
