Job Description

Job Description:

A vacancy for Communication Systems Product Manager has arisen within Airbus Defence & Space in Getafe. The successful applicant will join the Communications Architectures department.

MAIN RESPONSABILITIES AND TASKS

Your main responsibilities will include:

  • Overall Comms System Engineering Activities Coordination (Time, Cost and Quality)
  • Overall Comms L1 System Design and Documentation
  • Supporting L1 Integration and Verification, Certification, Security Accreditation & Qualification
  • Additionally, as member of a multi-programme team, contribute to other Transport A/C programmes architecture activities as backup and support.

Main role activitities will be the following:

MANAGE:

  • Support L1 and L2 technical baselines and technical reviews
  • Support Progress Reviews and Customer discussions
  • Participate in Configuration Management activities
  • Coordinate Comms L2/Systems contributions and design reviews
  • Interface with customer in Comms and MIDS Working Groups.

DESIGN and SUPPORT

  • Comms systems L1 Overall Design (FRDs, L1 Requirements, interfaces)
  • Validate Comms L2 documentation (SRDs, SDNs, EIRDs, SIDs) coming from Comms Teams.
  • Lead L1 Verification Requirements and Coverage Matrix
  • Support L1 Certification and Qualification Plans and Evidences
  • Coordinate System Reviews for Comms systems.
  • Coordinate and Configuration Control of associated systems

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.

REQUIRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE

We are looking for candidates with the following skills and experience:

  • Educated to degree level in Engineering , valuable PhD/Telecommunications Engineering Degree.

  • Experience in Engineering

  • Experience of >5 years in system engineering or Communications/Avionics related jobs.

- A/C and comms Architecture knowledge  of at least one of the following platforms:

  • Transport A/C - MRTT, C295/CN235/C212, A400M

  • UAS – Atlante, Neuron or any other

  • Be familiar with System Engineering processes (DPs / ABDs etc) applicable to at least one of the following platforms

  • MRTT, C295/CN235/C212

  • A400M

  • Be familiar with airworthiness and certification processes, EASA or INTA based.

  • Be familiar with Comms systems, at least one of the following disciplines.

  • Voice based Radio Communications (Radios, SATCOM, Intercom systems)

  • IP/Digital based communications (Datalinks, ROVER, SATCOM, LTE, etc)

  • Tactical Datalinks (Link16, Link22 etc)

  • Awareness on cyber security implications for airborne platforms will be highly considered.

  • Be familiar with following tools and competencies:

  • Project management and planning

  • Reporting activities generation (DOORS, DMS)

  • Model Based Systems Development as Simulink, Matlab, CAMEO

  • Problem solving and decision making,

  • Assertiveness

  • English & Spanish :Fluent
