Job Title: Production Supervisor – MED Shop (Experimental Development)

About Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (P&WC) is a global leader in aerospace innovation, proudly headquartered in Longueuil, Quebec, since 1928. We design and manufacture next-generation aircraft engines that power the world’s largest fleet of business, general aviation, and regional aircraft and helicopters.

For nearly 100 years, our teams have been at the forefront of propulsion technology, pushing boundaries to make flight more efficient and reliable. Our engines support missions that truly matter, from transporting people and essential goods, to enabling emergency medical services, humanitarian operations, and wildfire suppression around the world.

An Employer of Choice

Pratt & Whitney Canada was named one of Canada’s Best Employers for 2026 by Forbes, marking the 11th consecutive year the company has earned this recognition. Pratt & Whitney Canada also ranked #1 employer in the aerospace and defense industry in the country. In addition, our headquarters continues to be recognized among the top employers in the Montreal region. Together, these distinctions reinforce our reputation as an employer of choice in Montreal, across Canada, and around the world.

Why Join our Team?

Joining our team means having the opportunity to actively participate in the success of Pratt & Whitney Canada.

As Production Supervisor – MED Shop, you will report directly to the Senior Manager of the MED & Tooling Services sector which is part of the Longueuil Manufacturing Centre (LMC).

The MED Shop is an experimental development manufacturing center. The department comprises a team of over 55 machinists, sheet metal workers, welders, deburrers, and inspectors. It operates within an aerospace manufacturing environment focused on the development and introduction of technological innovations. Whether for prototype manufacturing, the repair of production parts, or technological development, our staff possesses the skills to meet tight deadlines while ensuring the quality standards required by our customers.

Your main objective will be to oversee the day-to-day operations as well as manage the department's employees. You will also be required to actively participate in the implementation of continuous improvement initiatives and projects by encouraging the participation of employees. The mandates associated with the position are varied, enriching and related to various engine programs.

The position is full-time from Monday to Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. in Longueuil and is exclusively on site.

What will your day-to-day look like?

  • Supervise daily operations related to the manufacturing of multiple families of aeronautical parts;
  • Address the prevention of health and safety incidents by actively promoting good practices and appropriate behaviours;
  • Ensure the achievement of key performance indicators (EHS, quality, delivery, performance, costs) and apply appropriate measures and solutions;
  • Communicate with production teams and support functions such as manufacturing engineering, programming, logistics, maintenance, project management, etc.;
  • Hire, monitor training of employees and support team leaders;
  • Actively participate in initiatives aimed at reducing non-quality and increasing efficiency;
  • Facilitate change management and promote employee engagement.

What do you need to be successful?

  • College diploma (DEC) or Bachelor’s degree with a minimum of 5 years of relevant experience in a manufacturing environment, including a minimum of 3+ years of supervisory experience;
  • French (fluent), English (functional);
  • Knowledge of continuous improvement concepts (5S, Lean Manufacturing, tier meetings, Gemba walks, visual management, etc.);
  • Knowledge of manufacturing, programming, and machining methods;
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams);
  • Comfortable working in an environment with competing priorities and tight deadlines;
  • Organized and able to manage multiple tasks simultaneously;
  • Ability to understand various production challenges and anticipate potential issues;
  • Team player with strong autonomy and ethical judgment;
  • Knowledge of logistics, planning tools, and production monitoring is an asset;
  • Good knowledge of ERP/MRP software such as SAP and ITDM is an asset;
  • Knowledge of quality criteria and inspection processes is an asset.

This position is part of a French-speaking work environment, however it requires a working knowledge of English to read and understand specialized instructions, plans, drawings and technical terms that may emanate from international organizations, as well as to write and sometimes communicate in English with international customers, suppliers or colleagues.

  • Merit or recognition program
  • Health and wellness program, including virtual health care
