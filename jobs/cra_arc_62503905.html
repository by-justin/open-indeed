About the job

            The CRA is committed to providing an inclusive and barrier-free work environment with a diverse workforce that reflects the Canadians we serve. It starts with the hiring process. To find out more about the CRA's actions in support of Employment Equity (EE), diversity and inclusion (DI), and read testimonials from our employees and executives, please visit the following CRA web sites: [Employment equity, diversity and inclusion](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/careers-cra/employment-equity-diversity-inclusion.html) and [Our workforce testimonials - Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/careers-cra/workforce-testimonials.html).

We encourage you to complete the EEDI Staffing questionnaire as part of your application if you wish to be considered for appointments using the EE and/or DI staffing requirement in this process. Also, ensure that you have provided consent in the EEDI staffing questionnaire for your EEDI information to be used for screening or selection. If you do not complete the questionnaire, you will not be considered for any appointments using the EE and/or DI staffing requirement in this process. If you decide to modify your response or fill out this questionnaire at a later date, please notify the contact listed at the bottom of this notice of job opportunity.

CRA's National Employment Equity (EE) Staffing Strategy strives to eliminate gaps for the four EE designated groups: Indigenous peoples, persons with disabilities, members of visible minorities, and women. Where an EE gap exists at the time of appointment, EE may be used as an appointment criterion for permanent and temporary appointments over six months made through this process.

Employees are required to work on-site in accordance with the CRA's requirement for on-site presence. Workplace arrangements are based on the position, work location, operational requirements and are subject to change. Specific on-site presence requirements will be shared with candidates selected for appointment. Workplace accommodations based on the duty to accommodate as well as individual exceptions may be granted depending on the circumstances and the nature of the work being performed.

Currently, employees at the Jonquière Tax Centre are required to work on-site for a minimum  of four days per week, but most job opportunities require an on-site presence five days a week .

This process will be used to staff various SP-01 and SP-02 positions for the tax-filing periods and the offers will be for temporary positions of less than 6 months . Rehire pools could be established to meet future staffing needs.

In order to minimize work interruptions during the Jonquière Tax Centre's recurring and fluctuating peak periods, once you have accepted a temporary job offer from this pool, you could no longer be eligible for other employment opportunities from this staffing process for the duration of your contract.

When you apply to this staffing process, you are not applying for a specific position, but for a pool of candidates that will be used to fill various SP-01 and SP-02 positions.

SP0619 - Electronic Data Capture Operator

Electronically enters information from returns and/or related documents and verifies, edits and corrects the data entered. The work requires the use of a computer keyboard and/or bar code scanner to enter data continuously for a period of 7 hours per day.

SP0301 - General Duties Clerk

Receives, examines and sorts incoming mail and attaches pertinent documentation, performs various handling activities, prepares a large quantity of documents, such as files and papers for processing, and forwards information to the appropriate area within the Agency and other federal agencies.

Here is an overview of the main functions, but not limited to :

Document processing; maintenance and updating of records and files related to taxpayer returns, schedules and forms. Analyze and evaluate information; receive, sort, prepare, distribute and file documents; enter and retrieve data; update accounts and reports; verify documents from computer system. Provide on-the-job training to teammates as required. Perform activities such as responding to correspondence and telephone inquiries. Process incoming and outgoing mail and maintain statistics on mail volumes. Collect classified waste from customers and arrange for its destruction.

*Receive, sort, system log, deliver and post mail and other materials. Stretching, climbing, bending, lifting and carrying heavy boxes, mailbags, pallets and furniture weighing more than 10 kilograms , and pushing heavy carts or equipment on a daily basis, up to 5 hours per day.

Work involves operating materiel handling equipment ( e.g. forklifts), using manual and power tools to repair, dismantle, move and erect office enclosures and office furniture and equipment. Work requires protective footwear.

Please note that all positions require the use of a computer. It is therefore important to have basic computer skills, such as typing text, browsing the Internet, managing files, using common software applications, and communicating by email.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
