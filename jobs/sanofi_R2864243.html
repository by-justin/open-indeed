SUMMARY OF PURPOSE / MAJOR RESPONSIBILITIES

岗位目标 / 主要职责概述

• Lead launch excellence (LEx) planning and execution for new vaccine products , ensuring successful market entry, accelerated commercial ramp-up, and optimized product performance through the full launch phase

主导新疫苗产品的上市卓越（LEx）规划与执行 ，确保产品成功进入市场，加速商业化进程，并在整个上市阶段优化产品表现

• Coordinate cross-functional new vaccine project planning and portfolio assessment for the China market, providing full business proposals to support management decision-making

协调跨部门新疫苗项目规划及产品组合评估，为管理层决策提供完整的商业计划书

维护并协调疫苗产品组合的10年CSP工作，整合外部环境影响，支持长期业务规划

• Collect and analyze market intelligence — including competitive landscape, policy changes, and pipeline developments — to generate actionable business insights and support strategic decision-making

收集并分析市场情报，包括竞争格局、政策变化及产品管线动态，形成可落地的商业洞察，支持战略决策

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES

关键职责

  1. New Vaccine Launch Excellence (LEx) Planning & Execution: 40%

新疫苗上市卓越规划与执行：40%

• Lead and coordinate cross-functional LEx planning for new vaccine products, establishing clear launch readiness milestones, accountability frameworks, and timelines across all relevant functions (Medical, Regulatory, Commercial, Supply, Market Access, etc.)

主导并协调跨部门新疫苗产品LEx规划 ，建立清晰的上市准备里程碑、责任框架及时间节点，覆盖医学、注册、商业、供应链、市场准入等所有相关职能部门

• Develop and drive go-to-market strategy and launch roadmap for new vaccine products, including target customer segmentation, channel strategy, pricing and access strategy, and pre-launch activation plans

制定并推动新疫苗产品的市场进入策略和上市路线图 ，包括目标客户细分、渠道策略、定价与准入策略及上市前激活计划

• Monitor and evaluate launch performance post-launch against pre-defined KPIs; identify gaps and propose corrective actions to minimize time to peak sales and maximize commercial outcomes

监测并评估上市后的产品表现 ，对照预设KPI识别差距，提出纠偏措施，以缩短达峰时间并最大化商业成果

• Facilitate alignment between global and China teams on launch strategy, ensuring global pipeline opportunities and launch standards are appropriately adapted to China market needs and regulatory context

促进全球与中国团队在上市策略上的对齐 ，确保全球产品管线机会和上市标准能够适配中国市场需求和监管环境

• Provide full business proposals for new vaccine products, including potential assessment, commercial strategy, sales forecast, and risk analysis, to support management decision-making and cross-functional endorsement

为新疫苗产品提供 完整的商业计划书 ，包括潜力评估、商业策略、销售预测及风险分析，支持管理层决策及跨部门认可

• Support the achievement of mid-to-long term financial objectives through robust launch planning and disciplined execution tracking

通过严谨的上市规划和规范的执行跟踪，支持公司 中长期财务目标 的实现

  1. 10-Year CSP Exercise Maintenance and Coordination: 30%

年度CSP维护和协调：30%

• Prepare documents and data needed for running the CSP vaccine 10-year forecast

市场竞争情报搜集及分析：20%

• Collect and analyze market intelligence data, competition challenges, local manufacturers' competency and capacity, possible policy changes and their impact; translate findings into business insights, proposals, and suggested action plans

收集并分析市场情报数据、竞争挑战情况、本土生产商能力与产能、潜在政策变动及其影响，并将其转化为商业洞察、计划书及建议行动方案

• Provide regular summary reports and insights to support identification of business needs, opportunities, and market trends

定期提供总结报告和分析，支持识别业务需求、市场机遇和市场趋势

  1. New asset opportunity assessment: 10%

协助评估新疫苗产品管线针对中国市场的机会，包括潜力评估、商业策略及销售预测
