Job Description

Job Title: Senior System Analyst

About Company:

Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. is the world leader in serving science, with revenues of more than $40 billion and approximately 130,000 employees globally. We help our customers accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver medicines to market and increase laboratory productivity.

Roles & Responsibilities:

As the Senior SOLIDWORKS PDM Administrator, you will serve as the primary technical expert responsible for the administration, optimization, and strategic evolution of the SOLIDWORKS PDM Professional environment. You will partner with Engineering, IT, Manufacturing, and Product Development teams to ensure efficient engineering data management and seamless integration with enterprise systems.

PDM Administration & System Management

  • Serve as the primary administrator and subject matter expert for SOLIDWORKS PDM Professional.
  • Configure, maintain, and optimize PDM vaults, workflows, lifecycle states, data cards, variables, templates, serial numbers, dispatch scripts, and automation.
  • Administer user accounts, security groups, permissions, and role-based access.
  • Maintain vault performance, system reliability, backup and disaster recovery strategies, and overall system health.
  • Monitor system utilization and recommend performance improvements.

Engineering Data Governance

  • Establish and maintain engineering data governance policies, standards, and best practices.
  • Ensure document integrity, revision control, engineering change management (ECO), and release processes.
  • Define and enforce CAD standards, naming conventions, metadata standards, and document control procedures.
  • Protect company intellectual property through appropriate security and access controls.
  • Ensure compliance with internal quality systems and applicable regulatory requirements.

System Architecture & Integration

  • Lead architecture planning and continuous improvement of the SOLIDWORKS PDM environment.
  • Design, develop, and maintain integrations with enterprise systems including:SAP or Oracle ERPAgile PLM / Oracle Cloud PLMActive DirectoryMicrosoft SQL ServerWindows ServerAWS infrastructure (where applicable)
  • Coordinate server migrations, software upgrades, SQL maintenance, and infrastructure enhancements with IT teams.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies and contribute to the long-term PDM/PLM roadmap.

Process Improvement & Automation

  • Develop and standardize workflows supporting:New Product Development (NPD)Engineering Change Orders (ECO)Document ReleaseManufacturingSupplier Collaboration
  • Drive automation initiatives to eliminate manual processes.
  • Identify opportunities to improve engineering productivity, operational efficiency, and data quality.

Technical Leadership & Support

  • Serve as the highest level of technical escalation for PDM-related issues.
  • Diagnose and resolve complex application, infrastructure, database, and workflow issues.
  • Collaborate with SOLIDWORKS VARs, software vendors, consultants, and IT support teams.
  • Review Statements of Work (SOWs) for software implementations and upgrade projects.
  • Lead system upgrades, migrations, validation testing, and enhancement initiatives.
  • Develop user guides, training materials, and best-practice documentation.
  • Deliver administrator and end-user training.
  • Mentor engineers and designers on PDM utilization, CAD standards, and engineering documentation practices.
  • Provide Level 2 and Level 3 technical support.
  • Lead PDM-related projects from planning through implementation.
  • Manage software licensing, maintenance agreements, and vendor relationships.
  • Participate in budgeting, software evaluations, and capital planning activities.

Required Skills & Experience:

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Systems, Informatics, or a related field. Equivalent industry experience will also be considered.
  • Minimum 8 years of experience administering SOLIDWORKS PDM Professional (Enterprise PDM).
  • 10+ years of experience supporting CAD systems within a manufacturing or product development environment.
  • Proven experience leading enterprise engineering system implementations, upgrades, and migrations.
  • Experience supporting global engineering organizations is preferred.
