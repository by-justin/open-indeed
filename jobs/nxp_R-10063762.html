Job Description

We are seeking a Segment Lead for Metering to drive strategy, customer engagement, and solution definition across global electricity and flow metering markets. This role combines deep technical expertise , system-level thinking , and market leadership to strengthen NXP’s position in smart metering and expand into next-generation energy intelligence architectures.

The successful candidate will operate across:

  • Semiconductor platforms (MCU, MPU, analog, connectivity, security)
  • System solutions (meters, smart panels, gateways)
  • Emerging trends (edge AI, secure infrastructure, cloud connectivity)

Key Responsibilities:

Strategy & Business Leadership

  • Define and execute the global strategy for the ISEM Metering segment (electricity + flow)
  • Collaborate across business lines to drive the opportunity pipeline, design wins, and long-term revenue growth
  • Position NXP to defend core metering share while expanding into next-gen architectures such as smart panels and energy gateways
  • Align segment priorities with overall Power & Energy roadmap and key customers

Customer & Market Engagement

  • Lead engagement with key customers including utilities, OEMs, and system integrators
  • Drive strategic discussions on evolution of metering architectures such as intelligent edge processing, and secure connectivity to cloud and grid infrastructure
  • Translate customer and regulatory requirements into system and silicon roadmaps
  • Represent NXP at customer engagements, industry forums, and executive discussions

Solution, Security & Technology Leadership

  • Define system-level architectures for smart electricity meters, flow meters, smart panels and energy gateways
  • Drive adoption of scalable platforms leveraging MCUs / MPUs, analog front ends and metrology, Connectivity (PLC, RF, Wi-Fi, BLE, Thread)
  • Promote platform approaches enabling reusable, certified, secure and scalable solutions

Cross-Functional Execution

  • Collaborate across product lines (processing, connectivity, security, analog), system and application engineering teams, sales and marketing organizations
  • Enable ecosystem partners and ensure scalable global solutions deployment
  • Drive alignment across siloed teams to accelerate time-to-market and design reuse

Required Qualifications:

Experience

  • Bachelor of Engineering or equivalent, with 10+ years in semiconductors, energy systems, or industrial markets
  • Proven track record in:Metering, energy management, or utility infrastructureSystem-level architecture or solution definitionStrategic customer engagement
  • Strong understanding of:Metering architectures (electricity and/or flow)Analog sensing and metrology (e.g., precision measurement, ADC-based systems)Embedded processing (MCU/MPU)Connectivity technologies (PLC, wired, wireless)
  • Familiarity with cybersecurity for embedded systems , including:Hardware-based security (secure elements, root of trust)Cryptographic protocols and secure communications Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) and long-term cryptographic resilience for infrastructure deployments

Leadership & Skills

  • Strong strategic thinking and business acumen
  • Excellent communication and executive presentation capability
  • Ability to lead and influence across a global, matrix organization
  • Proven ability to translate complex technologies into customer value
