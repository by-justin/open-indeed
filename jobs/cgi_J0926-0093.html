Position ID: J0926-0093 Employment Type: Full Time Position Description:

CGI Federal is seeking a Mid/Senior Level Momentum Developer to support federal clients in modernizing and enhancing Momentum based financial, procurement, and budget systems. The ideal candidate brings strong expertise in C++, Java, SQL, and modern full stack development with Microservices, Angular, and Spring Boot. This role will contribute to the design, development, integration, and maintenance of Momentum application components, APIs, and custom extensions. This position is located in our Lebanon, VA, Fairfax, VA, Or Lafayette, LA office; however, a hybrid working model is acceptable.

Your future duties and responsibilities:

Key Responsibilities . Design, develop, and maintain Momentum application components, including custom extensions, interfaces, and integrations. . Build and enhance backend services using C++, Java, Spring Boot, and Microservices architectures. . Implement front end enhancements and user interfaces using Angular (or similar modern UI frameworks). . Write optimized SQL queries, stored procedures, and data access components to support Momentum data models. . Collaborate with functional analysts and QA teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions. . Participate in Agile ceremonies (sprint planning, grooming, demos) and contribute to continuous process improvement. . Troubleshoot application defects, performance issues, and data inconsistencies across Momentum modules. . Develop and maintain documentation, including technical designs, interface specifications, and release notes. . Support integration efforts with external federal systems, service APIs, and enterprise platforms. . Ensure all development follows CGI Federal coding standards, security protocols, and compliance requirements. Ability to mentor developers who can review and approve estimates

Required qualifications to be successful in this role:

Required Skills & Experience . 5+ years of software development experience (mid/senior level). . Strong experience with Momentum (Momentum Financials, Acquisitions, Budget, or related modules). . Hands on experience in: . C++ (Momentum core customizations, legacy components) . Java and Spring Boot . SQL (Oracle, PostgreSQL, or SQL Server) . Microservices architecture and API development . Angular (v8+) or similar modern JS frameworks . Experience with version control (Git), CI/CD pipelines, and Agile delivery. . Ability to work with cross functional teams including analysts, QA, DevOps, and client stakeholders. Experience with Momentum 7.x or 8.x release upgrades, customizations, or API development.

Desired qualifications/non-essential skills required: . Knowledge of WebMethods, Momentum integrations, or federal financial system interfaces. . Familiarity with cloud-based environments (AWS, Azure) and containerized deployment. . Prior professional experience supporting federal systems or government clients.

Soft Skills . Strong analytical thinking and problem solving ability. . Excellent communication skills—both written and verbal. . Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast paced environment. . Attention to detail and commitment to delivering high quality work. Clearance requirement Ability to obtain agency-based clearance

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
