Job Description

The Opportunity

Adobe’s AI Edge team builds foundational capabilities for the next generation of creative agents. We work ahead of the production roadmap. We turn promising but unreliable AI workflows into practical technology. This technology will support products such as Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Express, and Firefly.

You will join a small, fast paced team whose members bring a diverse mix of AI, systems, product, and prototyping skills. You will explore new ideas, build working prototypes, evaluate what works, and partner with product teams to carry successful experiments forward. This is a role for someone who enjoys ambiguity, learns quickly, and loves making emerging technology tangible.

What you'll Do

  • Prototype agentic AI capabilities for creative workflows, from early concept through technical validation.
  • Build components for agent planning, tool use, orchestration, streaming, state management, and evaluation.
  • Build reusable platform interfaces that can work across different Adobe products and creative surfaces.
  • Turn new capabilities into focused, credible prototypes that help teams test assumptions and make decisions quickly.
  • Measure quality and reliability, so we can distinguish a compelling demo from a capability ready for broader investment.
  • Collaborate with engineers, applied scientists, designers, researchers, and product managers, then help production teams adopt the ideas that prove valuable.

What you need to succeed

  • Strong full-stack engineering experience building and shipping customer-facing products, from polished user interfaces through backend services and production infrastructure.
  • Fluency with modern web application development, such as React, TypeScript, API integration, asynchronous workflows, streaming experiences, and application state management.
  • Experience designing and operating backend systems, including APIs, distributed services, data stores, cloud infrastructure, observability, performance, and reliability.
  • Strong product and UX judgment, with the ability to turn emerging technical capabilities into intuitive, responsive, and credible user experiences.
  • Hands-on experience with LLM-powered applications or agentic systems, such as model APIs, tool calling, orchestration, retrieval, streaming, or evaluation.
  • The ability to move quickly from an ambiguous idea to an end-to-end working prototype, test it with users and partners, and improve it through feedback.
  • Comfort moving across frontend, backend, and infrastructure while making pragmatic technical tradeoffs appropriate to each stage of a project.
  • Clear communication, sound technical judgment, curiosity, and a practical approach to experimentation.
  • Preferred qualifications: Experience building creative tools, productivity applications, or other interaction-rich products; familiarity with cloud platforms, agent frameworks, design systems, or real-time collaborative experiences.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
