Position Overview:

The Senior Territory Service Representative (Senior TSR) – Operations is responsible for managing the timely pickup and delivery of customer shipments while ensuring high standards of service quality, operational efficiency and customer experience across assigned territories.

In addition to executing daily pickup and delivery activities, the role supports operational excellence by handling key customer routes, mentoring newly inducted TSRs, resolving operational exceptions and ensuring consistent adherence to Blue Dart's operating standards. Acting as an experienced frontline representative of the Blue Dart brand, the Senior TSR plays an important role in delivering service reliability, supporting branch productivity and maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism and customer engagement.

Job Purpose:

To deliver superior pickup and delivery service across assigned territories while ensuring operational excellence, customer satisfaction, accurate COD collections, regulatory compliance and mentoring frontline team members to achieve Blue Dart's service and productivity standards.

Key Roles & Responsibilities:

Pickup & Delivery Operations

• Execute timely pickup and delivery of shipments across assigned territories while meeting defined service commitments.

• Ensure accurate handling, loading, transportation and delivery of customer shipments.

• Optimize daily route execution to maximize delivery productivity and customer coverage.

• Maintain high first-attempt delivery success and minimize service failures.

• Support branch operations during peak business periods and contingency situations.

Customer Service & Brand Representation

• Deliver an exceptional customer experience during every customer interaction.

• Professionally represent the Blue Dart brand while interacting with customers.

• Build positive relationships with regular customers and business accounts.

• Resolve routine customer concerns and escalate complex issues appropriately.

COD & Documentation Management

• Collect and reconcile Cash on Delivery (COD) payments accurately.

• Ensure timely remittance of collections in accordance with company procedures.

• Obtain Proof of Delivery (POD) and complete all shipment documentation accurately.

• Ensure zero discrepancies in shipment documentation and cash handling.

• Maintain complete compliance with operational documentation requirements.

Operational Excellence

• Ensure timely system updates for pickups, deliveries and shipment exceptions.

• Strictly adhere to Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and operational guidelines.

• Identify operational issues and recommend improvements to enhance productivity.

• Support implementation of process improvements introduced by branch operations.

Coaching & Team Support

• Support induction and on-the-job training of newly joined TSRs.

• Guide team members on operational procedures, customer interactions and safety practices.

• Share best practices to improve delivery productivity and service quality.

• Assist Shift In-charges during operational contingencies.

• Promote teamwork and knowledge sharing across the operations team.

Safety & Compliance

• Ensure adherence to statutory and operational compliance requirements.
