About Us

Procter & Gamble (P&G) is a Fortune 100 company and one of the largest consumers packaged goods companies in the world. We have operations in over 75 countries with 65 trusted brands that improve the lives of 5 billion consumers worldwide. This gives our employees the opportunity to enjoy a diverse and rewarding lifelong career filled with new and exciting challenges.

Built in 1975, with a workforce of over 1000 employees, the Belleville Plant, located in Belleville, Ontario, is P&G’s largest feminine care products manufacturing facility. We manufacture bestselling P&G brands such as Always Pads, Liners and Discreet.

Job Responsibilities

P&G is recognized as an industry leader in the development of innovative production systems and technologies. Managers in our plant have far-reaching responsibilities which cover reliability, quality, safety of our equipment & processes and the motivation of the people working in the plant.

We are looking to fill a future vacancy for a Manufacturing & Engineering Technical Manager 2027 (Entry Level) to join our team in Belleville, Ontario at our Belleville Plant, starting Winter or Summer 2027.

As a Manufacturing & Engineering Technical Manager in the Operations Department , your responsibilities will require a hands-on approach and may include:

  • Analyzing and optimizing production processes, including centerline management and operational stability improvements.
  • Daily co-ordination and support for large teams to realize manufacturing targets
  • Implementation of new equipment upgrades and product innovation as a startup leader.
  • Technical manager of various manufacturing equipment, processes, and projects.
  • Quality leader for the department, driving quality event investigations, managing CAPAs (Corrective and Preventive Actions), and ensuring sustainable solutions.
  • Leading loss elimination initiatives focused on reducing scrap, line losses, and performance reliability (PR) losses to maximize efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

What else can you expect?

  • A comprehensive Corporate and Functional onboarding to accelerate your training and development
  • Intentional Impact Work Plan for meaningful projects that you will own and deliver, alongside receiving relevant capability building trainings
  • Integration into our affinity groups for socializing, mentorship, and resources
  • Competitive compensation and benefits package, including various work perks!
  • You may receive domestic relocation benefits to support your move

Job Qualifications

  • This is an on-site position based in Belleville, Ontario.
  • Preference is given to those holding university degrees (Bachelors, Masters, MBA) or in their final year of study in an engineering / technical undergraduate degree, but will consider degrees from any discipline.
  • Prior work experience in a manufacturing site is a plus

Other Traits That Make You a Great Fit

  • Being a People’s Person & Leader! This is a key skill for working in a large manufacturing site of over 1000 people!
  • Leadership and collaboration skills, demonstrated in academic / extracurricular activities
  • Strong communication skills that will enable you to thrive in a challenging and exciting work environment
  • Technical curiosity and thinking critically to uncover insights
  • Hands-on approach to problem solving with equipment and machinery, that will help build our million-dollar brands!
  • Flexibility to support an organization that runs 24/7
  • Willingness to be present on the production floor, get dirty, gaining firsthand understanding of operations to support continuous improvement and employee development.
