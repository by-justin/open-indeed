Help Drive the Energy Transition. Make Our Customers Successful.

Start your brilliant career at ABB.

ABB is a global technology leader in electrification and automation, investing heavily in the energy transition. The Netherlands faces a major challenge: moving away from fossil fuels toward a more sustainable energy future. That transition starts with smart, safe, and energy-efficient buildings. And that's exactly where you come in.

The Role: Channel Specialist with Project DNA

As a Channel Specialist Panel Builders in the commercial buildings sector, you are much more than a sales professional. You have a genuine affinity for projects. That means you not only close deals, but also understand how projects work, who is involved, and how you can contribute commercially to their success.

You will work with panel builders on projects such as hospitals, hotels, data centers, and distribution centers. You are the connecting link: inspiring panel builders, advising them on smart ABB solutions, and ensuring projects are successfully delivered.

What will you do?

  • Build and manage relationships with panel builders in the commercial buildings sector.
  • Understand how their projects operate, from planning through completion.
  • Promote ABB solutions, including intelligent power distribution, building automation, and EV charging solutions.
  • Maximize cross-selling opportunities by integrating multiple ABB products into a single project.
  • Involve ABB specialists and partners at an early stage of projects.
  • Deliver presentations and win projects. You are the face of ABB.
  • Provide technical support and consult customers on innovative solutions.

Contributing to the Energy Transition

This is more than just a job. It is a role with a mission.

The energy transition is one of the greatest challenges of our time. Buildings must become smarter, more sustainable, and more energy efficient.

What you do matters. You help panel builders select the right ABB technologies and ensure projects contribute to:

  • Reduced energy consumption
  • Increased use of renewable energy
  • Smarter energy management
  • Safer and more sustainable buildings

Your work has a direct impact on how the Netherlands uses and manages energy.

Making Customers Successful

At ABB, we think like partners. We are not here simply to sell products. We are here to make our customers successful.

That means:

  • Understanding what panel builders need and delivering more than they expect.
  • Acting as a trusted advisor, providing guidance based on their interests, not just ABB’s.
  • Solving challenges creatively, often together with internal experts.
  • Ensuring projects run smoothly, on time, within budget, and with the highest quality standards.
  • Building long-term relationships that customers value and want to continue.

Who are you?

You combine commercial drive with technical understanding. You can speak both languages. You are energetic, results-oriented, and confident enough to take initiative. You see panel builders not simply as customers, but as partners in project success.

Most importantly, you want to contribute to something bigger: a more sustainable Netherlands.

You recognize yourself in the following:

  • Bachelor's degree level of thinking and working, preferably in a technical or commercial field.
  • At least 3 years of commercial experience in electrical engineering or a related industry.
  • A passion for technology and a curiosity about how things work.
  • A project-oriented mindset and an understanding of project dynamics.
  • Excellent communication skills in Dutch and English.
  • Strong commercial instincts and the ability to close deals.
  • A proactive, independent approach and strong relationship management skills.
  • A valid driver's license (Category B).
  • A willingness to learn and take the next step in your career.
