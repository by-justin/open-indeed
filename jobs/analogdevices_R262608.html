Role Profile

Analog Devices is seeking a Field Robotics System Engineer to operate at the intersection of hands-on robotic system expertise, customer engagement, and platform enablement .

You will act as a system-level domain expert in the field , supporting customers from early architecture discussions through proof-of-concept (PoC), evaluation, and system support and integration, accelerating adoption of ADI technologies in real-world robotic systems.

This is a high-impact, customer-facing role focused on:

  • Scaling platform and solution engagements across robotics ecosystems (OEMs, disruptors, and partners), in collaboration with ADI BUs, Sales, Software, ADI Garage, Innovation Hubs, and AI teams.
  • Co-creating with customers and demonstrating value proposition
  • Providing domain expertise across robotic subsystems and platforms (motion, sensing, perception, control, power)
  • Accelerating customer decision cycles and drive design wins

This role supports priority segments including industrial robotics, humanoids, mobile robots, and service robotics, and is central to ADI’s strategy to move up the value stack—from components to subsystems and platforms.

Success in this role requires the ability to operate across complex ecosystems, influence technical and business stakeholders, and translate platform capabilities into working PoCs that accelerate customer adoption. At its core, this role executes ADI’s platforms & solutions strategy in the field—driving near-term business impact while scaling repeatable system solutions and strengthening ADI position within key robotics ecosystems.

Responsibilities

  • Build and integrate proof-of-concepts, demos, and reference systems that translate ADI platform and solution capabilities into compelling system-level demonstrations , clearly showcasing value for our customers
  • Work hands-on with robotic subsystems and platforms (motion, sensing, perception, control) aligned to real-world customer use cases
  • Support system bring-up, integration, and debugging across hardware, software, and control layers
  • Support ADI Catalyst (collaboration hubs) engagements and customer co-creation activities ; including workshops and boot camps
  • Lead customer engagements on platforms and system solutions with robotics companies, disruptors, and ecosystem partners
  • Develop and apply deep expertise across key areas of technology in Robotics
  • Provide guidance on system architecture trade-offs (system partition, performance, cost, power, scalability)
  • Convert engagements into design wins and scalable opportunities across accounts
  • Represent ADI through customer engagements, technical forums, and industry events
  • Contribute to enablement of FAEs and sales teams in system-level engagements

Skills & Experience

Required (Core Qualifications)

  • B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Robotics, or related field
  • 5+ years of experience in robotics system, subsystem, or technology design
  • Proven hands-on experience integrating robotic systems (prototype or deployed) or experience with advanced robotics (Perception systems and humanoids preferred)
  • Strong understanding of key robotic technologies , including motion control and actuation, multimodal sensing (IMU, force/torque, tactile, magnetic, vision, depth, audio), or real-time / embedded systems
  • Experience working at the hardware–software boundary (system integration, bring-up, debugging)
  • Familiarity with:C/C++ and/or PythonRobotics frameworks (e.g., ROS / ROS2)Simulation and virtual commissioning tools (e.g., Isaac Sim/Omniverse, Gazebo, RoboDK, MATLAB)
  • Exposure to AI/ML and perception systems
  • Ability to support customer engagements, evaluations, and technical discussions
  • Experience operating across matrixed teams
  • Able to bridge technical and commercial stakeholders , and translate customer needs into clear system requirements

Preferred Additional Qualifications

  • M.S. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Robotics, or related fields
  • 10+ years of experience in robotic systems and subsystem development
  • Experience with robotics platforms or simulation tools , such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano, Orin NX, Orin AGX
  • Demonstrated track record of delivering high-quality robotic hardware and/or software systems
  • Exposure to State-of-the-Art Physical AI, and Robotic perception / awareness systems
  • Experience working within robotics ecosystems (OEMs, startups, system integrators)
  • Experience supporting technical workshops, demos, or customer enablement activities

Strong candidates may not meet every qualification listed. If you bring a hands-on, system mindset and relevant experience, we encourage you to apply.
