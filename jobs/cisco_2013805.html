Position Overview:

We are seeking a highly accomplished Account Executive - Splunk (Global Financial Services Accounts) to join one of our strategic sales teams. This is a senior individual-contributor appointment carrying a named patch of two of the most significant global financial services institutions headquartered in Switzerland - spanning global banking and global insurance - with operations and decision-making structures that reach across every major region worldwide.

This role is designed for a proven operator at the top of the Swiss financial services market. We expect an established, current personal network at executive level within Swiss-headquartered global financial institutions and a verifiable history of closed, multi-million-dollar enterprise software transactions in this segment. You will be responsible for protecting and growing a substantial installed base - securing high-value renewals while developing expansion strategies across the security and observability portfolio - and for positioning Splunk as a strategic long-term platform partner at group level.

If you are ready for your next challenge and seeking an opportunity to grow within a company where you can truly thrive alongside a high-performing, results-driven team within the Cisco/Splunk family, please continue reading.

Responsibilities:

  • Client Relationship Management: Leverage and deepen existing executive relationships across global banking and insurance organisations - CISO, CTO, COO, Group Technology, architecture, and group procurement / category management - at both Swiss headquarters and global divisional level.
  • Account Development - Protect & Grow: Secure and renew a large installed base while identifying and capitalising on expansion opportunities across the portfolio, driving retention and growth in an environment of active vendor consolidation and cost scrutiny.
  • Global Account Orchestration: Navigate the matrixed buying environment of global financial institutions, where commercial authority and budget span group functions, divisions, and regions. Coordinate the extended account team across geographies, including out-of-country and local account roles engaged on the same customer.
  • Extended Team Leadership: Structure highly complex, multi-stakeholder situations toward defined outcomes by orchestrating System Engineers, Customer Success Managers, specialists, and partners - leading the virtual account team toward successful execution.
  • Program-based Execution: Run account engagements as structured programs, with clear close plans, mutual action plans, milestones, and disciplined project-based execution rather than opportunistic selling.
  • AI-Driven Selling & Productivity: Position Splunk's AI-powered security and observability capabilities with credibility in financial institutions - including AI governance, model risk, and resilience considerations - while applying internal AI tooling with versatility to accelerate account research, strategy development, and execution.
  • Negotiation & Closing: Lead large-scale contract negotiations, enterprise agreements, and executive presentations with C-level stakeholders and group procurement, ensuring mutually beneficial agreements that align with both customer and company goals.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Collaborate with internal teams (engineering, product, marketing, legal) and with partners and the Cisco field to develop tailored solutions for the specific demands of globally regulated financial institutions.
  • Market & Regulatory Intelligence: Stay ahead of industry trends, competitor activity, and technology shifts in banking and insurance. Understand the regulatory environment governing this market - including FINMA requirements on operational resilience, outsourcing, and cyber risk, DORA obligations affecting EU operations, banking secrecy, and Swiss/EU data-residency and privacy obligations - and reflect it in how you position security and observability.
  • Sales Forecasting & Reporting: Maintain accurate pipeline, forecast, and account plans across a concentrated, high-value portfolio, providing clear insight and recommendations for improvement.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with engineering, customer success, and support to ensure successful delivery and customer satisfaction across multi-workstream engagements.

Requirements:

  • 10+ years of enterprise software sales experience with a demonstrated history of consistent quota overachievement, the majority of it within financial services.
  • Proven, verifiable track record of closed multi-million-dollar enterprise software transactions with global banks and/or insurers headquartered in Switzerland.
  • Established and current executive-level network within Swiss-headquartered global financial institutions, with the ability to open the right doors from day one.
  • Deep understanding of how global financial institutions buy: group procurement, vendor risk management, outsourcing governance, global-versus-divisional decision rights, and budget cycles.
  • Strong command of the regulatory landscape shaping technology decisions in financial services - FINMA outsourcing and operational-resilience requirements, DORA, banking secrecy, and data-residency obligations.
  • Demonstrated ability to orchestrate an extended, virtual account team (System Engineers, Customer Success Managers, specialists, partners) toward a shared outcome without direct authority.
  • Experience running account engagements as structured programs - close plans, mutual action plans, and project-based execution on complex accounts.
  • Track record of both protecting a substantial installed base (renewals and retention) and driving expansion under vendor-consolidation pressure.
  • Strong AI fluency on two fronts: the ability to position AI-led value credibly within customer environments, and the versatility to operate internal AI systems and tooling to accelerate research, strategy formulation, and execution.
  • Mastery of MEDDPICC, or an equivalent qualification methodology.
  • Boardroom-ready executive presence with a proven ability to influence and close multi-million-dollar enterprise software deals at CxO level.
  • Accurate forecasting and commitment delivery across large portfolios and extended sales cycles.
  • Exceptional communication, negotiation, and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Deep understanding of enterprise IT ecosystems including cloud infrastructure, security, and observability / digital transformation initiatives.
  • Experience in SaaS, security, or observability environments.
  • Power user of enterprise CRM platforms (Salesforce), with strong operational discipline and reporting fluency.
  • Minimum of a Bachelor's degree; an advanced degree is a plus.
  • Fluent in German and English (written and spoken); additional languages a plus.
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland and internationally.
