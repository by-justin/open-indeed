Micron Memory Japan にて、最先端の NANDフラッシュメモリ技術の開発を担う不揮発性メモリ設計エンジニア（NAND Designer）を募集しています。

本ポジションでは、次世代NANDフラッシュメモリの中核となる回路設計・最適化に携わり、 業界の新たな標準を築く高性能NAND製品の実現に直接貢献していただきます。 意欲的で卓越したエンジニアが集うチームの一員として、最先端技術の創出に挑戦できる環境です。

Join our bold and extraordinary team at Micron Memory Japan as a Non-volatile Memory Designer! In this remarkable role, you will be at the heart of developing modern NAND Flash memory technologies. You will develop and optimize interface circuits, data transfer circuits, memory arrays, page buffer and string drivers, analog circuits, and logic controllers. Your contributions will be vital in successfully delivering powerful NAND products that set new industry standards.

Responsibilities:

  • Build the base die of NAND flash.
  • Run circuit simulations with industry-standard tools like FINESIM, HSPICE, and VERILOG.
  • Examine timing, area, and power aspects in NAND flash builds.
  • Support new NAND product opportunities by giving to the build, layout, and optimization of memory, logic, and analog circuits.

Qualifications:

  • Strong skills in analog and mixed-signal circuit building.
  • Confirmed ability to innovate and tackle problems in high-performance memory development.
  • Excellent analytical, communication, and collaboration skills, with the capacity to explain sophisticated technical ideas clearly.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • In-depth familiarity with NAND Flash operation and different NAND Flash products.
  • At least 5 years' experience in the memory industry with an MS degree or equivalent experience, or 10+ years with a BS degree (or equivalent).
  • A history of being a proactive and diligent phenomenal teammate who thrives in collaborative environments.
  • Ability to work well across build and verification teams to support technical alignment and execution.
