Procurement Project Specialist

We are seeking a dynamic and organized Procurement Project Specialist to join our Supply Chain Management team. You will play a crucial role in executing procurement activities across complex projects, acting as a key interface between Sales, Engineering, Production, Quality, and Suppliers. Your focus will be on ensuring timely sourcing, cost efficiency, supplier performance, and material availability throughout the entire project lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Project Procurement Execution: Serve as the SCM point of contact for assigned project procurement activities across defined categories (Steel, Plastics, Electronics, Non-Ferrous materials, and others)
  • Supplier Coordination & Sourcing Support: Cooperate with Category Managers in identifying, evaluating, and selecting suppliers
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as SCM interface with Sales, Engineering, Technical Centers, and Quality teams
  • Participate  in project meetings and follow up on procurement-related actions
  • Support  coordination with international teams and regional operations

Your background

  • Bachelor’s degree in supply chain management, Engineering, Business, or related field
  • 2-3 years of experience in procurement, supply chain, or project support roles, preferably from industrial or manufacturing environments
  • Experience with ERP systems, preferably SAP
  • Familiarity with planning tools such as IBP (Integrated Business Planning Strong stakeholder
  • Proficiency in English (additional languages are a plus)
