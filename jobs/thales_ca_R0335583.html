About Thales Aviation Global Services (AGS)

Thales Aviation Global Services (AGS) is the Business Line within Thales AVS (Avionics) dedicated to the through-life support and services of Avionics, In-Flight Entertainment (IFE) and AEC products for airlines, operators and aircraft manufacturers worldwide. AGS covers maintenance and repair contracts, linefit and retrofit programs, spares provisioning, and an expanding range of digital services, delivered through a global network of regional hubs.

Position Summary

The Senior Regional Sales Manager is a senior sales professional within AGS Middle East, Africa & Turkey (MEA), responsible for leading the region’s most complex, high-value captures (typically exceeding €50M), from early positioning through to negotiation and contract close. The role requires strong engagement with customers up to C-level , and the ability to build a compelling win-win value proposition. It also requires strong influencing skills to align a range of internal stakeholders – including central teams working across different time zones, the IFE Business Line, and the wider Thales international organization – in order to secure organizational accountability and advance commercial and customer satisfaction topics.

Job Objective

In the framework of the strategic growth of AGS in the Middle East, Africa & Turkey region, the Senior Regional Sales Manager:

  • operates as a senior, experienced sales professional, with a high degree of autonomy on the region’s most complex sales captures;
  • personally leads and closes major captures, with a track record on opportunities exceeding €50M across the region;
  • builds and maintains trusted relationships with customer stakeholders up to C-level, translating technical and commercial value into a compelling win-win value proposition;
  • autonomously runs bids and leads cross-functional bid teams, coordinating central and remote stakeholders across time zones to deliver competitive, on-time offers;
  • contributes directly to the region’s growth objectives (10% CAGR, over $100M USD order intake) through the sustained conversion of major opportunities;
  • works in close alignment with the Area Directors responsible for the region’s strategic accounts and the General Manager.

Roles & Key Responsibilities

The Senior Regional Sales Manager:

  • Leads capture strategy and execution on complex, high-value opportunities (typically >€50M), from qualification and lobbying through to negotiation and contract signature;
  • Engages and builds trust with customer stakeholders up to C-level, tailoring the value proposition to their strategic and operational priorities;
  • Develops and articulates a clear win-win value proposition, capturing the technical and commercial value of AGS offers and explaining it in terms that resonate with the customer;
  • Leads negotiations and drives captures to closure, balancing customer expectations with AGS commercial and margin objectives;
  • Autonomously organizes and leads bid teams, coordinating central functions (Programs, Engineering, Finance, Legal, Contracts) that often work remotely and across different time zones;
  • Builds strong working alignment with the IFE Business Line and the wider Thales international organization, ensuring consistent positioning and coordinated customer engagement across product lines;
  • Exercises strong influencing skills, without formal authority over most stakeholders involved, to secure organizational accountability and gain traction on commercial and customer satisfaction topics;
  • Works closely with the Area Directors responsible for the region’s strategic accounts and the General Manager, aligning capture strategy with account plans;
  • Identifies and develops business opportunities for new and adjacent digital services (e.g. Flight Optim, Electronic Flight Bag solutions, cybersecurity), alongside the core Avionics and IFE captures;
  • Maintains rigorous pipeline, forecast and CRM discipline for the captures under direct responsibility;
  • Represents AGS at industry events, customer forums and internal business reviews.
