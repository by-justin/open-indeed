Job Description

About the Opportunity

Digital Fractal Technologies is looking for a student or recent graduate with hands-on digital marketing experience to support an internal marketing campaign across Canada.

This is a project-based remote role requiring approximately 100 hours of work . The ideal candidate already has practical experience setting up, managing, and optimizing Google Ads, email marketing campaigns, and social media marketing assets. This is not a training-only role; we are looking for someone who can execute with guidance, follow a structured go-to-market plan, and help improve campaign performance against specific goals.

Project Overview

The selected candidate will be provided with campaign instructions, target audiences, messaging direction, and a go-to-market strategy. They will be responsible for setting up the required marketing assets, managing campaigns, monitoring performance, making optimizations, and reporting on progress.

The campaign will target audiences across Canada and may include paid ads, email campaigns, landing page coordination, social media content distribution, and performance tracking.

Project Details

Role Type: Project-based contract Estimated Hours: Approximately 100 hours Location: Remote, Canada Start Date: As soon as possible Schedule: Flexible, based on project needs Compensation: To be discussed based on experience

How to Apply

Please submit:

  • A short introduction explaining your relevant experience.
  • Examples of Google Ads, email marketing, or social media campaigns you have worked on.
  • Any results or metrics you can share, such as leads generated, CTR, conversion rate, engagement, or campaign growth.
  • Your availability and estimated timeline to complete a 100-hour project.

Applicants with a proven track record and practical campaign experience will be prioritized.

Skills and Qualifications

Responsibilities

The candidate will be expected to:

  • Set up and manage Google Ads campaigns based on provided instructions.
  • Create or configure campaign assets, including ad groups, keywords, ad copy, extensions, audiences, and tracking where required.
  • Support email marketing campaign setup, including lists, segments, templates, scheduling, and performance review.
  • Manage and schedule social media marketing content across relevant platforms.
  • Optimize campaigns based on performance data, including CTR, conversions, cost per lead, engagement, and campaign goals.
  • Monitor campaign performance and provide regular updates.
  • Make recommendations to improve results.
  • Ensure marketing assets are organized, accurate, and aligned with the provided campaign strategy.
  • Work independently and remotely while maintaining clear communication.

Required Experience

Applicants should have existing experience with:

  • Google Ads campaign setup and optimization.
  • Email marketing platforms such as Mailchimp, HubSpot, Constant Contact, Brevo, or similar tools.
  • Social media marketing on platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or X.
  • Basic marketing analytics and performance reporting.
  • Campaign optimization based on data.
  • Working with goals such as lead generation, website traffic, conversions, engagement, or awareness.

Preferred Qualifications

Preference will be given to candidates who can show:

  • Previous campaign results or examples of work.
  • Experience with Canadian B2B or technology marketing.
  • Familiarity with Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, landing pages, CRM tools, or marketing automation.
  • Strong writing ability for ad copy, social posts, and email content.
  • Ability to work independently and execute tasks without constant supervision.

Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a student or recent graduate in marketing, business, communications, digital media, or a related field who already has real campaign experience. They should be comfortable working remotely, following instructions, asking good questions when needed, and improving campaign performance over time.

This role is best suited for someone who has already run digital campaigns before and wants to apply their skills on a real business project.
