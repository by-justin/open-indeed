In order to support the ESACE Department Airbus Helicopters is looking for a

Export Control & National Security Officer (m/w/d)

Within the ESACE department (Export Control & Supplier Strategies), you will join our Aircraft Technical Services (ESA) team at Airbus Helicopters in Donauwörth. In this role, you will be at the interface of business continuity and regulatory compliance. You will actively shape the future of Aircraft Technical Services, contributing to our operational excellence and global aircraft availability by ensuring we meet the highest standards of technical documentation and maintenance engineering.Your Mission & Responsibilities

Your location

At our Donauwörth site, located on the banks of the river Danube, we develop helicopters from the initial idea to the airworthy product. Watch them take off and touch down on our own on-site test airfield.

Our Benefits for You

  • Fair Compensation & Extras: Attractive remuneration and individual additional benefits, such as company pension schemes, mobility offers (e.g., bike leasing), or corporate discounts with partner companies in accordance with our current guidelines.
  • Time for You (and Your Loved Ones): 30 days of annual leave based on our collective agreement (35-hour week); work-life balance through flexible working hours (flextime), mobile working, part-time options, job sharing, and sabbatical opportunities.
  • Growth Made Easy: Excellent professional development opportunities and international, group-wide career perspectives.
  • Feel Good at Work: On-site company doctor; comprehensive health programs (sports courses, preventive care, etc.), canteen and cafeteria, and local childcare facilities at selected locations.
  • Diversity Connects: Work in a diverse environment with more than 140 nationalities, where exchange, mutual support, and inclusion are part of our DNA.

Your tasks and responsibilities

  • Export Control (EC) & National Security (NS): Act as the ESA referent for classification topics. You will define ESA classification rules, manage the "Tech Rater" network, ensure license management for ESA in Germany, and lead the "Data Governance" work package within the Idefix project.
  • Strategic Direction: Lead strategic decisions regarding the Tech Data Product Policy, ensuring EC and NS requirements are integrated. You will prepare decision-making proposals for management and execute action plans accordingly.
  • Aviation Safety: Serve as the central ESAC focal point for Aviation Safety. You will cascade safety culture, classify hazards (Minor/Major) in accordance with Safety Management Systems (SMS), and support the ESAC management in promoting active safety awareness.
  • Stakeholder Management: Act as a connector between operational teams and various departments (Procurement, IT, Legal, Security) and Top Management across our hubs in Germany, France, and Spain.

Desired skills and qualifications

  • Education: Completed university degree (mandatory).
  • Experience: Several years of professional experience in Support & Service (S&S) (mandatory).
  • Qualifications: Strong knowledge of Export Control and National Security regulations and classification rules. Demonstrated experience in Aviation Safety (AH directives, processes, and technical competencies related to ESA activities).
  • Expertise: Proven experience in complex project management and transversal management.
  • Leadership: Strong ability to influence stakeholders at all levels, from operational teams to Top Management.
  • Languages: Negotiation-level English is mandatory.
  • Assets: Knowledge of S&S (Support & Services) activities is considered a plus.

Behavioral Competencies

You are a resilient, autonomous, and analytical professional with a structured approach to complex environments. You embrace a pro-active "Welcome Problem" attitude and possess excellent communication and intercultural skills. You are customer-oriented, a strong team player, and demonstrate rigor in everything you do.
