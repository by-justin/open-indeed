Job Description

職務概要

営業部の責任者として、営業戦略および組織運営をリードし、事業成長と収益性の向上を推進していただきます。本ポジションは経営メンバーの一員として、会社の中長期戦略の策定と実行に主体的に関与します。国内外の顧客・パートナーとの強固な関係を構築し、新規事業開発を牽引するとともに、既存顧客との関係深化を図ります。また、営業部の責任者として、事業機会の創出から受注獲得までをリードし、持続的な売上成長、収益性の向上、および顧客価値の最大化に責任を持っていただきます。

このポジションで得られる価値（魅力）

  • 日本・ASEAN・韓国・オセアニアを対象に、成長領域の事業拡大をリードできるポジションです。
  • 経営メンバーの一員として、中長期の事業戦略・重点投資・組織設計に関与し、大きな裁量を持って意思決定を推進できます。
  • 先進技術領域（ソフトウェア／システム、ADAS、E/E、サイバーセキュリティ、水素・燃料電池等）を含む幅広いポートフォリオを活用し、顧客課題に対する提案活動から受注獲得までをリードできます。
  • 海外拠点・ドイツ本社と連携しながら、グローバル環境でリーダーシップを発揮できます。

戦略立案・実行

  • 新規市場（East Asia）開拓に向けた事業計画の立案および推進
  • 市場・競合・顧客ニーズの分析（継続的モニタリング）および戦略のアップデート
  • 経営メンバーとしての中長期事業計画の策定、推進および達成責任

営業活動・顧客エンゲージメント

  • 新規顧客開拓に向けた提案・交渉・契約締結のリード
  • 国内外顧客（East Asia）との折衝および関係構築
  • East Asia拠点営業担当者への支援・調整

プロジェクトマネジメントチーム、エンジニアリングチームとの連携・提案推進

  • 顧客ニーズを社内関係部門へ適切に展開し、競争力のあるソリューション提案につなげる

Position Overview

As the Head of Sales Department, you will lead our sales strategy and organizational operations, driving business growth and profitability. This role is part of the executive management team, actively shaping and executing the company’s mid- to long-term strategy. You will build strong relationships with domestic and international customers and partners, spearhead new business development, and deepen engagement with existing customers. As the Head of Sales Department, you will lead the creation of business opportunities through to order acquisition, taking responsibility for sustainable sales growth, improved profitability, and maximizing customer value.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Planning & Execution

  • Develop and implement company-wide sales and market strategies
  • Formulate business plans for new market development (East Asia)
  • Conduct regular market analysis, competitive benchmarking, and customer needs assessment to update strategies
  • Act as a member of the management team, responsible for shaping and driving mid- to long-term business plans

Sales Activities & Customer Engagement

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with key customers; take ownership of critical deals and negotiations
  • Lead proposals, negotiations, and contract closures for new customer acquisition
  • Manage interactions with domestic and international customers (Japan, ASEAN, Korea, Oceania)
  • Coordinate and support sales representatives

Project Management/Engineering Collaboration & Proposal Development

  • Collaborate closely with both the project management and engineering teams to understand customer requirements and lead proposal activities based on technical feasibility
  • Effectively communicate customer needs to relevant internal stakeholders and translate them into competitive solution proposals

Organizational Management

  • Oversee the sales organization and ensure clear roles and responsibilities across the team
  • Lead goal setting, talent development, and performance management for the sales team

Domestic & International Collaboration

  • Maintain communication and alignment with headquarters in Germany
  • Collaborate with East Asian offices
  • Work closely with Bosch Japan divisions for business development and coordination

Target Markets & Product Portfolio

  • Markets Two-wheelers, passenger cars, commercial vehicles, off-highway (construction, industrial, agricultural machinery), MaaS, aerospace
  • Products Automotive: Steering systems, braking systems, E/E architecture, system engineering, ADAS/AD, VCU/ECU, cybersecurity, hydrogen engine and fuel cell solutions Non-Automotive: Millimeter-wave radar, ultrasonic sensors, cameras, motors, inverters, cybersecurity, hydrogen engine and fuel cell solutions
