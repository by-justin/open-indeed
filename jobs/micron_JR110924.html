2027 Micron Taiwan Semester Internship Program (Taoyuan / Tongluo / Taichung / Tainan)

Start your career journey with an internship and explore the semiconductor industry with Micron!

We are looking for talented and motivated students who are eager to gain hands-on experience in semiconductor technology and challenge themselves in a dynamic work environment. As a Micron Semester Intern, you will participate in meaningful projects, gain valuable industry experience, and build a strong foundation for your future career as a professional engineer.

• Project-Based Internship & Mentorship Program Each intern will participate in an 18-week professional internship and be paired with a dedicated mentor within the department. Your mentor will provide guidance and support to help accelerate your technical growth and career development.

• Learning & Networking Opportunities Through onboarding training, exclusive intern events, and final project presentations, you will have the opportunity to learn, connect, and collaborate with talented peers from universities across Taiwan as well as world-class engineers at Micron. We value not only your professional growth but also your overall internship experience.

• Location: Taichung Central Taiwan Science Park (Houli) / Taoyuan Hwa Ya Technology Park (Linkou) / Tainan Annan Industrial Park / Tongluo Science Park (Miaoli)

• Internship Period: February 22, 2027 – June 27, 2027 Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

• Eligibility: Senior undergraduate students, first- and second-year master's students (excluding executive or in-service programs), and Ph.D. students who are expected to graduate within one year

• Preferred Fields of Study: Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, Mechanical Engineering, Information Management, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Industrial Engineering, Engineering Management, or other related disciplines

• Academic Credit: Internship credits may be granted subject to your university's internship credit requirements

Outstanding interns may be considered for future full-time employment opportunities with Micron upon graduation.
