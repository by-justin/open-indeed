Job Description:

We are seeking an accomplished Senior Manager, Core Trade Finance Operations, with deep expertise in international trade finance and a demonstrated ability to lead high-performing teams within a complex, highly regulated operating environment. The successful candidate will bring comprehensive knowledge of Documentary Letters of Credit, Documentary Collections, Standby Letters of Credit and Guarantees, with a strong understanding of the operational requirements supporting core trade finance activities

Reporting to the Associate Vice President, Global Trade Finance Operations, the Senior Manager will lead the execution and performance of Core Trade Finance Operations across regional processing teams. This leader will oversee a high-performing organization through several Regional Managers, ensuring consistent operational delivery, effective risk management, strong controls, and an exceptional client experience across geographies and time zones.

The Senior Manager will work closely with Business and Front Office leadership to understand business priorities, enable growth, resolve operational matters, and ensure that the operating model effectively supports client and business needs. The role will establish strong partnerships with Product, Compliance, AML, Legal, Risk Management, Technology, and other Operations teams to deliver sustainable and controlled outcomes.

The successful candidate will provide clear operational leadership across the Core Trade Finance portfolio, establishing accountability for service delivery, productivity, quality, risk management, and client outcomes. The Senior Manager will ensure that regional teams operate as an integrated organization, leveraging standardized processes, effective governance, appropriate resource deployment, and strong collaboration across locations.

Operational excellence and continuous improvement are central to the mandate. The Senior Manager will identify opportunities to simplify and optimize processes, improve turnaround times, strengthen controls, and increase operational efficiency. The role will lead the implementation and adoption of process and technology enhancements, partnering with the AVP and relevant stakeholders to ensure changes are effectively delivered and embedded within the organization.

The Senior Manager will also serve as a senior operational escalation point for complex transactions, client matters, risk issues, and service challenges. With strong technical expertise and sound judgment, the incumbent will provide guidance to Regional Managers and their teams, ensuring decisions are aligned with applicable policies, regulatory requirements, risk appetite, and business objectives.

As a people leader, the Senior Manager will build and develop a strong leadership bench through the Regional Managers, establish clear expectations and accountability, and foster a culture of collaboration, ownership, continuous improvement, and client focus.
