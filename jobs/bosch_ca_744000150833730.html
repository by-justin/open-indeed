Job Description

The System Architect is responsible for defining, developing, and maintaining complex automotive embedded systems and system-of-systems throughout the product lifecycle.

The role requires deep understanding of customer use cases, stakeholder needs, system behavior, internal and external interfaces, and functional architectures. The System Architect acts as the technical owner of the system concept and ensures that requirements, architecture, interfaces, and verification strategies are complete, consistent, traceable, and aligned with customer expectations and industry standards.

Beyond individual contributions, the System Architect provides technical leadership across multiple engineering disciplines, including System, Software, Hardware, Integration, Verification & Validation, Security, and Functional Safety teams. The role drives technical decisions, supports customer discussions, and ensures successful execution of system engineering activities in compliance with ASPICE and Bosch development processes.

Main responsibilities

  • System Requirements Engineering Elicit, analyze, negotiate, document, and manage stakeholder requirements.Translate customer and stakeholder needs into clear, complete, and testable system requirements.Ensure consistency, completeness, feasibility, and verifiability of requirements.Define acceptance criteria and verification strategies for system requirements.Establish and maintain end-to-end traceability between stakeholder requirements, system requirements, architecture elements, and verification artifacts.Conduct impact analysis for requirement changes and coordinate implementation across affected domains.Review, approve, baseline, and release system requirements.
