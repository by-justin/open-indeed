Job Description

What is the opportunity?

Royal Bank of Canada is seeking an experienced Managing Director to lead our enterprise Foreign Exchange (FX) Operations function. This is a strategic leadership role overseeing FX settlement, clearing, trading support, and operational infrastructure across all business lines and global centers. You will drive operational excellence, manage enterprise-wide FX operations governance, and ensure world-class service delivery while maintaining the highest standards of regulatory compliance and operational risk management. In this role, you'll have the opportunity to shape the future of RBC's FX operations, lead a talented global team, and make a significant impact on one of the bank's most critical business functions.

What will you do?

  • Develop and execute enterprise FX operations strategy and governance frameworks aligned with regulatory requirements and RBC Group standards; establish strategic priorities and drive continuous improvement initiatives
  • Oversee FX settlement and clearing operations across all currency pairs and markets, including pre-settlement matching, SWIFT messaging, nostro account management, and clearinghouse interactions; ensure settlement integrity and minimize settlement risk
  • Direct FX trading support services including order management, trade execution support, client confirmations, and settlement coordination; ensure timely and accurate trade processing
  • Establish and maintain comprehensive operational and settlement risk controls, counterparty credit limits, and compliance procedures aligned with OSFI, FINTRAC, and international standards
  • Manage and develop a high-performing team of FX operations professionals across global centers; foster a culture of operational excellence, risk awareness, and continuous improvement
  • Oversee FX technology platforms, trading systems, settlement systems, and risk management infrastructure; identify and implement system enhancements to improve efficiency and resilience
  • Establish incident management and business continuity procedures; ensure timely resolution of operational issues and regulatory compliance
  • Collaborate with global markets trading teams, treasury, risk management, and external counterparties; serve as senior point of contact for FX operations matters with leadership and regulators
  • Drive performance metrics and reporting on settlement success rates, operational efficiency, and risk indicators; provide regular updates to senior management on operations performance and strategic initiatives

What do you need to succeed?

Must-have :

  • Minimum 12 years of progressive experience in FX operations, trading support, or settlement operations, with at least 6 years in a senior leadership or management capacity at a tier-1 global financial institution
  • Deep expertise in FX trading operations, settlement procedures, clearing processes, SWIFT standards, and nostro account management
  • Proven track record building and leading high-performing operations teams in a complex, fast-paced trading environment
  • Strong understanding of financial services regulations including OSFI guidance, FINTRAC requirements, and international FX market standards
  • Demonstrated expertise in operational and settlement risk management, with ability to establish effective controls and governance frameworks

Nice-to-have:

  • Master's degree (MBA or equivalent)
  • SWIFT certification, Certified Treasury Professional (CTP), or Certified Financial Risk Manager (FRM) designation
  • Experience with post-trade infrastructure, market infrastructure provider standards, or emerging technologies in operations (blockchain, distributed ledger technology)
  • Background with RBC or other tier-1 global banks in FX or treasury operations

We thrive on the challenge to be our best, progressive thinking to keep growing, and working together to deliver trusted advice to help our clients thrive and communities prosper. We care about each other, reaching our potential, making a difference to our communities, and achieving success that is mutual.

  • A comprehensive Total Rewards Program include competitive compensation and flexible benefits, such as 401(k) program with company-matching contributions, health, dental, vision, life, disability insurance, and paid-time off.
  • Leaders who support your development through coaching and managing opportunities.
  • Ability to make a difference and lasting impact.
  • Work in a dynamic, collaborative, progressive, and high-performing team.
