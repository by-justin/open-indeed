Description

At Meta Reality Labs, we build tools that help people feel connected anytime, anywhere. We’re on a mission to build the next computing platform by putting people at the center of emerging hardware and software technologies, whether on the go with wearables and augmented reality, in the home or at work, or fully immersed in alternate virtual reality worlds.

The Meta Reality Labs Product Marketing organization leads inbound product marketing, product launch strategy, and in-market business growth. The Product Marketing team influences early stage product development and roadmaps, creates go-to-market plans, supports in-market growth of the ecosystem, and provides direction for future investments.

Our Wearables teams at Meta Reality Labs are building products to enable people to be more present, connected, and empowered in the world. The portfolio includes both display and displayless AI glasses, other form factors, and AI software experiences. We’re looking for an experienced Product Marketing Director to build and lead a team of product marketers working on software experiences for emerging products for Wearables at Meta.

This person will be responsible for influencing early stage product development, leading go-to-market strategy, and driving growth and adoption in-market. Excellent strategic thinking skills, collaboration and partnership skills, cross-functional leadership, analytical and communication skills, and comfort with ambiguity are a must. The ideal candidate will also have a track record of building and leading high performing teams, a strong bias for action, a vision for how we can better meet the needs of consumers and our partners, and a passion for innovative products.

Responsibilities

  • Lead a high performing team of software product marketers, managing team performance and supporting career development and growth.
  • Manage a portfolio of AI products and experiences.
  • Work hands-on with key stakeholders to advance project milestones and drive product market fit, including but not limited to prioritizing audience use cases and needs, identifying and sizing key markets to target, and constantly monitoring engagement and NPS.
  • Partner with product leadership to influence product and business strategy with a view of customer needs and market opportunities.
  • Design and lead go-to-market strategy, communications, and execution in partnership with key stakeholders, such as Product Management, Marcom, Communications, and Product Growth teams to ensure we achieve our business goals and objectives.
  • Consistently build strong internal partnerships, influence, resolve conflict, build alignment, and work effectively across teams to execute strategy.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 15+ years of experience with product marketing
  • 15+ years of experience with consumer products
  • 12+ years of experience with team leadership, people management, cross-functional team leadership, or leadership
  • Track record of building partnerships with Product Management and cross-functional teams that shape multi-year product roadmaps and go-to-market strategies
  • Leadership experience in a global role and knowledge of diverse cultural, social and business landscapes
  • Experience owning and overseeing multiple portfolio areas and marketing vision while creating buy-in and enthusiasm from peers, individual contributors, and other leaders
  • Experience influencing and leading strategies across multiple lines of business and matrixed teams
  • Experience identifying and analyzing complex, long term, critical problems facing the relevant areas of the business and drove solutions for them
  • Understands organizational health, can articulate an organizational vision, and can effectively diagnose and make recommendations to solve complex issues that may span multiple teams (including decisions around resource prioritization, top-level goal alignment, and balancing impact with team longevity)
  • Bachelor's degree

Preferred Qualifications

  • MS/MBA degree
  • Experience managing a multi-disciplinary team
  • Experience bringing new consumer technology products to market
  • Experience and/or domain knowledge in AI, augmented reality, virtual reality, mixed reality, wearable devices, mobile devices, and/or hardware/software platforms
  • Experience driving organizational health and an organizational vision, and can effectively diagnose and make recommendations to solve complex issues that may span multiple teams (including decisions around resource prioritization, top-level goal alignment, and balancing impact with team longevity)
  • Experience with founding or leading a company or division and/or launching products and businesses
