You are responsible for

  • Deploy multiple digital propositions SaaS applications via automation & orchestration platforms.
  • Implement infrastructure for CI/CD for example Jenkins
  • Implement CI/CD pipelines
  • Consult with customer on CI/CD best practices
  • Implement monitoring across Philips wide Cloud based Platform
  • Create / version / test of scripts (Bash, PowerShell).
  • Automation of installation / configuration / management of n tier web applications in a secure environment, for example Apache Tomcat, MySQL and PostgreSQL– Java and Node.js.
  • Development Operations (DevOps), provision, run scripts and perform UAT / validation testing within secure environment.
  • Collaborate with software development to maintain test / staging / production environments to meet uptime, performance, cost, and security goals.
  • Deliver on SLA to both internal and external customers.

You are a part of

The HealthSuite Digital Platform is an open, cloud-based healthcare platform that will support data collection across the spectrum from clinical to consumer lifestyle, allowing for integration and analysis of ‘big’ data from multiple sources worldwide, including electronic medical records, diagnostic and treatment information, images, reports, notes, wearables and Philips connected consumer lifestyle products. The platform will utilize Philips’ clinical data models and medical device interoperability and is designed to be highly scalable with built-in comprehensive data security and privacy/permission control.

You will be part of a highly skilled cloud operations team responsible for overall IaaS and PaaS management at a global scale. This pivotal role will provide you the opportunity to leverage your diverse IT background including Windows, scripting, security, networking and cloud knowledge to deliver our healthcare platform services to a broad set of internal and external customers. Take your technical skills to the next level working on diverse and complex technical challenges in a dynamic and fast paced environment. As a part of this new organization you will have a unique opportunity to broaden your technical knowledge and problem solving skills while being at the forefront of healthcare innovation working on our advanced healthcare digital platform.

No other organization has the breadth and depth of the extended portfolio of health care solutions than Philips has across the entire health continuum, from the hospital into the home.  Philips is uniquely positioned as a health technology leader. We’ve been doing this for over 100 years, and leading the confluence of clinical and informatics is in our DNA.

  • Familiarity with RDBMS (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and NoSQL Database or related technologies.
  • Knowledge of RESTful web services, YAML, XML and JSON.
  • Familiarity with healthcare industry.
  • Experience working within a regulated environment.
  • Familiarity with Docker.

AWS Knowledge :

  • Hands-On experience using AWS compute, network, storage and database services.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree (BA / BS) in Business, Computer Science, Information Systems or related field preferred or equivalent experience with at least 3 years of experience working on a 24x7 Cloud or SaaS production operations team.
  • Experience deploying applications within a production public cloud environment (AWS or similar).
  • Understanding of elasticity and scalability concepts.
  • Understanding of networking concepts including configuration (DNS, VPN, proxies).
  • Expertise with CI/CD technologies
  • Familiarity with Linux systems administration (CentOS, Ubuntu, RHEL or similar).
  • Experience in scripting languages (Ruby, Bash, Python or similar).
  • Familiarity with Cloud Infrastructure & Application monitoring tools.
  • Experience with orchestration/ provisioning solutions (Chef, Salt, Terraform or similar).
  • Exposure to Cloud Foundry.
  • Ability to interact with a diverse set of stakeholders
  • Ability to interact with AWS (AWS SDK, AWS API, Command Line Interface, AWS Cloud Formation, and other AWS Services such as SNS, SQS, SWF, DynamoDB, S3, etc).
  • Skilled in automation technologies. For example using Chef, Salt, Ansible, Bash, Scripting, Python, PowerShell to perform day-to-day support functions and release management activities.
  • Exposure to information and application security including public key encryption, SSH, access credentials, and X.509 certificates.
