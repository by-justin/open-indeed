J.D. Irving, Limited, NS Woodlands Division is currently seeking candidates for experienced/skilled****Harvester Operator positions – there are multiple positions available. Operations will take place within Nova Scotia with the possibility of some work taking place in southeastern New Brunswick. Forest Machine Operators will be expected to operate the equipment in a safe, efficient and productive manner according to J.D. Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities:

  • Operate harvesting machine in an efficient, and productive manner according to J.D. Irving, Limited’s Best Management Practices and provide equipment maintenance as required.
  • Maintain productivity, production, and utilization targets, and work independently in remote environments.
  • May be assigned to related/ancillary tasks for short periods of time including but not limited to: equipment maintenance, tidying work areas, training, harvest and boundary line layouts, road maintenance activities, forest fire prevention activities

Qualifications

Qualifications:

  • Education Required: High School diploma at a minimum
  • Years of Experience Required: 2 years of experience with the operation of heavy forest machinery or a combination of one year of work experience with forest machinery and practical, vocational training in the industry of forest machine operations
  • General heavy equipment mechanical skills or Heavy Equipment Operator Certificate will be considered an asset
