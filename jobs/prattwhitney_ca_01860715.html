What You Will Do:

The Associate Director – Direct and Indirect Procurement – India will be part of the P&WC supply management organization a team of Direct (for P&WC) and Indirect Procurement team in India (at PW India level) to manage supplier activities and material flow to ensure timely deliveries and quality for P&WC needs. He will ensure adherence to P&WC processes and accelerate people development and consolidated synergies and cost / productive improvement in the overall activities.

The Candidate will report jointly to the PW India Leader and the VP of supply management and will work along the Director of Strategic Sourcing; Transitions in addition will work closely with Pratt & Whitney’s Global Supply Chain Strategic Sourcing & Transitions teams (located in the United States, Canada and Poland) and leading the Indirect Procurement activities with local and Global vendor efforts in India.

This regionally deployed team will help accelerate Pratt & Whitney’s efforts in improving product cost, supplier performance and security capacity to meet its program and customer needs.

Specific responsibilities of position:

In his function the Associate Director will lead teams of Procurement (Direct) for P&WC and Indirect procurement for all PW India Businesses (Commercial Engine; Engineering; Digital and Supply chain)

The Direct & Indirect Procurement Associate Director must demonstrate an advanced understanding of Pratt & Whitney's quality, resourcing and supply chain and Indirect procurement strategy and processes. The Candidate plays a key role in interfacing RTX internal and external customers. This person possesses the appropriate skills to develop and foster positive working relationships while maintaining customer and organizational priorities. The Candidate positions P&W to succeed by developing and executing along with the team and lead the direct and Indirect Procurement activities.

This role will work collaboratively with the following teams:

The Strategic Sourcing team will work closely with their global counterparts and are responsible for all aspects of the commercial, risk mitigation and sourcing strategy for the commodities under his/her leadership The Transitions team will work closely with their global counterparts and are responsible for all aspects of work transfers.

The Manufacturing Engineering team will work closely with their global counterparts and are responsible for all aspects of supplier and/or product development.

Direct Procurement activities:

The Direct Procurement Leader will be responsible for a team responsible for developing and executing procurement activities for P&WC – Supply Chain Management: Material Planning | Indirect Procurement | BPM | Support & Analytics. This team is already existing team size of 170+ People and have been processing operations activities for PW Canada for the past couple of years. Providing the overall strategic direction; and ensure that the customer commitments are met working closely with the Internal and external Client base in Canada and other parts of the world.

Indirect Procurement activities:

  • The Indirect Procurement Leader will be responsible for developing and executing procurement strategies across all indirect categories — including IT, HR, Facilities, Travel, Professional Services, and Capex-related services — to ensure cost optimization, supplier performance, and compliance with company policies.
  • This role requires a strategic business partner who collaborates across functions to drive savings, efficiency, and sustainability in indirect spend management.
  • Strategic Procurement Leadership Develop and implement category management strategies for indirect spend areas aligned with global and regional business goals. Partner with functional leaders (Finance, HR, IT, Admin, Legal, BD and so on) to identify sourcing opportunities and deliver value beyond cost savings. Lead negotiations, contract management, and supplier performanceimprovement initiatives. Drive supplier, sustainability, and digital procurement practices
  • Spend Management & Cost Optimization Manage total indirect procurement spend (typically >100 Cr Annually). Achieve annual cost-reduction and value-creation targets through strategic sourcing, demand management, and supplier collaboration. Benchmark pricing and service levels to industry standards and ensure compliance with audit requirements.
  • Governance, Compliance & Risk Management Ensure all procurement activities comply with corporate governance, legal, and ethical standards. Manage procurement documentation, vendor onboarding, and supplier risk assessments. Collaborate with finance and internal audit to maintain strong process controls and reporting integrity.
  • Supplier & Contract Management Build long-term relationships with key suppliers to ensure continuous improvement, innovation, and reliability. Oversee supplier performance metrics (SLAs, KPIs, service quality, and delivery). Lead periodic vendor business reviews and contract renewals.
  • People Leadership & Collaboration Lead and mentor a team of category managers, buyers, and analysts. Foster collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement. Drive the adoption of digital tools such as SAP Ariba / Coupa / Jaggaer / Oracle Procurement Cloud for automation and analytics.
  • Key Interfaces
  • Internal: Finance, Legal, HR, IT, Digital; Administration, Engineering, and Senior Leadership Teams.
  • External: Vendors, Strategic Suppliers, Consultants, and Industry Networks.
