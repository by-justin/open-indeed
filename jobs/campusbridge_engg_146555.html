About the job

Looking to launch your career at the cutting edge of healthcare? Join Sanofi for a chance to develop with mentoring and guidance from inspirational leaders while helping to make an impact on the lives of countless people worldwide. As Co-Op Student - E&M Reliability & Process Engineering in our E&M Reliability & Methods Department, you will learn to perform and support daily functions. Optimize the Preventive Maintenance (PM) tasks, schedules, & spare parts list (BOM) supporting the Equipment User/Functional Requirement Specifications (U/FRS) and Qualification protocol (IOQ) initiatives, as well as monitor for repetitive equipment failures to eliminate reoccurrence.

We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people's lives. We're also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started?

Main responsibilities:

  • Systematically monitors and refines the appropriate maintenance strategies to optimize the Preventive Maintenance (PM) tasks, schedules, & spare parts list (BOM) supporting the Equipment User/Functional Requirement Specifications (U/FRS) and Qualification protocol (IOQ) initiatives.
  • Assist Reliability Engineers to investigate repetitive failures and applies the appropriate blend of failure management tools to eliminate potential re-occurrences including Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Failure Report Analysis & Corrective Actions (FRACAS).
  • Provides technical support to assist production, maintenance, technical personnel and the Compliance Team (Lead Investigators) supporting Quality Notifications (QN's) and Corrective Actions & Preventive Actions (CAPA's), to improve the Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).
  • Assist Reliability to ensure the equipment performance expectations fulfill the user and functional requirements driving Preventive Maintenance Optimization (PMO) initiatives.
  • Utilize RCM the Reliability-centered Maintenance (RCM) tools to determine the maintenance requirements of the physical asset in its operating context.
  • Participate in Reliability projects including Predictive Maintenance (PdM) strategies and other facility/equipment initiatives. Examples include Vibration, Ultrasonic, and Infrared technologies.
  • Provide support to assist other departments through Reliability Engineering Request forms in initiating and processing PM task and BOM changes, along with assessing the viability of Functionally Equivalent components for replacing obsolete system critical components.
  • Develop Preventative Maintenance (PM) task lists by using Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) and Sanofi Trade Subject Matter Experts' (SMEs) recommendations for newly installed equipment.
  • Obtain Vendor Turnover Packages (VTODs) from OEM containing recommended PM plans, P&IDs, and Bill of Materials/Spare Parts Lists detailing the maintenance requirements of Sanofi assets.
  • Develop and optimize programs using Power Automate and Excel, to assist in streamlining Reliability Engineering initiatives and mitigate incorrect data entry into ERPs.
  • Proficient in use of MS Office 2010, including Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, Word
  • Proficient in use of MS SharePoint, InfoPath Designer, Power Automate (Flow)
  • Other duties as required.
