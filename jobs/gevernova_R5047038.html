Role Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated Lead Manufacturing Specialist to oversee daily plant operations in Gaspé. You will be responsible for planning and executing production activities, ensuring reliability, consistency, and adherence to safety and quality standards. This role involves direct oversight of front-line manufacturing employees and requires a leader capable of driving continuous improvement in a high-paced industrial environment.
