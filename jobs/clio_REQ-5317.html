Job Description

Clio is the global leader in legal AI technology, empowering legal professionals and law firms of every size to work smarter, faster, and more securely.

We are transforming the legal experience for all by bettering the lives of legal professionals while increasing access to justice.

Summary:

We are seeking a Support & Implementation Manager, Cloud Communications to lead our Customer Success VoIP team in Canada or the United States. This team supports and implements the Voice & Messaging pillar of Clio's Unified Communications initiative, bringing voice, messaging, and email into a single, consistent experience across the Clio product suite.

This is a player/coach role: you'll build and lead a high-performing technical team while staying hands-on enough to troubleshoot alongside them, jump into escalations, and set the technical bar. You're a people leader who hasn't lost your technical edge — comfortable coaching a technician through a tricky SIP issue in one meeting and setting quarterly team goals in the next.

What you'll do:

  • Lead, coach, and develop a team of VoIP Support & Implementation Technicians — set goals, run 1:1s, manage performance, and build technical skill on the team
  • Own team capacity and scheduling, including coverage for high-volume peaks and escalation windows
  • Personally handle a portion of complex VoIP/SIP troubleshooting and escalations (registration failures, one-way/no audio, jitter, packet loss, call quality) to stay technically sharp and unblock the team
  • Act as the final escalation point for technical and customer-relationship issues that exceed the team's scope
  • Review and refine call-flow configuration standards: forwarding, auto-attendants/IVR, ring groups, time-of-day routing, voicemail
  • Set provisioning and device management standards for desk phones/softphones, MAC registration, and extension assignment
  • Oversee numbers, porting, and messaging compliance workflows (LOAs, port rejections, 10DLC registration), ensuring quality and turnaround targets are met
  • Drive onboarding/implementation quality for new firms' phone systems, including migrations from prior systems
  • Build and maintain team documentation, knowledge base standards, and training materials
  • Partner cross-functionally with Engineering, Product, and CS leadership on Voice & Messaging pillar initiatives
  • Monitor team metrics (SLA adherence, ticket/case volume, CSAT, escalation rate) and report on team health and initiative progress
  • Hire, onboard, and ramp new team members
  • Lead or contribute to cross-functional VoIP-related projects
  • Other duties as required

What you should have:

  • 3+ years leading a technical support, CS, or implementation team, ideally in telecom, VoIP, MSP, or SaaS
  • Strong people leadership skills: coaching, performance management, feedback delivery, and team development
  • Working knowledge of VoIP/SIP fundamentals: registration, RTP audio path, NAT/firewall traversal, SIP ALG, codecs, call setup — enough to troubleshoot alongside the team and validate their diagnostics
  • Networking literacy: routers, firewalls, QoS, VLANs, WiFi, ISP/modem basics
  • Comfort with PBX/telephony configuration and desk phone/softphone provisioning (Yealink/Polycom)
  • Experience managing to SLAs and ticket/case-based workflows, including prioritization under shifting demand
  • Ability to stay hands-on: comfortable jumping into a live customer troubleshooting call when needed, not just managing from a distance
  • Strong judgment on when to solve personally, delegate, or escalate further
  • Composure and de-escalation skills with frustrated customers and high-pressure situations
  • Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, evolving environment where scope and process will shift
  • Comfortable working in an AI-assisted environment

Serious bonus points if you have:

  • Prior MSP, VoIP, or telecom help-desk background (strong preference with US telecom)
  • Kazoo PBX platform experience
  • Experience or knowledge in legal documentation and legal practice areas
  • 5+ years of experience in a Customer Success, Support or Professional Services environment, specifically in a customer facing role
  • Interest in joining a dynamic, high growth, and fast-moving SaaS company.
