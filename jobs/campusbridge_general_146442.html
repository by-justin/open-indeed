Job Description

Job Summary :

The Nurse Practitioner (NP) plays a vital role in advancing the mission of the Jasper Wellness Place Centre - Community Health Centre by providing high-quality, patient-centred care to individuals and families within the community. Reporting to the Clinical director, the NP collaborates with interdisciplinary teams to deliver comprehensive healthcare services, emphasizing health promotion, disease prevention, and the management of chronic and acute conditions.

Key Responsibilities :

  • Perform comprehensive patient assessments, including physical exams and medical histories.
  • Develop and implement individualized care plans, including prescribing medications, ordering diagnostic tests, and providing treatments within the scope of practice.
  • Provide counseling and education on health maintenance, disease prevention, and lifestyle management.
  • Collaborate with other healthcare professionals to ensure seamless patient care.
  • Facilitate health education workshops and initiatives tailored to the community's needs.
  • Act as a resource for patients and community members regarding health and wellness.
  • Build relationships with community organizations to enhance access to healthcare services.
  • Monitor patient outcomes and adjust care plans to ensure effective management of chronic and acute conditions.
  • Participate in the evaluation and development of clinic policies and procedures to improve service delivery.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date patient records in compliance with regulatory standards.
  • Contribute to regular reports on patient care outcomes, program effectiveness, and community needs.
  • Assist with quality improvement initiatives and adherence to health and safety protocols.

Qualifications :

  • Completion of a recognized Nurse Practitioner Program.
  • Active registration with the College of Registered Nurses of Alberta (CRNA) in the Family/All Ages Stream.
  • Experience in assessment, treatment, therapeutic interventions, and community-based care, especially with mental health and substance use challenges. Certification or relevant training in Opioid Agonist Therapy (OAT) preferred.
  • Proficiency with electronic medical records and related systems, security clearance, and experience with diverse populations.
  • Excellent communication, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong commitment to equity, inclusivity, and culturally sensitive care.

Knowledge and Skills

  • In-depth understanding of community health principles and healthcare systems.
  • Ability to work collaboratively with interdisciplinary teams and community stakeholders.
  • Familiarity with public health initiatives and programs.
  • Empathy: Patience, understanding, and respect for individuals, particularly those facing challenging circumstances.
  • Confidentiality: Commitment to maintaining confidentiality and impartiality.
