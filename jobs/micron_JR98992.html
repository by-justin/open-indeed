Job Overview｜職缺概述

Micron Taiwan is building a long‑term HBM talent pool to support the expansion of advanced memory manufacturing in Taichung.This evergreen posting is intended to attract talents across Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, Automation, Facilities, and Operations Leadership functions.

Micron 台灣持續擴展 HBM（High Bandwidth Memory）製造與營運團隊，此 evergreen 職缺用於建立長期人才庫，將依業務需求媒合合適職務。

Potential Roles｜涵蓋職務方向（不限於）

  • Manufacturing / Process / Test Engineer 製造／製程／測試工程師

  • Automation & Manufacturing Systems Engineer 自動化與製造系統工程師

  • Industrial Engineer / Cost Engineer 工業工程師／成本工程師

  • Product Quality Assurance / Quality Engineering (PQA / PQE) 產品品質保證／品質工程

  • HBM Manufacturing / Test Production Supervisor HBM 製造／測試產線主管

  • Assembly Equipment Engineer / Process Engineer 設備／製程工程師

What You Will Do｜工作內容摘要

Depending on role alignment, responsibilities may include:

  • Support and improve HBM manufacturing, assembly, test, automation, or facility systems 支援並優化 HBM 製造、封裝、測試、自動化或廠務系統
  • Drive process optimization and yield improvement, enhance product quality, and support new product introduction and production ramp 負責分析與優化製造製程，提升產品良率、品質與生產效率，並支援新產品導入與量產穩定性
  • Maintain and optimize manufacturing equipment, improve reliability and productivity, and enable new technology deployment for advanced manufacturing. 責維護與優化製造設備，提升設備穩定度與產能，並導入新技術以支援先進製程發展 Drive improvement in quality, yield, cost, cycle time, equipment or utility reliability 推動品質、良率、成本、交期、設備或公用系統穩定度之改善
  • Support new product introduction (NPI), qualification, and production ramp   支援新產品導入（NPI）、製程／系統驗證及量產爬坡
  • Collaborate with Manufacturing, Engineering, Quality, IE, Facilities, and Global teams 和 製造、工程、品質、IE、廠務及全球團隊跨部門合作
  • Participate in continuous improvement, safety, and operational excellence initiatives 參與持續改善、工安／EHS 及營運卓越相關專案
  • For supervisory roles, manage shift operations, staffing, safety, and daily execution 負責輪班管理、人力配置、工安與每日營運執行
