An overview of this role

As a Commercial Account Executive for Southern Cone, you'll be the primary point of contact for prospective and existing small and medium-sized business and mid-market customers in Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Paraguay and Bolivia. You'll help organizations with up to 1,999 team members modernize how they plan, build, secure, and ship software with GitLab's AI-powered DevSecOps platform. You'll own a broad book of business and act as a trusted advisor as you prospect, run complex sales cycles, guide customers toward clear business outcomes, support adoption, and reduce churn. You'll work closely with Sales Development, Customer Success, Renewals, Partners, Marketing, technical teams, and Sales leadership. You'll use value-based selling and structured methodologies such as MEDDPICC and Command of the Message to build a healthy pipeline, forecast accurately, and support revenue goals across a wide range of deal sizes.

This role will be 100% remote and will be based in Mexico.

What you’ll do

  • Own and manage a broad small and medium-sized business and mid-market book of business in Southern Cone, driving new and expansion opportunities from prospecting through close and renewal.

  • Focus on customer accounts with up to 1,999 team members, navigating a range of project sizes and complexity within the small and medium-sized business and mid-market segments.

  • Articulate the value of GitLab's AI-powered DevSecOps platform, tailoring your approach to each customer's business and technical needs to support clear, outcome-focused decisions.

  • Build and maintain a healthy pipeline through consistent outbound prospecting, qualification, and opportunity advancement, using structured methodologies such as MEDDPICC and Command of the Message.

  • Document buying criteria, decision processes, next steps, and key stakeholders to run disciplined sales cycles and support accurate territory and forecast management.

  • Collaborate with Sales Development, Customer Success, Renewals, Marketing, technical teams, and Partners to coordinate account strategies, co-sell, and deliver a cohesive pre- and post-sales experience.

  • Support customer adoption of GitLab solutions, proactively work to reduce churn and contraction, and participate in quarterly forecasting and territory planning.

  • Share win and loss insights, update the sales handbook, and serve as a clear voice of the customer through GitLab's public issue tracker.

What you’ll bring

  • Fluency in Spanish and experience selling software as a service (SaaS) solutions through value-based conversations with technical and business stakeholders, ideally including development or DevSecOps teams.

  • Experience owning and managing a broad small and medium-sized business and mid-market territory as the primary point of contact, from prospecting through close, adoption, and expansion.

  • Skill in building and maintaining strong customer relationships, including discovery, negotiation, and closing in support of long-term customer outcomes.

  • Comfort driving consistent outbound prospecting, creating and maintaining a healthy pipeline, and keeping territory and forecast documentation accurate.

  • Ability to collaborate with Sales Development, Customer Success, Renewals, Marketing, Partners, and technical teams to coordinate and carry out account strategies.

  • Clear and structured communication skills, including presenting GitLab's DevSecOps value proposition to different audiences and documenting customer insights.

  • Interest in GitLab, open source, and tools that support software development, with the ability to connect customer business challenges to GitLab's AI-powered DevSecOps platform.

  • Willingness to travel to meet customers and attend events as needed. We welcome transferable sales experience from a variety of backgrounds.

About the team

This role sits within GitLab's broader Sales organization, focused on helping small and medium-sized business and mid-market customers adopt and expand their use of our AI-powered DevSecOps platform. You'll work with a distributed group across account management, Sales Development, Customer Success, Renewals, Marketing, Partners, and technical teams. Together, the team collaborates asynchronously across regions to manage a broad book of business and a wide range of opportunity sizes. The team guides customers from initial evaluation through long-term value by developing new business, strengthening customer relationships, and supporting healthy product adoption. You'll use shared playbooks, the sales handbook, and GitLab itself to stay coordinated on territory plans, pipeline health, and best practices.
