Do you thrive in fast-paced environments where your problem-solving skills can shine? If so, this is the perfect opportunity for you!

We are looking for a passionate Data Engineer  with strong eCommerce domain expertise to own and evolve the data infrastructure powering our operations across Latin America. You will design end-to-end pipelines, integrate high-volume platforms and deliver data products that fuel analytics and intelligent commerce experiences.

You will join a renowned company improving lives of billions of consumers. You’ll be welcomed within a large team of Data Engineers with diverse backgrounds and seniorities whereby you will take ownership of eCommerce integration projects supporting Latin American markets.

With us you will:

Build scalable eCommerce data pipelines

  • Design, develop, and operate batch-oriented data pipelines using Azure Databricks, PySpark, Spark SQL, and Delta Lake.
  • Build and publish data products across domains: catalog, pricing, promotions, inventory, orders, payments, fulfillment, and returns.
  • Design incremental processing patterns supporting backfills, late arriving data, and schema evolution.
  • Create reusable pipeline components and ensure products meet performance, quality, and reliability standards.

Integrate eCommerce platforms and APIs

  • Build and maintain integrations with Amazon and other eCommerce, payment, logistics, and enterprise systems.
  • Develop ingestion solutions via REST APIs, webhooks, files, and cloud native interfaces.
  • Handle real-world API challenges: authentication, pagination, rate limits, retries, idempotency, and schema evolution.
  • Build monitoring and recovery mechanisms for critical integrations across Latin American markets.

Engineer for performance and reliability

  • Optimize Spark workloads through partitioning, join strategies, caching, and query-plan analysis.
  • Implement automated data quality controls and observability through metrics, alerting, and lineage.
  • Design pipelines that are scalable, idempotent, fault tolerant, and recoverable.
  • Drive root cause analysis and deliver permanent solutions for production issues.

What we offer you:

  • Relocation support to Panamá.
  • Responsibilities from Day 1, allowing you to take ownership of your projects.
  • A dynamic and supportive work environment that promotes agility and work/life balance.
  • Work in international teams with regional or global responsibilities.
  • A competitive salary and benefits package.
  • Long-term career with development and growth opportunities
  • Continuous mentorship from passionate colleagues and formal training opportunities.

Job Qualifications

  • 3-5 years  of experience in data engineering or software engineering in production environments.
  • Bachelor degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or similar.
  • Proven experience in eCommerce, digital marketplaces, retail, or high-volume transactional platforms.
  • Knowledge of eCommerce data domains: catalog, pricing, inventory, orders, payments, fulfillment, and returns.
  • Direct experience integrating with REST APIs.
  • Deep hands-on experience with  Python, PySpark and SQL  at scale.
  • Experience building production grade pipelines on  Databricks  or equivalent platform.
  • Solid understanding of batch processing, incremental ingestion, data modeling, and data quality practices.
  • Software engineering discipline: version control, automated testing, CI/CD, code reviews, and production monitoring.
  • Professional proficiency in  Spanish and English .

Nice-to-have:

  • Advanced Databricks expertise: Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, Workflows, Auto Loader, MLflow, and workload optimization.
  • Amazon Marketing Cloud experience.
  • Experience operationalizing machine learning or AI models in production.
