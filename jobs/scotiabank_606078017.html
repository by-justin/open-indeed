Purpose The Senior Processing Officer, TCI Processing Unit (PU), is responsible for controlling the operations and processing of physical and electronic clearing items, ensuring accurate and complete processing of salaries and settlement and reconciliation of related general ledger accounts for the host country as outlined in Service Level Agreement. He/she must manage the activities of these units and is directly responsible for signing off the daily Support Package for the department.

The Senior Processing Officer, TCI Processing Unit, must ensure all daily and periodic management controls are in place and effective as outlined in the Processing Support Centre Manual, Scotiaglobe, Aperta Image Proof of deposit System, Scotia Globe Delivery Manual and other applications used in the departments for which he/she is responsible. He/she ensures service provided is constantly of a high quality, the Unit adheres to instructions received by serviced transits, maintains the integrity of the financial records and maximizes the efficiency to contribute to the overall profitability of the Bank.  He/she is responsible for the successful implementation and ongoing sustainability, of all Bank initiatives/enhancements involving these areas.

The Senior Processing Officer, TCI Processing Unit is responsible by providing ongoing monitoring, coaching and performance feedback throughout the year to your subordinates.

The Senior Processing Officer, TCI Processing Unit is responsible for contributing to the provision of human, straightforward and knowledgeable service through your daily interactions to create a relationship of mutual trust and confidence with our branches and other employees.

In addition the Processing Officer is also responsible for providing administrative support to the Assistant Manager, Processing Unit, in the roll out of initiatives / releases and also in overseeing flow of work within the Unit in the Assistant Manager’s absence.

•    You may sign up to $99,999 (USD equiv.) with an appointed officer •    You may sign $100,000* (USD equiv.) and over with another management level officer.

You are responsible for working independently with those authorities granted to you. Complex issues are referred to the   Assistant Manager Customer Transaction Services Cheque Processing. You are required to prioritize and allocate the workload.

You have the authority to authorize transactions up to your Transaction Control System (TCS) limits; this includes transactions processed through IBP or any other applications, if applicable.

•    All transactions must be checked by an authorized officer before processing •    All transactions processed in an ITP Sundry Session or Bulk Session must be supported by an authorized source document that is retained with the posted vouchers.

The Transaction Control System (TCS) is an electronic tool that automates Bank controls over Teller transactions. All Officers have a TCS profile, which identifies transaction limits and systems access. TCS reports all exception conditions generated.  Transactions are authorized at the point of entry into the system prior to posting and will bear the electronic signature of the authorized employee(s). Authorized transactions are the responsibility of both the posting officer and the authorizing officer. Your signature on this document indicates that you acknowledge your understanding of your TCS profile. Any questions or concerns must be immediately addressed to your Supervisor.

You are responsible in ensuring that all operational procedures are followed as documented the Branch Services & Procedures Manual, Processing Support Centre Manual and other Bank operating manuals. You are responsible for being alert to suspicious transactions or circumstances.

You are to refer any transaction, which in your judgment may be a potential fraud/forgery or theft to a higher authority as described in Branch Services & Procedures Manual, Volume II – Section 6.3. When preparing / checking &/or authorizing, you are responsible for:

•    Ensuring each entry is authentic; •    Ensuring entries are correct, balanced and complete in all respects (i.e. all related revenues/expenses have been processed, both debit and credit are authorized at the same time); •    Ensuring all entries are being processed on the date value was given or received; •    Ensuring the disposition of entries is appropriate;
