Job Description

Evertz AV is looking for a Service Engineer to join our AV Service team with a specific focus on Control System customization and deployment with a specific focus on VUE, our software-defined control and monitoring platform. VUE unifies control of MAGNUM-orchestrated broadcast/AV infrastructure — routing, multiviewers, master control, and audio processing — into a single customizable, widget-based interface.  VUE is extensible to third-party equipment through device drivers and direct API integration . You will be responsible for extending VUE's reach into new and existing customer environments by working with consultants, system integrators and/or end users to understand workflows and/or specific UI requirements, create the control surface, test and deploy.  The role requires in-depth knowledge of VUE configuration options including building and modifying device drivers , scripting control and automation where needed and implementing direct API control (REST, SOAP, SNMP, WebSocket, serial protocols, and proprietary vendor SDKs) when a pre-built driver doesn't exist or needs customization.  You will be the technical bridge between customer requirements, third-party device documentation, and Evertz's control ecosystem — playing an integral role system deployment and functionality. What You'll Do - Configuration & Control Design

  • Design, build, and deploy custom VUE widget layouts and workspaces tailored to customer operational workflows
  • Integrate VUE with MAGNUM-OS for router control, multiviewer management, master control/branding, and playout systems
  • Configure alarms, salvos, UMDs, and automation/override widgets to match site-specific operational requirements Device Driver Development
  • Create new device drivers for third-party equipment not natively supported by VUE, based on vendor protocol documentation (serial, IP, etc.)
  • Modify and extend existing Evertz device drivers to support additional functionality, firmware revisions, or non-standard implementations
  • Test, validate, and document driver behavior against real hardware in lab and field environments
  • Work with Evertz product/R&D teams to escalate driver issues, contribute improvements back into the driver library, and stay current on VUE's driver framework as it evolves Third-Party API Integration
  • Evaluate third-party device APIs (REST, SOAP, WebSocket, proprietary SDKs) and determine the best integration path — existing driver, custom driver, or direct API call configuration within VUE
  • Configure VUE to communicate directly with device-native APIs where full driver development isn't required or practical
  • Troubleshoot authentication, polling/subscription behavior, and latency issues between VUE and third-party systems
  • Document integration patterns for reuse across future projects and customers Field & Customer Support
  • Commission VUE-based control systems on customer sites and deliver operational training
  • Respond to customer issues involving control logic, driver behavior, or third-party integration failures; troubleshoot to root cause
  • Partner with sales engineering and product management to scope control/integration requirements during the pre-sales and design phase
  • Author internal documentation (driver specs, integration notes, best practices) for the technical support and engineering teams What You Bring - Required
  • Degree or diploma in Computer Engineering, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline — or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Hands-on programming/scripting ability (e.g., C#, C++, Python, JavaScript, or similar) sufficient to build or modify device drivers from a protocol spec
  • Working knowledge of common device communication protocols: TCP/IP, UDP, serial (RS-232/RS-422/RS-485), SNMP, and RESTful/SOAP web APIs
  • Comfort reading third-party equipment documentation (protocol manuals, SDKs, MIBs, APIs and other standards specifications) and translating it into working control logic
  • Strong troubleshooting instincts — comfortable diagnosing whether an issue lives in the device, the network, the driver, or the VUE configuration
  • Willingness to travel to customer sites for commissioning, training, and escalated support
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to document technical work clearly for both internal teams and customers Strongly Preferred
  • Prior experience programming AV control systems (Crestron SIMPL/SIMPL#, AMX NetLinx, Extron Global Scripter/GUI Designer, Q-SYS)
  • Exposure to Evertz MAGNUM-OS, VistaLINK PRO, or broadcast router control concepts (sources/destinations, salvos, tally/UMD)
  • Experience integrating disparate systems via API (building or consuming REST/SOAP endpoints, handling auth tokens, parsing JSON/XML)
  • Familiarity with SDVN/SMPTE ST 2110, IP-based broadcast infrastructure, or Pro AV over IP environments
  • Working knowledge of Linux Nice to Have
