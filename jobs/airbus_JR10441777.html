Job Description

Job Description:

A vacancy for a  B2 Aircraft Maintenance Technician has arisen within Airbus Defence and Space, AD&S, in Getafe, Madrid. The successful applicant will join AD&S Final Assembly Line team.

Position details:

Getafe area

Double shift_doble turno Spain

RESPONSIBILITIES Working as part of Final Assembly Line FAL A330 MRTT Flight Line Centre - AG2 team, the role shall be:

  • Prepare, perform and certify base and line maintenance works as a B2 certifying staff, working with a very small group of non-licensed technicians, when needed.
  • Aircraft preparation for flight by implementing on A/C the items recorded in the Test Preparation Sheet, TPS, issued by FlightOps organization for every flight.
  • Carry out production works on the aircraft to prepare it for ground test managed by MRTT Design Office and supervised by FAL- Quality as well as the required support during tests execution.
  • Carry out retrofit electrical works and tests on the aircraft in accordance with applicable retrofit service bulletin or applicable Job Cards.
  • Perform aircraft discrepancy fixing, trouble-shooting, handling and servicing.
  • The position is based in Getafe but the candidate must be willing to work abroad at ADS customer’s sites during aircraft delivery, to support ground & flight tests campaigns in other sites.
  • This is a challenging position combining a desirable military background, EASA Part 66 license as well as EMAR/FR 66 and working in an environment which planning charges almost in a daily basis.
  • The candidate shall be willing to obtain National and NATO clearance, HPS Habilitación Personal de Seguridad.

SKILLS We are looking for candidates with the following skills and experience:

  • Aircraft maintenance EASA PART 66 category B2 without limitations.
  • Being in possession of a valid EASA PART 66 category B2 aircraft maintenance license will be recomended .
  • Candidates shall have previous experience in Airbus aircraft maintenance environment working in A330 (RR TRENT 700) and/or (GE CF6).
  • B1 license will be also desirable.
  • English: fluent level
  • Spanish: negotiation level.
  • Candidate MUST have a wide experience in test execution and troubleshoutings to solve problems detected after the test execution.
  • Holding of national and NATO security clearance (SC) will by highly appreciated.
  • The candidates shall have experience of working to high quality standards and have an understanding of work recording and job traceability.  They will have experience in reading and understanding detailed technical data and manufacturer’s maintenance manuals and instructions in English.  A high knowledge of Aircraft Health and Safety procedures and a good standard of computer literacy are required.
