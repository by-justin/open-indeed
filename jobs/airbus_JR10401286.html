Job Description

Job Description:

Within Satair, an Airbus company, we target to serve our customers with spare parts in time. Our logistics is a key contributor to make this happen. And we do so with a great team across functions and around the globe.

We search for a Logistics Process & Performance manager, combining logistics expertise with a digital mindset, with experience in design and implementation of process improvements and transformation in the logistics domain, with E2E “always on”.

We are a team of 10+ responsibles to drive our logistics process design and digital solutions in order to enable our ecosystem to run smoothly and deliver parts to the aviation market right in time. And we have a lot to work on and discover, following our logistics transformation journey where we reshape and modernize the warehouse setups in Copenhagen and globally. Your direct benefits are

  • A team located in Copenhagen, Hamburg and globally connected
  • Colleagues to count on in a fun to work in atmosphere
  • A lot of space to have an impact
  • Chance to further grow logistics competences and to develop yourself
  • Close collaboration across functions in our Airbus Satair company setup

What the job is about:

  • Responsibility for logistics processes in Copenhagen and globally - take ownership for the processes we run and develop them further
  • Drive change and transformation on our core logistics backbone (we will be changing from WM to EWM)
  • Lead the interface to IT to implement changes to our way of working for business continuity
  • Integrate with the functions (customer facing teams, supply chain, quality, product management) for a smooth E2E process design and execution
  • Support operations with expertise, resolutions and training

You will be located in Copenhagen. For this position, there might be a few but limited travels per year in Europe or to one of our warehouses based in the US or in Asia, depending on topic need and considering your interests.

Your profile:

We are looking for a proactive colleague to take ownership of our logistics processes, driving progress and transformation across our global operations. As a key focal point, you will leverage your digital mindset and process-minded approach to lead change, optimizing our core logistics backbone to ensure long-term business continuity.

You possess 3+ years of professional logistics experience (encompassing warehousing, shipping, and transport), and while hands-on experience with SAP WM/EWM is essential, your strength lies in your ability to lead our organisation through process design, Complex Problem Solving and drive the implementations of sustainable process improvements in a multifunctional and global stakeholder environment.

In our international environment, excellent command of written and spoken English is essential to effectively collaborate across diverse teams. Your academic background in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or a related field, combined with a passion for pioneering new ways of working, will enable you to generate real value within the Satair ecosystem.
