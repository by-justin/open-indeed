The Opportunity:

Dive into the fascinating world of petroleum geology with this opportunity! Embark on a journey of discovery as you analyze geological data, interpret subsurface formations, and contribute to challenging exploration or mining projects. If you have a passion for earth sciences, a keen eye for detail, and a desire for knowledge, this opportunity is your gateway to experience in the geology field. Take the first step towards a rewarding career in geology and apply now to be part of our dynamic team!

This opportunity provides a unique chance to gain hands-on experience across exploration or mining geology. Depending on the assignment, you may spend time or working with experienced geologists on exploration and development projects, in the core lab, or at an active mine site. You will analyze formations, interpret geological data, review core and subsurface information, and apply your knowledge to practical geology workflows.

You will work alongside experienced geologists who can mentor you while you gain valuable on-the-job training, including 3D modeling and geological software, mapping, cross-sections, petrophysical log interpretation, facies analysis, field tours, core description, and communicating geological concepts and findings to diverse audiences.

  • Job location: Calgary, AB (Head Office) or Fort McMurray, AB (Horizon or Albian Sands)

  • Shift schedules:

Calgary: 5 days on, 2 days off, 8 hours/day (Monday – Friday) Oilsands: 4 days on, 3 days off, 10 hours/day (Monday – Thursday)

  • Term lengths: 4 or 8 months

  • Term starts: January, May, or September 2027

  • Safety sensitive position: Yes

  • Application deadline: This posting will close once the successful candidate(s) have been selected. We encourage you to apply as soon as possible.

Key Accountabilities:

You will work closely with experienced geologists to gain valuable experience and skills in geology.

  • Work as an integral member of the asset team by preparing key mapping, cross-sections, and interpretations related to current and future development programs

  • Prepare regional interpretations and mapping for prospective geologic formations, high-grade areas with potential for development/optimization

  • Review and describe core from formations of interest to aid in understanding depositional environments and details of individual rock units

  • Contribute to day-to-day area activity by working with the teams in preparing government applications, planning of drilling programs, and evaluation of bypassed pay

  • Develop and strengthen skills in petrophysical log interpretation, facies analysis, geological data acquisition and analysis, mapping, geological software

  • Communicate your findings to the asset team and management in mid-term and final project presentations

Specifically for Mining Assignments

  • Work with the production geology and hydrogeology team to support active mining operations

  • Gain field experience validating geological models and reviewing mine development

  • Participate in core drilling, core description, and deposit characterization activities

  • Develop skills in McMurray stratigraphy, geophysical log interpretation, depositional environments, and sampling techniques

  • Build technical expertise in mine planning, reserves reconciliation, mining operations, geotechnical, and hydrogeological studies

What You Bring to The Role:

  • Currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree program in Geology; recent new graduates of an accredited institution may also be considered

  • Required to be physically and mentally fit for work

  • Motivated self-starter who can thrive as an independent worker and in team settings

  • A sense of ownership and responsibility for project outcomes, and the ability to work effectively in collaborative, interdisciplinary teams

Specifically for Mining Assignments

  • A pre-employment drug screening test must be successfully completed, where required by the assignment

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.
