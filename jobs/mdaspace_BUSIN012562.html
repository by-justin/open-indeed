Job Description

ABOUT 49North

49North is a Canadian defence company delivering secure, multi-domain C4ISR capabilities that provide decision advantage in complex and contested environments. A wholly-owned subsidiary of MDA Space, building on more than five decades of trusted Canadian defence delivery, 49North operates as a sovereign Canadian provider of mission-critical C4ISR systems, bringing disciplined prime-contractor execution to defence and security programs.

Visit 49North | An MDA Space Company.

49North is rooted in the execution of large, complex, and mission-critical programs developed within 49NORTH. This legacy reflects a record of delivery where engineering rigour, operational reliability, and national trust are non-negotiable.

49North Defence strengthens Canada’s sovereign defence capability by delivering integrated mission systems and multi-domain C4ISR integration, enabling trusted Canadian and coalition operations, assured interoperability, and sustained readiness.

Key Responsibilities:

Pipeline & Lead Management

  • Qualify inbound leads and assign them to appropriate account executives

  • Conduct initial outreach (email, phone, LinkedIn) to schedule discovery calls

  • Maintain the CRM (e.g., Salesforce) – update lead status, log activities, attach relevant documents, and ensure data cleanliness

  • Generate weekly pipeline reports and dashboard updates for the BD Director

Proposal & Quote Support

  • Gather pricing, product, and service information from cross‑functional teams

  • Compile and format proposals, RFP responses, and quotes using company templates

  • Track proposal deadlines and follow up with stakeholders to secure approvals

  • Log all submitted proposals in the CRM and monitor their progress

Market & Competitive Research

  • Conduct desk research on target industries, key prospects, and emerging market trends

  • Maintain a repository of competitor intelligence, pricing models, and product positioning

  • Summarize findings into concise briefing notes for the BD team

Event & Campaign Coordination

  • Plan and execute logistics for webinars, trade shows, networking events, and client visits

  • Coordinate promotional materials, booth design, and lead‑capture tools

  • Track event ROI and feed qualified leads back into the pipeline

Administrative & Reporting

  • Prepare weekly status reports, sales forecasts, and KPI dashboards for senior leadership

  • Process expense reports, purchase orders, and other financial documentation related to BD activities

  • Schedule internal meetings, track action items, and ensure follow‑through

Continuous Improvement

  • Identify bottlenecks in the sales process and recommend workflow enhancements

  • Participate in regular training sessions on product knowledge, sales methodology, and CRM usage

  • Contribute ideas for new business development tactics, tools, and best practices

Required Qualifications:

  • 3-5 years experience in sales support, communications and marketing.  Experience with government procurement is an asset

  • Degree in Science, Business, Marketing or Communications or equivalent experience

  • Proficiency in CRM platforms (e.g. Sales Force) and Microsoft Office

  • Excellent written and verbal communication with proven ability to draft clear, persuasive emails and presentation decks

  • Strong organizational and multi-tasking capability with attention to detail and ability to meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced, constantly evolving environment

  • Ability to interpret data, generate insights and present findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

  • Team Player with collaborative mindset and a service-oriented attitude toward both internal colleagues and external prospects

We’re a dream team of purpose-driven, collaborative and passionate people, and we are constantly looking for others to join 49North to ignite new and innovative approaches to problem-solving that push us forward.
