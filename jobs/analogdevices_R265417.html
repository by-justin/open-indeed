Developer & Partner Marketing Communications Specialist

Location:  Limerick, Ireland

Travel:  Yes (regional and occasional international) Reports to:  Director of Developer Relations, Director of Partner Programs

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI ) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, and software technologies into solutions that help drive advancements in digitized factories, mobility, and digital healthcare, combat climate change, and reliably connect humans and the world. Analog Devices continues to grow the software, systems, and solutions capabilities to support customer projects at scale. The Developer Relations function is a key driver of go to market awareness, product attach, and deep, lasting engagement with ADI. We’re‑ seeking a Developer Community Organizer (IC)  who will activate and amplify our developer & partner ecosystem through high impact community programmes - online and in person.

This role plays a central part in ADI’s ongoing pivot to a developer-first narrative, turning visibility and engagement into measurable outcomes for our business and ecosystem.

Role Summary

This is an individual contributor (IC) role focused on marketing execution at the intersection of Developer Relations and Partner Programs . The role owns the planning, delivery, optimisation, and measurement of developer and partner facing marketing programs, ensuring consistent messaging, strong co‑marketing outcomes, and effective conversion from awareness to action across ADI platforms. The role is accountable for high‑impact marketing delivery that supports developer adoption and partner amplification at scale.

Core Responsibilities

The DevRel & Partner Marketing Manager is responsible for aligning ADI’s narrative across developers and partners and executing marketing programs that drive reach, relevance, and conversion.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Own the end‑to‑end execution of marketing campaigns supporting Developer Relations and strategic partnerships, from campaign planning through delivery, optimisation, and reporting.
  • Align the external narrative between ADI, developers, and partners - ensuring clear, consistent storytelling that reinforces ADI’s value proposition and technical leadership.
  • Act as the marketing execution counterpart to DevRel and Partner teams, translating strategy into concrete programs, timelines, and assets.

Shared Responsibilities (DevRel & Partnerships):

  • Messaging & Story Alignment - Ensure external developers and partners are aligned to a shared story, positioning, and messaging framework that supports ADI’s goals.
  • Co‑Marketing Execution - Execute co‑marketing initiatives with partners in line with ADI brand, legal, and communications guidance - covering content, campaigns, launches, and promotions.
  • Web & Content Coordination - Own marketing‑driven web content execution: what content goes where, through which channels, and by when - ensuring consistency across developer.analog.com and partner‑facing surfaces.
  • Program Reach & Impact - Grow the reach and effectiveness of programs within remit through channel optimisation, campaign iteration, and performance‑driven decision making.

Partnerships‑Specific Responsibilities

  • Ensure all partner‑facing marketing content is co‑branded, compliant, and on‑message , working closely with legal, brand, and communications teams.
  • Coordinate partner marketing deliverables including landing pages, joint announcements, campaign assets, and supporting content.
  • Track and report on partner marketing performance, providing insight into reach, engagement, and downstream impact.

DevRel‑Specific Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver targeted developer marketing campaigns designed to attract developers to the ADI Developer Portal and related assets.
  • Oversee content distribution across key developer channels including social media, newsletters, blogs, and third‑party developer platforms.
  • Optimise the developer conversion funnel —from awareness to sign‑up, SDK download, repository access, or other defined activation points.
  • Partner closely with Developer Advocates and engineering teams to translate technical assets into compelling, marketing‑ready materials.

Outcomes & KPIs

  • Define, track, and report on marketing KPIs including reach, engagement, conversion, and program effectiveness.
  • Continuously optimise campaigns and content based on data, feedback, and performance insights.
  • Demonstrate clear contribution to developer adoption, partner amplification, and overall DevRel business impact.

Role & Responsibility (ADI Standard)

  • Lead by influence across Marketing, Developer Relations, Product, and Partner teams to deliver aligned, high‑quality marketing programs.
  • Demonstrate strong marketing execution capabilities in technical or developer‑adjacent domains.
  • Bring structure, clarity, and operational rigor to marketing execution in a fast‑moving, cross‑functional environment.
  • Demonstrate excellent communication and presentation skills; strong analytical problem solving; competitive drive; initiative; relationship‑building; and cross‑functional collaboration.

Requirements

  • Location:  EU (preferably Ireland)
  • Proven experience in B2B, developer, platform, or ecosystem marketing , with a strong focus on execution and measurable outcomes.
  • Demonstrated success running multi‑channel marketing campaigns, including web, content, and digital distribution.
  • Experience working with external partners, agencies, or vendors on co‑marketing initiatives.
  • Strong communication, collaboration, and stakeholder‑management skills.
  • Analytical mindset with comfort interpreting performance data and optimising campaigns accordingly.
  • Education:  3rd level Bachelor’s Degree (or equivalent experience); technical background preferred.
  • Travel:  Yes (as required for partner and marketing activities).
