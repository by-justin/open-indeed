Warehouse Associate, Service Distribution

Description

As a Warehouse Associate, you will support the Product Distribution Warehouse within the Service Operations. This individual will be held responsible for understanding and adhering to all warehouse processes, inventory accuracy improvements, and general operational checks and balances of our global parts distribution operations. If you have a strong work ethic, are reliable and value teamwork in a safety-focused environment we want to hear from you. Tesla will provide forklift training &amp; certification.&nbsp;&nbsp;

Responsibilities

<ul class="tds-list tds-list--unordered"><li>Accurately pick, scan, label, pack, audit, palletize, wrap, strap, and prepare outgoing materials for shipment using various methods and equipment to ensure secure and efficient transport</li><li>Assemble crates/containers and stage materials for loading onto delivery vehicles, trailers, conveyor systems, or other transport methods</li><li>Complete and process required documentation, including Inter-company Bills of Lading, to support compliant global parts distribution</li><li>Utilize warehouse management systems to input, retrieve, update, and maintain real-time inventory accuracy and parts data</li><li>Receive, stock, organize, and maintain inventory in correct bin locations while contributing to ongoing accuracy improvements and 5S standards</li><li>Perform general housekeeping to keep the work area clean, safe, and organized in alignment with Tesla safety and operational protocols</li><li>Safely operate material handling equipment (forklifts with provided training/certification, tuggers, pallet jacks, flatbed carts, etc.) to load/unload, move, and transport materials between warehouse areas, production floors, and vehicles while strictly following all safety guidelines</li></ul>

Maintains exemplary attendance and punctuality to scheduled shifts</li></ul><p> Essential Physical Demands:&nbsp;&nbsp;<br></p><ul class="tds-list tds-list--unordered"><li>Ability to perform physically demanding work for extended periods of time, up to 12 hours/day&nbsp;</li><li>Frequently and repetitively, lift, push and carry up to 50 lbs. &nbsp;<br></li><li>The ability to carry 20 lbs. up and down stairs &nbsp;<br></li><li>Frequently and repetitively, bend, lift and reach to install vehicle parts of varying size and weight overhead, accurately and in allotted timeframes  &nbsp;<br></li><li>Ability to stand and walk for up to 12 hours/day, including over varied and uneven terrain  &nbsp;<br></li><li>Stoop, lay, bend, reach, squat, kneel, crouch, twist and crawl for extended periods of time, including up to 12 hours/day &nbsp;<br></li><li>Climb and maintain balance on ladders, scaffolding or other high structures &nbsp;<br></li><li>Ability to find issues in a work process and receive safety signals using sight, touch, and hearing &nbsp;<br></li><li>Demonstrated ability to handle and manipulate tooling and mechanically fasten bolts within required cycle time, quickly and accurately during new hire training and maintaining ability throughout employment &nbsp;<br></li><li>Exposures to hazardous materials used in the painting process and ability to follow waste handling/disposal procedures &nbsp;<br></li><li>Wearing Personal Protective Equipment, including but not limited to safety glasses, safety shoes, bump caps, hearing protection, full-face respirators, gloves, and adhering to prescribed safety rules and guidelines</li></ul>
