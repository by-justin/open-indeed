Job Description:

AIRBUS Engineering Center in Bengaluru has over 500 engineers working across both fixed and rotary-wing aircraft programmes for Airbus (the A320, A330, A350 XWB and A380 airliners, and helicopters). It has strong capabilities in structural analysis, loads, aerodynamics, avionics software design and testing, system simulation, digital mock-up and system installation. Our cutting-edge products and services – which span the commercial aircraft, helicopter, defense, security and space segments – benefit from our wide-ranging expertise and continued emphasis on innovation. We are now ushering in the next wave of change and innovation for the aerospace industry and we are looking for bright minds to join us in this journey. If you are an innovator, if you are driven by challenges, and if you share our passion to build the best aircrafts, here is an opportunity that you don’t want to miss.

JOB DESCRIPTION Electrical System Engineer Job Code: Electrical System Engineer

As an Electrical System Architecture Engineer, you will be responsible for a host of activities like electrical load analysis, configuration management, defining /reviewing/analyzing electrical architecture. Your contribution will span through the entire aircraft life cycle starting from early development stages to post delivery of aircrafts. You will also be contributing to the state-of-the-art initiatives driven globally by Airbus.

Key Responsibilities: ● Delivery of Electrical load analysis for all Airbus Commercial aircrafts on Time, Cost and Quality ● Support on Electrical architectural studies ● Reviewing/analyzing the electrical load list ● Analysis of loads at various levels (busbar level, TRU level, generator level) and overload analysis ● Support (and develop to support) detailed design and analysis reports including, o Load flow analysis o Voltage drop analysis o Protection selection o Inservice issues ● Creation of ELA document and ELA Report ● Analyze and reply various Airline Electrical load analysis requests ● Allocation of load and creation of functional diagram ● Electrical structure network[ESN] analysis ● Feasibility studies for new modification proposals on electrical systems ● Define and perform trade off studies for various programmes ● Creation and maintenance of aircraft fleet allocation and configuration management

Allied Activities: ● Initiate optimization studies and propose innovative solutions. ● Create collaboration topics to improve the process, methods and tools

EDUCATION:

  • B.Tech. / B.E. / M.Tech. / M.E. in Electrical / Aerospace Engineering.

REQUIRED TECHNICAL EXPERTISE:

  • General awareness of aircraft and its systems
  • Good understanding of engineering fundamentals and use of first principles.
  • Good with mathematics and statistical analysis.
  • Exposure to Aircraft Electrical System
  • Good understanding of Configuration Management and Change process.

ADDITIONAL DESIRED SKILLS:

  • Experience with systems engineering: defining requirements, experimental verification, and qualification procedures
  • Experience in writing/interpreting coding languages (MATLAB, C++, Python, JavaScript) would be advantageous
  • Proficient understanding and application of MATLAB and Simulink
  • Experience with model in the loop, hardware in the loop, and software in the loop simulations
  • Basics of data analytics and data science

EXPERIENCE:

  • Training sessions will be conducted on different aspects of operations as needed

SOFT SKILLS:

  • Assertive communication and interpersonal skills

  • Ability to work in multiple projects and take innovation initiatives
  • Strong problem solving and logical thinking ability
