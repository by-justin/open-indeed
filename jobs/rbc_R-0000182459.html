Job Description

Job Description

What is the opportunity?

Build scalable trade data platforms. This role is part of RBC Capital Markets Data Strategy & Execution and is responsible for delivering trusted, governed, AI-ready data capabilities.

The Trade Data Engineer will define strategy, lead execution, partner across business and technology, and deliver measurable business value through modern data capabilities.

What will you do?

  • Develop domain strategy aligned with Capital Markets objectives.
  • Partner with business, technology, operations, risk and finance.
  • Define target-state architecture and roadmap.
  • Lead strategic transformation initiatives.
  • Drive data quality, controls and governance.
  • Establish business KPIs and value realization metrics.
  • Manage stakeholder communications up to executive level.
  • Champion cloud, AI and automation.
  • Develop reusable data products and APIs.
  • Mentor teams and foster innovation.
  • Ensure regulatory compliance and audit readiness.
  • Support investment planning and prioritization.

What do you need to succeed?

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Finance or related field.
  • 10+ years of relevant Capital Markets experience.
  • Strong communication and executive presentation skills.
  • Experience delivering enterprise-scale transformation.
  • Ability to lead cross-functional global teams.

Must-Have Skills

  • Deep Capital Markets domain expertise.
  • Proven leadership in enterprise data strategy, governance or engineering.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Graduate degree and experience with Databricks, Snowflake, Collibra, cloud platforms and AI-enabled data management.

Technical Skills

  • SQL/Python
  • Cloud Data Platforms
  • Data Modeling
  • Metadata & Lineage
  • API Integration

Leadership Competencies

  • Strategic thinking
  • Executive communication
  • Influencing without authority
  • Talent development
  • Outcome-driven execution

Success Metrics / KPIs

  • Data quality improvement
  • Reduction in manual processes
  • Delivery against roadmap
  • Stakeholder satisfaction
  • Business value realized

Key Stakeholders

Front Office, COO, Operations, Technology, Risk, Finance, Compliance, Architecture, Data Governance, AI teams.

Job Skills

Adaptability, Critical Thinking, Customer Service, Detail-Oriented, Financial Engineering, Financial Statement Analysis, Investments Analysis, Personal Development, Personal Initiative, Teamwork
