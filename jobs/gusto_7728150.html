About the Opportunity

Brand design is how Gusto earns trust, builds recognition and insight, and makes people feel something before they ever read a word. Our Brand is about how we show up in every moment and this team owns some of its most important attributes from how we use language to typography, from color and composition to illustration, motion.These aren't finishing touches. They're critical to how we show who we are and why we're different.

Gusto is expanding our in-house creative studio inside a fast-moving, late-stage startup. We launch products, educate customers, run brand campaigns, and show up at events—and we do all of it with a consistent visual language and voice that needs to work across digital channels, motion formats, physical environments and everything in between. In the same week, you might be designing the visual system for a product launch and art directing an illustration library that will scale across the entire brand.

We're looking for a Brand & Motion Designer who is a hands-on maker: someone who loves the craft of visual design and motion, takes real ownership of creative output, and wants to help define how Gusto looks, feels, and moves across our brand. This is a senior individual contributor role focused on brand craft, motion design systems, and the variety of deeply considered visual work that makes a B2B brand feel genuinely human.

In this role, you'll own creative work from early concept through final delivery. You'll make judgment calls about typography, composition, color, and motion, and increasingly, that judgment extends to the AI-assisted work happening around you. You'll know when a generated output is right for the brand and when it isn't, and you'll be able to say why. You'll help introduce motion as a foundational part of how we build brand as someone who thinks in motion and ensures it's part of the creative process from the start.

AI is part of how this team works. We use it to advance faster, explore more territory, and reduce the distance between a concept and something real. What we're looking for isn't just someone who uses these tools but someone who can direct them. Someone who sets the creative standard for what AI output is and isn't acceptable for the Gusto brand, and who helps us build the guardrails and workflows that let the whole team produce at a higher level.

If you're energized by being close to the work, care deeply about craft & preference, and enjoy improving both the output and the process, this role will be a impressive fit.

Here's what you'll do day-to-day

  • Design and execute visual and motion work across product launches, product education, brand campaigns, events, and digital channels

  • Design and evolve brand systems, including motion guidelines, templates, and guardrails, that help the broader organization produce on-brand work consistently

  • Integrate motion into the brand design process, establishing principles and patterns for animation that extend our visual identity into dynamic and digital formats

  • Direct and evaluate AI-generated creative work, setting the standard for what's on-brand, what needs to be pushed further, and what shouldn't ship

  • Use AI tools to compress the distance between concept and execution, exploring more territory, iterating faster, and raising the quality bar on every deliverable

  • Partner closely with other creatives and cross-functional partners across marketing, product, and growth from early concept through final delivery

  • Build repeatable templates and workflows that enable faster turnaround without sacrificing quality

  • Maintain brand integrity across a wide range of deliverables — including work where AI tools played a role in production — ensuring everything feels cohesive, human, and unmistakably Gusto

Here's what we're looking for

  • 6+ years of experience in brand design, visual design and motion for in-house studios, agencies, or content teams

  • A portfolio that demonstrates robust design skills and creative thinking is required. It should include brand identity, product marketing, campaigns, and digital content. Please provide examples that explain how AI was part of your work and the decisions you made.

  • Robust design instincts across typography, color, composition, and motion direction—you know what makes work feel distinctive versus generic

  • Expert proficiency in motion design principles and tools—Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud and motion tools like Calvary, Rive and Lottie

  • A demonstrated point of view on AI in creative work — how to direct it, when to override it, and what it means to maintain a high creative standard when AI tools are part of the process

  • Comfort migrating between big, brand-defining work and fast-turn executional needs

  • Proficient collaboration skills across creative, production, marketing, and product partners

  • A maker mindset—you enjoy improving how work gets made, not just making the work itself

  • Experience developing or contributing to creative standards for AI-assisted production — guardrails, evaluation criteria, or workflows that help a team produce at a higher level with these tools.
