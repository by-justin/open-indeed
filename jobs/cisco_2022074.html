Job Description

Meet the Team

You will join Cisco's fast-paced and growing Wireless LAN business unit in India, where the work environment has a startup-like culture. You will be part of a dynamic team responsible for the Cisco Enterprise Wireless Access Point product line. The team works with local and remote engineering, product, and customer-facing teams to validate product quality and stability from a system perspective that reflects real customer environments.

Your Impact

•  Design and develop tools for system-level testing of Cisco Enterprise Wireless Access Point products, validating functionality, quality, and stability in customer-like environments.

•  Plan and execute functional, system, manual, and automated testing for Access Point software features and modules.

•  Review Product Requirements Documents, SW Functional and Design Specifications, and other relevant documentation to extract test requirements and identify testability needs.

•  Provide test design and execution input, and build, maintain, and expand test automation to deliver sufficient coverage across ongoing software releases.

•  Investigate customer-found Access Point issues by designing reproduction scenarios, analyzing failures, validating fixes, and incorporating the learnings into regression coverage and test strategy.

•  Technically lead and guide junior engineers through test planning, automation, debugging, execution, and technical reviews.

•  Collaborate with local and remote teams to drive defects, dependencies, and open issues to closure while communicating risks and status clearly.

•  Advise management on the overall test progress, quality status, readiness, and release risks for assigned projects.

•  Increase test efficiency and effectiveness by applying appropriate test approaches, technologies, tools, and innovations based on product and customer needs.

•  Help the test team expand its wireless networking and test technology knowledge by keeping abreast of the latest developments and systematically archiving relevant documentation and equipment information.

Minimum Qualifications

•  Typically requires an MSEE or MS in Computer Science with 10+ years of related experience, or a BSEE or BS in Computer Science with 12+ years of related experience.

•  Extensive experience with Wireless LAN technologies, Access Point software, and Enterprise Wireless products.

•  End-to-end wireless domain knowledge, including the ability to design and troubleshoot Access Point deployments in medium-to-large customer-like environments.

•  Strong communication and presentation skills, assertiveness, and the ability to take discussions and open issues to closure with local and remote teams.

•  Very strong problem-solving skills.

•  Ability to exercise judgment within defined procedures to determine appropriate action.

•  Ability to interact cross-functionally and drive issues to the desired solution for customers.

•  Ability to work under minimal supervision and with very general instructions.

•  Strong scripting knowledge in Python, with experience understanding and coding programmability transports using native and OpenConfig models.

Preferred Qualifications

•  Self-motivated, with a strong sense of ownership and the ability to work effectively as part of a team.

•  Ability to handle multiple tasks simultaneously and work independently with minimal managerial oversight.

•  Experience supporting or working with Access Points in customer deployments.

•  Openness to continuous learning and commitment to ongoing self-improvement.

•  Experience initiating or participating in organizational efforts to improve group productivity and test effectiveness, and contributing to overall test strategies.
