Interested in launching an exciting engineering career by joining a world class manufacturing site of a leading global company, home of iconic brands like Ariel, Pampers, Gillette, Pantene, Always, Lenor or Oral-B? .

📝 YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:

Internship/Traineeship is the opportunity to be on the production floor meeting different challenges. It's up to you to make decisions and the results of those decisions through. Discovering our leading technologies which are world-renowned and also the P&G standards and methods. In the end, you will build your own capabilities through the job experience, mentoring and training. Internship/traineeship takes place in the 2 following areas:

Production - Packing Here you would get our products into their final packaging and set up in pallets. Your mission will be to create studies of performance loss (on equipment, skills, organization), identifying one or more key projects to achieve the performance targets of the packing lines, establishing methods for solving technical problems, maintenance systematization, implementation of production management tools to the production and work on the installation and startups of new equipments.

Production - Making This is the department that produces our products based on the recipes we get from Research & Development. Your mission will be to make the study and analysis of performance loss (equipment, organization, skills), ensuring smooth communication between different departments (logistics, packing, projects etc.), crafting learning programs for operators and team leader to help them to develop their skills, establishing systematization tools, simplifying process and work standardizing.

This role is an excellent and unique opportunity to learn real hand-on experiences you will not get from school. You’ll expand your knowledge based on personal experiences with industry experts and you'll work on the latest high-tech control and manufacturing technology. All of that supported by great managers who will invest in your development as part a multifunctional team in which trust, team spirit, accountability and respect are very important.

🌏 About P&G RAKONA

Rakona plant is located in the heart of the city Rakovník. Its magnificent history begun 150 years ago when František Otta, local soap manufacturer, founded the plant as the facility for his growing business. After the second world war the plant was nationalized and after the Velvet revolution it become part of the Procter & Gamble as first international acquisition in the Czech history. Nowadays Rakona has 3 main operation areas (Dry Laundry operation, Fabric Enhancers operation and Hand Dish Wash operation). Its modern industry 4.0 production, which was recognized by World Economy Forum as a Lighthouse of the Industry 4.0. We produce globally recognized brands and we grow the best business leaders in the industry. We serve consumers around the world with one of the strongest portfolios of trusted, quality, leadership brands, including Fairy®, Ariel®, Jar®, Lenor® and more.
