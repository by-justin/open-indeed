Job Description

About the Team

The ad sales team is responsible for the advertising vision, development and growth of our largest advertising partnerships. This team is seeking additional team members to build an industry-leading ads and promotions business across DoorDash’s core category, enterprise restaurant brands. The growing DoorDash ads business will quickly become the single best place for merchants and brands to market to local customers, in an ecosystem that benefits all parties.

You’ll leverage AI-powered insights, data-driven signals, and emerging tools to shape strategy, inform decision-making, identify opportunities, and drive growth across complex, enterprise-level engagements. By integrating AI and data-enabled analysis into day-to-day workflows, you’ll improve prioritization, execution, and business impact while partnering cross-functionally to translate evolving platform and AI capabilities into clear, tangible value for partners and the business.

About the Role

The Account Manager, Enterprise Ad Sales role will steer the company's efforts in adopting and partnering with major Restaurants across our strategic portfolio of merchants. You will build partnerships that create tangible value for both DoorDash and advertisers. In this role, you will manage the ads & promos strategy across a focused portfolio of accounts, and drive adoption of our growing ads and promotions offering with a keen focus on intra-quarter upsells and optimization of campaigns. You will also work cross-functionally with marketing, analytics, and product to innovate on our future approach and solutions. You will report into the Head of Ad Sales in our Enterprise Sales & Business Development organization.

You’re excited about this opportunity because you will…

  • Be part of the growing DoorDash Ads & Promos team

  • Facilitate end-to-end marketing conversations across a portfolio of restaurant advertisers with an emphasis on optimizations and upsells

  • Oversee the tactical execution with our largest partners

  • Steer the development, adoption and scaling of Restaurant ads & promos products

  • Build a marketing calendar with restaurant partners in your book of business

  • Develop long-term business plans alongside your Account Executive and our partners for their digital growth strategies

  • Create marketing campaigns; own post-campaign reporting

  • Optimize and upsell based on performance against advertiser KPIs

  • Work with partners across DoorDash to align companywide resources to help build-out our partners' digital businesses

We’re excited about you because…

  • You are a detail oriented account manager with 6+ years of experience in digital media

  • Impact Motivated -- You have a natural desire to improve the overall business

  • Curious -- You love digging into low levels of detail to extract insights

  • Strategic -- You love to find the win-win in novel agreement structures to grow business with partners

  • Diverse -- You are happy juggling many priorities concurrently

  • Bachelor's degree required, MBA or Masters Degree a plus

  • Business Development and Partnerships experience

  • Experience with Analytics and Presentation tools

  • You have proficiency in analytics and visualization tools
