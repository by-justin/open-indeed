Real Estate EHS Professional: Construction & Contractor Management, Americas

At Siemens Healthineers, we pioneer breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.

The Real Estate EHS Professional (EHS) supports Siemens Healthineers Real Estate by ensuring compliance with applicable Environmental, Health & Safety (EHS) regulations, corporate standards, and internal directives across construction projects, facility management, and contractor-managed operations throughout the United States.

This role serves as a trusted advisor to Real Estate Project Managers, Facility Managers, contractors, and site leadership, helping drive a proactive safety culture while minimizing risk and ensuring regulatory compliance. The ideal candidate has strong experience supporting construction and facilities environments, contractor safety management, and EHS program implementation.

Key Responsibilities

EHS Compliance & Regulatory Oversight

  • Provide technical EHS guidance to Real Estate, Facilities, and project teams to ensure compliance with applicable federal, state, and local environmental, health, and safety regulations.
  • Interpret and communicate OSHA, EPA, and company EHS requirements to project teams, contractors, and facility leadership.
  • Maintain current knowledge of evolving EHS regulations and ensure alignment with Siemens Healthineers standards and directives.
  • Support regulatory compliance across construction projects, facility operations, and contractor-managed work.

Construction & Project Support

  • Partner with Real Estate Project Managers and site teams to integrate EHS requirements into construction and facilities-related projects.
  • Review project-specific EHS plans to ensure compliance with company standards, contractual requirements, and applicable regulations.
  • Assist with planning, implementing, and monitoring EHS requirements throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Support implementation and continuous improvement of EHS programs across Real Estate locations.

Contractor Safety Management

  • Support contractor prequalification, onboarding, oversight, and project closeout activities.
  • Monitor contractor coordination on active project sites and identify hazards associated with contractor interactions and multi-employer worksites.
  • Conduct site inspections, audits, and assessments to evaluate contractor compliance and safety performance.
  • Document findings and collaborate with project teams to ensure timely corrective and preventive actions are implemented.

Risk Management & Incident Prevention

  • Identify workplace hazards and recommend appropriate engineering controls, administrative controls, and personal protective measures.
  • Partner with Project and Facility Managers to develop incident prevention strategies and improve workplace safety.
  • Support incident investigations, root cause analyses, corrective action planning, and lessons learned.
  • Ensure incident reporting complies with OSHA recordkeeping requirements where applicable.

Environmental Compliance

  • Support compliance with environmental regulations related to waste management, hazardous materials, spill prevention, and environmental protection.
  • Ensure environmental considerations are incorporated into construction activities and facility operations.
  • Assist with environmental risk assessments and mitigation strategies.

Reporting, Training & Continuous Improvement

  • Prepare and support internal and external EHS reporting requirements.
  • Analyze EHS performance data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Deliver EHS training and awareness programs for employees, contractors, and project teams.
  • Promote a strong safety culture through coaching, communication, and engagement across Real Estate operations.

Required Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Environmental Health & Safety, Occupational Safety, Engineering, or a related field preferred.
  • Equivalent combination of education, professional certification, and relevant experience will be considered.

Experience

  • Minimum of 3 years of Environmental, Health & Safety experience supporting construction, facility management, contractor-managed work, or capital projects in a regulated environment.
  • Working knowledge of OSHA regulations (29 CFR 1910 and 1926), EPA regulations, and construction safety practices.
  • Experience conducting site inspections, audits, hazard assessments, and incident investigations.
  • Ability to provide technical EHS guidance across multiple project teams and stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Five or more years of EHS experience supporting construction, facilities, real estate, manufacturing, laboratory, healthcare, or other regulated environments.
  • Demonstrated experience managing contractors, including prequalification, onboarding, field oversight, safety documentation review, and corrective action follow-up.
  • Experience supporting occupied healthcare, laboratory, manufacturing, or similar operational facilities.
  • Professional certifications such as OSHA 30, CHST, ASP, CSP, or equivalent construction safety certification.
  • Strong analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills with the ability to influence a positive safety culture.

Additional Information

  • Travel to construction and facility sites throughout the United States is required.
  • Ability to provide technical guidance and oversight across multiple locations and concurrent projects.
  • Experience supporting construction, facilities, or corporate real estate operations is strongly preferred.
