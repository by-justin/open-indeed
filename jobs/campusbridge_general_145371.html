About GAHL

GAHL is a leading global, consumer-facing automotive group, operating in eight countries, including key markets across North America, Europe, and the UK. With over C$11 billion in annual revenue, GAHL ranks among the largest automotive distributors worldwide and one of the largest privately held companies in North America.

From our origins in 2014 starting with zero revenue, we have grown into a global powerhouse through an ambitious, acquisition-driven strategy. Our strategic headquarters in Toronto, Canada, London, UK and Copenhagen, Denmark facilitate our international presence and growth.

At GAHL, our culture is defined by growth, meritocracy, accountability, and urgency. We move fast, think strategically, and invest in people who are driven to make an impact.

We are now seeking the next generation of global leaders to join our prestigious Global Management Training (GMT) Program. This is a unique opportunity to fast-track exceptional talent into high-impact leadership roles across our platforms.

About the Program

The Corporate Development, Finance & Data Analytics - GMT Program is designed for high-potential graduates interested in strategy, finance, and analytics. Over 18 months, participants rotate across multiple regions and corporate functions, working on high-impact initiatives in acquisitions, operations optimization and research, and corporate strategy.

This stream is ideal for graduates who want to develop expertise in data analytics, financial modeling, corporate development, treasury, and strategic planning within a fast-paced, global organization.

Program Structure

Rotations : 3 rotations (6 months each) across North America, UK, and Denmark.

Training : 2 months of structured training and coaching at the start of each rotation.

Special Projects : Strategic projects presented to senior leadership at the end of each rotation.

Mentorship : Ongoing coaching plus quarterly 1:1 sessions with GAHL's Executive Chairman.

Career Path : At month 15, participants and leadership align on placement opportunities. Upon graduation, successful candidates may be offered a full-time role and enter GAHL's Leadership Development Program.

Focus Areas

  • Finance & Treasury
