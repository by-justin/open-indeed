Are you looking for meaningful work while working with our greatest brands?

We are looking for a  Print Production & Color Quality Consistency Specialist  to join our creative production team. This is a hands-on, individual contributor role with no direct reports, ideal for someone passionate about achieving best-in-class color accuracy and print excellence.

You will act as the  subject matter expert in color management and print production execution , ensuring high-quality production of packaging and label materials while maintaining strict alignment with brand guidelines, design intent, and vendor specifications.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

As a Print Production & Color Specialist you will be responsible for:

Color Management & Enhancement :

  • Execute advanced color correction, grading, and enhancement using Photoshop, Illustrator, and related tools.
  • Apply color profiling and calibration techniques to ensure accuracy across print and digital outputs.

Print Production & Quality Assurance:

  • Oversee printing materials for CPG lpackaging, ensuring high-quality production and consistency.
  • Ensure compliance with brand guidelines, creative intent, and technical specifications.
  • Partner with vendors and printers to guarantee precise color reproduction across print runs.

Prepress & Proofing

  • Prepare, review, and approve color proofs prior to production.
  • Perform preflight checks and ensure files meet all technical and print requirements.
  • Troubleshoot printing issues and provide solutions during execution.

Color Consistency Across Media:

  • Ensure accurate color matching across multiple substrates (paper, plastic, labels, etc.) and printing processes (flexo, offset, digital).
  • Maintain consistency across different production environments and regions.

Graphic & Digital Asset Management:

  • Perform end-to-end assembly of Digital Design Templates, managing assets and data for primary and secondary packaging.
  • Ensure Design Intent Matrix and all graphic adaptations are in-keeping with approved Design Intent and brand identity guidelines.
  • Manage and organize digital assets and data within platforms (e.g., Egnyte), ensuring accessibility and accurate version control.
  • Contribute to Design Intent Mapping by defining representative SKUs for graphic adaptation based on project scope.

Standards & Documentation:

  • Build and maintain internal color libraries (swatches, specifications, and guidelines).
  • Define and continuously improve color and print standards, workflows, and best practices.

Job Qualifications

  • Up to 3 years of previous experience in related roles.
  • Previous experience in color management, prepress, or print production (preferably in packaging or CPG).
  • Advanced proficiency in English.
  • Strong knowledge of printing technologies and processes (flexo, offset, digital, dry offset etc.).
  • Experience with Adobe Creative Suite and prepress tools (Illustrator, Photoshop, preflight software, Esko or equivalent).
  • Solid understanding of substrates, inks, and print materials.
  • Strong attention to detail and color accuracy.
  • Problem-solving and troubleshooting in print production.
  • Effective communication and collaboration with cross-functional teams and external vendors.
