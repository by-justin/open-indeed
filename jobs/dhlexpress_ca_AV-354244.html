Job Description

DHL Global Forwarding is part of the DHL Group and is the global specialist in transporting goods by air, sea, and road. From everyday products such as clothing and electronics to larger shipments such as machinery and medical supplies, we ensure that everything arrives on time and safely at the right place, anywhere in the world.

Within the Industrial Projects department, we manage complex and unique shipments that are anything but standard. From oversized cargo to time-critical project movements, no two days are the same. As a specialized team within DHL Global Forwarding, we are looking for a new colleague to help us deliver exceptional logistics solutions. Ready to take on the challenge? Read on!

What will you do?

As a Forwarding Project Specialist , you make the impossible possible. From oversized (OOG) cargo and breakbulk shipments to complex industrial projects, you take ownership of challenging logistics movements from planning through to final delivery.

  • Manage high-value project cargo, OOG, and breakbulk shipments from A to Z
  • Coordinate air, ocean, road, and multimodal transport solutions
  • Work with customers, carriers, terminals, ports, and DHL teams worldwide
  • Arrange permits, route studies, heavy-lift operations, and specialized transport
  • Keep stakeholders informed and projects on track
  • Support quotations, tenders, and business growth opportunities
  • Ensure flawless execution, documentation, and operational control

No two projects are the same. Every day brings a new logistics challenge to solve.

Your profile:

  • You have extensive knowledge of sea and air freight, as well as knowledge of customs matters and related documentation.
  • You have at least 3 to 5 years of experience in Project cargo.
  • You can express yourself well both orally and in writing in both the Dutch and English language. French and German are a plus.
  • You are cost-conscious and result-oriented, which means that you can transport shipments at a maximum result.
  • Integrity Strong arithmetic skills are also important, as you will need to handle large amounts of money carefully.
  • You must have the ability to solve any problems in arranging transports.
  • A high level of accuracy is essential when recording operational and financial commitments.
  • You may occasionally have to work under great time pressure, so it is important that you can perform well in stressful situations.
  • It is important that you are familiar with various computer applications and have good telephone and e-mail skills.

Our offer

  • An open-ended contract with a competitive salary, a nice package of extra-legal benefits and an attractive leave scheme;
  • 39-hour week with the possibility of teleworking after a training period
  • A wide range of training courses;
  • Interesting career opportunities within a large company with strong family values and a professional working environment.

Application process

  • After completing the online application form, we will contact you for an initial telephone interview.
  • If the telephone interview goes well, we will invite you for an interview on site with the manager responsible for the position.
  • If there is a mutual connection, we will make you a salary offer.
  • Great news! You want to start working for our company. You come to sign your contract and we prepare your start and onboarding.

If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact us at jobs.globalforwarding@dhl.com
