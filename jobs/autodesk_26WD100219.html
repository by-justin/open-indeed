職務概要

Autodesk Consultingでは、製造または建築・エンジニアリング・建設（AEC）領域において、豊富な実務経験と高い実行力を備えた テクニカルコンサルタント を募集しています。本ポジションは、日本国内の大手企業に対する Autodesk製品の導入・展開を主導 する極めて重要な役割です。

Technical Advisory（TA）組織の一員として、顧客の複雑な課題を実行可能な技術ソリューションへと落とし込み、BIM、クラウド、工場自動化、デジタル設計などを通じて、日本の産業・建築のデジタル変革を現場レベルで推進します。

  • BIM導入、工場自動化、クラウドインフラ、資材計画、サプライチェーン統合に関するコンサルティング提供
  • 顧客の上級ステークホルダーに対する技術的アドバイザリーの提供

  • トレーニング、ワークショップ、技術支援プログラムの実施
  • 計画的・段階的なプロジェクト遂行による品質と一貫性の確保

Position Overview

Autodesk Consulting is seeking highly skilled and execution-oriented Technical Consultants with domain expertise in manufacturing or architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) . This role is pivotal in leading the successful implementation of Autodesk technologies for enterprise customers across Japan’s industrial and construction sectors.

As part of the Technical Advisory (TA) organization, you will translate complex business challenges into actionable, scalable, and technically sound solutions. This is not just a strategic role—it requires hands-on implementation and collaboration with some of Japan’s most innovative and demanding clients. You will contribute directly to real-world transformation through advanced technologies like BIM, cloud platforms, factory automation, and digital workflows.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deploy Autodesk solutions for enterprise clients in the fields of product design, digital manufacturing, and/or building lifecycle management
  • Execute full implementation cycles including configuration, development, workflow automation, and integration
  • Apply expertise in Autodesk tools such as Inventor, Vault, Fusion 360, Revit, and ACC to deliver value-focused solutions
  • Provide practical consulting in domains such as BIM, factory automation, cloud infrastructure, resource/material planning, and supply chain integration
  • Serve as a trusted technical advisor to senior client stakeholders.
  • Collaborate with internal and external teams to resolve technical challenges and ensure successful delivery
  • Facilitate training sessions, workshops, and technical enablement programs
  • Ensure quality and consistency through structured planning and iterative delivery

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Architecture, Computer Science, or related technical field (or equivalent professional experience).
  • Strong experience in product design, manufacturing, or architectural project execution
  • Proficiency in Autodesk technologies: Manufacturing : Inventor, Vault, Fusion 360 AEC : Revit, BIM 360, ACC
  • Knowledge in at least one of the following:Factory operations, cloud platforms, inventory/resource management, scripting/APIBIM practices (LOD, ISO 19650)Japanese building regulations and approval processes
  • Fluent in Japanese; English proficiency preferred
  • Demonstrated ability to plan, manage, and execute technical work independently

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in building materials, modular construction, or industrial product manufacturing
  • Experience delivering consulting or implementation services for enterprise-scale projects
  • Familiarity with supply chain systems, cloud infrastructure, or enterprise integration
  • Knowledge of Python, C++, or web-based development (HTML5, JavaScript, CSS)
  • Experience with Digital Twin, AR/VR/MR, and advanced data management
  • Proactive, solution-focused mindset with strong problem-solving capabilities
  • A leadership mentality aligned with Autodesk’s mission to lead innovation in the design and make industries
