Job Description

P&G Masterclass

Discover us and your potential!

Event date: Tuesday, November 17, 2026

Curious to know how things work in one of the  largest FMCG players ? Do you want to experience how the first months at P&G look like? This is possible in 1 day during our  P&G Masterclass event !

Through our  interactive program , you will become familiar with Procter & Gamble, the people working there, the culture and the job/internship opportunities that P&G offers in many functions such as Brand Management, Sales, Finance and Supply Chain.

You will meet P&G employees who will answer all your questions and tell you why they chose to join Procter & Gamble. Through this event you will have an  introduction on different functions and career paths at P&G and with a team of other applicants you will participate in an interactive challenge.

Finally, at the end of the day, you will have the opportunity to have  more informal conversations  during an amazing dinner with our colleagues and ask all the questions you still have.

Please note that the event will take place for a  full day at our Brussels Office  from 08:00 AM until 23:00. More detailed information on the day itself and the logistics will be shared once you received your confirmation to participate in the event.

Up for the challenge? Apply now!

Deadline for registration is: Nov 1st, 2026.

Deadline for completing online assessments: November 4th, 2026.

Job Qualifications

The steps you will need to take for this are:

Step 1 – Application

Apply directly to this posting and let us know your area of interest (Brand, Sales, Finance, or Supply Chain). We fully understand you might still consider more than one department, so please select  your priority  at this stage.

Step 2 – Assessment

Once the application is complete, you will be invited to pass our online assessments consisting of 2 parts: Peak Performance Assessment and Interactive Assessment. More details on the online assessments are available  here.

Step 3 – Initial Interview

If successful, you will be invited for a first video interview with one of our P&G colleagues.

More details on our interviews are available  here.

Step 4 – Confirmation of event attendees:

Based on the outcome of your first interview and the number of places available, we will confirm whether you have been selected to attend and share the full event details with you.

Please note that second or final interviews will take place after the event.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
