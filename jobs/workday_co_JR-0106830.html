About the Team

The Workday Extend Consulting team, part of Workday’s EMEA Services organization, is dedicated to implementing custom business applications, agents, and orchestrations on top of the Workday platform. We help customers solve unique business challenges by extending core HCM and Financials and, increasingly, by building intelligent, agentic experiences that feel native to Workday. We value career growth, learning, and collaboration. We work in an agile way, staying close to our customers for fast feedback and focusing on the business value our solutions deliver. With the introduction of Developer Agent as part of Extend Professional, our team is at the forefront of how professional developers build apps and agents across the full Workday Build stack using natural language and modern AI-assisted tooling.

About the Role

As a Senior Workday Extend Consultant, you will lead the design and delivery of solutions using Workday Extend and Developer Agent for EMEA customers.

You will work directly with business and IT stakeholders to:

  • Discover and shape requirements.
  • Design architectures that combine Extend apps, agents, orchestrations, and integrations.
  • Build and iterate on solutions, using Developer Agent and IDE plug-ins to accelerate development.
  • You will act as a trusted technical advisor on how customers should adopt Extend, Developer Agent, and related AI capabilities safely and effectively.

This is a hands-on, customer-facing role with strong technical depth and plenty of room for thought leadership across EMEA.

What you’ll do

  • Run discovery and design workshops to understand business processes and identify where Workday Extend apps with embedded AI experiences can add value (for example, conversational assistance and intelligent recommendations) within the app experience.
  • Use generative coding tools such as Cursor and Workday’s Developer Agent to generate, refine, and refactor application artifacts from natural language descriptions.
  • Define and implement integrations and data flows using modern API patterns (REST, Graph APIs, and, where needed, SOAP via orchestrations).
  • Lead technical workstreams, review designs from other consultants/partners, and promote best practices and reusable patterns.
  • Mentor colleagues in Extend and AI-assisted development and feed implementation learnings back to product and engineering teams.

About You

Basic Qualifications

You must be a UK citizen and able to go through SC (Security Check) level UK government security clearance (National Security Vetting) .

Basic Qualifications

  • Several years of experience in enterprise software implementation, technical consulting, requirements gathering and solution architecture, ideally on Workday or similar HCM/Financials platforms.
  • Hands-on experience designing and building solutions on Workday Extend, or strong equivalent experience with an enterprise extension / PaaS platform.
  • Strong domain understanding in at least one area such as Human Resources, Payroll, Financials, or related back-office processes.

Other Qualifications

  • Strong architectural design skills across data flows, integration patterns, and security models.
  • Experience with agentic / “vibe coding” tools such as Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Cline, or other VS Code–compatible AI development environments.
  • Comfortable working with open, text-based configuration and documentation formats (e.g., Markdown) to define skills, tools, or agent behaviors in a structured way.
  • Excellent communication skills, able to explain complex technical and AI/agentic concepts to non-technical audiences while going deep with developers and architects.
  • Proven ability to manage multiple workstreams, prioritize trade-offs, and drive work through to production with high quality.
  • Hands-on experience integrating with MCP - style patterns for connecting agents to external systems.​
  • Prior involvement in early adopter programs for new AI or platform capabilities.
  • Extensive knowledge of web service and integration technologies (REST, SOAP, JSON, XML) and modern auth standards (such as OAuth, JWT, SAML).
  • Fluency in English is mandatory.  Additional European languages are an advantage.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
