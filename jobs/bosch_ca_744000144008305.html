Company Description

Bosch Automotive Steering Systems (Jinan) Co., Ltd. , established in October 2005 and located in Jinan, Shandong Province, is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bosch Group. Its specializes in the R&D, production, and sales of steering systems for commercial vehicles. As a global leader in steering systems, we provide safe, reliable, and comfortable steering solutions to customers both domestically and internationally, fully meeting the steering demands of commercial vehicles in the era of intelligence and electrification.

At this critical juncture of technological transformation, commercial vehicle steering systems are rapidly transitioning from traditional hydraulic-assisted steering to electronically controlled systems. Leveraging Bosch Group's world-class R&D resources and technological expertise, we have achieved mass production of the Servotwin series of electrohydraulic steering systems and are actively advancing the development of the ServoE series of fully electric steering systems. Through cross-domain integrated innovation and functional development, we continuously enhance vehicle reliability, safety, and comfort, driving the commercial vehicle industry toward higher-level intelligent mobility.

博世汽车转向系统（济南）有限公司成立于2005 年10 月，位于山东省济南市，是博世集团在华全资子公司。公司专注商用车转向系统的研发、生产、销售，作为全球转向系统的引领者，为国内外客户提供安全、可靠、舒适的转向系统解决方案，全面满足商用车智能化、电动化的转向需求。

在技术变革的关键期，商用车转向系统正加速从传统机械液压向电控系统转型。依托博世集团全球顶尖的研发资源与技术积淀，我们已实现电液转向系统Servotwin 系列的量产，并全力推进纯电动转向系统ServoE 系列的研发，以跨域融合的创新功能开发，持续提升整车的可靠性、安全性与舒适性，推动商用车产业向更高阶的智能出行迈进。
