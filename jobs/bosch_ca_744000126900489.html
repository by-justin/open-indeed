Job Description

Purpose: The purpose of this position is to supervise product audits (functional, barrier, assemblies) across value stream & error free product & PPAP dispatches to Customers (external & plant to plant).

Primary responsibility:

  1. Product Quality / Process Audit

Conducting & supervising various product and process quality audits as per defined SOP across shifts

Regular check and release of blocked & re qualify SLE parts/pumps for production based on risk

Ensure SOP of new releases in case of deviation and regularize the same through concessions release of daily MIS

  1. Production part approval process

Coordination for PPAP pumps, Spec editing for new layouts, creation/ updation of CAVIS and audit systems

Supervise for mapping of pumps.

  1. Change Management & Q improvements

Inform & participate during various change management & improvement events (Concession & ECR, trial order approval, FMEA like walk, LL, IDC reduction etc) & VSO meetings (daily basis). Prepare for various external audits.

  1. Team Supervision

Orientation & Training to newly joiners (including felxi manpower) ensure skill evaluation & development as per 3 shift plan Support in completion & evaluation of improvement projects of associates (SCIP, suggestions, VSO, etc).

  1. Investigation/complaints Management

Participate in customer complaints Investigation (0 Km, field complaints) & internal rejections analysis to assess failure mode in product.

  1. Escalation management

Identify and escalate the risk associates with product quality based on various audits.

Qualifications

Education: Bachelor's degree in engineering Mechanical / Automobile / Electrical

Experience: 2+ years of relevant work experience in quality / manufacturing (assembly and component

manufacturing)

Additional Information

A) Knowledge

  1. Advance in problem solving methods (8D, 5W, basic quality tools) & preventive Q tools (FMEA, ISIR, PPAP)

  2. Competent technical knowledge of PS products, internal processes

B) Functional skills

  1. Advance in analytical skills, structured working method

  2. Advance in communication and presentation skills, basic regional local language

  3. Competent in persuasiveness & team building

  4. Advanced in PPAP process & requirement
