As a Senior AWS Infrastructure Engineer, you’ll use your deep technical expertise to design, build, and support secure, scalable infrastructure environments across both cloud and on-premise platforms. Working within a highly collaborative technical team, you’ll play a key role in delivering resilient Linux-based infrastructure solutions that support mission-critical services for public sector and defence customers, ensuring compliance, security, and operational excellence across highly regulated environments.

Responsibilities:

  • Lead the design, implementation, and support of secure AWS infrastructure platforms with a strong focus on Linux systems, enterprise infrastructure services, and cloud-native best practices

  • Design and manage AWS services, and cloud-native best practices

  • Design and manage AWS services including EC2, VPCs, IAM, Route53, CloudWatch, S3, EBS, Load Balancers, Auto Scaling, and backup/recovery solutions in line with public sector and defence security requirements

  • Manage and optimise Linux server environments, ensuring high availability, hardening, patching, performance, and compliance with security standards and operational policies

  • Support and maintain core infrastructure technologies including networking, middleware, DNS, VPNs, monitoring, logging, and enterprise tooling

  • Collaborate with engineering and delivery teams to integrate AWS infrastructure solutions into CI/CD pipelines and operational support models

  • Contribute to infrastructure automation initiatives using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and configuration management tooling such as Terraform, Ansible, and CloudFormation

  • Support containerised workloads and orchestration platforms including Kubernetes, Docker, and orchestration platforms including Kubernetes, Docker, and CRI-O across secure and scalable environments

  • Implement and support AWS security best practices, including IAM policies, encryption, network segmentation, logging, including IAM policies, encryption, network segmentation, logging, auditing, and vulnerability remediation

  • Monitor infrastructure health, availability, security, and capacity using enterprise monitoring and logging platforms such as Splunk and CloudWatch

  • Support disaster recovery, backup, business continuity, and resilience strategies across AWS and hybrid infrastructure environments

  • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to technical standards, operational documentation, and continuous improvement initiatives

  • Contribute to infrastructure strategy, operational roadmaps, cloud migration, and platform modernisation programmes within regulated public sector and defence environments

Join our team and play a key role in delivering scalable, secure, and resilient AWS infrastructure solutions that support critical business operations and long-term growth.
