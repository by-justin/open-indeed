Job Description

Philips Personalcare consumer products including: Electric toothbrushes, electric water flossers, electric shavers, hair dryers, beauty devices, etc.

Job Posting Description

In this role, you have the opportunity to

  • Build a high growth business that will help improve the lives of millions people in China, while advancing Philips digital transformation journey as a global leader in Health Technology
  • Location open for Shanghai or Shenzhen

You are responsible for

  • Responsible for sales of the full range of Philips products, mainly for Philips oral healcare products, including electronic toothbrush, water flossers, the clients include Wal-Mart, Sam’s Club and Watson's etc.
  • Accomplish the company objectives by formulating a business strategy towards KA customer and develop new opportunities for business
  • To program and execute marketing plans after implementing market research of sales channels and network in responsible areas
  • To manage assigned area authorized distributors: break down annual sell-in target, discuss with area distributors to plan and achieve monthly sales forecast

You are a part of

You will report to NKA Leader and collaborate with many other teams including Area Sales, Product Marketing, Commercial operation, Finance etc.

To succeed in this role, you should have the following skills and experience

  • At least 8 years domestic electronic/FMCG industry sales management experience;
  • Has solid authorized KAM management - with Sam's Club is a must;
  • Be expert in dealer management, special for assist or urge dealer to accomplish the sales target and business growth.

In return, we offer you

A path towards your most rewarding career. Philips is growing its marketing capability enterprise wide. Succeeding in this market-based role in a complex environment will open many doors for your long-term career, in other areas in Philips or otherwise. We also believe that we are at our best as a company when you are at yours as a person. Thus, we offer competitive health benefits, a flexible work schedule and access to local well-being focused activities.
