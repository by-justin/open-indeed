Job Description

RBC Insurance, through its operating entities, provides a wide range of travel, life, health, home, auto, wealth, and insurance products and solutions. It also offers creditor and business reinsurance services to both individual and group clients. We are one of the largest insurance companies within a Canadian bank and among the fastest-growing insurance organizations in the country. RBC Insurance employs more than 150 full-time actuaries globally and is the brand name for the insurance operating entities of Royal Bank of Canada. This opportunity will offer you exposure to the life insurance industry.

Your application will be reviewed for consideration across multiple positions with responsibilities aligned to those described in this job posting.

Please note:  Before submitting your application,  read the application process at the bottom of this description carefully to ensure all required documents have been submitted.  Your application will not be considered if you do not follow these directions.

What is the opportunity? As an Actuarial Co-op Student, you will receive an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the life insurance industry with real-world business problems, be challenged to resolve complex tasks, and provided with valuable on-the-job training. This role will offer you an exciting opportunity to be part of the largest bank-owned insurance company in Canada. You will apply, enhance, and develop experience and working knowledge to perform a combination of challenging tasks and operational assignments. RBC Insurance offers a very inclusive team that works cohesively together, which is integral to the success of continuing to grow all lines of business. We commit to formally structured access to leaders across our actuarial teams and other business areas to ensure you are learning all aspects of insurance and are prepared for a successful and rewarding career.

What will you do?

  • Learn, influence, and innovate end-to-end aspects of the business, ranging from Pricing and Modeling to Valuation and Capital. These roles will offer opportunities to gain exposure to all areas of the life insurance business.
  • Create life pricing and valuation models for complex and high-impact products from an actuarial perspective, including but not limited to asset strategy discussions with the RBC Insurance Investment team and liability cash flow management with RBC Risk Management.
  • Assist in validation of key assumptions, gathering and applying market competition insights, and using business insights to identify trends/opportunities.
  • Use software like AXIS, R, SQL, and MS Excel to produce and analyze results, communicate findings to respective stakeholders to obtain their approvals.
  • Develop, maintain, and enhance new and existing tools to better streamline processes.
  • Collaborate with fellow actuarial and non-actuarial colleagues to achieve success.
  • Conduct monthly and quarterly valuation reporting and communicate results and findings to management.
  • Model and analyze Special Quotes to better serve our clients’ needs.
  • Support monthly/quarterly capital measurement analysis and reporting.

What do you need to succeed?

Must have

  • Knowledge of MS Office Suite
  • Solid technical and analytical skills with strong problem-solving ability
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Highly motivated self-starter and team player

Nice to have

  • Pursuing a university degree in Actuarial Science, Finance, Statistics, or Mathematics
  • Actuarial exposure/background
  • 1-3 CAS/SOA exams completed
  • Previous experience with Moody’s/GGY AXIS
  • Knowledge of IFRS17, predictive analytics and Tableau are definite assets
  • Programing experience in R, VBA and SQL
