Job Description

MANNARINO Systems & Software Inc. is a leader in providing safety-critical systems, software, hardware and certification engineering services to the aerospace, defense, space, simulation & power generation industries. Our customer base is very large, covering traditional aerospace companies as well as new and exciting markets, including electric propulsion, Urban Air Mobility, Unmanned Aerial Vehicle and autonomous flight. The MANNARINO culture is focused on high quality, hard work, professionalism and teamwork within a highly respectful, motivating atmosphere geared towards long-term business growth.   • a comprehensive group insurance and RRSP plan
  • a flextime policy with paid overtime

  • other office perks such as catered weekly lunches and free hot beverages (espresso, coffee, hot chocolate and tea)
  • regular company outings Career Opportunity and Primary Roles / Responsibilities We are seeking a GNC System Engineer for design, integration, verification, and operational support of GNC subsystems for our current and next‑generation remote sensing and communications satellites. The role will require to design the complete subsystem, from algorithm development through hardware implementation, verification, and on‑orbit operation support. The role requires deep expertise in orbital dynamics, attitude‑determination & control, plus real‑time FSW definition and integration, and will work closely with subsystem and mission operations teams throughout the satellite life‑cycle. Tasks summary: Architecture Concept & Requirements • Derive GNC functional, performance, and safety requirements (pointing accuracy, slew rate, orbit‑maintenance ΔV, fault‑tolerance) from mission level requirements. • Produce System Requirements, Interface Control document (ICD), and Description Document. • Perform requirement management and maintain bidirectional traceability throughout the project lifecycle. Modelling & Trade‑Study • Conduct trade studies on sensor suites and actuator families. • Perform high‑fidelity dynamics simulations (Matlab/Simulink, STK) to meet pointing requirements, maintain error budgets, control‑law stability, fuel consumption, and survivability margins. Algorithm Development • Design and implement attitude‑determination algorithms and orbit‑determination solutions. • Develop control laws for 3‑axis stabilization, slew maneuvers, station keeping algorithms • Validate algorithms with Monte‑Carlo analysis, worst‑case scenarios, and support SIL/HIL testing on avionic test bench. Cross‑Functional Coordination, Stakeholder & Program Interaction • Translate algorithms into FSW requirements running on real‑time operating systems. • Collaborate with the C&DH and FSW teams to implement TM/TC, schedulers and FDIR logic for GNC. • Present technical status, risk analyses, and trade‑study results to senior management. • Negotiate and maintain ICDs with subsystems. • Support B&P (Phase 0 & pre-phase A). Hardware Selection & Design • Specify sensor and actuator hardware, perform interface definition (electrical, mechanical, thermal) and conduct trade on redundancy architectures. • Work with structures and thermal engineers to ensure proper mounting, thermal paths, and vibration survivability of GNC hardware aligning with payload requirements. • Review harness routing and power distribution for GNC electronics. Integration & Verification (IVV) • Author Verification Plans, Test Matrices, and Validation Procedures for unit, subsystem, and spacecraft level GNC tests. • Lead functional tests: sensor calibration, actuator command‑response, closed‑loop attitude‑control on testbeds, and on‑orbit checkout sequences. • Conduct failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA), fault tree analysis (FTA), and reliability prediction. Technical Leadership & Mentorship • Mentor, guide junior engineers and interns. • Act as technical liaison with customers, partners and external vendors • Act as Continuous Improvement stakeholder • Contribute to knowledge‑base, process enhancements, and standards. Configuration, Documentation & Standards Management • Maintain GNC standards (sensor/actuator interface, filter design, control‑law documentation). • Ensure all artefacts, analysis, software, deliverables and documentations are version controlled in PLM environment. • Support design reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR, MRR/TRR…etc.). • Support Technology Road‑Mapping & Innovation On‑Orbit & Operations Support • Support procedure developments (detumble, orbit‑maintenance, safe‑mode…etc.). • Provide real‑time GNC support during launch, early‑orbit checkout, and contingency events. • Analyse on‑orbit telemetry, update filter parameters, and generate performance reports. • Support SOOH development and associated operator training. Required Qualifications: • B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, Aerospace or Physics, (M.Sc. preferred), focused on flight dynamics, control theory, or navigation with 10+ of GNC/AOCS experience on LEO/MEO/GEO platforms. • Orbital mechanics, attitude dynamics, and control‑law design (classical and modern optimal control). • Proficiency with navigation sensors and actuators. • Experience implementing real‑time filtering and control algorithms in embedded code • Strong understanding of aerospace system engineering fundamentals. • Hands-on individual who can support systems integration processes and capability to test systems and resolve problems. • Experience with Confluence and JIRA. • Demonstrated capability to efficiently work with little supervision. • Ability to distill complex problems to fundamentals to solve with analysis, similarity, or creativity. • Demonstrate excellent written and verbal communications skills in English & French work environment.
