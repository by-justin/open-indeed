Job Description

Job Description Summary

At GE Vernova, we aren't just managing infrastructure; we are accelerating the energy transition. Our Evergreen offering is the heartbeat of our digital transformation—a high-growth initiative essential to scaling our Grid Software solutions globally. We are looking for a visionary DevOps leader to build the foundation of our future. If you are passionate about industrializing complex deployments and shaping the standards that define our global ecosystem, come build the energy grid of tomorrow with us

This role leads the DevSecOps implementation of GE Vernova Grid Software products at customer sites. This is a critical, high visibility, customer-facing role to ensure regular deployments to Customer Reference Environments, Dev, QA and Production using continuous deployment (CD) automation. The role also includes contribution towards definition of standards around deployment automation, security, ecosystem tooling to enable automation and test automation. The Evergreen offering has a high growth potential for GE Vernova and a core capability identified for scaling globally.

Job Description

In this role, you will:

  • Collaborate with the customer to define an end-to-end DevSecOps process to bring CI/CD & DevOps principles to deliver GE Vernova Grid Software products to the customer environments
  • Design, build, and maintain Customer Reference Environments (CREs) that provide a secure, scalable, and repeatable foundation for customer deployments.
  • Develop and manage infrastructure automation and configuration management using Ansible to ensure consistency, speed, and reliability across environments.
  • Build, manage, and optimize CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub to enable efficient software integration, testing, and deployment.
  • Manage artifact storage, versioning, and release promotion processes using Artifactory to support controlled and traceable software delivery.
  • Build deep knowledge of GE Vernova Products across performance and GridOS.
  • Working knowledge of Kubernetes on RKE2 (Rancher) is a plus.
  • Partner with software engineering, product, support, and services teams to industrialize environment setup, upgrades, patching, and ongoing maintenance activities.
  • Monitor environment health, troubleshoot deployment and infrastructure issues, and drive root cause resolution to improve stability and operational excellence.
  • Implement and enforce DevOps best practices for source control, automation, release management, and system reliability.
  • Contribute to the creation of standards, templates, runbooks, and reusable automation assets that accelerate Evergreen adoption across customers.
  • Support continuous improvement by identifying opportunities to enhance environment provisioning, operational efficiency, resiliency, and customer experience.Participate in readiness planning for new customer deployments and ensure environments are aligned to security, compliance, and supportability expectations

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or “STEM” Majors (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) with minimum ten (10) years of experience in infrastructure automation and DevOps engineering.
  • Hands-on experience with Ansible for configuration management and infrastructure automation
  • Proficiency with container orchestration using Kubernetes
  • Demonstrated experience building and managing CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub
