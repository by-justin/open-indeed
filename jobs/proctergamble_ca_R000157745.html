Job Description

Ready to build brands that billions of people know, trust, and love? Come to P&G where Brand Management was born!

Did you know P&G invented the concept of branding and data-driven brand management? Over the years, we have remained consistently among the top in marketing by finding new and exciting ways to reach consumers.

At P&G, we believe that diverse experiences help build strong leaders. Mobility is a key component of many management careers, providing opportunities to grow through different assignments, locations, and business challenges. Candidates should be prepared to consider relocation opportunities throughout their career as business needs and development opportunities arise.

At P&G the consumer is the boss. Working in brand management means being more than just a marketer; it means being a total business owner. Our work often crosses traditional boundaries to discover consumer insights and implement innovative marketing strategies that deepen the connection with our customers. With data-driven approaches, we lead efforts that touch and improve the lives of 5 billion people every day.

Your Role as a Brand Management Intern

As a Brand Management Intern, you will have the opportunity to take on meaningful responsibilities from day one. You will work on key brands and, as you grow, your impact and ownership will expand.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Consumer Engagement:  Understand and connect with consumers at every touchpoint: when they learn about our products, when they choose to buy, and when they use the products at home.
  • Project Leadership:  Lead a variety of Marketing and Brand Management projects that showcase your leadership capabilities.
  • Skill Development:  Participate in a tailored mix of classroom training and on-the-job experiences to enhance your marketing knowledge and management skills.
  • Coaching & Mentorship:  Receive continuous support and mentorship from your manager and team members to help you grow professionally.
  • Work Environment:  Experience a dynamic and respectful workplace that values individuality and encourages work/life balance.

Internships are 12 weeks long and you can start on one of the following dates:

May 17, June 1, June 14

Job Qualifications

Who We Are Looking For

We seek passionate and innovative students who are excited to drive positive change. The ideal candidate will:

  • Be a top talent junior acquiring an undergraduate degree or master’s degree (preferably with some business focus) in December 2027 or Spring 2028.
  • Exhibit excellent visionary, strategic, and fearless leadership skills.
  • Demonstrate strong teamwork, analytical abilities, and a solutions-oriented mindset.
  • Have a passion for leading complex, innovative projects and embrace new experiences.
  • Be able to manage multiple priorities under pressure while showing initiative and flexibility.

Important Information

  • This internship is a temporary position with a defined completion date.
  • Successful interns may be considered for full-time roles based on business needs.
  • We are committed to developing talent from within, ensuring a strong pipeline for future leaders.

Join Us!

If you are ready to make an impact and grow your career with P&G, we encourage you to apply for our Brand Management Internship. Together, let's continue to innovate and improve the lives of consumers around the world!
