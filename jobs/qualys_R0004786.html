About Qualys

Qualys, Inc. (NASDAQ: QLYS) is a pioneer and leading provider of cloud-based security, compliance, and IT solutions, trusted by over 10,000 customers across more than 130 countries -- including a majority of the Forbes Global 100. We help organizations consolidate security and compliance into a single platform, protecting their most critical assets in an increasingly complex threat landscape.

Our Pune office is a hub of innovation and one of the most dynamic centers in our global operation. The team here works directly with leadership, collaborates across time zones, and has a direct impact on how Qualys shows up in the world.

The Role

We are looking for a rare creative talent to join our Brand & Creative team as a Senior Social Media Producer. This is not a social media management role -- it is a content creation role. You will be the one concepting, scripting, filming, editing, designing, and delivering compelling content that makes people stop scrolling.

You will own your content lane end to end. That means figuring out what we need before we need it and building the plan to get there. You will plan not just around our flagship events but around campaigns, major news cycles, and product launches, all the way down to the everyday work of growing an audience, building a community, and shaping a distinct social identity for the brand.

You will work closely with the Manager of Video Productions to support Qualys's social presence across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X. You will transform long-form video and campaign content into platform-native short-form assets, cover flagship events with real-time content, and generate fresh ideas that reflect the pulse of where social media is heading. When the moment calls for it, you will also step in front of the camera.

The right person for this role is a self-starter who thrives without a script -- someone who sees a long-form interview and immediately visualizes five short-form cuts from it. You know what earns engagement and what gets ignored. You have a point of view on content, and you are not afraid to push ideas forward.

What You Will Do

Content Creation & Production

  • Concept, script, shoot, and edit original short-form video content optimized for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X
  • Repurpose long-form video and campaign assets into high-performing platform-native content
  • Film using both mobile and higher-end camera setups depending on production needs
  • Step in front of the camera as on-screen talent, host, or presenter when the content calls for it
  • Design social media graphics and templates including thumbnails, post graphics, and branded social templates using Canva, Figma, and Adobe Illustrator -- with the ability to flex into broader graphic design support as content needs arise

Strategy & Ideation

  • Bring original content ideas to the table consistently -- not just execute on briefs, but generate the briefs
  • Stay ahead of social media trends across platforms and recommend formats, sounds, and approaches that keep Qualys culturally relevant
  • Develop and pitch concepts for social campaigns tied to product launches, events, thought leadership, and brand moments
  • Actively identify opportunities to turn existing company content into social-first stories

Events & Live Coverage

  • Travel to and cover flagship events including ROCon and internal employee events at Qualys headquarters and beyond
  • Produce real-time social content from the field -- capturing energy, moments, and stories as they happen
  • Build shot lists and content plans for event coverage well in advance and execute them on the ground -- willing to go the extra mile to get exactly the right shot

Collaboration

  • Work closely with the Video, Brand, and Design teams to ensure content is on-brand and aligned with broader campaigns
  • Collaborate comfortably with stakeholders at all levels -- from engineers and product managers to executives and the C-suite -- to identify and develop stories worth telling
  • Hand off completed assets to the social media team with proper context, platform specs, and copy recommendations

What You Bring

Required

  • 5 to 7 years of experience in social media content production, digital content creation, or a closely related creative field
  • A strong portfolio of professional social media content you have produced -- including short-form video across at least two major platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or X). Applications submitted without a portfolio will not be considered.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and CapCut for video editing
  • Working knowledge of Adobe After Effects for motion graphics and basic animation
  • Graphic design skills in Canva, Figma, and Adobe Illustrator sufficient to produce social media graphics and templates independently
  • Comfort and confidence filming on both smartphone and higher-end camera equipment
  • Proven on-camera presence -- you have appeared as a host, presenter, or on-screen talent in professional content
  • Deep fluency across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X -- you understand not just what is trending but why it works
  • Strong scripting and concepting skills -- you can take a complex idea and turn it into a compelling 60-second video
  • Proficiency with AI tools for content production, ideation, and workflow efficiency
  • Working knowledge of video specs, aspect ratios, and export requirements across social platforms
  • Strong written and spoken English proficiency -- content will reach a global audience
  • Willingness to travel domestically and internationally up to 25 to 30 percent of the time
  • An advanced command of social media analytics and content performance metrics. You eat and breathe the numbers and use them to sharpen every concept, format, and post, with a clear read on what works, what does not, and why.

Preferred

  • Experience producing content for a B2B, technology, or cybersecurity brand
  • Familiarity with cybersecurity concepts, terminology, or the enterprise tech industry
  • Experience covering live events and producing real-time social content from the field
  • Background working within or alongside a brand or creative team in a corporate environment
  • Familiarity with content management and asset management tools such as Asana and Bynder
  • Experience working with content calendars and structured asset delivery workflows
  • Experience working with or for global teams across multiple time zones

Who You Are

  • A creative thinker who generates ideas independently and follows them all the way through to execution
  • Someone who has strong opinions about what makes content work -- and the craft to back it up
  • Equally comfortable behind the camera, in front of it, and at the editing timeline
  • A collaborator who communicates clearly and hands off work in a way that makes the next person's job easier
  • Naturally curious about social media, culture, and technology -- you are always learning
  • Organized and accountable in a fast-paced environment with multiple projects in motion
  • A self-starter who does not need to be told what to create next

What to Expect

This role sits within our Brand & Creative team and reports to the Manager of Video Productions. You will be one of the few people at Qualys who works at the intersection of creative production, on-camera talent, social strategy, and design. It is a high-visibility position with direct impact on how one of the world's leading cybersecurity companies communicates with its audience.

You will have meaningful creative ownership from day one. The work is challenging, varied, and moves quickly. We invest in talent, support relocation for the right candidate, and are serious about building a creative team that produces best-in-class content.
