Job Description

Senior Reliability Design Engineer (Hardware)

An experienced Senior Reliability Design Engineer (Hardware) with a strong Electrical/Electronic background to lead reliability engineering activities for complex hardware systems. The role is responsible for driving Design for Reliability (DfR), driving life-cycle performance improvement through reliability prediction, robust design, verification testing, and failure investigation.

高级可靠性设计工程师（硬件）

我们正在寻找一位经验丰富的 高级可靠性设计工程师（硬件） ，具备扎实的电气/电子工程背景，负责复杂硬件系统的可靠性工程工作。该岗位将主导产品可靠性设计（DfR），通过可靠性预测、稳健设计、验证测试及失效分析等手段，持续提升产品全生命周期的可靠性表现。

Your role:

  • Define and deploy Design for Reliability (DfR) methodology throughout product development, with emphasis on electrical/electronic architecture, component selection, derating, and design margins.
  • Establish reliability requirements, reliability budget allocation, prediction models, and reliability growth plans for electro-mechanical and electronic systems.
  • Lead reliability reviews from concept through commercialization.
  • Drive reliability risk identification and mitigation activities for electrical, thermal, mechanical, environmental, and operational stress conditions.
  • Apply robust design methodologies to reduce sensitivity to manufacturing variation, environmental stress, and component tolerances.
  • Develop design margins, derating strategies, and robustness criteria for key electrical/electronic components, assemblies, power/control circuits, sensors, interconnects, and motion-related hardware.
  • Ensure adequate robustness against thermal, mechanical, electrical, vibration, and operational stresses through analysis, design review, and verification planning.
  • Promote first-time-right design philosophy by embedding reliability thinking early.
  • Define reliability verification strategy, reliability testing plan, establish reliability demonstration tests.
  • Drive root cause investigations for failures identified during testing by leveraging relevant tools, i.e., failure tree analysis, DOE, Monte Carlo simulation, etc.

您的职责（Your Role）

  • 在整个产品开发过程中定义并推动可靠性设计（Design for Reliability, DfR）方法论的实施，重点关注电气/电子架构、元器件选型、降额设计（Derating）及设计裕量管理。
  • 建立机电系统和电子系统的可靠性需求、可靠性预算分配、可靠性预测模型以及可靠性增长计划。
  • 从概念设计阶段到产品商业化阶段，主导各阶段的可靠性评审工作。
  • 推动可靠性风险识别与缓解活动，包括电气、热管理、机械、环境及运行工况等方面的风险。
  • 应用稳健设计（Robust Design）方法，降低产品对制造波动、环境应力及元器件公差变化的敏感度。
  • 为关键电气/电子元器件、组件、电源及控制电路、传感器、互连结构以及运动相关硬件制定设计裕量、降额策略和稳健性标准。
  • 通过分析、设计评审和验证规划，确保产品具备充分抵御热应力、机械应力、电气应力、振动及运行工况应力的能力。
  • 倡导“ 一次设计正确（First-Time-Right） ”理念，在产品开发早期植入可靠性思维。
  • 制定可靠性验证策略和测试计划，并建立可靠性验证测试（Reliability Demonstration Test）。
  • 利用失效树分析（FTA）、实验设计（DOE）、蒙特卡洛仿真（Monte Carlo Simulation）等工具，主导测试过程中发现问题的根因分析和失效调查。

You're the right fit if:

  • Master’s or bachelor’s degree in electrical, electronic, reliability, or mechatronics engineering, or a related discipline.
  • 10+ years of engineering experience for complex hardware products, preferably involving electrical/electronic subsystems, power/control electronics, sensors, motion systems, rotating or linear-motion products.
  • 3+ years leading reliability engineering activities for complex hardware products (CT, MR, X-ray, Radiotherapy or other regulated high-reliability systems is highly valued).
  • Demonstrated success applying Robust Design, DFMEA, problem solving, physics-of-failure thinking, and data-driven reliability improvement in new product development.
  • Ability to influence design decisions and drive reliability improvements across global cross-functional teams.
  • Working knowledge of ISO13485, EU MDR, FDA 21 CFR, ISO14971.
  • Strong and practical knowledge of DFMEA, problem solving, Design for Six Sigma (GB/BB) certified candidates are preferred.
  • Good English communication skills (both verbal and written) required.

  • 能够影响设计决策，并推动全球跨职能团队开展可靠性改进工作；

  • 良好的英语听说读写能力，能够进行国际化沟通与协作。
