Description

Job Title: Project Engineer, Skilled中级项目工程师

  • In this position, the individual would be responsible for developing and detailing processes to produce machined components and assemblies. Also need to ensure project operation and implementation.

  • 该岗位主要负责开发生产加工组件及优化工艺流程，并需确保项目的运作和执行。

Performance Expectations

  • Specify special tools and fixtures where required

  • Estimate cycle times and cost for each operation in the process

  • Determine whether sub-contracted processes are to occur i.e., heat treating, plating, grinding

  • Set production targets/goals

  • Assist in the preparation of quotations

  • Maintain control of customer drawings and revisions

  • Ensure that the process is capable of producing parts to specifications

  • Modify and optimize the process and update all relevant documentation when required

  • Coordinate the materials, help to equipment selection

  • 识别特殊刀具和夹具的需求

  • 为每道工序测算加工节拍和成本

  • 确定是否需要外包部分加工内容，如热处理、电镀、磨削等

  • 设定生产目标

  • 协助销售完成报价准备工作

  • 维护管控客户图纸及版本的更新

  • 确保生产工艺能满足生产零件的规格

  • 修改或优化工艺流程，及时更新相关文件

  • 协调原材料，帮助设备选型

Credentials

  • University/College education in engineering or manufacturing or equivalence

  • Project Management and materials knowledge is required

  • 工程或制造类及其他相关专业，大学或大专以上学历

  • 具备项目管理，材料基础知识

Desired Characteristics

  • Ability to meet, realize and support customers’ product requirements, implement the knowledge of operation system and specific requirement

  • Three to five years’ experience in a machining or manufacturing environment.

  • Project Management working experience is required

  • Some familiarity and proficiency with personal computers

  • Able to communicate requirements clearly in written and drawing formats

  • Ability to work independently in a program to coordinate activities with others and with company personnel beyond boundaries.

  • Ability to prioritize tasks and allocate resources according to the order of importance.

  • Good Communication

  • Cost Saving concept is plus

  • Those who have been trained in APQP、SPC、FMEA、MSA、PPAP and 6 Sigma Yellow Belt  are preferred

  • 具备满足、实现、支持客户产品要求并实现运行体系及特殊要求的技能知识

  • 3到5年制造或机械行业工作经验

  • 项目管理经验

  • 精通电脑操作和应用

  • 熟练掌握工程绘图软件

  • 独立完成项目，能协调分配本部门、跨部门员工完成工作

  • 按轻重缓急分配和完成任务

  • 良好的沟通能力

  • 有成本控制理念

  • 接受过APQP、SPC、FMEA、MSA、PPAP五大工具以及6 Sigma黄带培训者优先
