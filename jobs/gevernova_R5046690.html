Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Leads follow-up activities for long-lead critical purchase orders (POs) in EPC hydroelectric power plant projects.
  • Manages supplier relationships throughout PO execution.
  • Addresses progress issues and schedule changes to ensure on-time delivery.
  • Maintains the ERP system with accurate and up-to-date forecasts.
  • Ensures timely and accurate processing of supplier payments.
  • Resolves issues using established procedures and technical expertise.
  • Collaborates with Engineering, Quality, Planning, and other stakeholders to ensure quality and on-time delivery.
  • Leads discussions with suppliers during execution regarding claims, scope changes, and related matters, in coordination with Project Teams.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline from a reputable university.
  • 6–12 years of experience in engineering, sourcing, and/or manufacturing of electrical and/or mechanical systems in Project based business environments.
  • Self-directed, detail-oriented, well-organized, and adaptable.
  • Strong skills in Microsoft Excel, Smartsheet, and other office productivity software.
  • Experience with contract execution in Procure-to-Pay processes using SAP-based ERP systems.
  • Fluent in English (spoken and written).

Desired Characteristics

  • Experience with electrical commodities for power plants.
  • Master’s degree in Supply Chain Management or a related discipline from a reputable university.
  • Knowledge of French or German is an asset.
  • Experience in the energy industry, particularly hydropower projects, is a strong plus.
  • Demonstrated ability and ownership to drive improvements and change with tangible results.
