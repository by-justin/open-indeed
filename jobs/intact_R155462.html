About the role

Are you passionate about mobile development and design? Do you advocate for high-quality code and design? Let’s talk!

As a Senior iOS Developer, you will play an essential role in the design and evolution of iceberg, Intact’s design system (Android, iOS, React, and Angular). You will facilitate the work of iOS developers within product teams and become a subject-matter expert for your peers, while actively contributing to the technical direction of the iceberg team.

What you’ll do here:

  • Analyze, plan, develop, maintain, and evolve the iceberg iOS library (SwiftUI, distributed through SPM and CocoaPods) to facilitate native mobile development.
  • Integrate Intact’s design system and ensure that it aligns with iOS design patterns and guidelines (Human Interface Guidelines).
  • Design, build, and maintain highly reusable, versioned, and documented code that supports multiple brands.
  • Collaborate with partner developers (Android, React, and Angular) within the iceberg team to ensure the consistency and future evolution of our solutions.
  • Act as the iOS point of contact for the iceberg team with product and design teams.
  • Support product design workflows by addressing questions and issues related to the iOS platform.
  • Contribute to the quality and reliability of components through automated testing (Maestro, Perfecto) and accessibility audits.
  • Participate in design reviews to validate the implementation fidelity of Android and iOS components.

What you bring to the table:

  • At least 5 years of experience in software development, including 3 years creating native iOS applications at a senior level.
  • Extensive experience with Swift and SwiftUI.
  • Experience with UIKit and knowledge of the iOS Human Interface Guidelines.
  • Experience designing and maintaining reusable frameworks/libraries distributed through SPM and/or CocoaPods.
  • At least one published mobile application, ideally in a high-volume user environment.
  • Examples demonstrating both strong iOS development skills and an understanding of iOS design patterns, including design patterns, architecture, and accessibility.
  • Experience with Git and Atlassian tools (Jira, Confluence).
  • Understanding of design tools (Figma) and the ability to collaborate closely with UX designers.
  • An asset: experience with mobile automated testing tools (Maestro, Perfecto) and cross-platform design systems (Android/Compose, React, Angular).
  • You enjoy working in a collaborative environment that emphasizes teamwork, innovation, open-mindedness, and having fun.
  • No Canadian work experience required however must be eligible to work in Canada.
  • Bilingualism. Need to interact on a regular basis with colleagues across the country.

About you

  • Creative, analytical, curious, and resourceful.
  • A team player who can give and receive constructive feedback.
  • Excellent at explaining your ideas to different stakeholders across product, design, and platform teams.
  • Deeply passionate about design, customer experience, and quality.
  • Organized, highly autonomous, and proactive.
  • Able to act as a mentor and technical reference for other iOS developers.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
