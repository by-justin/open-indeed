Your Team, Your Impact

The Associate Workplace Specialist ensures operational excellence, safety, and service delivery for our Santa Clara headquarters. This role acts as a key liaison between employees, site leadership, and Global Workplace Services (GWS), supporting daily building operations, vendor management, capital programs, budgeting, and health and safety compliance. The ideal candidate is a proactive, detail-oriented professional who thrives in a fast- paced, dynamic corporate environment and is committed to delivering a world-class workplace experience.

What You Can Expect

Workplace Experience & Customer Engagement

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for site leaders and employees; respond to service requests promptly and drive a culture of service excellence
  • Oversee day-to-day workplace services including events, catering, furniture, signage, and moves/adds/changes (MAC) coordination
  • Manage vendor escorts, badge access requests, and site security coordination as needed
  • Administer employee satisfaction surveys and act on feedback to improve service delivery
  • Participate in the Emergency Response Team (ERT); support emergency preparedness drills and
  • protocols
  • Support ISO audit walks, document findings, and help drive corrective actions to closure
  • Order and manage inventory of GWS supplies and materials
  • Draft, edit, and distribute internal communications, announcements, and site-wide updates
  • Maintain up-to-date signage, wayfinding, and site information for employees and visitors

Budgeting & Financial Management

  • Support development and management of operating budgets; track spending, forecast costs, and identify variances
  • Process invoices, purchase requisitions (PRs), and purchase orders (POs) in Oracle
  • Coordinate permits, city and county compliance filings, and tax documentation
  • Administer ChargePoint (EV charging) systems, including access, billing, and maintenance coordination
  • Source, evaluate, and onboard vendors; negotiate agreements, define service level agreements (SLAs), and conduct regular performance reviews
  • Reconcile monthly spend against budget and prepare variance reports for leadership

Program Management

  • Oversee a portfolio of facility programs, including capital improvements, space reconfigurations, and
  • workplace upgrades, ensuring alignment with broader GWS strategy and site roadmaps
  • Coordinate cross-functionally with IT, Security, Real Estate, and HR to align program timelines, budgets, and deliverables across multiple concurrent initiatives
  • Develop and maintain program governance tools, including status reporting, budget tracking, and risk registers, to keep leadership informed and initiatives on track
  • Standardize processes and templates across projects to improve consistency, efficiency, and repeatability
  • Track program-level milestones, dependencies, and risks; escalate issues proactively and recommend mitigation strategies
  • Maintain accurate program records, reporting dashboards, and SharePoint documentation
  • Partner with Real Estate on space planning and occupancy tracking to inform program prioritization and long-term site strategy

Health, Safety & Compliance

  • Support implementation of workplace health and safety programs, including inspections, hazard reporting, and corrective action tracking
  • Ensure compliance with local, state, and federal regulations (OSHA, fire/life safety, ADA, environmental)
  • Maintain accurate records for audits, incident reports, and safety training completion
  • Partner with EHS and Security teams on emergency preparedness and business continuity planning

What We're Looking For

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Facilities Management, Engineering, Business Administration, or related field preferred; equivalent experience considered
  • 3+ years of experience in facilities management, workplace operations, or a related field
  • Experience supporting distributed or multi-site operational environments (corporate, industrial, labs, logistics, or multi-office portfolios) preferred

Skills & Competencies

  • Excellent verbal and written communication and customer service skills across in-person and virtual channels
  • Proactive problem-solver able to manage competing priorities across multiple sites and stakeholders
  • Proficiency with Microsoft 365, Oracle (or similar ERP/procurement systems), and budgeting tools
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to use data to inform decisions and improve service delivery
  • Highly organized and detail-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple concurrent projects
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities and occasional after-hours support needs
  • Working knowledge of building systems, vendor management, and health/safety practices a plus

Certifications (Preferred)

  • Certified Facility Manager (CFM), Facility Management Professional (FMP), or Sustainability Facility
  • Professional (SFP)
  • LEED Green Associate or LEED AP
  • OSHA 10/30 or equivalent safety certification

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to walk large campus facilities, including stairs, and occasionally lift up to 25 lbs
  • Availability for occasional early morning, evening, or weekend support for events or emergencies
