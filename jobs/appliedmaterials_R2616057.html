Position Summary

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented  Direct Tax Specialist  with approximately  3 years of professional tax experience  to join our EMEA Tax team. The successful candidate will support direct tax compliance, tax accounting, reporting, audits, and tax projects across multiple countries within the Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) region.

This is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious tax professional to develop expertise in international taxation while working closely with finance, accounting, legal, transfer pricing, and external advisors across a diverse geographic region.

Key Responsibilities

Tax Compliance

  • Support the preparation and review of corporate income tax returns across EMEA jurisdictions.
  • Coordinate with external tax advisors to ensure timely filing of tax returns and related declarations.
  • Monitor tax filing deadlines and compliance requirements.
  • Assist with withholding tax, permanent establishment, and other direct tax matters.
  • Preparation of various intercompany charges.

Tax Accounting & Reporting

  • Assist with quarterly and annual tax provision calculations.
  • Prepare supporting schedules for current and deferred tax calculations.
  • Support effective tax rate (ETR) analyses and tax reporting requirements.
  • Reconcile tax-related balance sheet accounts.

Tax Audits & Controversy

  • Support responses to tax authority inquiries and audits.
  • Collect documentation and coordinate information requests from external advisors and internal stakeholders.
  • Maintain organized tax records and audit files.

International Tax & Planning

  • Assist with cross-border tax projects and transactions.
  • Support implementation of tax law changes and regulatory developments.
  • Participate in initiatives related to: Pillar Two / Global Minimum Tax Transfer pricing implementation Legal entity restructurings Withholding tax optimization

Business Partnership

  • Collaborate with finance, accounting, payroll, treasury, legal, and transfer pricing teams.
  • Support business operations by providing practical tax guidance.
  • Contribute to process improvements and tax technology initiatives.
