Job Description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

At our  Arndell Park Distribution Centre , we’re searching for dedicated team players to help deliver life-saving medical products to hospitals and patients across Australia and New Zealand. Every package you handle has the potential to change a life—this is your chance to be part of something bigger.

Why This Role?

You’ll play a key role in ensuring that healthcare professionals have the products they need to provide the best possible patient care. You’ll work in a supportive, team-oriented environment that values innovation. If you’re looking for a rewarding career with purpose, this is the opportunity for you.

What You’ll Do

  • Be part of a team that makes a difference  – ensure medical devices and supplies are stored, picked, packed, and shipped with care and accuracy.
  • Work in a modern, high-quality distribution centre  – use cutting-edge systems and processes to keep operations running smoothly.
  • Support patient care through efficiency and teamwork  – collaborate with colleagues to meet the needs of healthcare providers across the country.
  • Maintain high safety and quality standards  – help create an organized, safe, and effective warehouse environment.
  • Grow with us  – take advantage of training and career development opportunities in the medical device industry.

Who You Are

Required Skills & Experience

  • Eligible to work in Australia with full working rights, with a valid driver’s license and reliable transport.
  • Available to work the afternoon shift (10:30 AM – 6:30 PM).

Desired Skills & Experience

  • A  high enthusiasm individual with a strong willingness to learn  — you can have experience in any kind of background (graduates, retail, hospitality, sports, military or logistics).
  • A  team player  who enjoys working with others to  achieve shared goals .
  • Detail-focused and reliable , ensuring every order is processed with care.
  • Motivated to develop and grow , with an interest in building a long-term career in a meaningful industry.

Why Join Us?

  • A career with purpose  – every product you handle plays a role in improving lives.
  • Ongoing training and career development  – we invest in our people and their futures.
  • A team that supports each other – be part of a collaborative and positive work environment.

Work for a global leader in medical technology and innovation.

Ready to start a meaningful career? Apply today and become part of a team that’s helping to make healthcare better!
