About the Role

As a member of the Production Engineering Development team, you will play a critical role in the manufacturing of cutting‑edge semiconductor memory products. Your mission includes improving productivity, optimizing yield, and enhancing the overall manufacturing workflow. Because responsibilities vary widely, we will match each candidate to the most suitable department and position.

Key Responsibilities (Example Roles)

Process Engineer

  • Optimize chemical reactions and operating conditions
  • Introduce new process conditions and equipment into mass production
  • Serve as a subject‑matter expert in specific element technologies

Product Engineer

  • Develop and improve product manufacturing processes
  • Analyze root causes of defects and out‑of‑spec events
  • Implement process enhancements to stabilize quality and yields

Equipment Engineer

  • Deepen understanding of equipment structure and operation
  • Drive equipment upgrades and process improvements
  • Contribute to higher production efficiency and equipment stability

Manufacturing Engineering (Industrial Engineering) Engineer

  • Conduct data-driven analysis to identify improvement opportunities
  • Propose measures to maximize productivity
  • Support production planning and workflow optimization

Quality Engineering Engineer

  • Manage raw materials, cleanroom environment, and production processes
  • Oversee inspection systems and final shipment testing
  • Ensure high quality standards throughout the manufacturing cycle

Training & Development

We welcome candidates without prior semiconductor experience. Our comprehensive training programs include:

  • Semiconductor fundamentals training after onboarding
  • A structured mentorship program for continuous support
  • English language training with professional instructors
  • An internal English Help Desk for presentation and document assistance

You will grow alongside peers in a supportive and collaborative environment.

Work Environment

Our company embraces a global, open, and flat culture that values individual initiative. Employees can easily take paid leave, including for family-related events, reflecting a balanced blend of global corporate culture and long‑standing Japanese corporate values.

Additional highlights:

  • Generally no relocation required; long-term employment at assigned site
  • The manufacturing site is in Higashihiroshima, a growing city with abundant commercial and educational facilities
  • Supportive, diverse, and inclusive workplace

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or higher in an engineering, scientific, or other STEM-related field
  • Experience in high-volume manufacturing operations within the semiconductor industry or other manufacturing industries
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and logical thinking skills
  • Fundamental knowledge of manufacturing processes and operations
  • Japanese language proficiency sufficient to collaborate effectively with internal and external stakeholders (JLPT N2 level or above preferred)
  • English proficiency is not required at the time of application; however, a willingness and ability to develop English skills after joining the company is expected

Using AI:

  • Experience in utilizing AI for work process improvements and efficiency activities.
