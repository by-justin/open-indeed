Job Description

If you are a motivated, energetic, and challenge driven individual who is eager to succeed then imagine a career with us.

PAL Technical Services  is a leader in international aerospace and defense design, development and operation of airborne surveillance aircraft, heavy maintenance checks, and software applications. We provide a wide range of aviation and related services to domestic and international markets.

PAL Technical Services  is seeking qualified applications for the two  Maintenance Operational Controllers at our  St. John’s, NL  base.

Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Coordinate and communicate with Flight Operations, Maintenance Production, and Planning to ensure availability of aircraft in conjunction with the planned flight schedules and the Aircraft Maintenance Forecast
  • Communicate with Flight and Commercial Operations in establishing daily flight schedule adjustments when required to meet operational change requirements and On Time Performance
  • Assist Production Crew Chief and Flight Operations during Irregular Operations (IROPS) or OOS events for coordination and timely completion of aircraft repairs and aircraft return to service
  • Coordinate with the Production Managers and Maintenance Planners in scheduling all technical activities for the repair, inspection, and modification of company aircraft
  • Coordinate with the Purchase and Inventory department to ensure the timely delivery of parts and components for scheduled and unscheduled maintenance
  • Communicate with 3rd party maintenance providers when required to arrange for unscheduled maintenance repairs out of base, and assist Production Managers and Shift Supervisor to expedite the aircraft return to service
  • Provide administrative functions in Maintenance Operations Control to ensure technical dispatch requirements are met
  • Administrative support for AOG bookings related to AMEs operating out of base during IROPS
  • Maintain up to date tracking and Aircraft Defect Control and Handling processes for enroute aircraft maintenance and MEL control within limitations Coordinate with the Shift Supervisor for corrective action at a maintenance base.
  • Other duties as assigned

Qualifications:

  • Must have a thorough knowledge of the Maintenance Control Manual and Maintenance Control Systems.
  • Must possess a valid Transport Canada Aircraft Maintenance Engineer licence for a minimum of 5 years.
  • Thorough knowledge of all types of company aircraft operated and maintained.
  • Ability to organize and plan effectively for the timely completion of assigned tasks.
  • Ability to work and communicate effectively with other departments and maintenance staff.
  • Demonstrate strong interpersonal skills and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to shift focus given changing priorities and time constraints.
  • Proficiency with PC (Word, Excel, MS Outlook) is a definite asset.
  • Flexible working hours including weekends and night shifts.
