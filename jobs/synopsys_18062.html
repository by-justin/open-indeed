Job Description

We Are:

At Synopsys, we drive the innovations that shape the way we live and connect. Our technology is central to the Era of Pervasive Intelligence, from self-driving cars to learning machines. We lead in chip design, verification, and IP integration, empowering the creation of high-performance silicon chips and software content. Join us to transform the future through continuous technological innovation.

You Are:

  • The ideal candidate will drive Synopsys ICV overall PERC (Programmable Electrical Rule Checking) Product Strategies for established process nodes and advanced process nodes to support advanced market needs.

  • You will influence a team of experienced technical product managers and associated staff to drive adoption Synopsys ICV overall PERC (Programmable Electrical Rule Checking) Product Strategies for Established Process Nodes into the rapidly evolving established process node ecosystem due to the rapidly expanding 3D and Chiplet market.

  • You will be driving awareness for the need to consider product reliability for established node ecosystem across all process nodes that are used in a 3D system, including Foundries, Fabless Companies, and IP companies so coaching and explaining to designers how regardless of process node the complexity of designs require advanced reliability checking.

  • You should be able to work with the PMG Executive Directors and Global Sales to not only define product requirements for established PERC node, but their interaction with advanced process nodes as well.

  • You have the ability to influence at high levels, to work with the product management, R&D, and Sales to help expand the Synopsys relationships with key established node industry leaders to continuously move our ICV PERC initiatives forward.

  • You are highly motivated, with a passion for innovation and the ability to work collaboratively across engineering, product management and sales teams to ensure alignment to initiatives.

  • You are comfortable working as an individual contributor on some special projects in addition to helping create the Established Nodes ICV PERC Product team.

What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Support the creation and execution of the strategies for Synopsys ICV PERC established process node designs that align with both the associated advanced process nodes and the overall Synopsys ICV broader business objectives.

  • Be the established node ICV PERC visionary of this new organization within Synopsys EDA Product Management as it is grows in response to the market you will be defining.

  • Work closely with Synopsys ICV product management, R&D and sales teams to align ICV PERC established node needs with advanced process nodes Synopsys product development and go-to-market strategies.

  • Train and Assist Technical Sales to understand the PERC market needs along with the ability to help drive customer benchmarks that result in new business for Synopsys ICV.

  • Train and assist Sales to navigate complex partnership discussions and agreements, balancing Synopsys’ needs with mutually beneficial outcomes for the market.

  • Be an advisor/coach to R&D when requested to assist in negotiating the intent of the PERC elements of the Design Rule Manual such that they can be efficiently coded into the ICV design kit.

  • Drive collaborative PERC initiatives with partners that lead to innovative solutions that are unique to Synopsys ICV PERC product.

The Impact You Will Have:

  • Accelerate Synopsys’ ICV PERC strategic positioning in the 3D and Chiplet established node market via identification and development of competitive differentiation.

  • Strengthen Synopsys’ influence and thought leadership within the EDA and semiconductor ecosystem through compelling presentations and industry engagement as how established node ICV PERC solutions are necessary in the new 3D/Chiplet market for all process nodes used in a 3D chip stack..

  • Build and maintain trust-based relationships within Synopsys EDA to facilitate collaboration and proactive management of ICV PERC solutions.

  • Assist Sales to navigate complex partnership discussions and agreements required to support ICV PERC across the entire established node ecosystem, balancing Synopsys’ needs with mutually beneficial outcomes for the market.

What You’ll Need:

  • 15+ years of relevant EDA/semiconductor product management and marketing experience.

  • BSin Electrical Engineering and or Device Physics required.  MS in your field desired.

  • Deep understanding of Semiconductor ecosystem, and expertise with interpreting and negotiating Foundry PERC Design Rule Manuals to assist R&D to write efficient ICV PERC design kits.

  • Proven ability to work with senior management to help create a product management team that can support Synopsys Technical Sales, product teams, and customers worldwide.

  • Be known within the semiconductor ecosystem with relationships within the PERC organizations of leading semiconductor companies to carry the message of the need of ICV PERC in established process nodes.

  • Can influence within Product Management Group and all other organizations within Synopsys EDA .

  • Exceptional ability to drive and achieve results by yourself and others under time pressure.

  • Excellent presentation, communications, and interpersonal skills.

  • Willingness to travel up to 25% of the time to interact directly with customers and Technical Sales.

The Team You’ll Be A Part Of:

You will be working with senior management to create a dynamic team focused on greatly expanding Synopsys’ PERC product offerings and market positioning in the established node design environment. This team will work closely with Line of Business (LoB) product management, sales, and R&D to align PERC established node ecosystem offerings with Synopsys’ ICV overall strategic goals. As a key component of the overall Synopsys EDA Product Management Group, this you will identify and cultivate new opportunities, drive collaborative initiatives, and promote joint marketing programs to deliver PERC solutions to the semiconductor market that mutual customers need as the world moves into multiple ways to design their offerings using both established and advanced process  nodes. It is no longer just “let’s go to the next process node” but a “how do we best design this” world.
