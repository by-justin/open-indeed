Job Description

Who we are

We're Redis. We built the product that runs the fast apps our world runs on. (If you checked the weather, used your credit card, or looked at your flight status online today, you’re welcome.) At Redis, you’ll work with the fastest, simplest technology in the business—whether you’re building it, telling its story, or selling it to our 10,000+ worldwide customers. We’re creating a faster world with simpler experiences. You in?

About Redis

Redis is the world’s fastest data platform. From its open source origins to becoming the #1 cited brand for caching solutions, Redis has helped more than 10,000 customers build, scale, and deploy the apps our world runs on. With cloud and on-prem databases for caching, GenAI, and more, Redis helps digital businesses set a new standard for app speed.

The Team

We are looking for a Product Manager (P4) to join our Redis Cloud team in Vancouver, Canada.

In this role, you will help shape the future of Redis Cloud for three of our most important customer segments: enterprise customers modernizing mission-critical applications, digital-native companies building and scaling fast-moving products, and teams developing AI-powered applications. You will work at the intersection of customer insight, product strategy, and technical execution to define capabilities that help customers move faster with confidence.

This is a strong fit for someone who enjoys working closely with customers, translating complex problems into clear product direction, and partnering across engineering, go-to-market, and customer-facing teams to deliver meaningful business impact.

The Role

What type of work will you be doing:

  • Own product strategy and execution for Redis Cloud capabilities that serve enterprise customers, digital-native companies, and AI-native applications.
  • Define detailed product requirements in close partnership with engineering and key stakeholders, and manage the lifecycle of one or more product areas end to end.
  • Work directly with customers and internal stakeholders to understand pain points, validate priorities, and turn feedback into roadmap decisions.
  • Partner closely with sales, solutions, marketing, enablement, and customer success to support successful launches, adoption, and business alignment.
  • Collaborate across distributed teams and global stakeholders to prioritize work, communicate trade-offs, and drive high-quality execution.
  • Act as a product evangelist internally and externally, helping teams tell a clear story about customer value, product direction, and use cases.
  • Bring strong technical judgment to conversations spanning cloud infrastructure, modern application architectures, data platforms, and AI workloads.

What You’ll Bring to Redis

Your experience and strengths:

  • Experience in product management for cloud platforms, data infrastructure, databases, developer platforms, or AI-related products.
  • Strong technical depth in storage, networking, and modern software development processes, including Agile development practices and the effective use of AI tools to accelerate discovery, specification, iteration, and execution.
  • Adaptability to the changing role of product management in an AI-enabled SDLC, including faster feedback loops, AI-assisted engineering workflows, and evolving cross-functional ways of working, with the ability to understand architecture trade-offs and translate them into product requirements.
  • Solid understanding of cloud platform ecosystems across AWS, GCP, and Azure, including core infrastructure services, marketplace and procurement models, APIs, identity and access patterns, observability, and partner integrations.
  • Familiarity with the lifecycle of cloud products and services, including provisioning, upgrades, maintenance windows, reliability, operational readiness, supportability, and end-of-life considerations.
  • Strong product sense for enterprise and digital-native customers, with the ability to turn ambiguous customer, field, and market input into crisp requirements, prioritization decisions, and measurable outcomes.
  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

Why You’ll Love This Role

  • You will help define how Redis Cloud serves a broad and strategic set of customers, from large enterprises to digital-native innovators and AI builders.
  • You will work on products that matter to developers, operators, and businesses building modern applications at scale.
  • You will have the opportunity to influence product direction across customer needs, technical innovation, and commercial impact.
  • You will work with highly capable cross-functional teams in a company known for respected technology and real customer reach.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
