Job Description

Location: Malaysia - Puchong, Malaysia

Thales is a global technology leader trusted by governments, institutions, and enterprises to tackle their most demanding challenges. From quantum applications and artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and 6G innovation, our solutions empower critical decisions rooted in human intelligence. Operating at the forefront of aerospace and space, cybersecurity and digital identity, we’re driven by a mission to build a future we can all trust.

Thales established its presence in Malaysia in 1981 and is currently a strategic partner to key customers involved in the primary businesses of defence and security, aerospace, and cybersecurity and digital identity. Responsibilities:

Quality Management

  • Establish, maintain, and continuously improve the Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with ISO 9001 and other applicable standards.
  • Ensure compliance with customer requirements, contractual obligations, and regulatory standards.
  • Lead internal and external audits, certification audits, customer audits, and compliance assessments.
  • Monitor quality performance indicators and drive corrective and preventive actions.
  • Manage non-conformance investigations, root cause analysis, and improvement initiatives.
  • Ensure effective document control and quality record management.
  • Support operational excellence initiatives and continuous improvement projects.

Health & Safety

  • Develop, implement, and monitor workplace health and safety programs.
  • Ensure compliance with local occupational health and safety legislation and corporate requirements.
  • Conduct risk assessments, hazard identification, and safety inspections.
  • Investigate incidents, accidents, and near misses and ensure effective corrective actions.
  • Coordinate emergency response programs, evacuation drills, and business continuity activities.
  • Lead safety awareness campaigns and employee training programs.

Environmental Management

  • Implement and maintain Environmental Management Systems in accordance with ISO 14001 requirements.
  • Monitor environmental aspects, impacts, and compliance obligations.
  • Drive waste reduction, recycling, energy conservation, and sustainability initiatives.
  • Ensure compliance with environmental regulations, permits, and reporting requirements.
  • Monitor environmental performance metrics and improvement programs.

Compliance & Risk Management

  • Act as management representative for QHSE-related certifications and audits.
  • Ensure compliance with customer security, quality, and operational standards.
  • Maintain risk registers and support business risk assessments.
  • Develop and maintain QHSE policies, procedures, and work instructions.
  • Support regulatory inspections and customer assessments.

Training & Leadership

  • Promote a strong QHSE culture across the organization.
  • Develop competency programs and conduct QHSE awareness training.
  • Prepare and present QHSE performance reports to management.
  • Lead cross-functional improvement teams and special projects.

Continuous Improvement

  • Drive Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen, and operational excellence initiatives.
  • Analyze operational data to identify improvement opportunities.
  • Establish measurable objectives and monitor achievement of QHSE targets.
  • Support customer satisfaction and business growth initiatives through process improvements.

Additional Responsibilities

  • Serve as the focal point for all QHSE matters within the site.
  • Liaise with regulatory bodies, customers, auditors, and certification agencies.
  • Perform any other assignments directed by management.

Special Requirements

  • Knowledge of ISO 9001 and risk management systems.
  • Certified Internal Auditor or Lead Auditor qualification preferred.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office and reporting tools.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
