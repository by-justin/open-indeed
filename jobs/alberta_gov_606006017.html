Role Responsibilities Are you a strategic fleet management professional with a passion for innovation, operational excellence, and public service? Alberta Forestry is seeking a Provincial Fleet and Mobile Asset Specialist to provide leadership over one of the province's most critical operational programs. In this role, you will oversee the readiness, modernization, safety, and performance of a fleet of more than 600 vehicles and 200 specialized wheeled and tracked assets that support forestry and wildfire operations across Alberta. This is a unique opportunity to influence provincial fleet strategy, shape specialized equipment design, and ensure frontline teams have the tools they need when they need them most. Reporting to the Director of Warehousing, Facilities, and Technical Services, the Provincial Fleet and Mobile Asset Specialist provides provincial oversight, strategic direction, and operational coordination for the Forestry Division's fleet and mobile asset program. This role leads the technical, financial, and operational performance of the program while supervising fleet analytics and coordination functions. Combining advanced mechanical expertise with strategic business management, the position is responsible for the full lifecycle of specialized vehicles and equipment, ensuring a safe, reliable, cost-effective, and modern fleet that supports forestry and wildfire response operations across Alberta. Your key responsibilities include, but is not limited to: Leading strategic fleet lifecycle planning, modernization initiatives, and long-term capital investment strategies. Analyzing fleet utilization, performance, and lifecycle costs to support evidence-based decision-making. Forecasting multi-year capital requirements and guiding procurement decisions for specialized mobile assets. Optimizing province-wide fleet allocation and deployment through advanced business analytics and utilization data. Developing and maintaining provincial fleet standards, upfitting specifications, and specialized vehicle designs. Engineering custom equipment solutions to address unique operational and field requirements. Role Responsibilities Continued Collaborating with procurement teams, vendors, manufacturers, and stakeholders to ensure fleet standards are achieved. Managing province-wide preventative maintenance and service contracts to maximize fleet reliability and uptime. Evaluating contractor performance, resolving service-related issues, and ensuring value for government investments. Providing expert technical leadership and guidance on complex mechanical and operational challenges. Auditing repair quality, maintenance practices, and compliance with mechanical and safety standards. Establishing operator training requirements, safety compliance standards, and regulatory programs for specialized fleet operations. Partnering with occupational health and safety professionals to promote a proactive culture of safety. Reporting strategic insights, fleet health metrics, financial performance, and operational risks to senior leadership. The Job Description is provided for Further details on this position. About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.
