What is Insights Day?

Our Insights Day program is a two-day, in-person experience at Scotiabank’s Houston, TX office. This selective program offers students an inside look at Investment Banking at Scotiabank through interactive learning, networking, and professional development sessions.

Highlights include:

  • A “day in the life” of a Scotiabanker

  • Interactive workshops and panels

  • Interview preparation for Investment Banking roles

  • Networking with prospective mentors and hiring managers

The program concludes with a Superday Interview for the 2028 Investment Banking Summer Analyst Internship.

For students outside commuting distance, Scotiabank will cover travel and lodging expenses. Attendance is expected at all Insights Day events during the two-day experience.

About the Internship Program

The 2028 Investment Banking Summer Analyst Program offers hands-on experience in areas such as debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, and financial advisory work. Interns will:

  • Support client pitches and deal execution

  • Build financial models and perform valuations

  • Conduct industry and company research

  • Prepare marketing materials and presentations

By applying for the Houston Insights Day, you will be considered for Houston Power & Utilities team

As an intern, you’ll receive in-depth training, ongoing coaching, and access to exclusive events like leadership panels and technical workshops.

Eligibility

  • Pursuing an undergraduate degree with graduation between December 2028 and Spring 2029, ideally within Finance, Economics, Accounting, or Business

  • Available to attend Insights Day in-person on November 19-20, 2027

  • Proven record of outstanding achievement in academic and extracurricular activities

  • A clearly defined interest in Investment Banking

  • Strong quantitative skills focused on financial analysis, accounting, and financial theory

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills

  • A high level of attention to detail

  • The ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously while maintaining a high standard of work

  • Demonstrated ability to quickly adapt to new situations

  • A strong sense of personal integrity and teamwork

  • A high level of energy and a keen desire to learn new concepts

  • Independent thinker and proven ability to make decisions
