Job Description:

The employee is expected to adhere to all Company policies, procedures, and regulations. This position is part of the Airbus Customer Services team. The Aftermarket Supplier Development Manager (SDM) is responsible for executing local missions for Airbus Customer Services supply chain & quality teams, providing the proximity, reactivity, and industrial expertise needed to drive the development of Aftermarket suppliers. You will report directly to the Head of Supplier Management in America, with a day to day reporting line to the Head of Airbus Aftermarket Supplier Development in Europe. You will work with Airbus aftermarket suppliers based across the United States. The position will involve an estimated travel ratio of more than 50% to the supplier facilities. You will closely work with the Supply Chain and Quality managers in charge of Aftermarket Suppliers monitoring. Upon SCQM request, you will assess the suppliers’ capability/capacity and identify potential weakness in the industrial processes. You will monitor supplier improvement (short-term) and development (long-term). You will also intervene on supplier industrial issues to implement containment, mitigation and corrective actions (short-term). You will actively contribute to secure supplier's performance and guarantee customer satisfaction. Training and development will be provided to ensure the required skills and competences are attained. Core Business In support of the Aftermarket SCQMs in charge of Suppliers’ compliance and performances, ensure:

● Supplier Development: Lead supplier development missions & projects to improve aftermarket supplier performance across Quality, Cost, and Delivery. Scope includes all Airbus Customer Services segments, such as Fleet Efficiency, Upgrades, Aircraft Lifecycle, FHS, Satair as well as Airbus Corporate Jet and Asset Management.

● Supplier Audit & Assessment: Coordinate and execute a comprehensive suite of assessments on Suppliers capacity, capability, Industrial maturity & performance:

  • ○ Derisking & surveillance assessments (Transfer of Work, Industrial Ramp-Up, out of production programs)
  • ○ Aftermarket Capacity Management (CMA) for spares and repairs
  • ○ Recovery Assessment (8D, Supplier Events, industrial improvement…)
  • ○ Approval and Surveillance assessments: Support Process Capability Assessment (SPCA) for OEMs and Customer Fleet Services Assessment (CFSA) for MROs.

● Risk Identification & Mitigation: Identify and monitor industrial risks, such as volume fluctuations, long-term support, industrial changes and organizational restructuring and propose effective mitigation strategies to the SCQM.

● Stakeholder Communication: Facilitate transparent communication regarding supplier performance and development plans. Support the development of Customer services operations in America by providing information on Suppliers and connecting with the Aftermarket Supplier Management community as well as the line-fit Supplier Development community.
