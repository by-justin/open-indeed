Job Description

Nutrien is a leading provider of crop inputs and services, and our business results make a positive impact on the world. Our purpose,Feeding the Future, is the reason we come to work each day. We’re guided by our culture of care and our core values: We put safety first. We act with integrity. We are stronger together. We deliver with excellence.

Through the collective expertise of our nearly 26,000 employees, we operate a world-class network of production, distribution, and ag retail facilities. We efficiently serve growers' needs and strive to provide a more profitable, sustainable, and secure future for all stakeholders.  Help us raise the expectation of what an agriculture company can be and grow your career with Nutrien.

What You'll Do:

  • Conduct turnover meetings with off going shift Supervisor

  • Conduct beginning of shift toolbox meetings/safety discussions

  • Validate timesheets for time coding accuracy and overtime reason coding accuracy

  • Verify completion of critical items and ODR lists

  • Schedule and verify operator attendance for external training-OSHA, site specific environmental training, hearing tests, etc.

  • Identify competent SOP trainers

  • Write work requests/emergency work orders as needed

  • Sign off on job certifications for demonstrated skills

  • Perform KPA’s and LCA’s

  • Sign off to initiate critical bypass sheets

  • Perform lockouts as needed

  • Check and read e-mails

  • Active supervision by making periodic rounds to control rooms and field to review work, SOP validation, monitor progress of work

  • Monitor plants with walk through

  • Ensure attainment of shift work schedule

  • Inspect plant for housekeeping issues for end of shift sign-off

  • Complete shift write-up

  • Participate in housekeeping inspections as scheduled

  • Ensure shift crew fall protection equipment properly inspected

  • Attend monthly management safety meetings

  • Conduct monthly crew safety meetings

  • Monthly reports as required

  • Maintain attendance and disciplinary records for crew members

  • Conduct disciplinary actions as required

  • Maintain personal safety action plan

  • Attend day-shift weekly maintenance planning meeting

  • Conduct SOP reviews and crew SOP re-certification

  • Monitor employee callouts and crew unplanned or unbudgeted hours

  • Review environmental and production end of shift statistics

  • Monitor for MOC compliance on shift

  • Conduct tank and containment environmental inspections

  • Conduct PPE inspections periodically

  • Conduct equipment inspections and inventories periodically

  • Perform end of month raw material usage tasks

  • Maintain raw material inventories

  • Order supplies

  • Contribute to SOCL development

  • Conduct new employee orientation

  • Write requisitions

  • Perform performance reviews

  • Perform site specific training for contractors

  • Perform compliance training as appropriate

  • Support Chemical Services during the off shift

  • Monitor LIMS samples and product quality

  • Coordinate shift maintenance activities

  • Stand as a role model for safety by inspecting and operating vehicles in a safe manner

  • Ensure that PPE is properly worn at all times

  • Ability to work unscheduled hours to cover the needs of the company.

  • Be active and promote our SWAT safety processes

What You'll Bring:

  • Chemical/Mining/Industrial background with 5+ years of work experience, required

  • 3 to 5 years of Supervisory Experience or Prior leadership experience with direct reports, preferred

  • High School Diploma or Equivalent, required

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
