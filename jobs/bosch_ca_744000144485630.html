Company Description

🏢 About Bosch Group

The Bosch Group is a leading global supplier of technology and services. It employs roughly 418,000 associates worldwide. The company generated sales of 90.3 billion euros in 2024. Its operations are divided into four business sectors: Mobility, Industrial Technology, Consumer Goods, and Energy and Building Technology. With technology that is “Invented for life,” Bosch wants to help improve quality of life and conserve natural resources. The Bosch Group comprises Robert Bosch GmbH and its roughly 490 subsidiary and regional companies in over 60 countries. At 136 locations across the globe, Bosch employs some 87,000 associates in research and development.

🏢 About Bosch Rexroth

As one of the world’s leading suppliers of drive and control technologies, Bosch Rexroth ensures efficient, powerful and safe movement in machines and systems of any size. The company bundles global application experience in the market segments of Mobile and Industrial Applications as well as Factory Automation. With its intelligent components, customized system solutions, engineering and services, Bosch Rexroth is creating the necessary environment for fully connected applications. Bosch Rexroth offers its customers hydraulics, electric drive and control technology, gear technology and linear motion and assembly technology, including software and interfaces to the Internet of Things. With locations in over 80 countries, around 32,600 associates generated sales revenue of 6.5 billion euros in 2024.

To learn more, please visit www.boschrexroth.com.

Job Description

💼 Role Overview

As a Project Engineer specializing in Hydraulics, you'll take ownership of end-to-end engineering projects while developing expertise in hydraulic and electro-hydraulic system design. This role offers a unique opportunity to work across the complete project lifecycle—from initial customer engagement through successful commissioning—while collaborating with cross-functional teams including customers, suppliers, sales, and technical partners. You'll gain hands-on experience in system design, technical problem-solving, and project delivery, positioning you for growth within a dynamic engineering environment.

🎯 What You Will Do (Key Responsibilities)

Project Lifecycle Management

  • Own engineering projects from order intake through final handover, ensuring seamless execution and customer satisfaction.
  • Coordinate project schedules, milestones, and resource allocation to maintain timelines and quality standards.
  • Monitor project costs, identify risks, and implement mitigation strategies to meet business objectives.

Technical Design & Engineering

  • Design and develop innovative hydraulic and electro-hydraulic solutions leveraging Bosch Rexroth product portfolio.
  • Perform system sizing, component selection, and circuit design based on customer requirements and industry best practices.
  • Prepare comprehensive technical specifications, engineering drawings, bills of materials (BOMs), and project documentation.
  • Ensure all designs comply with customer specifications, relevant industry standards, and safety regulations.

Stakeholder Collaboration & Communication

  • Liaise with customers, suppliers, sales teams, and project partners to define requirements, clarify specifications, and maintain strong working relationships.
  • Provide technical support and customer training, including engineering reviews, technical presentations, and system commissioning guidance.

Testing, Commissioning & Continuous Improvement

  • Support factory acceptance tests (FAT), site acceptance tests (SAT), commissioning activities, and troubleshooting to ensure system performance and reliability.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives and engineering standardization activities to enhance processes and product quality.

Qualifications

👤 What We're Looking For (Qualifications & Skills)

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Mechanical Engineering, Hydraulics, or related engineering discipline.
  • Foundational knowledge of hydraulic systems, including pumps, motors, valves, accumulators, and control systems (academic or hands-on experience welcome).
  • Familiarity with AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or similar engineering design software; willingness to develop proficiency.
  • Strong organizational and project coordination abilities with the capacity to manage multiple tasks effectively.
  • Self-motivated with the ability to work independently while seeking guidance when needed.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills with a passion for technical problem-solving.
  • Flexibility and enthusiasm for on-site visits for customer meetings, commissioning activities, or project support as required.
  • Eagerness to learn and grow within a dynamic engineering environment with mentorship from experienced professionals.
