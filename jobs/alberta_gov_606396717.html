Job Information Job Title: Field Supervisor Job Requisition ID: 87709 Ministry: Forestry and Parks Location: Calgary Full or Part-Time: Full Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary: Permanent Scope: Open Competition Closing Date: October 8, 2026 Classification: Natural Resources 5 Salary: ​$2,073.86 to $2,624.09 Bi-weekly ($54,127 - $68,488/year) Forestry and Parks are responsible for managing our forests, public lands and provincial parks. Our Ministry accountabilities include: • Growing Alberta’s environmentally responsible wood products and forestry sector • Protecting Alberta’s forests from wildfire • Preserving our provincial parks for the recreation and enjoyment of Albertans • Enabling access to public lands for recreation and purposes while prioritizing conservation • Managing public lands and balancing sustainable rangelands and grazing leases for Alberta producers Within the Ministry, Land Operations Division, delivers an integrated approach to the management of provincial Crown Land. The work of the Division reflects the full range of users of the province’s Crown land including conservation, recreation, tourism, energy, forestry, agriculture and traditional land uses. Role Responsibilities We are seeking a dynamic and skilled Field Supervisor for our Forestry and Parks, Lands Division. This critical role requires a unique blend of operational, technical, and supervision expertise to oversee the on-the-ground delivery of our Crown land recreation mandate. Reporting to the district Recreation Management Specialist, the Field Supervisor plays a key role in planning, supervising, and delivering recreation infrastructure projects within a multi-use landscape that includes forestry, grazing, oil and gas activities and protected areas. This is a hands-on position that demands an understanding of the local landscape and the ability to adapt and implement new work methods as needed. In addition, the Field Supervisor is responsible for the recruitment and supervision of seasonal recreation staff. The position is based in the Calgary operations district, which includes the Ghost, McLean Creek Off-Highway, Sibbald Snow-Vehicle, and the Kananaskis Public Land Use Zones. As a Field Supervisor, you will spend a significant portion of your time in the backcountry, managing and maintaining trails, recreation infrastructure, and ensuring compliance with safety standards and legislative requirements. You will be responsible for coordinating and overseeing equipment and materials, managing occupational health and safety protocols, and providing leadership and technical oversight to seasonal wage staff. Your role also involves close collaboration with external partners, volunteers, and stakeholders to ensure that projects are delivered effectively and that our recreation programs are aligned with both departmental and community goals. This position offers the opportunity to make a tangible impact on public land management, requiring you to apply strong problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and creativity in challenging environments. Your ability to lead projects, navigate complex regulatory landscapes, and foster positive relationships with stakeholders will be key to your success in this role. If you are passionate about outdoor recreation and have the expertise to manage multifaceted projects in remote areas, we encourage you to apply.
