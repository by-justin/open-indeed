Job Description

Job Description Summary

À propos du poste En tant que Senior Manager – Ingénierie Qualité Globale et Amélioration Continue pour le portefeuille de produits de postes blindés au gaz (GIS), vous jouez un rôle clé dans la définition, le déploiement et la pérennisation des processus d’ingénierie qualité et d’assurance qualité sur l’ensemble du cycle de vie des produits.

Vous pilotez la mise en œuvre des outils fondamentaux de l’ingénierie qualité, notamment les AMDEC Produit et Process, les plans de contrôle, la MSA et le SPC. Vous définissez les standards, les règles de gouvernance et la documentation nécessaires afin d’assurer un déploiement harmonisé de ces méthodologies à travers le portefeuille GIS et les sites de fabrication concernés.

Vous garantissez également la bonne exécution de l’ensemble des livrables Qualité NPI, en conformité avec le processus et les check-lists Confluence NPI, et veillez à la conformité complète avant chaque jalon BOD (stage-gate).

En tant que référent en analyse des causes racines (RCA) et en résolution structurée de problèmes, vous accompagnez et développez les équipes transverses afin de renforcer durablement les pratiques de résolution de problèmes au sein de l’organisation.

Expert du domaine Qualité et des produits GIS, vous pilotez l’investigation et la résolution de problématiques qualité complexes, aussi bien sur les sites industriels que chez les clients. Vous veillez à la mise en œuvre d’actions correctives et préventives robustes, contribuez à l’amélioration du rendement au premier passage (FPY) et assurez l’intégration systématique des retours d’expérience issus de la performance terrain, des retours clients et des opérations de fabrication.

Vous conduisez des initiatives d’amélioration continue à fort impact, notamment à travers des démarches Kaizen Qualité, des audits structurés des procédés de fabrication, le déploiement de stratégies de Qualité intégrée (Quality Built-In) ainsi que l’accompagnement des projets de transfert d’activité / transfert de production (TOW).

Enfin, vous définissez et supervisez la collecte, l’analyse et le suivi des indicateurs de fiabilité et de disponibilité, notamment le MTBF et les taux de défaillance, afin de soutenir des démarches d’amélioration continue fondées sur les données.

As Senior Manager - Global Quality Engineering & Continuous Improvement for Gas Insulated Substation (GIS) product portfolio, you will lead the definition, deployment, and sustainment of quality engineering processes across the complete product scope. This role defines and implements core quality tools (FMEA, Process control plans, MSA, SPC), establishes governing processes and standards, and ensures all NPI quality deliverables are executed in line with the Confluence NPI process. This position leads and coaches structure root cause analysis, ensuring effective deployment and sustainment of problem-solving methodologies. As a subject matter expert in Gas Insulated Substation (GIS) products, this position leads investigation and resolution of complex quality issues, drives first pass yield improvements, and ensures systematic integration of lessons learned from field performance, customer feedback, and manufacturing operations. This position defines and leads “Quality Built-In” strategies, ensures successful production transfers, and governs the capture and analysis of reliability metrics to drive continuous improvement.

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Responsible for defining, deploying, and sustaining quality engineering and assurance processes across the product lifecycle.
  • Leads the implementation of core quality engineering tools—including Design and Process FMEA, Process Control Plans, MSA, and SPC—and defines the governing processes, standards, and documentation required to deploy these methodologies consistently across Gas Insulated Substation (GIS) product portfolio /GIS product manufacturing sites.
  • Lead and ensure completion of all NPI Quality deliverables in line with the Confluence NPI process and checklists, securing full compliance prior to each BOD stage-gate approval.
  • Serves as a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and problem-solving coach, training and supporting RCA leaders across functions to ensure that structured problem-solving methodologies are effectively deployed, embedded, and sustained.
  • As an expert in Quality domain and subject matter, leads the investigation, Structured root cause analysis and resolution of complex quality and product failures, both at manufacturing sites and at customer locations, delivering robust corrective and preventive actions. Also leads and Gas Insulated Substation (GIS) products which do not meet first pass yield (FPY) targets and secure that the FPY results are achieved and sustained across all models.
  • This position is also to define and lead the systematic integration of lessons learned from Field performance, Root causes analysis and corrective actions & preventive actions for quality, technical issues and associated quality nonconformities from customer sites and at the manufacturing lines /routine test beds. These learnings are translated into Design FMEA and Process FMEA updates, driving continuous product and process robustness.
  • Drive high-impact Quality Kaizen initiatives across plants by leading and enabling plant quality teams.
  • Identify and drive harmonization initiatives across product groups to enhance product quality and simplify Quality control and Quality assurance processes.
  • Implement structured manufacturing process audits across plants to identify gaps and enhance product quality, covering all processes—including routine testing—for Gas Insulated Substation (GIS) products.
  • In addition, the role defines and deploys “Quality Built-In” strategies, focusing on:
  • Reduction of high-severity failure mode occurrence
  • Implementation of mistake-proofing (L1 process controls)
  • Deployment of early warning systems (L2 process controls)
  • Strengthening detection controls and defect escape prevention mechanisms
  • The role provides strong quality leadership for Transfer of Work (TOW) projects, ensuring quality is planned and achieved in assurance mode, enabling smooth production ramp-up and stable serial production through proactive risk identification and robust process controls.

Additionally, this position defines and governs the systematic capture, analysis, and monitoring of reliability and availability metrics, including MTBF and failure rates, to support data-driven quality and reliability improvement initiatives
