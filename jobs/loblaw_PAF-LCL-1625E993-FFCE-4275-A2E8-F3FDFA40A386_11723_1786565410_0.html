Position Overview

Reporting to the Operations Manager, the successful candidate will be responsible for optimizing the daily operational activities of their shift while considering employee well-being, customer satisfaction, and the objectives of the Distribution Centre. Employment Status: Permanent, Full-Time Shift: 6h00 am. to 4:00 p.m. (4 days : Friday to Monday)

Key Competencies

  • Empathy and Interpersonal Understanding: Ability to put yourself in others’ shoes to better understand their perspectives and needs, fostering positive and harmonious working relationships.
  • Effective Communication: Ability to communicate information clearly and actively listen to team members.
  • Collaborative Leadership: Ability to encourage participation from everyone and foster a positive team environment.
  • Time Management: Ability to prioritize tasks effectively and meet deadlines.
  • Problem Solving: Ability to identify challenges and develop innovative solutions.
  • Adaptability: Flexibility in responding to change and adjusting strategies accordingly.
  • Cultural Awareness: Awareness of and respect for the diverse perspectives and experiences within the team.

Responsibilities

  • Lead a team of 10 Operations Supervisors .
  • Manage a shift consisting of more than 150 unionized colleagues .
  • Optimize operating costs while maintaining customer satisfaction and employee well-being.
  • Organize, coordinate, and oversee day-to-day operational activities.
  • Ensure the implementation and management of human resources activities for the shift, including Occupational Health and Safety (OHS), workforce planning, hiring, training, performance evaluations, coaching, and mentoring .
  • Maintain ongoing communication with other members of the Distribution Centre management team to ensure consistent and effective management practices.
  • Lead weekly OHS and operations meetings .
  • Ensure compliance with applicable policies and standards within the department.
  • Ensure the accuracy and quality of short-term operations.

Job Requirements

  • The successful candidate must hold a Diploma of College Studies (D.E.C.) and have five (5) years of experience managing operations in a distribution centre . Any equivalent combination of education and experience will also be considered.
  • In addition, you have experience managing employees in a unionized environment , are an effective communicator, and possess strong organizational, planning, and team follow-up skills. You are also recognized for your leadership abilities .
  • Knowledge of WMS (Warehouse Management Systems) and bilingualism would be considered assets.
