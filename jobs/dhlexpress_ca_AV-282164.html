JOIN OUR TEAM IN BONN FROM SEPTEMBER AND FOR 6 MONTHS

Intern (m/f/x) Programm GoTeach

DO YOU WANT TO MAKE A DIFFERENCE AND IMPROVE LIFE?

BECOME PART OF OUR GOTEACH TEAM.

DHL Group is the world's leading postal and logistics service provider. As one of the world's largest employers in over 220 countries and territories, we look at the world with different eyes. Become a part of our team. Learn how an international network focused on service, quality, and sustainability brings people together around the world and improves their quality of life through the benefits of global trade. This applies not only to our customers, but also to every single member of our team. Welcome with your talents to Deutsche Post and DHL.

Your tasks

You will support our team in the area of Corporate Communications & Sustainability. In doing so, you will be involved in the Sustainability Communication and Programs department. You will take on sub-projects in the international field, among other things, and thus support our GoTeach program.

GoTeach pursues the goal of improving the career opportunities of disadvantaged young people worldwide and thereby promoting sustainable individual and social development together with partners, such as SOS Children's Villages or Teach For All in our more than 80 partner countries. You will support and accompany the development and further development of existing projects both strategically and in operational implementation. We rely on you to play an active role in the team and support us in the internal and external communication of our projects and initiatives.

  • Assist in the creation and implementation of the communication strategy for the GoTeach program including the creation of social media content, campaigns, videos, presentations, etc.
  • Support in setting up the platform and information landscape for the GoTeach program, e.g. Mentoring HUB, intranet pages
  • Support in the implementation of our Mentoring4Integration program (e.g. organizing events, matching process, communication with participants, etc.)
  • Assist in organizing and conducting meetings, events, conferences, and other engagement activities, collect data, and support GoTeach reporting
  • Conducting benchmark research on sustainability and identifying successful procedures/best practices

Your profile

  • Mandatory internship or completed bachelor's degree, gap year or current master's degree
  • Ideally a degree in communication, marketing, sustainability
  • Initial knowledge of content marketing, digital communication & creative writing style
  • Very good communication skills
  • Very good German, both written and spoken;
  • Very good written and spoken English; Extended language skills are an advantage
  • Trained in the use of MS Office, e.g. SharePoint, MS Teams
  • Initial knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite or comparable software
  • Good strategic thinking and skills
  • Team player and organizational talent with an independent way of working
  • Thorough research skills and database management

About Open Indeed
