Job Description

Are you someone who thrives on helping others succeed, enjoys making an impact, and takes pride in guiding customers to the right solutions for their projects? If you’re also naturally curious and eager to keep learning, consider starting or growing your career with us at The Home Depot.

Position Overview:

Askuity is a Toronto-based retail analytics software company operating as an innovative new division within The Home Depot. Askuity's mission is to transform merchant-vendor collaboration between The Home Depot and its product suppliers by enabling best-in-class data-driven decision-making and real-time retail execution.

As a Business analyst, you are primarily responsible for maintaining the integrity of our information systems data, corporate reporting, managing the US contract process and providing tool access to our users. You are a problem solver who has a strong attention to detail, can work with a diverse set of stakeholders to address issues, works to make processes more efficient and has a customer-first mindset.

Key Responsibilities:

Responsible for the day-to-day program operations for the Supplier Analytics Program; this includes but is not limited to:

  • Executing on contract management and being the main point of contact (internal and external) for any contract related questions
  • Providing access, supporting user onboarding, and maintaining user data in information systems
  • Working with product partners and cross-functional team members to resolve any issues
  • Building infrastructure / processes to support program operations processes and reporting

Maintain the data integrity of our information systems and support administration of information systems

  • Extracting data from internal databases and manually loading into our information systems
  • Performing regular audits to validate data accuracy
  • Reconcile data, coordinate and communicate with stakeholders as needed
  • Support various administrative tasks related to information systems

Responsible for producing various corporate reporting across the entire Supplier Analytics program

  • Regularly update KPIs on agreed upon cadence in different information systems
  • Perform ad-hoc analysis to better understand data, support strategic decisions and business presentations

Support project work as needed and work to improve and streamline processes within own function through automation or enhancements

  • Work with project teams to define and deliver on project requirements
  • Identify and execute on process improvements; coordinating and communicating with stakeholders as needed
  • Maintain accurate documentation by contributing to organizational knowledge base and project roadmaps

Basée à Toronto, Askuity est une entreprise offrant une plateforme logicielle d’analyse en commerce de détail qui exerce ses activités en tant que division de Home Depot. Par l’entremise du programme d’analyse des fournisseurs, sa mission est de permettre aux fournisseurs et aux négociants de Home Depot de prendre des décisions rentables fondées sur des données et d’assurer l’exécution des initiatives en temps réel.

L’analyste commercial principal a pour responsabilité première la gestion et la maintenance des systèmes d’information et de l’architecture des données qui sous-tendent la production de rapports et les activités commerciales de l’entreprise. Il répond aux besoins de l’entreprise en trouvant des solutions évolutives en matière de données, assure la précision des rapports et l’intégrité des données, et accroît notre capacité à offrir une valeur inégalée à notre clientèle. Le titulaire du poste fait preuve de polyvalence, gère plusieurs priorités tout au long des diverses étapes de la gestion de projet et a une vue d’ensemble des priorités. Doué pour la résolution de problèmes, il cerne les occasions d’amélioration, peut travailler seul et en équipe à la mise en œuvre des changements, et crée de la valeur par l’entremise de processus efficaces et d’améliorations.
