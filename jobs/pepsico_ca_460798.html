Overview Key Account Manager - Away From Home - Beverages This role is based in Montreal, Quebec and services Quebec customers. Successful candidate must be bilingual. The Key Account Manager (KAM) is accountable for delivering sales and profit targets across a portfolio of Away From Home (AFH) accounts that distribute PepsiCo Beverage products.
