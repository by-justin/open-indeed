Job Description

This posting is for two (2) full-time Temporary positions ending on or before April 30, 2027, and September 30,2027

The Edmonton Police Service has an opportunity for two (2) full-time Temporary Curriculum Designer in our Professional Development Section to assume the following responsibilities:

  • Designing and developing classrooms, eLearning and blended learning courses. Courses developed must employ instructional techniques and evaluation methods that are grounded in the principles of adult education.

  • Conducting needs assessments, formulating project plans, synthesizing course content, and designing instructional techniques and evaluation methods.

  • Collaborating with instructors, subject matter experts and other curriculum designers about the coordination and development of curricula (including learning objectives, course manuals, lesson plans, course training standards and evaluation methods) and preparing subject matter experts for course facilitation.

  • Maintaining partnerships with external agencies and training institutions while creating educational opportunities for Edmonton Police Service members.

  • Conducting coaching and advisory sessions with subject matter experts to discuss expectations and standards in the development of training initiatives. Provide recommendations on the design and delivery methods of courses.

  • Designing and preparing instructional aids, such as training manuals, handouts, and PowerPoint presentations for courses.

  • Designing and preparing eLearning content as stand-alone modules or pre-course work for in person courses.

  • Other related duties. Qualifications

  • University graduation from a recognized program in education, such as a Bachelor of Education Degree.

  • Minimum of two (2) years’ experience working in the field of adult education and/or developing course curriculum.

  • Experience with development of Articulate Storyline Projects or comparable e-Learning Software is essential. Examples of curriculum design products may be requested.

  • Strong skills with Microsoft Office are required.

  • Strong skills with Adobe Suite are required.

  • Strong interpersonal skills are required.

  • Strong communication skills are required.

  • Strong ability to provide guidance and advice to subject matter experts to prevent conflicting training goals.

  • Must be able to work independently and in collaboration with others and as part of a team.

Hours of Work: 40 hours a week, 8 hours per day, Monday - Friday. Hours of work may be subject to the terms and conditions of variable hours of work program.

Salary Range: 21B, Grade 021: $39.16 - $49.22 (Hourly); $3,132.80 - $3,937.70 (Bi-Weekly); $81,766.08- $102,773.45 (Annually). The rates quoted are in accordance with a collective agreement between the Union and the City of Edmonton.
