The Experience

We're looking for a Sr. Production Engineer who thrives at the intersection of live event production, technical operations, and platform engineering. You'll be hands-on with every aspect of our broadcast operations—from pre-event planning and encoder configuration to real-time troubleshooting during live streams and post-event analysis.

This role is perfect for someone who gets excited about:

  • The adrenaline of live production where every second counts

  • Deep technical problem-solving under pressure

  • Building systems and processes that scale globally

  • Working on high-visibility projects that impact the entire company

  • Enjoys flexibility of operating in various production roles

What You’ll Actually Be Doing

Event Production & Execution (40%)

Live Event Operations:

  • Execute live streaming for company-wide events including All-Hands meetings, Kickoffs, executive communications, and learning programs
  • Operate and monitor live broadcast systems including encoders, streaming servers, CDN distribution, and player deployment
  • Manage multi-channel streaming for simultaneous events (main stage + breakout rooms)
  • Coordinate with event producers, AV vendors, and internal stakeholders to ensure seamless technical delivery
  • Execute real-time adjustments to optimize stream quality, audio/video sync, and viewer experience
  • Monitor live captions, graphics overlays, and interactive elements during broadcasts
  • Support Live Event technical operations such as Technical Director, Producing, A1, and Streaming Engineer

Pre-Production Planning:

  • Conduct technical pre-production meetings with event stakeholders
  • Design streaming architecture for complex multi-location, multi-channel events
  • Configure and test encoders, streaming channels, backup systems, and failover processes
  • Coordinate network requirements, firewall rules, and bandwidth provisioning with IT teams
  • Create technical run-of-show documents and contingency plans

Post-Event Operations:

  • Process and publish video-on-demand (VOD) content with captions and metadata
  • Conduct post-mortems to identify issues and improvement opportunities
  • Generate analytics reports on viewership, engagement, and technical performance
  • Archive recordings and maintain content libraries

Technical Operations & Platform Management (35%)

Platform Operations:

  • Manage and optimize Primary Streaming platform including channel configuration, server health, and performance tuning
  • Monitor streaming infrastructure health using platform-native monitoring tools
  • Troubleshoot and resolve platform issues including buffering, caption failures, player errors, and encoding problems
  • Coordinate with vendors support for escalations, root cause analysis (RCA), and platform maintenance windows
  • Manage CDN configurations, ECDN deployments, and network delivery optimization

Incident Management:

  • Participate in on-call rotation for 24x7 support of critical streaming events
  • Lead incident response during live event issues with rapid diagnosis and remediation
  • Document incidents, coordinate vendor escalations, and track issues to resolution
  • Perform root cause analysis and implement preventative measures
  • Maintain incident tracking and vendor accountability (RCA completion, SLA adherence)

System Integrations:

  • Integrate streaming platforms with internal systems including Salesforce (CRM), Slack, email notifications, and analytics platforms
  • Build and maintain automation for repetitive tasks (channel creation, VOD publishing, reporting)
  • Develop monitoring dashboards and alerting for proactive issue detection

Engineering & Continuous Improvement (25%)

Process Engineering:

  • Design and document standard operating procedures (SOPs) for event production workflows
  • Build tools and scripts to automate manual processes
  • Create self-service capabilities for internal customers where appropriate
  • Develop training materials and runbooks for team knowledge sharing

Platform Engineering:

  • Evaluate new streaming technologies, platforms, and vendor solutions
  • Design and implement improvements to streaming architecture for scalability and reliability
  • Work with infrastructure teams on network optimization, firewall management, and capacity planning
  • Contribute to platform migration projects and technology refresh initiatives
  • Partner with Enterprise Security teams to conduct software assessments for new and existing production tools.  Develop and implement alternative solutions when platforms fail to meet security requirements

Quality & Reliability:

  • Establish and track SLOs/SLIs for streaming service reliability
  • Implement testing frameworks for pre-event validation
  • Conduct failure mode analysis and build redundancy into critical systems
  • Drive continuous improvement based on incident trends and vendor performance data

You’re Our Person If…

  • Experience troubleshooting complex technical issues in real-time during live productions

  • Proficiency with CDN concepts, network troubleshooting, and bandwidth optimization
