Job Information Job Title: OHS Advisor Job Requisition ID: 87671 Ministry: Public Service Commission Location: Edmonton Full or Part-Time: Full Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent Scope: Open Competition Closing Date: October 1, 2026 Classification: Human Resources 1 (HR 1) Salary: ​ $2,287.59 to $3,389.59 bi-weekly ($59,706 - $88,468/year) The Public Service Commission (PSC) ensures Alberta has a professional, non-partisan and diverse public service that proudly serves Albertans. The expertise and talent that PSC staff bring to their work is critical to our overall success, as our Ministry is responsible for delivering HR services in all HR functional areas, internal communications, and employee engagement to the Government of Alberta. The Labour Relations Policy and Programs (LRPP) division develops and delivers corporate labour relations and occupational health and safety policies and programs. The Labour Relations and Occupational Health and Safety Branch provide corporate leadership and expertise in labour relations, dispute resolution, workplace investigations, and occupational health and safety across the Alberta Public Service. To learn more about the work that we do, please visit: https://www.alberta.ca/public-service-commission.aspx Role Responsibilities The Public Service Commission is seeking an OHS Advisor to join the Labour Relations Policy and Programs, Labour Relations and Occupational Health and Safety Branch. Reporting to the Occupational Health and Safety Team Lead, this non-supervisory Human Relations 1 position supports the development, implementation, administration, and continual improvement of the Government of Alberta’s corporate Occupational Health and Safety Program. The position works with OHS team members, client areas, Human Resources, Employee Relations, service providers, and the broader Government of Alberta OHS community. This is a developmental OHS professional role that combines program administration, data analysis, technology support, training coordination, audit activities, and stakeholder engagement. You will apply OHS requirements to practical situations, work with complex information and systems, respond to time-sensitive matters, and provide clear guidance that supports consistent program delivery across a large and diverse organization. Roles and Responsibilities Administer the Occupational Health Tracker program: Review audiometric test results, maintain accurate information on employees requiring periodic hearing testing, and support compliance with applicable OHS requirements. Coordinate with audiologists, WCB, medical services providers, and client areas to support workers when a change in hearing is identified or reported, including supporting appropriate investigation and WCB claim management processes. Liaise with Public Service Commission colleagues and Technology and Innovation regarding digital tool functionality and improvements. Manage OHS digital sites and workspaces: Maintain internal and external OHS collaboration sites, resources, records, and working-group information so required information is appropriately collected, managed, and protected. Provide technical support to clients for accessing OHS digital resources and tools, identify commonly asked questions, and develop practical resources that provide clear and consistent OHS guidance. Advise client areas and OHS Consultants on incident investigations, Sodales Incident Management entries, root-cause identification, and completion of corrective actions. Collect, analyze, and report OHS information: Support the collection of leading indicators, including completed orientations, inspections, training, and drills. Analyze OHS data to identify trends and improvement opportunities, make recommendations, and prepare performance reports for leadership. Role Responsibilities Continued Coordinate OHS learning programs: Work with internal and external stakeholders to develop training resources in areas requiring additional support. Serve as Learning Administrator for Government of Alberta OHS courses, advise client areas on required and recommended training, manage courses housed in the learning management system, and administer the OHS Certificate Program. Support internal OHS audits: Coordinate audit activities with client areas and OHS Consultants, conduct documentation reviews against OHS legislation and standards, and analyze audit survey results. Track the completion of audits and associated action plans and prepare progress reports for Occupational Health and Safety leadership. Represent the employer on the GoA-AUPE OHS Steering Committee, represent the branch in OHS working groups, and participate in meetings with other stakeholder groups. Work constructively with departmental clients, OHS professionals, Human Resources and Employee Relations partners, external stakeholders, vendors, and service providers on OHS programs, reporting systems, and emerging issues. Apply sound judgment and practical problem solving: Interpret OHS legislation and program requirements, respond to time-sensitive issues, work through complex datasets, and provide technical guidance and troubleshooting support for systems such as Sodales Incident Management, Occupational Health Tracker, and SharePoint. Maintain professional OHS knowledge: Stay current with changes in legislation, industry practices, and interjurisdictional approaches, seek professional-development opportunities, and obtain or maintain an applicable OHS designation where appropriate. Qualifications Minimum recruitment standard : A two-year diploma in Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) or a related field and a minimum of one (1) year of related experience in OHS consulting services, compliance support, and safety program implementation; or equivalent combinations of education and experience. Two-way Equivalency: The following equivalent education/experience will be considered: A related 1-year certificate from a recognized post-secondary institution and 2-years of related experience. Directly related education or experience considered based on 1-year of education for 1-year of experience; or 1-year of experience for 1-year of education. An OHS designation such as National Construction Safety Officer (NCSO), Health and Safety Administrator (HSA), or another related equivalent, plus a minimum of two years of experience, will be considered as an equivalency. Additional Job Requirement General knowledge of OHS legislation, OHS principles, and the development, implementation, and operation of OHS programs. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong proficiency with Microsoft applications, including Excel, Word, Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, Microsoft Visio, Microsoft 365, and SharePoint Online.
