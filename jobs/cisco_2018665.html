Job Description

Who you’ll work with

We change the world, you will become passionate about your employer and the brand you represent. Everything is converging on the Internet, making networked connections more meaningful than ever before in our lives. Our employees' groundbreaking ideas impact everything. Here, that means we take creative ideas from the drawing board to dynamic solutions that have real world impact. You'll be part a team that cares about its customers, enjoys having fun, and you'll take part in changing the lives of those in our local communities. Come prepared to be encouraged and inspired.

The Country Acceleration team partners with People & Communities (HR) stakeholders and business leaders across India and South Asia to enable business growth through compliant, people-centered HR strategies. The team serves as the bridge between global People & Communities (HR) programs and local business needs, ensuring country-specific compliance, employee engagement, and organizational change are delivered effectively. As the Leader, People Consulting, you will lead a team of two Country Consultants while partnering closely with the India P&C Leader, Country Leadership Board, Inclusive Communities, and the broader HR ecosystem. Together, the team helps shape Cisco's employee experience, protects the business through proactive compliance, and supports Cisco's continued recognition as a Great Place to Work® in India.

What You'll Do

As a Leader, People Consulting for India and South Asia, your role has two parts:

  • Lead a team of 2 Country Consultants to provide country-wide, function agnostic support to all employees in India and South Asia. Specifically, you Provide direction and support to your team in day-to-day operationsCultivate a positive, inclusive culture and foster collaboration among team members and stakeholders.Enhance team synergy to deliver maximum value to the business.Coach and mentor team members for professional growth and navigate changes effectively.Contribute to the overall management of APJC CA teamRole model setting standards for solution first thinking, strategic alignment and proactive ownership and develop your team members against these expectations.

  • Act as a Country Consultant for India and South Asia to drive compliance, change and employee engagement in country.

  • Partner with Country P&C (HR) leader, People Partners (HRBP) and other Country Consultants in India to understand the need and demand of the business to ensure local activities and initiatives match the business strategy in Country.Co-own complex country compliance and legislation requirements in countryPartner with P&C stakeholders (e.g. Country Support, Benefits, ER) to ensure country relevance in global/regional/country programsStay current on local legislative developments and, when something changes or is on the horizon, align with appropriate stakeholders to build a response plan.When global programs are to land in country, identify local compliance risks and cultural nuances, come with a clear point of view on how to resolve them, and where possible, feed that insight upstream into global design before rollout — bring the county lens with a solution attached.   Support execution of cross-functional projects e.g. restructuring, mergers, acquisitions and divestitureSupport critical Employee in Crisis escalationsRepresent HR externally and drive local Cisco brand strategyAct as a connector across P&C, country leadership, EROs etc – bringing the right people together, driving integrated outcomes and ensuring the ecosystem stays aligned.Use AI to reimagine how work gets done, not just to complete it faster.  Documenting what works, sharing it and building institutional knowledge that benefits the broader team.
