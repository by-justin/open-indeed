Overview

The Tableau Success Architect provides strategic technical guidance to our most important Tableau customers, helping them maximize value from their Tableau and Salesforce investments. This role combines deep Tableau/BI expertise with strong communication skills to translate customer business needs into effective technical solutions and architecture recommendations.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as a trusted technical advisor for strategic customers, providing architectural guidance and best practice recommendations for Tableau deployments.
  • Analyze customer requirements and business objectives, and translate them into clear, actionable technical recommendations and solution designs.
  • Conduct architecture reviews and provide optimization recommendations across data sources, dashboards, governance, and performance.
  • Support post-sales activities, resolving complex software issues, and ensuring smooth product configuration and optimization.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams (Sales, SE, Technical Support, Product) to deliver consistent and valuable customer experiences.
  • Build and present customized solutions and live product demonstrations to showcase how Salesforce and Tableau can accelerate business strategies. (Nice-to-have)
  • Manage multiple concurrent customer engagements, coordinating timelines, stakeholders, and deliverables.

Qualifications

Required

  • B.S. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, MIS, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • 10+ years of experience in Technical Account Management, Solution Engineering, Technical Support, or a related field.
  • Strong ability to analyze and structure customer requirements into clear technical recommendations and solution designs.
  • Solid knowledge of relational databases, enterprise BI, and web technologies.
  • Experience troubleshooting mission-critical software applications.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both English and Korean.

Preferred

  • Project Management experience — able to plan, coordinate, and drive multi-stakeholder engagements to completion.
  • Salesforce and/or Tableau certifications.
  • Proficiency in business intelligence tools like Tableau and/or building analytics data pipelines.
  • Familiarity with Python/R, web development, and AI/statistical analysis.
