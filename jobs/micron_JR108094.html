Job Description

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

PCRB & Quality Governance

  • Lead or support PCRB reviews for process, equipment, material, and system changes
  • Ensure conversion activities comply with quality governance frameworks (GCP, PCRB, Conversion Control)
  • Perform risk gatekeeping for Class 2 and Class 3 conversions and track open issues to closure

Risk Assessment & Analysis

  • Execute and review risk assessments (e.g., PPA, FMEA, scenario‑based analysis)
  • Collaborate with SMEs, PI, PE, QE, CPIE, CH, and other stakeholders to ensure risks are fully identified and mitigated
  • Support investigation and resolution of post‑conversion quality issues (DPM, UE, yield drift, etc.)

Cross‑Functional & Cross‑Site Collaboration

  • Work closely with Site PCRB teams, PI, Process, Equipment, and Material teams
  • Support cross‑site initiatives and transfers (e.g., OMT, F15, F16, Falcon) including One PCRB operations
  • Act as a quality gatekeeper by providing independent technical judgment and risk‑based recommendations

Systems & Process Improvement

  • Participate in continuous improvement of systems and processes (GCP, PCRB, Auto‑CPM, dashboards)
  • Maintain conversion review records, metrics, and management reports
  • Support internal and external audits (e.g., IATF, customer audits) through evidence preparation and response coordination

Qualifications

Required

  • Bachelor’s degree or above in Engineering or Science (Material Science, Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Semiconductor‑related fields preferred)
  • Experience in semiconductor manufacturing, process integration, quality engineering, or equipment/material engineering
  • Strong risk assessment, analytical, and cross‑functional communication skills
  • Ability to make data‑driven decisions and uphold quality gatekeeping standards

Preferred

  • Hands‑on experience with PCRB, Conversion, NPI, Transfer, or Qualification activities
  • Familiarity with quality systems and methodologies (GCP, FMEA, SPC, UE/DPM, Auto‑CPM)
  • Cross‑site or multicultural collaboration experience
  • Proficient in English for professional communication and documentation
