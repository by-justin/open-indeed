Position Overview

If you are reviewing this job via other websites, we recommend you apply directly through our website and complete the online application at www.kiewitjobs.com.

We are seeking a highly motivated Mechanical Engineer with strong HVAC design and project leadership experience to join our growing Buildings Group.  This position is ideal for an engineer who is ready to expand beyond traditional design responsibilities and take on a leadership role, guiding projects from initial client engagement through construction completion. The successful candidate will lead multidisciplinary teams, mentor junior staff, collaborate directly with clients, and deliver technically sound solutions for complex facilities.  This role provides a clear path toward senior leadership and practice development responsibilities.

What Success Looks Like

Within the First Year

The successful candidate will:

  • Independently lead multiple projects.

  • Serve as the primary client contact for assigned projects.

  • Mentor and develop junior engineering staff.

  • Support strategic growth within healthcare and federal markets.

  • Establish themselves as a technical leader and trusted advisor.

Within Three to Five Years

The successful candidate will:

  • Lead major programs and key client accounts.

  • Build and develop high-performing engineering teams.

  • Support business development and market growth initiatives.

  • Influence the technical direction of the organization.

District Overview

Kiewit Architectural & Engineering (A&E) delivers fully integrated design and engineering solutions for some of North America’s most complex and high‑visibility infrastructure and building programs. Working hand‑in‑hand with Kiewit’s construction operations, the district provides coordinated design‑build and EPC execution that reduces risk, accelerates delivery, and drives innovative, cost‑effective outcomes.

Powered by a multidisciplinary team of architects, engineers, and designers, KAE focuses on creating high‑performance, resilient facilities across key growth markets including Federal, Aviation, Mission Critical, Healthcare, Sports & Entertainment, and Commercial Buildings. The team supports sophisticated projects in sectors such as government, nuclear, transportation/transit and education—delivering scalable, forward‑looking solutions tailored to each client’s operational, regulatory, and long‑term needs.

Location

For mid-career and Senior, this role can be based in any of the following offices:   Lenexa Kansas office, Houston Texas office, Dallas or Forworth Texas or Lone Tree Colorado.

We will consider individuals with 15+ years’ of Building Mechanical experience with EPC experience, in other locations, with strong experience in one or more of our market sectors., that have experience leading projects and teams, with PE licensure.

We offer relocation assistance to the Lenexa/Kansas City area (our Industrial headquarters) only.

Responsibilities

What You'll Do

Technical Leadership

  • Lead HVAC system design for complex facilities, supporting various contract models from concept to commissioning (EPC, D/B).

  • Develop Basis of Design reports.

  • Perform load calculations and energy analyses.

  • Design central utility plants and campus distribution systems.

  • Oversee the preparation of construction documents.

  • Conduct technical reviews and mentor engineering staff.

Project Leadership

  • Lead projects from conceptual planning through construction.

  • Develop project scopes, budgets, and schedules.

  • Facilitate owner workshops and stakeholder meetings.

  • Coordinate multidisciplinary engineering teams.

  • Manage project risks and maintain quality standards.

Team Development

  • Mentor engineers and designers.

  • Support recruiting and onboarding initiatives.

  • Help establish engineering standards and best practices.

  • Foster a culture of accountability and technical excellence.

Client Engagement

  • Build and maintain strong client relationships.

  • Participate in business development activities.

  • Support proposal development and interview efforts.

  • Serve as a trusted advisor throughout project delivery.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical or architectural engineering.

  • 12+ years of experience in Building design, performing design functions as outlined above for Texas Kansas and Colorado locations.

  • PE license is a plus.  (For individuals looking for more Sr/Lead roles, must have licensure).

  • Experience with HVAC systems including chilled water, dehumidification, hydronic heating, steam, and gas piping.  Technical expertise in tthe following areas highly designers: Cental Utility plants, Chilled and Heating Water systems, Medical Gas Systems, Critical environment design, Lab HVAC, Energy Modeling, Building Automation Systems, Life Cycle Cost Analysis Energy Modeling, UFC Criteria

  • Proficient in HVAC load modeling, psychrometric analysis, ductwork and pipe sizing.  Technically astute with HVAC Codes and Standards (ASHRAE, NFPA, SMACNA, FGI)

  • Must have experience using building modeling software such as Trane Trace 700 or Carrier HAP.

  • Strong understanding of mechanical systems in high-reliability environments (e.g., N+1, 2N redundancy, cleanroom standards, radiation shielding).

  • Proficient in Microsoft Office and design documentation tools.

  • Skilled in reading and developing design drawings and writing specifications.

  • High degree of self-motivation with experience leading and inspiring others.

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.

  • Experience leading teams and projects, which includes budget/schedule/scope management is required for more Senior Engineers.
