Ce poste s’adresse à un(e) analyste expérimenté(e), rigoureux(se) et curieux(se), capable de faire parler les chiffres et de les transformer en véritables insights stratégiques.

English below

We’re looking for a Senior Sales Business Analyst to drive commercial performance and data excellence across all CDI Business Lines. You’ll consolidate business insights and coordinate management teams to deliver accurate, actionable forecasts. You will produce recurring sales reports and performance analyses, developing metrics to track commercial efficiency and identify opportunities for improvement.

As the CDI Business Process Owner for the CRM Sales Pipeline, you’ll collaborate with IT and business stakeholders to optimize tools, processes, and data quality. You’ll also contribute to cross-functional initiatives spanning Marketing, Master Data, Finance, and Key Account Management, helping ensure alignment and consistency across the organization.

This role is ideal for a data-driven professional who combines analytical rigor with strong business acumen and enjoys turning complex data into strategic insight.

Responsabilities :

Reporting & Performance Analysis

Build a transversal understanding of commercial activity across all CDI BLs. Gather information through BOR, KAM, direct inquiries, etc.

Lead the bi-annual SER/OIR processes for CDI (5yrs forecasting that feeds SBP end of Q1 and MYB end of Q3) for CDI, as per Group instructions.

Gather BLs’ input (data and synthetic analysis)

upon request, facilitate synchronization between CDI and regional teams

Build CDI input, incl. split per destination or key account for Management Review

Support additional pipeline / forecast reviews e.g. UK and Noram executive quarterly reviews

Perform recurring sales reporting, monthly with feedback to regions. Issue 4Q for UK, and Noram.

Develop relevant analyses and define metrics to measure Sales performance.

Propose improvement actions.

Process and Tools Management

CDI Business Process Owner of Sales Pipeline

Contribute to Digital Marketing processes (esp. Contacts) owned by CDI Marketing team

Contribute to Revenue Forecast process owned by CDI Finance.

Contribute to Top Accounts Management process, owned by CDI Key Account management team.

Data Stewardship

Lead Data Steward for CDI Customer Master Data.

Contribute to the quality of sales opportunities and forecasts by sharing analyses with

sales management and improving related processes and tools.

Profile :

10-15 years’ experience as a Business analyst, Business or Project controller, or Sales manager.

Advanced knowledge of Excel & CRM tools. Advanced knowledge of PowerBI and Copilot for PowerBI nice-to-have.

Fluent in English.

Think Big: Systematically builds an OI ambition that exceeds committed budget.

Make It Happen: Systematically proposes next best actions.
