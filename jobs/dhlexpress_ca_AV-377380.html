How will you contribute to the success of DHL?

Pricing

  • Implement the Network Pricing Strategy set by the Global Revenue Management group and monitor its execution by the sales organization at regional level

  • Ensure consistent business approach across all market while taking into consideration individual market specifics

  • Prepare & submit for validation Minimum Selling Rates. Ensure their implementation in the region

  • Set & validate the pricing levels for non-structural/Ad-hoc business

  • Create and maintain published tariffs, fees schedules, and pricing terms and conditions

  • Review all negotiated contract pricing requests (BSA’s, lane sector agreements, discount tariffs) validating rates, terms, and volume commitments

  • Ensure rate homogeneity and consistency around the region and avoid cross border selling issues

  • Elaborate Medium Sales Plan - Propose & implement rate increase in case of need (capacity reduction, change of trend,…) and/or promotional rates when needed in order to maximize capacity utilization during low season

  • Maximize the Net contribution by ensuring the close monitoring of the costs involved in transporting cargo in his region and make sure all rates are covering these variable costs

  • Reduce the Gap-To-Market -Understand the market, economic & competitive market conditions and guide the regional sales team to take appropriate decisions

  • Lead regional RFQs Sales Steering

  • Monitor & analyze market information (market intelligence tools & internal info) and direct opportunities to regional sales

  • Optimize the Network Dynamics - Analyse the traffic flows and the flight trend in order to maximize revenues. Ensure ACS sells the right flows on the various O&Ds and at the right price, in order to maximize the Network Contribution.

  • Monitor capacity utilization from his region and constantly interact with Regional Sales to guide their actions: steer the demand on open sector & maximize the revenues on constrained routes

  • Provide tools, reports & overall support to Regional Management to develop marketing and business strategies, priorities, and plans.

  • Drive the process for responding efficiently to pricing requests for country Key Account customers, including best-in-class analytics to maximize margins and yield Allocation Planning & Capacity setting Process

  • Lead the Seasonal Allocation Process in his region, ensuring timely & qualitative submission from its region. Act as the main point of contact with the central Revenue Management teams

  • Periodically review allocation performance and ensure corrective actions are in place where needed

  • Provide the Capacity Management team with traffic mix (origin destination) / overbooking levels / product mix (service and product allocations) on constrained flights

  • Work with Network Planning and other forecast improvement resources to improve forecast accuracy

  • Conduct regular reviews of customer profitability using Revenue/Contribution per m3.

  • Work with Network Planning, Hubs & Gateways, and ACS operations to review and improve density Revenue Optimization Initiatives

  • Lead the regional deployment of Revenue management continuous improvement initiatives

  • Create, enhance and automate pricing tools, reports and dashboards

  • Work side by side with Sales, and Operations in the development of value added products and services

Do you have what it takes?

  • University degree or equivalent.  College degree in a quantitative field (Ops Research, Economics, Math & Statistics) or Business discipline, with strong quantitative skills. Advanced degree preferred
  • Min 7 years in Aviation/Cargo industry in related area of responsibility
  • Fluent in English and preferably a native language from the region
  • Attention to details, strong analytical, problem solving and decision making skills.
  • Strong Commercial awareness
  • Understand the business principles of Revenue Management; including the fundamentals of finance & profitability
  • Strong interpersonal / communication skills to be able to deal with people across different functions and seniority levels
  • Ability to work in a matrix organization, with the ability to persuade others in developing a shared solution
  • Ability to think laterally, come up with innovative solutions and challenge the ideas of others.
  • Strong customer service orientation
  • Results driven
  • Ability to cope with tight deadlines.
  • Knowledge of Access, Excel, VBA / experiences with the creation and manipulation of Access databases
  • A team player.
