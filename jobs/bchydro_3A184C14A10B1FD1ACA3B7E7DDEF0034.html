What you'll do

The 24x7 Real-Time Operations Control Room function has evolved into two distinct streams:

  1. Generation and Transmission (Grid), and 2. Distribution. This role supports the Distribution stream. Reporting to the Manager, Real-Time Operations, T&D System Operations, the Senior Manager provides strong people leadership, operational expertise, and technical insight to support the safe, reliable, and efficient 24/7 operation of BC Hydro’s electricity grid. The role leads a team of operations professionals responsible for real-time grid operations in a fastpaced, high-consequence environment. The successful candidate demonstrates sound judgment and the ability to make timely, effective decisions, while prioritizing and delegating in a dynamic operational setting. A key focus of the role is building and sustaining a highly engaged, capable workforce to deliver on the Real-Time Operations mandate. Some of the key duties include, but are not limited to:
  • Lead the safe, reliable, and efficient 24/7 operation of BC Hydro’s integrated electric distribution system.
  • This is a resident position based at FVO, working Monday to Friday 37.5 hours/week.
  • Foster a safety-first, inclusive, and respectful work environment across managers and IBEW staff.
  • Provide leadership, coaching, and development to front-line managers to strengthen performance in Safety, Reliability, and Operational Excellence.
  • Develop and implement strategies, plans, and processes to achieve operational and business objectives.
  • Monitor and improve operational performance through metrics, workforce optimization, and continuous improvement of tools and processes.
  • Oversee operating orders and support effective shift operations, scheduling, and workforce planning.
  • Contribute to RTO leadership team priorities, including strategic planning, decision-making, and operational direction.
  • Support audit related processes within Real-Time Operations.
  • Lead workforce planning activities, including hiring, performance management, succession planning, and employee development.
  • Represent RTO internally and externally, as required.
  • Provide advice to the Manager, RTO on safety, operations, training, and business priorities.
  • Perform other related duties as assigned.

What you bring

  • The ideal candidate will posses a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering
  • 10+ years of experience preferably within an electric utility environment; with a minimum 3 years’ experience as a people leader in an Operations environment.
  • Consideration for this position will be extended to applicants with equivalent or lesser qualifications with a minimum of 10 years experience in the operations, maintenance or management of Utility power systems
  • Applicants who do not meet the full qualifications but have experience in the operations, maintenance or management of Utility power systems will be considered for a lower-level position.
  • Experience leading and supervising technical teams, preferably within a unionized environment.
  • Knowledge of integrated electrical systems, including transmission, sub-transmission, and/or distribution operations is an asset.
  • Strong critical thinking and decision-making skills, with the ability to lead in high-pressure, time-sensitive operational environments.
  • Proven leadership capabilities with strengths in communication, change management, coaching, and team engagement.
  • Ability to quickly assess complex issues and determine effective courses of action.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to build effective working relationships with diverse stakeholders.
  • Self-motivated, adaptable, and able to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Demonstrated commitment to safety and a safety-first culture.
