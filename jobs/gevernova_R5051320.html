Job Description

Job Description Summary

The Lead SPE (Supplier Performance Engineer) develops and maintains relationships with suppliers to ensure they are executing our purchase orders in accordance with GE Grid Solutions requirements. Participate in the company initiative to expand the supply base globally. Collaborate with all stake holders (internal & external) for sustained annual performance targets across Quality, Delivery, and Cost with nominated critical poor performing Suppliers.

Job Description

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Drive Supplier selection by doing audits (SRG, Qualification and process audit)
  • Perform Periodic & Surveillance supplier audits and coordinate supplier self-assessments as required.
  • Support, Collect, Evaluate and Perform audit requests from the Product lines.
  • Works with Product Lines and Global Commodity Leaders to address critical issues related to customer needs and supplier performance.
  • Non-conformity Management – drive Root cause analysis, effective implementation and sustaining the actions
  • Identify competency gaps within the supply chain process by analysis of Key Performance Indicators and intelligence gathering to develop, introduce, and lead Supplier Improvement Plans
  • Supplier development – Work with supplier / supplier team to improve performance in terms of Quality and delivery performance
  • Develop and maintain communications with all levels of staff and suppliers by communicating openly and honestly, treating all people with dignity, respect, and courtesy.
  • Product development & approval – Work with Global suppliers located in India to develope parts / products to meet the abroad unit’s requirements.
  • Define product / process / documentation requirements and ensure compliance by the suppliers
  • Communicate with colleagues abroad to ask and / or provide clarification on technical topics
  • Provide leadership during investigations of major problems and or special projects focusing on developing working relationships with suppliers and the supply chain about product development and procurement of parts.
  • Pre-shipment inspection – of parts developed / serial production parts

Required Qualifications

  • Expected: Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from four-year college or university; and 14 + years related experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience.
  • Preferred: Previous quality engineering, Supplier quality engineering, product development engineering experience in a Electrical / electronic manufacturing environment is required.
  • Low cost country supply chain experience. Including Incoterms / packing requirements / documentation requirements
