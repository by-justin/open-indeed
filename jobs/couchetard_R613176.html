Manager, Capital allocation & Governance

_______________________________________________________________

Department: Corporate Finance

Location: On site, Laval, QC (4204 boulevard Industriel, H7L 0E3)

Type of employment:   Permanent, Full Time (40 hours)

THE ROLE

Reporting to the Director of Capital Planning & Governance, the position will be part of the Acquire to Retire team supporting the execution of enterprise-wide fixed asset investment (Capex) processes and portfolio tracking. You will play a key role in ensuring capital projects are well-structured, properly tracked, and supported by reliable data, enabling better decision-making and visibility across a diverse portfolio of investments.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

Want to start your career with us? Here's an overview of the main responsibilities of this position:

  • Coordinate fixed asset/Capex project approval processes, including tracking submissions, decisions, and follow-ups
  • Maintain and monitor Capex project data, including budgets, forecasts, and key changes
  • Provide clear visibility on portfolio performance, risks, and upcoming decisions across fixed asset investment projects
  • Consolidate information across multiple fixed asset investment categories (e.g., technology, real estate, operations)
  • Support senior stakeholders by preparing structured analyses and insights
  • Own and improve portfolio tracking tools and processes
  • Ensure alignment between approved projects and financial setup (e.g., budgets, cost tracking, asset creation/capitalization readiness)
  • Support quarterly forecasting and deployment tracking for strategic initiatives, ensuring alignment with business planning assumptions

WHAT YOU’LL NEED

We want you to join our team! Here's what we're looking for:

Education:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance.

Experience:

  • 3-5 years relevant experience in financial analysis, accounting, or budgeting and forecasting, preferably in environments involving Capex, fixed assets, project-based investments, or investment governance .

Knowledge and skills:

  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with attention to detail and ability to synthesize complex information
  • Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills
  • Ability to structure and manage complex datasets, processes, and workflows
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to communicate clearly across all levels of the organization
  • Interest in data, automation, and process improvement (e.g., Power BI, Copilot, workflow tools)
  • Proactive, organized, and solution-oriented mindset
  • Comfortable operating in a dynamic environment with evolving priorities
  • Motivated by complex, cross-functional projects in an international environment

Language skills:

  • Bilingualism required (Advanced French and English) both spoken and written, as the person will be required to work and communicate regularly in English while performing the tasks outlined above. Specifically, the role requires collaboration with global teams.
