About the Team

We’re building the partner ecosystem that powers how Canva shows up in the world. The Ecosystem Partnerships and Go-To-Market team sits at the intersection of product, growth, and platform strategy, working with the world’s most influential technology companies to unlock new value for Canva users. This team oversees the lifecycle of partnerships and developer community that brings innovation into Canva and into the 3rd party tools that our users rely on.

We believe the best products are built together, and we collaborate deeply across Canva to turn partnerships into meaningful user experiences and scalable growth engines. Our work directly supports Canva’s mission to empower the world to design by making Canva seamlessly connected to the tools marketers and creators already use every day.

About the Role

As a product partnerships lead, we’re trusting you to define and lead Canva’s strategy with key AI platforms and platform partners, including Anthropic, Microsoft Copilot, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Meta, AWS, Notion, agent-centric platforms, and more.

You’ll own senior-level relationships, set long-term partner strategy, and turn those partnerships into tangible product and go-to-market outcomes. We work cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Design, Marketing, Legal, PR, Sales, Solution Engineering. and Operations to launch integrations, improve user experiences, and scale adoption and joint GTM success. You will shape the evolution of Canva’s product and platform - including the Apps SDK, Connect API, and other extensibility surfaces - ensuring that it meets the needs of developers, partners, and users.

This role has real scope, visibility, and impact — and is ideal for someone who loves a combination strategy, product, business development, partnerships, and growth. You will own partner and developer performance outcomes, such as active users for partner apps and integrations, partner-influenced revenue, and penetration among customer segments.

What you'll do

• Define and execute Canva’s AI technology partnership strategy, balancing long-term vision with near-term impact

• Lead complex partner engagements end-to-end, from strategy and negotiation through launch and scale

• Foster win-win partnerships that create value for Canva users and our partners alike

• Maintain deep, active relationships with partners, proactively resolving business and technical challenges

• Work cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Design, Solutions Engineering, Marketing, Legal, PR, Sales, and Operations to launch and scale partnerships

• Analyze qualitative and quantitative performance data to inform roadmap decisions and strategic opportunities

• Act as an internal champion for partners and an external ambassador for Canva

What we're looking for

You’re a strategic thinker who loves building things with other people. You’re equally comfortable in executive-level conversations and rolling up your sleeves to get things done. You bring a rare mix of business judgment, product intuition, and relationship savvy — and you thrive in fast-moving, ambiguous environments.

You have a strong product mindset and instinctively think in end-to-end user workflows — from first touch, to activation, to long-term value. You know how to translate partner capabilities into great product experiences and scalable go-to-market motions, whether that’s through product-led growth, sales-led distribution, or a thoughtful mix of both.

You’re able to spot monetization and growth opportunities early, validate them with data and partners, and turn them into launches that drive real adoption and impact.

Most importantly, you’re energized by building things that scale — for users, for partners, and for the business.

• Leadership: Proven experience (10+ years) leading strategic, product-led partnerships in AI, collaboration, platforms, SaaS, and / or developer ecosystems

• Partnership: Exceptional relationship-building and presentation skills, from C-suite to working teams

• Influence: Strong ability to influence across product, engineering, marketing, and GTM teams

• Product and GTM: A thoughtful balance of technical understanding, product sense, and commercial acumen (product management experience is a big plus)

• Organizational: Highly organized and analytical, with experience using data to guide decisions

• Hands on: Comfortable operating as both a hands-on IC and a strategic leader

• GSD mindset: Energized by change, rolling up your sleeves, complexity, as well as building something that scales

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
