About the Role

We are looking for an Expert AI/Agentic Engineer who combines deep hands-on engineering excellence with a strong architectural mindset. You will design, build, and operate enterprise-grade AI/ML and Generative AI solutions within the SAP ecosystem - spanning intelligent assistants, AI copilots, agentic workflows, and RAG-powered applications. You are first and foremost an engineer who writes production-quality code, and you bring the architectural thinking to ensure what you build scales, performs, and lasts. You will work at the intersection of cutting-edge AI engineering and SAP's technology landscape, contributing to systems that deliver measurable business impact.

What You Will Do

AI/GenAI Engineering & Architecture

  • Design and build scalable LLM-powered applications, intelligent assistants, and AI copilots aligned to business transformation goals.

  • Architect and implement advanced RAG pipelines, knowledge retrieval systems, and agentic / multi-agent workflows using commercial and open-source models.

  • Write clean, well-tested, production-grade code across the full AI application stack - from data ingestion and embedding pipelines to orchestration logic, API layers, and evaluation harnesses.

  • Integrate vector databases, semantic search, prompt orchestration, tool/function calling, memory, and context-aware reasoning into enterprise-grade solutions.

  • Optimize prompts, inference pipelines, and evaluation frameworks; contribute to fine-tuning, alignment, and model optimization efforts.

  • Independently design scalable, AI-native application systems - moving quickly from ambiguity to working, deployable prototypes and iterating through execution.

SAP Ecosystem Integration

  • Build agentic workflows on SAP AI Core and integrate LLM-based features into CAP /Fiori applications with strong coding standards and maintainability.

  • Design and implement AI agents for enterprise domains - including formula explanation, computation assistance, and analytical workflows - using SAP AI Core and Python as the primary stack; CAP/Java as secondary.

  • Contribute to the design of ontology-grounded agents that encode business rules, formula structures, and domain relationships as structured knowledge, enabling reliable agent reasoning beyond prompt-only approaches.

  • Architect and deploy solutions on SAP BTP, with hands-on experience in multi-tenant SaaS patterns (MTX, service-manager, IAS/AMS).

  • Knowledge of SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC) - including data products, HANA Data Lake, Delta Sharing, and the semantic layer - is a strong advantage.

Platform, LLMOps & Observability

  • Establish and implement LLMOps practices covering deployment, monitoring, observability, governance, and guardrails - with hands-on implementation, not just design.

  • Instrument OTEL tracing across microservice fleets and build Cloud Logging dashboards for cross-service visibility.

  • Optimize latency, throughput, and cost efficiency - including HANA Cloud query optimization and AI Core inference cost management.

  • Apply SRE principles in practice: monitoring, alerting, incident response, and root cause analysis.

  • Build and own evaluation and benchmarking frameworks that measure AI system quality, reliability, and performance - including domain-specific test harnesses validating LLM outputs against known good results.

Research, Innovation & Collaboration

  • Contribute to PoCs, accelerators, and reusable AI solution ; actively participate in innovation incubation.

  • Support publication of technical white papers and assets; contribute to patent ideation where applicable.

  • Partner with platform, data, product, and business teams to design robust, production-ready solutions.

  • Mentor junior engineers, conduct rigorous code reviews, and raise the engineering bar across the team.

What You Bring

Experience & Education

  • 10–12 years of hands-on experience in AI/ML, Generative AI, Data Science, and/or Enterprise Architecture, with a proven track record of delivering production-grade LLM and AI solutions.

  • Degree in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field.

Coding & Engineering Expertise (Strong - Non-Negotiable)

  • Python (primary, expert-level): You write clean, modular, well-tested Python code for AI pipelines, agent frameworks, orchestration logic, APIs, and evaluation systems.

  • Java / CAP (secondary): Comfortable integrating AI features into SAP CAP Java microservices; able to read, debug, and contribute to Java codebases confidently.

  • SQL & HANA Cloud: Strong SQL skills including query optimization, analytical functions, and working with HANA-specific constructs.

  • You use AI coding tools to accelerate development but you read, challenge, and own every line — you catch bugs, design flaws, and wrong architectural calls in AI-generated code.

  • Proficiency in building and consuming RESTful and event-driven APIs; strong understanding of asynchronous programming patterns.

  • Experience with unit testing, integration testing, and test harness design for AI systems.

  • Comfortable with version control (Git), CI/CD pipelines, code review practices, and engineering hygiene at scale.

Technical Expertise

AI/ML & GenAI:

  • Hands-on experience with LLMs, foundation models, Agentic AI, and deep learning.

  • Strong applied skills in prompt engineering, RAG, AI agents/multi-agent systems, embeddings, semantic search, fine-tuning, model evaluation, and guardrails.

  • Experience building and owning evaluation frameworks for AI systems, including domain-specific validation of LLM outputs.

Frameworks & Tools:

  • LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, Hugging Face, OpenAI APIs.

  • Vector databases: Pinecone, Weaviate, ChromaDB, FAISS, or HANA Cloud Vector Engine.

  • TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn.

Architecture & Platforms:

  • Distributed systems, microservices/APIs, scalable application design with resiliency and fault tolerance.

  • Cloud AI platforms: SAP BTP (primary), AWS, Azure, or GCP.

  • Kubernetes/Docker, CI/CD for AI systems.

Data Engineering:

  • Spark/Databricks, data pipeline design, and big-data ecosystems.

  • HANA Cloud query design and optimization.

SAP Ecosystem Knowledge

  • Hands-on experience with SAP AI Core and the SAP Generative AI Hub.

  • Practical experience with CAP (Cloud Application Programming Model) in Java or Node.js.

  • Understanding of SAP BTP services, multi-tenancy patterns (MTX), IAS/AMS.

  • Knowledge of SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC) - data products, object store, Delta Sharing, semantic layer - is a distinct advantage.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Open-source contributions, publications, or patent filings.

  • Hands-on experience in GenAI transformation, enterprise search, copilots, or autonomous agent frameworks.

  • Strong understanding of Responsible AI, governance, and AI security.

  • Experience with AI cost management and performance benchmarking in production environments.
