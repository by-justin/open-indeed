At Enbridge, we are committed to delivering safe, reliable energy while driving financial excellence and innovation. We are seeking a Market Access Scheduler to join our team!

In this role, you will plan, coordinate, and optimize the movement of oil through Enbridge's pipeline and storage network, ensuring safe, reliable, and efficient service for customers across North America. You will work closely with customers, operations teams, and internal stakeholders to balance system capabilities, customer commitments, and operational requirements while solving complex scheduling challenges.

Bring your analytical mindset, collaboration skills, and passion for operational excellence to help keep energy moving safely and reliably every day!

Note:

  • This is a temporary, full-time role for 12 months with the possibility of extension.
  • This position is not eligible for relocation / international relocation.
  • This position is not eligible for employment-based sponsorship now or in the future. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the country where the position is located without requiring sponsorship from Enbridge now or in the future.

What You Will Do:

  • Develop and maintain daily and short-term schedules for crude oil movements across Enbridge's pipeline and storage systems.
  • Monitor system conditions, storage levels, maintenance activities, operating constraints, and customer commitments to ensure safe and reliable operations.
  • Coordinate with Control Centre Operations, Field Operations, Petroleum Quality, Power Optimization, customers, and other schedulers to resolve issues and keep product movements on track.
  • Prepare and communicate daily operating instructions, schedule updates, and impacts to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Analyze scheduling scenarios, assess operational feasibility, and support commercial initiatives, optimization opportunities, and business development projects.
  • Negotiate practical solutions to customer requests and scheduling conflicts while maintaining compliance with contracts, regulations, and operating requirements.

Who You Are:

  • University degree in Commerce, Engineering, Business, Supply Chain, Logistics, or a related discipline.
  • 2+ years of related experience in scheduling, logistics, transportation, energy operations, or a comparable field.
  • Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills with the ability to assess competing priorities and make sound decisions.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to influence and collaborate effectively across diverse stakeholder groups.
  • Proven ability to perform in a fast-paced environment, manage multiple priorities, and meet challenging deadlines.
  • Experience with pipeline scheduling, energy transportation, logistics coordination, or related operational planning processes is considered an asset.

Working Conditions:

  • Office environment.
  • Stress levels can be above normal as numerous, often competing interests must be managed concurrently.
  • Minimal travel (<5%). Ability to travel throughout Canada and United States.
  • A valid driver's license and maintenance of a responsible driving record is required which will be validated before hire.
  • Work hours include core business hours and non-core business hours (evenings and/or weekends, as necessary).
