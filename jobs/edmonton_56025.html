Job Description

The City of Edmonton is looking for some dedicated people to join us as Light Rail Train (LRT) Cleaners. If you like keeping things tidy and making a real difference for your community, this may be the perfect job for you!

As an LRT Cleaner, you will be a key part of making sure our trains and train stations are clean, safe, and welcoming for everyone who uses them. Your hard work will keep our city moving smoothly and make people feel good about riding the train. This is a great chance to work with a friendly team and do important work for our city.

What will you do?

  • Inside the train: You will sweep and vacuum the floors, wipe down seats and railings, and make sure the driver’s area is clean. You will also hand wash the inside walls and windows and get rid of things like gum and graffiti
  • Outside the train: You will wash the outside of the trains and clear away snow and ice to keep things safe
  • Keep things stocked: You will make sure each train has the equipment and supplies it needs
  • Keep the shop clean: You will help keep the areas where the trains are worked on tidy, by sweeping, emptying garbage bins, and cleaning any spills
  • Help out: You will help report if a train is damaged and report lost and found items on the train. You may also help the team with other tasks as they come up

This job involves being active and paying attention to details. If you are a hard worker who is reliable and ready to learn new skills, we invite you to apply!

  • Ability to communicate well with others both verbally and in writing
  • Ability to comprehend and interpret MSDS sheets.Ability to follow WHMIS
  • Demonstrated work ethic and a willingness and ability to learn on the job and update skills Committed to safety using Occupational Health and Safety and environmental standards and procedures

  • Ability to wear a face mask for a long period of time
