Job Description

Calling all AI and machine learning experts!  We have a fantastic opportunity to join our team in Hong Kong.

Artificial intelligence is shaping the future of insurance. Our latest lab is located in the Hong Kong Science and Technology Park to turn this vision into a reality. As Canada's largest home, auto and business insurer, we're here to cultivate the next generation of technology and talent. Together, we're helping our customers protect what matters to them most through the power of data science, machine learning and AI.

Life in the Lab

Technology is only as good as the people behind it. Every day at work, you’ll inspire and be inspired by the brilliant minds of our greater lab team: actuaries, data scientists, machine learning experts, geomathematicians, meteorologists, software engineers and designers.

Together, you will tackle challenging problems and have the freedom to imagine, research, create, test, and bring innovative solutions to life that will have a profound impact on our business and the needs of our customers.

As our company grows, your career will grow with it.  There will always be an opportunity for you to develop your leadership skills, strengthen your expertise or take your work in a new direction.  Whether it’s leading a new, exciting project or moving into a different A.I. product team in Canada or Hong Kong, your career will always be on the move.

The Intact Lab, headquartered in Montreal, Canada, is a fast-growing digital innovation hub backed by Intact Financial Corporation (TSX: IFC, HK$110 billion in market cap), Canada's largest home, auto, and business insurance company.

Intact Lab Hong Kong is our first AI R&D centre outside of Canada. This expansion into the Asia Pacific region marks an exciting new chapter in our journey, positioning us within one of the world's most vibrant technology ecosystems.

Artificial intelligence is radically transforming the insurance industry, and Intact is leading the charge. Our Hong Kong hub is a fast-growing, agile team bringing world-class AI innovation to the region.

We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join our mission: to become the best insurance AI shop in the world.

Life in the Lab

In our Lab, you’ll have the freedom to imagine, research, and build. You won't just be writing code; you'll be solving complex, real-world problems alongside a highly diverse team of world-class experts—including data scientists, machine learning experts, actuaries, meteorologists, software engineers, and designers. As our company grows, so will your career. Whether you want to lead a new project, dive deeper into research, or explore roles across our global offices in Hong Kong and Canada, your career trajectory is limitless.

What you’ll do here:

  • Use advanced machine learning and statistics to build pioneering solutions for tangible customer and business problems.
  • Play a key role in influencing the AI roadmap for the largest property and casualty insurer in Canada.
  • Transform complex data into meaningful conclusions and deploy predictive models using our modern tech stack
  • Enjoy the freedom to explore and integrate new technologies into our solutions.
  • Share your expertise, mentor interns and fellow engineers, and champion a data-driven culture across the organization.
  • Achieve all of this while maintaining a healthy, genuine work-life balance.

What you bring to the table:

  • A Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative discipline (Math, Science, Engineering, Statistics, AI, Computer Science). An MSc or PhD is a strong asset.
  • Proven track record in predictive modeling, machine learning, and advanced statistics to solve business challenges.
  • Strong programming skills in Scikit-learn, Xgboost, Pytorch, Tensorflow, LightGBM, Pandas, Numpy.
  • Unlimited curiosity, a passion for challenging the status quo, and the ability to tackle vaguely defined problems with a creative approach.
  • Excellent communication, time management, and organizational skills.

What we offer

Working here means you'll be empowered to be and do your best every day. Here is some of what you can expect as a permanent member of our team:

  • A financial rewards program that recognizes your success
  • A dynamic workplace learning ecosystem complete with learning journeys, interactive online content, and inspiring programs
  • An extensive flex pension and benefits package, with access to virtual healthcare
  • Flexible work arrangements
  • An annual wellness account that promotes an active and healthy lifestyle
  • Access to tools and resources to support physical and mental health, embracing change and connecting with colleagues
  • Inclusive employee-led networks to educate, inspire, amplify voices, build relationships and provide development opportunities
  • Inspiring leaders and colleagues who will lift you up and help you grow
  • A Community Impact program, because what you care about is a part of what makes you different. And how you contribute to your community should be just as unique
