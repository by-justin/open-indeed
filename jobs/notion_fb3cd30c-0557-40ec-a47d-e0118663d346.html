Job Description

Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

About the Role:

The Notion EMEA Recruiting team acts as trusted business partners who craft a thorough, inclusive, and curated interviewing experience. We believe hiring exceptional talent, at the right time, is the best path forward to achieve our company’s ambitious goals.

We’re looking for a Go-to-Market Recruiter to support our growth across key Go-to-Market (GTM) teams in EMEA, with an emphasis on Sales. In this role, you'll help shape our recruiting strategy, manage full-cycle hiring efforts, and ensure a delightful yet rigorous hiring process.

This role will be based in Paris. We work from our offices on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays (our Anchor Days) because we do our best thinking and building together in person. We’re looking for someone who’s excited to work alongside the team during those days.

What You'll Achieve:

  • Serve as a trusted recruiting partner to leaders across the GTM org, owning the full recruiting lifecycle from sourcing through successful close
  • Design and implement creative sourcing strategies to attract diverse top talent across EMEA markets
  • Ensure scalable and structured hiring plans with consistent evaluation processes while creating thoughtful candidate experiences
  • Partner with cross-functional teams including Total Rewards, People Operations, and People Partners to drive holistic talent strategies
  • Leverage AI tools, to innovate recruiting processes and provide data-driven insights on market conditions
  • Champion and scale best practices while thoughtfully adapting for regional nuances

Skills You'll Need to Bring:

  • 5+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience in high-growth, technical, GTM functions
  • Fluency in French and English
  • Proven track record of identifying, qualifying, and closing exceptional talent across various levels
  • Experience coaching and advising hiring managers through our recruiting process while keeping talent density as a priority
  • Ability to build diverse candidate pipelines and guide hiring managers in creating inclusive teams
  • Outstanding communication, attention to detail, and interpersonal skills with a commitment to delivering high-quality recruiting experiences
  • Ability to thrive in fast-paced, dynamic environments

Nice to Haves:

  • Passionate about leveraging technology and AI to transform recruiting processes

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
