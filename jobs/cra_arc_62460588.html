Intent of this Staffing Process

The Winnipeg Tax Centre is looking for qualified and motivated individuals who are proficient in both of Canada's official languages— English and French —to fill several SP-003 and SP-004 term (temporary) positions. This staffing process will primarily be used to staff full-time roles at the Winnipeg Tax Centre, located at 66 Stapon Road in Winnipeg, Manitoba. Some of these roles may require employees to work on-site at the Winnipeg Tax Centre for up to 100% of their scheduled hours.

This process will be used to staff positions in Winnipeg, Manitoba only. By submitting an application, you are confirming that you are either currently living within commuting distance of Winnipeg or are willing to relocate within commuting distance of Winnipeg. Virtual reporting is not an option.

Please note that this is a bilingual imperative staffing process; English-essential positions will not be filled through this process.

Duties and Responsibilities

All positions filled through this process will involve;

  • Regular telephone interaction with taxpayers, delivering accurate information and providing clear guidance in response to inquiries.
  • The use of a variety of computer systems and software applications. This includes, but is not limited to, Microsoft Office tools such as Outlook (for communication and scheduling), Teams (for collaboration), Word (for document preparation), and Excel (for data tracking and analysis). Proficiency in navigating these systems efficiently and accurately is essential to performing job functions successfully.

The duties and responsibilities of these roles include, but are not limited to, the following:

  • Reviewing and assessing pre-populated forms and supporting documentation to ensure completeness, accuracy, and compliance.
  • Proactively identifying potential issues, escalating complex or sensitive matters as appropriate, and conducting thorough research using both internal systems and external resources to effectively resolve concerns.

Please note that we are reviewing the duties and salary of the job.

The following pools will be created from this requisition:

When you apply to this staffing process, you are not applying for a specific job, but rather to an inventory for future vacancies.

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) currently has restrictions on certain hiring activities. As a result, any staffing from this process will be limited to temporary opportunities. Permanent staffing may be considered if circumstances change while this pool is still valid.

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.
