Job Description

  • The Egypt Global Security Manager is accountable for implementing and advising the  security program following Global Security standards and direction. May be called upon occasionally to provide security consultation for other organizations located across the Asia-Pacific Middle East Africa (AMA) Region.
  • Provides consultation in identifying, developing, implementing, and maintaining security processes across various business units to reduce financial loss to the Company. Identify significant risks, design, and implement strategies and programs to prevent/reduce loss of Company assets. Achieve risk reduction through increased security awareness.
  • Provides advisory for Travel Security and support company travelers with guidelines on travel risks and protection especially for high-risk travel.
  • Previous experience in remote monitoring and remote access and ability to craft of site security solutions (access control systems, CCTV, intrusion detection alarms, etc.)
  • Following global mentorship, this role will coordinate and implement security operations and activities to ensure protection of executives, managers, employees, physical and proprietary assets while ensuring efficient use of personnel and equipment. Develops and ensures services in response to criminal financial loss, crimes against persons, sabotage, threats, emergencies, illegal acts, and property or environmental crimes.
  • Researches and deploys groundbreaking technology solutions and innovative security management techniques to provide total asset protection for Company assets.
  • Participates in and occasionally advises approach, deployment, and execution of investigations.
  • Develops close relationships with law enforcement agencies, security agencies, and private sector counterparts across the geographical region while guiding development of these same relationships for Facility Security Owners.
  • Briefs senior management on status of security issues. Provides leadership direction to the management and professional staff within the geographical region’s various business units.
  • Supports supply chain business partners in the logistics, transportation, distribution, and storage of company products and assets to ensure security standards are implemented.
  • Carries out periodic risk assessment to identify, evaluate and counter security threats to company personnel, Information, Infrastructure, and business operations/activities in a timely and efficient manner.
  • Performs other duties as assigned by Regional Security Leader relating to advisory and consulting services, security risk & site assessment, surveys and related security concerns and responses.
  • In addition, to the above-mentioned responsibilities, this role will also support Global Security on sophisticated or high-impact investigations and projects, as needed.
  • Provides direction for and completion of Asset Protection Rating assessments for the geographical region ensuring all Company facilities maintain the appropriate level of security.
  • Stays up-to-date on current events to assess changes to geopolitical risks and provides updates as vital.
  • Consults for crisis management and business continuity planning as it relates to security.
  • Maintains communication with Security Representatives at plants.
  • Takes Direction from AMA ME and Africa Security Director

Job Qualifications

  • University Degree or equivalent 3-5 years of physical security experience, including work with CCTV and access control systems, security guard force management, site security design, security and risk assessments, supply chain security, and investigations.
  • Experience and fair cultural awareness MENA Fluency in English and proficiency in Arabic a must.
  • Niche education/certifications in Security management and physical security are desirable. Preferred law enforcement experience
  • Experience networking and engaging with law enforcement agencies
  • Creative problem solver who is outspoken and well-adapted to lead change. Knowledge of Microsoft Office software. Training and presentation skills.
  • Proven teamwork skills – can lead internal/external multi-functional teams and meet externally driven deadlines despite multiple priorities.
