Job Description

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Spares Engineering Engineer,   you will:

Be a key driver in executing and managing programs within the Spare Engineering function — spanning both  Spares Initiatives for Cost, Productivity, Quality  and  Test Wafer management . You will work in a fast-paced semiconductor environment, applying your technical expertise and program management skills to optimize efficiency, control test wafer utilization, align with demand, and deliver measurable results — all while collaborating across global teams and Sites

Responsibilities

Spares Engineering

  • Lead and execute spares engineering programs to drive operational and cost efficiency
  • Analyze metrics and demand trends to identify and act on improvement opportunities
  • Drive programs in a structured and systematic manner, ensuring milestones, timelines, and deliverables are consistently met
  • Identify process gaps and bottlenecks within spares engineering and implement effective corrective actions
  • Develop and present program status updates, reports, and recommendations to key stakeholders
  • Support technical aspects for Spares Related issues and drive for consistent improvement through collaboration with Suppliers
  • Be a category leader leading global strategy for Spares Lifetime, CCRR, Alternate Source and consumption reduction activities

Test Wafers

  • Coordinate with process and equipment engineering teams to optimize test wafer usage and minimize waste as well as fab additional space creation.
  • Establish and maintain test wafer planning processes aligned with engineering demand and capacity
  • Monitor test wafer utilization trends and provide data-driven recommendations to reduce costs and improve efficiency
  • Ensure proper documentation, traceability, and compliance for all test wafer transactions and usage records

Shared / Common

  • Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, operations, supply chain, and leadership teams
  • Travel to multiple sites or Supplier locations as required to support program execution
  • Maintain accurate documentation and ensure compliance with engineering standards and best practices

Cross ‑ Functional & Cross ‑ Site Coordination

  • Enable collaboration across stakeholders including FE central planning functions, fab planning teams, SMAI, OCT and other central teams.
  • Act as a central integration point to ensure alignment across regions, functions
  • Support recurring global forums and programs specific reviews to ensure consistent execution and escalation of issues.

External Benchmarking and Analysis

  • Lead analysis and benchmarking efforts with competitors from Spares and Test Wafer front
  • Identify opportunities for improvement

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or higher in Engineering (Electrical, Mechanical, Industrial, Materials, or related discipline) from an accredited university, OR equivalent engineering training/certification
  • ~ 6 years of experience in the semiconductor industry
  • Demonstrated experience in engineering program management or productivity-related initiatives
  • Strong understanding of productivity concepts, demand planning principles
  • Familiarity with semiconductor wafer processes, test wafer usage protocols, or related engineering operations

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in spare parts management, equipment engineering, or semiconductor manufacturing environments
  • Familiarity with structured methodologies such as Lean, Six Sigma, or PDCA
  • Proficiency in data analysis tools such as Excel, Power BI, or equivalent platforms
  • Experience presenting to and influencing senior stakeholders
  • Hands-on experience with test wafer management, fab cost control, maxout programs
  • Understanding of wafer process flows and how test wafers are consumed at each engineering stage

Additional Requirements

  • Willingness and ability to travel domestically and/or internationally as business needs require
  • Self-motivated and results-driven with a proactive, ownership mindset
  • Strong organizational, analytical, and communication skills
  • Ability to manage multiple programs simultaneously with minimal supervision
  • Attention to detail and strong data ownership mindset for tracking and reporting
