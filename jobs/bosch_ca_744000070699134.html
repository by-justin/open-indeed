Participate in safety analyses (HARA, FTA, FMEDA, FMEA, DFA)

Support software algorithm design to meet corresponding safety goal

支持系统验证和验收

Support system verification and validation

Support the safety assessment

Create and track the safety plan

车辆工程相关专业

3 years or above experienced in the area safety development of ECUs/SW in the automobile industry.

VCU或底盘控制背景优先

VCU or chassis control background is mostly preferred.
