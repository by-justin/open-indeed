About the role

Work Location: Hybrid format: 1 day per week at the office (closest to your region) and 4 days of remote work following the training period.

3-Month Training Program:

  • The training follows a hybrid model as well, requiring up to 4 days per week in the office, depending on needs.
  • Daytime schedule: 35 hours/week.

Post-Training Schedule:

  • Full-time: 35 hours/week.
  • The work schedule is determined monthly based on operational needs. Please note that shifts may include days, evenings, and Saturdays.

Working here means:

  • Accelerating your career within a respected organization and industry leader.
  • Receiving paid training to obtain your Property and Casualty (P&C) insurance license from the Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF).
  • Joining a company where diversity and mutual support are at the heart of our values.
  • A comprehensive salary package with great flexibility in choosing your benefits.
  • Working from the comfort of your home (80% of the time).
  • An inspiring and award-winning work environment that supports employees and recognizes excellence.
  • Stimulating, ambitious projects and professional development opportunities to help you grow your skills and advance your career.
  • A complete financial benefits plan that rewards your success.
  • An Employee Share Purchase Plan that is among the best in the industry, with a matching contribution of 50% on net shares purchased.
  • A flexible dress code that encourages you to be yourself.
  • A Wellness Spending Account providing a base annual amount of $600 to promote an active lifestyle.

What you will accomplish with us:

  • Play a key role in ensuring the best possible protection for our clients' most valuable assets by providing expert advice and answering product questions.
  • Analyze client needs, promote products and services, and propose innovative solutions.
  • Guide auto and home insurance clients on coverage solutions.

In Quebec, our agents must pass their AMF certification to practice. To be considered for our training program, you must meet one of the following requirements:

  • You have a Diploma of College Studies (DEC), a Bachelor’s degree, or a Master’s degree; OR
  • You have an Attestation of College Studies (AEC) in Damage Insurance; OR
  • You have a High School Diploma (DES) AND 3 years of full-time work experience in the Canadian labor market;
  • If your diplomas were obtained outside of Canada, a comparative evaluation from the MIFI is mandatory.

What you bring to the table:

  • A strong team spirit.
  • A dynamic, client-focused approach.
  • Strong advisory and consulting skills.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation abilities.
  • Strong computer skills and the ability to multitask across several systems simultaneously.
  • Bilingualism (French and English) is required: Must be able to handle calls and provide services to English and French-speaking clients on a regular basis.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
