Job Description

The Opportunity:

The Production Supervisor plays an integral role within our manufacturing facilities.  You will be the coach on the playing field, providing leadership and guidance to our team members.   You lead by example and inspire your team to deliver top results.  You are passionate about safety and promote a culture where safety comes first.   You thrive in an environment that is fast paced and have a keen sense for continuous improvement.  Your approach is collaborative and offer a forum where everyone feels comfortable to bring forward new ideas.

Any MLF team member interested in being considered for this role are encouraged to apply online by September 16. Applications received beyond that date are not guaranteed consideration.

Snapshot of a Day-in-the-Life:

  • Ensure daily production targets are met while maintaining quality, safety, and efficiency standards.

  • Supervise and coordinate floor staff, including assigning tasks, monitoring performance, and providing coaching or corrective action as needed.

  • Enforce food safety, hygiene, and GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) in compliance with regulatory and company standards.

  • Monitor production processes and equipment to identify issues or inefficiencies and ensure minimal downtime.

  • Complete and verify production records, batch sheets, and shift reports with accuracy and timeliness.

  • Support implementation of HACCP, BRCGS, and Maple Leaf food safety standards, ensuring team compliance during operations.

  • Facilitate daily shift meetings and communicate updates, changes, or concerns to team members and management.

  • Train new employees and cross-train existing staff on equipment, procedures, and safety protocols.

  • Promote a culture of safety by ensuring proper PPE use, conducting safety audits, and responding to incidents appropriately.

  • Collaborate with maintenance, FSQA, H&S and planning teams to resolve issues and maintain a smooth production flow.

  • Demonstrate an active involvement/commitment to a progressive health and safety program and food safety program.

  • Perform other duties as assigned.

What You’ll Bring:

  • Relevant work experience within a unionized manufacturing environment.

  • Effectively supervises, motivates, and develops frontline staff to achieve production goals and maintain a high-performing team.

  • Strong interpersonal, oral and written communication skills.

  • Strong understanding of manufacturing processes, equipment, and workflow optimization to ensure consistent output and quality.

  • Skilled in using SAP to track performance, report issues, and support data-driven decision-making and strong competency in Microsoft Office tools.

  • Ability to identify issues on the floor, analyzes root causes, and implements corrective actions to minimize downtime.
