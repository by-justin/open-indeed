Job Description

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

＜職務概要 / Position Overview＞ RDA（リアルタイム デフェクト アナリシス＝工程内欠陥検査）チームにおいて、製造ライン全体の品質・Yield改善およびExcursion防止をリードするSUSTAININGエンジニア。欠陥分析及びデータに基づき、複雑かつ横断的な課題の根本原因を特定し、改善戦略の立案・推進・標準化までを担う。また関係部門をリードしながら迅速な意思決定と実行を推進し、Fab全体のKPI改善に貢献する。

Lead quality and yield improvement and excursion prevention across the entire manufacturing line as a Sustaining Engineer within the RDA (Real-time Defect Analysis) team. Leverage defect analysis and data-driven insights to identify root causes of complex, cross-functional issues, and drive end-to-end improvement strategies from planning through execution to standardization. Drive rapid decision-making and execution while leading cross-functional stakeholders, contributing to Fab-level KPI improvement.

  1. Technical Leadership •    Lead root cause analysis of complex defect and abnormality issues (e.g., unknown OOC, high repeat issues) •    Enhance trend monitoring and early abnormality detection using SPC and large-scale data analysis •    Drive improvement initiatives with quantified impact (e.g., Wafer at Risk, Yield) •    Prevent and contain excursions through early detection and rapid response
  2. Improvement Execution & Leadership •    Lead cross-functional teams to drive improvement activities •    Define priorities and execute resource allocation based on impact •    Drive escalation and decision-making in a proactive manner •    Quantify impact on Wafer at Risk, Cycle Time, and WIP to determine priorities
  3. Fab Contribution •    Lead optimization of CPM and monitoring capabilities •    Drive standardization and deployment of best practices across the Fab •    Promote automation and systemization of monitoring and data analysis processes •    Drive digital transformation through automation, rule-based systems, and data integration in RDA operations
  4. Knowledge & Talent Development •    Mentor and develop junior engineers •    Deploy analytical methodologies and problem-solving best practices
