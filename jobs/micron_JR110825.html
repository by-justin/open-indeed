Subcontractor Quality Governance & Strategy

  • Develop and execute subcontractor quality strategies aligned with Micron quality objectives and business needs.
  • Establish quality governance framework, KPIs, and performance scorecards for outsourced manufacturing partners.
  • Drive subcontractor accountability through regular quality reviews, audits, and executive business reviews.

  • Serve as the quality subject matter expert for subcontractor manufacturing processes and systems.
