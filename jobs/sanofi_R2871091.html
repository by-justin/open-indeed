Medical Affairs Intern

Milan, Italy

About The Job

Looking to launch your career at the cutting edge of healthcare? Join Sanofi for a chance to develop with mentoring and guidance from inspirational leaders while helping to make an impact on the lives of countless people worldwide.

As Medical Affairs Intern, you'll be part of a highly specialized team and you'll learn to manage scientific activities related to ongoing projects in Medical Affairs with a special focus on Transplant setting.

We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people's lives. We're also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started?

You will be involved in the following activities:

  • Support the Transplant Medical Affairs team , working in close collaboration with cross-functional stakeholders including Marketing, Market Access, Medical Information and Regulatory Affairs, to ensure alignment of scientific and strategic objectives.
  • Contribute to the development, review and update of high-quality scientific materials to support Medical Affairs activities (e.g., slide decks, CLM contents), ensuring scientific accuracy and alignment with internal standards.
  • Assist in scientific activities , including: scientific literature research , medical consultancies and advisory boards , review of promotional and non-promotional materials in line with compliance requirements
  • Support the preparation and continuous update of scientific documentation for internal medical training, contributing to knowledge sharing and capability building within the organization.
  • Analysis of the adoption of medical KPIs and optimization of structured reporting to enable data-driven decision making across medical CV team in collaboration with Medical Effectiveness Manager
  • Provide support in the coordination and tracking of Medical Affairs projects and activities (e.g., timelines, deliverables), contributing to the effective execution of the medical plan.
  • Support the organization and execution of medical education initiatives and scientific engagement activities (e.g., meetings, events, training sessions), including preparation of materials and operational coordination.

About You

  • Master's degree in Medicine, Pharmacy, Biotecnology or related fields
  • Fluent English (level B2+ or higher)
  • Good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint
  • Accuracy and organizational skills, team working
