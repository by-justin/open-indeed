Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, maintain, and support business intelligence solutions, dashboards, reports, and scorecards using Power BI, Tableau, and other reporting tools.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather, document, and translate business requirements into analytical and reporting solutions.
  • Develop and maintain SQL queries, data extracts, data models, and analytical datasets to support operational and strategic reporting needs.
  • Support the design, development, and optimization of ETL/ELT processes and data pipelines using Databricks and other enterprise data platforms.
  • Analyze and interpret business data to identify trends, opportunities, risks, and areas for operational improvement.
  • Perform data validation, reconciliation, testing, and root cause analysis to ensure data accuracy and integrity.
  • Create and maintain reporting documentation, data definitions, process guides, business rules, and reusable analytical assets.
  • Partner with Data Engineering, IT, Finance, Service Operations, Supply Chain, and Business teams to support analytics initiatives.
  • Develop ad-hoc analyses, dashboards, and reports to support management decision-making.
  • Support KPI reporting, metric standardization, and enterprise data governance initiatives.
  • Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT), production deployments, release validation, and ongoing support activities.
  • Identify opportunities to automate manual reporting processes and improve operational efficiency.
  • Research and resolve reporting issues, data discrepancies, and business data challenges.
  • Leverage AI-enabled tools, automation, and modern analytics technologies to improve productivity and business insights.
  • Provide training and guidance to business users on self-service analytics and reporting tools.
