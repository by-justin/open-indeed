Company Description

Do you want beneficial technologies being shaped by your ideas? Whether in the areas of mobility solutions, consumer goods, industrial technology or energy and building technology - with us, you will have the chance to improve quality of life all across the globe. Welcome to Bosch.

Job Description

  • Lead technical communication with customers Act as the technical interface between customer engineering teams and Bosch cross-functional teams throughout the project lifecycle from acquisition to series production.
  • Customer requirements management Analyze and manage customer requirements and ensure proper breakdown into system, component, and software requirements.
  • Technical solution coordination Coordinate internal development teams to define and align the technical solution for the LCV eAxle system according to customer requirements.
  • Vehicle integration support Support the integration of the LCV eAxle system into the customer vehicle, including system layout, thermal management, and E/E architecture interfaces.
  • Calibration and validation coordination Organize and coordinate calibration, testing, and validation activities (including winter and summer testing) within customer projects.
  • Technical issue resolution Lead technical problem analysis and troubleshooting during the development phase and drive cross-functional teams to resolve issues.
  • Technical milestone management Monitor project technical milestones, identify potential risks, and drive mitigation actions together with relevant teams.
  • Technical readiness for project delivery Ensure technical readiness of the system, components, and software to support project milestones and customer deliverables.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Control Engineering, Vehicle Engineering, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in electrified powertrain development for BEV or FCEV applications, with strong exposure to electric drive systems and power electronics.
  • Solid understanding of electric drive system architecture, including multi-in-one systems (inverter/DCAC, DC/DC converter, OBC), MCU, motor control, and vehicle E/E interfaces.
  • Familiar with motor control strategies, power electronics control, and integration of electrified powertrain systems.
  • Familiar with automotive quality management systems and core quality tools, including APQP, FMEA, SPC, MSA, and PPAP.
  • Strong technical leadership capability, good teamwork spirit, and high sense of responsibility.
  • Good command of English in both written and spoken communication for technical discussions with international teams and customers.
  • Experience in OEM customer projects or technical coordination in electrified powertrain development is preferred.
