À propos de Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (P&WC) est un leader mondial de l’innovation aérospatiale, dont le siège social est situé à Longueuil, au Québec, depuis 1928. Nous concevons et fabriquons des moteurs d’aéronefs de nouvelle génération qui propulsent la plus grande flotte mondiale d’avions et d’hélicoptères dans les segments de l’aviation d’affaires, de l’aviation générale et de l’aviation régionale.

Depuis près de 100 ans, nos équipes sont à l’avant-garde des technologies de propulsion, repoussant les limites afin de rendre nos moteurs plus performants et fiables. Nos moteurs soutiennent des missions essentielles, du transport de passagers et de marchandises aux services médicaux d’urgence, aux opérations humanitaires et à la lutte contre les feux de forêt, partout dans le monde.

Un employeur de choix

Pratt & Whitney Canada a été reconnue comme l’un des meilleurs employeurs au Canada en 2026 selon Forbes, et ce, pour une 11e année consécutive. L’entreprise s’est également classée au premier rang des employeurs de l’industrie de l’aérospatiale et de la défense au pays. De plus, notre siège social figure toujours parmi les meilleurs employeurs de la région de Montréal. Ensemble, ces distinctions confirment notre réputation d’employeur de choix à Montréal, au Canada et à l’échelle mondiale.

Pourquoi rejoindre notre équipe ?

En tant que leader de l'organisation, le ou la Gestionnaire, Approvisionnement et Logistique, GAL est responsable de la gestion des approvisionnements en pièces de rechanges, la gestion des fournisseurs, de l’inventaire ainsi que de la planification des opérations de l’usine. Le ou la GAL s’assure du bon déroulement des activités quotidiennes notamment, celles assurant le respect des normes et processus, la production et l’efficacité opérationnelle, la qualité des produits, les activités de conformité ainsi que le respect des objectifs de coûts et budget opérationnel.

Le poste s’effectue à notre établissement de St-Hubert , du lundi au vendredi, selon des horaires flexibles et exclusivement en présentiel.

Job Title: Senior Manager, Supply Management and Logistics

About Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (P&WC) is a global leader in aerospace innovation, proudly headquartered in Longueuil, Quebec, since 1928. We design and manufacture next-generation aircraft engines that power the world’s largest fleet of business, general aviation, and regional aircraft and helicopters.

For nearly 100 years, our teams have been at the forefront of propulsion technology, pushing boundaries to make flight more efficient and reliable. Our engines support missions that truly matter, from transporting people and essential goods, to enabling emergency medical services, humanitarian operations, and wildfire suppression around the world.

An Employer of Choice

Pratt & Whitney Canada was named one of Canada’s Best Employers for 2026 by Forbes, marking the 11th consecutive year the company has earned this recognition. Pratt & Whitney Canada also ranked #1 employer in the aerospace and defense industry in the country. In addition, our headquarters continues to be recognized among the top employers in the Montreal region. Together, these distinctions reinforce our reputation as an employer of choice in Montreal, across Canada, and around the world.

Why join our team?

As the leader of the organization, the Manager, Supply Management and Logistics, MSL is responsible for the supply of spare parts, the management of the suppliers, the inventory as well as the planning of the operations of Saint-Hubert’s plant. The MSL ensures the smooth running of daily activities, in particular those ensuring compliance with standards and processes, production and operational efficiency, product quality, compliance activities as well as compliance with cost and operational budget.

The position is based at our St-Hubert location, from Monday to Friday, with flexible hours and exclusively on-site.

What will your day-to-day look like?

  • Responsible for the material procurement and management of repair suppliers team at the Saint-Hubert plant.
  • Lead strategic initiatives for the supply of spare materials;
  • Establish solid and trusting relationships with our various internal and external partners;
  • Monitor supplier performance by using relevant KPIs and define required improvement strategies;
  • Implement best practices in performance management and professional development of team members, in particular by acting as a coach and mentor;
  • Work in collaboration with operations in the development of delivery objectives;
  • Ensure alignment of priorities and their communication across the various entities;
  • Collaborate with other functional organizations to develop solutions and ensure that supplier performance meets the needs of the plant organization;
  • Provide a world-class employee experience;
  • Provide a world-class customer experience;
  • Understand the context of our customers to personalize our offer;
  • Provide the highest value, highest quality, on-time repair and overhaul services and solutions;
  • Make health and safety a priority in everything we do and protect the environment in which we operate;
  • Ensure commitment to compliance - Ethics, SOX, EH&S, ITC and Quality;
  • Achieve targeted business plans on sales, EBIT, ROS, inventory turnover and cash flow;
  • Monitor and manage inventory to reduce financial exposure, support working capital efficiency, and identify redistribution or utilization opportunities across the P&WC network;
  • Lead continuous improvement projects focused on operational efficiency, process standardization, and performance optimization across the MRO facility, leveraging Lean, Kaizen, or Six Sigma methodologies;
  • Contribute to the development of a strategic vision for the department (1–5 years);
  • Achieve ambitious goals to capitalize on growth opportunities;
  • Promote innovation and industrialize new technologies;
  • Drive operational excellence and production efficiency through continuous improvement.

What do you need to be successful?

  • Bachelor's degree in logistics, supply or business management with more than 5 years of management experience; MBA or other advanced management degree preferred
  • Experience in a leadership role and ability to be promoted through demonstrated performance and proven ability
  • Demonstrated success in supply management, with excellent interpersonal and organizational skills
  • Proven ability to lead projects in the following areas impacting operations: IT, Artificial Intelligence, HR, Quality, etc.
  • This position is part of a French-speaking work environment, it requires a very good knowledge of English to read and understand, on a daily basis, documentation and technical terms that may emanate from international organizations, as well as to write and communicate in English with customers, suppliers or international colleagues.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of Lean transformation principles and an understanding of turbine engine assembly, testing and machining processes are assets.

  • Merit or recognition program
  • Health and wellness program, including virtual health care
