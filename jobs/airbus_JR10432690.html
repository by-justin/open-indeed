Job Description:

Airbus US Manufacturing Facility is looking for a Manufacturing Engineer II - RVP to join our  A320 Engineering  Department based in Mobile, AL.

Responsible for the manufacturing assessment of non-conformities, late changes to support production, and supporting and identifying continuous improvement opportunities for the manufacturing process . Responds to technical questions and explains work orders/drawings, especially for aircraft mechanical related items. Establishes and maintains effective communications with FAL production and serves as interface to the on site back office for work order adaptation.

Meet the team:

Our Engineers at the Airbus U.S. Manufacturing facility take our aircraft from concept to customer delivery – working with production teams on every aspect of assembly from managing technical information, quality control, maintenance tasks and health & safety – along flowlines and flightlines. Join our team and watch our aircraft come to life.

Your working environment:

Nearby on ‘Airbus Way’ you will find the Airbus U.S. Manufacturing Facility for commercial aircraft. Opened at the Mobile Aeroplex at Brookley in 2015, the facility produces A320 Family aircraft and added a second product line for the A220 in 2019. The facility, which strengthens the U.S. aerospace industry, is part of Airbus’ strategy to enhance its global competitiveness by meeting the growing needs of its customers in the United States and beyond.

Your challenges:

  • Create specific work orders for FAL rework (Cabin, Systems, Electrics, or Structures by Station) , establish/verify the technical content of non-conformities, and validate in documentation.
  • Provide technical support to production on all issues including oversight of directed changes.
  • Responsible for incorporating implicit knowledge into work instructions and refine them in terms of language, level of detail and regulation assignment to ensure operations has correct information for aircraft build.
  • Initiate availability of replacement parts, jigs, and tools required for work
  • Serves as focal point for complex topics and applies knowledge and problem solving to resolve issues.
  • Manage continuous improvement measures with reoccurring non-conformities in cooperation with quality.
  • Participate as an active team member to support ME activities in the FAL.
  • Creates and updates KPIs for the team on assigned projects.​
  • Communicates with European colleagues at other manufacturing sites for guidance on repeat or complex issues.
  • Participate as an active team member to support ME activities in the FAL.
  • Participate as an active team member learning and following dedicated training plans from current team members. including providing orientation support to new colleagues and trainees.
  • Provides training and guidance to new team members.
  • Acts as representative and leader for ME TSFS in projects and meetings as required.
  • Delegate for HO ME TSFS as required.
  • Additional projects as assigned.
  • Other duties as assigned.
