Senior Developers are responsible for server-side development of next generation products and services. The role handles all the development tasks of building services, system interfaces, and/or user interfaces

What you will do:

  • Responsible for the technical / systems analysis, design, development, unit testing, system integration testing, implementation and support of software applications, using existing and emerging technology platforms. Role includes custodian of the established architecture assuring that documented standards and procedures are adhered to and ensure the smooth interpretability of the applications.

  • Collaborating with IT Platform Management, work with the business/BSA to obtain the necessary clarity of the requirements at a function point context. Work with project managers, sponsors and other stakeholders to develop and plan technical strategies, agree upon project priorities and deliver applications that support business requirements. Work with the front end developers, server-side developers and tech lead to design and build API services. Also responsible for the Systems integration testing of applications prior to code release to Quality Assurance.

  • Responsible for writing new systems of moderate complexity, working with basic application designs and specifications, utilizing standard procedures and techniques.

  • Lead and Coordinate the development of design requirements, designs, unit and system integration tests, perform code reviews, document and implements systems and programs.

  • Provides application and user support, troubleshooting, application testing, system integration testing and post-implementation review of new/changed applications. Reviews release notes and other relevant documentation.

  • Applications Development professionals are responsible for working with their managers to assure their own continuing professional development for both technical and soft skills and that of their teams.  At the more senior levels, application developers are expected to train others, participate in company and industry working groups, and encourage learning, sharing culture that supports development of innovative and effective business solutions.

  • Applications Development professionals will develop a thorough understanding of the businesses they support along with well-developed people and project management skills. They may apply these skills either within a narrow business focus or broadly across the firm.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
