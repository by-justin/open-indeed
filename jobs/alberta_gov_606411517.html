Job Information Job Title: Manager, Provincial Grant Programs Job Requisition ID: 87604 Ministry: Transportation and Economic Corridors Location: Edmonton Full or Part-Time: Full Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent Scope: Open Competition Closing Date: October 7, 2026 Classification: Manager Zone 2 Salary: $3,920.66 to $5,276.07 bi-weekly ($102,329 - $137,705 /year) The Alberta Public Service works to build a stronger province for current and future generations. We make a difference in the lives of Albertans through rewarding and diverse career opportunities. Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors (TEC) is a centre of excellence for transportation in North America. We contribute to Alberta's prosperity and quality of life by providing and supporting a safe, innovative and sustainable provincial transportation system and water management infrastructure. With Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors, you can engage in a fulfilling career with diverse room to grow. We believe in building careers, providing opportunities for growth within the organization, and striving to provide our staff with a work-life balance including reasonable hours of work to ensure time with family and to accommodate outside interests. The ministry promotes a vital and diverse economy by: Managing transportation safety. Supporting municipalities with public transportation and water/wastewater facilities. Expanding and enhancing a well-integrated transportation system and enabling market access. Preserving and developing the provincial highway network and water management infrastructure. For more information, visit https://www.alberta.ca/transportation-and-economic-corridors . Role Responsibilities As the Manager, Provincial Grant Programs, you will: Lead and coach a team responsible for delivering provincial infrastructure grant programs, fostering a high-performing, collaborative, and inclusive work environment. Manage recruitment, onboarding, performance development, and succession planning to build and retain a skilled workforce. Oversee unit operations, budgets, and resources to ensure priorities are delivered effectively and aligned with branch objectives. Provide financial oversight of grant programs, including funding commitments, expenditures, forecasts and financial pressures, to support effective stewardship of public funds. Oversee program performance, compliance and risk, ensuring grants are administered in accordance with approved policies, program guidelines, funding agreements and government requirements. Provide strategic leadership and oversight for the development, implementation and administration of provincial infrastructure grant programs, including water, wastewater, local roads and bridges, community airports, and other funding initiatives. Build and maintain strong relationships with municipalities, water commissions, First Nations communities, Metis Settlements, and other stakeholders to support program delivery and funding decisions. Serve as a key point of contact for grant program initiatives, providing advice, analysis, and recommendations on funding opportunities and municipal priorities. Represent the department on internal, cross-ministry, and external committees, contributing to program development, operational improvements, and policy discussions. Lead the development of briefing materials, correspondence, funding recommendations, research reports, and responses to issues and inquiries. About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
