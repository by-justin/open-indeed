Job title: KAM Trade and ecommerceLocation – Chennai

About the job

Our Team:

Trade distributer contribution to Sanofi business in terms of reach and penetration is significant.Building long term and sustainable partnership model with trade distributers for delivering volume growth for Sanofi brands is critical moving forward.

About Sanofi:

Sanofi is an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Now, with its Take the Lead Strategy the objective is to make a greater impact on patients, healthcare systems, and society at large scale. This means (i)  pioneering an innovative pipeline and executing transformative launches at scale to bring life-changing new medicines and vaccines to more people, more quickly (ii) modernizing and deploying resources with agility (iii) building a sustainable business that tackles the most urgent healthcare, environmental, and societal challenges of our time

Sanofi India  is implementing an ambitious transformation plan  aligned with our Take the Lead Strategy and Values,  with 3 main objectives (i) achieve our target growth for the Diabetes portfolio in Public and Private market (ii) Foster empowerment and autonomy at customer level to create a customer centricity approach (iii) Implement  contemporary ways of working for better efficiency

Main responsibilities:

Build long lasting relationships with top 650 key trade distributers (70% business contribution approx. 1800 Cr) and include them as partners in growth journey. Deliver growth with focussed approach. Improve efficiency.

1.Trade Strategy Implementation and Coordination

Drive & ensure sales delivery by trade distributers for Sanofi Rx brands. Execute the value-added activation plans for enhancing reach and penetration through trade distributers of Sanofi Rx brands. Scaleup retail coverage through digital connect and drive incremental opportunities to accelerate sales growth for targeted Rx brands. Identify opportunities to innovate and drive first competitive advantage for Sanofi and the customers. Define ways of working, engaging cross functionally, to achieve agility in trade practices and Sanofi processes.

2.Customer Insights & Planning

Monitoring Top distributers Secondary sales data before month closing to develop actionable insights & build accountability for secondary sales demand generation. Engage with new retail/ wholesale and adopt new practices future ready. Contribute to detect emerging opportunities and needs in the market. Develop Trade Distributer account management Capability (Insights, Digital and Content, Data Analytics, etc.…), Embed new practices in ways of working. Maintaining optimal SIT days >=24 days Work effectively with Trade marketing and Channel management to accelerate growth and profitability.

3.Impact & Influence Trade Channels

Improve efficiency in terms of Service – Ontime delivery, settlement of claims, payment realization Stock availability – All SKU’s availability and optimal SIT days New product launches – Acceleration of distribution curve and geographical reach Reducing breakage & expiry – Maintaining desired ratio of <0.5% vs sales volumes Develop powerful content and experiences that will generate value for our customers by using retail platforms like Retail IO and Pharmarack. Sales Marketing Experience of 8+ years with exposure to different geographies and Experience of distributer / wholesale management, digital marketing would be preferred. Worked in leadership roles with an experience of analytics for 2-3 years will be an added advantage.
