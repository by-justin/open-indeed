DHL Express currently has an opening for a Casual Warehouse position in our Edmonton, AB location.

Shift: Must have availability from Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm. There are no set shifts for this role

Wage: $23.38/hour and will increase as per the CBA

How will you contribute?

  • Complete assigned deliveries and pick ups and/or other tasks assigned by supervisor
  • Vehicle pre-trip and post-trip inspections
  • Scan freight as per agreed procedures
  • Organize and sort all freight to appropriate routes
  • Move freight within the warehouse area as per requirements
  • Accurately sort & process, by deadlines, in-bound material.
  • Assist Tracing Department with thorough warehouse checks for missorts, on-hands, etc.
  • Participate in the organization and clean-up of the warehouse area.
  • Participate in maintaining a safe and co-operative work environment
  • Assist Tracing Department with thorough warehouse checks for missorts, on-hands, etc.
  • Perform Front Counter Duties
  • Perform HIC duties
  • Perform accurate reporting
  • Other warehouse duties as assigned by the shift supervisor.

Do you have what it takes?

  • Ability to lift and carry parcels, up to and including 70 lbs per piece, throughout shift.
  • Forklift certificate
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively and on-time within tight time constraints.
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively in a team environment.
  • Meet security requirements
  • Broad general working knowledge of warehouse functions.
  • Demonstrated knowledge and experience with the DHL operations system.
  • High School Diploma

All applicants are required to pass interviews, a criminal check and other background check in order to qualify for the position.
