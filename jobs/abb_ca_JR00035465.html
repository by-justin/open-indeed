Your role and responsibilities (Mandatory) In this role, you will have the opportunity to optimize the Production organization’s assembly methods, processes, and equipment to minimize labor hours in accordance with business strategies related to quality, efficiency, costs, and safety. Each day, you will ensure appropriate production methods, cycle time, equipment performance, and materials/components in support of production targets. You will also showcase your expertise by defining the working phase sequence of a new production process to achieve the targeted product cost and equipment investment budget.

The work model for the role is on site

Introduction of the position

Industrial Engineer is responsible for the production running support activities in terms of Master data maintenance, problem solving, production set-up of new production line, quality improvement and continuous improvement.

You will be mainly accountable for :

  • New Product Introduction (NPI)Manage the project and process of introducing new products into production for the factoryTransfer product, process knowledge and the required technical package (includes but not limited to design drawings/BOM/test specification/process map/WI/fixture drawing/control plan) from production responsible units or design authorities to local team, train and qualify operator to ensure they have proper skills to produce new product.Design the process flow diagram, production Layout (efficient flow of material and process) and value stream mapSet up new production line equipment and fixture, after it works stable transfer all knowledge and document to equipment engineer for daily operationSet up product testing process to ensure that the product meets the requirement specificationCreate documents/drawing/template etc. to ensure local production smoothly and properly manage them.Vendor tooling or measurement system design and setupSupport the setup of manufacturing process and capabilities in other factories when required
  • Set up master data in SAP as per super BOM, conduct a PDR (Material Master, Variant Configurator, Bill of Materials & Routing) transfer to other factories or loading to local SAP.
  • Support Certification (NEPSI, PAC, etc.) application and take sub-project as required
  • Support part localization and sample inspection
  • Daily production support and timely update the WI to ensure a smooth and trouble-free operation
  • Support the vendor tooling & measurement system troubleshooting and problem solving
  • Follow the Change Management Process to implement the Engineering Change Notification (ECN) and Design Change Notification (DCN), make changes in SAP and ensure the proper roll out of the changes in production
  • Responsible for design for manufacturing and process standardization, raise ECR to production responsible units or design authorities to improve product quality, cost and efficiency if see any opportunity
  • Continuously improve process with LSS tools to reduce manufacturing time, cost, improve efficiency, quality, inventory
  • Lead or support on automation and smart factory activities
  • other task related to improvements projects and coordinate with other teams to achieve production targets, Our team dynamics (Optional) You will join a active and collaborative team, where you will be able to thrive.

More about us (Mandatory)

The Measurement & Analytics Division is among the world’s leading manufacturers and suppliers of smart instrumentation and analyzers, working at the heart of industrial digital transformation. The Measurement & Analytics Division’s portfolio consists of analyzers measuring compositions of gases and liquids; instrumentation measuring process variables such as temperature, pressure, flow, and level; force measurement solutions measuring parameters such as flatness, thickness, and tension; and advanced digital solutions for device management, device health check and predictive maintenance. The Measurement & Analytics Division serves key industries such as oil and gas, chemical, water and wastewater, power, hydrogen, batteries, as well as the marine industry. The Division enables the optimization of industrial processes by providing and analyzing data collected from sensing and smart measurement devices. Parameters such as emission levels and production inputs are measured by providing ‘before’ and ‘after’ values, enabling efficient operations and environmental sustainability through measurement.
