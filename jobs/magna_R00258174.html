Job Responsibilities:

Compensation: $25 per hour

Hiring Shift: Night

PRIMARY FUNCTION

  • Ensures that material, tools and packaging are in place and ready for production
  • Operate scheduled production equipment on a daily basis
  • Operate production equipment and ensure that production targets are met
  • Perform inspection of product produced by following internal and external established requirements a (using standard and advanced measuring equipment)
  • Complete quality documentation as per internal and external requirements
  • Perform TPM inspection and communicate open issues thru internal documentation
  • Participate in continuous improvement
  • Participate in the team environment and communicate issues with production support
  • Train new operators on equipment
  • Responsible for area housekeeping and upkeep of presses, process centers, confined spaces etc.
  • Operates equipment according to training matrix
  • Ensure that crane inspection checklist is being completed before usage and applicable certificates are current
  • Complete changeovers as per of the team and setup bolsters with the next scheduled dies
  • Perform minor adjustments without assistance

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Minimum Grade 12 education
  • Strong command of the English language (verbal and written) process -, tooling improvements
  • Sound knowledge of statistical techniques to identify process improvements opportunities
  • Ability to use basic and advanced measuring equipment
  • Good documentation and organizational skills
  • Strong command of the English language (verbal and written)
  • Working knowledge of TS 16949, ISO 14001 systems and WHIMS
  • Valid Forklift and Crane Operator license
  • Confined space - / Lock-out / Tag-out  and WHIMIS training
  • SPC Level 1
  • Advanced knowledge of presses including vibra machines and part washers
  • Minimum of 1 year experience in a press environment

DUTIES AND RESPONSIBLITITES

  • Basic equipment operation including start up and shut down of equipment within the process center (press, vibra deburr, part washer)
  • Operating production equipment and produce parts to outlined requirements by using the tools provided (quality and quantity)
  • Must be willing to upgrade skills through on the job training
  • Perform die setup / press change over according to schedule
  • Complete all production and quality documentation on time and as per requirement
  • Ensure that the machine has a valid first off prior to start of operation
  • Perform part inspection by using equipment outlined in the inspection and record results as per the outlined requirements
  • Pack product according to the packaging instructions
  • Follow standardized work instructions
  • Produce parts to close tolerances, specifications, on time to ensure high productivity and part quality
  • Follow ANDON system(Press the button for help on Shoplogix Whiteboard) to call Team leader/Supervisor, if Stop operation for any reason(i.e. Safety, Quality, tooling, maintenance, and leaving work cell for any reason)
  • Ensure that process center is clean at all times, follow downtime GAP PLAN as per 5S standard
  • Train new operators on equipment, tooling and product according to requirements
  • Perform TPM inspection
  • Perform machine setups and first off’s as schedule
  • Complete and verify changeover checklist and ensure that all aspects are completed before running
  • Able to maintain and adjust all press controls
  • Ensure that error proofing devices are working and that abnormal conditions are being communicated to your Supervisor (double blank detection, press force monitoring)
  • Communicate changes with fellow employees and Supervisors
  • Actively participate in all areas of Continuous improvement
  • Identify and update any preventative maintenance requirements using maintenance repair log
  • Ensure that all safety devices are in place and operational
  • Participate in the setup and problem solving in regards to new tooling
