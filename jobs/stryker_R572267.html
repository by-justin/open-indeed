Job Description

Work Flexibility: Not available

Your Mission

Join Stryker's R&D Lifecycle Management team and contribute to projects that help improve how critical product information is managed across the organization. As part of this MedTech internship, you will support digital transformation initiatives, work with modern analytics tools, and help optimize business processes that support product quality and compliance.

From your first day, you will take ownership of meaningful projects, collaborate with specialists across multiple functions, and gain practical experience in data analytics, automation, and process improvement. This opportunity is ideal for students looking to strengthen their technical and business skills while creating measurable impact in a global environment.

What You'll Do

  • Support the migration and improvement of product lifecycle data within modern digital systems.
  • Create and maintain documentation, standards, and best practices for data management processes.
  • Build reports and dashboards using  Excel ,  Excel Macros , and  Power BI  to generate business insights.
  • Identify opportunities for process optimization and support automation initiatives using  Power Automate .
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve data quality, accessibility, and operational efficiency.

​

What You'll Bring

  • You are currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Business Informatics, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or a related field.
  • Good knowledge of  Excel  and an interest in data analytics and process improvement.
  • Initial experience with  Power BI ,  Excel Macros , or similar analytical tools through coursework, projects, or previous internships.
  • Interest in  Power Automate , automation concepts, or digital transformation initiatives.
  • Good English language skills (minimum B2), good German language skills (minimum B2) are a plus

​

What's In It for You

  • Paid six-month full-time internship (voluntary internship alongside studies, mandatory internship, or a combination of both possible)
  • Start date: March or April 2027
  • Location: Schönkirchen (near Kiel, Germany)
  • Flexible working hours and attractive holiday allowance
  • Mentoring and training to support your growth
  • Hands-on projects with real-world impact in an international environment
