Job Description

Clio is the global leader in legal AI technology, empowering legal professionals and law firms of every size to work smarter, faster, and more securely.

We are transforming the legal experience for all by bettering the lives of legal professionals while increasing access to justice.

Summary:

Clio is the global leader in legal technology and legal AI, and the first legal practice management unicorn. While Clio's growth has historically been driven by North America, international expansion represents one of the company's most significant growth opportunities over the coming years.

Today, Clio serves customers in more than 130 countries worldwide. EMEA is a strategic growth region for Clio, encompassing a diverse set of markets at different stages of maturity, from established markets such as the UK and Ireland to rapidly growing opportunities across Spain, the Nordics, Benelux, South Africa, and the Middle East.

As Senior Manager, EMEA Marketing, you will lead Clio's regional marketing strategy across key EMEA markets, driving awareness, demand generation, customer engagement, and market expansion. Reporting to the Director of International Marketing, you will be responsible for developing and executing regional growth plans, leading a team of marketing professionals, and partnering closely with Sales, Customer Success, Product Marketing, Brand, Communications, and executive stakeholders.

This is a highly visible leadership role that combines strategic planning, people management, and operational execution. You will play a critical role in shaping Clio's international growth strategy and establishing the company as the leading legal technology platform across EMEA.

Who You Are

You are a commercially-minded marketing leader with deep experience building and scaling regional marketing programs in complex international environments. You have successfully led multi-market marketing strategies, balancing regional consistency with local market relevance. You understand how brand, demand generation, events, partnerships, communications, and customer marketing work together to drive business growth.You are equally comfortable setting strategy, managing budgets, coaching teams, and influencing senior stakeholders. You bring strong analytical rigor, operational excellence, and a growth mindset to everything you do.Most importantly, you thrive in high-growth environments where you can build, scale, and create meaningful business impact.

Role Summary

Regional Marketing Strategy

Develop and execute Clio's EMEA marketing strategy, ensuring alignment with company growth objectives, market opportunities, and regional revenue goals.

Team Leadership & Development

Lead and develop a team of regional marketers across demand generation, field marketing, and market-specific initiatives, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Demand Generation & Pipeline Growth

Partner closely with Sales and Demand Generation leadership to create integrated marketing programs that drive pipeline, revenue growth, and customer acquisition across EMEA.

Brand & Market Development

Strengthen Clio's brand awareness and market leadership position across key EMEA markets through integrated campaigns, events, partnerships, communications, and thought leadership initiatives.

Operational Excellence

Own regional planning, budgeting, forecasting, performance reporting, and resource allocation to maximize marketing effectiveness and ROI.
