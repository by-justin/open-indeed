Position Summary

We have a current vacancy for a Maintenance Coordinator to join our Team in Victoria, BC. The Maintenance Coordinator provides work package support to the Ship Maintenance Leads and the broader Service Delivery team managing and coordinating assigned Technical Support Network (TSN) (sub-contractors) during each Work Period. The Maintenance Coordinator ensures that: TSN's complete all agreed maintenance activities to the required quality in a safe and environmentally friendly manner; TSN's deliver complete Objective Quality Evidence (OQE) for all completed tasks; management of all special tools and test equipment loaned to TSN's; and management of all material expended or returned by the TSN's.  Reporting to their respective NEM Liaison & Service Delivery Team Lead, the Maintenance Coordinator assists Ship Maintenance Leads in generation and management of reports along with management of additional documentation required to facilitate and close out each work period.

Key Areas of Responsibility

  • Provision of general project support and administration activities to the Waterfront Service Delivery Team;
  • Drafting, reviewing and ensuring that maintenance instructions are complete and sufficient for service providers to complete assigned maintenance operations;
  • Overseeing maintenance activities being conducted by service providers on board naval vessels ensuring that the work is being conducted to the required quality in a safe and environmentally friendly manner;
  • Compiling Work Item closure reports, ensuring completeness and accuracy, and entering into the designated project tool for Work Item closure;
  • Generating and managing documentation and reports in various formats (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) as and when required in support of the Service Delivery Team;
  • Ensuring subcontracted service providers have the necessary paperwork and materials to complete designated maintenance tasks;
  • Attending meetings and preparing meeting agendas and minutes;
  • Generating purchase requisitions for Suppliers and Subcontracted Services, maintaining purchase orders and ensuring the efficient financial closure of Subcontracts on behalf of the Service Delivery and Procurement teams;
  • Ensuring service provider records are complete and up to date;
  • Ensuring compliance with Health and Safety requirements;
  • Assisting the Service Delivery team with keeping accurate and timely records; and
  • Other tasks as required.
