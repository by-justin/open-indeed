Job Description

Event Sales Street Team Representative

Temporary Assignment | June 2026 – December 2026

Position Summary

ATCO Energy is launching an onsite Event Sales Street Team to support in-person activations across Alberta. In this role, you will work closely with Marketing to represent ATCO Energy at shopping malls, sporting events, home shows, and community events. This opportunity is ideal for individuals who thrive on customer interaction, are motivated by sales targets, and are looking to build hands-on, customer-facing sales experience. Given the nature of event-based work, the majority of activations take place during evenings and weekends, when customer traffic is highest and there are strong opportunities for engagement and conversion.

Key Responsibilities

  • Act as a frontline ambassador for ATCO Energy at live, in-person events
  • Proactively engage customers with confident, friendly conversations
  • Clearly explain ATCO Energy plans, pricing, and value proposition
  • Support onsite customer enrollments and lead generation
  • Contribute to individual and team acquisition targets
  • Partner with Marketing and Operations teams on event execution and continuous improvement
  • Assist with setup and teardown of event booths and kiosks
  • Represent the ATCO Energy brand professionally at all times

What We’re Looking For

  • Outgoing and confident interacting with the public in busy environments
  • Motivated by goals, targets, and performance outcomes
  • Strong communication and customer-focused mindset
  • Resilient and adaptable in fast-paced, high-energy settings
  • Reliable and able to work independently and as part of a team
  • Interest in skill development and growth
  • Previous experience in customer service, sales, promotions, or events is an asset but not required

Compensation & Perks

  • $20/hour base pay
  • $10 commission per completed customer sign-up (paid monthly)
  • Additional performance-based incentives
  • Travel costs covered for required events

Schedule & Commitment

  • Part-time / casual contract role
  • Primarily evenings and weekends
  • Events across Alberta (local travel required)
  • Shifts are scheduled in advance based on the event calendar, with consideration for availability where possible

Why Join This Team

  • Gain hands-on experience in a growing customer acquisition channel
  • Build sales, communication, and customer engagement skills
  • Collaborate with Marketing and Operations on a high-visibility initiative
  • Develop transferable skills in relationship-building and performance delivery

If you enjoy engaging with people, are motivated by results, and want to be part of a high-energy pilot program, we encourage you to apply.

At ATCO Energy, we’re purpose-driven to help customers feel comfortable and confident. Every day, we lean on our core values to shape the future of our industry in a fast-paced and agile environment.
