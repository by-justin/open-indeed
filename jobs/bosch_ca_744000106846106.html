  • グローバルな環境で多様なバックグラウンドを持つメンバーと共に働くことで、国際的な視野と実践的なコミュニケーション能力を養うことができます。
  • 最新のセンサー技術（Radar, Video）と先進的な制御技術を組み合わせたシステム開発を通じて、専門性の高い知識とスキルを習得できます。

  • Acquire specialized knowledge and expertise in system development that integrates the latest sensor technologies (Radar, Video) with sophisticated control algorithms.

[Job Responsibilities]

  • Lead the design and implementation of advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) such as ACC (Adaptive Cruise Control), AEB (Automatic Emergency Braking), LKAS (Lane Keeping Assist System), and AHB (Automatic High Beam Control) using Radar and Video sensors within the XC-AS JP (Cross Domain Computing Solutions-System, Software & Services Japan)department.
  • Conduct detailed specification alignment and requirement analysis with customers, taking a leading role in the overall system design.
  • Drive continuous process improvements to enhance software quality, contributing significantly to both development efficiency and product excellence.
  • Be deeply involved in cutting-edge ADAS development, from initial concept and design through to implementation and testing.

[Current Challenges & Expected Roles]

  • For the JOEM project, we expect you to take the lead in all phases of the driver assistance system software development, from detailed requirements analysis to software design, implementation, and rigorous system performance evaluation.
  • You are required to accurately interpret complex and advanced customer requirements and translate them into precise and actionable technical specifications.
  • We anticipate your significant contribution to enhancing both software quality and development efficiency through continuous process improvement proposals and their successful implementation.
  • As driving assistance technology progresses rapidly, we expect you to proactively keep abreast of the latest technological trends and actively integrate them into our development processes and products.
