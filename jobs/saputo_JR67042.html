Operate knock out machine. Knock out manually into brine tanks.

How You Will Make Contributions That Matter

  • Responsible for the quality and appearance of the product.
  • Complete appropriate data entry/PLC machine set up.
  • Maintain 701 or 704.
  • Washer chemical concentration.
  • Conduct aseptic cheese sampling.
  • Operate natamycin bath and maintain concentration.
  • Operate punch machine and metal detector.
  • Must be able to appropriately handle and use cleaning chemicals.
  • Monitor and complete punch machine needle checks.
  • Stack aging baskets.
  • Requires regular attendance.

You Are Best Suited For The Role If You….

  • High school diploma or general education degree (GED); or one to three months related experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience.
  • Ability to read and understand instructions.
  • Proficient in use of the computer.
  • Must have a solid organizational, analytical problem solving and communication skills.
  • Follow good sanitation and housekeeping practices.
  • Compliance with food safety policies.
  • Follow GMPs and SOPs.
