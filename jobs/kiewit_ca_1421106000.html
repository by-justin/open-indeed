District Overview

As Senior Project Controls Manager, you will lead the integrated project controls function for a large, complex commercial nuclear EPC project. You will be accountable for establishing and maintaining the project's cost, schedule, progress, change, risk, forecasting, and performance management systems from baseline development through engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, and turnover.

This role is a key member of project leadership and leads a multidisciplinary team of cost engineers and analysts, schedulers and planners, progress and reporting specialists, and Advanced Work Packaging / WorkFace Planning resources. You will convert project data into actionable insight, drive forecast accuracy and early identification of trends, and partner with project management, commercial, engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, client, and joint-venture stakeholders to protect project objectives.

This position will support all KNS office locations and job sites-project sites as required.

District Overview:

Kiewit Nuclear Solutions (KNS) is a full-service engineering, project management, and construction provider operating across North America. Our capabilities span commercial nuclear power, small modular and advanced reactors, nuclear security and environmental missions, and complex energy and infrastructure programs.

As an integrated engineering and construction organization, KNS draws on Kiewit's 135+ years of execution experience and its broader network across power, industrial, infrastructure, buildings, and heavy civil markets. We bring constructability, self-perform experience, disciplined project management, and integrated digital delivery to complex nuclear work.

Our teams are committed to safe execution, continuous improvement, and delivery for our clients while living Kiewit's core values of People, Integrity, Excellence, and Stewardship.

Responsibilities

  • Lead all project controls activities for assigned EPC projects, including cost management, scheduling, forecasting, change management, risk analysis, reporting, governance, and team development.

  • Establish and maintain the integrated project controls framework, including WBS/CBS/OBS structures, control accounts, progress measurement methodologies, coding standards, reporting requirements, and data governance processes.

  • Own the development, maintenance, and change control of integrated cost and schedule baselines, ensuring alignment of scope, budgets, resources, milestones, and contractual requirements.

  • Direct project financial performance, including budgets, commitments, actual costs, accruals, cash flow, contingency management, labor productivity, Estimate-to-Complete (ETC), and Estimate-at-Completion (EAC) forecasts.

  • Lead the development and maintenance of integrated Primavera P6 schedules, including critical path analysis, resource planning, schedule quality reviews, recovery planning, and progress measurement.

  • Integrate engineering, procurement, fabrication, construction, commissioning, turnover, and subcontractor activities into a comprehensive project execution plan.

  • Lead change management, trend tracking, schedule impact assessments, and risk analysis to support accurate forecasting, proactive decision-making, and successful project delivery.Develop and present project controls reports, dashboards, forecasts, and executive briefings, clearly communicating project performance, key risks, corrective actions, and strategic recommendations to leadership and clients.

  • Ensure project controls data is accurate, auditable, and integrated across financial, procurement, scheduling, document management, and reporting systems.

  • Ensure compliance with contractual requirements, project procedures, nuclear quality standards, security requirements, and Earned Value Management System (EVMS) requirements, where applicable.

  • Recruit, mentor, and develop project controls personnel while fostering a culture of accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.

  • Support project planning, proposals, procurement strategy, subcontract management, project execution, and closeout activities throughout the project lifecycle.
