工作目标Primary Objectives of the Position

Responsible for electrical design and technical support of MV switchgear. 负责中压开关设备的电气设计及技术支持工作

主要工作职责Essential Duties & Responsibilities

1、    Have abilities to design switchgear according to the specific electrical engineering requirements. 根据电气工程具体要求进行开关柜电气方面的设计。 2、    Cooperate with DI to update the major solution design in order to meet the requirements of products; Give the answer to the question raised by our customers towards products. 负责协助设计院调整方案以满足自身产品特点要求,解答客户针对产品提出的问题。 3、    Cooperate in designing the project schedule to make sure the project on time. 协助制定项目计划并保证相关环节的准时完成按照项目计划完成设计工作。 4、    Design new scheme according to the market requirements, extend product functions, optimize original design. 对市场要求设计新的方案，拓展产品功能，优化原设计方案。 5、    Support on design scheme confirmation before contract receiving. 支持售前的设计方案确认。 6、    To write electrical and digital technical documentations. 编写电气及数字化技术文件。 7、    Cooperate in the production issue. 协调处理生产中产生的问题。

岗位要求Qualifications & Requirements

Bachelor degree or above, major in electrical automation/ electronic and information engineering or relevant discipline 本科及以上学历，电气自动化/电子信息工程或相关专业。 At least two years working experience for switchgear 至少有两年的开关柜工作经验 Good communication skill. 良好沟通技能。 Highly responsible, self-motivated and Willing to take on additional tasks. 工作主动负责，愿意承担额外工作任务。 A very strong learning ability and pragmatic spirit. 具备较强的学习能力和团队精神。 Has a positive progressive attitude under pressure to work. 能在压力下保持积极的工作态度。 Ability to communicate in English, both written and oral. 英语流利。
