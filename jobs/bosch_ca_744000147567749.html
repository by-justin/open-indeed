Sales Manager Industrial Boilers Solutions

Company: Bosch Canada Location: Providencia, Región Metropolitana, Chile URL: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/BoschGroup/744000147567749-sales-manager-industrial-boilers-solutions

Company Description

At Bosch Chile, we are looking for an Industrial Sales Manager to lead the growth and commercial development of our Industrial Boilers business within the VP division .

Job Description

Responsibilities

Business Development & Commercial Strategy

  • Design and implement the strategic commercial plan for the Industrial Boilers business in Chile.
  • Identify priority markets, segments and high-potential customers.
  • Develop new business opportunities across key industrial sectors.
  • Analyze market trends, competitors, customer needs and emerging technologies to identify opportunities for growth and differentiation.
  • Develop and execute strategies for the introduction of new products, services and solutions.
  • Translate market and field insights into commercial priorities and long-term business strategies.

Sales & Strategic Customer Management

  • Manage the full sales cycle for complex, high-value industrial solutions, from prospecting and opportunity qualification to negotiation, closing and after-sales support.
  • Establish and maintain strong, long-term relationships with current and potential industrial customers.
  • Engage with different decision-makers and stakeholders, including C-Level executives, Operations, Maintenance, Engineering and Procurement teams.
  • Expand and develop a portfolio of strategic customers by understanding their operational and technical needs and translating them into valuable solutions.
  • Lead the development of complex and high-impact technical-commercial proposals, ensuring profitability and compliance with Bosch quality standards.

Industrial Boilers & Technical Solutions

  • Drive commercial opportunities related to industrial steam and hot water boilers, water-tube boilers, combustion systems, steam and energy generation, energy efficiency and industrial thermal processes .
  • Understand customers' industrial processes and technical challenges to identify the most appropriate Bosch solutions.
  • Translate complex technical concepts into clear commercial value for technical, operational and executive audiences.
  • Identify opportunities to optimize thermal processes, improve energy performance and generate long-term value for customers.

Project & Operations Management

  • Collaborate closely with Engineering, Projects and Technical Service teams to ensure successful implementation of customer solutions.
  • Coordinate commercial and technical stakeholders throughout the different stages of the project.
  • Monitor implemented projects and solutions, ensuring technical, financial and timeline objectives are achieved.
  • Maintain close contact with customers throughout the project lifecycle to ensure satisfaction and identify future business opportunities.

Team Leadership & People Management

  • Lead, motivate and develop a team of sales professionals and/or application engineers, fostering a high-performance environment.
  • Establish clear objectives and provide coaching and follow-up to achieve individual and collective goals.
  • Maintain close contact with sales teams, technical specialists and customers in the field.
  • Connect field reality with business strategy, transforming customer and team insights into commercial actions and priorities.
  • Adapt leadership and communication style to different audiences and organizational levels.

Business Performance & Reporting

  • Manage the business P&L , ensuring sustainable and profitable growth.
  • Monitor commercial performance, sales projections, pipeline and market opportunities.
  • Use CRM tools for opportunity management and tracking.
  • Provide management with relevant market analysis and business insights to support decision-making.

Qualifications

Mandatory Requirements

  • Degree in Industrial Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering or a related engineering field .
  • 5–10 years of proven experience in business development, commercial management and/or sales leadership within the industrial sector.
  • Strong experience developing business with industrial customers in Chile.
  • A strong network of contacts and demonstrated experience with relevant customers in sectors such as mining, energy, food & beverage, pulp & paper, manufacturing and other process industries .
  • Experience selling complex technical solutions, industrial equipment, thermal solutions, energy systems and/or heavy machinery.
  • Strong negotiation, closing and executive-level relationship management skills.
  • Ability to identify and develop new business opportunities with a proactive and results-oriented approach.
  • Experience managing commercial performance, budgets and P&L.
  • Advanced English proficiency is mandatory , including the ability to conduct business discussions and deliver strategic presentations to international senior management.
  • Valid Class B driver's license.
  • Availability to travel frequently within Chile and occasionally within the region.

Technical Knowledge

  • Industrial steam boilers.
  • Water-tube boiler systems.
  • Steam and energy generation systems.
  • Combustion systems and technologies.
  • Energy efficiency.
  • Industrial thermal processes and optimization.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's degree or relevant postgraduate studies.
  • Direct experience with industrial boilers, steam generation, combustion systems and/or energy efficiency .
  • Experience leading commercial and/or technical-commercial teams.
  • Experience working within multinational industrial organizations and interacting with regional or global stakeholders.

This is an opportunity to lead the growth of Bosch's Industrial Boilers business in Chile , combining commercial strategy, industrial technology, customer development and people leadership.

The role requires someone who can be equally effective in the field and in the boardroom : building strong relationships with sales teams, technical professionals and industrial customers while also being able to analyze the business, define strategy and present it confidently to senior international management.
