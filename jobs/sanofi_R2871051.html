Job Description

Main Responsibilities / 主要职责 The Project Specialist Intern will assist in liaising with relevant Business Partners, local team members, and Country Medical Leads to support appropriate HCP data transfer to the Tiering system. The intern will support quality checks on collected data, learn to assess HCP and non-HCP tiering documents under supervision, assist in compiling reports, and help identify process and system improvements. 项目专员实习生将协助与相关业务伙伴、当地团队成员及国家医学负责人保持联系，支持医疗保健专业人士（HCP）数据向分级（Tiering）系统的准确传输。实习生将协助对所收集的数据进行质量检查，在指导下学习评估HCP与非HCP分级文档，协助编制报告，并帮助识别流程和系统的改进机会。

People / 人员协作

Build relationships with internal peers and stakeholders within the allocated geography 在所负责的区域内，与内部同事及相关方建立良好合作关系

Participate in connects with FMV leads and local country medical teams 参与与FMV（现场医学拜访）负责人及当地国家医学团队的沟通交流

Financial and Reporting / 财务与报告

Assist in preparing monitoring and verification reports as per agreed timelines and quality standards 按照约定的时间表和质量标准，协助编制监控与核实报告

Support data quality assessment under guidance, including identifying abnormalities in monitored data 在指导下协助进行数据质量评估，包括识别监控数据中的异常情况

Learn customer engagement and scientific external expert reporting processes 学习客户互动及科学外部专家（External Expert）报告相关流程

Process / 流程管理

Support management of requests within the centralized mailbox/request platform, helping to prioritize based on business need 协助管理集中邮箱/请求平台中的各类请求，并根据业务需求协助排定优先级

Participate in process training and learn tools used locally 参与流程培训，学习当地使用的相关工具

Assist in analyzing expert credentials and expertise under supervision to support tier level determination 在指导下协助分析专家的资质与专业领域，为分级等级的确定提供支持

Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned 完成上级安排的其他相关工作职责

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
