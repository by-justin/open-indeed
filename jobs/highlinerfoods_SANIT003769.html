Job Description

Essential Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide daily on-site support and oversight of third-party sanitation activities, including ensuring work is completed safely, effectively, and in alignment with the MSS, food safety requirements, regulatory standards, and site policies.

  • Serve as the primary third-shift High Liner Foods contact for the sanitation contractor, supporting timely communication and follow-up while escalating significant risks, performance gaps, or compliance concerns to the Plant Food Safety and Quality Manager.

  • Support pre-operational readiness by confirming sanitation work is complete, deficiencies are addressed, verification activities are completed as required, and areas are ready for line set-up and QA pre-operational review.

  • Partner with QA, Operations, Maintenance, Warehouse, Dry Storage, the sanitation contractor, and the chemical provider to resolve sanitation-related issues impacting cleanability, food safety, quality, and production readiness.

  • Support sanitation validation, verification, CAPA, and root cause activities in partnership with Plant Food Safety and Quality leadership.

  • Communicate sanitation status, pre-op concerns, production readiness, KPI updates, and follow-up needs to appropriate internal stakeholders, site and contractor leadership.

  • Build and maintain a productive working relationship with contractor leadership by setting clear expectations, coordinating daily priorities, supporting timely follow-up, and reinforcing a shared commitment to food safety, quality, and effective sanitation execution.

Chemical & Equipment Management

  • Partner with the chemical provider and sanitation contractor to support proper chemical selection, concentration, titration, and usage verification.

  • Verify contractor practices for chemical storage, handling, dispensing, PPE, tools, and sanitation equipment to align with site requirements.

  • Identify chemical, equipment, supply, or process gaps and coordinate follow-up with the appropriate internal or external partner.

Documentation, Compliance & Communication

  • Review sanitation records for accuracy and completeness, including MSS completion, chemical logs, titration records, equipment cleaning records, verification activities, and contractor-provided documentation.

  • Ensure that SSOPs are documented, maintained, updated as necessary, and are aligned with actual sanitation practices followed.

  • Support enterprise and sanitation audit preparedness and execution, maintaining the site audit ready every day.

  • Provide shift summaries, KPI updates, and vendor performance feedback to the Plant Food Safety and Quality Manager and site leadership team.

  • Participate in routine shift handover meetings and regular sanitation performance meetings with contractors and High Liner leadership.

  • Engage with the plant food safety team to ensure sanitation is an active participant. Support changes, and improvements to the site HACCP Program as necessary.

Continuous Improvement

  • Use KPI trends, pre-op findings, environmental results, audit observations, and contractor feedback to identify and support sanitation improvements.

  • Partner with key internal and external stakeholders to improve sanitation methods, tools, equipment cleanability, pest control practices, chemical systems, and overall program effectiveness.

Qualifications

  • 3–5 years of experience in food manufacturing sanitation, food safety, or sanitation program support; experience working with third-party sanitation contractors preferred.

  • Bachelor’s degree preferred but not required.

  • HACCP certification and experience with third-party audits strongly preferred.

  • Strong knowledge of sanitation requirements, chemical safety, pre-operational inspection expectations, GMPs, and regulatory standards.

  • Demonstrated ability to influence without direct authority, build strong vendor partnerships, and hold external partners accountable to established performance expectations.

  • Strong communication, follow-up, conflict resolution, critical thinking, and decision-making skills.

  • Ability to work a third shift (approximately 11pm-7am) and flex schedule as needed to support business demands.

  • Reliable, professional, and able to remain calm and effective under pressure.
