Job Description

Do you want to orchestrate the operations of Distribution Centers across Europe from Greece? In our Warehouse Centre of Excellence you can!

The Warehouse Centre of Excellence team, part of Product Supply in P&G, one of the world's leading FMCG companies, focuses on the operational improvement of our European Distribution Centers, centralizing core supply chain processes in a digital, innovative and effective way. Our goal is to build a connected network of intelligent DCs through centralization, standardization and digitalization of Europe Warehousing indirect tasks.

We are looking for motivated individuals to join our WCoE organization in 📍 Aspropyrgos, Athens !

💰Compensation details: for this role the  monthly starting salary is 2.150   EUR Gross (yearly starting salary 30.100 EUR).  We consistently benchmark our salaries against top competitors and every P&G employee’s salary is reviewed at least annually so there is ample room for growth.

As Europe Warehousing Operations Coordinator , you will play an essential role in the company‘s structure for using, maintaining, and improving the Warehouse Operations work processes and system digital solutions. Your efforts will help ensure that the site work process meets high operational standards and capability, executing the work process across the organization through End Users documentation and training.

Additionally, you will have the opportunity to lead individual projects that make a meaningful impact!

Collaborating with a diverse group of professionals and working closely with colleagues not just locally, but across Europe, in a truly international setting, this will be an opportunity to be part of a top-ranked Supply Chain Company recognized by Gartner, where your contributions will drive meaningful change and innovation.

What we offer:

  • Business responsibilities from Day 1 – You will start working on key brands from the beginning. Over time, as you expand your impact on the business, your responsibility and ownership of a brand will quickly grow.
  • Continuous coaching & mentorship – We are passionate about our work and your growth. We will make sure you receive formal training alongside regular mentorship from your manager and experienced colleagues, fostering a culture of continuous learning and development.
  • Opportunity to work in a dynamic and respectful work environment – We live our Purpose, Values, and Principles daily and focus on safety, respect, and inclusivity for all. We value every individual and encourage initiatives promoting agility and work/life balance.
  • Recognition of Your Contributions – Your hard work and achievements will not go unnoticed. We celebrate success and recognize individual and team contributions to our collective goals.
  • Competitive salary and benefits program – Regular salary revisions and possible promotions - all in line with your results, performance and potential.

Job Qualifications

  • Recent Graduates of BSc studies in Technical, Engineering, Science or Business school.
  • Proficiency in English , both verbally and in writing.
  • Completed/Permanently exempt from Military Service Requirement (if applicable).
  • Advanced analytical skills, Power BI and PowerApps are plus.
  • Experience in Systems: SAP QM & SAP QR, WMS, Production Execution Basic, Transportation Operation Basic are strong assets.
  • Experience in creating and managing action plans, including follow up to eliminate gaps.
