About the role

The project associated with this role will be the implementation and ongoing evolution of our Google CCaaS environment (Contact Center as a Service), a cloud-based contact center solution. The successful candidate will play a pivotal role in leading , orchestrating , and driving the design, implementation, and adoption of new operational processes. They will be responsible for overseeing production deployments, managing cross-functional collaboration between Google, the Lab, Operations and Planning, and IT teams, mitigating delivery risks , and championing continuous improvement initiatives to enhance service delivery and business outcomes.

This position is an 18-month secondment.

What you'll do here:

Overall project planning

  • Co-ordination and Execution of remaining project phases
  • Follow and control the project evolution and communicate progresses accomplished
  • Identify and execute improved Workflow Processes and Tools
  • Participate in New Requirements Definition Process
  • Business Analysis, Stakeholder Management & Product Ownership
  • Support Deployment plans and strategies
  • Participate in Business Roadmaps

Initiation & Planning

  • Responsible for developing overall project plans, track and report on project status, progress, issues and costs according to Intact Methodology or approved deviation
  • Kickoff Projects with stakeholders and ensure all stakeholders required for the project are identified, with support from the project team
  • Support the IT PM and build an effective project organization structure

Project Execution, Monitor & Control

  • Supervise and coordinate, indirectly, activities of a multi-tasked team.
  • Logging of issues, decisions, actions, dependencies consulting with Program Director. Pro-actively manage all issues, risks and dependencies; working with project stakeholders to review and update on a regular basis (at least monthly).
  • Logging of issues, decisions, actions, dependencies and consulting with IT PM
  • Effectively facilitate meetings and creates presentations relative to milestones, budget expenditures, issue resolution, project status
  • Using project management tools, analyzes the project schedules  and advises / warns on potential missed milestones
  • Integrate and evaluate the Change Management Approach. Monitor implementation efforts and transition issues to ensure business risks are identified and solutions are recommended

Documentation

  • Make sure an appropriate methodology is used during the lifecycle of the project. Make sure team has all tools available to do their job
  • Plan for documentation, tracking, reporting and follow-ups
  • Conduct overall project / program reporting
  • Facilitate status meetings, responsible for coordination, booking, monitoring attendance distribution of materials taking meeting minutes
  • Creates presentations relative to milestones, issue resolution, project status
  • Ensures accuracy in preparing and promptly completing all requirements supporting documentation (ie reports, change requests, presentation, invoices, correspondence tables charts files etc.

What you bring to the table:

  • University degree or college diploma in Business Administration, Project Management, Information technology , Operations Management or any relevant applicant fields
  • Project Management Professional (PMP) certification (asset)
  • Previously led high profile and large scale project
  • For candidates located in Quebec, bilingualism is required considering the necessity to interact on a regular basis with English-speaking colleagues across the country.
  • No Canadian work experience required however must be eligible to work in Canada.

Core Competencies

  • Strategic Thinking, Planning and Organizing
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Influencing and Negotiation
  • Change Leadership
  • Communication and Presentation Skills
  • Decision Making
  • Continuous Improvement Mindset
