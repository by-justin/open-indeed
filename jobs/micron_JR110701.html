Job Description

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Staff SSD Validation Engineer at Micron Technology, Inc., you will be responsible for defining and driving comprehensive validation strategies for Micron NAND-based SSD systems and solutions. You will lead development of test plans, test cases, and automation frameworks to validate SSD firmware across functional, system-level, performance, reliability, interoperability, and customer qualification domains. You will serve as a technical leader, driving validation excellence, automation, and innovation while collaborating closely with firmware, hardware, systems, product engineering, and hyperscale customers throughout the product lifecycle.

The ideal candidate will possess deep expertise in NVMe/PCIe technologies, SSD architecture, customer qualification requirements, and automated validation methodologies. This role also requires a strong focus on leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) and advanced automation technologies to improve validation productivity, product quality, and time-to-market.

Key Responsibilities

  • Define validation strategies, test plans, test cases, and automation frameworks for SSD firmware validation.
  • Develop and maintain automated test solutions covering functional, system, performance, reliability, interoperability, and customer qualification requirements.
  • Lead validation efforts across all phases of the product lifecycle, including development, qualification, customer enablement, and sustaining activities.
  • Demonstrate strong understanding of NVMe, PCIe, OCP Cloud SSD specifications, storage protocols, and customer-specific requirements.
  • Partner with Micron's hyperscale and datacenter customers to improve product quality, customer satisfaction, and qualification cycle times.
  • Drive issue identification, debug, root cause analysis, and closure through effective collaboration with firmware, hardware, system architecture, and product engineering teams.
  • Analyze validation results, identify quality risks, and provide clear recommendations to stakeholders and leadership.
  • Communicate validation status, technical risks, mitigation plans, and project progress effectively across organizations.
  • Influence and lead cross-functional teams to execute validation strategies and achieve program milestones.
  • Operate effectively in ambiguous environments and independently drive high-impact initiatives to completion.
  • Mentor engineers and promote technical excellence, automation, and best practices across the validation organization.
  • Leverage AI-powered engineering tools and approved enterprise AI solutions to improve test development, debug efficiency, data analysis, and reporting workflows.
  • Identify opportunities to apply AI and machine learning techniques for automating validation workflows, failure analysis, anomaly detection, and predictive quality analytics.
  • Drive adoption of AI-enabled solutions to reduce manual effort, enhance test coverage, accelerate issue triage, and improve validation throughput.
  • Utilize Generative AI tools for code generation, test case development, log analysis, technical documentation, and productivity enhancement while adhering to company policies.
  • Champion responsible and ethical AI practices, ensuring compliance with Micron AI governance, security, privacy, and data protection requirements.
  • Mentor teams on effective AI adoption, prompt engineering techniques, and AI-assisted engineering workflows to improve organizational productivity and innovation.

Required Experience

  • 6-12 years of experience in Embedded Firmware, Storage Systems, or SSD Validation Engineering.
  • 4+ years of SSD Firmware Validation experience is highly preferred.
  • Strong understanding of SSD architecture, NAND Flash technologies, and storage subsystems.
  • Deep knowledge of NVMe, PCIe, OCP Cloud SSD, and enterprise storage specifications.
  • Experience with Datacenter Compute and Storage environments such as Microsoft Hyper-V, VMware vSphere, VSAN, and cloud infrastructure is a strong plus.
  • Strong hands-on experience debugging NVMe/SAS protocol issues using protocol analyzers such as LeCroy and JDSU.
  • Experience developing comprehensive validation plans involving I/O workloads, NVMe protocols, media management, endurance, and reliability testing.
  • Experience using storage validation platforms and tools such as Medusa, SanBlaze, OakGate, UNH, PCIe-CV, and related qualification suites.
  • Experience integrating tests into automated validation frameworks and continuous integration environments.
  • Proven ability to drive complex technical issues to closure across multiple organizations.
  • Experience reviewing, analyzing, tracking, and documenting validation results and product quality issues.
  • Demonstrated leadership in driving validation strategies for large-scale programs and customer engagements.
