Company Description

Robert Bosch Power Tool Elektromos Szerszámgyártó Kft. was founded in Miskolc in November 2001, and today it has become a determining factor not only within the company group, but also in the region, becoming the largest Bosch power tool factory worldwide.

Our main products are:

  • Green power tools and gardening tools for home use
  • Blue power tools for industrial use
  • Batteries for eBike and power tools

Our goal is to develop both ourselves and our products with the help of our coworkers.

Join our factory with more than 20 years of history, where approximately 4,000 employees currently enjoy the stability provided by our various industries.

Job Description

  • Development of manufacturing processes mainly laser welding processes
  • Coordination and execution of development considering costs, quality, deadlines, and functionality
  • Definition and coordination of technical requirements for manufacturing equipment
  • Technical consulting for welding-compatible design of interfaces within the framework of the simultaneous engineering process
  • Knowledge transfer and conducting training sessions

Qualifications

  • Experience in the field of welding technology, preferably in laser welding
  • Broad knowledge in the field of materials science
  • Degree in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, materials science, or a comparable degree program
  • Goal-oriented
  • Structured
  • Self-responsibleTeam player
