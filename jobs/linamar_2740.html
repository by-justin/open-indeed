Job Title: Quality Engineer 质量工程师

  • Develop quality standards for the organization's raw materials, in-process products, and finished products and design and implement the measures, tests, sampling methods, and other procedures that ensure that these standards are met.

  • To provide a means of communication directly with a Customer to develop and supply a component which meets or exceeds the Customer requirements

  • To assist in the development of subcontractors and serve as an interface for information flow between the Customer, Company and the subcontractor

  • Assist in the general operation and the day to day demands of the Q.A. Dept.

  • 制定公司原材料、半成品和产成品的质量标准，设计新的复杂或创新措施、测试、样品和其他程序，达到标准。

  • 与客户建立直接沟通的渠道，并提供符合或超越客户要求的零件

  • 协助供应商的发展，为客户、公司和供应商之间提供有效沟通

  • 协助日常生产运营和质量部的日常工作

Performance Expectations

  • Ensure compliance with all legislative requirements in Linamar’s geographic locations and:

The Global Operating System Plays

ISO 45001 Health and Safety management system standard

ISO 14001 Environmental Management system standard

IATF 16949 Technical Specification Management and Linamar Quality Basics system standards

  • Support, Foster, Promote and Demonstrate Linamar’s Core Values and Leadership Behaviors.

  • Perform quality analysis and inspections, for example data analysis, benchmark testing, localization, and destructive/non-destructive analysis. Evaluate raw materials, components, and other inputs to the production process and liaise with external vendors and suppliers to ensure that their products meet the organization's quality standards. Evaluate the organization's manufacturing processes, systems, and technology and recommend changes that will improve the quality of the organization's finished products. Maintain and analyze quality records to prepare regular and ad hoc reports; check that established testing procedures are being followed; identify and address recurring problems either with the quality of the product or the reliability of testing procedures. Educate and train quality assurance inspectors and other staff to ensure that they understand and comply with established quality procedures and standards.
