Job Title: Manufacturing Engineer -Machining

Job Category: Manufacturing Engineer

Job Level: Global Grade 9

Why join Rolls-Royce?

At Rolls-Royce, we take pride in being a business that has played a pivotal role in shaping the modern world and remain dedicated to driving progress forward; powering, safeguarding, and connecting people across the globe. Joining Rolls-Royce means you will work on innovative, world-class engineering solutions, supported by a culture that values inclusion, creativity, and your ongoing professional development.

As a Manufacturing Engineer at the Rolls-Royce Power Systems Aiken plant, you will play a critical leadership role in optimizing and executing cost-effective manufacturing processes at both local and global levels. You will be responsible for machining production flow, assembly methods, and equipment selection, ensuring that all processes meet product specifications and quality standards across the organization. This role involves leading continuous improvement initiatives, mentoring team members, and ensuring a strong compliance and safety culture with a focus on both local and global operations.

What you will be doing:

  • Provide day-to-day manufacturing engineering support for Machining assembly lines, focusing on production stability and performance.
  • Support implementation and sustainment of assembly processes, tooling, fixtures, and equipment.
  • Troubleshoot manufacturing issues and support root-cause analysis and corrective actions.
  • Develop and update work instructions, standard work, and process documentation.
  • Participate in continuous improvement activities targeting quality, efficiency, safety, and ergonomics.
  • Collaborate with Operations, Quality, and Maintenance teams to support daily production needs.
  • Support change management activities related to product, process, or equipment updates.
  • Collect and analyze production data to support performance monitoring and improvement initiatives.
  • Promote compliance with safety, quality, and company standards.
  • Perform additional engineering or improvement tasks as assigned.

.

Position requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, or Manufacturing Engineering, and 3 years of experience in machining engineering; or 7 years of experience in machining engineering
  • “Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the U.S without sponsorship. We are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of an employment Visa at this time.”

Preferred qualifications:

  • Experience in a manufacturing or assembly environment.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills related to both CNC and traditional machining operations.
  • Proficiency in CNC programming (e.g., G-code, CAM software) and machining process optimization.
  • Strong knowledge of CNC machine tools, manual machining equipment, cutting tool technology, and fixture design.
  • Strong experience with process capability analysis, statistical process control (SPC), and lean manufacturing principles.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills to work effectively with operators, engineers, and management.
  • Ability to interpret technical drawings, GD&T, and specifications for machining applications.
  • Proficient in CAD/CAM software (e.g., PTC Creo, Mastercam, Siemens NX).
  • Ability to travel domestically and internationally as needed.
  • Ability to work overtime as required to support production or project work.
  • Strong knowledge of materials and methods of manufacturing.
  • Good ability to read and interpret technical drawings and specifications.
  • Strong organization, planning, and follow-up skills.
  • Good project management skills.
  • Proficient with PC and MS Office Suite.
  • Knowledge of Rolls-Royce Solutions products, policies, and procedures.
  • Experience with SAP for production planning and manufacturing execution.
  • Experience with automation and robotics in machining.
  • Experience with CNC machines, manual machining equipment, Computer, Copier, Phone, Fax.
  • Familiarity with Industry 4.0 and digital manufacturing technologies.
  • 3D CAD experience with PTC Creo Parametric.
