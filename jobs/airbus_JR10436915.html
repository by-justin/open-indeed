Job Description:

Product Owner

About the Role

We are a dynamic, results-driven team at the forefront of digital transformation within the aviation industry.

We do not just build software; we architect the data-driven future of our operations.

Our mission is to secure today’s operations and shape the future of Customer Services by bridging digital with business and customers.

We are seeking a visionary Product Owner with deep expertise in AI-driven product development to manage next-generation products that utilize massive datasets to deliver tangible business value and operational excellence.

Our Core Mission

We empower Airbus customers by leveraging the foundry platform—the aviation industry’s most powerful cloud-based data ecosystem. As a Product Owner, you will spearhead the design and deployment of intelligent applications in connectivity, maintenance training, tech data and

Key Responsibilities

  • AI Product Strategy: Define and execute the product vision and roadmap, specifically focusing on integrating Machine Learning and AI capabilities into our core aviation offerings.
  • Strategic Backlog Management: Develop and maintain a high-value, prioritized backlog of user stories. Ensure all development efforts are aligned with strategic business goals and customer outcomes.
  • Stakeholder Orchestration: Collaborate closely with Business Owners, System Architects, Release Train Engineers, and Product Managers to align product vision with technical feasibility.
  • Lifecycle Ownership: Own the full product lifecycle, from concept and experimentation to industrialization. Define iteration objectives, Acceptance Criteria, and the Definition of Ready (DoR) / Definition of Done (DoD).
  • Lean-Agile Leadership: Demonstrate strong Lean-Agile principles. Facilitate Program Increment (PI) planning and coordinate across Agile Release Trains (ARTs) to ensure seamless delivery.
  • Value Delivery: Assess business value, develop robust business cases, and ensure development work consistently delivers maximum impact on efficiency, fleet safety, and profitability.

Required Domain Background

  • Aviation Expertise: Solid understanding of Aircraft/Airline Operations, Training, Maintenance, and Engineering.
  • Technical Proficiency: Extensive experience with Big Data, Time Series concepts, and sensor data integration.
  • AI/ML Fluency: Demonstrated experience in translating complex business problems into AI-powered solutions.

Essential Skills and Qualifications

  • Education: BE / B. Tech / M. Tech / MBA or equivalent advanced degree.
  • Experience: 5+ years of relevant product ownership experience, with a specific track record of delivering AI or machine learning-enabled products.
  • Certification: Certified Product Owner (SAFe, CSPO, or PSPO) is highly preferred.
  • Agile Mastery: Deep knowledge of Agile methodologies (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban).
  • Soft Skills: Exceptional communication, stakeholder management, and interpersonal skills. Ability to lead cross-functional teams through ambiguity.
  • Mindset: Strategic and creative thinker with a strong focus on Design Thinking and user-centric problem solving.
