Description de l'emploi :

Vous avez une expérience dans l’industrie aéronautique, vous avez travaillé en ingénierie de conception de structure avion et/ou d’ingénierie de maintenance structure et vous souhaitez évoluer et développer de nouvelles compétences? Ce poste est pour vous !

Le programme d'avions commerciaux A220 recherche un analyste programme de maintenance structure (h/f) pour rejoindre notre équipe d'ingénierie de maintenance basée à Mirabel (Québec, Canada), où vous travaillerez conjointement avec des équipes diverses et variées d’Airbus telles que, sans s’y limiter, l’équipe d’ingénierie de conception structure avion, l'équipe d’ingénierie de réparation en service, l’équipe du bureau du programme de l’A220 ou bien encore les équipes de navigabilité, fiabilité et sécurité avion.

Vous ferez partie de l’équipe de gestion du programme de Maintenance chargée de / qui développe développement d’un programme de maintenance cohérent et économique pour toutes nouvelles conceptions structure ou amélioration de ces dernières pour le programme d’avion commercial A220..

L'organisation de l'équipe est établie de manière à promouvoir la collaboration transversale de manière autonome, en se soutenant mutuellement et en apprenant collectivement.

Votre rôle en tant qu’Analyste programme de maintenance structure avion sera de développer, surveiller, remettre en question et améliorer tous les requis de maintenance planifiée des structures, zones, harnais électriques d'interconnexion (EWIS) ainsi que les dispositifs de protection au foudroiement et champs rayonnés à haute intensité (L/HIRF) dont vous serez responsable. Pour ce faire, vous devrez principalement adopter une approche systémique et hiérarchique d’analyse des structures significatives et zones avion en vous appuyant sur la méthodologie de maintenance centrée sur la fiabilité (MSG-3) ainsi que l'expérience en service tout en veillant à rester conforme à la réglementation en matière de maintien de la navigabilité..

Job description :

Do you have experience in the aerospace industry, have you worked in airframe structure design engineering and/or airframe structure maintenance engineering and would you like to progress and develop new skills? Then this job is for you!

The A220 commercial aircraft Programme is looking for an structure maintenance program analyst (f/m) to join our Maintenance engineering team based in Mirabel (Québec, Canada), where you will work jointly with various Airbus teams such as but not limited to the Airframe structure Design engineering team, the In-Service repair engineering team, The A220 program office team and the Airworthiness, reliability & safety teams.

You will be part of the Maintenance program management team responsible for developing a consistent and cost effective maintenance program of new airframe structure designs or improvements of the latter on the A220 commercial aircraft Programme.

The team organization is established to promote cross-collaboration autonomously, supporting each other and learning collectively.

Your role as structure maintenance program analyst will be to develop, monitor, challenge and improve all the scheduled maintenance requirements of the aircraft structure, zonal, Electrical wiring interconnection system (EWIS) as well as  lightning and high intensity radiated field (L/HIRF) protection devices you will be accountable for. To do so, you will have to primarily apply a holistic and top-down analysis approach on significant structural items and aircraft zones based on reliability centered maintenance methodology (MSG-3) and in-service experience in accordance with the continuous airworthiness regulation.

Your working environment:

The A220 commercial aircraft Programme is headquartered in Mirabel, in the greater Montreal area, where the vibrant city of Montreal is just a stone’s throw away. Known for its peaceful and safe environment, the region offers a high quality of life for younger or experienced professionals and families, perfectly balancing career opportunities and outdoor activities that makes it a truly special place to call home.

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, annual bonus, group insurance plan, pension plan and share purchase plan.
  • Work / Life Balance:  A hybrid working policy, on-site cafeterias and a highly competitive holiday policy.
  • Wellbeing/Health: Employee Assistance Program (EAP), Discount Program,  on-site Medical Service, shuttle service between Montreal and Mirabel and carpooling application.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects  with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

Your challenges:

  • Develop, optimize and update the recommended scheduled maintenance tasks based on MSG-3 methodology for the A220 airframe structures, zones, EWIS and L/HIRF you are accountable for, in order to guarantee an efficient aircraft maintenance program from safety, availability and economic perspectives.
  • Identify and lead initiatives to optimize the mandatory scheduled maintenance tasks derived from the type certification process (Airworthiness limitations).
  • Take ownership and assess any A220 design development or improvement to influence any potential design modifications and/or incorporation schedule adjustment to target an efficient maintenance program implementation.
  • Consider all the A220 fleet in-service issues and feedback from our customers to improve the A220 maintenance program and enable an increased operational performance and customer satisfaction.
  • Coordinate with a team of 6 people to jointly identify the scope of work, then define, coordinate and drive the plan to complete all required activities on due time and provide visibility to your management.
  • Develop, review and approve all scheduled maintenance tasks under your responsibility through “paper” validation and on-aircraft validation.
  • Identify, develop and implement, on your own initiative, any processes and standalone projects to make  the A220 maintenance program development more effective
  • Prepare, present and strongly support all Structure, Zonal, EWIS and L/HIRF Maintenance program updates you are accountable for in front of A220 customers (airlines) and Civil aviation Authorities for approval.

Your boarding pass:

  • Graduated with a Bachelor of engineering degree in Mechanical or Aerospace.
  • Minimum of 5 years of professional experience preferably with Airlines or MRO in Airframe structure design engineering or Airframe structure maintenance engineering.
  • Expertise on some of the following ATA chapters: 32, 52, 53, 54, 55.
  • Good general knowledge in the maintenance engineering concepts including structure, zonal, EWIS and L/HIRF and the continuous airworthiness regulation applicable to modern civil aircraft.
  • Leadership and project management skills.
  • Customer focus, quick learner, adaptative and Excellent presentation skills.
  • Proficiency in standard Google suite tools mandatory for the job. Comfortable with digital solutions.
  • Minimum Language skills: advanced level of english, beginner level of french.
