Voici quelques exemples :

  • Fournir un soutien pour le rapprochement des comptes fournisseurs : Analyser les causes profondes des éléments non rapprochés et élaborer un plan d'action pour résoudre les problèmes.
  • Soutenir le processus d’audit interne.
  • Supporter le processus semi-annuel de revue des inventaires.
  • Produire des rapports financiers mensuels.
  • Développer des documents de formation.

Why join our team?

The intern will have to provide support on various financial operations governance activities as well as on ad hoc requests from team members. He must demonstrate intellectual curiosity and resourcefulness in order to accomplish his various mandates.

What will your day-to-day look like?

Here are some examples :

  • Provide support for accounts payable reconciliation: Analyze the root causes of unreconciled items and develop an action plan to resolve the issues.
  • Support the internal audit process.
  • Support the semi-annual inventory review process.
  • Produce monthly financial reports.
  • Develop training materials.
