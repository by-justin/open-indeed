Shape the future of Neurovascular marketing across EMEA.

At Stryker, we're looking for an exceptional marketing leader who wants to influence strategy, develop high-performing teams and drive meaningful commercial impact across one of our fastest-growing EMEA businesses.

It's an opportunity to lead experienced marketers, shape portfolio strategy across EMEA, influence senior leadership decisions and help build the next generation of marketing capability within Neurovascular.

Neurovascular: Neurovascular | Stryker

What you will do

As Senior Manager, Brand Marketing , you'll be the EMEA brand expert responsible for setting strategic direction and ensuring successful execution across a diverse portfolio.

You'll lead and develop a team of experienced Brand Managers while partnering closely with commercial leadership, local marketing teams and global stakeholders to deliver sustainable portfolio growth.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Lead, coach and develop a high-performing team of Brand Managers, creating an environment where talent can thrive.
  • Define and execute the annual and long-term EMEA portfolio strategy aligned to global business objectives.
  • Drive portfolio growth through innovative marketing campaigns, customer engagement initiatives and commercial excellence.
  • Develop scalable EMEA marketing assets, campaigns and best practices that can be successfully localized across markets.
  • Build strong partnerships with local marketing teams, commercial leaders, supply chain partners, key opinion leaders and global stakeholders.
  • Create and deliver annual marketing plans and develop a 3–5-year portfolio roadmap.
  • Partner with divisional leadership on portfolio development, new product launches, lifecycle management and M&A integration opportunities.
  • Lead EMEA product lifecycle management, including product launches, portfolio optimisation and product phase-outs.
  • Analyse business performance, market trends, customer insights and competitive dynamics to identify growth opportunities and recommend strategic actions.
  • Develop customer engagement strategies including congresses, hands-on training, education programmes and facility visits.
  • Partner with the Education team to identify capability gaps and create impactful internal training programmes.
  • Drive brand positioning, messaging and pricing strategy based on customer, market and competitive insights.
  • Develop and strengthen relationships with key opinion leaders across EMEA.
  • Mentor junior Brand Managers and help build the next generation of marketing leadership.
  • Act as a visible role model for Stryker's values, culture and leadership behaviours.

Who we're looking for

We're looking for an experienced marketing leader who combines strategic thinking with exceptional people leadership.

You enjoy simplifying complexity, coaching others to perform at their best and translating commercial insight into actionable marketing strategies.

You are:

A Talent Developer

You build high-performing teams by recruiting exceptional people, developing future leaders and creating an environment where individuals can grow.

A Strategic Business Partner

You understand how marketing drives commercial performance and can confidently influence senior stakeholders through insight, data and clear strategic thinking.

A Hands-on Leader

You're comfortable rolling up your sleeves, challenging ideas, facilitating strategic discussions and coaching teams through complex business decisions.

A Matrix Collaborator

You build strong relationships across functions, countries and leadership teams, influencing without relying on formal authority.

A Performance Driver

You continuously improve processes, raise standards and inspire teams to deliver measurable business impact.
