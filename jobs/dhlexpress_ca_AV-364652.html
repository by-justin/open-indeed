Sales Executive (m/f/d)

Location: Warsaw, Poland und Wien, Austria employment: Full-time

Join us at Hillebrand Gori – a company of DHL Global Forwarding

Are you passionate about shipping, beverages, innovation and exceptional customer service? Hillebrand Gori is the beer, wine, and spirits logistics powerhouse of the DHL Group. We go beyond moving containers, we connect people with products they love.

With a global reach across the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe and Middle East & Africa, we are one of the most renowned beverage logistics company in the world. Start your career with our amazing team! Learn more about us: https://www.hillebrandgori.com

We are looking for a motivated and results-oriented Sales Executive (m/f/d) to support the growth of our business through a combination of existing and new customer management and pricing activities . This role requires a proactive sales professional who can independently manage customer relationships, identify new opportunities and contribute to pricing strategies that support sustainable business growth.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and develop relationships with existing customers to ensure high customer satisfaction and retention.
  • Identify, address and win new customers in the Polish market.
  • Prepare, negotiate and follow up on commercial offers and contracts.
  • Develop and execute sales strategies to meet revenue and growth goals.
  • Analyze customer requirements and provide customized commercial solutions.
  • Support pricing activities, including price calculations, price analysis, quoting, and execution of pricing strategies.
  • Work closely with internal teams, including operations, customer service, and finance.
  • Monitor market trends, customer needs, and competitor activity to identify growth opportunities.
  • Keep accurate customer and sales data in the CRM system.

Qualifications

  • Several years of successful sales experience, preferably in a B2B environment.
  • Proven track record in both account management and new business customer development.
  • Experience in pricing, commercial analysis or quotation management is highly desirable.
  • Native or fluent Polish speakers and good English skills are required.
  • Knowledge of German is an advantage.
  • Strong communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills.
  • Self-motivated, proactive and able to work independently.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Willingness to travel within the assigned region if necessary.

What we offer

  • A challenging and diverse sales role with a high degree of autonomy.
  • Flexible working arrangements.
  • An international and dynamic working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for professional and personal development.
  • Competitive compensation package and modern work tools (incl. Company Car).

Interested? We look forward to receiving your application, including your earliest possible start date and salary expectations.
