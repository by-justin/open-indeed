JOB SUMMARY

Provide onsite quality support across all areas of the plant through the assessment, implementation, and maintenance of quality systems that meet or exceed customer and IATF/TS standards. This role is heavily focused on plant-floor presence, hands-on support of manufacturing processes, formal problem-solving, and driving continuous improvement in product quality, cost, and customer satisfaction. The position works closely with production, engineering, suppliers, and customers to strengthen process controls, resolve issues quickly, and support a high-performing manufacturing operation. The ideal candidate is self-motivated, action-oriented, technically strong, and comfortable working in a fast-paced plant environment.

JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Provide daily onsite quality support across manufacturing areas, working directly with plant teams to assess issues, support process control, and improve product quality

  • Work closely with production, engineering, maintenance, and other plant functions to support issue resolution and sustain effective manufacturing process controls

  • Conduct internal quality system audits and monitor the progress and verification of corrective actions

  • Serve as the primary customer contact for APQP, warranty issues, and miscellaneous quality-related concerns

  • Analyze returned product for warranty resolution, coordinate laboratory analysis as needed, and document and distribute results

  • Serve as the primary supplier contact for APQP, PPAP, and other quality planning processes

  • Interface directly with suppliers to coordinate containment, corrective action, and resolution of failures related to parts and services

  • Partner with the corporate Purchasing Team to support resolution plans for supplier quality concerns

  • Support product and process changes for house accounts, including launch RFQs, design change requests, print specification review, material recommendations, and prototype launches as required

  • Provide training in quality systems and related areas such as APQP, BOS, problem solving, and SPC

  • Instruct employees on Statistical Process Control fundamentals, including the use of electronic and paper-generated SPC charts

  • Obtain, analyze, and report statistical data from internal and external sources as required by plant leadership, customers, and other stakeholders

  • Assist in the development of process flow diagrams, control plans, inspection methods, and related process documentation

  • Create, maintain, and improve standard procedures and quality system documentation

  • Create operating instructions and supporting quality documentation, including R&R studies, Cpk/CPH analysis, and containment documentation

  • Follow Quality Management System requirements outlined in applicable procedures and work instructions, and identify opportunities for continual improvement
