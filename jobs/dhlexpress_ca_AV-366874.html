Position Overview:

The Executive / Senior Executive – SMART will be responsible for servicing small and mid-sized customer accounts by

ensuring timely query resolution, shipment tracking, complaint handling, and SLA adherence. The role ensures accurate

service execution, strong customer communication, and operational coordination to maintain customer satisfaction.

Job Purpose:

To provide reliable and responsive customer service support for SMART segment customers, ensuring high service

quality, reduced escalations, and consistent SLA adherence.

Key Roles & Responsibilities:

Customer Query Management

• Handle inbound and outbound customer communication.

• Provide accurate shipment status updates.

• Resolve queries related to pickups, deliveries, transit delays, and billing.

Complaint Handling

• Log complaints in system and track closure within TAT.

• Coordinate with Operations to resolve service issues.

• Escalate unresolved cases to Manager – SMART.

Shipment Monitoring

• Monitor critical shipments and provide proactive updates.

• Track service deviations and ensure timely closure.

Data Accuracy & System Compliance

• Ensure 100% accurate data entry in CRM systems.

• Maintain call quality standards and documentation accuracy.

• Adhere to defined service scripts and communication standards.

Customer Retention Support

• Build positive relationships with customers through consistent communication.

• Support Manager – SMART in account performance tracking.

EMS

• Ensure that EMS is properly implemented and that environmental matters are properly handled at all stages

• The incumbent should be ISO 14001 :2015 trained or has a good awareness about Environmental initiatives/ ESG

• Should be able to handle environmental customer complaints smoothly
