Job Description

MANNARINO Systems & Software Inc. is a leader in providing safety-critical systems, software, hardware and certification engineering services to the aerospace, defense, space, simulation & power generation industries. Our customer base is very large, covering traditional aerospace companies as well as new and exciting markets, including electric propulsion, Urban Air Mobility, Unmanned Aerial Vehicle and autonomous flight. The MANNARINO culture is focused on high quality, hard work, professionalism and teamwork within a highly respectful, motivating atmosphere geared towards long-term business growth. As a member of our team, you will benefit from:

  • a comprehensive group insurance and RRSP plan
  • a flextime policy with paid overtime
  • discretionary bonuses
  • a fitness reimbursement program
  • other office perks such as catered weekly lunches and free hot beverages (espresso, coffee, hot chocolate and tea)
  • regular company outings Career Opportunity and Primary Roles / Responsibilities
  • Join the Test Means and Tools Development team supporting the Integration, Verification, Validation & Qualification (IVVQ) organization in the aerospace sector.
  • Design, develop, integrate, test, and maintain physical and virtual test bench solutions for embedded avionics systems.
  • Develop and integrate software applications on physical and virtual test platforms.
  • Assemble, configure, and integrate hardware components for aerospace test benches.
  • Develop models and virtual representations of aircraft equipment and systems.
  • Create test tools, support applications, automated scripts, and acceptance test suites.
  • Integrate test tools and automation scripts into existing engineering processes and workflows.
  • Participate in supplier evaluation, technical selection activities, and follow-up after purchase order issuance.
  • Produce and maintain technical documentation related to test means, tools, and test bench solutions.
  • Support change control activities and continuous improvement initiatives for test means and tools.
  • Support and participate in IVVQ test campaigns, troubleshooting activities, and system integration efforts.
  • Analyze electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, and system interfaces to support integration and troubleshooting. Required Competencies
  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Experience integrating hardware and software components in complex systems.
  • Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to investigate and resolve integration issues and system failures.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.
  • Experience programming in one or more languages such as LabVIEW, Simulink, C/C++, Python, Rust, or database technologies is considered an asset.
  • Experience developing real-time applications is considered an asset.
  • Knowledge of object-oriented programming principles is considered an asset.
  • Knowledge of hydraulic systems is considered an asset.
  • Experience with CAD tools and technical drawings is considered an asset.
  • Ability to read, analyze, and interpret electrical drawings and wiring schematics.
  • Strong communication, collaboration, and technical documentation skills. Miscellaneous Requirements
  • Work can be done remotely.
  • For employees based in Montreal, the work model is hybrid (at least one day at the office).
  • The position might require travel to visit customers (in North America) Knowledge of English is required to communicate and work with customers outside the province of Quebec. / La connaissance de l'anglais est requise pour communiquer et travailler avec nos clients situés à l'extérieur de la province de Québec.
