The Department of Hospitals & Surgical Health Services establishes the Government of Alberta’s strategic direction for health, including advising government on health policy, legislation and standards, and public health concerns; monitoring and reporting health system performance; setting policies and priorities for the electronic/digital health environment; and providing oversight and ensuring accountability across the health system. For further information, visit the ministry website at https://www.alberta.ca/hospital-and-surgical-health-services This position resides in the Ministry of Hospitals & Surgical Health Services under the Acute Care Division, within the Cancer and Surgical Services Branch. Role Responsibilities Reporting to the Executive Director, Cancer & Surgical Services, the Director, Medical Assistance in Dying (MAID) provides strategic leadership and operational oversight for Alberta's Medical Assistance in Dying (MAID) portfolio. This role leads the development and implementation of policies, programs, and initiatives that support safe, equitable, and effective access to MAID services across the province. Key Responsibilities: Lead MAID policy development, strategic planning, and program implementation. Provide expert advice to senior leadership, the Minister's Office, and key stakeholders on complex policy and operational issues. Oversee issues management, legislative and policy reviews, and emerging trends impacting MAID services. Build and maintain strong relationships with government partners, healthcare organizations, and stakeholders to advance Ministry priorities. Lead cross-functional teams and collaborative initiatives to improve access, service delivery, and patient outcomes. Represent the Ministry on cross-ministry, provincial, and national committees and working groups. Foster innovation, continuous improvement, and evidence-based decision-making within the MAID portfolio. This position requires a collaborative and strategic leader with strong policy, stakeholder engagement, and relationship management skills, capable of navigating complex healthcare issues and driving meaningful outcomes for Albertans.
