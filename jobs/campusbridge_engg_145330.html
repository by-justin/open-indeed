Job Description

Interested students must apply on the company website and campusBRIDGE to be considered for the job

Tourmaline Oil Corp. is a senior Canadian crude oil and natural gas exploration and production company. We are focused on long-term growth through an aggressive exploration, development, and acquisition program in the Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin. We are currently seeking a Data and Analytics Intern Student to join our Calgary office. This is a term position beginning in January 2027 and open to either 8, 12 or 16-month work terms.

Job Requirements

Key Responsibilities

The successful candidate will be responsible for, or assist with, the following duties, but not limited to:

• Support the Capital Operations teams by helping develop, improve, and maintain applications, automations, and reports using Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI.

• Work with team members to translate business needs into clear technical requirements and help build the solutions that address them.

• Assist in building reports and dashboards that communicate information to internal stakeholders, support financial and capital analysis, and help track project efficiency and risk.

• Look for opportunities to optimize existing processes and apply technology where it makes sense; bring a curious, improvement-minded approach to the work.

• Collaborate with internal developers and data analysts to learn from and contribute to existing tools.

• Ensure proper data governance through integration with the Tourmaline Information Technology Systems (ITS) data team.

• Other duties as required.

Qualifications

• Currently enrolled in and completing third or fourth year of an engineering program.

• Ability to think strategically and logically.

• Detail oriented.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to explain ideas clearly to others.

• Knowledge of Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI), SQL, or Power Query is considered an asset, but not required.

• Interest or coursework related to oil and gas is considered an asset.

• Strong problem-solving skills and a natural curiosity about how things work and how they could work better.

• Team-oriented with a willingness to learn, ask questions, and take initiative.

• Comfortable working independently in a fast-paced environment.
