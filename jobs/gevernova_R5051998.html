Job Description (English)

  1. Job Title HRSG Production Process Control Engineer

  2. Department Production Team

  3. Location GE Power Systems Korea (Changwon, Gyeongsangnam-do)

  4. Job Summary We are seeking an expert to manage the overall manufacturing process of HRSG (Heat Recovery Steam Generator). This role will lead core production operations—ranging from production planning and field process optimization to subcontractor management and cost settlement—to establish a stable and efficient product manufacturing system.

  5. Key Responsibilities

Production Planning & Execution: Establish production schedules based on project milestones and manage weekly/monthly production plans. Manage process lead times and resolve bottlenecks to ensure on-time delivery. Cross-Functional & Subcontractor Coordination: Collaborate closely with internal departments (Engineering, Procurement, Quality) to resolve production issues. Coordinate production schedules with internal and external partners (manufacturing/installation). Cost Management & Progress Billing: Review subcontractor progress billing based on project completion rates. Identify cost-saving opportunities and manage manufacturing cost settlements through production efficiency analysis. Process Optimization & KPI Management: Manage productivity KPIs and perform on-site improvement activities (e.g., process simplification, improvement proposals). Analyze production issues and promote quality/safety improvements through work standardization.

  1. Qualifications

Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or related fields. Experience: 3+ years of experience in production or process control within the HRSG, plant, or heavy equipment manufacturing industry. Core Competencies: Understanding of large-scale mechanical structure fabrication processes and Project Management (PM). Proficiency in production scheduling tools (MS Project, Primavera, etc.) and ERP systems. Strong communication and coordination skills with both internal stakeholders and external partners. Ability to create productivity reports through data analysis (Excel, Power BI, etc.). Preferred Qualifications: Holders of relevant professional certifications (General Mechanical Engineer, Welding Engineer, Industrial Safety Engineer, Quality Management Engineer).

  1. Key Competencies

Communication: Strong oral and written communication skills in English for effective business performance. Execution: Proficiency in documentation, planning, execution, problem-solving, and decision-making. Leadership: Team management skills and a collaborative leadership style. Mindset: Strong awareness of quality and safety; a commitment to Continuous Improvement (CI). Technical Knowledge: Professional technical understanding of welding and fabrication processes. Systems Experience: Experience in operating SAP or similar ERP systems. Strategic Planning: Experience in flexible production scheduling and alternative sourcing strategies in light of global supply chain issues. Other: Candidates must pass mandatory pre-employment procedures, including a health check. Failure to meet requirements may lead to the cancellation of the offer.

  1. Ideal Candidate Profile

Someone who can logically analyze complex process issues and propose effective alternatives. A leader who can respond flexibly to changing site conditions and foster effective collaboration. An individual with a strong sense of cost-consciousness and a drive to maximize production efficiency.
