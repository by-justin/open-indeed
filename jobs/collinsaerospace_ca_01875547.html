Collins Aerospace is looking for a Continuous Improvement Specialist for its Airbus A220 nacelle integration site in Mirabel, Quebec.

This position is Onsite at Collins Aerospace - Mirabel location - Quebec - Canada

What You Will Do

  • Participates in execution of operational plans in support of Goal Alignment goals and initiatives for the organization.
  • Drives improvement of metrics and ensuring targets are met including event/project closure results achieved.
  • Supports and leads projects/transformations that cut across organizational, functional and internal boundaries.
  • Actively participates/leads in resolution of difficult or complex problems.
  • Actively improves and implements current and new processes and standards that will have impact on the achievement of functional results.
  • Coaching and training individuals and teams within the business on their Continuous Improvement journey.
  • Leads the identification and sharing best practices across the organization.
  • Measures and drives CORE/Continuous Improvement progression within direct team.
  • Leads kaizen/ Continuous Improvement events including leadership weeks.
  • Builds networks across the organization and discipline.
  • Identifies knowledge gaps within direct team and supports closure plan.
  • Champions the use of lean tools to drive cultural transformation.

Qualifications You Must Have

  • Typically requires: Bachelor’s Degree and minimum 5 years of prior relevant experience or Advanced degree and minimum 3 years of prior relevant experience.
  • Bilingual English and French
  • Experience implementing Lean principles for example: 5S, Standard Work, Visual Controls, RTPR / QCPC, Kaizen events.
  • Experience in Data and Statistical Analysis and 8 Ds.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Demonstrated experience working in a team environment.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong customer focus.
  • Effective interpersonal skills, strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong desire to drive process improvement.
  • Leadership skills and experience

This position requires communicating orally and in writing with working teams and customer located in the United States. Following an analysis, we have determined that the tasks related to the position of production continuous improvement analyst require knowledge of English (verbal and written). We have also determined that the knowledge of English already required of other employees does not allow the performance of the tasks due to its highly technical nature.
