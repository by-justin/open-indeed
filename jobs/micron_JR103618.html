Job Description – Solution Lead – EPS Planning & AI Enablement (BY COE)

“Lead the transformation to a cognitive, AI-driven supply chain by integrating planning (IBP) and execution (SCE) into a unified, closed-loop decisioning ecosystem”

Role Overview

This role will drive end-to-end supply chain transformation across Planning (IBP) and Execution within the Blue Yonder COE, with focus on:

  • Cognitive Business Planning (CBP) adoption
  • AI/GenAI-enabled decision intelligence
  • Tight integration between planning and execution layers

The leader will ensure synchronized decision-making from strategy → planning → execution , enabling faster, more reliable and scalable supply chain outcomes.
