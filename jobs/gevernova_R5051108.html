Job Description

Job Description:

GE Vernova delivers reactive power compensators such as Static Synchronous Compensators (STATCOM) and Static Var Compensators (SVC), on which we are the global FACTS Solution Line based in Finland, India, Brazil and US. These systems are used to control power system voltage and reactive power flow as well as to improve the power system stability and transmission system efficiency. Via our projects, you truly have a chance to make the world a better place by helping our global customers in saving vital energy and securing stable electricity distribution.

We are looking for Engineering Professionals to join the FACTS Solution Line team. The position will be based in Noida, India with remote working flexibility. You will be working on cutting edge technology and varied international high-profile projects. In this position, you will be reporting to Engineering Manager – Secondary Engineering, Noida office and it can be considered a top priority position to perform in a critical engineering discipline for overall FACTS business.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Control & Protection Hardware Engineering

  • Prepare detailed hardware specification, bill of materials for Substation & STATCOM Projects.
  • Develop control and protection panel designs, GA drawings, schematics, relay list, bay control units, marshalling kiosks, and automation panels.
  • Prepare:
  • Protection single line diagrams
  • Control schematics using EPLAN/E3/EBASE etc.
  • Interconnection diagrams
  • GA drawings
  • Cable schedules
  • Terminal plans
  • Panel layout drawing
  • Bill of Materials (BOM)
  • Rack layout
  • Design AC/DC auxiliary supply distribution schemes
  • Develop I/O allocation and interface engineering for protection relays, RTUs, BCUs, SCADA, STATCOM Controllers, Ethernet Switches etc.
  • Prepare station-wide control and interlocking philosophy implementation.
  • Design reviews with clients/consultants
  • Participate in FAT/SAT activities where required

STATCOM Specific Engineering

  • Develop hardware engineering for:
  • STATCOM Control & Protection systems
  • Valve cooling control interface
  • Transformer protection system
  • Line bay protection
  • MSC/MSR bay protection
  • Harmonic filter protection
  • Coordinate interfaces between Unit Controller, Station Controller, PPC, HMI & Grid Automation system.
  • Prepare communication architecture drawings and interface documentations.

Protection Systems

  • Knowledge of protection schemes for:
  • Transmission line
  • Power transformer
  • Busbars
  • Reactors
  • Capacitor banks
  • Harmonic filter
  • STATCOM branches

Site commissioning support

  • Pre-commissioning work of Control & Protection systems at site
  • Pre-STATCOM control system
  • Review and apply design stage documents/settings at site.
  • Review site modifications and prepare engineering updates
  • Perform primary and secondary injection testing of protection relays
  • Support energization and performance testing activities.

Standard knowledge

  • IEC 61000
  • Utility technical specifications

Engineering Documentation

  • Relay setting calculation
  • Maintain documentation as per project quality plans
