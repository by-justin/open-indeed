Job Description:

Department Overview

Join Our Business Intelligence and Insights (BII) Team within the Monitoring Coverage and Capabilities, where you will play a pivotal role in advancing our Financial Crime and Risk Management (FCRM) initiatives. Become a key player in our BII team, where you will help design robust data platforms, transform data into actionable insights, support critical investigations with analytics expertise, and drive innovation by developing cutting-edge tools and technologies. Make a real impact on how our organization leverages data to inform strategic decisions and accelerate growth.

Job Description Summary

Provide specialized end to end BI support, visualization / presentation, and solutions for a significant business/function and/or projects/initiative. This role requires strong experience in SQL,  Oracle development, Microsoft Azure Cloud (Rahona), Python, PowerBI and Databricks. May be considered a subject matter expert in BI and Reporting. Accountable for the development, adoption and ongoing evolution of a best-in-class reporting and/or BI capability and may deliver on ad-hoc projects as assigned.

Job Description

  • Build basic layouts and templates to be utilized by internal partners.

  • Develop meaningful and insightful commentary/ insights on new and existing reporting packages.
  • Work with business partners to understand business objectives and develop value-added reporting and provide ad-hoc data extracts.
  • Adopt, implement and effectively evolve report automation and/or visualization standards.
