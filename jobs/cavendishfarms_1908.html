Description

Are you a results-driven leader who can manage people?

Do you want to join a world-class team and take your career to the next level?

We are seeking a high-performance Production Supervisor to join our management team in our state-of-the-art Kedgwick Sawmill facility located in Kedgwick, NB.  This opportunity is ideal for candidates looking to build their career in a rapidly growing, global company that can challenge you and enable you to reach your full potential. Candidates located in the Kedgwick area or candidates open to relocation are encouraged to apply.

Reporting to the Assistant Site Manager, the Production Supervisor drives on-site employee performance toward the achievement of company business objectives related to safety, productivity, quality, continuous improvement, and efficiency.  The Production Supervisor achieves results through others by providing clear communication, effective coaching, and addressing employee concerns to promote a culture of teamwork, accountability, and strong engagement. As the Supervisor, you will work alongside your team to motivate and enhance their capabilities through effective performance management, addressing key performance indicators, training, and team development.

You will receive:

•    Competitive base salary and production bonus

•    Full benefits: Health, Vision, Dental, Life, and ST/LT Disability Insurance

•    RRSP matching (5%)

•    Career development and education reimbursement assistance

•    Leading in-house leadership development and technical training

•    Apprenticeship, internship, and co-op support and opportunities available

Responsibilities

You will deliver:

•    Leadership, support, and direction to a production team of 16-20 employees

•    Ensure quality, safety, engagement, and environmental standards are being met

•    Monitor productivity throughout the shift and adjust tasks, resources, scheduling, or inputs so that production targets are met and resources are used efficiently

•    Training and development for team members

•    Support continuous improvement efforts (gather suggestions, report problems, lead team problem-solving sessions, coach problem resolution)

•    Administrative support such as scheduling personnel, submitting weekly payroll, and daily reporting

Qualifications

You will bring:

•    Post-secondary degree, diploma, or trade certification - preferably in industrial technology or engineering, forestry, business administration, or management would be an asset

•    Ideal candidates will possess 1+ years of supervisory experience along with the demonstrated the ability to lead a team. Experience in an industrial or production environment is preferred

•    Enjoy working in a fast-paced, dynamic environment

•    Possess excellent communication skills, both written and verbal

•    Committed to top performance in a safe work environment

•    Demonstrate the ability and interest to take part in additional training and take on progressively more responsible roles within the organization.

•    Available to work flexible hours (day and night shifts)

•    This position requires the candidate to be bilingual
