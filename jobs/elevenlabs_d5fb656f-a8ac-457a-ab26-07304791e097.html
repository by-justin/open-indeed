About the Role

As a Brand Designer at ElevenLabs, you’ll help shape and evolve our visual identity across different products, platforms, and touchpoints. You’ll work across a broad range of projects, from campaigns, launches, and digital communications to events, merchandise, and physical brand experiences.

This role goes beyond producing individual assets. You’ll develop strong visual concepts, translate them into flexible design systems and toolkits, and ensure they work consistently across formats and teams. You’ll join the Brand Design team and collaborate closely with Marketing, Product Design, Video, Events, and other teams across the company.

You will work closely with Nev Flynn and Marina Kondratenko from the Design Team, as well as members of the Growth Team and Operations Teams to support asset creation.

What You’ll Do

  • Develop visual concepts and creative directions for product launches, campaigns, events, and other high-visibility projects.
  • Evolve ElevenLabs’ visual identity and translate it into scalable design systems, toolkits, and guidelines.
  • Design brand assets across digital and physical touchpoints, including social media, editorial content, presentations, web pages, events, merchandise, and print.
  • Build flexible templates and tools that enable other teams to create consistent, high-quality communications.
  • Art-direct imagery, illustration, typography, and other visual elements, working with internal teams and external partners when needed.
  • Maintain a consistently high quality bar while finding thoughtful ways to experiment and push the brand forward.
  • Collaborate closely with Brand, Marketing, Product Design, Video, Events, and Operations teams from initial brief through final delivery and production.

Who you are

We're looking for exceptional individuals who combine technical excellence with ethical awareness, who are excited by hard problems and motivated by human impact. You’ll strive with us if you:

  • Are passionate about audio AI driven by a desire to make content universally accessible and breaking the frontiers of new tech.
  • Are a highly motivated and driven individual with a strong work ethic. Our team is aware of this critical moment of audio AI evolution and is committed to going the extra mile to lead.
  • Are analytical, efficient, and strive on solving complex challenges with a first principles mindset.
  • Consistently strive for excellence, delivering high-quality work quickly and exceeding expectations.
  • Take initiative and work autonomously from day one, prioritizing learning and contribution while leaving ego aside.

What you bring

  • A strong portfolio showcasing brand experience across a range of mediums.
  • Excellent skills in typography, color theory, layout, and composition.
  • Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and other relevant software.
  • Ability to adapt to ElevenLabs' brand guidelines while bringing fresh, innovative ideas.
  • Strong visual storytelling skills to effectively communicate complex AI concepts.
  • Excellent time management skills and ability to juggle multiple projects.
  • A keen eye for detail and a passion for creating polished, high-quality work.
  • Effective communication skills to present design concepts and rationales.
  • Self-motivated with the ability to work independently in a remote setting.

Nice to have:

  • Experience working with tech or AI-focused companies.
  • Knowledge of current design trends in the tech and AI space.
  • Illustration skills for creating custom graphics and icons.
  • Basic animation or motion graphics capabilities.
  • Understanding of accessibility and inclusive design principles.

If you're passionate about brand design, storytelling through visuals, and want to be part of shaping the future of voice technology, we'd love to hear from you!
