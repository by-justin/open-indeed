JOB DESCRIPTION

Adient – Normal, Illinois

Job Description:  Launch Manager

Department:  Operations

Salary Range: $98,200-$164,000

Reports To: Plant Manager

Essential Duties and Responsibilities

  • Serve as the central point of communication between the plant and the SDT Team.
  • Act as the primary coordinator across all departments within the plant.
  • Develop detailed launch plans and drive completion of all key milestones to ensure a successful product launch.
  • Manage all items within the New Product Launch Manufacturing Readiness Report, ensuring timely completion and proactively notifying appropriate management of any risks that could impact a successful plant launch.
  • Support the resolution of open product issues.
  • Lead the resolution of process launch issues and track all actions through closure.
  • Conduct component design reviews with the Launch SDT to ensure all quality and manufacturing objectives are achieved.
  • Ensure appropriate process controls (poka-yoke) for all SC and CC dimensions are identified in the Control Plan and FMEA and effectively implemented within the assembly/manufacturing process.
  • Ensure timely resolution of all Launch PRRs and customer concerns.
  • Initiate actions to prevent non-conformities related to products, processes, and quality systems, and drive the implementation of effective solutions.
  • Identify, document, and track issues related to products, processes, and quality systems through successful closure.
  • Control further processing, delivery, or installation of non-conforming products until deficiencies or unsatisfactory conditions have been corrected.
  • Develop training plans and tracking methods to ensure all employees are adequately trained on new products and process assignments.

Qualification Requirements

  • Maintain customer trust through a strong commitment to customer satisfaction.
  • Expedite design and process changes in response to received QRs.
  • Coordinate QS9000 implementation for existing systems.
  • Coordinate process development for ODS in Place and Visual Management initiatives.

Education and Experience

  • Post-secondary education required; Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering (BSME), Industrial Engineering (BSIE), or an equivalent field preferred.
  • 5 to 10 years of manufacturing experience.
  • 5 to 10 years of product design experience.
  • Experience in customer management.
