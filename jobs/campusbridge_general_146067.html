Electrical Engineer-in-Training (EIT)

Shell Scotford | Fort Saskatchewan, AB

Full-Time | Start Date: August 2027

The Opportunity

Shell is seeking an Electrical Engineer-in-Training (EIT) to join the Scotford Manufacturing Complex starting in August 2027.

As an Electrical EIT, you will work alongside experienced engineers and technical professionals to support the safe, reliable, and efficient operation of one of Canada's largest integrated refining, upgrading, and chemicals manufacturing sites. You will apply your engineering knowledge to real-world industrial challenges while developing expertise in electrical power systems, equipment reliability, asset management, and project execution.

This role offers the opportunity to contribute to meaningful work from day one while building the technical and leadership skills needed for a successful engineering career.

What You'll Do

As an Electrical EIT, you may:

  • Support the design, maintenance, troubleshooting, and improvement of complex electrical power systems across the Scotford site
  • Work with electrical equipment and technologies including: Electrical motors and motor control systems Electric Heat Tracing (EHT) Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) Protection relays, breakers, and fuses Digital fault recording systems Fiber optic communication systems Cybersecurity and operational technology systems Substation and power distribution equipment
  • Assist with equipment assessments, reliability improvement initiatives, and root cause investigations
  • Support power system studies and electrical engineering analyses
  • Participate in the Management of Change (MOC) process to safely implement modifications to operating facilities
  • Review and update electrical drawings, standards, and technical documentation using tools such as AutoCAD
  • Support project development and execution activities, including design reviews, commissioning, and startup support
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, including operations, maintenance, reliability, process engineering, and project teams
  • Participate in field inspections, testing, and troubleshooting activities in operating units and substations
  • Apply Shell's HSSE and electrical safety standards in all work activities
  • Progress toward professional engineering designation through mentorship and practical engineering experience

Skills and Qualifications

What We're Looking For

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering completed by August 2027 or within the past three years
  • Eligible for registration or currently registered as an Engineer-in-Training (EIT) with APEGA
  • Less than three years of relevant professional experience
  • Minimum 3.2 GPA
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively in collaborative team environments
  • Ability to work in both office and industrial field settings
  • Legal authorization to work in Canada on a full-time basis

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous co-op, internship, research, or engineering experience in industrial, manufacturing, energy, utilities, or related environments
  • Knowledge of electrical power systems, substations, protection and control, electrical machines, or power distribution systems
  • Familiarity with electrical drawings, specifications, and engineering documentation
  • Experience using AutoCAD or similar engineering design software
  • Interest in electrical reliability, asset management, power systems engineering, or project engineering
  • Demonstrated leadership through extracurricular activities, student organizations, volunteer work, athletics, or community involvement
