Job Description

As part of the Thermo Fisher Scientific team, you’ll discover meaningful work that makes a positive impact on a global scale. Join our colleagues in bringing our Mission to life every single day to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. We provide our global teams with the resources needed to achieve individual career goals while helping to take science a step beyond by developing solutions for some of the world’s toughest challenges, like protecting the environment, making sure our food is safe or helping find cures for cancer.

Summary: Join our professional team as a Transportation Manager to contribute to optimizing transportation networks. You will be responsible for managing inbound, outbound, intercompany, and direct shipments while balancing service excellence with cost efficiency. Working with the transportation management team and Global Transportation Group, you will develop and execute transportation strategies that enhance operational effectiveness.

How will you make an impact?

In this role, you will design and optimize road/air networks, negotiate with carriers, and implement process improvements across all transportation modes. You will collaborate with internal partners to create efficient inbound strategies and develop optimal solutions for imports. Your expertise in managing dangerous goods and multi-modal transportation networks will contribute directly to our mission of making the world healthier, cleaner, and safer.
