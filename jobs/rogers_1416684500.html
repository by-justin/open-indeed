Job Description

Are you ready to take your career to new heights and be a part of a dynamic team at Rogers Sports & Media? We believe in creativity, innovation, and collaboration in everything we do, and we are looking for people who share this mindset to join us. With a monthly reach of 30 million Canadians, you can help shape the future of sports, news, e-commerce, and entertainment. At Rogers, we value diversity and inclusivity and believe that every voice matters. Join us today and be a part of a team that is redefining the future of media.

Are you passionate about storytelling? Do you have the courage to be creative? ThenCityNews is looking for you! We have an opening for a Full-Time Video Journalist in Edmonton.

What you will do:

  • Work with a dynamic team to produce creative stories which capture daily life in Edmonton

  • Come up with original story ideas and produce original stories that you will shoot and edit.

  • Deliver stories which will stand out from the competition, emphasizing creativity over traditional methods.

  • Report for our 24/7 CityNews Alberta livestream. These are sometimes live, sometimes on tape and often include a talkback with our anchor.

  • Provide original and enterprise content for our new prime time news specials.

What you will bring:

  • A passion for storytelling - you know what makes a great story and know how to tell it.

  • Ability to find original stories about people and issues which reflect Edmonton’s exciting and diverse communities.

  • Experience in shooting and editing.

  • A self-starter with a can-do, solution-oriented attitude

  • An understanding of multi-platform coverage, not confined by a ‘typical’ television reporter package.

  • A conversational live-reporting style

  • News moves fast! You must be able to multi-task and deliver for multiple daily deadlines

  • Because news never stops - you are prepared to work evenings and weekends.

  • Valid driver’s licence and clean driving record.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
