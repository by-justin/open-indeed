Role Opportunity: Content Marketing Manager, APeC

  • The Content Marketing Manager is responsible for developing and executing a regional content strategy that strengthens DHL Express's brand authority, accelerates demand generation, and supports commercial growth across the customer lifecycle.
  • This role combines strategic storytelling, thought leadership, SEO/AEO-driven content marketing, and digital performance optimisation to ensure content contributes directly to pipeline generation, customer acquisition, engagement, retention, and revenue growth.
  • The role acts as the bridge between Brand & Content and Growth Marketing, ensuring content is not only engaging and on-brand but also measurable, discoverable, and commercially impactful.
  • The Content Marketing Manager will lead the development of an AI-enabled regional content ecosystem that supports DHL Express's position as the leading international logistics provider for SMEs and multinational businesses across Asia Pacific.

Your Task

  1. Content Strategy & Planning
  • Develop and own the Asia Pacific excluding China (APeC) content strategy aligned with marketing and commercial objectives.
  • Build annual and quarterly content roadmaps.
  • Establish content priorities based on business needs, customer insights, SEO opportunity, and campaign requirements.
  • Manage editorial calendars across multiple markets.
  1. Thought Leadership & Brand Authority
  • Position DHL as a leading voice in: International trade, Cross-border eCommerce, SME growth, Supply chain resilience, Industry vertical solutions and Heavyweight express shipping.
  • Develop White papers, Trend reports, Executive POVs, Articles, Case studies, Podcasts and Webinar content.
  • Partner with regional and country leadership to amplify expert voices and executive thought leadership.
  1. Organic Growth (SEO / GEO / AEO)
  • Develop content strategies designed to improve: Organic traffic, Search visibility, Generative search visibility, Lead acquisition.
  • Work with Growth Marketing teams to: Identify search opportunities, Build content clusters, Optimize content architecture, Improve answer engine visibility (AEO), Support Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
  • Monitor overall search trends, algorithm changes, influence of AI on search and discovery.
  1. Digital Content & Customer Journey Management
  • Own content across: Website, Discover Content Hub, Email marketing, Social media, Video, Webinars, Landing pages, Marketing automation journeys.
  • Design content journeys across: Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Retention, Advocacy.
  1. Demand Generation Support
  • Partner closely with: Brand Marketing, Martech & Automation, Performance Marketing to develop content that supports: Lead generation, MQL creation, ABM initiatives, Conversion optimisation, Customer nurturing programmes,
  1. AI-Enabled Content Operations
  • Use AI technologies to accelerate content development and localisation.
  • Establish standards for AI-assisted content creation.
  • Explore emerging opportunities in: Generative AI, Personalisation, Agentic workflows, Content automation.
  1. Measurement & Optimisation
  • Monitor and report: Organic traffic, Engagement, AI Citations and Mentions, Content consumption, Search visibility, Rankings, Lead generation, Conversion influence, Pipeline attribution.
  • Use data and insights to continuously improve content effectiveness.
  1. Agency & Stakeholder Management
  • Manage content agencies, freelancers and creative partners.
  • Coordinate content production across regional and country teams.
  • Ensure brand consistency and governance across all markets.

What You Bring

Required:

  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Business, Digital Marketing, or a related field.
  • 12+ years of experience in Content Marketing, Digital Marketing, Growth Marketing, Editorial Content, and/or B2B Marketing.
  • Proven track record of developing content strategies that drive engagement, lead generation, and business growth.
  • Strong expertise in SEO, GEO, AEO, website content optimization, marketing automation, and social media content strategy.
  • Exceptional writing, editing, and storytelling skills with business-fluent English proficiency.
  • Experience managing multilingual content localization across APeC markets is highly advantageous.

Nice to Have:

  • Master's degree or MBA.
  • Certifications in Content Marketing, SEO, Google Analytics, AI in Marketing, or Marketing Automation.
  • Experience within Logistics, Technology, SaaS, B2B Services, or Professional Services industries.
  • Experience leading regional or global content marketing programs.
  • Hands-on experience with AI-powered content creation and optimization tools.
