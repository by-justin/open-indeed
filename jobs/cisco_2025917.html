Job Description

About the Role

Cisco is looking for a senior technology leader to help Service Providers across APJ modernise the networks that underpin their businesses.

As a Solutions Development Architect (SDA) within Cisco Customer Experience, you will work with senior technology leaders across telecommunications, service providers and telco-cloud organisations to shape solutions that modernise IP routing, optical transport, automation and network assurance .

You will engage with customers at Senior Director, VP and Technology Director level , understanding their business and technology challenges and translating them into practical transformation and services strategies.

This is a role for someone who can operate at both strategic and technical levels — someone who can have a conversation about network transformation with a CTO or Technology Director, then work with architects and engineers to turn that vision into an achievable solution.

You will work across APJ, with a particular focus on Australia and New Zealand, partnering with Cisco Sales, Service Sales, delivery teams, Systems Integrators and technology partners.

We're interested in people who have operated at a senior level within Service Providers, Telecommunications, Technology Services or large enterprise environments .

What You'll Do:

·       Shape customer transformation (entire lifecycle)

·       Engage senior technology and network leaders to understand their priorities, challenges and transformation objectives.

·       Help customers develop practical roadmaps for modernising their transport networks.

·       Connect technology decisions to business outcomes such as cost reduction, network scale, operational efficiency, reliability and faster service delivery .

·       Build trusted relationships and become a strategic technology advisor to Service Provider customers.

·       Lead solution strategy

·       Shape CX Professional Services solutions across Service Provider Routing, Optical Transport, Routed Optical Networking, Automation and Observability .

·       Apply industry best practices and Cisco architectures to solve complex customer challenges.

·       Identify opportunities to simplify networks, improve capacity and reduce operational complexity.

·       Work with Sales and customers to develop compelling solutions and support RFI/RFP activity.

·       Identify opportunities to expand customer adoption and support longer-term transformation journeys.

·       Bring deep technical expertise

You will bring significant experience in one or more of the following areas:

·       Service Provider IP Routing and Core

·       IOS XR, Cisco 8000, ASR 9000 or equivalent platforms

·       Segment Routing / SRv6 and MPLS

·       Routed Optical Networking and IP-over-DWDM

·       DWDM / ROADM and coherent optics

·       ZR / ZR+ pluggables

·       Network automation and programmability

·       Network telemetry, observability and assurance

·       Closed-loop automation and AI-enabled network operations

·       You don't need to be an expert in every technology listed. Deep expertise in one core domain, combined with a strong understanding of the broader transport ecosystem, is what matters.

·       Connect architecture with delivery

·       Ensure proposed solutions are practical, commercially viable and deliverable.

·       Work closely with CX delivery teams to establish realistic scope, effort, timelines and migration approaches.

·       Use your technical and delivery experience to anticipate implementation risks and customer challenges.

·       Support the transition from solution design into successful delivery and customer adoption.

·       Lead through influence

·       Act as a technical advisor and SME across Cisco, customers and partners.

·       Mentor and guide architects and engineers.

·       Work across Sales, CX, engineering, delivery and partner organisations.

·       Bring new ideas around automation, AI and modern network operations to customer and internal conversations.

You will demonstrate:

·       Significant experience designing or transforming large-scale service provider or telecommunications networks.

·       Deep expertise in IP routing, optical transport or both .

·       Experience engaging senior customer stakeholders and translating technical challenges into business outcomes.

·       Strong consulting, architecture and solution-development skills.

·       Experience working across complex, multi-vendor or multi-country environments.

·       The ability to move comfortably between executive conversations, architecture discussions and technical delivery teams .

·       A strong interest in automation, observability and the future of AI-enabled network operations.

Certifications

An expert-level networking certification such as CCIE Service Provider or equivalent is highly desirable.

Certifications in automation, programmability, Segment Routing, IOS XR, optical networking or related technologies are also valued.
