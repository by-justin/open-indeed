The role

We are looking fora mid-career Technical and Policy Advisor on Construction Codes to support our Construction Research Centre. The Technical and Policy Advisor on Construction Codes would be someone who shares our core values of Integrity, Excellence, Respect and Creativity.

The incumbent provides technical advice and administrative support to the Canadian Board for Harmonized Construction Codes (CBHCC) and its committees in the development of Canada’s National Model Codes. They directly support code development committees in developing recommendations on proposed changes which includes technical and administrative support for the committees and requires liasing with policy advisor and research colleagues as well as colleagues from other federal departments and provicincial and territorial governments.

Key Activities

Developing and maintaining a knowledge and understanding of the initiatives, policies and programs at NRC and in the construction industry in general, and an awareness of the technology events and trends that impact Canada’s National Model Codes to advise the code development committees and aid them in consensus-based decision making.

Maintaining a knowledge and understanding of the building sciences, engineering and economics involved in the application of technology to design and construction, and maintaining a comprehensive knowledge of Canada’s National Model Codes, referenced standards and the technical developments in these documents applicable but not limited to:

  • Housing and small buildings

  • Modular construction

  • Fire protection, safety and prevention

  • Accessibility

  • Building envelope

  • Alterations to existing buildings

  • Plumbing

  • HVAC

  • Occupant safety in use

  • Energy efficiency

  • Greenhouse gas emissions

  • Building structures

The role (cont'd)

Identifying areas requiring study, undertaking literature reviews and scoping and supporting research in those areas, assisting in technical discussions, and summarizing and presenting the findings to the code development committees.

Advising technical committees on alignment with CBHCC policies and work plans.

Responding to technical inquiries from the Provinces and Territories concerning the requirements in the National Model Codes and, where appropriate, researching historic records to determine the intent of the requirement under consideration.

Writing summaries, analyzing current code requirements and identifying considerations when developing code changes, including background, history and justification (technical and economical where applicable), through reference to research papers and economic studies.

Providing administrative support to the code development committees by planning meetings, preparing meeting material, preparing meeting summaries, and following up on actions arising from meetings.

Preparing or managing contracts in support of codes related research and development.

Administering the code development system in accordance with the Canadian Board for Harmonized Construction Codes’ Operating Procedures.

Representing Codes Canada on external technical and standards committees related to subject matter of the supported committee(s) as required.

Maintaining close, effective and harmonious relations with:

  • Provincial and territorial departments responsible for adoption and enforcement of Canada’s National Model Codes;

  • Building officials;

  • Researchers in the area pertinent to committee work; and

  • Industry associations.

Making and giving presentations to various external groups on code-related matters such as education and training initiatives, CBHCC identified code priorities and introduction of changes to the codes.
