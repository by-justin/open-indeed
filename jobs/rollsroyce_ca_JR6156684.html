Role Purpose:

The GBS Finance Operations Specialist (Closing & Master Data Focus) ensures reliable, accurate, and timely financial data processing within Group Reporting environments. The role is responsible for master data governance, consolidated data loads, and operational stability during month‑end closing, thereby enabling compliant reporting and high data quality for internal and external stakeholders.

Role Description:

This role focuses on the operational execution and governance of Group Reporting processes, with a strong emphasis on SAP BW master data, TAMIS data models, and consolidated data loads. The position plays a key role during working days 1–5 by ensuring process stability, data completeness, and transparent communication with legal entities and Group stakeholders. It supports continuous harmonization of templates, reporting standards, and user enablement across the GBS Finance landscape.

Key Duties & Responsibilities:

Develop Annual Operating Plan / Budget

• Support preparation and pre‑population of planning input templates (Excel, VBA‑based).

• Validate and maintain data consistency across planning and reporting systems.

Data & System Governance

• Maintain SAP BW master data and TAMIS data models.

• Execute updates and actively communicate data model and structure changes to stakeholders.

• Monitor and report status of consolidated group data loads to TAMIS (Oracle HFM).

Month ‑End Closing Activities

• Open and close periods in Group Reporting Systems (SalesDB, FSM, TAMIS); manage release/revoke process levels.

• Ensure timely execution of reporting tasks during working days 1–5.

• Collect, validate, and stage source data for FSM and SalesDB (SAP BW applications).

• Prepare SalesDB submission sheets for legal entity (SDE‑FN).

• Consolidate and validate COPA revenue with VIS order intake (SAP BW process chain via Fiori Launchpad).

• Execute consolidated group data loads to TAMIS and monitor load status.

• Prepare feedback files and reports for Rolls‑Royce Plc.

• Provide first‑level support during month‑end closing, with focus on TAMIS‑related topics.

General Support & Housekeeping

• Provide first‑level support for access rights, onboarding, and operational issues.

• Support harmonization of input templates and reporting standards.

• Assist users with system‑ and data‑related questions.

Project Support & Ad ‑Hoc Topics

• Support initiatives to harmonize templates and reporting processes.

• Prepare and deliver user trainings and documentation.

• Handle one‑time ad‑hoc requests driven by urgent business needs or management inquiries.
