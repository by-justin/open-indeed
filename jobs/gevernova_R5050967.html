Job Description

What Impact You’ll Make

As a  Contract Management Intern  on the  Americas New Units Heavy Duty Gas Turbines Contract Management Team , you will be an active contributor to the team's efforts to protect GE Vernova's global commercial interests, drive cross-business contract compliance, and support the successful execution of  multi-billion-dollar Heavy Duty Gas Turbine power plant projects .

Key Responsibilities:

  • Support the Contract Management team in the  analysis and interpretation  of project-wise commercial issues  from initial identification through final resolution .
  • Read, review, and interpret  new power plant contracts  with customers spread across the United States, Canada, and Latin America.
  • Assist in the development of  automated tools  to streamline change order management processes.
  • Support assigned contracts in execution,  working closely  with Contract Managers, Project Managers, and customers.
  • Contribute to the  preparation of contractual correspondence , including change orders, letters, and customer notifications.
  • Assist in  tracking and reporting  on risk mitigation, vendor recovery, and commercial deliverables.
  • Develop and deliver  presentations on contract topics  to internal stakeholders.
  • Complete special projects as assigned,  contributing to continuous improvement initiatives  within the team.
  • Organize and maintain  legacy contract documents and electronic files  to ensure accuracy and accessibility.

Employment Dates:

May or June 2027 to August 2027 (Summer).

Required Qualifications:

  • Currently enrolled in an accredited undergraduate or graduate degree program in  Business, Engineering, Law, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in  Microsoft Office Suite , including Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, with the ability to prepare detailed analyses and presentations.
  • Strong  written and oral communication skills  in English, including the ability to present complex information clearly and concisely.
  • Demonstrated ability to  manage multiple tasks simultaneously  and deliver results in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
  • Problem-solving attitude,  ready to work on high-level, complex problems  while developing customer-centric GE Gas Power solutions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be  legally authorized to work in the United States  without requiring current or future sponsorship.
  • Must be available to  work onsite in Schenectady, NY for a 12-week Summer 2027 internship experience .

Preferred Qualifications:

  • You have a passion for  commercial contract management, energy infrastructure, or power generation  and a curiosity for how complex projects are structured and executed.
  • You have a passion for  data-driven problem solving , including using tools like Excel or other platforms to build automated solutions that improve team efficiency.
  • You are someone who brings  strong attention to detail and organizational discipline , capable of managing and maintaining critical documentation with a high degree of accuracy.
  • You are someone who  communicates with confidence and clarity , both in writing and in presentations, and can translate complex commercial concepts for diverse audiences.
  • You have experience with  database management, contract analysis, or project coordination  through coursework, prior internships, or extracurricular activities.
  • You have experience  working in cross-functional or team-based environments , demonstrating the ability to collaborate effectively across different functions and stakeholders.
  • You are eager to apply AI tools and emerging technologies  to solve problems in a professional contract management setting.
