Job Description

Senior AI Engineer

The Opportunity

We are seeking an accomplished Senior AI Engineer to contribute to the design, development, and implementation of AI-driven solutions within our software development lifecycle. This role offers the opportunity to work on enterprise-scale AI integration initiatives, establish best practices, and advance the technical maturity of our engineering organization. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive AI adoption and innovation across the organization.

What You'll Do

AI Engineering & Implementation

  • Design and build MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers and tools for seamless AI integration
  • Design and implement AI-driven automation solutions, including LLM-powered automation, prompt engineering, and conversational AI
  • Develop ML model integration solutions and AI orchestration workflows with monitoring and evaluation
  • Integrate UiPath with enterprise AI platforms and APIs
  • Support implementation of agentic automation and advanced AI use cases

AI/ML Expertise & Governance

  • Integrate LLM APIs and apply prompt engineering techniques
  • Design and maintain AI workflow orchestration systems
  • Implement Intelligent Document Processing and NLP solutions
  • Develop model evaluation, tuning, and governance frameworks
  • Design and manage vector databases and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems
  • Support responsible AI practices and governance model development

AI-Driven Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)

  • Contribute to AI/ML solutions that enhance code generation, testing, deployment, and monitoring
  • Integrate AI technologies into existing SDLC workflows and development tooling
  • Support development and implementation of governance models for AI and responsible AI practices

Technical Contributions & Collaboration

  • Participate in technology evaluation and assessment for organizational adoption
  • Collaborate with enterprise architecture, security, and compliance teams on AI initiatives
  • Contribute to technical training and knowledge-sharing initiatives
  • Support code reviews, design discussions, and mentoring of junior team members

What You Need to Succeed

Must Have

  • 4–6 years of experience building and operating production software systems at scale
  • 1–2 years of hands-on experience with LLMs and generative AI in production environments or serious prototyping
  • Proficiency in .NET/C#, Python, SQL, Git, and CI/CD pipelines
  • Strong expertise in API architecture (REST/SOAP)
  • Solid working knowledge of agentic AI patterns: tool use, agent orchestration, ReAct, multi-step reasoning, memory and context management
  • Proven proficiency in LLM API integration and prompt engineering
  • Cloud platform experience (AWS, Azure, or GCP), including containers (Docker/Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines
  • Experience building or contributing to RAG systems (vector databases, embeddings, retrieval strategies)
  • Demonstrated experience designing and building MCP servers and tools with familiarity of agent-tool integration frameworks
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills; comfortable with code reviews, design discussions, and pair programming
  • Ability to explain technical decisions clearly to both technical peers and business stakeholders
  • Understanding of security, data governance, and responsible AI practices

Nice-to-Have

  • Production experience with agentic automation and advanced AI solutions
  • Open-source contributions to automation or AI/ML projects
  • Certification in cloud platforms (e.g., AWS Developer Associate, Azure Developer Associate)
  • Experience with additional languages or frameworks (Java, Go, Rust)
