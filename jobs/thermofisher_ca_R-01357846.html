Job Purpose

The Engineering Team Leader is responsible for the day-to-day supervision and coordination of the engineering team, ensuring efficient execution of maintenance and engineering activities on the shop floor.

This role provides a critical link between the Engineering Manager and the engineering team, improving operational control, consistency of standards, and responsiveness to plant needs. It enables the Engineering Manager to focus on strategic initiatives, continuous improvement, and long-term performance optimisation.

As a Manufacturing Engineering Supervisor at Thermo Fisher Scientific, you'll implement continuous improvement initiatives, oversee technical projects, and ensure efficient production of high-quality products that enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. Collaborate with R&D, Production, Quality, and other teams to optimize processes, implement new technologies, and solve technical challenges. Support and develop your team while fostering an environment of innovation, safety and operational excellence.

Key Responsibilities

1. Team Leadership & Supervision

  • Provide direct supervision and leadership to a team of approximately 10–12 engineers/technicians.
  • Allocate daily tasks and ensure effective workload distribution.
  • Monitor team performance and provide coaching, guidance, and support.
  • Promote a strong team culture focused on safety, quality, and accountability.

2. Maintenance & Engineering Coordination

  • Oversee execution of planned and reactive maintenance activities.
  • Ensure timely response to breakdowns and production issues.
  • Coordinate engineering support to minimise downtime and maintain operational efficiency.
  • Act as first point of escalation for technical and operational issues.

3. Standards & Compliance

  • Ensure all engineering activities are carried out in line with company standards, procedures, and health & safety requirements.
  • Drive consistency in maintenance practices and documentation.
  • Support auditing activities and ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards.

4. Shop Floor Presence

  • Maintain a strong, visible presence on the shop floor to ensure priorities are understood and delivered.
  • Provide hands-on support where required to resolve technical issues.
  • Ensure effective communication between shifts and departments.

5. Performance & Continuous Improvement

  • Support the implementation of continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Identify opportunities to improve reliability, efficiency, and maintenance processes.
  • Monitor KPIs such as downtime, response time, and work order completion.
  • Drive improvements in team productivity and performance.

6. Planning & Communication

  • Work closely with the Engineering Manager to prioritise workload and align with business objectives.
  • Support short-term planning and scheduling of maintenance tasks.
  • Ensure clear communication within the team and with production, operations, and other stakeholders.

7. Development & Accountability

  • Support training and development of team members skill matrix/Task lists
  • Conduct performance reviews and provide constructive feedback.
  • Promote ownership and accountability within the team for delivering high-quality work.
