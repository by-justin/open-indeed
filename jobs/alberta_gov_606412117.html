Job Information Job Title: Clinical Advisor – Continuing Care Job Requisition ID: 87517 Ministry: Assisted Living and Social Services Location: Edmonton Full or Part-Time: Full-Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent Scope: Open Competition Closing Date: October 7, 2026 Classification: Medical and Health 4 Salary: ​$3,711.12 to $4,726.90 bi-weekly ($96,860 - $123,372/year) The Alberta Public Service works to build a stronger province for current and future generations. We make a difference in the lives of Albertans through rewarding and diverse career opportunities. The Ministry of Assisted Living and Social Services leads housing, assisted living and continuing care, seniors, income, employment and homelessness supports, disability services, and other social-based programs. For more information about ALSS, visit our website: https://www.alberta.ca/assisted-living-and-social-services . The Continuing Care Homes Branch is seeking a highly skilled and experienced Clinical Advisor to support the transformation of Alberta's continuing care system. This position plays a critical role in advancing the mandate of the Continuing Care Division through the development of strategic policy, legislation, standards, and initiatives that improve the quality and sustainability of continuing care services across the province. Role Responsibilities Reporting to the Manager, System Improvement, the Clinical Advisor provides clinical leadership, strategic advice, policy analysis, and project management expertise to support priority government commitments and strategic transformation initiatives within continuing care. The job responsibilities include: •    Provide clinical expertise and strategic advice to support the development, implementation, and evaluation of continuing care policies, programs, legislation, and standards. •    Research and analyze emerging issues, trends, risks, and best practices to develop evidence-based policy recommendations and strategic solutions. •    Lead complex, multi-stakeholder projects that advance continuing care transformation and support government priorities. •    Monitor and evaluate policy effectiveness, clinical outcomes, and quality indicators, recommending improvements to enhance care quality and system performance. •    Build collaborative relationships with internal and external stakeholders, including healthcare providers, regulators, professional associations, and government partners. •    Represent the branch on committees, working groups, and consultation activities, translating stakeholder feedback into policy and program recommendations. •    Prepare briefing materials, reports, correspondence, presentations, and strategic documents to support decision-making and issues management. •    Provide timely advice on current and emerging continuing care issues, risks, and opportunities while supporting informed decision-making across the Ministry. Qualifications Minimum Recruitment Standards Degree related to nursing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, health, policy, public administration, or a related field and four (4) years of related experience. Must be a regulated member of the appropriate college if required by the Health Professions Act. Equivalency: Diploma related to the position assignment and six (6) years of related experience. Master’s degree related to the position assignment and three (3) years of related experience. The following experience is required: •    Knowledge of Alberta's health and social care systems, continuing care services, and relevant legislation, standards, and policies. •    Knowledge of Alberta's continuing care partners, including Alberta Health, Assisted Living Alberta, Alberta Health Services, and contracted service providers. •    Strong project management, analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills. •    Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience preparing briefings and reports for senior leaders. •    Experience building collaborative relationships with stakeholders, service providers, regulatory bodies, and community partners. The following experience is considered as assets: •    Experience in facility-based continuing care, designated supportive living, or long-term care settings. •    Experience interpreting and applying continuing care legislation, standards, and policy requirements. •    Experience leading cross-functional projects, committees, or stakeholder engagement initiatives. •    Clinical experience with a strong focus on quality care, client-centred care, and quality of life outcomes. •    Experience in policy analysis, research, and developing recommendations to support program and policy initiatives. About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
