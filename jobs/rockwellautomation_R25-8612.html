Job Description

You will be responsible for managing a wide range of HR functions, including employee lifecycle management, labor relations, recruitment, training, and policy administration. This role supports organizational growth and enhances employee engagement through effective HR practices.

You will be reporting to HR LEAD - JAPAN.

Your Responsibilities:

  • You will plan and execute recruitment activities (job postings, candidate communication, interview coordination)
  • You will manage onboarding and offboarding processes
  • You will administer and improve HR policies and procedures
  • You will support payroll preparation and social insurance tasks
  • You will handle employee relations and ensure compliance with labor laws
  • You will develop and execute employee engagement initiatives
  • You will maintain and analyze HR data, prepare reports

The Essentials - You Will Have:

  • Minimum 3 years of HR experience
  • Basic knowledge of labor laws and social insurance systems
  • Strong communication, coordination, and problem-solving skills
  • High ethical standards and responsibility in handling confidential information
  • Native-level Japanese and Business-level English

The Preferred - You Might Also Have:

  • Someone who wants to work in a multicultural environment This role is ideal for individuals who want to collaborate with global teams and experience diversity through daily interactions with people from different cultures. Working alongside colleagues with diverse backgrounds will help you broaden your perspective.
  • Someone with strong communication skills This position suits those who are confident in exchanging information with employees, understanding challenges, and making appropriate and effective proposals.
  • Someone who is proactive and takes initiative When faced with challenges, you take action on your own initiative or work closely with the team to find solutions. This is an ideal workplace for those who want to approach their work with energy and ownership.
  • Someone with a strong drive to exceed expectations and grow as an HR professional If you are highly motivated to continuously go beyond expectations and aspire to become a human resources professional, this role is for you.In this position, you will have the opportunity to leverage your skills and passion while taking on new challenges in a growth-oriented environment. We look forward to receiving your application!

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
