About bp

bp operates at the heart of the global energy system, helping countries meet their energy needs and serving millions of customers every day. With world-class assets, technology, trading capability and partnerships, we deliver energy at scale today while investing with discipline in the energy transition.

Our internships

Make an impact on cutting-edge projects. Help find solutions to complex challenges. Be part of a company that invests in you and your growth. You can do it all as an intern at bp. Our 12-week summer program gives you the opportunity to build your technical knowledge, develop key skills and learn more about life at bp. You'll be part of one team throughout your internship, supported by a dedicated line manager and buddy. There'll be opportunities to connect with other interns, take part in training and grow your network across the business.

About the Supply, Trading, & Shipping Internship Program:

The summer internship program within BP's Supply, Trading & Shipping organization provides an exceptional introduction to a dynamic global business! During the 12-week internship, our interns work on individual and group projects with direct impact to our bottom line. Each intern is placed on a team and encouraged to be part of their success. BP emphasizes intern growth and development through learning and networking opportunities throughout the summer. Our interns learn from some of the most hardworking professionals within Analytics, Marketing & Origination, Operations, and Trading. As an intern, you will be part of a dynamic and fast-paced environment that is supportive of learning and building new skills.

Interns will receive support from both their managers and mentors during the program. As employees in a company with global operations, our interns interact with a variety of people, understand a global business, and add distinctive value to our group through project work. Successful interns may be considered for a full-time role within our Supply, Trading, and Shipping program upon earning their degree.

Minimum Requirements for the Supply, Trading, & Shipping internship:

  • Working toward Bachelor's degree. We are open to all majors.
  • Graduating between December 2027 and May 2028
  • Current Cumulative GPA Minimum 3.0
  • Eligible to work in Canada with no restrictions (bp will not support Canadian Immigration sponsorship for full-time or long-term employment)
  • Must be available to start 12-week internship on May 17, 2027 or June 14, 2027 (will be required to pass all background and medical checks at least one week prior to start date)

Key skills required include:

  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment with specific timelines
  • Strong analytical skills
  • Ability to handle competing priorities
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills
