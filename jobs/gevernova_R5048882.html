Job Description

Job Description Summary

The SaaS Sr. Sales Manager role for GE Vernova’s Power & Energy Resources Software Team (PERS) is responsible for sales of our SaaS solutions in the attractive markets segments such as Power Generation, Oil and Gas, Metals and MIning to ensure attainment of orders and revenue and growth targets in the United States. In this Sales position, the Sr. Sales Manager will be responsible for identifying sales opportunities within existing and new accounts, drive prospecting and discovery assessments, strategy and account planning, deal strategy and path to close, build executive relationships. The Sr Sales Manager will work closely with the members of cross functional peers in PERS as well as other GE Vernova Business Units (GEV BU), to drive and lead growth in software sales and value-driven revenue generation. They will drive the creation and development of sales plays, healthy pipeline, growth programs, an overall sales readiness vision for territory. This role will require a motivated achiever and self-starter whose interests align with teaming with experts on staff to help maximize customer engagement, rapidly assess qualification of fit, and helping our solution to support the future of energy with regards to performance, emissions, reliability and safety of our customers’ plants and operations.
