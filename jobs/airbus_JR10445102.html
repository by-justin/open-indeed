Missions & Responsabilités:

Airbus Protect Continued Airworthiness & Certification team APAB2A is looking for an Operational Certification Consultant to reinforce its ETOPS & MMEL activities.

Technical Perimeter

As Operational Certification Consultant, you will be part of a team responsible for a large and varied ETOPS and MMEL activities with a transversal view on Airbus aircraft and several interdependencies on ATA chapters, eg.:

  • ATA 21 Air Conditioning
  • ATA 24 Electrical Power
  • ATA 26-23 Cargo Fire Protection
  • ATA 49 Airborne Auxiliary Power
  • ATA 72 Engine
  • etc.

Main Activities

Working in direct relationship with Airbus Operational Certification department (1IAAVO), the Airbus Protect ETOPS & OSD Operational Certification Consultants are responsible for:

  • Coordinating with Airbus Design Office and Technical Documentation department (STL) to produce MMEL change projects;
  • Redacting or contributing to the certification plan for the ATA041 ETOPS aspect.
  • Interacting with EASA on the major change projects and/or managing the approval for minor changes.
  • Following-up of the Airbus ETOPS fleet reliability and Continuing Airworthiness through the tracking of engine shut down and review of in-service events.
  • Producing ETOPS Technical documentation in both envelope version or version customized to operators for aircraft deliveries, and maintaining other ETOPS related documentation such as Training presentation material, In-Service information Articles.
  • Interacting with Airbus Customer Support on a daily basis to provide quick response to customer queries on ETOPS topics and MMEL related problems, for the whole Airbus commercial fleet and all customers.

Requirements

  • Team player - Highly concerned by Customer satisfaction.
  • Engineering degree either specialized in aeronautics, or with previous experience in Operational Suitability Data, airline operations (incl. ETOPS) or Technical Documentation.
  • Good coordination skills to deal with Customers, Airbus Customers Support departments and Design Offices,
  • Autonomous - able to quickly adapt to constantly changing priorities.
  • Good presentation skills, both oral and written.
  • As an international company, we work in a multicultural environment: a(n) Negociation level in English is mandatory.
