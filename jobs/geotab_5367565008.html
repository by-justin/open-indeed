Job Description

Who we are:

Geotab ® is a global leader in IoT and connected transportation and certified “Great Place to Work™.” We are a company of diverse and talented individuals who work together to help businesses grow and succeed, and increase the safety and sustainability of our communities.

Geotab is advancing security, connecting commercial vehicles to the internet and providing web-based analytics to help customers better manage their fleets. Geotab’s open platform and Geotab Marketplace ®, offering hundreds of third-party solution options, allows both small and large businesses to automate operations by integrating vehicle data with their other data assets. Processing billions of data points a day, Geotab leverages data analytics and machine learning to improve productivity, optimize fleets through the reduction of fuel consumption, enhance driver safety and achieve strong compliance to regulatory changes.

Our team is growing and we’re looking for people who follow their passion, think differently and want to make an impact. Ours is a fast paced, ever changing environment. Geotabbers accept that challenge and are willing to take on new tasks and activities - ones that may not always be described in the initial job description. Join us for a fulfilling career with opportunities to innovate, great benefits, and our fun and inclusive work culture. Reach your full potential with Geotab. To see what it’s like to be a Geotabber, check out our blog and follow us @InsideGeotab on Instagram.

Who you are:

As a Solutions Delivery Intern, you are a highly motivated and eager individual with a strong interest in project management and technology. You are keen to learn the ropes of managing complex projects and contributing to the successful delivery of innovative products. Your strong communication skills, eagerness to learn, and commitment to continuous improvement make you a valuable asset to our organization. You thrive in collaborative environments and are excited to work alongside experienced professionals, supporting them in streamlining processes and driving product initiatives.

What you'll do:

In this role, you will gain hands-on experience in the world of solutions delivery by supporting our Solutions Delivery Managers and cross-functional teams. You will assist in streamlining processes, contribute to complex projects, and gain exposure to the full technical, operational, business and market lifecycle of a feature or product – from conception to release. Your responsibilities will include assisting with defining project scope, supporting the management of timelines, and ensuring smooth execution of tasks, all while continuously learning and developing your skills.

How you'll make an impact:

  • Support product initiatives throughout the product lifecycle, gaining valuable insights into the journey from conception to release.

  • Assist in defining project scope, goals, and deliverables. Contribute to the creation and management of project timelines/schedules, ensuring timely delivery of product milestones.

  • Provide support to complex projects and product initiatives involving coordination with multi-disciplinary teams at Geotab. Assist in facilitating effective communication, aligning visions, and ensuring accountability for required work/deliverables.

  • Assist in coordinating internal team activities and supporting project meetings. Help maintain detailed meeting minutes and follow up on action items.

  • Contribute to establishing and maintaining project deliverables, timelines, and milestone updates for sponsors and key stakeholders.

  • Assist in analyzing project issues and developing solutions. Learn to anticipate potential problems and proactively address them.

  • Assist in gathering and documenting stakeholder requirements for specific products and translating requirements into detailed technical specifications.

  • Participate in post-mortem reviews post-product delivery or as required within the project delivery process.

  • Support project communication and reporting. Assist with project tracking and analysis. Contribute to communicating vital information to the project team and broader product teams.

  • Assist in preparing and presenting regular progress reports to stakeholders and senior management.

  • Engage in establishing best practices, templates, policies, tools, and partnerships to enhance organizational capabilities.

  • Maintenance Domain: Optimize vehicle availability while reducing the average duration required for repairs for our client base.
