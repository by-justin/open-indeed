Job Description

Key Responsibilities / 主要職責

  • Monitor and operate HVAC systems for semiconductor cleanrooms and facility areas to ensure stable environmental conditions for production, including Chilled Water, Heating Water, Makeup Air, Recirculation Air, Exhaust, CDA (Clean Dry Air), PV (Process Vacuum), and General HVAC systems.監控並操作半導體無塵室及廠務區域的空調系統，確保生產環境穩定，涵蓋冷凍水、熱水、新風、循環風、排氣、CDA（潔淨乾燥空氣）、PV（製程真空）及一般空調系統。
  • Perform routine inspections, preventive maintenance, and troubleshooting for HVAC equipment such as AHUs, chillers, pumps, cooling towers, and control systems.執行空調設備的例行檢查、預防保養及故障排除，包含空調箱、冰水機、泵浦、冷卻水塔及控制系統。
  • Respond promptly to HVAC alarms and emergencies to minimize impact on production and environmental conditions.迅速應對空調警報及緊急狀況，確保對生產及環境條件的影響降至最低。
  • Support commissioning and start-up activities for new HVAC installations and system upgrades.協助新空調設備安裝及系統升級的試車與啟動作業。
  • Maintain accurate logs and reports of HVAC system status, incidents, and maintenance activities.維護空調系統狀態、事件及維護活動的準確紀錄與報告。
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve temperature, humidity, and airflow issues and implement corrective actions.與跨部門團隊合作，解決溫度、濕度及風量問題並執行改善措施。
  • Ensure compliance with ISO 9001, IATF 16949, and site-specific safety and operational standards.確保符合 ISO 9001、IATF 16949 及廠區安全與操作標準。
  • Participate in TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) initiatives and continuous improvement programs to enhance system reliability.參與 TPM 全員生產保養及持續改善計畫，以提升系統可靠度。

Qualifications / 資格要求

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering or related field (preferred); equivalent work experience considered.機械工程或相關領域學士學位（優先）；具同等工作經驗者可考慮。
  • Fresh graduates with a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering or related fields, or other science and engineering disciplines, are welcome to apply.歡迎大學以上電機工程或相關領域、理工相關科系畢業新人應徵。
  • 3–5 years of experience in HVAC systems operation and maintenance; semiconductor or advanced manufacturing experience preferred.具 3–5 年空調系統操作與維護經驗；具半導體或先進製造業經驗者優先。
  • Familiar with chilled water systems, AHUs, pumps, cooling towers, and HVAC control systems.熟悉冷凍水系統、空調箱、泵浦、冷卻水塔及空調控制系統。
  • Ability to perform troubleshooting under pressure and respond to emergencies effectively.能在壓力下進行故障排除並有效處理緊急狀況。
  • Basic knowledge of SMART monitoring systems and predictive maintenance tools is a plus.具智慧監控系統（SMART）及預測性維護工具基本概念者佳。
  • Strong sense of responsibility, teamwork, and communication skills; basic English communication skills preferred.具高度責任感、團隊合作及溝通能力；具基本英文溝通能力者佳。
  • Flexible to support shift operations and on-call responsibilities when needed.願意配合輪班及必要的待命工作。
