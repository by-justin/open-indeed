DHL has an immediate opening for a Cargo Agent at our MIA Aviation station in Miami, Florida. This position complies with cargo export and import procedures established to provide an effective and efficient service at the highest level. In addition, the jobholder must exercise judgment and initiative to efficiently handle day to day ACS situations.

Key Accountabilities

• Assists other stations in any special Cargo related issue

• Maintains well organized and updated GFS operational files

• Prepares paperwork and reports required for import and export flights and manifests

• Provides feedback to direct superior about any irregularity observed in the Import or Export warehouse, in order to take corrective actions

• Inputs the TGV system for all required procedures and maintains it updated

• Manifests outbound flights using OCNT system complying with all required procedures

• Ensures accuracy of outbound manifest against shipments on board

• Updates OH and SM’s check points, for ACS shipments, within timeline

• Verifies arrival of cargo and documents against reservation log and assist supervisors with loading priorities

• Manifests and clears outbound flights, and obtain CBP clearance for outbound flights

• Clear inbound flight’s documents and crew under the CBP regulations

• Coordinate outbound paperwork with DHL Express Control Center, obtaining core material manifest and airway bills for the flights with the corresponding custom’s back-ups

• Reviews and ensures that all outbound shipments are covered by all Government Entities documentation required for export

• Prepares flight sets for outbound flights complying with Federal and local authorities’ requirements

• Reconciles pallet tags against manifests before closing the flights, to ensure accuracy of loaded shipments

• Complies with ACS GSOP standards performing 100% CIA imaging of all export documents

• Complies with each foreign stations¨ requirements in regard of documents required by local customs

• Submits in a timely manner, outbound flight sets to CBP audits.

• Reviews accuracy inbound flight sets provided by the Third-Party Service Provider

• Clears in transit perishables at USDA.

• Supports Company guidelines to accomplish required ACS Score Card result

• And other assigned duties as required

Problem Solving

The job holder must ensure Cargo operation solutions based on Company standards.

Decision-Making

The jobholder should be able to take clear and prompt decisions within his main operational responsibilities. Other decisions are subject to prior consultation with higher levels.

Planning and organizing

It is an open task to plan and organize a self-cargo operation.

Job Challenge

Comply and surpass ACS high-quality standards established by the company.

Skills and Qualifications

• Functional knowledge in handling air cargo and warehouse operations

• Knowledge of DHL‘s business and core processes to make relevant assessments and recommendations

• Decision making skills to take clear and prompt decisions within main operational responsibilities.

• Organizational skills plan and optimize the different operational procedures related to Flight Operations, ACS, the Warehouse, and the staff in charge, to achieve compliance with the standards and internal    requirements needed to meet the schedules of the aircraft

• Excellent communication skills and demonstrated fluency in written and spoken English. Bilingual in English and Spanish plus

• Ability to identify, analyze and solve Flight Operations and ACS operational problems, to guarantee a timely and effective operation, in compliance with the GSOP of the company

• Surpass the high standards of quality, efficiency, and safety and security with respect to the management of flight coordination and the warehouse operation, as well as delivery times and reception off         loads

• Ability and willingness to work flexible hours including day or night shift, including weekends and holidays

• Ability to work independently and exercise sound judgment

• Minimum two years’ experience in a supervisory position. Preferably in air operations, controlling operational processes, handling Air Cargo, and Warehouse.

• High School or GED equivalent. Preferably technical studies/university

• Drive company vehicle inside airport roads and obtain CBP Clearance for out bound flights

• Drive company vehicle inside airport roads and clear inbound flight crews and documents under CBP regulations

*** Must possess valid Driver's License and pass pre-employment MVR Check ***
