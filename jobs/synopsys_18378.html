Job Description

We Are

Synopsys is the leader in engineering solutions from silicon to systems, enabling customers to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. We deliver industry-leading silicon design, IP, simulation and analysis solutions, and design services. We partner closely with our customers across a wide range of industries to maximize their R&D capability and productivity, powering innovation today that ignites the ingenuity of tomorrow.

You Are

You have built your career at the intersection of analog circuit design and silicon validation, where theoretical understanding meets real-world measurement. Over the years, you have developed the ability to diagnose complex signal integrity issues, correlate lab data with design intent, and guide teams through the technical challenges that arise when high-speed circuits are tested under actual operating conditions. You understand that effective validation requires both deep circuit knowledge and disciplined test methodology.

Leading a team means more than delegating tasks. It means building technical capability in your engineers, establishing robust processes that scale across projects, and maintaining the quality of deliverables that design teams and customers depend on. You have managed validation projects from test plan development through final reporting, and you know how to balance hands-on technical involvement with the broader responsibilities of team leadership. You communicate technical findings clearly to cross-functional stakeholders, and you make decisions that keep projects moving forward even when data is incomplete or ambiguous. At Synopsys, you will join our established validation engineering operation in Lisbon, leading a team focused on high-speed analog IP that enables next-generation connectivity solutions.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Lead silicon validation operations for high-speed analog integrated circuits at our Lisbon site, managing lab resources, equipment utilization, and test execution across concurrent projects

  • Direct testing and characterization of SerDes implementations using oscilloscopes, bit error rate testers, network analyzers, and other precision measurement equipment

  • Oversee silicon debug activities and results analysis, identifying root causes of performance issues and providing technical guidance to design engineering teams

  • Manage the development of comprehensive validation reports that document silicon performance, compliance with specifications, and recommendations for design improvements

  • Supervise the design, fabrication, and bring-up of custom test boards and hardware fixtures required for accurate circuit characterization

  • Build and develop your validation engineering team through mentorship, technical training, and performance management

  • Collaborate with analog design, digital design, applications engineering, and product management to resolve technical challenges and support product development schedules

  • Maintain and enhance lab infrastructure, ensuring optimal equipment performance and availability for validation activities

The Impact You Will Have

  • Your validation results will directly inform design decisions for high-speed IP deployed in USB, PCIe, Ethernet, and other industry-standard connectivity protocols

  • The characterization data and validation reports your team produces will define performance specifications that customers use to qualify Synopsys IP for their products

  • Debug methodologies you establish will accelerate issue resolution and reduce silicon respins, directly impacting time to market and development costs

  • Test infrastructure and procedures you implement will improve efficiency and consistency across validation programs at the Lisbon site

  • Engineers you develop will advance their technical skills and grow into senior contributors capable of leading complex validation efforts independently

  • Your technical leadership will strengthen collaboration between validation and design organizations, improving product quality and customer satisfaction

  • The validation excellence you drive in Lisbon will reinforce our site's reputation as a center of technical expertise within Synopsys

What You'll Need

  • 5+ years of experience in silicon validation, test, or characterization of analog or mixed-signal integrated circuits

  • Demonstrated experience managing technical teams, including responsibility for project planning, resource allocation, performance evaluation, and professional development of engineers

  • Proven track record of building team capability and delivering results through others while maintaining technical quality standards

  • Degree in Electronic Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline

  • Strong foundation in analog IC design principles with the ability to apply circuit theory to analyze silicon behavior, performance limitations, and measurement anomalies

  • Solid understanding of SerDes validation methodology including eye diagram analysis, jitter characterization, equalization techniques, and signal integrity principles

  • Working knowledge of high-speed communication protocols such as USB, PCIe, or Ethernet, particularly at the physical layer

  • Proficiency in programming and scripting for test automation and data analysis using MATLAB, Python, or equivalent tools

  • Experience with FPGA-based test systems is beneficial, familiarity with Verilog or VHDL for custom test logic is desirable

  • Strong English communication skills for technical documentation, presentations, and cross-functional collaboration

Who You Are

  • You can analyze measurement data from lab equipment, identify patterns that indicate circuit issues, and develop hypotheses that guide the next phase of testing

  • You balance technical involvement with management responsibilities, knowing when to provide hands-on support and when to empower your team to solve problems independently

  • You communicate technical information effectively to diverse audiences, from detailed circuit-level discussions with design engineers to summary updates for program management

  • You have experience developing test hardware and understand the importance of proper board design, signal routing, and fixturing for obtaining reliable characterization data

  • You manage multiple concurrent validation projects with attention to schedules, deliverables, and dependencies without losing sight of technical quality

  • You provide constructive feedback when test plans require refinement or when technical approaches need adjustment based on practical constraints

  • You thrive in an established technical environment where continuous improvement and operational excellence are valued

The Team You'll Be Part Of

You will join our established validation engineering operation in Lisbon, leading a team of skilled engineers dedicated to characterizing high-speed analog IP. Our Lisbon site has built a strong reputation for technical excellence in silicon validation, with well-equipped lab facilities and established processes that support rigorous testing and analysis. Your recruiter will share more details about team structure and current initiatives during the interview process.
