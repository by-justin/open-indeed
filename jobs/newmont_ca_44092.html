Job Description

Job Location: Brucejack Camp Workplace Type:   Onsite Pay Grade: 002   Closing Date:

Our Brucejack Gold Mine, a high-grade underground mine in beautiful northern British Columbia, is located approximately 65 kilometres north of Stewart. Brucejack is a fly-in/fly-out operation with pick-up points across Western Canada, making it easy for people from different places to join us. We work closely with local First Nations communities including the Nisga’a, Tahltan, Gitanyow, and Gitxsan, to build respectful partnerships and celebrate our shared community.

As a member of the Brucejack family, we put your health, comfort, and safety first. You’ll stay in private rooms with individual washrooms, enjoy balanced meals prepared by our exceptional culinary team, and have access to a 24-hour snack and sandwich bar. We also have plenty of ways for you to relax and stay active, with a gym, fitness classes, a golf simulator, and seasonal activities like snowshoeing and hiking. For creative downtime, our music room is a fully equipped space where you can play or listen to music. Our Brucejack Recreation Committee, run by employees on a volunteer basis, organizes activities and events to make camp life fun and bring people together. The committee also plans holiday decorations, charity raffles, and outdoor events, helping create a warm and friendly community.

Role Purpose

As a Light Equipment Operator – Winter Snow Removal Team , you will support Surface Operations by ensuring safe and reliable snow removal during the winter season. Your work is essential to maintaining site accessibility, operational continuity, and a safe working environment in challenging weather conditions. This is a temporary role .

Key Roles and Responsibilities

  • Operate light equipment including Rock trucks, skid steers, crew trucks, and small excavators
  • Perform daily equipment inspections and routine maintenance checks
  • Complete job hazard assessments and adhere to all safety procedures
  • Participate in safety meetings and maintain compliance with site regulations
  • Support snow removal activities in remote, winter conditions
  • Contribute to a safe, productive, and team-focused work environment

Required Skills, Knowledge, and Experience

  • Valid Class 5 driver’s license with a clean driver’s abstract
  • 2-3 years of experience operating equipments
  • Demonstrated ability to safely and efficiently operate light equipment
  • Physically fit and capable of lifting and moving up to 50 lbs
  • Comfortable working 12-hour shifts in all weather conditions
  • WHMIS certification preferred
  • Previous remote site or camp experience considered an asset
  • Strong commitment to safety and operational excellence

Your Impact

By joining our Winter Snow Removal Team , you will play a critical role in keeping operations moving safely throughout the winter season. Your expertise and dedication will help maintain site access, protect colleagues and equipment, and support the success of our operations in a remote camp environment.

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly rate: CA$38.99/hour
  • Remote camp setting with private accommodations provided
  • Onsite meals, gym, recreation, and entertainment facilities
  • Travel allowance and transportation arrangements included
  • Rotational schedule of 13 days on / 13 days off
  • Drug- and alcohol-free workplace (pre-employment testing required)
