Position Overview:

The Area Manager is responsible for driving commercial performance across the assigned geography by leading a team of Associate Territory Managers and Territory Managers to achieve revenue, customer acquisition, customer retention and profitability objectives. The role ensures disciplined sales execution, market development and customer engagement while strengthening Blue Dart's presence across the assigned territory.

Reporting to the Branch Manager, the incumbent serves as the frontline commercial leader responsible for translating branch business objectives into field execution. The role focuses on sales productivity, people development, customer relationship management and market expansion while collaborating closely with Operations, Customer Service and other support functions to deliver sustainable business growth and superior customer experience.

Job Purpose:

To lead and develop a high-performing field sales team that consistently delivers revenue growth, customer acquisition, customer retention and market expansion by driving sales excellence, coaching sales teams, strengthening customer relationships and ensuring disciplined commercial execution across the assigned geography.

Key Roles & Responsibilities:

Sales Leadership & Territory Management

·       Lead the commercial performance of the assigned geography by driving achievement of revenue, customer acquisition and customer retention objectives.

·       Translate branch sales plans into territory-wise execution plans and monitor performance against agreed business targets.

·       Drive disciplined sales execution across Associate Territory Managers and Territory Managers to maximise market coverage and business growth.

·       Review sales performance, pipeline health and territory productivity regularly and implement corrective actions where required.

·       Ensure effective territory coverage, customer engagement and sales execution across the assigned geography.

Customer Acquisition & Revenue Growth

·       Drive acquisition of new customers across SME, Corporate and Retail segments in line with business growth objectives.

·       Support the sales team in identifying high-potential prospects, developing account strategies and closing key business opportunities.
