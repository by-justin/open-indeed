Position Overview:

Canada Goose is transforming how it engages customers in its owned channels. We are moving from a high-volume, mass, untargeted send model to a precision channel — one that earns attention and drives greater results through personalisation, intelligent segmentation and disciplined subscriber base health management. The Director, Email Marketing will lead that transformation.

This role owns the strategy, execution and optimisation of Canada Goose’s global customer communications across Email, SMS and related owned channels. You will re-architect our contact strategy, deepen personalisation and lifecycle capabilities, and rebuild the programme around long-term engagement and subscriber health. You will drive customer engagement, retention and revenue through highly personalised, data-driven and automated marketing programmes across campaign, lifecycle and triggered communications.

Working cross-functionally with Loyalty, E-commerce, Retail, Technology, Creative, Brand/Marketing and Customer Experience teams, you will deliver personalised customer lifecycle journeys and campaigns that align with customer needs and business priorities.

You are critical in shaping the Email & SMS roadmap globally, improving customer contact strategy and ensuring the channel continues to drive revenue while protecting long-term subscriber health. You will stay ahead of customer communication trends, CRM and CDP technology, marketing automation, deliverability, SMS best practices, AI-driven personalisation and privacy — acting as a thought leader who sets the standard for what a modern, precision owned channel looks like.

What You'll Do:

Channel Strategy & Transformation

  • Lead the transformation of the global Email & SMS programme from a high-volume, mass send model to a precision, personalisation-led channel measured on relevance, engagement quality and lifetime value.
  • Own the global Email & SMS strategy, roadmap and performance against engagement, revenue, retention and subscriber-health objectives.
  • Lead annual channel strategy, planning, forecasting and investment prioritisation — including channel budget ownership — to drive sustainable, efficient growth across Email and SMS.

Personalisation, Lifecycle & Segmentation

  • Develop customer segmentation, targeting and personalisation strategies that replace one-size-fits-all sends with relevant, individualised communications and improve business performance.
  • Oversee lifecycle and triggered programmes across onboarding, engagement, retention and win-back to drive personalised engagement, incremental revenue and customer lifetime value.
  • Lead planning, execution and optimisation of campaign Email and SMS communications — including brand, commercial and consumer moments — with precision targeting as the default, not the exception.
  • Partner with Data and Analytics to advance first-party and zero-party data capture, identity and activation, and to apply predictive models (propensity, churn, next-best-action) that power data-driven personalisation.

Subscriber Base Health & Deliverability

  • Establish contact strategy, frequency management, suppression logic, audience eligibility and trigger governance that protect subscriber health and inbox placement across Email and SMS.
  • Own subscriber base health as a core discipline — list hygiene, engagement-based sunsetting and re-engagement, consent and preference management, and active protection of sender reputation.
  • Monitor and optimise deliverability and inbox placement in partnership with deliverability partners.

Measurement & Experimentation

  • Drive testing, experimentation and incrementality measurement to continuously improve campaign, SMS, triggered and lifecycle performance.
  • Monitor and optimise key channel KPIs — demand revenue, traffic, engagement, conversion, subscriber growth, unsubscribe/complaint rates, deliverability and customer lifetime value.
  • Build clear performance reporting and translate results into decisions and investment for senior leadership.

Technology, Data & Vendors

  • Own the marketing automation, CRM and CDP technology roadmap, including ESP, journey orchestration and personalisation capabilities.
  • Manage strategic vendor, agency and deliverability partnerships to support customer communications, marketing technology and channel performance.

Team & Cross-functional Leadership

  • Partner with Loyalty, CRM, E-commerce, Retail, Technology, Analytics, Creative, Brand, Marketing and regional teams to align owned-channel communications with business priorities.
  • Lead, coach and develop the Email & SMS team, building the capability, operating model and accountability required to run a precision channel.

  • Demonstrated experience leading channel change — evolving an email programme from high-volume, batch sending toward precision, personalisation-led communications and stronger subscriber health.
  • Experience with customer journey orchestration, lifecycle marketing, behavioural triggers, personalisation, segmentation and retention strategies.
  • Experience managing marketing technology vendors, agencies, deliverability partners and external execution resources.   • Strong understanding of Email deliverability, inbox placement, sender reputation, suppression logic, preference management and subscriber health.

  • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret performance data, identify trends and turn insights into precision-targeting actions.
