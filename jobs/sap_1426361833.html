Value Senior Advisor

Role Overview

The Value Advisor is a senior, customer-facing role partnering with C-level executives (CEO, CIO, CFO, CHRO) to drive enterprise-wide transformation and measurable business value.

The role requires strong financial acumen, including the ability to analyze financial statements, mid-term business plans, integrated reports, and executive KPIs, in order to ground transformation discussions in quantitative value.

Working closely with SAP sales and delivery teams, the Value Advisor shapes executive agendas, develops compelling business cases, and positions SAP as a trusted transformation partner.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead executive engagements to assess strategic priorities, transformation objectives, and value opportunities.

  • Develop executive- and board-level business cases using financial and non-financial metrics.

  • Drive Value Management, including:

  • Value-based GO / NO-GO decision support

  • Definition of clear value targets for transformation initiatives

  • Measurement of value realization and guidance for continuous improvement

  • Partner with sales teams to shape account strategies, support strategic deals, and accelerate pipeline.

  • Lead complex engagements with cross-functional SAP teams and customer stakeholders.

  • Build long-term, trust-based relationships with executive sponsors.

Methods & Continuous Learning

  • Apply SAP’s global value methodologies, frameworks, and tools.

  • Continuously stay current with evolving methods and tools developed by SAP global teams and the Value Advisory community.

  • Incorporate new approaches to maintain relevance and thought leadership.

Experience & Qualifications

  • 15+ years of experience in enterprise roles, management consulting, or digital/business transformation.

  • Proven engagement with CEO, CIO, CFO, and CHRO.

  • Strong financial and business analysis skills.

  • Executive presence and excellent communication skills.

  • Experience with SAP solutions is strongly preferred.

Skills & Mindset

  • Ability to translate complex financial and operational analysis into clear executive narratives.

  • Strong team player who listens well, collaborates across functions, and builds trust through respectful engagement.

  • Adapts communication to stakeholder needs rather than pushing predefined answers.

  • Self-starter who identifies issues, mobilizes stakeholders, delivers quick wins, and scales them into repeatable practices.

  • Demonstrates the ability to generate organizational impact through individual initiative, beyond personal contribution.
