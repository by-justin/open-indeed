Job Description:

Description:

IT Corporate Affairs & Legal  (DMGC) is responsible for the delivery of External websites on Airbus group communication perimeter.

DGMC allows the Corporate Communication department to effectively help promote the digital transformation for the company ensuring delivery of the right message to the right audience. Major axes are:

  • Digital Communications channels - publish and adapt content for multiple channels, analysis of channels traffic
  • Digital Communication backbone - creation of communication content
  • Events - organize and manage corporate and public events
  • Branding - provide to consumers all products linked to the brand

Methodology is evolving from a traditional V cycle and single agile teams to the SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) methodology to be able to scale up to large development teams.

Responsibilities:

To be successful in this job, the individual must accomplish the following results:

  • Act as a point of contact for escalations around IT processes in the organisation. Analyses console messages, diagnoses system failures and takes corrective action in order to ensure continuity of operations.
  • Track and perform ITSM process, performance review across IT, provide status to IT leadership and recommend improvements. Performs the ITSM based support processes that include incident, problem, change, configuration, and event management.
  • Ensure that ongoing service delivery and support meet agreed customer requirements.
  • Conduct in-depth discovery meetings, targeted at understanding, capturing and identifying the complex business requirements and operational objectives of the customer, with recommendations on automation.
  • Serves as liaison between product management teams and technical resources during the product development and implementation to ensure the integrity of the solution delivered.
  • Monitors and tracks system performance against negotiated service-level agreements (SLAs).
  • Communicates improvement, updates and obsolescence management of products to the IT Product Manager.
  • Run Operations & Platform Stability - Communication Suite Operations: Drive end-to-end "Run" operations for the Communication Product suite, including Webfactory, Media Gallery, Airbus TV (ATV), WHIP, Social Media Platform, and Web Analytics.
  • Availability & Stability Management: Accountable for ensuring continuous platform availability, stability, and mobile optimization across all hosted applications.
  • Service Level Objectives (SLOs) & MTTR: Oversee the Quality Plan and SLO frameworks in agreement with business stakeholders, actively working to maintain and reduce Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR).
  • Incident, Problem & Defect Management -Incident & Defect Management (E2E Responsible): Primary owner for handling and resolving incidents and defects across the operational landscape.
  • Major Incident Management: Lead emergency response and transversal communication during major operational disruptions.
  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA) & Problem Management: Perform L2/L3 component-level troubleshooting, document problem tickets, and conduct RCAs to prevent recurring issues.
  • Deployment, Release & Change Management-Weekly Deployment Planning: Help Service delivery manager and Technical lead to organize weekly code/feature deployment schedules.
  • Release & Access Management: Control platform releases, user permissions, and environment access.
  • Cutover & Events Planning: Responsible for cutover execution, deployment readiness, and event support.
  • Change Advisory Board (CAB) & Go/No-Go: Prepare and deliver presentations for CAB meetings, participating directly in Go / NO-GO release decisions.
  • Change Management & User Readiness: Support smooth transitions and user readiness during new platform releases or upgrades.
  • Service Governance, KPI Tracking & Reporting -KPI Dashboards & Monthly Reviews: Maintain operational KPI dashboards and lead Monthly Operational Reviews with business leadership.
  • Service Catalog Creation: Build, maintain, and expand the Service Catalog aligned with business demands.
  • Application Monitoring: Oversee continuous application monitoring and system health tracking.
  • Operational Maintenance, Compliance & Cost Optimization -Certificate & Security Maintenance: Oversee SSL/TLS certificate migrations and renewals.
  • Operational Excellence: Drive continuous improvement initiatives across operational processes.
  • Framework & Governance Compliance (Support Role): Support compliance with LBIP+, EN.IM frameworks, export control, and security checklists.
  • License & Cost Support: Assist with license renewals and cost-optimization efforts.
