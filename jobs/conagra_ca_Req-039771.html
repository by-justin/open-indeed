Key Responsibilities:

  • Material Handling:
  • Operate lift trucks to transport goods within the facility.
  • Load and unload materials from storage bins, racking, trailers and staging areas.
  • Pre-stage materials for production, labelling or shipments.
  • SAP R/3 System Use:
  • Effectively and efficiently use the mounted or handheld scanners (training will be provided as needed)
  • Use SAP R/3 to track inventory movements and update stock levels.
  • Assist in performing cycle counts and reconcile inventory discrepancies using SAP.
  • Safety & Equipment Maintenance:
  • Conduct daily pre-operational checks (oil, water, propane, hydraulics, lights, horn).
  • Report equipment malfunctions and perform minor troubleshooting.
  • Adhere to OSHA and company safety regulations.
  • Refill propane cylinders as required (propane handling certification will be provided)
  • Communication & Coordination:
  • Work closely with team leads, shift supervisors, shipping clerks, and warehouse supervisors.
  • Ensure accurate execution of transactions and proper documentation.
  • Communicate effectively regarding inventory issues.

Qualifications:

  • Certifications:
  • Valid forklift/lift truck operator certification (will provide if needed)
  • Propane Handling certification (will provide if needed)
  • Skills:
  • Familiarity with RF scanners and barcode systems.
  • Basic computer literacy for SAP and inventory systems.
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.

Working Conditions:

  • Ability to work in indoor/outdoor environments.
  • Must be able to 50 lbs. and perform physical tasks.
  • Exposure noise, moving equipment, and variable temperatures.
