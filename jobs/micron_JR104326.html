■ Job Summary

As a Photo Scanner Equipment Engineer, you will be responsible for a broad range of activities including equipment start-up, performance optimization, and sustaining stable operation of photolithography scanner systems. You will lead the evaluation and implementation of new equipment as well as modifications to existing systems, supporting productivity improvement and quality enhancement for both current and next-generation products.

This role requires close collaboration with cross-functional teams such as manufacturing, facilities, development organizations, and equipment suppliers to continuously improve equipment performance and operational efficiency. The position also requires the ability to work independently and to deliver projects successfully within defined timelines.

■ Responsibilities and Tasks

• Complete all required safety training and perform duties in compliance with applicable safety standards and procedures • Lead the installation, start-up, and performance evaluation of photolithography scanner equipment • Plan and manage equipment installation, upgrades, and modification activities • Drive optimization and sustainment of equipment performance in terms of availability, productivity, and accuracy • Perform systematic troubleshooting of equipment-related issues and conduct Root Cause Analysis (RCA) to ensure effective recurrence prevention • Contribute to product quality and yield improvement through optimization of equipment conditions and settings • Collaborate with equipment suppliers to drive performance improvements and technological enhancements • Develop and maintain documentation related to equipment specifications, operating procedures, maintenance requirements, and performance metrics • Support internal and external audits and promote the implementation of corrective actions where required

■ Required Skills and Qualifications

• Basic communication skills in Japanese (preferred: equivalent to JLPT N2 or higher)
