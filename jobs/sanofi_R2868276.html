À propos de Sanofi

Sanofi est une entreprise biopharmaceutique qui innove en R&D et exploite l'IA à grande échelle pour améliorer la vie des gens et créer de la croissance à long terme. Notre compréhension approfondie du système immunitaire – et notre pipeline innovant – nous permet d’inventer des médicaments et des vaccins qui traitent et protègent des millions de personnes dans le monde entier. Ensemble, nous poursuivons les miracles de la science pour améliorer la vie des gens.

À propos du poste

Rejoignez l’équipe qui transforme la manière de soigner les personnes ayant des problèmes immunitaires, des maladies rares, des cancers et des affections neurologiques. Dans les soins spécialisés, vous contribuerez à fournir des traitements révolutionnaires qui apportent de l’espoir aux patients ayant certains des besoins les plus élevés.​

Le Spécialiste des comptes clés est un rôle sur le terrain qui combine une forte implication scientifique avec la gestion stratégique des comptes. Vous serez responsable de développer votre territoire, d'aider à façonner le paysage des traitements dans le myélome multiple et de renforcer la présence de Sanofi en tant que partenaire de confiance à long terme au sein de la communauté oncologique. Pour réussir, il vous faudra une solide connaissance de la maladie, un instinct commercial et la capacité de travailler efficacement avec plusieurs groupes de parties prenantes et fonctions internes.

About the job

Join the team transforming care for people with immune challenges, rare diseases, cancers, and neurological conditions. In Specialty Care, you’ll help deliver breakthrough treatments that bring hope to patients with some of the highest unmet needs.​

The Key Account Manager – Multiple Myeloma is a field-based role that combines deep scientific engagement with strategic account management. You will be responsible for growing your territory, helping shape the treatment landscape in Multiple Myeloma, and building Sanofi’s presence as a trusted, long-term partner within the oncology community.

To be successful, you will need strong disease knowledge, commercial instincts, and the ability to work effectively across multiple stakeholder groups and internal functions.

Main Responsibilities:

Commercial Performance & Business Leadership

  • Meet or exceed sales and performance objectives while managing territory resources responsibly.
  • Develop, execute, and continuously refine a data-driven territory business plan, incorporating market dynamics, customer insights, and brand strategy.
  • Identify risks and opportunities in a timely way and adjust your approach to protect and grow business results.
  • Analyze market trends, customer needs, and competitive insights to inform strategy and identify new growth opportunities.

Strategic Account Management

  • Build and maintain deep, value-based partnerships with Hematologic Oncologists, nurses, pharmacists, and key allied health professionals.
  • Serve as the primary commercial contact for Sanofi within your accounts and coordinate cross-functional support from Marketing, Medical, Patient Support, and other internal partners.
  • Understand account decision-making structures and adapt your engagement approach accordingly.
  • Support institutions and key accounts at all levels by guiding formulary and budget approval processes.

Scientific & Clinical Engagement

  • Conduct high-quality scientific exchange aligned with the Product Monograph and in accordance with Sanofi’s ethical and compliance standards.
  • Clearly articulate the clinical and patient value of Sanofi therapies within evolving Multiple Myeloma treatment paradigms.
  • Educate and train healthcare teams on the clinical benefits, administration, and real-world use of Sanofi therapies.
  • Facilitate peer-to-peer learning between community-based and academic oncologists to optimize Multiple Myeloma treatment pathways.

Stakeholder Influence & Advocacy

  • Build meaningful local advocacy through consistent, credible relationships with key stakeholders.
  • Support regional and national educational programs (e.g., OLAs, learning initiatives, congresses).
  • Maintain a current and accurate territory and account database to inform national planning and execution.
  • Utilize omnichannel and digital tools to enhance customer engagement and drive impactful conversations.

Operational Excellence & Compliance

  • Execute all activities in full alignment with Sanofi’s regulatory, legal, and compliance standards.
  • Maintain timely, accurate CRM documentation, call reporting, expense management, and other administrative requirements.
  • Successfully complete all mandatory training and stewardship of company assets.
  • Participate in national and regional meetings, conferences, and training programs.
