Job Description

Overview

As one of the world’s leading consultancies operating in the built and natural environment, we offer unparalleled opportunities for talented individuals in search of a rewarding career around the world.

As a provider of multi-disciplined technical services to the built environment, AtkinsRéalis is committed to design excellence in the delivery of first-class solutions which combine innovation, imagination, and best practice.

Due to our continuing expansion, we currently have an opportunity for an Engineer II on multi-disciplinary offshore executed projects from office in Bengaluru (India)

Working in a multidisciplinary team of designers and engineers providing a professional design service to our clients, this is an ideal opportunity for individuals looking to develop their international experience with a world-renowned consultant.

Your Role

You will be an Engineer within the Bridges team carrying out the design and assessment of associated with highway/railway projects.  You will be responsible for:

·        Maintaining technical quality standards

·        Delivering projects to time and budget

·        Liaising with Canada based customers in a responsive and client focused manner.

The successful candidate will need to

·        Be highly self-motivated and ambitious.

·        Be able to lead a small team.

·        Should initiate their professional accreditation with Canada (P.Eng.) or UK (CEng)

·        Be able to work with minimal supervision.

·        Have the ability to adapt to different cultures and working environments to build rapport with customers.

·        Be able to build excellent working relationships with colleagues and clients.

Training

·        AtkinsRéalis develops individuals through a portfolio of training and development courses designed to help you make the best of your abilities and talents.  We are an accredited Investor in People and run a Continuous Development Program.

About You

·        5 to 8 years of relevant experience in engineering consultancy, with a strong focus on bridge engineering and associated civil structures.

·        Experience across preliminary, concept, and detailed design stages of infrastructure projects.

Technical Competencies

·        Proficient in structural analysis and design software such as CSiBridge and MIDAS.

·        Demonstrated experience in the design and analysis of:

·        Reinforced Concrete (RC) Bridges

·        Prestressed Concrete (PSC) Bridges

·        Structural Steel Bridges

·        Composite Structures

·        Strong understanding of:

·        Soil-Structure Interaction (SSI)

·        Seismic Analysis and Design, including:

·        Response Spectrum Analysis

·        Non-linear Static (Pushover) Analysis

·        Time History Analysis

Ability to interpret and utilise inputs from

·        Highway/Rail Alignments

·        Drainage and Utility Drawings

·        Landscape Schemes

·        Hydrological Studies

·        Geotechnical Investigation Reports

·        Project Delivery & Coordination

·        Experience working within multidisciplinary project environments and coordinating with various engineering disciplines.

·        Ability to provide technical direction and design inputs to BIM technicians for the production of engineering drawings and deliverables.

·        Experience in preparing technical reports, design calculations, specifications, and related documentation.

Quality & Leadership

·        Familiarity with quality management systems, standards, and project procedures.

·        Capable of working independently with minimal supervision.

·        Experience in mentoring and guiding junior engineers.

·        Proven ability to review and check the work of junior team members to ensure technical quality and compliance.

·        Software & Communication Skills

·        Proficient in Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).

·        Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English.

Desirable Attributes

·        Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

·        Commitment to delivering high-quality engineering solutions.

·        Ability to work effectively within collaborative, multicultural project teams.
