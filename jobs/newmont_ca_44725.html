Job Description

Newmont produces metals the world needs - safely, responsibly and with care for people, communities and the environment. With a 100+ year history, we continue to shape the future of mining through innovation and strong operational performance. By joining our inclusive, world-class team, you can help shape the future of mining and make a meaningful difference.

Red Chris is an open-pit copper-gold mine in the famous Golden Triangle of northern British Columbia, situated on the traditional territory of the Tahltan Nation. As a fly-in, fly-out operation, Red Chris is reached via charter flights from multiple locations in BC and Alberta, with teams working on a rotational schedule. Currently an active open-pit producer, Red Chris is poised for an exciting transformation – we are developing a high-value underground block cave that will extend the mine’s life for decades and unlock even more resources. The remote location fosters a close-knit “camp” community where safety and teamwork come first. Working at Red Chris offers an adventurous career at the frontier of mining innovation, with the opportunity to shape a major expansion while enjoying the natural beauty of northern Canada. Our team values strong partnerships with local First Nations and is committed to operating in a safe, inclusive and environmentally responsible way.

Your Impact
  • Repair, adjust, maintain and improve stationary equipment in a 30,000 ton per day crusher plant and concentrator
  • Follow OHS, Mines Act and Red Chris Policies and Procedures
  • Able to work outdoors in severe conditions
  • Able to work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Able to work day or night shift as required
  • Provide mentorship and assist training of apprentices
  • Champion safe work practices
  • Required to work in maintenance management systems
  • Required to log information and create reports
As Part of the Team

To perform maintenance and construction duties as a fixed plant mechanic (Millwright) within the Fixed Plant Maintenance team. To safely perform scheduled and unscheduled tasks to optimize equipment availability, and the Mill Maintenance team’s performance.

  • Working closely with Electrical, Instrumentation, and Mill Operations team to achieve equipment availability targets and efficiency
  • Positive relationships with departmental teams on site
  • Clear and regular communications with departmental teams on site
Prerequisites
  • Interprovincial Red Seal Millwright
  • Valid Class 5 driver’s license required
  • Computer skills
  • Overhead crane, Aerial Work Platform (AWP), Rigging certificates an asset
  • Welding certificates an asset
  • Equipment Operator certificate an asset
  • Extensive experience with pumps, conveyors, mills, and piping in a mining environment
  • Experience with Gyratory Crushers
  • Experience living and working in remote camp environment
  • A team player that works well with others
  • Strong oral and written communication skills
Work Environment

This is a Fly-in, Fly-out position at our Red Chris Mine, operating on a 15x13 schedule with alternating day/night shifts.

The expected rate for this role is CA$59.07. This range is tied to the British Columbia market for jobs performed in British Columbia. The salary offer to the successful candidate will be based on job-related education, training, and/or experience.  The salary offer will not be based on a candidate’s pay history at other jobs, and by law, Newmont will not seek information about pay history, and candidates should not share such information with Newmont.
