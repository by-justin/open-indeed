Position at DNEG

Technical Recruiter – Product & Engineering Location: Mumbai Reports To: Head of Recruitment Role Summary Prime Focus and DNEG are seeking an enthusiastic and proactive Technical Recruiter to support hiring across Product Management, Software Engineering, Platform Engineering, Core Services, Cloud Infrastructure, Data, and AI/ML functions. This role is ideal for a recruiter with 3–6 years of experience in technology hiring who is passionate about identifying and attracting high-calibre technical talent. The recruiter will work closely with hiring managers, engineering leaders, product leaders, and the Head of Recruitment to build world-class teams that support the company’s technology, innovation, and business objectives. While based within a media and entertainment technology organization, this role should be approached as a technology talent acquisition role. The successful candidate will recruit talent comparable to that found in product companies, SaaS organizations, gaming companies, AI companies, and digital engineering firms. The role will be responsible for managing end-to-end recruitment processes, building talent pipelines, delivering an exceptional candidate experience, and developing a strong understanding of technology and product functions. About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
