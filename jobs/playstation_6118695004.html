Client Partner, Advertising Sales

Who We Are

The Sony Interactive Media Solutions team is a dynamic, global advertising sales organization focused on driving innovation and delivering value across the gaming and entertainment ecosystem. By connecting brands with PlayStation’s highly engaged audience, we develop impactful advertising solutions that create value for our partners while supporting the continued growth of the PlayStation business.

As we continue to expand our advertising business, we are looking for an experienced, client-focused sales professional who is passionate about building strategic partnerships and delivering exceptional business outcomes.

Role Overview

As a Client Partner, Advertising Sales, you will serve as a trusted advisor to a portfolio of strategic advertising partners, driving long-term business growth through consultative sales and relationship management. You will work closely with advertisers, media agencies, and cross-functional teams to develop innovative marketing solutions that leverage the unique strengths of the PlayStation ecosystem.

This role plays a key part in expanding PlayStation's advertising business by identifying growth opportunities, supporting the launch of new advertising products, and delivering measurable value for both clients and PlayStation. Success in this role requires a passion for gaming and entertainment, strong commercial acumen, analytical thinking, and the ability to build trusted relationships across a complex, global organization.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Develop trusted, long-term relationships with strategic advertising partners and serve as their primary business contact.

  • Understand clients' business objectives and marketing challenges through a consultative sales approach.

  • Recommend integrated advertising solutions that maximize the value of the PlayStation platform.

  • Drive revenue growth through client retention, account expansion, and adoption of new advertising products and solutions.

  • Build strategic account plans and identify opportunities to grow existing partnerships.

  • Manage sales pipeline, revenue forecasting, and business reporting with accuracy and transparency.

  • Collaborate with Sales, Marketing, Product, Operations, and other cross-functional teams to develop compelling advertising solutions and business cases.

  • Partner with media agencies to successfully bring new advertising products and capabilities to market.

  • Act as the escalation point for client issues, ensuring an exceptional customer experience.

  • Represent PlayStation with integrity, professionalism, and the highest ethical standards in all external engagements.

Why Join Us

  • Help shape the future of advertising within one of the world's most recognized entertainment brands.

  • Collaborate with talented teams across global markets and diverse business functions.

  • Play a key role in launching innovative advertising products and commercial solutions.

  • Build strategic partnerships with leading brands and media agencies while driving meaningful business impact.

  • Grow your career in a fast-paced, collaborative, and innovative environment at PlayStation.

What We're Looking For

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in gaming, entertainment, digital media, marketing, advertising, or a related industry.

  • Experience in advertising sales, account management, client partnerships, or consultative B2B sales.

  • Proven success in driving revenue growth and managing strategic client relationships.

  • Experience developing and executing multi-channel advertising campaigns.

  • Strong understanding of digital advertising products, industry trends, and best practices.

  • Experience working across cross-functional teams in a matrixed organization.

  • Excellent communication, presentation, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills.

  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret business data and translate insights into commercial opportunities.

  • Passion for PlayStation, gaming, and the broader entertainment industry.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience working with media agencies or major advertisers.

  • Experience launching or selling new advertising products or marketing solutions.

  • Experience in a large global organization or a fast-growing business environment.

  • Knowledge of the gaming and digital entertainment ecosystem.

  • Experience collaborating with international teams across multiple regions.

Language Skills

  • Business proficiency in English.

  • Japanese language proficiency sufficient to build relationships with domestic clients and internal stakeholders.

Ideal Candidate

  • A client-first professional who builds trusted, long-term partnerships.

  • A collaborative team player who thrives in cross-functional and global environments.

  • A strategic thinker who combines commercial insight with strong execution.

  • A proactive self-starter who embraces change and continuously seeks opportunities to improve.

  • A person of high integrity who represents the PlayStation brand with professionalism and accountability.
