Job Description

Job Description

The Thomson Reuters Foundation is the corporate foundation of Thomson Reuters, the global news and information services company. As an independent charity, registered in the UK and the USA, we leverage our media, legal and data-driven expertise to strengthen independent journalism, enable access to the law and promote responsible business. Through news, media development, free legal assistance and data intelligence, we aim to build free, fair and informed societies.

TrustLaw is the world's largest pro bono legal network. We work with leading law firms and corporate legal teams to facilitate free legal support, research and resources for civil society organisations (CSOs), social enterprises and non-profit independent media around the globe. By spreading the practice of pro bono worldwide, TrustLaw wants to strengthen civil society and drive change.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the recruitment, vetting, management and engagement of NGOs, social enterprises and legal teams of the TrustLaw network.
  • Support the collection of feedback from our network of NGOs, social enterprises, and legal teams, identifying testimonials and success stories that help showcase the impact and value of TrustLaw services.
  • Support project follow-up and troubleshooting to ensure successful progress and members’ satisfaction with the service.
  • Assist in preparation and follow up of our team’s initiatives, webinars and events in Latin America.
  • Assist in communications with our members, including in the promotion of TrustLaw publications, among others.
  • Translate documents from English to Spanish and vice-versa.

Required Qualifications

  • Advanced students of:  law, international relations, translation, communications
  • A strong interest and motivation for legal pro bono, civic space, human rights, social entrepreneurship, independent media and the third sector.
  • Advanced/bilingual proficiency in English and Spanish (both written and spoken) is essential.
  • Excellent communication, organizational, and detail-oriented skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • A proactive, resourceful individual with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a dynamic environment.
  • A strong ability to work collaboratively in a multicultural team.
