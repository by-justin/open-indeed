If you are ready to excel, innovate, and truly enjoy where you work, apply now for the Corporate Counsel (India operations).

The Opportunity: Marvell is looking for a motivated Corporate Counsel who will provide legal support for Marvell’s India operation. Marvell has over 8,000 employees worldwide and approximately 2,000 employees at its India locations in Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Pune. This role will provide legal support and advice on a variety of employment, privacy, intellectual property, regulatory, and litigation matters. This role will perform legal research, draft legal documents, and serve as a trusted advisor to the business teams. You will work within a dynamic global Legal team and report to Senior Counsel, Marvell India. This position will be based in Marvell’s Bangalore office and requires onsite presence five days per week.

What You Can Expect

  • Provide legal support and advice to within Marvell’s India operations on a variety of commercial, employment, and regulatory matters
  • Assist with drafting, reviewing, and revising contracts and other legal documents
  • Conduct legal review of Marvell’s business initiatives, provide solution-oriented practical advice to facilitate innovation
  • Support negotiations by preparing research, first drafts, and issue lists
  • Support litigation and claims, and negotiations of disputes
  • Prepare, revise, and enhance Company policies and procedures in line with global policies and standards
  • Work with external legal counsel regarding specific matters requiring expert advice
  • Conduct and oversee internal investigations
  • Support design and delivery of training
  • Collaboratively work with cross functional, and geographically diverse stakeholders to drive matters to completion
  • Identify, assess, and mitigate risks
  • Learn Marvell’s products and services, industry and regulatory standards, and internal practices and ways of working
  • Adapt to ambiguity, remain open to new ideas, and adjust to changing needs and priorities
  • Perform other legal support tasks as assigned by Senior Counsel, India

What We're Looking For

  • Qualified to practice law in India with active Bar Admission
  • Bachelor’s degree in law from top tier law school
  • 3-6 minimum years of relevant in-house or law firm experience (as licensed counsel)
  • Corporate legal related legal disciplines strongly preferred
  • Proven knowledge of relevant legal and regulatory frameworks in India
  • Strong legal research and writing skills
  • Demonstrated understanding of contract law, corporate governance, and regulatory frameworks
  • Ability to apply foundational legal or regulatory theories to legal matters; learns quickly through coaching
  • Proactively takes initiative, responds effectively to changing priorities, and delivers results with urgency across a broad range of matters.
  • Ability to support and conduct negotiations
  • Experience drafting demand letters, discovery, litigation briefing, and settlement agreements preferred
  • Experience conducting and/or advising on workplace investigations preferred
  • Accepts feedback; proactively learns and identifies learning opportunities
  • Adapts to ambiguity, is open to new ideas, and adjusts to changing needs
  • Ability to handle tasks independently with minimal supervision, while receiving regular direction from Senior Counsel or other legal team members
  • Accountable and proactive self-starter, comfortable working independently
  • Excellent organizational skills and ability to manage and prioritize multiple assigned tasks
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in the English language
  • Experience in a fast-paced and dynamic organization, with demonstrated willingness to be flexible and adaptable to changing priorities
  • Highly approachable, collegial, and collaborative team player
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook)
  • Ability to work individually and as part of a team in a cross-cultural environment
  • Willingness and ability to travel within country as needed for business purposes This role is eligible to participate in Marvell's bonus and equity plans, including a new hire equity grant and annual equity refreshers, ensuring you are rewarded for your contributions and remain invested in the company's long-term success.
