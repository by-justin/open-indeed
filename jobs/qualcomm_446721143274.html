Role Overview

At Qualcomm Canada, our MSIP Silicon Validation team  is looking for validation engineering interns interested working on the most cutting-edge wireline technologies in the world. Under the supervision of experienced engineers, the intern will contribute to the validation of High-Speed SerDes and Die-to-Die IP Prototypes critical for Qualcomm products.  No previous background of silicon validation is required.

Responsibilities may include:

  • Characterizing analog blocks and performing PHY/Link Layer/Protocol compliance against various industry standards such as PCIe, Ethernet and UCIe
  • Bringing up silicon, debugging issues, presenting test results, and generating validation reports
  • Defining test plans and developing test methodologies for various tests
  • Developing and standardizing test scripts for various tests and automating test equipment
  • Determining equipment and accessories needs for various tests
  • Studying signal integrity and power integrity for evaluation boards and for silicon
  • Developing and automating a robotic handler to do regression and volume validation and develop scripts to automate data analysis

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience with lab equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies and multimeters
  • Relevant academic or personal project experience
  • Relevant industry experience (internship, summer work, or others)
  • Knowledge of scripting languages (Python, Perl, TCL, Shell)
  • Strong problem-solving skills
  • Excellent verbal, written, and presentation skills
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team in a fast-paced environment

Education Requirements:

  • In study towards a bachelor’s degree in one of the following: Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Engineering Science, Computer Science, or related field
  • For 16-month internships: must be available May/June 2027 - August/September 2028
  • Expected graduation date of November 2028 or later.
  • 1+ years of academic experience with programming languages such as C, C++, Python, etc
