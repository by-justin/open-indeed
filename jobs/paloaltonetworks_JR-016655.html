Job Summary

As cyber threats evolve in sophistication and security becomes a critical business imperative for organizations worldwide, Palo Alto Networks is seeking a Principal Technical Marketing Engineer to serve as the technical face of our NGFW and Quantum Security strategy. You will be THE subject matter expert (SME) for Next-Gen Firewalls and Quantum Security at Palo Alto Networks—a technical visionary who thrives at the intersection of deep firewall architecture and strategic market positioning. This role uniquely positions you to shape how the industry leader in network security continues to establish dominance in the rapidly evolving cybersecurity market. As the bridge between Engineering, Product Management, Sales, and the field, you will translate complex NGFW and Quantum Security concepts—AI-powered threat prevention, zero trust enforcement, cloud-native security, and advanced persistent threat detection—into compelling narratives, hands-on demos, and scalable enablement programs. You'll influence the product roadmap by bringing real-world insights from the enterprise security ecosystem while ensuring our sales teams and customers understand both the 'how' and the 'why' behind our solutions. You will be an industry thought leader: positioning Palo Alto Networks as the network security leader through speaking engagements at major industry events , authoring whitepapers that shape industry discourse, and working with cutting-edge technologies like Quantum Security, PA-Series/VM-Series/CN-Series NGFWs, ML-powered threat intelligence, and cloud-native security architectures. This is a fast-paced environment where you'll shape the narrative as the market evolves—building demo environments that showcase our security efficacy, conducting technical deep-dives for customers and Sales Engineers, and collaborating cross-functionally with Engineering, Product Management, and channel partners to drive market impact.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop high-impact whitepapers, architecture diagrams, video demos, and technical articles on Next-Gen Firewall architecture, Quantum Security, and advanced threat prevention.
  • Document technical concepts, including best practices for PA-Series hardware firewall deployments, security policy optimization, and Quantum Security implementation.
  • Develop and maintain premier demo environments showcasing NGFW and Quantum Security efficacy, including automated deployment scripts and reusable assets.
  • Design and execute enterprise-specific threat simulations, including APTs, ransomware, and zero-day exploits, to demonstrate ML-powered prevention and Quantum threat intelligence.
  • Lead enterprise Executive Briefings (EBCs), positioning Palo Alto Networks NGFW and Quantum Security solutions against evolving threats.
  • Conduct technical workshops and 'Train the Trainer' programs for Sales Engineers (SEs) and channel partners on hardware firewall architecture and Quantum Security capabilities.
  • Influence the product roadmap by providing real-world enterprise deployment feedback to Engineering and Product Management.
  • Serve as the technical liaison between the field and engineering to align product development with market demands and go-to-market strategy.
  • Establish Palo Alto Networks as the enterprise security leader through thought leadership, speaking at major events, and publishing content.
  • Maintain deep expertise in firewall technologies, PA-Series hardware, Quantum Security, the advanced threat landscape, and security compliance frameworks.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • 10+ years of experience in Technical Marketing, Systems Engineering, Escalation or related roles within the networking/security industry.
  • 4-5 years of direct enterprise firewall and datacenter security experience.
  • Hands-on experience with hardware firewall deployment and management (PA-Series Next-Generation Firewalls).
  • Direct experience with hardware firewall platforms such as Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, Fortinet, Check Point, and others.
  • Deep networking expertise, including routing, switching, datacenter network architecture, and core protocols (BGP, OSPF, VXLAN, EVPN).
  • Direct experience with datacenter architectures, including spine-leaf topologies, software-defined networking (SDN), and network virtualization.
  • Strong experience with datacenter technologies, including load balancing, firewalling at scale, high-availability clusters, and multi-tenant security architectures.
  • Deep understanding of Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) principles, micro-segmentation, and threat detection/response.
  • Knowledge of enterprise-specific security threats, including APTs, ransomware, zero-day exploits, lateral movement, and C2 communications.
  • Understanding of enterprise compliance frameworks (PCI-DSS, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA).
  • Expertise in threat intelligence specific to enterprise and critical infrastructure sectors.
  • Knowledge of datacenter and security protocols (IPsec, SSL/TLS, SSH, SNMP, syslog, NetFlow/IPFIX).
  • Understanding of post-quantum cryptography principles and quantum-safe security implementations.
  • Proven track record in technical content creation (whitepapers, blogs, technical documentation, reference architectures).
  • Hands-on experience building demos, proof-of-concepts, and technical environments.
  • Experience in sales enablement and technical training delivery for field teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of enterprise datacenter security architectures and distributed firewall deployments.
  • Hands-on experience with PA-Series hardware firewall implementations, high-availability clustering, and multi-tenant security environments.
  • Familiarity with distributed firewall deployments, campus security architectures, and branch office security solutions.
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation) for automated firewall provisioning.
  • Experience with container orchestration platforms (Kubernetes, Docker).
  • Proficiency in scripting and automation (Python, Bash, Go) for firewall policy management and API integration.
  • Understanding of DevSecOps practices in enterprise environments, including CI/CD pipeline security.
  • Industry Certifications such as PCNSE (preferred), CISSP, CCIE Security/Enterprise, or CCNP Security/Enterprise.
  • Ability to translate complex firewall architectures into business value for executives.
  • A passion for turning complex security architectures into compelling narratives that resonate with technical and executive audiences.
  • Proven ability to work seamlessly and build consensus across Engineering, Product Management, Sales, and external partners.
  • A track record of driving alignment and action across diverse stakeholder groups through technical credibility and relationship building.
  • World-class written and verbal communication skills, with experience authoring technical whitepapers and delivering keynote presentations.
