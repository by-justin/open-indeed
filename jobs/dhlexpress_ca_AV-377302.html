About DHL Blue Dart Express India:

Blue Dart Express Limited , a listed company, is a leader in courier, integrated air express package distribution and eCommerce in South Asia, offering a wide range of logistics solutions. Founded in 1983, it is recognised for reliable, time-bound and secure delivery services across 55,000+ locations in India as well as internationally.

Its competencies span evaluation and contracting, planning and transition, delivery and execution, supported by differentiated logistics and supply-chain capabilities. We pride ourselves on innovation, digitalisation, service excellence and long-term customer partnerships that enable businesses to thrive in an increasingly dynamic logistics environment.

Blue Dart wholly owns Blue Dart Aviation Limited, India’s only scheduled air express cargo airline, and Concorde Air Logistics Limited.

Blue Dart Express Limited is part of the DHL Group’s DHL eCommerce division, leveraging the global logistics leader’s network across 220 countries and territories to provide comprehensive express and logistics solutions to customers.

We are proud to be consistently ranked among India’s Top 100 Great Places to Work , together with DHL Group’s Top Employer recognition. Being an Employer of Choice is integral to who we are, creating a workplace where people are valued, empowered and proud to be Blue Darters.

Position Overview:

The National Sales Head is a senior Commercial leadership role responsible for defining and driving Blue Dart’s Pan-India Field Sales strategy and execution, with accountability for profitable revenue growth, customer acquisition, market development, sales productivity and commercial excellence across regions.

Reporting directly to the Chief Commercial Officer, the role will provide functional leadership to the Regional Sales Heads through a dotted-line reporting structure, ensuring that national commercial priorities are translated into consistent regional and territory-level execution.

The role will establish the national Field Sales agenda, sales operating rhythm, performance standards, pipeline and forecasting discipline, customer acquisition priorities and productivity framework, while enabling Regional Sales Heads to execute effectively within their respective markets.

The role will work closely with National Accounts, Sales Development, Product Marketing & Strategy, Pricing & Revenue Assurance, Strategic Partnerships, Customer Success, Operations, Finance and other Commercial teams to ensure an integrated approach to customer growth and profitable revenue delivery.

Job Purpose:

To build and lead a high-performing, commercially disciplined and customer-focused Pan-India Field Sales organisation that consistently delivers profitable and sustainable growth across regions.

The role will create a common national Field Sales framework while enabling regional teams to respond effectively to local market opportunities.

The National Sales Head will drive profitable revenue growth, new customer acquisition, customer retention, wallet-share expansion, market penetration, product adoption, sales productivity, pipeline quality, forecast accuracy and commercial discipline across the Field Sales organisation.

Key Roles & Responsibilities:

Commercial Strategy & Business Growth

  • Develop and execute the Pan-India Field Sales strategy in alignment with Blue Dart’s overall commercial strategy and business priorities.
  • Translate national growth ambitions into actionable regional, territory, customer-segment and product-level sales plans.
  • Own delivery of revenue, volume, yield, profitability and growth objectives for the Field Sales channel.
  • Establish national priorities across customer acquisition, retention, wallet-share growth, product penetration and market development.
  • Identify emerging growth opportunities across industries, geographies, customer segments and evolving logistics requirements.
  • Work with Regional Sales Heads to develop market-specific plans while ensuring alignment to national priorities.
  • Continuously assess competitor activity, customer behaviour, market trends and industry developments to recommend timely commercial interventions.
  • Drive a balanced growth agenda across new business, existing customer growth, retention, profitable volume and revenue quality.
  • Partner with the CCO on strategic commercial initiatives, national sales programs and business transformation priorities.

Customer & Key Account Management

  • Build and strengthen senior-level relationships with key customers and decision makers across India.
  • Provide leadership on strategically important customers, large opportunities, renewals and complex commercial engagements.
  • Drive account growth strategies for high-value customers to improve retention, wallet share, product penetration and long-term customer value.
  • Sponsor critical customer relationships and intervene in major escalations where required.
  • Support Regional Sales Heads in significant commercial negotiations in collaboration with Pricing, Finance, Legal and Operations.
  • Ensure customer propositions are commercially sustainable and operationally deliverable.
  • Identify opportunities for cross-selling and upselling Blue Dart products, services and value-added solutions.
  • Strengthen customer engagement to support retention, growth and long-term partnerships.

Financial & Commercial Management

  • Deliver national Field Sales revenue, profitability, yield and growth targets in line with approved business plans.
  • Monitor performance across regions, territories, products and customer segments and initiate corrective action where required.
  • Drive disciplined pricing and commercial governance in partnership with Pricing & Revenue Assurance.
  • Ensure sales growth is achieved with the right balance of volume, yield, customer profitability and revenue quality.
  • Review customer and portfolio profitability and drive interventions for low-yield or structurally unprofitable business.
  • Drive achievement of collection targets and strengthen receivables discipline in partnership with Finance and Regional Sales Heads.
  • Monitor sales productivity, resource utilisation and return on commercial investment.
  • Provide accurate and timely sales forecasts and performance visibility to the CCO.

Sales Operations & Commercial Excellence

  • Lead execution of the national sales plan through Regional Sales Heads and the Pan-India Field Sales organisation.
  • Establish a disciplined sales-management cadence covering pipeline, customer acquisition, conversion, revenue, profitability and productivity.
  • Drive strong CRM adoption and ensure customer and opportunity information is accurate and actionable.
  • Improve forecasting accuracy across regions and strengthen visibility of future revenue opportunities and risks.
  • Monitor regional, branch and territory-level sales performance and ensure timely corrective action.
  • Standardise critical sales processes, commercial governance and customer-management practices across regions.
  • Drive sales productivity through improved territory management, customer prioritisation, analytics and digital tools.
  • Ensure robust business reviews, dashboards and performance reporting to support decision-making.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify sales processes and improve frontline effectiveness.

Cross-functional Collaboration

  • Work with Pricing & Revenue Assurance to strengthen pricing discipline, yield, customer profitability and commercial governance.
  • Collaborate with Finance on collections, customer profitability, forecasting and commercial performance.
