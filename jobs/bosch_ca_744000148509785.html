Company Description

Bosch Charleston is a part of a global network with close to 400,000 associates who make products that are “Invented for Life.”

The Bosch Charleston plant has a long history in the Lowcountry and was established in 1974. We Drive. to provide a safe and inspiring environment for our associates. We make a difference and delight our global customers by creating state of the art products.

Over the past five years we have contributed over 1 million USD to Science, Technology, Engineering and Math (STEM) and environmental organizations in the Lowcountry through the Bosch Community Fund. Additionally, Bosch Charleston works closely with our partners to support these initiatives through volunteerism. There are many opportunities to get involved and we hope you will join us as we make a difference in the Lowcountry.

Job Description

  • Design or management of building and utility design.
  • Review annual thermal imaging of electrical systems.
  • Maintenance of emergency power systems including generators, automatic transfer switches and universal power supplies.
  • Annual maintenance activities of power distribution including transformer DGA, Substation secondary injection testing, lubrication and exercising according to NFPA 69 and FM Global guidelines.
  • Utilities planning to ensure proper supply for new equipment.
  • Maintain electrical spares inventory
  • Maintain breaker panel schedules and update as circuits change
  • Responsible for the value stream mapping and process flow oriented to power distribution network.
  • Develop and maintain facility strategies for equipment, layouts, labor and material flow to meet strategic goals; including utility planning.
  • Manages projects as needed for the Facilities Construction and Maintenance (FCM) Team.
  • Coordinate contractor activities toward completion of tasks focusing on quality, cost, and timely completion.
  • Lead projects to improve cost, quality, delivery and safety of site and support CIP activities.
  • Understands Bosch and Regional safety rules and promotes a safety first environment.
  • Provide oversight of contractors to ensure conformance to Bosch, OSHA, NFPA, ICC, and safety requirements.
  • Perform and/or oversee planning, design, estimating, and execution of complex projects related to: facilities maintenance, renovation and restoration, production equipment installation, relocation, and removal.
  • Develop requests for quotes (RFQ) in coordination with purchasing department.
  • Procure equipment, materials, and construction services by contract or use of in-house resources.
  • Schedule/coordinate project phasing, outages and disruptions.
  • Recommend design modifications when field conditions warrant.
  • Perform continual on-site inspection of construction activities and follow-up with customers throughout project to ensure safety, quality, timeliness, and budget expectations are met.
  • Conduct final project walk-through with customer.
  • Maintain records of construction documents to include budgets, communications, contracts, change orders, drawings/specifications, meetings, invoices, payments, purchase orders, etc.

General FCM Activities

  • Maintain budget control for expenditures (capital and expense) related to Electrical Repairs and Maintenance
  • Participate in development and achievement of departmental goals and objectives, including active participation in facilities emergency response team.
  • Develop and maintain facility strategies for proper building value preservation plan.
  • Oversee projects to improve safety, including working closely with Health, Safety, and Environmental department.
  • Performs Audits and Gemba walks to ensure services are being provided by FCM team as needed
  • Promotes technology and digitalization and looks to improve and optimize processes.
  • Supports Global Real Estate (GR) organization and participates in GR events as needed.

  • Assist Energy Engineer with CO2 reduction and Energy Management Analysis of energy usage and recommendation for cost improvement.
  • Assist Energy Engineer with analysis of energy usage for cost improvement.
  • Effective use of Bosch maintenance best practices.
  • All other tasks as assigned.
