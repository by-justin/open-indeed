Job Title: Quality Inspector - Level 2

Job Summary

This role involves operating CMMs, gear analyzers, and other measurement equipment to conduct in-process inspections and First-off approvals. Ensure all products meet strict company and customer specifications while maintaining accurate inspection records. Success in this fast-paced environment requires strong collaboration with internal departments to uphold and enhance quality standards.

Responsibility

  • Conduct inspections of parts using appropriate gauging or measuring equipment to ensure product aligns with customer standards through various process, including:

  • First off inspections: Inspect part specifications and authorize full production.

  • Receiving Inspections: Assess incoming material based on product specifications to ensure required standards.

  • In process Inspections: Monitor and inspect parts during production process to ensure ongoing compliance with quality standards.

  • Product Audits: Conduct full dimensional layout of part by measuring all part features to ensure compliance with drawing specifications.

  • Final release inspections: Evaluate parts and authorize shipment of parts to the customer.

  • Capability Studies: Use data from samples to predict the ability of process to produce parts conforming to customer specifications.

  • Operate CMM and special purpose gauges (i.e. Gear Analyzer, Vision System Scanner etc.) with basic understanding of programming.

  • Apply IATF standards for handling non-conforming material, including identification and inspection procedures.

  • Use appropriate documentation related to quality inspections.

  • Communicate and report any product deviations found to relevant individuals.

  • Adhere to all Health and safety, environmental legislation, policies and procedures.

  • Provide support in Metallurgy Lab, such as: cutting parts, mounting and conducting basic inspections.

Academic/Educational Requirements

  • High School Diploma or equivalent

  • Quality Assurance – manufacturing and Management certificate or equivalent experience

  • Blueprint Reading Training (Internal/External)

  • GDT Training (Internal/External)

Required Skills/Experience

  • Minimum 2 years industry related work experience. Capable of meeting all Level 1 requirements.

  • Ability to run CMM and special purpose gauges (i.e. Gear Analyzer, Vision System Scanner etc.) equipment with basic understanding of programming.

  • Knowledge and ability to use measuring equipment, such as micrometers, verniers, height and bore gauges.

  • Intermediate knowledge of reading and interpreting blueprint drawings and GD&T symbols.

  • Intermediate knowledge of applying Statistical Process Control (SPC) concepts.

  • Basic understanding of metallurgical principles.

  • Ability to continuously learn new skills and developments in the field.

  • Ability to communicate clearly and effectively in both individual and team settings.

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel and Outlook.

  • Ability to learn new software applications.
