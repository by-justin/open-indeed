Job Profile Summary:

As a Senior LV Secondary & AC Control Automation Engineer, you will be a part of LV Secondary team, reporting directly to the Senior Engineering Manager, and interact with all HVDC disciplines (Tendering, Sourcing, manufacturing, Installation and commissioning) to support design and delivery of LV Secondary scope for all HVDC projects around the world. LV Secondary Engineering for HV/EHV/HVDC system: • System architecture and deliverables definition • Hardware design • Control and Automation • Protection

This role includes developing team talents and competencies, support Engineering process application and improvement, provide technical guidance, mentoring, drive consistent design practices and common global processes for projects implementation and provide technical solutions that meet project delivery and Tender objectives.
