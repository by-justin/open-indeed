Job Description:

Join One of Ayrshire’s largest Engineering Employers

Prestwick Aerosystems is proud to continue Ayrshire’s long-standing aircraft manufacturing tradition. From our Prestwick site, we manufacture aircraft wing structures used on Airbus commercial aircraft worldwide. As part of the Airbus family, we play a key role in delivering safe, reliable and high-quality aerospace products.

The Opportunity:

Accountable for the development and optimisation of manufacturing systems including robotic and numerically controlled programmes to satisfy automation equipment.

How you will contribute to the team: •    You will be responsible for providing manufacturing systems solutions through the application of industrial engineering. •    You will create and amend CNC programs. •    You will support the creation of programming data through liaison with the Design and Manufacturing disciplines. •    You will be responsible for the creation and maintenance of programming simulation models. •    You will be responsible for manipulating 3D data to support manufacturing processes. •    You will provide technical guidance and training to the business to support efficient manufacturing systems programming. •    You will be responsible for the management of tools and cutters to support manufacturing operations.

About you: •    Experience of Manufacturing Systems Programming Techniques •    Conversant in 3D modelling techniques •    Have a good knowledge of the capabilities of a range of different PDM tools •    Knowledge of automated manufacturing systems and equipment •    HND level or equivalent experience in Manufacturing or Technology related discipline – desirable •    Excellent communication and inter-personal skills – interface with customers and suppliers. •    A good working knowledge of Microsoft Packages – Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Access
