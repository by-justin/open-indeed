Job Title:            Associate General Counsel, EMEA

Location:           UK (remote with quarterly travel to the London Office)

Reports To:        General Counsel, e-Storage

P osition Summary:

e-Storage is seeking an experienced lawyer to lead its UK and EMEA legal team.  The Associate General Counsel’s role will oversee the legal and compliance functions for the e-Storage business unit in the UK and EMEA, with a primary focus on project contracts and execution – including EPC contracts, equipment supply contracts, BESS Integrator contracts, long-term service contracts, warranties, and performance guarantees. The ideal candidate will be a confident self-starter with strong interpersonal and organizational skills and a demonstrated ability to effectively provide sound legal advice, exercise good business judgment, and work with senior management, outside counsel and customers.

Main responsibilities

Legal Leadership & Strategy

  • Act as trusted advisor to senior management on all legal, regulatory, and strategic matters across the UK and EMEA region.
  • Lead the regional Legal Department, managing and mentoring a high-performing team of commercial & transactional lawyers (based in Germany and the UK).
  • Provide proactive, business-focused legal advice across a range of commercial, regulatory, and corporate areas.
  • Collaborate closely with global legal, compliance, and operational teams to align legal strategy with corporate goals.

Commercial Legal Support

  • Lead negotiations and draft complex, high-value commercial contracts EPC agreements, equipment supply agreements, subcontractor agreements, long-term services agreements, operation and maintenance agreements, product warranties, performance guarantees and various other contracts.
  • Assist with all aspects of project management and project execution, including drafting legal notices, changing orders, interpreting contract language and providing strategic and business-oriented advice.
  • Litigation and dispute resolution management.
  • Oversee legal risk exposure and mitigation strategies in relation to product liability, IP, transportation, tariffs/duties, and cross-border sales.

Regulatory & Compliance Oversight

  • Own the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of the EMEA compliance framework, including training, reporting, and risk controls.
  • Provide legal support for compliance with EU-level and national regulations applicable to the e-Storage business unit, including the EU Batteries Regulation, WEEE, supply chain due diligence, anti-forced labour rules, and human rights standards.
  • Manage anti-bribery and corruption controls, KYC/AML due diligence, sanctions screening, and regulatory investigations.

Governance & External Counsel Management

  • Develop and implement regional legal policies, contract templates, and internal governance protocols in coordination with global e-Storage legal team.
  • Manage relationships with external counsel to ensure quality advice and cost-efficiency.
  • Act as company secretary or legal representative on governance matters where required by law or corporate structure.

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Solicitor qualified in England & Wales is a must.
  • 10+ years of post-qualification experience, gained at a top-tier law firm and/or in-house legal department within a multinational business.
  • Proven track record advising at executive level on complex legal, contractual, and compliance issues across multiple jurisdictions.
  • Experience and willingness to lead negotiations of complex commercial contracts.
  • Deep knowledge of commercial law, international trade, and regulatory frameworks in the EU and UK.
  • Experience in the renewables, infrastructure, or energy sector highly advantageous.
  • Demonstrated leadership experience in building and managing legal teams.
  • Fluent in English; Spanish or German fluency strongly preferred; other European languages a plus.
  • Willingness to travel within UK and EMEA as needed.
