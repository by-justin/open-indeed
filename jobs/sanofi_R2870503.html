Job Purpose

  • Manage efficient and compliant Order to Cash (O2C/COMET) processes and customer-centric activities
  • Responsibilities include empowering teams to optimize daily operations such as Customer Fulfillment Support (S&OE process); SC Customer Engagement, Order to Serve processing  contact center management, order, and claim reconciliation
  • Additionally, managing relationships with reverse O2C and logistics internal & external partners, ensuring proper customer crediting, and compliance with internal and SOX controls are key tasks
  • The focus is on optimizing stock processes, streamlining the S&OE process, and enhancing SC Customer Engagement to improve overall customer experience in the fulfilment process

Key Accountabilities

  • Order to Cash Process Activities

  • Managing E2E order to serve processing COMET to ensure timely fulfilment and process adherence

  • Empowering Customer Fulfilment teams to conduct day-to-day operations efficiently and effectively

  • Implementing lean principles to streamline order fulfilment processes and reduce lead times for OTIF improvement

  • Optimizing stock processes to minimize stockouts and excess inventory

  • Streamlining the Sales and Operations Execution (S&OE) process to enhance D2C and forecasting planning

  • Ensuring proper customer crediting in compliance with internal policies and SOX controls

  • Conducting regular audits of logistics operations to identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement

  • Developing contingency plans to mitigate risks such as supply chain disruptions or transportation delays

  • Analysing sell-in and sell-out data to understand market demand and adjust production and distribution accordingly.

  • Monitoring stock in trade levels to ensure optimal inventory management and minimize carrying costs

  • Analysing SISO data to identify sales trends, market opportunities, and potential risks.

  • Continuously evaluating and adjusting SIT and SiSO strategies to meet changing market conditions and customer demands.

  • Defines backorders policy and prioritization decisions with trade

  • Simplification

  • Drive development & execution of simplification plans

  • Ensure simplification benefits are realizable & tracked

  • Collaboration

  • Engaging with key stakeholders to ensure alignment and collaboration across the organization.

  • Contributing to trade and pricing initiatives to optimize root-to-market strategies and distribution costs

  • Analysing customer feedback and order data to identify opportunities for process improvement and enhance customer satisfaction

  • Process Effectiveness & Analytics

  • Monitor the E2E fulfilment process running a defined set of reports and analysis to detect issues/inefficiencies and drive continuous improvements

  • Implementing performance metrics and KPIs to track the effectiveness of customer fulfilment and logistics operations and drive continuous improvement efforts

  • Communicate root cause analysis of issues found

  • Executes additional process analysis when requested

  • Support local team with reporting & KPIs

  • Prepare/extract KPIs for fulfilment processes

  • Support month end closing to ensure all steps defined in the local policies are properly executed

  • SISO & SIT consolidation & analysis

  • Manage VMI customer analytics & replenishment

Leadership and team management

Outsourced Activity Governance
