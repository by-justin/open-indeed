Job Description:

Summary:

As the single-point leader for hospital access in the assigned region, RHAL will translate IPSEN’s national hospital affairs strategy into executable regional plans that secure product listing and safe operation in Tier-1/2 hospitals and key networks. You will orchestrate high-impact customer engagements and coach a high-performing team to deliver NRDL, NEDL and VBP landing results while ensuring full compliance and patient-centric access.

Main responsibility:

Regional Strategy & Target Delivery

  • Cascade the national hospital access strategy into quarterly regional OKRs (listing rate, safe-operation score, speed-to-patient).
  • Allocate budget, headcount and project resources across provinces to maximize ROI.
  • Track leading indicators (hospital readiness, formulary timeline, compliance index) and trigger counter-measures early.

Customer & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Map the decision-making chain of target hospitals and aligned with mastery growth plan; maintain live heat-maps of access risk/opportunity.
  • Lead face-to-face negotiations with hospital presidents, pharmacy chiefs and procurement heads to secure formulary addition and safe-use protocols.
  • Orchestrate regional advisory boards, multi-disciplinary meetings and patient-flow workshops to strengthen IPSEN scientific branding.

Team Leadership & Development

  • Coach Hospital Affairs Managers on stakeholder segmentation, value storytelling and compliant influencing skills.
  • Run monthly field-ride days and quarterly capability clinics; build succession bench strength through 70-20-10 learning plans.
  • Own performance management cycle: set SMART goals, give real-time feedback, calibrate ratings with NHAD.

Cross-Functional Integration

  • Partner with regional Commercial, Medical, BEX and Finance to create one integrated customer plan per flagship account.
  • Escalate policy roadblocks early and co-design solutions with Government Affairs & HEOR.
  • Share best-practices horizontally across regions via the IPSEN Access Community of Practice.
