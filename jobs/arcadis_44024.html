Role Description

This role is part of Arcadis’ Global Excellence Centre (GEC) and will join our 3,000+ talented individuals working in India, Romania, and Philippines, in partnership with Arcadis’ offices globally. The GEC brings key capabilities and capacity to support our clients seamlessly around the world.  For those in GEC there are options to be dedicated to one country; developing deep knowledge and strong relationships, as well as opportunities to be part of teams working truly globally as a centre of excellence, offering diversity and variety.  Arcadians are by nature, globally minded and culturally aware, and Arcadis strongly believes in flexibility which enables change over time as your career progresses. The GEC model is well embedded in Arcadis, and the strong relationships that exist between our global offices are truly unique.

Role accountabilities:

  • Carry out a range of activities to support the development and implementation of engineering solutions.

  • Typically assigned to provide a substantial technical role for a single discipline on smaller, less complex tasks or elements of more extensive, less complicated tasks within a project.

  • Stakeholder management and ensuring the design is based on project standards and code requirements.

  • Coordinate with Client, Region and other GECs activities required to produce engineering deliverables.

  • Can be assigned to a Task Management role.

  • Conduct training and guidance to junior team members.

  • Develop industry knowledge of international design standards through internal technical training, certifications and continuing education.

  • Lead a team on a project.

  • Automate design process and workflows.

  • Ensure correctness and quality of project deliverables.

Knowledge and Experience:

  • Experienced in using management system requirements to support the delivery of design and engineering projects.

  • Able to actively participate in safe design processes which consider safety during construction, operation and decommissioning through the use of hazard identification and risk assessment during design development.

  • Able to input into discussions around identifying, reviewing and selecting techniques, procedures and methods to undertake engineering tasks.

  • Possess excellent time management skills consistently demonstrated through performance on projects.

  • Experience on large scale infrastructure metro projects (above and below ground station design) is an advantage.

Qualifications & Experience:

  • Possess a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering degree.

  • A Masters degree is an advantage.

  • Possess a license in Civil Engineering preferred

  • Working towards Charterships with International Institutes in regions such as the UK, US, Canada or Australia is an advantage.

  • Have a minimum of 4 to 6 years of professional experience in structural engineering.

  • Have experience working in a design consultancy firm.

  • Proficient in using engineering software like: SAP2000, ETABS, SAFE, RISA, Robot, TEDDS, Tekla

  • Structures, or SCIA.

  • Knowledge in Revit and Navisworks is an advantage.

  • Proficient in the application of international design standards: US, Canada, UK&I, European and Australian experience is an advantage.

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in the English language.

  • Programming knowledge using C++, Python, Dynamo is an advantage.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
