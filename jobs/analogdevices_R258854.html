About Analog Devices: Analog Devices International is a global leader in high-performance analogue, mixed signal, and digital signal processing (DSP) integrated circuits. At our Limerick site, we manufacture integrated circuits used in everything from industrial systems to consumer electronics.

Why Join Us? This internship offers hands-on experience in a Class 10 cleanroom wafer fabrication facility , working with advanced semiconductor manufacturing tools. You’ll learn from experienced technicians and engineers while contributing to real projects that impact global technology.

What You’ll Do:

  • Assist with planned and unplanned tool maintenance and repair.
  • Support troubleshooting and root cause analysis under supervision.
  • Help with tool development and improvement projects.
  • Work alongside mentors on practical investigations and solutions.
  • Prepare and present findings and recommendations.
  • Learn to interpret technical manuals, schematics, and specifications.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Preventive maintenance and standard work procedures.
  • Analytical troubleshooting techniques (Root Cause Analysis, 5-Why).
  • Exposure to advanced toolsets such as Applied Materials Implanters, Nikon Steppers, LAM Etchers, and more.
  • Technologies including electronics, high vacuum systems, pneumatics, and robotics.

Ideal Candidate:

  • Currently pursuing a technical qualification or degree in Engineering, Electronics, or related field (NFQ Level 6–8).
  • Interest in semiconductor manufacturing and equipment maintenance.
  • Strong communication skills and willingness to learn in a team environment.
  • Ability to adapt to a dynamic, fast-paced setting.
