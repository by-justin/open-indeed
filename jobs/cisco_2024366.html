Are You Our New Software Engineer Intern?

Meet the Team

Cisco Norway is a global leader in developing video conferencing technology - helping millions of people connect and collaborate worldwide. At our development center outside Oslo, you’ll join 300+ engineers working with hardware and software - from idea to design, mechanics, audio, media processing and artificial intelligence. Our solutions are trusted by businesses, universities, governments, and organizations around the world.

Spend your summer with a hard-working, collaborative group that thrives on innovation, creativity, and a bit of fun - think team sports, social activities, and free ice cream on sunny days. In our inclusive and supportive culture, you’ll have the chance to grow, make an impact, and enjoy a healthy work-life balance - all while making great memories along the way.

Your Impact

During a summer internship, you will get the opportunity to explore a variety of technologies while working collaboratively with an existing team. We prioritize ensuring our summer interns feel included and contribute to real-life projects that are relevant and useful to Cisco.

The specific project you will work on will be determined based on your field of interest and our current needs. You will receive a mentor to guide you along the way and answer your questions, making this an exciting opportunity to grow your career. If you have a great idea, you might even get the opportunity to pursue this during your internship.

The following examples are projects that previous interns have been working on:

  • office room analytics application based on live and historical sensor data from our devices
  • noise reduction with deep learning
  • synchronous camera focusing for immersive systems
  • showing personal calendars in meeting rooms based on face recognition
  • using Falco on Kubernetes for threat detection
  • implement and accelerate our Head Detection model in a FPGA camera
  • depth estimation of meeting rooms using deep learning

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Informatics, Electrical Engineering, or a closely related subject.
  • Ability to live and work in Norway for the duration of the summer internship (no visa or relocation support is provided for this position)
  • Upon applying, please upload your  cover letter, CV, and university grade transcript  as part of your application. *****

Preferred Qualifications:

Your dedication, curiosity, and willingness to learn are just as important as your track-record. We are looking for people who want to become a guide in their field through working with us. To succeed, you also need to be a team player. We need to combine our brains, competences, and expertise to deliver top-of-the-shelf products.

In addition, do you have:

  • practical engineering experience, for example within programming, testing, signal processing, network protocols or cloud technology?
  • a genuine interest in technology and continuous learning?

If so, we’d love to hear from you!
