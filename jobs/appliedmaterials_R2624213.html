We are seeking a highly skilled Physical Security Technologist with hands-on expertise in Avigilon physical security solutions to support the design, deployment, administration, and optimization of enterprise security systems. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining and enhancing video surveillance, access control, and security monitoring platforms while collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure a secure and resilient environment.

Key Responsibilities

Video Surveillance Systems

  • Administer, configure, and support Avigilon Control Center (ACC) video management systems.
  • Install, maintain, and troubleshoot Avigilon cameras, NVRs, encoders, and related infrastructure.
  • Configure video analytics, appearance search, facial recognition (where applicable), and event monitoring.
  • Optimize camera placement, image quality, retention policies, and storage solutions.

Access Control & Security Integration

  • Support and maintain Avigilon Alta Access or Avigilon ACM systems.
  • Configure access control hardware including card readers, controllers, door contacts, and locking devices.
  • Manage access permissions, credential management, and security policies.
  • Integrate access control with video surveillance and alarm systems.

Security Operations

  • Monitor system health and performance across enterprise security platforms.
  • Respond to security incidents, system outages, and hardware failures.
  • Conduct root cause analysis and implement corrective actions.
  • Support investigations through video retrieval, exporting, and evidence preservation.

Infrastructure & Technology Support

  • Collaborate with IT teams on network connectivity, VLANs, bandwidth planning, and cybersecurity requirements.
  • Configure and troubleshoot IP-based security devices and PoE infrastructure.
  • Support system upgrades, firmware updates, and vulnerability remediation.
  • Ensure compliance with organizational security standards and best practices.

Project Management

  • Participate in new security technology deployments and facility expansion projects.
  • Develop technical documentation, as-built drawings, SOPs, and maintenance procedures.
  • Coordinate with vendors, contractors, and stakeholders during implementation activities.
  • Conduct system acceptance testing and commissioning.

  • Strong knowledge of:IP networkingVideo surveillance systemsAccess control technologiesSecurity system integrations
