We’re looking for a Sr. Product Marketing Specialist, EMEA with a foundation in core Product marketing (PMM) disciplines to drive regional go-to-market execution, product launches, positioning, and sales enablement across Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEA). This role is perfect for a strategic marketer with a passion for storytelling, market insights, and equipping sales teams with impactful content.

Based in London, you will collaborate closely with global and regional stakeholders to drive localised GTM impact by:

  • Translating Global Strategy to Local Reality: Adapting global product positioning, value frameworks, and launch plans to align with regional buying behaviors and local industry dynamics.

  • Bridging Global & Regional Teams: Serving as the strategic liaison between global and regional teams, ensuring regional market feedback directly shapes product roadmaps and global messaging.

  • Tailoring High-Impact Collateral: Refining customer-facing narratives, campaign assets, and enablement tools to drive pipeline velocity across EMEA.

What You’ll Do

Regional Go-to-Market Execution

  • Support product launches and GTM strategies across EMEA, ensuring alignment with regional needs.

  • Localise global marketing campaigns for key and emerging EMEA markets.

  • Optimise product messaging and positioning for diverse audiences.

Sales Enablement

  • Adapt global sales content for EMEA, ensuring relevance and effectiveness.

  • Equip sales and partners with the right messaging, competitive insights, and use cases.

  • Gather regional feedback to refine positioning and enablement strategies.

Content & Messaging

  • Tailor product messaging to resonate with EMEA customers while maintaining global consistency.

  • Work with content teams to adapt marketing assets (case studies, blogs, web pages) for the region.

Market Insights & Competitive Intelligence

  • Conduct research on customer trends, industry shifts, and regulatory considerations in EMEA.

  • Analyse competitors and identify opportunities for differentiation.

  • Share insights with global and regional teams to inform strategy.

What You Bring

  • 5+ years hands-on experience in Product Marketing, B2B SaaS marketing, or a related field. Familiarity with Identity & Access Management (IAM), cybersecurity, or cloud security solutions.

  • Proven PMM Toolkit: Demonstrated track record of owning product messaging frameworks, tier-based launch plans, buyer persona creation, and competitive battlecards from end to end.

  • Communication Excellence: Strong written and verbal storytelling skills, with experience of clear, concise product messaging for complex technical products. Written and verbal communication required in English (business proficiency)

  • **Market Knowledge:**Experience launching solutions to the EMEA region. Solid understanding of enterprise buying behaviors, regulatory landscapes, and business cultures across key EMEA sub-regions.Strategic & Action-Oriented: Ability to utilise data-driven market research and performance analytics to adapt global messaging, sales enablement, and campaign assets into relevant content tailored to regional EMEA market needs.

  • **Education:**Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).

  • **Working environment:**Collaborative, self-motivated, and comfortable working in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.
