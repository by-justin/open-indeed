26WD100656, Software Development Manager

Position Overview

Autodesk is looking for an experienced Software Development Manager to lead a high-performing engineering team within the Growth Experience Technology (GET) organization. The team builds and operates the post-purchase account experience — the customer-facing platforms that power account management, subscription management, personalization, and experimentation for customers after they've purchased. This includes the Account Portal, voluntary churn and retention flows, and the personalization/experimentation infrastructure that underpins them.

In this role, you will lead, hire, nurture, and grow a team of talented developers while staying close to technology. You will provide technical direction, review designs, guide delivery, develop proof of concepts when needed, and collaborate closely with Architects, Product Managers, Program Managers, and cross-functional engineering teams. We are looking for a hands-on, technology-forward engineering leader who embraces AI-assisted development, modern engineering practices, and technology disruption. The ideal candidate is creative, innovative, curious, and motivated to go above and beyond to deliver meaningful customer and business impact.

Responsibilities

  • Lead, mentor, hire, nurture, and develop a team of high-performing software developers
  • Build an inclusive, collaborative, ownership-driven engineering culture focused on accountability and continuous learning
  • Manage performance, career development, coaching, onboarding, and team growth
  • Create clarity around team priorities, roles, responsibilities, expectations, and success measures
  • Drive delivery planning and execution across quarterly and multi-quarter initiatives, with a durable multi-quarter roadmap and LOE-backed solution plans rather than sprint-to-sprint re-scoping
  • Collaborate with Product, Program, Architecture, Design, and other engineering teams to translate business goals into technical execution plans
  • Manage dependencies, risks, priorities, and trade-offs while communicating progress clearly to stakeholders
  • Remove technical, organizational, and process blockers to help teams deliver high-quality software predictably
  • Provide technical guidance for applications and services built using JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, databases, AWS, and serverless technologies
  • Own the account portal's migration and evolution on Experience Builder (from legacy "experience service" architecture)
  • Guide the team's experimentation platform work — feature flagging and A/B testing via LaunchDarkly, identity/session handling via Oxygen ID and 3-legged OAuth token flows
  • Guide personalization capability for post-purchase account experiences — ML-driven and rules-driven surfaces (e.g., tailored account/learn content, subscription recommendations)
  • Provide technical direction for conversational-AI-powered account flows (e.g., AI-assisted cancellation/retention experiences), including safe experiment ramps, event instrumentation, and cross-system fallback handling (support/sales handoff)
  • Participate in architecture discussions, design reviews, code reviews, and technical decision-making
  • Partner with architects and senior developers to ensure scalable, maintainable, reliable, and secure system design
  • Promote strong engineering practices including automated testing, CI/CD, secure coding, observability, operational excellence, and production readiness
  • Support and improve build, deployment, and release processes using Jenkins and modern CI/CD pipelines
  • Develop or guide proof of concepts to validate new ideas, technologies, and architectural approaches
  • Champion responsible adoption of AI-assisted engineering practices, coding assistants, automation tools, and LLM-based development workflows
  • Encourage practical use of tools such as Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and similar AI-assisted coding platforms
  • Identify opportunities to use AI to improve engineering velocity, code quality, testing, documentation, debugging, and operational efficiency — including applying it to customer-facing personalization and experimentation, not just internal dev workflows
  • Foster creative and innovative thinking, encouraging the team to go beyond basic execution and continuously improve

Aperçu du Poste

Autodesk recherche un responsable du développement logiciel expérimenté pour diriger une équipe d'ingénieurs hautement performante au sein de l'organisation Growth Experience Technology (GET). L'équipe développe et gère l'expérience client post-achat, c'est-à-dire les plateformes destinées aux clients qui permettent la gestion des comptes, la gestion des abonnements, la personnalisation et l'expérimentation pour les clients après leur achat.

Cela inclut le portail client, les flux liés au désabonnement volontaire et à la fidélisation, ainsi que l’infrastructure de personnalisation et d’expérimentation qui les sous-tend.

À ce poste, vous dirigerez, recruterez, encadrerez et développerez une équipe de développeurs talentueux tout en restant au plus près de la technologie. Vous fournirez une orientation technique, examinerez les conceptions, guiderez la mise en œuvre, développerez des preuves de concept si nécessaire et collaborerez étroitement avec les architectes, les chefs de produit, les responsables de programme et les équipes d’ingénierie interfonctionnelles. Nous recherchons un responsable d’ingénierie pragmatique et à la pointe de la technologie, qui adhère au développement assisté par l’IA, aux pratiques d’ingénierie modernes et aux innovations technologiques disruptives. Le candidat idéal est créatif, innovant, curieux et motivé à se dépasser pour générer un impact significatif tant pour les clients que pour l’entreprise.

  • Gérer les performances, le développement de carrière, l’accompagnement, l’intégration et la croissance de l’équipe

  • Piloter la planification et l’exécution des livraisons pour les initiatives trimestrielles et pluritrimestrielles, avec une feuille de route pluritrimestrielle pérenne et des plans de solution étayés par une analyse des exigences (LOE), plutôt que de redéfinir la portée à chaque sprint
  • Collaborer avec les équipes Produit, Programme, Architecture, Conception et autres équipes d’ingénierie pour traduire les objectifs métier en plans d’exécution techniques
