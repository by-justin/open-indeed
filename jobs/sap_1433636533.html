SAP NS2 Vice President, SaaS Platform & Operations

Job Description:

Operate and continuously evolve SAP NS2’s enterprise SaaS operating model. Success is measured by accelerating secure software delivery, improving operational resilience and customer experience, increasing productivity through platform capabilities and automation, and building a scalable operating model for SaaS growth. Proximity to an NS2 office (Herndon, VA) is strongly preferred.

Required Qualifications
