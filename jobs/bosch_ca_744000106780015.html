JMP_Purchasing Management(M)

Company: Bosch Canada Location: Suzhou, Jiangsu, China URL: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/BoschGroup/744000106780015-jmp-purchasing-management-m-

Company Description

Do you want beneficial technologies being shaped by your ideas? Whether in the areas of mobility solutions, consumer goods, industrial technology or energy and building technology - with us, you will have the chance to improve quality of life all across the globe. Welcome to Bosch.

Job Description

Responsibilities:

  1. Commercial purchasing (mechanical or electronics or software).

  2. Project management purchasing (mechanical or electronics).

  3. Overseas assignment in commercial or project management (electronics, mechanics can be mixed).

  4. Liaison with external purchasing stations (LOG, controlling, plant, sales, JV's).

Potential rotation opportunity: In various areas (incl. commercial buyer, project buyer, purchasing risk management, purchasing strategy management).

岗位职责：

1.负责商务采购工作（涵盖机械、电子或软件类产品）。

2.负责项目采购管理工作（机械或电子类相关项目）。

3.承担海外派驻相关的商务或项目管理工作（可兼顾电子、机械领域）。

4.对接外部采购站点（包括物流、内控、工厂、销售及合资公司等相关站点）。

潜在轮岗机会：可轮岗至多个领域，包括商务采购岗、项目采购岗、采购风险管理岗及采购战略管理岗。

Qualifications

Qualifications:

  1. Master’s degree or above, major in commercial management (e.g. supply chain management) or engineering background with strong business sense.

  2. At least 1–3 years of working experience with good achievement in related industries/functions and positions.

  3. Demonstrates high leadership potential with strategic thinking and good overview, ambitious to lead the team, and passion for innovations.

  4. Good communication & storytelling and presentation skills, fast learner with open-minded, flexible in adapting to different working environment and topics.

任职要求：

1.硕士及以上学历，工商管理类专业（如供应链管理），或具备工科专业背景且拥有较强的商业思维。

2.拥有1-3 年及以上相关行业 / 职能 / 岗位工作经验，且在工作中取得良好业绩。

3.具备出色的领导潜力，拥有战略思维与全局视野，具备团队领导志向，且对创新工作抱有热忱。

4.具备优秀的沟通表达、方案叙事及演示汇报能力，学习能力强、思维开放，能够灵活适应不同的工作环境与业务主题。
