Job Description

Job Position: Integrated Warehouse Specialist (IWS)

Reports To: Warehouse Team Lead

Location : YYZ - Mississauga

Company Profile

DHL Global Forwarding is a Freight Forwarding organization that is the expert in global air, ocean and ground Freight Forwarding as well as Customs operations and Compliance. Our passion is to enable international trade utilizing the best teams in the industry. We simplify our customer operations and maximize their opportunities to achieve results.

As a Warehouse Specialist at DHL Global Forwarding, you will be responsible for controlling the movement of freight in and out of the warehouse, ensuring proper distribution within the facility. Your role will involve operating forklifts, maintaining records of freight movements, and tracking shipments for the IWS Department. You will handle the pickup and delivery of IWS cargo while adhering to company policies and procedures and ensuring safe and efficient operation of equipment.

Main tasks & Key Responsibilities:

  • Task Execution: Follow instructions from the Warehouse Team Lead to prioritize and complete daily tasks efficiently.
  • Cargo Inspection: Inspect and handle all cargo, performing test inspections for piece count, weight, dimensions, and physical condition.
  • Documentation: Issue documents verifying test inspections to the IWS Department
  • Non-Conforming Cargo: Identify and segregate non-conforming and dangerous goods cargo, advising the applicable department and Team Leader of such items.
  • Packaging: Package cargo safely and securely for transport within and outside the warehouse facility.
  • Labeling: Label cargo in accordance with the requirements of the relevant departments.
  • Staging: Transfer cargo to the staging area for dispatch according to generated manifests.
  • Loading and Unloading: Load and unload trucks, trailers, ocean containers, and airline pallets, ensuring all trucks are chocked and in gear during the process.
  • Safety Compliance: Maintain clean working areas and adhere to company safety codes and regulations.
  • Storage: Safely and efficiently store cargo within warehouse racks and designated bulk areas.

Minimum Requirements (Education, experience and skills):

Skills

  • Knowledge of shipping and receiving documentation processes.
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks.
  • Team player with a dependable work ethic.
  • Flexibility to work rotating shifts as required.

Education: minimum secondary diploma. College or trade accreditation preferred

Experience: Minimum of 3 years driving a forklift.
