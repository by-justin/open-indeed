Software Engineer

Role Summary

As a global leader in 3D design, engineering, and entertainment software, Autodesk helps people imagine, design, and make a better world. Autodesk accelerates better design through an unparalleled depth of experience and a broad portfolio of software, giving customers the power to solve their design, business, and environmental challenges.

This is an exciting time to join us on our multi-year journey to disrupt the Design to Manufacture world with  Fusion  by delivering unprecedented value and converging workflows with cloud-based technology.

We are looking for a  Software Engineer  to join our  Birmingham -based scrum team  within the Fusion organisation in the  Design and Manufacturing Organisation , part of the  Product Design and Manufacturing Solutions  division. In this role, you will help build  Autodesk Fusion , a CAD/CAM desktop application, delivering production-quality features, using  AI-assisted development  as part of everyday engineering work, and helping solve real customer problems.

Responsibilities

AI-assisted development

  • Use AI-assisted tools (e.g., Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude) for planning, implementation, refactoring, debugging, test generation, and documentation, always validating output for correctness, security, performance, and maintainability.
  • Apply AI effectively in troubleshooting and code review; know when AI accelerates work and when deeper manual analysis is required.
  • Share effective AI-assisted workflows with teammates and help raise team standards for responsible use.

Object-oriented software engineering

  • Design and implement scalable, maintainable features using  object-oriented C++ : clear abstractions, encapsulation, inheritance, and sound use of design patterns.
  • Write, test, review, and maintain high-quality code across the full software development lifecycle.
  • Take ownership of features or components from design and implementation through deployment and support.
  • Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve software defects, performance bottlenecks, and production issues.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, including design specifications and implementation details.

CAD domain

  • Develop and optimise production-grade capabilities across  CAD  (parametric modelling, geometry, design workflows).
  • Apply  computational geometry  and geometric modelling principles to robust, performance-sensitive desktop software.
  • Collaborate with Product Management, Experience Design, and Quality Assurance to deliver customer-facing Fusion capabilities.

Collaboration & delivery

  • Work effectively within distributed global teams; participate actively in Agile ceremonies (sprint planning, estimation, stand-ups, retrospectives).
  • Expand technical breadth across adjacent domains and technologies while deepening expertise in core team areas.
  • Contribute to a culture of collaboration, code quality, and continuous learning.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • 2 to 4 years  of professional software development experience.
  • Proficiency using AI-assisted development tools  (e.g., Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude) as part of daily development, with disciplined review and validation of generated code.
  • Strong object-oriented programming  in  C++ , including modern C++ practices and proficiency with the  C++ Standard Library (STL) .
  • Solid software engineering fundamentals:  data structures, algorithms , object-oriented design, and design patterns.
  • Strong programming, debugging, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Working knowledge of CAD/CAM concepts , computational geometry, or geometric modelling, or equivalent experience building geometry-heavy or mathematically intensive software.
  • Experience with source control and project management tools such as  Git  and  Jira .
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience developing  CAD and/or CAM desktop applications  (Fusion or similar products).
  • Hands-on experience with  computational geometry , geometric modelling, or computer graphics.
  • Cross-platform  application development (Windows, macOS, Linux).
  • Experience designing and developing  APIs and SDKs .
  • Familiarity with  Qt ,  CMake , and  Python .
