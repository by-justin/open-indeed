Position Title: Senior SAP S/4HANA PCE Operations & Integration Lead Department: IT Applications Location: Kitchener, Ontario or Dallas, TX

This position requires regular onsite/in-office attendance.

Position Summary Sunshine Group is seeking an experienced SAP S/4HANA PCE Operations & Integration Lead to support the production operation, governance, and continuous improvement of the group SAP S/4HANA Private Cloud Edition landscape.

This is not a traditional infrastructure-heavy SAP Basis role. The SAP environment is hosted and managed through SAP S/4HANA PCE, so the successful candidate will focus on production monitoring, incident triage, transport and release coordination, SAP service-provider coordination, integration monitoring, and operational governance.

The ideal candidate has strong SAP Basis knowledge, practical experience supporting SAP production environments, hands-on exposure to SAP Transport Management, strong troubleshooting skills, and experience with SAP CPI / SAP Cloud Integration / SAP Integration Suite. SAP BTP experience is highly preferred, as Sunshine Group plans to expand and upgrade BTP capabilities over time.

Duties & Responsibilities

SAP S/4HANA PCE Production Operations & Governance

  • Support day-to-day production operations for the SAP S/4HANA Private Cloud Edition landscape serving Sunshine Group.
  • Monitor system availability, performance, capacity indicators, jobs, interfaces, RFCs, queues, dumps, spool, update processing, and overall system health.
  • Perform first-level SAP technical triage and coordinate escalation to SAP PCE, managed service providers, infrastructure teams, functional teams, and business stakeholders as needed.
  • Review and coordinate SAP maintenance windows, planned downtime, patching schedules, upgrades, service requests, incident tickets, and root-cause-analysis reports.
  • Maintain production support procedures, escalation paths, monitoring checklists, operational

Production Incident Management & Troubleshooting

  • Troubleshoot SAP production issues using strong Basis knowledge and standard SAP monitoring tools and transactions.
  • Analyze common SAP technical issues including dumps, failed jobs, RFC/connectivity failures, interface errors, queues, locks, update failures, spool issues, certificate issues, and user access problems.
  • Coordinate cross-functional incident response across SAP, integration, functional, infrastructure, cloud operations, and business teams.
  • Drive timely issue resolution, business communication, post-incident review, and preventive action tracking.
  • Support production readiness, cutover, go-live, and hyper-care activities for new SAP capabilities and business rollouts.

Transport, Release & Change Management

  • Manage and coordinate SAP Transport Management System activities across Development, QA, and Production environments.
  • Validate transport sequencing, dependencies, approvals, release timing, testing status, and production change-control requirements.
  • Coordinate normal releases, emergency transports, production fixes, and post-release validation with functional, development, integration, and business teams.
  • Support release calendars, change advisory reviews, blackout periods, backout planning, and audit evidence collection for production changes.
  • Ensure transport and release processes are well documented, controlled, repeatable, and aligned with IT governance expectations.

Migration, Refresh, Upgrade & Hypercare Support

•    Support SAP migration, upgrade, system refresh, cutover, and post-go-live stabilization activities from a customer-side technical operations perspective.

  • Coordinate with SAP PCE / managed service providers for system refreshes, client copies, environment readiness, backup validation, disaster recovery testing, and technical maintenance activities.
  • Perform customer-side validation after refreshes, upgrades, maintenance windows, and production changes.
  • Help ensure system availability, business continuity, and operational readiness during major SAP lifecycle events.

SAP CPI / Cloud Integration / Integration Suite Operations

  • Support SAP integration operations using SAP CPI / SAP Cloud Integration / SAP Integration Suite as the integration gateway for SAP and external systems.
  • Monitor integration flows, message failures, queues, connectivity issues, certificates, authentication, destinations, and interface exceptions.
  • Coordinate troubleshooting across SAP S/4HANA, CPI / Integration Suite, external applications, functional teams, vendors, and business users.
  • Support integration testing, migration, cutover, post-go-live stabilization, and interface monitoring procedures.
  • Maintain integration support documentation, escalation paths, and operational runbooks for critical interfaces.

SAP BTP Readiness & Future Platform Support

  • Support the future SAP BTP roadmap, including BTP upgrade planning, operational readiness, integration capabilities, and platform governance.
  • Work with internal teams and external partners on SAP BTP subaccounts, connectivity, security roles, Cloud Connector, destinations, certificates, and integration monitoring as needed.
  • Help define support procedures and governance models for BTP-based services as Sunshine Group expands its SAP cloud platform capabilities.

SAP Security, User Administration & Compliance Support

  • Support SAP user administration, role assignments, authorization troubleshooting, and access lifecycle activities.
  • Use working knowledge of PFCG roles, authorization objects, and authorization trace tools to analyze access issues.
  • Support periodic user access reviews, audit evidence collection, security compliance activities, and basic Segregation of Duties concepts.
  • Collaborate with business process owners, functional teams, and security teams to maintain controlled and auditable access practices.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field; equivalent experience will also be considered.
  • 5+ years of SAP Basis, SAP technical operations, or SAP production support experience.
  • Experience supporting SAP S/4HANA production environments.
  • Experience working with SAP S/4HANA Private Cloud Edition, RISE with SAP, SAP managed cloud, or other hosted SAP environments.
  • Strong SAP Basis knowledge, including system monitoring, troubleshooting, background jobs, dumps, RFCs, spool, queues, update processing, user administration, and interface monitoring.
  • Strong experience with SAP Transport Management System, including Dev / QA / Production transport flow, release coordination, dependency management, emergency transports, and production change control.
  • Experience coordinating with SAP, managed service providers, infrastructure teams, functional teams, developers, integration teams, and business users during production incidents and planned changes.
  • Experience supporting SAP migrations, upgrades, system refreshes, cutover, and hyper-care activities.
  • Experience with SAP CPI / SAP Cloud Integration / SAP Integration Suite, including interface monitoring and troubleshooting.
  • Working knowledge of SAP security roles, PFCG, authorization troubleshooting, user provisioning, and audit support.
  • Strong documentation, communication, stakeholder-management, incident-management, and root-cause-analysis skills.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • SAP BTP experience, especially SAP Integration Suite, Cloud Integration, Cloud Connector, subaccounts, destinations, certificates, connectivity, and security roles.
  • Experience with SAP Cloud ALM, SAP Solution Manager, or similar SAP monitoring and application lifecycle management tools.
  • Experience with SAP Fiori administration, SAP Gateway, and Fiori Launchpad configuration.
  • Experience supporting manufacturing, supply chain, finance, sales, or power electronics business processes in SAP.
  • Experience with ITIL-based incident, problem, change, and release-management processes.
  • Basic scripting or automation knowledge using PowerShell, Bash, Python, or similar tools is a plus.
  • SAP certification in S/4HANA, BTP, Integration Suite, or Basis-related areas is a plus.
