The Controllership team is seeking a motivated finance technology professional to support digital transformation initiatives and deliver innovative solutions that enhance operational efficiency, compliance, and strategic decision-making across our organization.

Position Summary

We are looking for a talented Senior Analyst to join our Finance Technology and Innovation function, reporting to the Sr. Manager of Finance Technology and Innovation. This role requires a detail-oriented problem-solver with strong finance operations knowledge and hands-on technology skills. The ideal candidate is eager to work with emerging technologies, including AI/ML, to streamline processes, support cost reduction, and deliver measurable business value, while working closely with senior team members to execute the team's vision.

Key Responsibilities

Transformation Support

  • Support finance technology transformation initiatives that align with organizational strategic goals
  • Partner with the Controllership Operations team to identify opportunities for technology-enabled process improvements
  • Assist with change management efforts to support smooth adoption of new technologies and processes
  • Help track progress against the technology roadmap for the Controllership and broader Finance organization

Technology Implementation

  • Research and evaluate emerging technologies including Agents, AI/ML, automation tools, and advanced analytics platforms
  • Support the delivery of technology projects that improve efficiency, compliance, accuracy, and operational insights
  • Participate in innovation programs that encourage experimentation and adoption of emerging technologies
  • Prepare analysis and recommendations for finance leadership on technology and process enhancement opportunities

Collaboration & Development

  • Work alongside team members on project workstreams, contributing analysis and execution support
  • Contribute to a collaborative culture that encourages innovation, continuous improvement, and professional growth
  • Coordinate effectively across stakeholder groups and competing priorities
  • Continue developing capabilities in emerging technologies and modern solutioning practices

Process Improvement & Compliance

  • Help design and implement finance process improvements that reduce manual effort and increase operational effectiveness
  • Support technology solutions in maintaining compliance with regulatory standards and internal control requirements
  • Assist in maintaining frameworks for internal controls and global policy compliance
  • Support Internal Audit, SOX, and compliance teams with audit readiness activities

Stakeholder Engagement

  • Communicate technology solutions and project updates to stakeholders across Finance and IT
  • Translate technical concepts into clear, accessible summaries for non-technical audiences
  • Support customer demonstrations and internal presentations showcasing innovative solutions
  • Build strong working relationships across Finance, IT, and Operations

Required Qualifications

Experience

  • 6+ years of experience in finance operations, technology delivery, or related fields
  • Experience supporting or contributing to technology projects or initiatives
  • Demonstrated ability to deliver assigned work on time and to a high standard
  • Experience working in global organizations with distributed teams across multiple regions is a plus
  • Familiarity with prototyping AI solutions, testing machine learning models, and wrangling small/medium sized datasets
  • Exposure to Salesforce (Agents, Tableau, Slack, Einstein, Data Cloud, etc.) preferred
  • Exposure to Snowflake preferred

Technical Expertise

  • Solid understanding of finance processes, accounting principles, and financial reporting requirements
  • Working knowledge of AI/ML technologies and interest in applying them to business problems
  • Working knowledge of data visualization tools (Tableau or similar), Python, and modern ERP systems
  • Familiarity with cloud data platforms such as Snowflake is a plus
  • Working knowledge of the Salesforce platform and/or custom application development is a plus

Skills & Competencies

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
  • Ability to translate data into actionable insights
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills
  • Eagerness to learn new technologies and adapt to change
  • Solid organizational and time-management skills; able to manage multiple priorities

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field
  • Progress toward or interest in professional certifications such as CPA, or relevant technology certifications
  • Early exposure to presenting work to internal stakeholders or teams
  • Interest in early adoption and implementation of emerging technologies
  • Previous experience in SaaS, technology, or fast-paced growth companies is a plus

What Success Looks Like

  • Reliable delivery of assigned work that contributes to measurable ROI through technology implementations (cost savings, efficiency gains, error reduction)
  • Growing proficiency with new technologies and processes across the finance organization
  • Development of solutions that support team and organizational goals
  • Building a reputation as a dependable, detail-oriented contributor
  • Active participation in a high-performing team culture of innovation and continuous improvement
