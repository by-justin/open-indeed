Job Description

Program Summary:  Are you ready to kick-start your career with a global leader? The Thermo Fisher Scientific ANZ Graduate Leadership Program is designed for high-potential graduates in science, engineering, business, and commerce. This program equips you with the tools, skills, and support to accelerate your professional development. With opportunities to secure a permanent role post-program, you'll be guided by a dedicated Program Manager and senior leaders.

Program Design: How You’ll Make an Impact  Over two years, you'll experience three job rotations, gaining exposure to various facets of Thermo Fisher’s core processes, technologies, and leadership. Rotations are tailored to your aspirations, development needs, and business priorities, all based at our ANZ HQ in Scoresby. You'll dive into diverse functional areas, business units, and products/services.

Your Growth Journey:  While most learning happens on the job, a structured program supports your development. Focus areas include self-awareness, learning from others, and developing critical competencies for success.

Learning Opportunities:

  • Induction Program:  Meet the company, core functions, peers, managers, and leaders.
  • Cohort Activities:  Share experiences, ideas, and feedback.
  • Business Impact Projects:  Work on projects and interact with our Senior Leadership team.
  • Skills Development Trainings:  Enhance communication, influencing skills, self-awareness, and management fundamentals.
  • Online Learning Library:  Access a wealth of resources.
  • Career Mentoring:  Get guidance in your second year to prepare for your next steps.
  • Rotations:  Gain exposure to Sales, Marketing, Portfolio Management, Quality & Regulatory, Finance, and Supply Chain.

What’s in it for You:  By the end of the program, you'll have produced results, gained credibility, and developed a strong company network. You'll be ready to take on various roles that align with your long-term career aspirations.

How Will You Get Here:

  • Bachelor’s Degree: Graduating in 2026 or graduated in 2025 with a Bachelor’s degree in Science or Engineering . A combined or double degree incorporating Business, Commerce, Management, or a related discipline is highly regarded.
  • Relevant Work Experience:  Including internships.
  • Commercial Mindset: An interest in sales, business, or the commercial side of science and technology.

Knowledge, Skills, Abilities:

  • Career Ambition:  Desire for a leadership role within a global organization.  Adaptability:  Energized by change and able to adapt to new assignments, managers, and businesses.
  • Interpersonal Skills:  Ability to interact with employees and leaders at all levels.
  • Attention to Detail:  Strong focus on results.
  • Commercial Savvy:  Understanding of metrics, budgets, and strategy aligned with business objectives.
  • Communication:  Excellent interpersonal skills and professional presence.
  • Authorization:  Ability to work in Australia without restriction.
  • Willingness to Travel:  Access to a vehicle and willing to travel as needed throughout the program.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
