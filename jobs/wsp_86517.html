Your Impact

  • Deliver geotechnical studies, analyses, and engineering reports that guide safe, sustainable project decisions across diverse northern terrains.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to develop geotechnical solutions for highways, bridges, buildings, dams, industrial facilities, tunnelling, and more.
  • Leadgeotechnical investigation programs, including scoping, bid documents, contractor evaluations, and technical oversight throughout project delivery.
  • Assesscomplex geotechnical challenges such as settlement, liquefaction, slope stability, frost effects, and groundwater behaviour—bringing forward clear, data‑driven recommendations.
  • Support field programs including boreholes, CPTs, test pits, piezocone soundings, embankment construction, subgrade assessments, and geosynthetics installation.
  • Share knowledge across WSP’s national network, contributing to innovative approaches and best practices in geotechnical engineering.
  • Contribute to a culture of safety, inclusion, and technical excellence while mentoring junior team members and supporting project delivery across the region.

The Skills That Set You Apart

  • You hold a B.Sc. in Geotechnical or Civil Engineering with a geotechnical focus, and you are already registered as a P.Eng. in Canada, with eligibility for registration with EGBC.
  • You bring 6+ years of hands‑on geotechnical consulting experience in Canada, with a strong foundation in applied geomechanics and soil/rock behaviour.
  • You have experience navigating real‑world challenges in northern or remote project environments and thrive in both office and field settings.
  • You’re fluent with geotechnical modelling suites such as GeoStudio, Rocscience, or similar tools, and you apply strong engineering judgment when interpreting outputs.
  • You have a proven track record of delivering projects on time and on budget, balancing technical rigor with practical solutions.
  • You bring initiative, curiosity, and a self‑directed mindset—paired with a valid Canadian driver’s license.
