Job Description

The Opportunity

We are hiring a Data Platform Engineer to manage and improve Adobe's enterprise data and AI platform. In this position, you will work closely with engineering, analytics, and business units to deliver consistent, secure, and scalable data services that support reporting, machine learning, and innovative AI projects. This role allows you to engage with modern cloud tools and help teams gain value from extensive data sets.

What you'll do

  • Coordinate and support enterprise Databricks environments across Azure and AWS.
  • Handle cloud-based data storage systems like Azure Data Lake Storage (ADLS Gen2) and Amazon S3.
  • Support data integration and replication pipelines connecting enterprise platforms.
  • Provide operational support and mentorship for platform users.
  • Support analytics and visualization platforms including Power BI and Tableau.
  • Monitor platform health, performance, and usage while developing automation.
  • Partner with security teams to implement governance, compliance, and data protection controls.
  • Find opportunities to optimize cloud spending and improve resource utilization.
  • Evaluate and support emerging AI and Generative AI capabilities.
  • Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to troubleshoot issues and improve platform capabilities.

What you need to succeed

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent experience.
  • 2+ years of hands-on experience coordinating Databricks environments in production.
  • 3+ years of hands-on experience in Azure/AWS Cloud Infrastructure, supporting enterprise-scale cloud data platforms.
  • 1+ year of hands-on administration experience with Tableau, Power BI, or Microsoft Fabric.
  • Experience handling data lake platforms like ADLS Gen2 and Amazon S3.
  • Experience troubleshooting and supporting production data, analytics, or business intelligence platforms.
  • Experience developing scripts or automation using Python, Bash, or similar technologies.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to collaborate effectively across technical and business groups.

Preferred qualifications:

  • Previous SQL Server Administration experience to provide operational support during the transition to new platforms.
  • Experience with Apache Airflow, Kubernetes (AKS or EKS), or Apache Kafka.
  • Familiarity with MongoDB, Hive Metastore, MySQL, or vector databases.
  • Experience with monitoring and observability platforms such as Databricks System Tables, Splunk, or Prometheus.
  • Familiarity with Generative AI technologies, LangChain, LangSmith, vector search, or LLM-based tools.
  • Experience using Jira, ServiceNow, or similar operational support platforms.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
