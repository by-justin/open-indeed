Job Description

Kick-start your geoscience career with Ovintiv. As a summer intern, you will work alongside industry experts, contribute to meaningful projects, and gain hands-on experience on some of North America's premier energy assets. Working within multidisciplinary teams, you will develop technical skills, learn from experienced mentors, and see how geology helps drive business decisions and operational success.

Our Geoscience interns will be placed in a position that will give them exposure to the many roles of a professional geoscientist, including Exploration, Reservoir Characterization, Development and Operations.

Responsibilities

  • Log interpretation, geologic mapping, structural modelling, core analysis.
  • Investigating critical parameters that influence productivity and economics.
  • Identify opportunities for new well locations or pad optimization.
  • Supporting operations through drilling and completions as it relates to rock properties.
  • Multi-disciplinary integration (e.g. Geophysics, Petrophysics, etc.).
