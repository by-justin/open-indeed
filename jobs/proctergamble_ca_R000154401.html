The role

Are you a leader who possesses an entrepreneurial spirit, a real passion for influencing strategy, and the ability to build collaborative partnerships? Would you like the opportunity to work with billion-dollar brands? How would you like to represent these billion-dollar brands to P&G's retailers on Day 1? How would you like to help develop the strategy and improve the business models of global companies? Do you have an ambitious spirit, a real passion for winning, and an interest in traveling? If you have proven analytical skills and are looking for a rewarding job which will allow you to grow personally and professionally, then come be a Senior Account Executive with us!

At Sales at P&G, we offer a career that spans multiple different sales roles, both internal and external. As we hire for future opportunities, successful candidates may be placed into any of these roles, or other opportunities within Sales, based on business needs at the time and the best match for their skills, experiences, and strengths.

While the responsibilities may vary, all Senior Account Executive roles require strong analytical skills, the ability to draw insights from data, critical thinking, and a passion for delivering business results. Below are examples of the types of opportunities available within our Sales organization:

1. Senior Account Executive - MS&P (Market Strategy & Planning)

The Senior AE MS&P (Market Strategy & Planning) role is a key contributor to the execution of business strategies. This role is responsible for enabling Sales teams to deliver NOS, share, and productivity targets. This includes:

  • Market strategy role to develop national market strategies aligned with the overall brand strategy and deploy to the Sales Organization for execution
  • Leverage analytical tools and integrate dynamic data systems to develop new category/brand insights
  • Leverage consumer insights and partner with key customers to develop winning joint business strategies and plans
  • Partner with North American Business Unit to launch breakthrough innovation in Canada

Key Responsibilities:

  • Contribute to and execute strategies in team / organization
  • Identify and lead developments of new ideas, techniques, procedures, services, or products
  • Collaborate effectively within immediate team or work domain to maximize job impact and personal learning
  • Accomplish tasks as an individual contributor with limited supervision
  • Develop knowledge and skills necessary for current job requirements
  • Awareness of relevant external (legal, tax) and internal stewardship requirements and act accordingly

2. Senior Account Executive - Customer Sales

As a Sales Senior Account Executive, you will:

  • Leverage consumer insights and partner with customers to develop winning joint business plans
  • Lead and improve our distribution, pricing, shelving, and merchandising
  • Develop collaborative partnerships with our customers
  • Execute business plans to meet or exceed our financial objectives
  • Use analytical tools and integrate various data systems to develop new department and category insights
  • Improve our selling advantage by driving retailer category growth and contributing to their category strategies

3. Senior Category Development Account Executive

As a Senior Category Development Account Executive, you will:

  • Serve as a trusted advisor to key customers for product assortment, placement, pricing and category strategies
  • Leverage analytical tools and integrate dynamic data systems to develop new category insights
  • Analyze and communicate planogram changes, while working towards best-in-class shelving principles
  • Execute selling opportunities that will accelerate category growth, support ad-hoc data analysis requests, and monitor competitive activity
