🚨What You’ll Be Doing

As a Material Handler, you’ll help keep production running efficiently by ensuring injection molding presses and blenders have a constant supply of raw materials and supplies. You’ll also support inventory control, scrap management, equipment operation, and daily production reporting.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Maintain a consistent flow of raw materials and supplies to injection molding presses and blenders.
  • Monitor silo levels and coordinate Gaylord deliveries while completing all required documentation.
  • Operate a forklift to safely transport scrap bins, partially completed Gaylords, raw materials, and other goods throughout the facility.
  • Assist the Scheduler with recording scrap quantities and maintaining accurate material inventory levels.
  • Separate and monitor the granulation of material from surge bins.
  • Perform minor repairs and troubleshooting on loading system equipment.
  • Identify and direct non-conforming material to the appropriate designated area.
  • Follow all work guidance sheets, Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) procedures, and quality alerts.
  • Accurately record and report required production data to maintain daily production records and provide information to the Production Manager for entry into the data collection system.
  • Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Production Manager.

  • Ability to perform minor equipment repairs and troubleshoot loading-system equipment.​
