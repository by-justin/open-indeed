Job Description

What you can expect

Zoom is looking for a hunter and prospector to create and win opportunities for our full suite of Zoom solutions. This team focuses on working with existing and new growing organizations with 501~ employees in Japan. As a Majors/Commercial Account Executive, you will position, sell and provide them with our cutting-edge AI Zoom workplace solutions based on their business needs. You will develop potential clients in Singapore and be in charge of end-to-end sales activities. You will report to the local Sales Leader and work with the local sales and cross-functional teams to drive Zoom's business growth in Japan.

About the Team

The Zoom sales team is a dynamic force driving the company's success. With a focus on delivering cutting- edge communication solutions for clients across all sectors, the team passionately engages with clients worldwide. Through strategic partnerships and a customer-centric approach, they play a pivotal role in expanding Zoom's global presence and ensuring businesses harness the full potential of virtual collaboration.

What we’re looking for

  • Have 5+ years of successful Account Executive experience in high growth SaaS/IT company
  • Have experience with Customer Relationship Management (CRM) tools and other sales applications.
  • Understand of outreach methods to approach new clients, and a good new business hunter profile with an ownership mindset.
  • Have excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to build relevant, strategic narratives and messaging.
  • Develop new accounts and expand business within Zoom's existing accounts, leveraging partnerships and handling complex sales scenarios and large projects.
  • Have a good skill cross collaboration and relationship building.
  • Have native Japanese skill (mandatory) and Business Fluency in English (highly welcomed)

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
