Job Description

Job Description:

At Airbus, our purpose is to “ pioneer sustainable aviation for a safe & united world ”. To do so, we have the ambition to act as a catalyst for the development of Sustainable Aviation Fuels (SAF) and Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) worldwide.

As a member of Airbus’ Chief Sustainability Officer organization, you will join a team of highly motivated and valuable people who are all contributing to the decarbonisation of commercial aviation.

We are looking for talented candidates who can support the activities of the team. We expect the candidates to demonstrate motivation to contribute to the decarbonation of aviation, ability to work in a transnational environment and flexibility to adjust to the evolving needs of the business. The role will be available from April 2027 for a 6 months internship.

Your Accountabilities:

As part of Airbus SAF & CDR team and to accelerate the availability of SAF and CDRs, under the management of the HO SAF & CDR business development with producers, the intern will be asked to:

  • Use Gemini to optimize the business monitoring and business reporting tools used by the team
  • Support the team members in the establishment and follow-up of the partnerships with SAF related companies worldwide
  • Liaise with the engineering team and other SAF related departments to ensure the lessons learnt from the different projects are best capitalized within Airbus

Who we are looking for

Because Airbus CSO organization is preparing the future of decarbonised aviation, we look for highly motivated and passionate interns, able to bring their energy and expertise into that journey.

Skills and Experience:

  • Development of Gemini based GenAI tools
  • Educational background in business and management
  • Solid knowledge about and interest in sustainability topic
  • Knowledge about and interest in the aviation and / or energy sectors
  • Comfortable to work in English and to communicate with different cultures
  • Ease to communicate and with teamwork

What We Offer

You will work in a dynamic and fast-paced business environment that has a strong impact on the decarbonation of commercial aviation. You will benefit from joining a dynamic and highly experienced team that will allow you to skill-up quickly in the domain of sustainability.

Come Join Our Team!

Decarbonisation of aviation is one of the biggest challenges that commercial aviation has gone through since its inception. If you have the passion, ambition, energy and competence to make a step change in this transformation - we want you! Your dream job starts here!

If your profile matches with our requirements and you are interested in joining our dynamic and multicultural team of professionals, you can complete your application online (providing your CV and cover letter outlining your motivation).

Join Airbus now and embrace the opportunity to make a difference. We make it fly and you can too!
