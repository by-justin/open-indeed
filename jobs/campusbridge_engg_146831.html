Job Description

Highlights of the Opportunity

We are hiring one (1) Engineering Co-op Student to join the Systems Monitoring team within EPCOR Water Services, based out of Aurum Road in Edmonton, AB.

Position: Winter 2027 Engineering Co-op Student, Systems Monitoring - Engineering and Technical Services

Term: 4 Months starting January 2027 Year: 4th and 5th Term Preferred Discipline: Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Chemical Engineering (CPC Option Preferred)

Are you interested in seeing how real-time monitoring data supports one of Edmonton's most critical infrastructure systems?

Join EPCOR Water Services and gain hands-on experience working with operational technology, industrial communications, automation systems, instrumentation, and data analytics. Working alongside engineers and technologists, you'll support the systems that collect, validate, and visualize field data from across Edmonton's wastewater collection network.

In this role, you'll gain exposure to SCADA systems, remote monitoring technologies, industrial communications, database management, and sensor analytics while supporting infrastructure that helps protect public health and the environment

Who We Are

For over 100 years, EPCOR Water Services has provided safe, reliable water and wastewater services to communities across Western Canada. Wastewater Collection operates and maintains Edmonton's wastewater, stormwater, and combined sewer systems, supporting critical infrastructure throughout the city.

The Systems Monitoring team is responsible for the installation, maintenance, and operation of flow, rainfall, and air monitoring equipment throughout Edmonton. The data collected from these monitoring systems supports operations, planning, engineering projects, and long-term infrastructure decision-making.

About the Opportunity

We're seeking an engineering student to support our Systems Monitoring team through a four-month co-op placement.

This is an opportunity to gain practical engineering experience while working with technologies that support real-time monitoring and operational decision-making across Edmonton's wastewater collection system. You'll collaborate with engineers and technologists while contributing to projects involving SCADA systems, instrumentation, communications networks, and industrial data management.

What You'll Learn

As a successful candidate, you'll develop an understanding of:

  • Operational Technology (OT) systems and industrial control environments.
  • SCADA architecture and remote monitoring technologies.
  • Industrial communication protocols and data acquisition systems.
  • Sensor performance, troubleshooting, and data integrity management.
  • SQL databases and operational data management.
  • Python-based data analytics and quality assurance processes.
  • Wastewater collection system operations and monitoring practices.
  • Engineering approaches to monitoring, instrumentation, and system commissioning.

Technical Experience You'll Gain

Throughout your work term, you'll gain practical exposure to:

  • Configuring and maintaining monitoring stations within Ignition SCADA and WISKI systems.
  • SQL database management and historical data migration activities.
  • Creating and testing SCADA tags and operator displays.
  • Remote telemetry and communication network technologies.
  • Node-RED and FlowFuse automation applications.
  • Data validation, quality control, and troubleshooting techniques.
  • Monitoring station commissioning and deployment activities.
  • Instrumentation design, sensor configuration, and engineering support processes.

Additional opportunities may include tours of EPCOR's Water and Wastewater Treatment Plants to gain a broader understanding of municipal water and wastewater operations.

Key Responsibilities

You'll be involved in a variety of engineering and technical support activities, including:

  • Assisting engineers and technologists with configuring and maintaining monitoring stations within SCADA and data management systems.
  • Creating, testing, and commissioning database tags and operator displays.
  • Supporting the migration of historical operational data into SQL databases.
  • Assisting with testing and deployment activities for monitoring system upgrades and enhancements.
  • Supporting remote data acquisition systems and communications infrastructure.
  • Performing data quality assurance and analysis activities using Python and related tools.
  • Assisting with permanent flow monitoring projects, instrumentation reviews, and sensor configuration activities.
  • Supporting engineers, technologists, and leaders with operational and project-related initiatives as required.

What You'll Need to Succeed

To be successful in this role, you should:

  • Be enrolled in a full-time Engineering Co-op program.
  • Have an interest in operational technology, automation, industrial systems, or data acquisition.
  • Possess a strong technical foundation, including a general understanding of computer systems, control systems, data acquisition, or automation technologies.
  • Demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Be comfortable working independently and as part of a collaborative team.
  • Possess strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Exhibit a high level of attention to detail and curiosity for learning new technologies.

Assets

  • Experience working within a SCADA environment.
  • Experience with SQL databases.
  • Experience with Python programming or data analysis tools.
  • Exposure to industrial communications, networking, or automation systems.

Ideal Candidate

You are curious about technology, automation, and how data supports critical infrastructure. You enjoy solving technical problems, learning new systems, and working with both software and hardware. You're eager to apply your academic knowledge in a practical environment while contributing to systems that help support safe, reliable wastewater services for the City of Edmonton.
