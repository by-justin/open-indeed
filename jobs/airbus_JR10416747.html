Description de l'emploi :

Vous avez une expérience 5 à 10 ans d’expérience en Gestion de la configuration Électrique et Avionique, vous avez travaillé Agent Méthodes et vous souhaitez évoluer et développer de nouvelles compétences? Ce poste est pour vous !

Le programme d'avions commerciaux A220 recherche un Agent Méthodes Configuration en Installation des systèmes électrique et avionique pour rejoindre notre équipe Méthode Configuration basée à Mirabel (Québec, Canada), où vous conjointement avec l’équipe Ingénierie à introduire les changements de la configuration dans les cahiers de montage.

Vous ferez partie de l’équipe Méthodes Configuration A220 chargée de traduire les requis d’ingénierie en séquence d’assemblage optimale servant à l’assemblage de l’avion A220..

L'organisation de l'équipe est établie de manière à promouvoir la collaboration transversale de manière autonome, en se soutenant mutuellement et en apprenant collectivement.

Votre rôle en tant qu’Agent Méthodes sera de préparer les instructions visuelles de travail pour l’installation de composants électriques et avioniques sur l’avion en utilisant différents logiciels tels que SAP, CATIA V5 et 3DVia.

Job description :

Do you have between 5 and 10 years of experience in Configuration change management on electrical installation, have you worked in Manufacturing Engineering and would you like to progress and develop new skills? Then this job is for you!

The A220 commercial aircraft Programme is looking for a Method Agent to join our Manufacturing Engineering team based in Mirabel (Québec, Canada), where you will produce visual work instructions to assemble the A220 Aircraft.

You will be part of the A220 Manufacturing Engineering team responsible for translating engineering requirements into an optimal assembly sequence for the assembly of the A220 aircraft..

The team organization is established to promote cross-collaboration autonomously, supporting each other and learning collectively.

Your role as Method Agent will be to prepare the visual instructions and work sequences, with respect to engineering requirements and industry specifications, for electrical and avionic system installation on aircraft, using different software such as SAP, CATIA V5 and 3DVia.

Your working environment:

The A220 commercial aircraft Programme is headquartered in Mirabel, in the greater Montreal area, where the vibrant city of Montreal is just a stone’s throw away. Known for its peaceful and safe environment, the region offers a high quality of life for younger or experienced professionals and families, perfectly balancing career opportunities and outdoor activities that makes it a truly special place to call home.

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, annual bonus, group insurance plan, pension plan and share purchase plan.
  • Work / Life Balance: A hybrid working policy (based on operational needs), on-site cafeterias and a highly competitive holiday policy.
  • Wellbeing/Health : Employee Assistance Program (EAP), Discount Program,  on-site Medical Service, access to a public shuttle service between Laval (Montmorency metro station) and Mirabel, and a carpooling application.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects  with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

Your challenges:

  • Define and write instructions and work sequences, with respect to engineering requirements and industry specifications, for electrical, mechanical and as an asset avionic system installation on aircraft, using different software such as SAP, CATIA V5 and 3DVia;
  • Participate in the investigation of root causes of problems and analyze potential solutions by assessing the costs, impacts and risks of a change in production;
  • Participate in the planning and implementation of production changes and introduction of new configuration;
  • Support the daily activities of production;
  • Participate in the definitions of tooling requirements, assembly and the implementation of various production equipment according to the optimal methodology taking into account the health / safety component.

Your boarding pass:

  • You have 5 to 10 years of experience in configuration management for electrical and avionics systems, either as a Methods Agent in the aerospace industry or in a manufacturing setting;
  • You have completed a DEC in aeronautics, ideally avionic specialty, or engineering electrical degree or other related education;
  • You have a good understanding of ERP system principles and an extended knowledge of SAP Methods transactions, 3D visual software such 3Dvia considered an asset;
  • You have a good level of knowledge in French and English spoken and written;
  • You have good organizational and planning skills in order to meet your commitments;
  • You are resourceful, autonomous, motivated with good problem-solving skills;
  • You have good judgment and you work well in a multidisciplinary team.
