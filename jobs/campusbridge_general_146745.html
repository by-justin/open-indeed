Job Description

Who We Are?

Driven by a vision to eliminate poverty in our community, Bissell Centre's data-driven approach is rooted in a deep belief in the dignity, strength, and potential of every individual. Through compassion, optimism, and strong relationships, our prevention and stabilization strategy and programs empower people by supporting our community to secure housing and build sustainable livelihoods. Our community comes from all walks of life, and so does our team-united by a shared commitment to create real, lasting change. By joining Bissell Centre, you will become part of a fast-paced and dedicated team that works together to realize our vision.

Currently, Bissell Centre is looking for an Intake Coordinator to join our dedicated Family Intervention Services team as we journey with the Community towards an elimination of poverty. If you're ready to bring your heart to a fast-paced, collaborative environment, join us in making an impact in our community.

How You'll Make an Impact?

The Intake Coordinator acts as the essential first point of contact for families seeking support, ensuring a welcoming and efficient entry into the organization's services. This role is responsible for the professional management of the referral pipeline, conducting preliminary assessments to determine program eligibility, and streamlining the administrative transition to ongoing case management. By bridging the gap between initial inquiry and active support, this position ensures that families in need are connected to the right resources with minimal delay and maximum clarity.

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for all incoming referrals, ensuring a timely and professional response to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Conduct initial intakes to gather baseline participant information and assess immediate needs against program eligibility criteria.
  • Facilitate the transition of participants into the program by coordinating and confirming intake appointments between families and the Family Support Workers (FSWs).
  • Ensure all initial referral data is accurately captured and organized to support seamless caseload assignment and program reporting.
  • Relay messages as appropriate or transfer to appropriate extension number/contact number. Staff member must exercise discretion and diplomacy on obtaining information from callers for persons not immediately available.
  • Provide updated information to participants on accessing basic needs, employment, childcare and family support, housing, income tax services, etc.

What You'll Bring?

  • Certificate, Diploma or Degree in Social Work, Human Services, Community Support Work or combination of other relevant education and experience.
  • Employees with Social Work Diploma/Degree are required to provide and maintain ongoing registration, in good standing, with Alberta College of Social Workers (ACSW) as per Alberta Health Professions Act.
  • Minimum one year of employment experience working with individuals in crisis (e.g., high-risk populations, case management, mental health and addictions).
  • Prior experience supporting adults in crisis, addictions, mental health or other complex barriersis highly preferred.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
