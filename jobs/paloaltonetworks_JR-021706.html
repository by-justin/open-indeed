Job Summary

Strategy & Business Development

  • Own the global IDIRA strategy and business plan for NTT DATA, including growth priorities, target markets, sales motions, pipeline objectives, and execution milestones.

  • Build IDIRA into a strategic growth pillar within the broader Palo Alto Networks and NTT DATA partnership.

  • Identify and develop new business opportunities where Palo Alto Networks IDIRA capabilities can be integrated into NTT DATA cybersecurity, identity, cloud, infrastructure, and transformation services.

  • Develop joint value propositions and repeatable GTM plays that enable NTT DATA sellers and delivery teams to take IDIRA solutions to their customers.

  • Identify priority customer segments, accounts, use cases, and market opportunities where NTT DATA and Palo Alto Networks can jointly win.

  • Develop strategies to position Palo Alto Networks IDIRA capabilities within NTT DATA's existing identity security practices, customer transformation programs, managed services, and strategic accounts.

Partnership Development

  • Establish and maintain senior relationships across NTT DATA's cybersecurity, identity, cloud, services, sales, and regional leadership organizations.

  • Build the required executive sponsorship, sales alignment, technical sponsorship, and delivery engagement within NTT DATA to scale the IDIRA business.

  • Act as the primary global IDIRA partnership lead for NTT DATA and the central point of coordination across both organizations.

  • Develop a strong understanding of NTT DATA's identity capabilities, services portfolio, customer base, competitive landscape, and investment priorities.

  • Identify gaps in NTT DATA capabilities, offerings, enablement, or field readiness and establish joint plans to address them.

  • Influence NTT DATA investment and resource prioritization around IDIRA opportunities and joint GTM initiatives.

GTM & Pipeline Creation

  • Drive measurable IDIRA pipeline creation and revenue growth with NTT DATA globally.

  • Build and execute targeted sales plays with NTT DATA across priority industries, regions, and strategic accounts.

  • Work directly with Palo Alto Networks and NTT DATA field organizations to identify, qualify, develop, and accelerate IDIRA opportunities.

  • Establish account-mapping and opportunity-development programs that convert NTT DATA customer relationships and Palo Alto Networks installed-base intelligence into actionable pipeline.

  • Develop repeatable demand-generation and sales acceleration programs with regional teams.

  • Engage directly on strategic opportunities where partnership leadership, executive alignment, or cross-functional orchestration is required to advance or close the opportunity.

  • Track pipeline creation, progression, conversion, bookings, and other business outcomes against agreed objectives.

Solution & Capability Development

  • Partner with Palo Alto Networks Product, Product Management, Engineering, and technical teams to ensure NTT DATA understands the IDIRA strategy, roadmap, differentiation, and customer value proposition.

  • Work with NTT DATA to develop joint IDIRA solutions and services that combine Palo Alto Networks technology with NTT DATA consulting, implementation, integration, and managed services capabilities.

  • Drive the development and launch of repeatable NTT DATA IDIRA offerings that can be taken to market globally.

  • Establish technical, sales, and services enablement plans required to build NTT DATA capability and field confidence.

  • Coordinate certification, enablement, solution development, and field-readiness activities across relevant NTT DATA teams.

  • Capture NTT DATA and customer feedback and provide a structured feedback loop into Palo Alto Networks Product and GTM organizations.

Global Execution & Governance

  • Translate the global IDIRA strategy into regional execution plans across NAM, EMEA, JAPAC, and LATAM.

  • Establish clear targets, owners, milestones, and success metrics for joint initiatives.

  • Lead regular business reviews covering pipeline, bookings, sales plays, solution development, enablement, field engagement, risks, and required actions.

  • Maintain executive visibility into IDIRA partnership performance, key opportunities, investments, barriers, and decisions required.

  • Identify execution gaps and proactively drive corrective actions across Palo Alto Networks and NTT DATA.

  • Coordinate cross-functional resources across Sales, Product, Marketing, Services, Enablement, Finance, and Operations to support the global business plan.

Qualifications

Required

  • 10+ years of experience in strategic partnerships, business development, alliances, enterprise sales, or go-to-market roles within enterprise technology or cybersecurity.

  • Demonstrated experience building and scaling businesses with Global Systems Integrators, strategic partners, or large enterprise technology organizations.

  • Strong understanding of identity security, identity and access management, cybersecurity, cloud security, or adjacent security markets.

  • Proven ability to develop GTM strategies and translate them into measurable pipeline and revenue.

  • Experience working with global field sales organizations and developing opportunities across multiple regions.

  • Demonstrated ability to build new businesses, offerings, or sales motions from early-stage strategy through execution and scale.

  • Strong executive presence with the ability to develop trusted relationships with senior partner and internal stakeholders.

  • Proven ability to lead complex cross-functional initiatives across Sales, Product, Engineering, Services, Marketing, and Operations.

  • Ability to influence and drive accountability across teams without direct reporting authority.

  • Strong commercial judgment with an understanding of partner economics, services-led business models, pipeline management, and enterprise sales cycles.

  • Excellent communication, presentation, negotiation, and executive-level storytelling skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working directly with NTT DATA or another major Global Systems Integrator.

  • Experience developing cybersecurity solutions or managed services with a global partner.

  • Deep knowledge of Identity Security, IAM, Identity Threat Detection and Response, privileged access, identity governance, or related technologies.

  • Experience launching and scaling new cybersecurity products or solution categories through a partner ecosystem.

  • Experience managing global business plans and coordinating execution across NAM, EMEA, JAPAC, and LATAM.

  • Demonstrated success creating partner-sourced or partner-influenced pipeline and converting strategic partnerships into measurable revenue.
