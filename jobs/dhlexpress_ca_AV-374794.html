Job Description

Primary focus: Building, managing, and continuously improving transport service products for domestic and international parcel distribution, including B2C, B2B, clearance products, lanes, markets, customer enablement, IT requirements, vendor service alignment, commercial viability, and product governance.

Role Purpose:

The Product Manager is responsible for building, managing, and improving transport service products that enable customers to send parcels through domestic and international distribution networks. The role defines product propositions across B2C, B2B, clearance options, delivery speeds, lanes, destinations, markets, service features, operational rules, customer requirements, and technology enablement, documenting and managing all of this information in product specifications.

The role translates customer and market needs into structured transport products, ensuring the required operational capability, vendor services, IT functionality, commercial logic, data visibility, clearance processes, and service governance are in place to support reliable, scalable, and profitable parcel distribution.

This is a management role suited to a commercially minded logistics and product professional who can connect customer propositions with transport network capability, vendor service offerings, operational processes, and digital customer enablement. A significant part of the role is the ability to engage, influence, and persuade senior executive stakeholders within DHL and external supplier organisations, building confidence in product direction, securing alignment on priorities, and gaining support for commercially and operationally sound decisions. The position has strong career progression potential into senior product, commercial, network, or logistics leadership roles.

Key Accountabilities:

  • Product strategy and roadmap: Define and maintain the transport product roadmap across domestic and international parcel distribution, including B2C, B2B, clearance products, lanes, markets, delivery options, returns, and value-added service features.
  • Product design: Translate customer, market, operational, regulatory, and commercial needs into clear product definitions, service specifications, eligibility rules, pricing logic, transit time commitments, routing options, clearance requirements, and customer-facing features.
  • Senior stakeholder influence: Engage confidently with DHL executive stakeholders and senior supplier representatives to present product direction, influence decisions, resolve competing priorities, and secure endorsement for product, commercial, operational, and technology outcomes.
  • IT requirements and customer enablement: Lead the development of business requirements, user stories, process flows, data requirements, acceptance criteria, and business testing to enable customers to access and use transport products through digital platforms, integrations, portals, labels, manifests, tracking, and reporting tools.
  • Transport vendor alignment: Engage with carriers, linehaul providers, customs brokers, injection partners, last-mile delivery partners, and other vendors to match available network services to product requirements and identify gaps requiring commercial, operational, or technical solutions. Use strong supplier engagement and negotiation skills to challenge constraints, influence service improvements, and align suppliers to DHL’s product and customer commitments.
  • Market and lane development: Build and govern product capability for specific markets, destinations, lanes, service levels, clearance models, delivery options, and customer segments. Assess product extensions and new market opportunities using competitor offerings, customer demand, lane economics, regulatory feasibility, and market intelligence.
  • Commercial and service viability: Assess product economics, cost-to-serve, margin impact, vendor rates, surcharges, clearance costs, operational handling costs, customer pricing inputs, and volume assumptions to support viable product decisions. Ensure product profitability and compliance across customs, duty and tax, security, sanctions, data-sharing, and applicable trade regulations.
  • Operational readiness and launch management: Coordinate product launches and changes across sales, operations, customer service, IT, finance, commercial, compliance, and vendor teams, ensuring readiness plans, training, documentation, controls, and support processes are in place.
  • Product performance governance: Monitor product performance using volume, revenue, margin, transit time, delivery quality, scan compliance, clearance performance, exception rates, customer experience, and vendor performance measures. Maintain end-to-end product ownership through a formal product lifecycle management approach, from concept and launch through optimisation, governance, and retirement.
  • Continuous improvement: Lead improvements to product functionality, customer experience, operational efficiency, service reliability, digital enablement, and market competitiveness. Own the voice of customer, customer journey mapping, and translation of NPS-led customer pain points into product improvements and digital capability enhancements.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Develop and maintain product definitions, service specifications, product rules, operating models, customer eligibility criteria, service levels, and product governance documentation.
  • Own the transport product roadmap for domestic and international parcel distribution, including new products, enhancements, market expansions, lane changes, and retirement of obsolete services.
  • Partner with sales, commercial, operations, customer service, finance, compliance, and IT teams to convert customer and market opportunities into deliverable transport products. Actively manage cross-functional stakeholders to align priorities, resolve dependencies, and maintain accountability across product design, delivery, launch, and ongoing governance.
  • Prepare and deliver clear, commercially grounded recommendations to executive-level stakeholders, using data, customer insight, operational impact, and supplier capability to influence decisions and obtain senior-level endorsement.
  • Lead business requirement gathering for customer-facing and internal systems required to support transport products, including booking, manifesting, labelling, routing, clearance data, tracking, billing, reporting, and exception management.
  • Prepare user stories, process maps, acceptance criteria, test scenarios, product configuration rules, and business readiness requirements for IT development and system change delivery.
  • Engage transport vendors to understand available service capability, market coverage, transit performance, clearance models, operational constraints, data requirements, and commercial terms. Build productive senior-level supplier relationships and influence supplier action where product success depends on external service capability, responsiveness, or investment.
  • Match vendor services to product propositions and identify service gaps, integration needs, network constraints, customer impacts, and improvement opportunities.
  • Support the commercial assessment of product options, including cost-to-serve, pricing inputs, margin assumptions, forecast volumes, surcharge exposure, and business case development.
  • Coordinate product launches, product changes, market entries, lane activations, service withdrawals, and customer onboarding activities.
  • Monitor product performance and customer outcomes using agreed dashboards, scorecards, exception reporting, financial reporting, and product review rhythms.
  • Maintain accurate product documentation, including service guides, operational rules, customer requirements, vendor dependencies, IT requirements, change logs, and decision records.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives that strengthen customer usability, service quality, digital capability, operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, and product profitability.

Required Knowledge and Experience:

  • Demonstrated experience in product management, logistics, parcel distribution, freight forwarding, express delivery, transport operations, eCommerce logistics, or international supply chain.
  • Strong understanding of domestic and international parcel products, including B2C, B2B, tracked and untracked services, clearance models, delivery options, returns, market rules, lanes, and destination capabilities.
  • Experience translating customer, market, and operational requirements into product specifications, product rules, process flows, business requirements, and technology change requirements.
  • Proven ability to engage and persuade senior stakeholders, including executive leaders, cross-functional decision makers, and senior supplier representatives, through clear communication, structured recommendations, commercial reasoning, and confident handling of challenge or ambiguity.
  • Working knowledge of IT delivery practices, including business requirements documentation, user stories, acceptance criteria, configuration rules, business testing, release readiness, and change management.
  • Experience engaging external transport vendors such as carriers, linehaul providers, customs brokers, injection partners, delivery partners, and platform or technology providers.
  • Understanding of parcel lifecycle processes, including customer integration, order capture, manifesting, labelling, pickup, sorting, linehaul, customs clearance, injection, depot handling, delivery, returns, tracking, billing, and customer service.
  • Ability to interpret product performance, transport cost, service quality, customer experience, volume, revenue, and margin data to support product decisions.
  • Knowledge of regulatory, customs, security, data, and market requirements affecting domestic and cross-border parcel products.
