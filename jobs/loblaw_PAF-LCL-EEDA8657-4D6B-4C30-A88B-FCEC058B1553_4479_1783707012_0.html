The Impact You'll Make The Senior Director, Store Front End Technology  is responsible for the strategy, performance, modernization, and operational excellence of all customer-facing store technology across Loblaw’s national retail network. This portfolio includes Point of Sale, Payments, Self-Checkout, Electronic Shelf Labels (ESL), Labels & Signs, Beauty technology solutions, and Store Technology Validation & Testing. Reporting to the Vice President, Store and Merchandising Technology, the Senior Director will lead the vision and execution of front-end technology platforms that enable faster checkout experiences, reduce shrink, improve operational efficiency, and lower the total cost of ownership across thousands of stores. This role partners closely with Technology Platforms, Store Operations, Asset Protection, Merchandising, Finance, Digital, and external technology partners to deliver innovative, secure, scalable, and reliable solutions that improve the customer and colleague experience.
