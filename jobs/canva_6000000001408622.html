Where and how you can work

Our flagship office is in Sydney, Australia, but we've made our way from down under, to a campus in Austin, Texas which is now home to our US operations. This is a hybrid role in Austin, Texas, so you must be able to commute to our office 1-2 times a week.

About the Team

This role sits within Canva’s GTM Operations team, which partners with Sales, Customer Success, Partnerships, Finance, Revenue Systems, and Data to build the operating system behind Canva’s B2B growth.

You’ll report to the Head of GTM Operations and work closely with the Head of AMER Sales and the wider global GTM Operations team. We are a practical, high-impact team that combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution, helping Revenue leaders make better decisions, remove friction, and scale the business with clarity and discipline.

About the Role

As the strategic business partner to the Head of AMER Sales, you’ll help shape how Canva grows across the Americas. You’ll connect commercial strategy with disciplined execution, bringing the insight, planning, and cross-functional alignment needed to help the business scale. This is a high-impact, hands-on role for someone who can move comfortably between strategy and detail, challenge assumptions, and help Sales leaders make faster, better-informed decisions.

What you'll do

  • Strategic business partnership: Serve as the primary strategic partner to the Head of AMER Sales, supporting regional planning, execution, and decision-making.
  • Forecasting & performance management: Own the AMER forecasting and performance-management rhythm, including forecast rollups, risk assessment, scenario modeling, executive readouts, and action follow-through.
  • Business cadences: Lead core operating cadences, including pipeline inspections, deal reviews, QBRs, and performance reviews in partnership with AMER Sales leadership.
  • Performance visibility: Build repeatable views of AMER performance across bookings, pipeline, conversion, coverage, capacity, productivity, attainment, and key growth initiatives.
  • Performance diagnosis: Diagnose trends across segments, markets, customer motions, products, and routes to market, translating data into clear recommendations for leaders.
  • Planning & resource allocation: Lead AMER inputs into annual and in-year planning, including headcount, capacity, territory and segmentation design, account assignment, productivity assumptions, and investment cases.
  • Cross-functional partnership: Partner with Finance, central GTM Operations, Revenue Systems, and Data to represent AMER in company planning and ensure trusted data for decision-making.
  • GTM efficiency: Identify structural opportunities to improve pipeline quality, deal velocity, coverage, efficiency, and predictability across the AMER sales motion.
  • GTM model evolution: Help evolve the AMER go-to-market model as the business scales, including the right balance of direct, Customer Success, partner, and reseller motions.
  • Global-to-regional execution: Bring regional insight into global GTM design while driving adoption of global commercial policies, processes, and standards.
  • Executive advisory: Act as a trusted advisor to AMER Sales leaders, balancing operational rigor, commercial pragmatism, and speed.
  • Executive communication: Translate complex data into clear narratives, insights, and recommendations for senior stakeholders.

What we're looking for

You’re a strategic, analytical thinker who knows how to turn complexity into clarity. You are equally comfortable advising senior Sales leaders and rolling up your sleeves to dig into a forecast, pressure-test a pipeline, or build a model from scratch.

You understand what it takes to build a predictable, scalable sales motion. You can spot risk early, identify the levers that will improve performance, and turn insight into a clear story and action plan. You thrive in complex, fast-moving environments and enjoy bringing structure to ambiguity without adding unnecessary process.

Most importantly, you are a trusted partner who combines analytical depth with strong business judgment and a practical, people-first approach. You are energized by helping teams operate better and building systems that scale as the business grows.

  • Experience: 7+ years in Sales Strategy, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or GTM Strategy, ideally in a high-growth B2B SaaS environment.
  • Leadership partnership: Proven ability to act as a strategic thought partner to senior Sales or Revenue leaders and translate insights into action.
  • Planning and commercial design: Experience with annual planning, territory and capacity planning, segmentation, account coverage, productivity modeling, and investment cases.
  • Forecasting and performance management: Deep experience with forecasting, pipeline health, scenario modeling, conversion, coverage, capacity, and attainment.
  • Analytics: Exceptional analytical and modeling skills, with advanced Excel/Google Sheets capabilities; SQL is required.
  • Business judgment: Strong instincts for balancing operational rigor, governance, and speed, with the ability to distinguish symptoms from root causes.
  • Influence: Clear, confident communicator who can influence across Sales, Customer Success, Finance, GTM Operations, Revenue Systems, and Data without direct authority.
  • Hands-on: Energized by ambiguity and comfortable moving between high-level strategy and hands-on execution.
