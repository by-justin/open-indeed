Job Description

DHL Group  – we are the world’s leading logistics provider. Our company connects people and markets and enables global trade. Our strategic goal is to be the first choice for customers, employees, and investors worldwide.

The Security Architecture department is part of the Group Chief Information Security Office, which protects DHL Group against cyber-attacks and strengthens the Group’s activities in the field of cybersecurity. It covers the relevant technological aspects and is responsible for the domain-specific development and further enhancement of a Group-wide security architecture, with the goal of ensuring and continuously improving IT security and cyber resilience for DHL Group. Sounds like an exciting challenge? Then we look forward to receiving your application as

Enterprise Security Architect – Identity and Access Management (m/f/d)

Bonn | Full-time | Permanent

Your responsibilities

  • Analysis of cybersecurity IAM trends relevant to DHL Group (e.g., regulations, technology developments e.g. AI, Cloud, NHI).
  • Creation of IAM specific risk assessments as part of the technical security architectures design work (cost, benefit, risk reduction, etc.).
  • Evaluate IAM threat scenarios and design high‑level IAM security architectures (incl. standards, baselines, and reusable artefacts such as patterns and blueprints).
  • Drive stakeholder alignment and steer solution design/implementation as SME, incl. presentations in group‑wide security forums.
  • Assess the IT security posture vs. best practices and provide actionable recommendations (incl. ISO/NIST‑aligned improvements).

Your profile

  • Completed university degree, preferably in Computer Science or comparable qualification.
  • Many years of relevant professional experience in the Identity and Access management domain, preferably proven experience delivering IAM architecture in large organizations.
  • Excellent knowledge and experience in security architecture roles – developing technology-specific and enterprise-level security architecture (target state, roadmaps).
  • Experience in the architecture and design of IAM solutions with deep technical and professional expertise, preferably with the following or similar technologies: IAM platforms: Microsoft Entra ID / Active Directory, Ping Identity, Okta PAM: CyberArk / BeyondTrust IGA: SailPoint / Oracle OIG Zero Trust: NIST 800 207
  • Deep understanding of attackers’ mindset and offensive security, preferably offensive security training (OSCP+/ CEH or similar)
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership of complex technical topics.
  • Strong problem‑solving mindset with a structured and solution‑oriented approach.
  • Very good English language skills, German language skills is an advantage
