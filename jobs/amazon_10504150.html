Description

Are you passionate about using AI to change customers' lives for the better? The Alexa International team is looking for a Senior Solutions Architect to drive adoption of Alexa+ across some of our most strategic 3P partners in the Americas, with a focus on Canada, Mexico, and Brazil.

Alexa+ is Amazon's next-generation personal AI assistant, rebuilt from the ground up on a modern architecture that orchestrates large language models (LLMs), agentic pipelines, and connected services at scale. Available across Echo devices, mobile, Fire TV, and Alexa.com. Alexa+ is designed to get things done through natural, capable, multi-turn interactions.

As a Senior Solutions Architect for the Americas, you'll serve as the technical face of Amazon for our partners, owning the integration lifecycle from first technical engagement through production launch. You'll work hands-on with partner engineering teams to design, validate, and ship integrations that feel native to Alexa+, while building the playbooks, reference architectures, and documentation that will scale the program beyond 1:1 engagement. As a founding contributor, the patterns and practices you establish will define how we work with partners across categories for years to come.

This role sits at the intersection of deep technical execution and strategic influence. You'll translate complex integration requirements into clear business value for partner stakeholders, drive alignment across Amazon's business development teams, product, and engineering organizations, and exercise strong judgment as integration patterns continue to evolve.

The ideal candidate brings hands-on experience with modern integration protocols (including MCP), agentic system design, strong programming skills, and full-stack fluency across backend services, API layers, and front-end rendering. You have a track record of delivering complex multi-party integrations and can move fluidly between a deep technical conversation with a partner's engineering team and a strategic discussion with their leadership.

Key job responsibilities

Technical Delivery & Partner Integration:

• Design and deliver end-to-end partner integration solutions leveraging modern protocols and agentic patterns (e.g., MCP, tool-calling, REST), ensuring experiences feel native to Alexa+ while respecting partner brand and capabilities. • Work hands-on with partners to assess technical readiness, architect solutions across the full stack — from backend services and API design to front-end rendering — and troubleshoot complex integration challenges across multiple device surfaces. • Work with internal engineering teams to test and deploy software packages required to onboard partners to Alexa+. • Exercise strong judgment in ambiguous technical situations, determining appropriate solution strategies and making clear trade-off recommendations (e.g., partial vs. full E2E integration, link-out vs. native rendering).

Frameworks & Scalable Enablement:

• Create reusable integration patterns, technical documentation, and onboarding playbooks that accelerate future partner launches and reduce hands-on SA effort over time. • Develop reference architectures that leverage horizontal capabilities (compositional rendering, streaming, smart routing) to address common partner needs across categories.

Partner & Product Influence:

• Own technical relationships with partner engineering and product teams, guiding integrations from commitment through launch. • Collaborate with product leadership during early capability formation, directly shaping the roadmap based on real partner integration learnings.

A day in the life We're looking for a highly motivated Sr SA to join the Alexa+ International team as we build out the third-party integration program from the ground up. You'll engage in deep technical design discussions, solve novel challenges spanning agentic architectures and front-end rendering, and coordinate feature implementations across partner categories. You'll represent the partner perspective with internal product and engineering teams, using integration learnings, data-driven analysis, and a working-backwards approach to influence roadmap priorities. This is a founding-team role: you'll shape how we work, what we build, and how we scale.

About the team Our mission is to enable third-party partners to bring their agentic capabilities and services to Alexa+ through the Americas, creating high-quality experiences across consumer categories that help customers get things done. We are a small, high-impact team of technologists bridging BD, internal product and engineering, and external partners. We're in our foundational year — building the integration model, establishing the playbook, and launching our first wave of strategic partners. If you thrive in ambiguity, enjoy building from scratch, and want to shape a program that will scale to hundreds of partners, this is the team.

Basic Qualifications

  • 8+ years of specific technology domain areas (e.g. software development, cloud computing, systems engineering, infrastructure, security, networking, data & analytics) experience
  • 10+ years of IT development or implementation/consulting in the software or Internet industries experience
  • Experience communicating across technical and non-technical audiences, including executive level stakeholders or clients
  • Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, mathematics or equivalent, or 8+ years of IT development or implementation/consulting in the software or Internet industries experience
  • Must be fluent in English plus one of the following (Spanish, Portuguese, and/or Canadian French).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's degree in engineering or equivalent, or experience in software development
