Job Description

As a Human Resources intern , you will be given real responsibility from day one, partnering with key stakeholders to help shape the employee experience, strengthen our culture, and improve HR processes through innovative and digital solutions.

You will contribute to projects that simplify the way we work, enhance employee engagement, and support the organization's people agenda. This role offers a unique opportunity to combine technology, data, and human-centered thinking to create meaningful impact for employees and the business.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the development and execution of HR initiatives focused on employee experience, organizational culture, and engagement.
  • Assist in identifying opportunities to improve, simplify, and digitize HR processes and ways of working.
  • Participate in the design and implementation of innovative solutions that enhance efficiency while maintaining a people-first approach.
  • Collaborate with HR teams and business partners to support change management and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Assist in the adoption of digital tools, automation, and AI-enabled capabilities to optimize HR services and employee support.
  • Build relationships and collaborate effectively with stakeholders across the organization.
  • Embrace learning opportunities and contribute fresh perspectives, ideas, and solutions to HR challenges.

What You'll Bring

  • A passion for improving how people experience work.
  • A continuous improvement mindset and willingness to challenge the status quo.
  • An interest in leveraging digital tools, data, automation, and AI to create better employee and business outcomes.
  • The ability to balance innovation with empathy, ensuring that people remain at the center of every solution.

What We Offer

  • Responsibility from Day 1 – Own your work from the start.
  • Development Opportunities – Engage in a career path in HR by contributing to projects that enhance the employee experience, strengthen organizational culture, and improve HR processes.
  • Meaningful Work – Impact business results in a diverse and flexible environment.
  • Coaching and Mentorship – Receive ongoing support throughout your internship.
  • Collaborative Environment – Work in a dynamic team that values innovation.

  • Analytical mindset with strong problem-solving skills.
