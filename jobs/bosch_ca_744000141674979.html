企業概要

The history of Bosch is the history of car evolution. As a top-class automotive equipment supplier, Bosch Corporation supports market expansion/inroads domestically as well as globally. Bosch will continue to pursue forward-looking technologies such as the new generation of technologies for ecology, economy and safety as well as next-generation energy technologies, to keep cars as a part of the society and of the world. With its innovative technologies, Bosch Corporation has expanded into various fields such as consumer goods and building technologies. To become a key partner for every customer. Bosch will continue to drive for greater expectations.

[Job Description]

  • Construct and manage the operation of networks in Bosch's domestic locations (factories, warehouses, offices) in line with global concepts.
  • Manage IT rooms and support all domestic IT infrastructure.
  • Support the provision of comfortable IT services through system troubleshooting and consulting.
  • Responsible for asset planning, procurement, and management of IT infrastructure equipment, promoting strategic IT investments for the future.
  • Actively participate in global/regional projects and internal/external audits, contributing to overall organizational improvement.

[Current Challenges / Expected Role]

[必須の経験・スキル]

  • CCNA認定、または同様のレベルのネットワークスキルをお持ちであること。
  • 3年以上のネットワーク設計構築および運用経験があること。
  • ITILプロセス（インシデント/問題/変更管理など）の知識および経験があること。
  • チームワークを重視し、円滑なコミュニケーションを図れること。

[Required Experience and Skills]

  • CCNA certification or equivalent level of network skills.

  • Knowledge and experience in ITIL processes (incident/problem/change management, etc.).

  • Proficiency in both Japanese and English communication (Japanese: Native level, English: TOEIC 700+).

[Preferred Experience and Skills]

  • Experience in building SDN (Software-Defined Networking).
