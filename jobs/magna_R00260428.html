HR MANAGER

Position purpose

• create a collaborative environment by supporting organizational growth and ensuring optimal working conditions; • ensure that the organization has the necessary human resources to operate efficiently and competitively, contributing to the overall success of the company; • manage industrial relations and ensure that the company complies with all labor laws and regulations, including collective bargaining agreements and internal policies; • support management in defining and implementing the company strategy through HR policies aligned with business objectives; • lead organizational change processes, ensuring consistency, sustainability, and employee engagement

Job requirements

• degree in law or humanities, with a specialization in human resources; • knowledge of labor law and industrial relations (mandatory); • experience in manufacturing/production plant environments (preferred); • experience in reorganization and change management processes; • knowledge of HRIS systems (e.g., Workday) and HR data analysis tools; • excellent command of English, both written and spoken (mandatory); • good knowledge of Microsoft Office (mandatory); • experience in human resources (mandatory).

Responsibilities/duties

• ensure, in compliance with applicable regulations and in line with company policies, the proper definition, recruitment, and management of plant personnel, while ensuring and developing their professional capabilities; • ensure, in line with company policies, the implementation of employee development and merit-based programs within the plant; • support functional managers in resource management by providing guidance on organizational, managerial, and development matters; • ensure alignment between production needs and HR policies (workforce planning); • develop and manage compensation policies and competitive benefits packages to attract and retain talent, ensuring internal and external equity; • design, promote, and implement organizational initiatives aimed at increasing efficiency and optimizing resource utilization; • monitor and evaluate employee performance, providing constructive feedback and support to improve productivity and achieve business objectives; • ensure effective management of industrial relations and legal matters, including any disputes, fostering continuous improvement of the company climate; • ensure proper personnel administration in compliance with applicable laws, collective agreements, and internal policies; • monitor and analyze HR KPIs (absenteeism, turnover, labor cost, productivity), identifying improvement actions; • support management with reporting and analysis for strategic decision-making; • ensure an adequate security service and the protection of company assets; • collaborate with senior management to ensure the proper application of the employee charter principles; • ensure the efficiency and effectiveness of the plant’s occupational health service, maintaining high quality standards and compliance with applicable regulations; • manage change management processes at plant level (reorganizations, operational transformations, new organizational models); • support the implementation of new organizational structures and competency frameworks; • promote a positive and inclusive company culture, enhancing internal communication and fostering employee engagement and satisfaction; • coordinate and develop the plant HR team, ensuring service effectiveness and efficiency; • adopt a “management by walking around” approach; • promote a safe and healthy working environment, understanding, complying with, and enforcing policies that prohibit workplace violence and harassment; • maintain strict confidentiality of all written and verbal information handled within the HR function.

Key skills

• excellent communication skills at all organizational levels; • high level of confidentiality and discretion; • strong energy, motivation, and creativity; • proven leadership capabilities; • excellent organizational and planning skills; • strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities; • advanced analytical skills; • strong business acumen, with a clear understanding of business dynamics and economic drivers; • change management capability; • advanced negotiation skills, both in industrial relations and managerial contexts; • data-driven mindset; • ability to influence without formal authority; • emotional intelligence and conflict management skills.
