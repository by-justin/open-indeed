Customer-Focused Software Engineer

About Check Point

Mobile Security Product

The Mobile Solution Group builds Check Point’s mobile security platform, combining on-device protection with scalable backend services.

Our backend systems power policy management, integrations, data processing, and enforcement across mobile clients and multiple Check Point products, operating at scale and under strict reliability and security requirements.

Your Team

We are looking for a passionate and driven Customer Focus Developer Engineer to join our Backend development team. This unique role combines deep technical development work with direct customer engagement, giving engineers the opportunity to make a real impact on customer success and product quality.

In this role, you will work closely with customers, Support, Product, QA, and Engineering teams to investigate complex technical issues, resolve production challenges, and improve the overall customer experience. You will gain deep exposure to enterprise environments, large-scale backend systems, and real-world security challenges while helping shape the future of our products.

This is an exciting opportunity for engineers who enjoy solving complex problems, understanding customer environments, and working on meaningful technical challenges in a fast-paced and collaborative environment.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve complex customer-reported issues in production environments
  • Analyze logs, debug backend services, and perform root-cause investigations across distributed systems
  • Develop bug fixes, enhancements, and stability improvements for backend services and APIs
  • Work directly with customers to understand their environments, technical challenges, and business needs
  • Collaborate closely with Support, QA, Product Management, and Engineering teams to drive fast and effective issue resolution
  • Improve product reliability, performance, and customer experience through proactive engineering efforts
  • Participate in code reviews, testing, and continuous improvement of development processes
  • Contribute to maintaining high engineering standards in a large-scale, enterprise-grade security product

Skills and Requirements:

Technical:

  • B.Sc. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical experience
  • 1–3 years of experience in backend software development
  • Basic understanding of backend services and distributed systems concepts
  • Hands-on experience or academic background in backend programming using Python and/or Java
  • Familiarity with backend architectures, REST APIs, and database interactions
  • Experience working with cloud platforms or containerized environments through projects, internships, or coursework
  • Basic knowledge of relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL
  • Familiarity with Git, CI/CD concepts, and modern development workflows
  • Exposure to AI/ML technologies, including experience working with AI-powered tools, automation workflows, or integrating AI-based services and APIs
  • Familiarity with modern AI-assisted development practices and tools (such as code assistants, automated debugging, or AI-driven productivity solutions)

Qualifications

Personal:

  • Strong English communication skills, both written and oral
  • Excellent problem-solving ability with a methodical, analytical approach to diagnosing complex technical issues.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical details clearly to both customers and internal teams.
  • Customer-focused mindset with a sense of ownership and urgency in resolving issues that impact production environments.
  • Ability to switch seamlessly between quick bug-fix tasks and longer, deep-investigation assignments.
  • Collaborative team player who works well with Support, QA, Product, and Engineering while maintaining a high level of professionalism under pressure.
  • Curious and adaptive mindset with openness to learning emerging technologies, including AI-driven solutions and workflows
