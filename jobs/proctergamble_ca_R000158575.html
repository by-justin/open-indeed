Role details

We are currently hiring for a 4-month paid internship/co-op in Sales & Marketing from May 3, 2027 – Aug 20, 2027 at our Toronto headquarters.

What does the work look like?

During your 4-month work term in a Sales and Marketing Internship, you will have the opportunity to experience working in both areas of the business.  You will own multiple projects critical to the success of your brand team and largest retailers, leading work impacting the biggest brands in Canada.

Depending on the projects assigned you will get a variety of experiences, like:

  • Completing robust business analyses and developing marketing plans that contribute to the growth of our brands and business.
  • Conducting analysis related to the category, consumer, competition, and the market to develop insights, uncover trends and present recommendations based on your findings.
  • Learning and applying conceptual selling techniques and developing data-driven analyses and presentations.
  • Leading unique projects that involve selling and account management responsibilities with retailers. With coaching from your manager, you will play an important role with our Customer Teams, having a direct impact on the business.
  • Using local insights to influence marketing plans, including the creation of new initiatives, innovation, packaging, products, strategies and ideas.
  • Delivering the expected output of these projects by the end of your internship period (e.g. a report, an analysis, a presentation, a toolkit, an event etc.) and systematically attain the required resources to complete them.

As a P&G Intern, You'll…

  • Work on brands that reach millions of Canadians: From Day 1, you'll contribute to meaningful business challenges that shape the products and experiences consumers use every day.
  • Grow faster than you thought possible: Gain hands-on experience, personalized mentorship, and best-in-class training in a company built on developing talent from within.
  • Build a global network: Collaborate with interns, leaders, and teams across functions, categories, and geographies.
  • Get rewarded for your impact: Receive competitive pay and the opportunity to accelerate your career with the world's leading consumer goods company.
  • Launch your career with confidence: Successful interns may be considered for return offers and future career opportunities at P&G.
  • You may receive domestic relocation benefits.

Job Qualifications

We are Looking for Candidates Who:

  • Are currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, with planned graduation in mid-late 2028
  • Have no more than 3 years of prior full-time experience (excluding intern/coop work terms)
  • Can start the role on May 3rd, 2027 from Toronto, Ontario (domestic relocation benefits may apply)
  • If successful in internship, available to start full-time role within 12 months of internship end date.

À quoi ressemble le travail ?

Pendant votre stage de travail de 4 mois en ventes et marketing, vous aurez l'occasion de découvrir les deux aspects de notre entreprise. Vous serez responsable de plusieurs projets cruciaux pour le succès de votre équipe de marque et de nos plus grands détaillants, dirigeant un travail ayant un impact sur les plus grandes marques au Canada.

Selon les projets qui vous seront assignés, vous acquerrez une variété d'expériences, telles que :

  • Réaliser des analyses commerciales solides et élaborer des plans marketing qui contribuent à la croissance de nos marques et de notre entreprise.
  • Mener des analyses liées à la catégorie, au consommateur, à la concurrence et au marché afin de développer des perspectives, de découvrir des tendances et de présenter des recommandations basées sur vos conclusions.
  • Apprendre et appliquer des techniques de vente conceptuelles et élaborer des analyses et des présentations basées sur les données.
  • Diriger des projets uniques qui impliquent des responsabilités de vente et de gestion de comptes auprès des détaillants. Grâce à l'encadrement de votre gestionnaire, vous jouerez un rôle important au sein de nos équipes clients, ayant un impact direct sur l'entreprise.

  • Livrer le résultat attendu de ces projets d'ici la fin de votre période de stage (par exemple, un rapport, une analyse, une présentation, une boîte à outils, un événement, etc.) et obtenir systématiquement les ressources nécessaires pour les mener à bien."
