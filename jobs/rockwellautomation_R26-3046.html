Job Description

Rockwell Automation is looking for a dedicated Software Systems Engineer to develop high-quality MES, IIoT solutions that positively impact business operations. You will emphasize personal and professional development within an integrated global team, responsible for developing critical business systems. You will oversee successful system deployments and contribute to IIoT/Industry 4.0 solutions for smart connected operations. Using advanced troubleshooting abilities, the engineer will implement and refine DevOps tools. Collaboration with product owners and business analysts is important to understand requirements, specifications, and to develop, test, automate, and integrate applications or solutions that deliver value to Rockwell plants.

You will report to the Global Digital Manufacturing Architect & Build Lead

SCOPE: You will support internal manufacturing software Development for Rockwell Automation.

Main Responsibilities:

  • Develop essential business systems to ensure plant performance.
  • Support deployment and grow the manufacturing business systems.
  • Maintain server/platform reliability, scalability, and security. Facilitate upgrades to new technologies.
  • Troubleshoot, isolate, and resolve application, system, and technical issues.
  • Pursue opportunities to automate deployment and test scripts.
  • Monitor system needs to maintain healthy operations
  • Participate in Agile Scrum teams and related ceremonies.
  • Communicate across global cross-functional teams to provide and evolve solutions.

The Essentials – You Will Have

  • Bachelor's degree in engineering or computer science discipline required
  • Advanced English

The Preferred – You Might Also Have

  • 3 years' experience in application design, integration, testing, or DevOps support.
  • Demonstrated hands-on experience with C++, .Net, Python, JavaScript, Java, web, or related technologies.
  • Knowledge of API development and familiarity with REST API, web services, RabbitMQ.
  • Proficiency with relational dB, schema, including writing queries, scripts for data import/export
  • Experience with IIoT, data patterns, and AI concepts.
  • Commitment to learning and application of modern technologies.
  • Familiar with Printed Circuit Board Assembly Process, the Generic Equipment Model (GEM) / Connected Factory Exchange (CFX) protocol, and CAD tools
  • Travel requirements are less than 10%, based on need.
