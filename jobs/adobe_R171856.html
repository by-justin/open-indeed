Job Description

The Opportunity

At Adobe, our mission is to empower creatives, marketers, and IT professionals with application-based products that transform their daily work and accelerate progress towards their business goals. Our Technical Pre-Sales (TPS) organization plays a critical role in preparing customers to purchase Adobe’s integrated solutions by understanding their business needs, defining and validating their technical requirements, and demonstrating their ideal future state with Adobe technology.

The Enterprise Architecture (EA) team is within the TPS group. This role will be supporting the Enterprise Architecture (EA) team as an intern in TPS.

Learn More about Enterprise Architecture

Adobe’s Enterprise Architecture team sits within the TPS organization and works directly with Adobe customers to understand their current technology architecture and usage, evaluate its effectiveness as it relates to their larger business objectives, and create a future-state technology architecture to best support their long-term marketing vision.

As an Enterprise Architecture Intern, you will be learning from the best as you support experienced Enterprise Architects in their engagements with Adobe customers. You will collaborate on projects with Adobe’s top customers to provide guidance on strategies and technology to support digital experience growth and transformation.

You will learn how the team runs, how they interact with customers, real-world customer problems mapped to Adobe software solutions, and what key results they are responsible for building all while supporting and influencing the growing team’s operational strategy.

What You’ll Do

  • Learn to connect the dots between customers’ business problems and Adobe’s solution offerings to demonstrate value for our customers and their business
  • Collaborate with cross-organizational partners including sales teams, solution experts, digital strategists, customer success managers, and more to develop innovative solutions to help them through various stages in the sales cycle
  • Gain experience with conducting current-state technology analyses and developing high-impact recommendations for future-state technology
  • Learn to build custom architecture diagrams and phased implementations that showcase how Adobe technology can best support our customers’ objectives
  • Create presentations to best communicate technical information and architecture mapping to our customers’ business needs
  • Leverage Agentic AI technologies to enhance our current offerings and help to design the future for our customers’ evolving digital landscapes
  • Help brands fully capitalize on the era of digital & agentic transformation, so they can deliver personalized and meaningful experiences across all devices and channels for their customers by aligning to Adobe Customer Experience Orchestration solutions.

What You Need to Succeed

  • Currently enrolled full time and pursuing a Bachelor's or Graduatedegree graduating between December 2027 and June 2028
  • Ability to participate in a full-time internship between May-September 2027
  • Proven interest in business strategy, digital marketing, technology, analytics or experience design through coursework, extracurricular activities, or work/internship experience
  • Curiosity and/or application across agentic AI technologies, including agents, LLMs and AI tooling
  • Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills including conducting research, developing hypotheses, and synthesizing recommendations
  • Strong interpersonal skills with ability to work effectively with people at all levels in an organization
  • Ability to communicate complex ideas optimally both verbally and in writing; ability to deliver compelling customer presentations communicating technical concepts
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
  • Passion for new knowledge, resourceful and able to absorb concepts quickly
  • You enjoy challenges and learning about industry and technology trends
  • You bring a positive, can-do attitude to your work

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
