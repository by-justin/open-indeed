Job Description

At ABB, we help industries outrun - leaner and cleaner. Here, progress is an expectation - for you, your team, and the world. As a global market leader, we’ll give you what you need to make it happen. It won’t always be easy, growing takes grit. But at ABB, you’ll never run alone. Run what runs the world.

This role sits within ABB's Robotics business, a leading global robotics company. We're entering an exciting new chapter as we’ve announced the plan for SoftBank Group to acquire ABB Robotics. SoftBank is a globally recognized technology group and investor/operator focused on AI, robotics, and next-generation computing.  By joining us now, you’ll be part of a pioneering team shaping the future of robotics—working alongside world-class experts in a fast-moving, innovation-driven environment.

This Position reports to:

Global IS Dept. Manager, Data Analytics

In this role, you will have the opportunity to support the organization’s vision and strategic direction. Each day, you will apply IT knowledge and expertise to help solve complex technical or professional activities. You will also showcase your expertise by being a key resource, a definitive source of knowledge about services and solutions, to contribute your expertise to enhance organizational efficiency.

Your responsibilities

  • Architect, support and administer technical business applications and related services.
  • Design and manage IAM - Identity and Access Management  and UAM - User Access Management processes, including user onboarding, access requests, and role management for relevant applications
  • Architect, Configure and support interfaces, integrations, and data transfers between systems.
  • Contribute to application implementation, upgrades, and infrastructure-related projects.
  • Investigate and resolve application, connectivity, and access-related issues.
  • Collaborate with business users, infrastructure teams, and external vendors.

Qualifications for the role

  • Experience in application support, administration, or IT operations.
  • Experience with IS applications architecture and overall architecture governance
  • Knowledge of Identity and Access Management (IAM) and User Access Management (UAM).
  • Understanding of system integrations, interfaces, APIs, and data transfer processes.
  • Familiarity with hosting environments and networking concepts.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.

What we offer

  • A chance to work with business-critical applications supporting a global organization and contribute to the reliability and security of key digital services.
  • Opportunities to develop expertise in Identity and Access Management (IAM), application integrations, hosting environments, and enterprise technologies.
  • Participation in international projects involving application implementations, infrastructure upgrades, and process improvements.
  • Collaboration with experienced global teams, technical specialists, and business stakeholders across different countries and functions.
