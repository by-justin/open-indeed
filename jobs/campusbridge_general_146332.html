Job Description

Join a company that is passionately committed to the pursuit of a better world through positive change. With more than 70 years of business and technical expertise in mining , energy , and infrastructure , our 10,000 colleagues in 150 countries tirelessly work toward bettering the industries in which we operate. With practical solutions that are safe, innovative and sustainable, we think globally while acting locally. Are you looking for an opportunity to join a diverse group of professionals who are committed to remaining exceptional? We look forward to hearing from you.

Environment , People, and Place shape projects that are sustainable, resilient, and people-focused. We bring environmental intelligence and community-centered design to every stage, creating places that thrive socially, economically, and ecologically.

Looking to take the next step in your career?

Hatch is seeking a highly motivated Environmental Scientist / Engineer-in-Training new graduate to join our Environment, People, and Place (EP&P) team, with interests in environmental and social impact assessments, urban planning and design, and community and indigenous engagement. Reporting to the Regional Director, EP&P, and receiving guidance and direction from project managers and other intermediate and senior specialists you will be working with, you will have the opportunity to collaborate with planners, designers, engineers, environmentalists, architects, scientists, biologists, ecologists, and economists across our global offices, while working from one of our Calgary, AB location.

We're entrepreneurs with a technical soul. Our flat organizational structure creates an atmosphere of openness and participation. We build practical solutions that are safe, innovative, and sustainable.

As part of the EP&P team, you will:

  • Be given opportunities to contribute to complex projects around the world in meaningful ways
  • Work on problems and gain experience relevant to your field of study in an office and/or field environment
  • Collaborate with diverse teams locally and globally
  • Work in an exciting environment where you can apply your academic learning daily
  • Benefit from informal mentorship from some of the leading minds in environmental planning, impact assessments, engagement, and urban planning
  • Participate in local Lunch and Learns, Hatch Charity Events, and more to develop your skills
  • Join charitable, social, and sports activities with other Hatch employees

As the successful candidate, you will:

  • Contribute to baseline ecological or environmental studies, environmental assessments, environmental management strategies, and environmental plans including research, report writing, field work, and stakeholder and regulatory consultation.
  • Collect and synthesize scientific literature, existing data sets and secondary source material for technical reports.
  • Identify potential environmental impacts caused by land use changes and recommend suitable mitigation strategies to avoid and / or reduce potential impacts.
  • Support permitting and approvals related to the natural environment (e.g., Environmental Protection Act , Fisheries Act , Impact Assessment Act )
  • Maintain an up-to-date knowledge of existing and emerging regulatory requirements, wildlife and vegetation assessment methodologies, and best practices.
  • Coordinate work with other environmental specialists (e.g., noise, air, archaeology, heritage, fisheries, terrestrial ecologists, etc.)
  • Deliver high-quality environmental services to agreed budgets and deadlines

Skills and Qualifications

You bring to the role:

  • A bachelor's degree or advanced diploma in Biology, Environmental Engineering, Environmental Studies, Environmental Planning, Geography, Ecological Restoration, Environmental Management & Assessment, Environmental Technician / Technologist, Natural Resources Technician, or similar
  • 0-2 years of experience working in a fast-paced, consulting environment; experience working in the field is an asset
  • Knowledge of natural heritage policies, survey protocols, environmental legislation, and environmental and social impact assessment processes; knowledge of environmental and social due diligence is an asset
  • Flexibility to travel periodically
  • Excellent verbal and written communications skills and demonstrated ability to develop personal networks and work collaboratively
  • Ability to work in a multi-disciplinary and integrated team environment
  • Interest in continuous learning and commitment to personal accountability
  • Proficiency in French is an advantage
  • Experience communicating with design and engineering teams is an advantage
  • Eligible to hold CAN-CISEC and MTO RAQs certification is an advantage

Competencies (knowledge, skill, attributes):

  • Ability to think critically and creatively to create solutions for our clients' toughest challenges
  • Quick learning, interest in taking on new challenges, high level of curiosity, and willingness to ask questions
  • Collaboration and being a team player with excellent interpersonal skills
  • Excellent organizational skills with attention to accuracy and detail
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Strong mathematical and data analysis skills (including using Excel)
  • Performing geospatial analyses using GIS software is an advantage
  • Canadian flora and fauna identification skills is an advantage

We look forward to hearing from you! Please submit your resume and optionally, a short cover letter explaining why you're passionate about Environmental services or any additional materials (such as a portfolio, project work, authored articles) that could help us understand you better through your application.
