Your mission

You'll support our sales teams and customers across Belgium and Luxembourg in understanding and deploying distribution automation solutions that modernize electrical grids. From your first customer conversation to project completion, you'll own the full technical and marketing narrative — backed by a powerhouse team and world-class technology.

Your day-to-day includes:

  • Delivering accurate and competitive technical documentation by coordinating with engineering, technical, product management, and software teams.
  • Managing all aspects of solution positioning and bid support, including technical reviews, risk assessments, and participation in customer presentations.
  • Building strong relationships with utilities, infrastructure operators, and system integrators — contributing to an excellent customer experience through technical depth and market insight.
  • Strengthening sales efficiency by providing product knowledge, training materials, market trends, and competitive intelligence on distribution automation solutions.
  • Translating complex customer requirements and grid modernization challenges into clear internal guidelines and go-to-market strategies.

What makes you right for this role?

We're looking for someone with the technical confidence to engage with utilities and the marketing fluency to position advanced grid solutions. Someone who's energized by the intersection of software, automation, and electrical infrastructure — and who understands the business drivers behind digital grid transformation. You're an independent decision maker. When to schedule a customer workshop or where you'll be working? It's your call.

You are also:

  • A technical professional with 3–5+ years of experience in product marketing, technical sales support, or business development — preferably in electrical systems, grid automation, software solutions, or related infrastructure sectors.
  • Someone who thrives at the intersection of technical depth and commercial thinking — comfortable translating software and automation concepts into customer business value.
  • Fluent in Dutch and English — French is a strong plus.
  • Familiar with distribution automation, SCADA systems, grid management software, or motivated to quickly develop expertise in these technologies.
  • A passionate collaborator on electrification and grid modernization projects that accelerate the energy transition and improve grid resilience.
  • Strong communication skills across diverse stakeholder groups — from engineers to C-suite decision makers.

More about us

ABB's Distribution Solutions Division facilitates the efficient and reliable distribution, protection, and control of electrical power by improving power quality and strengthening grid resilience. Distribution Automation is a key growth area within the Division, combining advanced software, monitoring, and control technologies to enable utilities and infrastructure operators to modernize their grids and optimize operations in real time.

The Division's portfolio includes distribution automation software, SCADA systems, intelligent communication networks, advanced monitoring and diagnostics, and grid-edge technologies that integrate distributed energy resources and enable predictive maintenance. These solutions support utilities and operators in achieving safe, smart, and sustainable electrical grid operations while enhancing reliability and operational efficiency.

With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions at its core, we deliver industry-specific platforms and services for utilities, industry, and critical infrastructure segments globally.
