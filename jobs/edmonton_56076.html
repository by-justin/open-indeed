Job Description

Drive Strategic Impact as Our Senior Intergovernmental Strategist

The City is seeking an exceptional strategic leader to join the Intergovernmental and Regional Affairs team during a pivotal era of growth and regional alignment. Operating within a high-performing strategic portfolio model, the Senior Intergovernmental Strategist serves a core role in translating the City’s Government Relations Strategy into tangible achievements.

In this position, you will lead proactive, corporate-wide advocacy efforts that ensure the City commands a unified, influential voice across federal, provincial, regional, and intermunicipal partnerships. You will be instrumental in advancing Council’s highest-priority outcomes—driving progress across Economic Development, Growth Management, Safety, and Quality Services . If you are a sophisticated strategist eager to shape Edmonton’s government relations landscape  and build high-value partnerships, we invite you to lead the way.

What will you do?

  • Serve as the dedicated corporate lead for a specific Council Priority portfolio, aligning city objectives with corresponding provincial and federal ministerial structures to advance strategic goals

  • Drive corporate-wide, proactive advocacy campaigns to secure favorable policy alignment and essential provincial and federal funding for major City initiatives

  • Deliver politically sharp, outcome-focused advice and high-level briefing materials (reports, memos, briefings) to the Executive Leadership Team, and City Council

  • Facilitate and strengthen strategic relationships with senior federal, provincial, and regional leaders—including Ministers, Deputy Ministers, and elected officials—by preparing executive briefing packages and official correspondence

  • Represent the City of Edmonton independently or alongside senior leadership at critical intergovernmental and regional forums

  • Lead the City’s participation in provincial and federal policy consultations and direct external government relations consultants to maximize diplomatic reach and advocacy impact

  • Formulate proactive government relations strategies, tactical plans, and high-impact advocacy materials that translate complex municipal initiatives into clear policy wins

  • Monitor and analyze political, legislative, and media landscapes—including provincial and federal budgets—to pinpoint strategic opportunities and mitigate emerging risks to municipal interests

  • Track regional and national developments to identify emerging intergovernmental issues, leading corporate response strategies when swift action is required

  • Direct intergovernmental research projects grounded in academic literature, industry trends, and municipal best practices to support evidence-based policy positioning Qualifications

  • Completed University degree in Political Sciences, Economics, Sociology, Public Administration, Business Administration, Urban Planning, Arts or a related field

  • Minimum of 7 years of progressively responsible experience in the municipal, provincial or federal levels of government including previous experience in working with elected officials

Assets:

  • Certification in project management, or relevant coursework/certifications in government relations

Skills required for success:

  • Political & Municipal Acumen: Deep expertise in municipal operations and intergovernmental structures, backed by keen familiarity with political dynamics across provincial, federal, and regional orders of government
  • Strategic Leadership & Agility : Proven ability to take initiative, navigate complex political landscapes, and adapt rapidly to shifting strategic priorities and emerging trends
  • Relationship Building & Influence: Advanced interpersonal skills to establish, nurture, and sustain high-value partnerships across diverse government departments, agencies, and regional bodies
  • Analytical & Communication Mastery: Exceptional capacity to analyze complex legislative and media environments, identify cross-jurisdictional opportunities, and synthesize technical concepts into clear, high-impact communications
  • Alignment to our Cultural Commitments and Leadership Competencies ( https://bit.ly/4brRrXm )
  • Embracing a culture of equity, diversity, reconciliation, and inclusion
  • Applicants may be tested

Work Environment:

  • This position may be eligible for a hybrid work arrangement with the flexibility to work from both home and the worksite as per the Letter of Understanding between the City of Edmonton and Civic Service Union 52.
  • This position is eligible for the EDO program (Earned Day Off).
  • The weekly hours of work for this position are currently under review and may change at a future time. Any changes will be made in accordance with the City of Edmonton/Civic Service Union 52 collective agreement and the incumbent will be notified in advance.
