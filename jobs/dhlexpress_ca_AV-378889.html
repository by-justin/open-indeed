We are looking for a highly experienced and hands-on Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) who can work across the complete technology stack, from frontend and backend development to infrastructure, DevOps, cloud, and AI-enabled engineering.

The ideal candidate is an engineering all-rounder with deep expertise in enterprise full-stack development and a strong ability to translate complex business requirements into scalable, secure, and production-ready solutions. The candidate will work closely with product teams, engineering teams, business stakeholders, customers, and platform specialists to design, build, integrate, and deploy intelligent enterprise solutions.

This is not purely advisory, AI, or proof-of-concept role. The successful candidate is expected to remain highly hands-on, contribute production-quality code, resolve complex technical challenges, and take end-to-end ownership of solution delivery.

The role will involve rapid prototyping and iterative solution development, often in environments where requirements are evolving. Candidates should be comfortable navigating evolving requirements, collaborating with stakeholders, validating ideas through prototypes, and turning concepts into scalable enterprise solutions.

  1. Full-Stack Solution Development
  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable enterprise applications across frontend and backend technologies.
  • Build secure APIs, microservices, and user interfaces using Java, Spring Boot, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Angular, and Node.js.
  • Ensure high standards of code quality, performance, security, and maintainability.
  1. Enterprise Product Development and Delivery
  • Own the end-to-end software delivery lifecycle, from solution design through production deployment and support.
  • Design and implement solutions that integrate seamlessly with enterprise applications, platforms, and data ecosystems.
  • Deliver production-ready solutions that meet enterprise requirements for scalability, reliability, and compliance.
  1. AI-Assisted Engineering and Intelligent Automation
  • Leverage AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot to improve engineering productivity and solution quality.
  • Apply AI, traditional automation, or hybrid approaches based on business needs, risk, and technical suitability.
  • Validate AI-generated outputs through appropriate testing, governance, and human oversight.
  1. Agentic AI and LLM Solutions
  • Design and implement AI-enabled solutions using LLMs, Agentic AI, RAG, prompt engineering, and tool-calling frameworks.
  • Integrate AI capabilities into enterprise applications and business workflows.
  • Ensure responsible, secure, and reliable use of AI technologies in production environments.
  1. DevOps, Cloud and Infrastructure Engineering
  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automated deployments, and cloud-native solutions.
  • Deploy and operate applications using Docker, Kubernetes, and major cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  • Support monitoring, performance optimization, troubleshooting, and operational excellence.
  1. Technical Leadership and Collaboration
  • Collaborate effectively with developers, architects, product teams, business stakeholders, and customers.
  • Provide technical leadership, contribute to architecture decisions, and help resolve complex technical challenges.
  • Mentor team members and promote engineering best practices and continuous improvement.
  1. Product and Engineering Feedback
  • Identify opportunities to improve products, platforms, and engineering processes through real-world delivery experience.
  • Contribute reusable patterns, automation, and best practices that accelerate future implementations.
  • Drive continuous innovation across software engineering, DevOps, and AI capabilities.

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
