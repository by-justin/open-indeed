Company Description

With its five product lines of compact hydraulics Bosch Rexroth Oil Control SpA , with factories in the province of Modena and Reggio Emilia and over 1,300 employees, has been producing and marketing compact hydraulic components for multiple applications for over 40 years, from aerial platforms to cranes, earth-moving machinery, agricultural machinery, industrial applications.

What characterizes Bosch Rexroth Oil Control S.p.A. is the ability to study customized solutions in relation to each specific customer requirement. For this reason, the products of Bosch Rexroth Oil Control S.p.A. they are synonymous with quality and represent a guarantee for the global market.

In industrial hydraulics, Bosch Rexroth is actively shaping the transition from conventional to intelligent and connected hydraulics. This opens up enormous potential for the easy and economical integration of machines and systems into modern industrial environments.

Our R&D department based in Nonantola (MO) is a center of global competence for Compact Hydraulic products.

Job Description

Are you ready to join Bosch Rexroth Oil Control SpA?

Are you a final-year student, recent graduate, or soon-to-be graduate in Mechanical Engineering or a related field ?

This is your opportunity to challenge yourself, showcase your skills, and take the first important step towards an exciting professional career at Bosch Rexroth Oil Control SpA .

Where? Bosch Rexroth Oil Control SpA – Nonantola (MO), Italy

Your professional future starts here!

If you are passionate about the industrial sector , curious, motivated, and eager to develop both technical and commercial skills , this could be the opportunity you have been waiting for.

We are looking for young talents ready to embark on a professional development journey in a technical-commercial environment , with the goal of building strong expertise in hydraulics and industrial applications .

What do we offer?

A 24-month professional development program through an apprenticeship contract in Bosch Rexroth Oil Control SpA – Nonantola (MO), designed to help you develop technical, application, and commercial skills through the support of experienced professionals and hands-on experience in the field.

At the end of the program, based on the skills acquired, your demonstrated aptitude, and organizational needs, you may pursue one of the following career paths:

APPLICATION ENGINEER

Key responsibilities will include:

  • Technical Support: analyze customer requirements and size and select the most suitable hydraulic components for different applications.
  • Solution Development: collaborate with the Engineering Department to define and customize product configurations.
  • Technical & Commercial Offers: prepare technical documentation and preliminary drawings to support commercial offers.

SALES ENGINEER

Key responsibilities will include:

  • New Business Development: identify and approach potential customers and distributors.
  • Customer Relationship Management: act as the main point of contact for customers, understanding their technical needs and application requirements.
  • Commercial Offers: prepare commercial quotations, define standard prices and terms, and support delivery planning in collaboration with the Technical Office.

Qualifications

What distinguished you?

  • Degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related field
  • English language proficiency (B2 level) and Italian language proficiency (C1 level)
  • Willingness to travel in Italy and abroad
  • Category B driving licence
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
  • Interest in the industrial sector and in developing both technical and commercial expertise
