Job Information Job Title: Leadership Development and Strategy Consultant Job Requisition ID: 87406 Ministry: Public Service Commission Location: Edmonton Full or Part-Time: Full Time Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent Scope: Open Competition Closing Date: September 21, 2026 Classification: Program Services 4 Excluded (PS 4E) Salary: $3,148.2 to $4,126.82 bi-weekly ($82,168 - $107,710/year) The Public Service Commission provides human resource services to the Government of Alberta. Within the Leadership and Talent Management Branch, we help build leadership capacity across the Alberta Public Service, with a focus on executive and senior leadership development. We are strengthening our leadership development function to design, modernize, and deliver enterprise strategies, frameworks, and flagship programs that support leadership capability, readiness, and long-term public service capacity. Role Responsibilities Are you excited by the opportunity to help build leadership capacity across the Alberta Public Service through practical strategies, frameworks, programs, and tools that support leaders at all levels — from emerging leaders to senior executives? Leadership capacity is essential to a strong, responsive public service. The Leadership and Talent Management Branch is strengthening its corporate approach to leadership development. We design and deliver strategies, frameworks, programs, tools, and learning experiences that support current and future leaders at key points in their development. As a Leadership Development and Strategy Consultant, you will help translate workforce priorities, leadership frameworks, talent insights, and evidence into scalable, implementation-ready solutions. Your work will connect leadership development with talent management, performance, succession, and workforce planning priorities across government. What You’ll Do: As a member of this team, you will: Design, develop, and modernize leadership development strategies, frameworks, programs, learning resources, and supporting tools. Lead projects from concept through approval, design, implementation, evaluation, and continuous improvement. Use workforce, talent, assessment, program, and environmental scan data to identify leadership gaps and emerging development needs. Prepare briefing notes, presentations, reports, business cases, and recommendations to support senior leader decision-making. Plan and lead stakeholder engagement activities, including consultations, working groups, focus groups, and project meetings. Partner with senior leaders, HR portfolio teams, Talent Management, Executive Search, vendors, and corporate services partners. Develop communications, launch materials, project and implementation plans, and change supports to encourage awareness, adoption, and participation. Monitor progress, manage risks and issues, track deliverables, and coordinate activities across multiple partners and workstreams.
Support program evaluation by collecting feedback, assessing outcomes, and identifying opportunities for improvement. Provide advice and consultation to leaders, HR partners, and stakeholders on leadership development approaches, tools, and practices. Why This Work Matters This work helps ensure leaders across the Alberta Public Service have the support they need to navigate complexity, develop their teams, and deliver results for Albertans. By connecting leadership development with talent, performance, succession, and workforce planning priorities, this role contributes to a stronger and more prepared public service. What We’re Looking For We're looking for a strategic and practical consultant who can move initiatives from concept to implementation. The ideal candidate combines expertise in leadership development with strong project management, stakeholder engagement, and implementation skills. Role Responsibilities (Continued) What You’ll Gain This role offers meaningful exposure and growth for professionals who want enterprise-wide impact. You will: Help shape leadership development approaches, tools, and programs used across the APS. Work on high-impact initiatives that support leadership readiness, development, and long-term public service capacity. Gain exposure to enterprise strategy, executive development, talent management, and public sector leadership transformation. Contribute to work that supports leaders, strengthens organizations, and benefits Albertans. Join Us If you are energized by complex systems work, enterprise impact and building leadership capacity for the future of public service, this is a rare opportunity to do meaningful work at scale. APS Competencies Competencies are behaviors that are essential to reach our goals in serving Albertans. We encourage you to have an in depth understanding of the competencies that are required for this opportunity and to be prepared to demonstrate them during the recruitment process. Systems Thinking: Integrates broader context, organizational trends, workforce priorities and enterprise risks into leadership development strategies and program planning. Agility: Identifies and manages change, anticipates obstacles and adjusts approaches in uncertain or shifting environments while keeping enterprise outcomes in view. Drive for Results: Takes responsibility for complex outcomes, manages scope and timelines, and ensures leadership development initiatives deliver practical value for the APS. Build Collaborative Environments: Involves senior leaders, HR partners, Talent Management, vendors and other stakeholders to build shared understanding and support for enterprise approaches. Creative Problem Solving: Uses evidence, consultation, learning design, analytics and stakeholder input to improve complex leadership development systems and address emerging needs. A practical understanding of leadership development, adult learning, talent management, and/or current HR best practices.
