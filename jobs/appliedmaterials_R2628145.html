  As a Mechanical Engineer, you’ll leverage your strong foundation in physics, mathematics, and materials science to process and resolve mechanical problems, design and implement custom mechanical tooling and associated processes, and enable the handling, assembly, and disassembly of parts, components, sub-assemblies, and final assemblies throughout the product life cycle. You'll be responsible for designing, developing, and modifying mechanical engineering layouts, schematics, and specifications. You will oversee manufacturing and installation processes, ensuring adherence to industry standards and regulations-- and you'll coordinate the procurement and assembly of mechanical components and equipment for design projects, identifying sources of critical parts and subsystems to resolve technical issues.

  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Mechanical architecture: Define and own the mechanical package, including mobile platform integration, robot mounting structure, FOUP load port and enclosure design.
  • Robot integration & automation: Select robots and end-effectors, run kinematics, reach and payload studies, and resolve hand-off requirements jointly with the tool team.
  • Docking & levelling: Develop docking and auto-levelling mechanisms, including vibration control through the docking sequence.
  • Wafer-handling environment: Engineer FFU/ETU airflow enclosures and cleanliness-compliant structures suited to wafer transfer duty.
  • Analysis (FEA): Run structural, modal, vibration and thermal FEA across chassis, robot mounts and mechanical interfaces, and close out design gaps ahead of build release.
  • CAD & GD&T: Deliver large-assembly 3D models, GD&T-controlled drawings to ASME Y14.5 and tolerance stack-ups in NX / Inventor / SolidWorks, maintaining full PDM/PLM discipline.
  • DFM & source: Drive DFM/DFA and design reviews, own the BOM, and support supplier selection, quotations and long-lead procurement.
  • Build & qualification: Lead prototype build, assembly and debug, and qualify docking, levelling, battery/charging integration and throughput through to tool deployment.
  • Required Skills (Must-Have)
  • Robotics & automation: Hands-on industrial robot integration, kinematics, reach/payload studies, end-effector design and automation cell build-up.
  • AGV integration: AGV chassis integration covering drive, navigation, battery and charging.
  • Wafer handling: Strong working proficiency in wafer-handling environments and their cleanliness requirements.
  • FEA: Structural, modal, vibration and thermal simulation of frames, mounts and moving assemblies, with design decisions closed out from the results.
  • GD&T: ASME Y14.5 dimensioning and tolerance stack-up analysis to secure repeatable docking and alignment.
  • CAD: Ownership of large assemblies in NX / Inventor / SolidWorks, including drawing release and design documentation for procurement.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to lead design reviews and collaborate across global teams.
  • Qualifications & Experience:
  • B.E./B.Tech or M.E./M.Tech in Mechanical or Mechatronics Engineering
  • 8–12 years of experience across semiconductor capital equipment, robotics or factory automation.
  • Proven ownership of at least one system taken from concept through prototype build to deployment.
  • Effective cross-functional working with electrical, controls/software, safety and tool-integration teams.
  • Preferred to Have
  • SPM domain expertise.
  • Exposure to SEMI S2/S8 safety standards and interlock-aware mechanical design.
  • AGV/AMR deployment experience inside a fab or other high-cleanliness environment.
  • DFMEA, supplier development and cross-site program execution.
