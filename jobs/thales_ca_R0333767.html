Job Description

Location: Mumbai, India

Thales is a global technology leader trusted by governments, institutions, and enterprises to tackle their most demanding challenges. From quantum applications and artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and 6G innovation, our solutions empower critical decisions rooted in human intelligence. Operating at the forefront of aerospace and space, cybersecurity and digital identity, we’re driven by a mission to build a future we can all trust.

Present in India since 1953, Thales is headquartered in Noida and has other operational offices and sites spread across Delhi, Gurugram, Bengaluru and Mumbai, among others. Over 2200 employees are working with Thales and its joint ventures in India. Since the beginning, Thales has been playing an essential role in India’s growth story by sharing its technologies and expertise in Defence, Aerospace and Cyber & Digital sectors. Thales has two engineering competence centres in India - one in Noida focused on Cyber & Digital business, while the one in Bengaluru focuses on hardware, software and systems engineering capabilities for both the civil and defence sectors, serving global needs. The Group has also established an MRO (Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul) facility in Gurugram to provide comprehensive avionics maintenance and repair services to Indian airlines and support the growth of the local aviation industry.

Role Summary:

The Cyber Security Sales Specialist is responsible for driving revenue growth across the Thales Cyber Security Products portfolio by developing strategic customer relationships, identifying new business opportunities, and working closely with account teams, channel partners and technical specialists.

The role focuses on helping India’s largest enterprises, financial institutions, government agencies, telecommunications providers and digital-native organisations protect their most critical data, applications and identities.

Success will be measured through new business acquisition, expansion within existing customers, pipeline development, forecast accuracy and strategic account engagement.

Key Responsibilities:

Revenue Growth

  • Achieve and exceed annual bookings and revenue targets.
  • Build and maintain a high-quality sales pipeline with at least 3x coverage.
  • Drive both new logo acquisition and expansion opportunities.
  • Deliver accurate monthly and quarterly forecasts.

Strategic Account Development

  • Develop executive relationships with CISOs, CIOs, CTOs, Heads of Security, Risk and Infrastructure.
  • Create account plans with the sales team for strategic customers.
  • Position Thales CSP as a trusted cybersecurity advisor.
  • Drive long-term platform adoption across multiple security domains.

Partner Development

  • Work closely with distributors and strategic partners.

  • Increase partner-led pipeline and revenue.

Sales Execution

  • Execute value-based and outcome-focused sales engagements.

Customer Success
