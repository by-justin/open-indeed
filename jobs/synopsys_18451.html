Job Description

We are

Synopsys is the leader in engineering solutions from silicon to systems, enabling customers to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. We deliver industry-leading silicon design, IP, simulation and analysis solutions, and design services. We partner closely with our customers across a wide range of industries to maximize their R&D capability and productivity, powering innovation today that ignites the ingenuity of tomorrow.

You are

You are an engineer who finds real satisfaction in helping others solve challenging problems.

You have experience in high-frequency electromagnetic analysis, including signal integrity (SI) troubleshooting for high-speed PCBs and optimization of RF components. When simulation results do not match measurement data, you can evaluate the situation from multiple angles, develop hypotheses, systematically isolate the root cause, and drive the problem toward resolution.

You take the time to understand the challenges your customers are truly trying to solve and work with them to determine the best path forward. Sometimes customers may not yet be able to clearly articulate the underlying issue. In those situations, you can uncover the real problem through thoughtful discussion and recommend the most effective approach.

You are also able to explain complex technical challenges in a clear and practical way. You bring fresh perspectives, help customers see new possibilities, and guide them toward the next step.

You are organized, accountable, and execution-focused. You manage opportunities effectively, follow through on commitments, and pay attention to the details that build trust with customers.

Working directly with customers energizes you, and helping them succeed is what motivates you every day. You enjoy continuously learning new technologies and tools while developing your own skills. Most importantly, you take pride in knowing that your work helps engineers around the world create innovative products and turn new ideas into reality.

あなたは、お客様や仲間の課題を解決することに大きなやりがいを感じるエンジニアです。

高速PCBのシグナルインテグリティ（SI）課題のトラブルシューティングやRF部品の最適化など、高周波電磁界解析に関する経験をお持ちです。シミュレーション結果と測定データが一致しない場合でも、状況をさまざまな角度から分析し、仮説を立てながら原因を体系的に切り分け、解決へ導くことができます。

あなたは、お客様が本当に解決したい課題を理解するために時間を惜しみません。お客様自身が課題を明確に言語化できていない場合でも、対話を通じて本質的な課題を整理し、最適なアプローチを提案することができます。

また、複雑な技術課題をわかりやすく、実践的な形で説明することが得意です。新たな視点や気づきを提供し、お客様が次の一歩を踏み出せるよう支援します。

あなたは責任感と実行力を兼ね備えています。案件やプロジェクトを適切に管理し、約束したことを確実に実行します。細部にも目を配りながら、お客様との信頼関係を築いていくことができます。

お客様と直接関わり、その成功に貢献することがあなたの原動力です。新しい技術やツールを学び続けることを楽しみ、自らの成長につなげることができます。そして何より、自分の仕事が世界中のエンジニアのものづくりを支え、新しいアイデアを革新的な製品へと変えることに貢献していることに誇りを感じられる方です。

What You'll Be Doing

  • Engage directly with customers working on chip, package, and PCB designs to diagnose signal integrity, power integrity, and EMC challenges and recommend the right simulation approach using Ansys HFSS, SIwave and Q3D

  • Train customer engineering teams on Ansys simulation products, from first-time users learning the interface to experienced engineers looking to optimize runtime or accuracy

  • Collaborate with the Synopsys product development team to communicate customer requirements, test beta releases, and provide feedback that shapes future product roadmaps

  • Support the sales team in technical evaluations, competitive benchmarks, and proof-of-concept projects that help customers understand the value of moving to or expanding their use of our solutions

  • 半導体チップ、パッケージ、PCB設計に携わるお客様と直接連携し、シグナルインテグリティ（SI）、パワーインテグリティ（PI）、EMCに関する技術課題を分析します。Ansys HFSS、SIwave、Q3Dなどを活用しながら、お客様に最適なシミュレーションアプローチを提案し、課題解決を支援します。

  • お客様の設計チームに対して、電磁界製品の技術指導やトレーニングを実施します。初めて利用するユーザーから経験豊富なエンジニアまで、それぞれのレベルに応じて活用方法やベストプラクティスを提供し、設計・解析業務の効率化と精度向上を支援します。

  • 製品開発チームと連携し、お客様の要求や市場ニーズを製品開発へフィードバックします。新機能やベータ版の評価を通じて、将来の製品ロードマップの策定にも貢献します。

What You'll Need

  • Bachelor’s, Master’s, or Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field, with a focus on electromagnetics, signal integrity (SI), power integrity (PI), EMC, or related disciplines.

  • 3 to 5 years of hands-on experience in signal integrity, power integrity, EMC, or related areas, preferably within a design, validation, or product development environment.

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with customers and colleagues. Experience working in an international environment or using English in a professional setting is a plus.

  • Ability to work independently while managing multiple customer projects simultaneously and consistently meeting commitments, deadlines, and deliverables.

  • Experience with Ansys software or other commercial CAE, CAD, and EDA tools is a strong advantage.

  • Willingness and ability to travel domestically up to 50% of the time.
