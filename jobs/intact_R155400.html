About the role

Launching your career at Intact means joining a diverse community of experts that trust and value your skills in an industry that is constantly evolving. Here, you’ll receive mentorship and coaching in a dynamic hands-on environment. You’ll drive innovation and make a meaningful impact in our organization, for our customers, and across our communities. Here, your career will take off 🚀

We are looking for Operations and Planning Business Analysts to join our growing team!

What you’ll do here:

  • Provide proactive coordination and administrative support to PMs and leadership, including workshop/meeting prep, consolidating outcomes, driving follow-ups, and supporting team engagement activities.
  • Document requirements obtained from the business community using defined templates.
  • Analyze information obtained from various sources, detail the high-level view, and provide users with recommendations to their problems.
  • Conduct analysis and develop comparisons/summaries to support informed, data-driven decision-making.
  • Assist team in improving productivity and reducing risks and high costs through effective analysis.
  • Identify the need for change to assess and communicate the impacts.
  • Ensure the link between the business units, the technology teams, and the support team.
  • Support the Quality Assurance team during Test Planning and Execution

What you bring to the table:

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Business Management, Business Strategy, Project Management, or anything relevant;
  • Highly develop analytical skills facilitating problem solving.
  • Demonstrated ability to cope with change effectively.
  • Client-oriented.
  • Must have demonstrated initiative and autonomy.
  • Skills for time and priority management.
  • Can manage multiple initiatives at the same time.
  • Team spirit.
  • Good communication skills (oral and written).
  • Experience in Functional analysis.
  • Intermediate/advanced knowledge in micro-computing (word processing, spreadsheets).
  • Knowledge of the insurance industry (asset)
  • Available 35 hours a week from January 4 to April 23, 2027
  • Must be an active student during your internship and/or returning to school in next semester following your internship

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
