Your role

While working in the Hydropower and Geotechnical Division at AtkinsRéalis, you could put your skills to work in one of these key areas:

  • Hydropower: Design, test, and support electrical systems used in hydropower facilities while gaining hands-on experience in renewable energy projects.
  • Geotechnical: Assess ground conditions and design slopes, retaining structures, excavations, tunnels, and underground infrastructure to support safe, resilient, and sustainable construction projects.
  • Hydro Civil Structures: Design and deliver water infrastructure projects, including hydroelectric facilities, dams, waterways, roads, stormwater systems, and shoreline protection solutions, from planning and design through construction support.

About you

  • New graduate with a bachelor's degree in Geotechnical, Civil/Structural, Electrical or Mechanical engineering (Graduation in 2027).
  • Excellent communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to solve problems creatively and effectively.
  • A valid driver's license may be required for some positions.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
