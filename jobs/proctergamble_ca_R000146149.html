Job Description

🗓️ We are looking for candidates available to start as of September 2026 and onwards.

Ready to fuel the success of world-class brands like Dash, Gillette, Pantene and Oral-B that millions of consumers can’t live without? Ever dreamt of a career where you are fully supported by best-in-class training and development to progress to leadership? Want to work for a company consistently recognized as one of the top graduate employers?

Where to start from? In one of the world’s best  Strategic Sales I nternship  program! From data to winning propositions, you will gain the trust of a variety of customers across all industries.

As a P&G Sales intern, you will get the opportunity to:

  • Build positive relationships with customers and effectively collaborate with them in critical business areas: promotion support, developing the right assortment, shelving, pricing and merchandising strategies;
  • Contribute to the development of sales concepts, product branding and go-to-market strategies and tools;
  • Be involved in the design business plans that will deliver sales and market share objectives;
  • Help customers to develop programs which will build the business for them and for us;
  • Collaborate with a diverse group of people from different functions (Finance, Marketing, Manufacturing sites, Suppliers, Product Supply) and countries to deliver key results.

What we offer you:

  • You will have business responsibilities from Day 1  – You will start of working on key brands from the beginning. Over time, as you expand your impact on the business, your responsibility and ownership of a brand will quickly grow;
  • You will receive  continuous coaching & mentorship – We are passionate about our work. We will make sure you receive both formal training and as regular mentorship from your manager and others;
  • You will work in a  dynamic and respectful work environment – We live our Purpose, Values, and Principles daily. We value every individual and inspire initiatives promoting agility and work/life balance.

We are looking for people who:
