About the job

            The CRA is committed to providing an inclusive and barrier-free work environment with a diverse workforce that reflects the Canadians we serve. It starts with the hiring process. To find out more about the CRA's actions in support of Employment Equity (EE), diversity and inclusion (DI), and read testimonials from our employees and executives, please visit the following CRA web sites: [Employment equity, diversity and inclusion](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/careers-cra/employment-equity-diversity-inclusion.html) and [Our workforce testimonials - Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/careers-cra/workforce-testimonials.html).

We encourage you to complete the EEDI Staffing questionnaire as part of your application if you wish to be considered for appointments using the EE and/or DI staffing requirement in this process. Also, ensure that you have provided consent in the EEDI staffing questionnaire for your EEDI information to be used for screening or selection. If you do not complete the questionnaire, you will not be considered for any appointments using the EE and/or DI staffing requirement in this process. If you decide to modify your response or fill out this questionnaire at a later date, please notify the contact listed at the bottom of this notice of job opportunity.

CRA's National EE Staffing Strategy strives to eliminate gaps for the four EE designated groups: Indigenous peoples, persons with disabilities, members of visible minorities, and women. Where an EE gap exists at the time of appointment, EE may be used as an appointment criterion for permanent and temporary appointments over six months made through this process.

Employees are required to work on-site in accordance with the CRA's requirement for on-site presence. Workplace arrangements are based on the position, work location and operational requirements, and are subject to change. Specific on-site presence requirements will be shared with candidates selected for appointment. Workplace accommodations based on the duty to accommodate as well as individual exceptions may be granted depending on the circumstances and the nature of the work being performed.

This staffing process will be primarily used to staff positions at the Winnipeg Tax Centre located at 66 Stapon Road, Winnipeg, MB.  Some positions may require employees to be physically on site at Winnipeg Tax Centre up to 100% of their scheduled work hours.

When you apply to this staffing process, you are not applying for a specific job, but to an inventory for future vacancies. As positions become available, applicants who meet the essential qualifications will be contacted.

Please note, this staffing process is meant to staff short-term temporary appointments only and will have a total duration of six (6) months or less. These short-term contracts will primarily be from two (2) to six (6) months, starting as early as January 2027. No lateral move appointments will be made from this staffing process.

Although there will be no permanent employment from this staffing process, this is a great opportunity within CRA.

Shifts for these positions may vary, including:

Day: 7:00 am – 4:00 pm

Evening: 4:00 pm – 12:00 am

Midnight: 11:00 pm – 7:00 am

The duties and responsibilities of these roles include, but are not limited to, the following:

SP01 Positions

  • Receives, examines and sorts incoming mail and attaches relevant documentation, performs various handling activities, prepares large volumes of material such as files and documents for processing and forwards the information to the appropriate area within the Agency or other federal organizations.
  • Updates computer information by inputting data.
  • Screens, sorts, files, retrieves and maintains various information relating to taxpayer or benefit recipient records and files.

SP02 Positions

  • Analyzes and assesses the information and/or documentation received from taxpayers and/or their representatives, takes the appropriate action in order to complete assessment or reassessment of the returns and, in some cases, processes accounting documents.
  • Communicates with taxpayers and/or internal clients orally and/or in writing to obtain additional information or documentation in order to take measures to complete their returns or claims or to explain changes made to their returns or claims.
  • Completes certain assessment, reassessment or accounting forms; sorts, distributes, researches and files documents in order to ensure that the documents are delivered to the proper location and/or processed within the prescribed time period.
  • Operates electronic, computer and/or automated equipment associated with various computer systems in order to enter data, keep information updated and distribute documents to the relevant individuals or sectors.

To apply

Who can apply

  • Persons residing within reasonable commuting distance (approximately 125 kilometres) of Winnipeg, MB.
  • AND eligible veterans and Canadian Armed Forces members
  • AND individuals with preferred status who live or work within the general rule of minimum distance of the position(s) being staffed.

Preference for appointment will be given to veterans first, followed by Canadian citizens and permanent residents.

Please note that this process will be used to staff positions in Winnipeg, Manitoba only. If you are a candidate from the general public, you will have to pay for any expenses related to your participation in this staffing process, as well as any relocation costs if you receive an offer of employment from this process.

Qualified members and former members of the Canadian Armed Forces who receive an offer of employment are not entitled to relocation costs from the CRA. They may, however, be entitled to a final relocation from the Department of National Defence.

We encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible as the closing date and time on this notice of job opportunity may change.

We may give preference to the first 1,500 candidates who meet the staffing requirements. Additional candidates may be considered as needed. If you are considered further, we will notify you in writing of the next step in this staffing process.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
