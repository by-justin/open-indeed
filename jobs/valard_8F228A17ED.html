Job Description

Our Distribution group is currently looking to add a Traffic Control / Flag Person to our team, based in Calgary, AB.

Reporting to the Supervisor, Traffic Control, we are seeking someone with previous traffic control experience. This position will be responsible for flagging two-way traffic, installing construction signage, barricades, barrels, delineators, and assisting with preparation for on-road construction activity while maintaining a safe working environment.

Please note: this position will work Monday – Friday, 8-10 hours / day with occasional weekends and out of town work.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Control and direct traffic and pedestrians at construction sites or other locations to ensure the safe and efficient flow of vehicles
  • Set up traffic control signs, cones, and barriers to redirect traffic away from work areas
  • Stop traffic whenever required by the progress of work or keep traffic moving at reduced speeds to avoid tie-ups and delays
  • Communicate with drivers and pedestrians to provide instructions and ensure compliance with traffic regulations
  • Coordinate with other traffic control personnel to ensure a smooth flow of traffic
  • Maintain a safe work environment with protocols and proper protective equipment
  • Always maintain safety awareness and work in compliance with applicable health and safety regulations and policies

Requirements

  • Prior flagging / traffic control experience
  • Class 5 driver’s license with clean abstract
  • Physically fit, willing / able to work outdoors for long hours
  • Comfortable working in and around traffic and pedestrians
  • CSTS training

Preferred Skills / Qualifications

  • Traffic Control Persons for Construction / Alberta Temporary Traffic Control training

Benefits

This position offers an hourly rate of $20 – $24, depending on experience and qualifications.

We offer a comprehensive and competitive total rewards package that incorporates a complete range of employee benefits, including a 5% RRSP matching program, to ensure you have the tools necessary to manage, maintain, and improve your health and wellbeing.
