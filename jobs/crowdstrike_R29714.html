About the Role:

This is a technical sales leadership role. You will lead the Enterprise Sales Engineering team, own the technical sales strategy across your territory, and coordinate every technical resource CrowdStrike can bring to bear in support of a customer.

You are the technical counterpart to the Regional Sales Director. Together you own the territory. Your team runs the technical campaign that earns the technical win; you make sure it is qualified, resourced, inspected and closed out with evidence.

Approximately a quarter of the role is outward facing. You will present at industry and customer events, chair executive round tables, publish point of view and technical content, and shape how the market thinks about modern security. We are looking for a leader who can hold a room of CISOs, then go back and build the team that wins the work.

This is a hybrid position open to candidates in Sydney or Canberra.

What You'll Do:

Lead the technical sales campaign

  • Own the technical sales strategy for the territory, and own the technical narrative in forecast calls, deal reviews and business reviews.
  • Qualify technical evaluations before they start. Approve the value hypothesis, success criteria and evaluation plan, then inspect progress against them on a regular cadence.
  • Govern evaluation quality end to end, from scoping and differentiation through to the wrap-up report, evidence of proof against each success criterion, and sign-off of the technical win.
  • Maintain the currency and accuracy of technical qualification and opportunity records so the business can forecast with confidence.
  • Bring leadership analysis and recommended action on pipeline health, technical risk, competitive position and capability gaps.

Coordinate technical resources in support of the customer

  • Act as the single point of coordination for technical engagement supporting a customer to ensure a consistent experience: sales engineers, product and solution specialists, threat intelligence, professional services, support, and partner technical teams.
  • Broker product management and engineering engagement to remove technical blockers, escalating early and with a recommended path rather than a problem alone.
  • Review team capacity and priorities continuously, delegate deliberately, and raise resourcing or capability risk before it affects the business.
  • Enable partners and distributors to run technical evaluations and represent the platform credibly to their own customers.

Lead and develop the team

  • Provide operational line management for the team, including performance management, reviews, career development, and the appropriate conversations that go with them.
  • Coach. Run development-focused one-to-ones with a written plan and visible follow-up to support your people achieving their maximum potential.
  • Create growth deliberately. Delegate high-visibility work, allocate stretch assignments, and position your people for executive and customer exposure.
  • Proactively build enablement for new and acquired capability, and ensure the team and their sales counterparts can position, demonstrate and support the platform with confidence.

Serve customers and the market

  • Lead senior technical engagements personally, as meeting owner or as the technical leader in the room, and maintain in-person presence with customers and teams across the territory.
  • Bring industry and market segment insight that informs and influences a customer's security strategy, not product capability alone.
  • Represent CrowdStrike publicly. Present at industry and customer events, chair and contribute to executive round tables, author point of view papers and technical content, and support analyst, partner and media engagement as required.
  • Monitor and assess the competitive and market situation in the region, and feed it back into technical strategy, differentiation and team enablement.

Partner with sales

  • Establish and maintain a weekly operating cadence with your sales counterpart covering territory strategy, pipeline, technical risk, priorities and agreed actions.
  • Prepare jointly for customer and internal business reviews and own the technical elements end to end.
  • Work with sales leadership to achieve and exceed assigned territory targets.
  • Provide leadership with clarity and certainty on the technical position of the business.

What You'll Need:

Essential

  • Proven people leadership within a technical sales or pre-sales function, including leading distributed teams.
  • Deep pre-sales experience and strong cybersecurity domain knowledge spanning endpoint, identity, cloud and modern security operations.
  • A track record of leading complex enterprise technical evaluations through to a technical win, and of holding scope and quality under pressure.
  • Credibility across altitudes. Able to hold a technical discussion with an architect and a business discussion with a CISO or CIO in the same meeting.
  • Demonstrated public presence, such as conference presentations, executive round tables, published papers or equivalent market-facing work.
  • Excellent written communication, including the ability to author customer-facing and market-facing material to a publishable standard.
  • Structured and disciplined execution. Sets and manages expectations with senior stakeholders, and completes what is agreed by the date agreed.
  • Experience working with channel partners and distributors.
  • Willingness to travel across the territory to customers and teams.
  • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.

Advantageous

  • Prior operator-side experience within a security or technology function.
  • Familiarity with adversary tradecraft, common malware families, and detection and evasion techniques.
  • Experience applying a structured enterprise qualification methodology.
  • Formal qualifications through Tertiary education and security certifications such as CISSP, CISM, GIAC, Security+ or CEH.
