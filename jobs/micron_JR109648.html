Job Description

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Responsibilities

Technical Leadership

  • Serve as the technical subject matter expert for Assembly & Test supplier quality.
  • Define and lead large, highly technical supplier quality improvement projects with significant business impact.
  • Drive implementation of new quality technologies, methodologies, monitoring systems, and supplier quality initiatives.
  • Establish and deploy supplier quality standards, best practices, and governance processes across the supplier base.
  • Provide technical consultation and strategic recommendations to engineering, manufacturing, procurement, and quality organizations.
  • Mentor engineers and SQEs while building organizational capability through technical leadership.

Supplier Quality Excellence

  • Ensure suppliers consistently deliver products and services that meet Micron quality, reliability, and operational requirements.
  • Drive supplier compliance to Quality Management Systems (QMS), industry standards, STC requirements, and Micron quality expectations.
  • Lead strategic supplier quality assessments, gap analyses, and performance improvement initiatives.
  • Develop and execute long-term supplier quality roadmaps focused on process capability, defect prevention, and operational excellence.
  • Enable a shift-left quality culture through advanced process controls, monitoring systems, and proactive defect prevention methodologies.
  • Drive supplier accountability through performance reviews, corrective actions, and continuous improvement programs.

Risk Management and Problem Solving

  • Identify, evaluate, and mitigate supplier quality risks that could impact Micron operations, customers, or business objectives.
  • Lead investigations and resolution of highly complex supplier quality excursions, reliability concerns, and process deviations.
  • Apply advanced analytical tools and quality engineering methodologies to drive root cause analysis and sustainable corrective actions.
  • Evaluate supplier manufacturing controls, technology readiness, business continuity, and operational resilience.
  • Develop risk mitigation strategies that reduce supplier-related quality and operational disruptions.

Cross-Functional Influence

  • Act as the primary technical interface between suppliers and Micron internal stakeholders.
  • Partner with Assembly, Test, Product Engineering, IQC, Reliability, Procurement, and Operations teams to achieve business objectives.
  • Influence supplier leadership teams to adopt quality improvements, advanced controls, and best practices.
  • Communicate highly complex technical information effectively to both technical and executive audiences.
  • Build strong partnerships and alignment across global functions and supplier organizations.

Innovation and Standards Development

  • Identify opportunities for innovation in supplier quality systems, manufacturing controls, and operational processes.
  • Drive development of new quality methodologies, metrics, and governance models.
  • Create scalable frameworks and standards that can be adopted across multiple suppliers and technologies.
  • Support new process qualifications, technology transfers, supplier transitions, and new product introductions.
  • Lead benchmarking and industry best-practice adoption initiatives.

Digital Transformation & AI Enablement

  • Leverage AI-enabled, AI-assisted, and data-driven tools to improve supplier quality monitoring, risk assessment, defect prevention, root cause investigations, and operational decision-making.

  • Drive adoption of AI-supported and advanced analytics solutions to enhance engineering productivity, supplier performance visibility, process control effectiveness, and quality governance.

  • Evaluate and apply Generative AI, Agentic AI, and other emerging digital technologies where appropriate to accelerate problem-solving, streamline workflows, and improve supplier quality outcomes.
  • Promote responsible and compliant use of Artificial Intelligence technologies, ensuring alignment with company governance, data security, quality, and business requirements

Impact and Scope

  • Decisions directly influence supplier quality performance, project success, and operational effectiveness.
  • Leads complex supplier quality initiatives with site, regional, or global impact.
  • Recognized as a key technical authority within the Supplier Quality Engineering function.
  • Influences business outcomes through technical expertise, data-driven decision making, and cross-functional leadership.
  • Defines practices and standards that are broadly adopted within the organization.

Safety, Compliance and Ethics

  • Ensure adherence to company quality, safety, security, labor, environmental, and ethics requirements.
  • Proactively identify and escalate compliance, quality, or supplier-related risks.
  • Support supplier compliance with Micron policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Foster a culture of integrity, accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering, Semiconductor Engineering, Materials Science, or related discipline, with 8 years of semiconductor industry experience.
  • Strong technical expertise in active semiconductor components, including PMICs, RCDs, Data Buffers, Hubs, Temperature Sensors, ASICs, and controller devices, with knowledge spanning silicon technology, assembly, test, qualification, and reliability.
  • Solid understanding of semiconductor manufacturing processes, including wafer fabrication, wafer sort/probe, process integration, yield improvement, process controls, and reliability engineering.
  • Experience working with foundries, OSATs, and component suppliers to drive supplier qualification, technology transfers, process changes, quality improvements, and risk mitigation.
  • Proven ability to lead complex quality investigations using statistical analysis, electrical characterization, reliability assessment, failure analysis, and root cause methodologies to drive effective corrective and preventive actions.
  • Strong working knowledge of quality systems and tools, including APQP, PPAP, SPC, FMEA, MSA, 8D, CAPA, ISO 9001, IATF 16949, and supplier auditing practices.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and technical influencing skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across global engineering, operations, procurement, quality, and supplier organizations.
  • Self-driven and highly accountable, with the ability to manage multiple priorities independently and support domestic/international travel (20-30%) and global business engagements across multiple time zones.
