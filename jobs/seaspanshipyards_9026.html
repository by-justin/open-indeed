Description

The Supervisor, Production Support, will provide leadership to a team of Planners (Production Control Technologists/Schedulers) within designated Stages of Construction (SoC). This role will drive operational schedules, monitor performance, and help resolve cross-trade production issues by collaborating with Engineering, Detail Planning, Supply Chain, and Operations Management.

Note: Due to business needs, this position is required to be fully on-site based at 50 Pemberton Avenue, North Vancouver.

What you’ll do

  • Provide leadership to team members, including mentorship, setting expectations, measuring performance, providing constructive feedback, documenting skill gaps, and identifying training requirements.
  • Administer company policies, verify timesheets, and set annual goals.
  • Hire and onboard new employees as needed.
  • Ensure adherence to schedules, production plans, resource profiles, and build strategies.
  • Coordinate with production leadership to maintain and align operational schedules with project requirements.
  • Develop, document, and improve processes, standards, and workflows for the production support team.
  • Complete and implement continuous improvement projects for the production support team.
  • Support multiple trade value streams by proactively resolving production issues and facilitating coordination across value streams.
  • Conduct regular communication meetings with team members to ensure alignment and address challenges.
  • Represent the department in internal and external meetings.
  • Oversee the accurate implementation, tracking, and collection of production data to support new construction projects.
  • Foster collaboration and communication across teams to promote a culture of efficiency and teamwork.

What you’ll bring

  • A bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business or a technical field of study, or relevant Trades experience is an asset
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in shipbuilding or manufacturing environment
  • 3-5 years of supervisory experience in a manufacturing or production environment.
  • Extensive knowledge of shipbuilding operations, with an understanding of procurement, design, production, and testing processes.
  • Proficiency with enterprise-level MRP and ERP systems as well as independent planning systems (Oracle/Microsoft Project).
  • Knowledge of EVMS principles for monitoring, reporting, and controlling work activities.
  • Strong technical skills, including the ability to read and interpret complex specifications and engineering drawings.
  • Knowledge of CAD/CAM software
  • Proven ability to direct work, delegate effectively, and lead a team in achieving goals.
  • Strong problem-solving, decision-making, and analytical skills, with a results-oriented mindset.
  • Strong customer service focus and interpersonal skills.
  • Demonstrated initiative, creativity, integrity, and adaptability to changing situations.
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize, and solve problems effectively.
  • Excellent digital literacy, including Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).
  • Ability to build an effective team and foster collaboration across diverse groups.
  • Ability to schedule work to a sequence that supports the production methodology

Why you’ll love working here

  • This is a full-time, permanent position. With multiple operations and long-term projects, Seaspan employees enjoy job stability as we look ahead to build ships for decades to come.
