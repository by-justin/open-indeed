Job Description

Would you like to develop your engineering & technical skills as the next generation of leaders at one of Canada’s Top 100 Employers for Young People (2026)?

Our Product Supply group is responsible for the manufacturing and delivery of all our products. It's the bread and butter of what makes P&G such a great company to work for. If you are passionate about inventing, developing, reapplying and delivering manufacturing processes and technologies, then this role is perfect for you.

We are currently hiring for 4, 8 and 12 month co-ops, starting January 2027 at P&G’s largest Feminine Care products manufacturing site, located in Belleville, Ontario, where we manufacture bestselling P&G brands such as Always Pads, Liners and Always Discreet.

What can you Expect?

This is an on-site role.

Based on your skills, experience and our business needs, you could be matched with the following roles in any one of our product modules:

  • Operations Co-Op
  • Process & Engineering Co-Op

You can expect meaningful work from Day 1 that creates real value for the company and our consumers:

  • You will be given a detailed scope of work to deliver exciting manufacturing and engineering projects during your term, which could range from process control and engineering, systems improvements to quality improvement, etc.
  • A Corporate and Functional onboarding to accelerate your training and development
  • Integration into our affinity groups for socializing, mentorship and resources
  • Providing honest and constructive feedback on your performance, we learn from each other!
  • Competitive Salary

Job Qualifications

Candidates must be enrolled in full time schooling. Preference is given to individuals who have completed at least 3 years in Electrical, Chemical, Physics or Mechanical engineering programs at an accredited University.

Other Skills That Make You a Great Fit

  • You possess natural leadership and business skills, demonstrated in academic and/or extracurricular activities.
  • You have strong communication skills that will enable you to thrive in a challenging and exciting work environment.
  • You have technical curiosity and enjoy thinking critically to uncover insights and are ready to take on a hands-on approach to problem solving and executing solutions, that will help build our million dollar brands!
  • You enjoy working with your hands on machinery and equipment, and enjoy a mix of "on the floor" and office type work

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
