Group Summary:

Cosma provides a comprehensive range of body, chassis and engineering solutions to global customers. Through our robust product engineering, outstanding tooling capabilities and diverse process expertise, we continue to bring lightweight and innovative products to market.

Job Responsibilities:

POSITION SUMMARY:

To represent Karmax in front of customers in the pursuit of securing business opportunities and executing actions based on annual business plan. Working with internal departments, Cosma cross functional teams, integrators, tool shops and suppliers to develop cost effective solutions in response to customer RFQ’s and engineering changes. This position is responsible for directing the activities of multiple workgroups in a just in time, lean manufacturing environment in accordance with the principles of the Employee Charter and the policies and procedures set out in the Karmax Heavy Stamping Employee Handbook. The Department Manager oversees resources to ensure safety, quality and production levels are maintained in order to meet Customer requirements.

MINIMUM JOB REQUIREMENTS:

· Related Certification College Diploma or University Degree in Mechanical Engineering and, or Business and/or Combination of Education and Work Experience

· 5 years Karmax Heavy Stamping, Cosma, Magna and/or Automotive/Manufacturing related Experience

SKILLS AND COMPETENCIES

· Excellent Verbal and Written Communication Skills

· Excellent mathematical, analytical, and organizational skills

· Excellent leadership, coaching, mentoring, interpersonal, problem solving, motivational and negotiating skills.

· Knowledge of Magna production tools and policies including safety policies

· Good understanding of costing and business proposal development.

· Experience dealing with automotive OEM’s and capable of presenting technical & commercial proposals.

· Good knowledge of manufacturing processes used in the production of welded assemblies,

· Must be proficient in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint.

· Experienced in costing and margin growth.
