Job Description

The Enterprise Architect (EA) operates as the principal strategic technology guide and technical force behind delivering high-quality Digital Experience solutions to Adobe's enterprise customers in Japan. Adobe is pursuing a new generation of digital experiences built around Generative AI and Agentic AI, and the EA serves as the technical vanguard driving customer business transformation through these capabilities.

The EA ensures customer business goals match Adobe solutions through scalable, integrated architectures that generate sustained value. This cross-functional strategic position operates within both Custom Service, which is project-focused consulting, and Integrated Services, Adobe's subscription advisory service. Few roles span these two engagement approaches.

The EA partners with customers across three phases:

  • Plan and align: Conduct architecture discovery sessions, define future-state solution architecture, and align Adobe solutions with IT strategy
  • Design and guide: Recommend integration and data flows, provide cross-solution architecture guidance, review and validate technical designs, and advise on governance and scalability
  • Enable and Optimize: Verify and validate value, measure path to value and return on investment, and find opportunities to showcase success

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop complete architectures throughout the Adobe CX Enterprise (formerly Adobe Experience Cloud) portfolio, incorporating connections with third-party solutions
  • Translate intricate business needs into technical solutions, ensuring all delivery team actions correspond with documented customer business goals
  • Develop project scope definitions, delivery estimates, resource allocation plans, and project timelines
  • Develop roadmaps in collaboration with business collaborators, IT teams, partners, and executive leadership
  • Propel project advancement in partnership with global delivery teams and the partner network, and review technical deliverables to ensure quality
  • Investigate advanced features of AEM and Adobe CX Enterprise, including Agentic AI, to aid the development of new service models
  • Advise customers on concrete use cases and architectural approaches for applying Generative AI and Agentic AI to their business

Opportunities Offered

  • Lead the technical architecture behind digital transformation for leading Japanese enterprise customers
  • Work directly with Adobe's Product and Engineering teams
  • Contribute to the creation of new services powered by Adobe's leading-edge AI portfolio, including Firefly, GenStudio, and Agentic AI
  • Take on a distinctive role that combines both Custom Service and Integrated Services—situating you at the forefront of changes in the consulting field

Required Skills and Experience

  • 6+ years of relevant experience in either following:(a) Enterprise Architect or Consultant, delivering enterprise-scale web applications(b) Digital Marketing Product Management
  • Foundational knowledge of CMS and web application technologies
  • Experience designing and planning high-performance web system architectures, including CDN
  • Proven capability to transform intricate business needs into technical solutions and coordinate delivery team efforts accordingly
  • Experience developing project scope definitions, delivery estimates, and resource allocation plans
  • Ongoing familiarity with technology trends such as Generative AI and Agentic AI, with the ability to propose concrete applications for customer business
  • Skilled in written and verbal communication, capable of adapting for technical, marketing, or executive audiences
  • Business-level Japanese (required). Working proficiency in reading and writing English is sufficient

Skills That Set You Apart

  • Hands-on experience with Adobe CX Enterprise solutions
  • Experience in analytics implementation
  • Knowledge of modern web frontend stacks such as React and TypeScript
  • Working knowledge of the end-to-end digital marketing journey, including Ad Tech (CDP/DMP, DSP), marketing automation, omni-channel personalization, and CRM
  • Experience employing Generative and/or Agentic AI solutions to optimize daily workflows, or hands-on experience building AI agents
  • English proficiency adequate to negotiate and work directly with international teams
  • Experience leading cross-functional, multi-region delivery teams

Candidate Profile

We encourage candidates who demonstrate the following qualities:

  • Deep understanding of customer business challenges, with the ability to translate them into solutions powered by modern tools
  • Strong communication skills that build trust as a bridge between technical teams and executive collaborators
  • A genuine interest in fast-moving technology trends, including AI, and a drive to explore practical applications
  • Leadership that can engage multiple collaborators while taking ownership of a project through to completion
  • A readiness to think beyond current frameworks to contribute to the creation of new service models
  • Comfort collaborating with mixed onshore/offshore global teams
