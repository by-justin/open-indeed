Job Description

Job Overview:

This 2nd Shift Production Supervisor will manage assigned processes and personnel to meet or exceed company and departmental goals. Oversee efficient production of quality products through effective communication with managers, subordinates, and all other departments within the organization.

Essential Duties and responsibilities

  • Plan and coordinate daily production activities by reviewing schedules, processing specifications, equipment needs, materials, staffing, and line set-ups to meet production goals.

  • Partner with Production Scheduling, Sanitation, Warehouse, Maintenance, Accounting, R&D, and other departments to support efficient, cost-effective operations and successful project execution.

  • Assign employees to work areas and tasks, communicate expectations clearly, and use labour effectively to maximize productivity and efficiency.

  • Lead, coach, train, and develop employees through formal training, performance management, employee involvement, and continuous improvement initiatives.

  • Manage employee-related actions in partnership with Human Resources, including hiring, disciplinary action, time and attendance, leave, promotions, reviews, and training requirements.

  • Promote and maintain a safe, healthy, and compliant work environment by monitoring work areas, reinforcing safe work practices, following labour laws and regulations, and supporting all required safety programs.

  • Ensure production quality, food safety, and documentation requirements are met, including HACCP, raw material usage, finished production, downtime, staffing, accident reports, work orders, and related records.

  • Model and reinforce Company policies, the Code of Conduct, vision, and values while fostering a positive work environment and completing other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

  • Bachelors degree is desired

  • Management and Supervision courses and/or training coupled with the ability to successfully coach, guide and direct team members

  • Demonstrated ability to analyze and solve problems

  • Demonstrated ability to understand and evaluate costs involved with production

  • Proven ability to communicate with team members whom have limited English or experience communication barriers

  • Minimum three (3) years of manufacturing supervisory experience

  • Experience with Food Manufacturing desirable

  • Experience with HACCP desirable

  • Agreement to obtain USDC HACCP Certification

  • Agreement obtain and regularly update Red Cross CPR/First Aid Certifications

About High Liner Foods

Founded in 1899 in Lunenburg, Nova Scotia , High Liner Foods has grown from a small salt fish company into a leading North American processor and marketer of value-added frozen seafood . Our trusted brands — including High Liner, Fisher Boy, Mirabel, Sea Cuisine, C.Wirthy & Co, Mrs. Paul’s, Van de Kamp’s, and Icelandic Seafood — are enjoyed by families, restaurants, and institutions across Canada and the United States.

Guided by our purpose of Reimagining Seafood to Nourish Life , we combine over a century of expertise with innovation, consumer insight, and responsible practices to deliver high-quality seafood people can trust.

At High Liner Foods, we are a people-first organization . We invest in our employees through development opportunities, collaborative teamwork, and a culture where safety, well-being, and growth are priorities. Whether in our offices, plants, or distribution teams, our people work together to deliver excellence and drive the future of seafood.

Our Values

Serve with Purpose – We put our customers and consumers at the heart of everything we do, creating quality products that help to nourish lives and foster connections that go beyond the plate.

Do the Right Thing – We lead with integrity, act responsibly, and make choices that support our people, our partners, and our planet. .

Innovate & Reimagine – We challenge the norm, embrace fresh ideas, and turn innovation into impact.

All Hands on Deck – Powered by people and driven by purpose, we lead with courage, hold one another accountable, and celebrate as a team
