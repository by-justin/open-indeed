The SEA & India MCO, Rare Disease (RD) Medical Lead will lead the Medical and scientific (non-promotional) strategy for the SEA & India MCO, One Rare and Transplant portfolio and therapeutic areas in close partnership with the other functional RD leads and in alignment with the overall One Rare and transplant strategy.
