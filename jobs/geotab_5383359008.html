Who you are:

The Technical Learning & Development Intern is a technical contributor within the Global Enablement team, supporting workflow automation, AI-powered tool development, content management, and learning program operations. This role works closely with the AI Solutions Developer and Senior L&D Specialists to build automation solutions, maintain content systems, support multimedia production, and assist with event coordination and data reporting. A core objective is to support the team's AI-first direction — leveraging tools like GIA, Google Workspace automation, and emerging AI platforms to increase team efficiency and delivery quality.

What you'll do:

As a Technical Learning & Development Intern, your key accountabilities will include: (1) supporting workflow automation and AI tool integration within L&D operations; (2) assisting with content management, audits, and digital asset organization; (3) providing technical support for learning programs and internal events; (4) contributing to multimedia content development; and (5) gathering and reporting on program data and engagement metrics.

How you'll make an impact:

Workflow Automation & AI Tool Support:

  • Support the design and implementation of SOPs and automation workflows for L&D team operations using Google Workspace tools (Apps Script, Workspace Flows) and AI platforms (GIA, Claude Code)

  • Assist the AI Solutions Developer in building and testing AI-powered tools, integrations, and automation scripts

  • Contribute to technical projects such as knowledge base integrations, content pipeline automation, and LMS workflow improvements

  • Research and prototype emerging AI tools relevant to the team's learning, enablement, and content management work

  • Support the DnA onboarding initiative: build and maintain GIA skills and automated resources for new Data practitioners onboarding to the team

Content Management & Knowledge Governance:

  • Conduct audits of digital assets across organizational platforms (IRIS knowledge base, Google Shared Drives, Bynder, MyLearning) to identify gaps, duplicates, and optimization opportunities

  • Support the application of content taxonomy and metadata standards to ensure content is accurate, discoverable, and AI-ready

  • Assist in maintaining and updating content across the team's digital asset library, ensuring consistent organization and version control

  • Contribute to platform consolidation projects and help document content migration and archival processes

Learning Program & Event Technical Support:

  • Provide technical support for internal learning events: setup of virtual and hybrid platforms, tech troubleshooting, materials preparation, and post-event data collection

  • Assist with coordination and logistics for learning programs and team-organized events (summits, lunch-and-learns, workshops)

  • Support the production of multimedia learning content: video editing, eLearning module updates, slide decks, and digital job aids using tools such as Camtasia, Canva, or Google Workspace

Data Reporting & Analytics:

  • Gather and compile data on program effectiveness, LMS completion rates, AI tool adoption, and content engagement

  • Maintain dashboards and reports that give the team and leadership visibility into learning program outcomes

  • Assist in preparing materials for quarterly reporting (e.g., State of AI Enablement report, program impact summaries)

General L&D Team Support:

  • Support on-demand L&D team requests: document formatting, research tasks, meeting summaries, and administrative coordination

  • Actively use AI tools (GIA, Google Gemini, Claude) as primary working tools — not supplementary — to deliver work faster and at higher quality

  • Participate in team rituals, stand-ups, and retrospectives; communicate progress, blockers, and attendance proactively

Content & Multimedia:
