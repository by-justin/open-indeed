Position Overview:

The Executive/Senior Executive – Sales Support – Branch is responsible for managing end-to-end billing, invoicing, and collections coordination for branch customers, ensuring accuracy, timeliness, and adherence to contractual and company guidelines.

The role acts as a key interface between Sales, Finance, and Customers to ensure smooth revenue realization, effective dispute resolution, and strong process discipline at the branch level.

Job Purpose:

To ensure accurate and timely billing, efficient collections follow-up, and resolution of customer billing queries, thereby supporting revenue realization, improving receivables performance, and enhancing customer experience.

Key Roles & Responsibilities:

Billing & Invoicing Management

• Coordinate with Branch Finance & Accounts teams for timely bill generation

• Validate accuracy of invoices as per contractual terms and company norms

• Ensure customization of invoices as per customer agreements

• Ensure timely dispatch of invoices to customers

Collections & Receivables Management

• Work closely with Sales teams to ensure timely collections

• Follow up on pending, short, or delayed payments

• Track receivables ageing and escalate as per timelines

• Support improvement of DSO at branch level

Customer Query & Dispute Resolution

• Coordinate with Finance and Sales teams to resolve billing disputes

• Ensure closure of disputes before payment due dates

• Maintain proactive customer communication

Reporting & Governance

• Maintain billing, dispatch, and collection MIS

• Share reports with Branch Finance and Sales leadership

• Ensure adherence to SOPs, audit, and documentation standards

Sales & Branch Coordination

• Align billing cycles with contractual agreements

• Support onboarding of customers from billing readiness perspective

• Ensure coordination across Sales, Operations, and Finance

Process Discipline & Continuous Improvement

• Adhere to defined SOPs and processes

• Identify recurring issues and highlight to supervisor

• Suggest improvements to enhance accuracy and turnaround time

• Support automation and process improvement initiatives

• Apply learnings from errors and audit observations

People & Team Support

• Provide operational guidance to branch sales teams

• Ensure process adherence and performance discipline

• Support manpower planning inputs where required
