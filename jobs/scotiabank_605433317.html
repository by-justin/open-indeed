Purpose:

The Market Lead is responsible for driving sustainable and profitable growth in the assigned market by enabling Portfolio Managers, Associate Portfolio Managers, Wealth Managers, Wealth Associates, and administrative teams to deliver exceptional client service with greater consistency, efficiency, and capacity. This role combines market leadership, relationship management, operational discipline, compliance oversight, and people leadership to ensure the business is well positioned to retain, deepen, and grow high net worth and ultra-high net worth client relationships.

The incumbent acts as a trusted business partner to Portfolio Managers and regional leadership by strengthening day-to-day operating routines, reinforcing risk and compliance expectations, supporting human resources and workforce planning activities, and removing operational friction that may limit client-facing capacity. By building a disciplined, well-controlled, and highly engaged market environment, the Market Lead helps Portfolio Managers focus more time on value-added client conversations, business development, referrals, and holistic wealth advice—thereby improving the client experience and accelerating market growth.

Major Accountabilities:

  1. Enables exceptional client service and relationship growth by strengthening the support model around Portfolio Managers and client-facing teams by:

  • Ensuring client relationships receive timely and proactive attention by helping coordinate service delivery, clarify ownership, and remove operational barriers that may impact the client experience;

  • Consistently delivering the desired client experience during all client interactions;

  • Using strong knowledge of the Bank’s, Canadian Wealth Management and Scotia Jarislowsky Fraser offerings;

  • Partnering with all internal product/service business partners, as required ensuring client needs are met;

  • Responding to client, resolving problem situations autonomously when they arise;
