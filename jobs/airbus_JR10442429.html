About us

As a successful candidate, you'll have the opportunity to join a dynamic team straddling two exciting worlds of Aircraft industry: Engineering and Manufacturing.

Our “Airframe Engineering Wide Bodies Product Delivery” team's mission is to provide Airframe Engineering Support to the WB (A330 / A350) production teams in FAL (Final Assembly Lines), to the Delivery Center, as well as to Flight Test teams. Well known as “MAP” (Mise au Point), we deliver engineering solutions to all non conformities encountered in the last production and delivery steps of our products. This internship position is open on the Electrical and Mechanical Systems Installation perimeter, offering a broad insight on Structures and Systems through their final assembly process.

Internship's Main Activities & Tasks

  • ​Usage of 3D tools (CATIA) for developing new solutions of system installation for R&T / New Dev projects on WB.
  • Propose and define improvements on definition Dosssier to reduce production cycle via FET team (FAL Eng Team).

Main goals

. Deliver improved engineering solutions for Systems Installation repetitive non-conformities and new systems installation (R&D)

. Bring both A350 / A330 programs to optimized means of treatment for Systems Installation non conformities and increase knowledge sharing within Systems Installation teams

About you - Required skills:

You are preparing a Master degree or equivalent (engineering diploma) in Aerospace / Mechanical / Electrical Engineering

  • You are enthusiast about digitalization - data science and programming skills would be very much appreciated
  • You are curious and methodic, with a good team spirit
  • Negotiation level in French and/or English
