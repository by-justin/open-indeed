Job Description

Are you a creative individual with a problem-solving mind-set? Do you have a passion for technology and a desire to learn and grow continuously? Then this opportunity as IT Marketing Technologist Intern at Procter & Gamble is the one you have been looking for!

Information Technology (IT) professionals are specialists in combining information, technology and business knowledge to build creative solutions that change the way we do business. Their work will enable faster and smarter decision-making through the use of ground-breaking technology, business intelligence, and outstanding collaboration tools.

YOUR CONTRIBUTION TO P&G’S SUCCESS:

  • Building, deploying, innovating, and/or operating the MarTech technology stack within a specific brand/channel/domain area. This includes making strategic choices regarding data, technology, and architecture to effectively integrate, activate/execute, and enrich the system to meet business needs.
  • Delivering real-time insights and recommendations for business transformation that enhance key performance indicators, inform new business models, and optimize digital/ecommerce operations.
  • Operating evolving capabilities by developing and executing end-to-end with hands-on involvement and documenting processes/capabilities for scalability. You will be the trusted leader, influencer, and practitioner of the MarTech technology stack, driving business results for your respective brands, category, and/or region.
  • Utilizing strong business acumen to bridge the gap between Marketing and IT, acting as an educator and translator to ensure that technology aligns with business needs.
  • Collaborating and communicating effectively with cross-functional teams, and external partners/agencies to align plans, solve problems, and achieve results.
  • Project managing technology requests from the marketing department, translating between technical and marketing requirements, and ensuring compliance with IT policies, including cybersecurity and privacy mandates.​

Please note we offer a fulltime internship for the period of 6 months.

Possible Start Dates: January 2027

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
