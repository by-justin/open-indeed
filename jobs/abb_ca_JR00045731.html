Your role and responsibilities

In this role, you will have the opportunity to provide reliable and timely analysis of financial results, budget, and forecasts. Each day, you will support the business management and other stakeholders at various organization levels in decision making processes. You will also showcase your expertise by supporting in the evaluation of the impact of strategic, financial, or other major investment decisions.

The work model for the role is: Hybrid.

This role is contributing to Robotics Division in Japan. Main stakeholders are Country Business Managers and Finance Managers in ABB Robotics Japan K.K..

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Support monthly financial reporting package preparation in the closing period, consolidate and analyse financial result to track and assess business performance against targets and benchmarks.
  • Facilitate financial planning, forecasting, and performance analysis activities, including budgeting, rolling forecasts, management reporting, and variance analysis to support business decision-making.
  • Provide business analytical services to support business decisions and actions, by performing profitability analysis, cost & expense analysis from different dimension.
  • Support business controllers and other business management to improve the operation performance KPI through ad-hoc reports and various analysis based on business requirements.
  • Coordinate cost allocation methodologies across different business lines, secure and increase profitability by monitoring SG&A, support cash generation and NWC improvement by monitoring Balance sheet.
  • Enhance controlling excellence through process standardization and continuous improvement, by supporting global initiatives to implement financial tools and applications in local country.
  • Optimizes own performance to increase productivity by developing automated solutions, eliminating duplications, coordinating information requirements.
  • Support financial governance and reporting compliance through statutory audit and handle retained finance transactions remained in the country, by collaborating with country finance and shared service organization including Treasury and Global Invoices.

Qualifications for the role

  • Have 3-5 years of experience in FP&A and Controlling or business finance within multi-national organizations.
  • Passionate about demonstrating your analytical skills and ability to translate financial insights into actionable recommendations for stakeholders, to make better decisions.
  • Possess an enhanced knowledge of ERP and reporting platforms, preferably SAP, SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), BI tool, Microsoft Excel, and other FP&A tools.
  • Good communication skills and problem-solving skills
  • English proficiency in speaking and written
  • JLPT N1 level
  • Working Experinece in Japan
