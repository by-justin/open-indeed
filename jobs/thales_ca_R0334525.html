  • Anticiper les risques potentiels et mettre en place des plans d’action pour prévenir les crises ou les gérer.

  • Piloter et déployer les ressources et les compétences nécessaires, afin de répondre aux enjeux de développement fournisseurs à l’échelle de l’ensemble du Groupe.

Do you have the ambition to lead a high-stakes, internationally oriented organization that has a strong impact on achieving the objectives of Thales’ various activities/businesses and on customer satisfaction?

Do you want to get involved in the operational leadership of an international team, whose main goal is to improve the maturity of our suppliers’ industrial processes and achieve operational excellence?

Within the Procurement organization, you will take charge of leading a team of managers in order to develop our suppliers so that they reach the productivity and efficiency levels expected in their operations. In this role, you will be responsible for:

  • Defining the strategy for suppliers’ operational development, in line with the governance established within the Supplier Operations entity

  • Building relationships with stakeholders to understand Thales’ external supply chain challenges, anticipate potential risks, and define action plans to prevent or manage crises

You hold an engineering degree in electronics and/or mechanical engineering and have proven experience in:

  • Engineering in an industrial setting, with a strong technical perspective, including process engineering, quality, production, or supply chain

  • An international and multicultural environment
