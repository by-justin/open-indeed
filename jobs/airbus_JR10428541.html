  • Vous êtes reconnu pour votre autonomie, votre rigueur, votre esprit d'équipe, votre orientation vers les résultats, votre capacité d'adaptation et vos compétences en communication.

Airbus Helicopters is looking for a Product RETEX Engineer Super Puma (m/f) to join our Support and Services department based in Marignane, France .

You will be part of the In-service Data & Product RETEX team.

You are looking for :

  • Strong technical focus and variety of topics addressed
  • Contact with technical specialists from different fields
  • Global vision of the machine in its real operating environment, as well as Support & Services activities,
  • Concrete contribution to improving the operational situation of customers
  • Acquiring experience in project management,
  • In a stimulating work environment

Then join the Airbus Helicopters Support & Services In-service Data & Product RETEX department to fill this MMH Focal Point position.

To find out more about our activities, watch our video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=flu57j6g-0g

Your main missions will be to:

  • Conduct lessons learned (RETEX) analyses on topics involving several business specialties and raise associated alerts
  • Provide the elements necessary for the program to establish the product improvement plan
  • Implement concrete actions to improve the operational situation
  • Communicate in-service issues encountered to the design office
  • Contribute to establishing processes and specifying the tools necessary for data collection and analysis

Required Skills:

  • General knowledge of aircraft
  • Curiosity and good understanding of technical subjects
  • Ability to navigate the organization and gain buy-in for a cross-functional need
  • Rigor
  • Resilience
  • Ability to define and specify the IT tools necessary to optimize the activity
  • Customer focus

Your profile:

  • Holding an engineering degree, you have more than 10 years of experience in the fields of the Design Office or helicopter maintenance.
  • You have a good knowledge of how helicopters operate and associated maintenance management.
  • You are recognized for your autonomy, your rigor, your team spirit, your results-orientation, your adaptability, and your communication skills.
  • An advanced level of French and English is mandatory.

This position requires a security clearance or eligibility for clearance by recognized authorities.

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.
