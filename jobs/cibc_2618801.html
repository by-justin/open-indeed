What you’ll be doing

As a Summer Analyst, you will work with engineers, traders and quantitative professionals on software, data and automation problems that matter to the business.

In this role, you will work directly with experienced engineers, traders, and quantitative professionals who will provide mentorship, feedback, and exposure to how technology is used within a modern electronic trading business.

You'll gain exposure to electronic trading, market structure, quantitative decision making, and the technology platforms that support a modern capital markets business.

Depending on your interests and the team's priorities, you may:

  • Design systems where microseconds matter and where software decisions directly impact trading outcomes.
  • Create real-time data pipelines, analytics and visualization tools.
  • Apply machine learning to market, client or operational datasets where it is the right tool.
  • Design APIs and reusable services for front-office applications.
  • Design and implement workflow automation that increases efficiency and reduces operational risk.
  • Contribute to electronic trading platforms, market data systems, and client-facing technology.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. To successfully perform the work, you’ll be on-site full-time.

The expected annualized base salary for this role is $100,000.

The base salary is determined by a variety of factors, including the candidate’s skills, experience, and job-related knowledge, as well as geographic location and specific business needs. Our compensation philosophy supports ongoing growth and development, enabling progression within the salary range as team members advance in their roles.

We are committed to providing fair, equitable, and competitive compensation opportunities for all employees, and we encourage open conversations about compensation throughout the recruitment process.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
