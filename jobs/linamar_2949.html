Job Title: Engineer-TS - Senior   TS体系工程师-高级

  • In charge of TS16949/ISO14001/OHSAS18001 System; Coordinate the 8D/CAR program & layered audit system.

  • 负责TS16949/ISO14001/OHSAS18001体系；协调8D/CAR 项目和分层审核体系。

Performance Expectations

  • Work in compliance with TS 16949 / ISO 14001 Procedures/Systems

  • Support to set up OHSAS18001

  • Conduct the regular TS16949/ISO14001 System audit and maintenance the documents

  • Coordinate all phases of the 8D / CAR program (planning, scheduling, preparation, performance, reporting and follow-up)

  • Develop, implement and maintain the layered audit system and schedule

  • Work closely with all departments to develop and implement Preventive Actions within the Quality, Environmental and Safety System

  • Follow-up on action items from Internal Audits

  • Conduct TS Awareness, ISO Awareness training per schedule

  • 支持、培养、促进和展示利纳马核心价值观及领导力行为

  • 按照TS 16949 和SO 14001体系要求工作

  • 为建立OHSAS18001体系提供支持

  • 定期进行TS16949 / ISO14001体系审核并维护文档

  • 协调8D/CAR项目的所有阶段（计划，规划，准备，审核，报告和后续跟踪）

  • 制定、实施及维护分层审核体系及分层审核计划

  • 与各部门紧密合作，制定质量、环境和安全体系并采取预防措施
