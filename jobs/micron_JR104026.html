-------------------------------------------------------------------■ Job Responsibilities

In this role, you will be responsible for process integration activities for next-generation DRAM technology development. Your key responsibilities will include:

  • Development and integration of process technologies for Future DRAM nodes
  • Design, development, and optimization of capacitor modules
  • Integration across multiple process modules to ensure consistency and performance
  • Design and execution of experiments (DOE) and data analysis to optimize process conditions
  • Identification and resolution of yield and quality-related issues
  • Cross-functional collaboration with process, design, device, and quality teams

■ Required Qualifications

Technical Skills

  • Fundamental knowledge of semiconductor processes
  • Understanding of process integration or related engineering fields
  • Knowledge of DRAM devices and structures is a plus
  • Over 5 years of experience in semiconductor engineering

Communication Skills

  • Ability to communicate effectively in English, as you will frequently participate in meetings with overseas teams
  • Proficiency in Japanese for daily business communication
  • Strong interpersonal skills to collaborate and negotiate with cross-functional teams

■ Desired Attributes

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work proactively in a team-oriented environment
  • Persistence and ownership in addressing technical challenges
  • Using AI
