About the Program

Your Journey to Manufacturing Leadership Starts Here

The M&S Graduate Program is Sanofi's flagship early career accelerator, designed to fast-track exceptional talent into M&S China's core leadership pipeline. Over 3 years, you will gain cross-regional, cross-functional business immersion across critical M&S operations — building deep operational expertise, strategic thinking, and global perspective through real-world challenges. With industry-leading executive support — including a Site Head as your Career Sponsor and a Global M&S Leader as your dedicated mentor — you will benefit from direct access to M&S China Cluster and Global Business Leaders through regular executive dialogues, a personalized development journey with external professional coaching, and targeted capability building in Digital & AI, critical thinking, and leading change. This is your express lane to becoming a strong successor in M&S China's core leadership team.

Main responsibilities 职责描述：

Production Operations & Root-Cause Analysis Development

  • Support daily production operations and progressively develop root-cause analysis capability for technical deviations, building toward independent ownership by Year 2–3
  • Learn to organize and manage manufacturing resources — balancing facility/equipment capacity, workforce, and production cost under senior guidance

Process Improvement & Validation

  • Contribute to process optimization initiatives (parameter refinement, equipment adjustment) to enhance quality and output, practicing the structured problem-solving that defines future production leaders
  • Support the planning and execution of process validations to ensure quality stability through process changes — progressively taking ownership of validation projects

GMP, Quality & HSE Compliance

  • Implement cGMP practices in daily operations, aligned with Sanofi global standards, building rigorous compliance discipline from Day 1
  • Take accountability for HSE within your scope, learning to allocate resources effectively for risk assessment, training, and safety program execution
  • Participate in deviation, OOS, and complaint investigations alongside cross-functional teams, following up on CAPA implementation
  • Support change control, equipment/cleaning validation, and batch record/SOP authoring and review

Team Building & Cross-functional Project Leadership

  • Progressively build and develop a professional production team, strengthening GMP awareness and technical capability among peers as you grow into a people leader
  • Take on project leadership for cross-departmental initiatives — practicing influence, planning, and execution skills critical to future leadership roles
  • Ensure strict compliance with psychotropic drug safety management regulations, reporting abnormalities promptly and completing required certifications

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
