Primary Purpose of the Role:

The Project Design Authority will be responsible for Solution Compliance for a Bid or Project. The PDA acts as the primary technical leader of the bid or project team and as the lead technical interface with the Customer.

Key Responsibilities and Tasks:

  • Analyses Customer needs/requirements and assesses Solution architecture and technical choices.
  • Ensures Solution compliance with Customer needs, Product Policy, MTB Strategy & applicable legislation, rules & regulations.
  • Reviews the technical risks and opportunities and the related mitigation plans throughout the bid or project.
  • Ensures best trade-off between Customer requirements & Product Policy consistent with schedule, costs, MTB strategy & risks/ opportunities.
  • Checks the production and the maintenance/service aspects of the Solution with the Production Process/Technology Manager (PPTM) and the Service Engineering Manager.
  • Approves the technical specification and source selection of subsystems/products to be outsourced, by taking into account Make-Team-Buy policy, export restrictions and offset requirements.
  • Checks that the Engineering Environment (E2) for the Solution is consistent with Country/Company E2 Instruction, and overall Solution cost, technical risks & schedule.
  • Responsible for ensuring, with the support of Specialty Engineering experts & Safety/HSE stakeholders, that the Solution design takes into account contract requirements and applicable legislation, rules & regulations, including product safety & security and Environmental/HSE requirements & standards, and that related certification activities are performed adequately and correctly.
  • Approves the Design & Justification File (D&JF) prepared by the Solution Architect (ARC).
  • Approves the completion of integration, verification, validation and qualification results.
  • Performs the Complexity Profiler analysis with the Solution Architect (ARC) & SEM. Take into account the resulting actions during the bid or project.
  • Alerts first the DA & then upward within CBU/Domain, Country, BL or GBU Technical Directorate.
  • Leads the main bid/project Engineering Reviews and approves the main Engineering deliverables at each project decision milestone.
  • Organises when appropriate technical meetings and peer reviews together with the SEM (System Engineering Manager) and other technical stakeholders and experts, in order to perform the Solution technical assessment and validation.
  • Sound understanding of the development lifecycle and lifecycle products.
  • Interfacing with the client (internal/external), to define the solution.
  • Keep abreast of technical development both within and outside of the company.

UK Eyes Only:

Due to the nature of the work that we do at Thales, many of our roles are subject to security restrictions. This role requires you to be a UK National and achieve Security Clearance (SC) without any caveats. It would be advantageous if currently held, however, if not currently held, it is a requirement that the successful applicant undergo, achieve, and maintain SC Clearance prior to commencing employment.  If approved by the MOD, a dual national from a non-ITAR country may be considered.

To be eligible for full SC, you generally need to have resided in the UK for the last 5 years.  In some circumstances, a minimum of 3 years’ residence in the UK over the last 5 years may be accepted, with additional overseas checks.

Please visit the UKSV website for further guidance:  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/united-kingdom-security-vetting-clearance-levels/national-security-vetting-clearance-levels .
