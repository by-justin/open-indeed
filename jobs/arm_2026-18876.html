Job Description

Role Purpose The Physical IP Division in Arm Noida creates Memory IPs which are extensively used by thousands of SoC designers around the world to design leading edge chips. We are currently developing memory fast cache instances on advanced technologies like 14A, 2nm from the leading foundries of the world. The various SRAM solutions are optimized for high speed, low power and high density, to cater to a variety of applications, ranging from Server to mobile and IoT.

Role & Responsibilities

Candidate will be responsible for working on design , margining , characterization, verification and release of Memory instances . This activity will give candidate exposure to different types of memories like Single port/dual port SRAM/Register File; different types of views like Verilog, liberty, schematics, lef and gds; different EDA tools used for the development and verification flow. It will also give candidate opportunity to learn automation techniques which is one of the essential skills in the industry today.

Qualification & Experience

  • Bachelors or Master’s Degree in Electronics Engineering with excellent academic credentials

  • 2-5 years experience in Memory design or characterization.

Essential Technical Experience

  • Very good understanding of SRAM, digital circuits fundamentals and SOC concepts

  • Good understanding of device physics

  • Understanding of computer architecture (optional)

  • Working knowledge of scripting (Shell, TCL) and AI usage

Characteristics & Requirements

  • Enthusiastic and self-motivated

  • Quick learner, good problem solving and debugging skills

  • Willingness to be flexible and accept new challenges

  • Capable of working as a team player or independently

  • Good analytical and reasoning skills

  • Good communication skills
