Job Description

Job Purpose: Provide technical support and resolve IT issues for end-users to ensure continuous and efficient operation of the company's information systems.

Key Responsibilities:

  1. Technical Support:
  • Receive and handle technical support requests from users via phone, email, or ticketing system.
  • Diagnose and troubleshoot hardware, software, and network issues.
  • Guide users on how to use software and IT equipment.
  1. System Management:
  • Install, configure, and maintain IT equipment such as computers, printers, phones, and network devices.
  • Manage user accounts and access rights within the system.
  1. Security and Maintenance:
  • Ensure information security and system integrity.
  • Perform regular maintenance tasks and software updates.
  1. Documentation and Reporting:
  • Record detailed information about issues and solutions in the ticketing system.
  • Report status and emerging issues to the IT Manager.

Job Requirements:

  1. Education:
  • Major in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Can work full time at least 04 days/week.
  1. Experience:
  • Prior experience in a similar role is advantageous but not mandatory.
  1. Skills:
  • Basic knowledge of operating systems (Windows, macOS, Linux).
  • Understanding of computer networks and basic network protocols.
  • Skills in troubleshooting hardware and software issues.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work in a team.
  1. Certifications (if any):
  • Relevant IT certifications such as CompTIA A+, ITIL Foundation, or other related certifications.
  1. Personal Attributes:
  • Willingness to learn and improve.
  • Professional and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to handle pressure and manage time effectively.

Benefits:

  • Insurance and benefits as per company policy.
  • Opportunities for training and career development.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.
