Job Description

Company Overview Docusign brings agreements to life. Over 1.5 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the process of doing business and simplify people’s lives. With intelligent agreement management, Docusign unleashes business-critical data that is trapped inside of documents. Until now, these were disconnected from business systems of record, costing businesses time, money, and opportunity. Using Docusign’s Intelligent Agreement Management platform, companies can create, commit, and manage agreements with solutions created by the #1 company in e-signature and contract lifecycle management (CLM). What you'll do We're looking for a hands-on Senior Manager, Web Production & Operations to own the day-to-day production, execution, and continuous improvement of Docusign's global web presence. Docusign.com is where prospects meet our product for the first time, and this role owns that experience - partnering with demand generation, product marketing, and design to build the pages and experiences that move buyers through the funnel. This role will drive continuous optimization, ensure timely initiative execution, be an expert in website best practices, and effectively communicate with stakeholders within a fast-paced organization. The ideal candidate brings a production-first mindset, a sharp eye for quality, and fluency with AI tools that help the team move faster and personalize at scale. Comfortable working on multiple projects at once, communicating with global stakeholders and vendors, and holding an advanced understanding of web fundamentals, this person will be hands-on producing some of our most complex web pages, experiments, and personalization on our most visible pages - while also leading a team of web developers/producers and championing best practices as new capabilities roll out. This position is a people manager role reporting to the Director, Program & Operations.
