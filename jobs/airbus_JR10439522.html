Your mission, should you choose to accept it:

An internship opportunity titled "Human Resources Ethics & Compliance Intern (M/F)" has just opened at Airbus, based at the Blagnac Wings Campus.

Since integrity is one of your core values ​​and you wish to help strengthen Airbus’s Ethics & Compliance culture, you will join a 30-person team within the "Human Resources Ethics & Compliance" department. This team is responsible for preventing and handling reports regarding harassment, discrimination, and non-compliance with HR procedures.

You will participate in the department's various activities, contributing your knowledge and skills in Human Resources and/or Ethics & Compliance.

This internship will begin in February 2027 and last for 6 months , depending on your academic program.

Your main responsibilities will include:

Under the guidance of your mentor, you will develop your skills by actively participating in the following international activities:

Change management to build employee awareness regarding the prevention of harassment and discrimination

Contribution to the 2027 prevention plan:

  • Adapting and optimizing existing materials to make them accessible to various countries worldwide (adapting distribution channels, adjusting formats, etc.)
  • Monitoring the progress of the prevention plan globally and supporting the creation of new prevention resources
  • Providing ad-hoc support to various prevention-related working groups
  • Supporting the development and monitoring of targeted communication plans
  • Contributing to the implementation of a mentoring system for occasional investigators

Skills & Prerequisites:

You are pursuing a Master’s-level degree (5 years of higher education) in Human Resources, Instructional Design, or Compliance.

You possess the following knowledge and skills:

You are meticulous, curious, agile, and autonomous·

·         You are recognized for your organizational, analytical, writing, and synthesis skills, as well as your ability to handle confidential data ·         You are a team player

·         You are capable of communicating in English within an international context

·         Language skills:

  • English: Negotiation level
  • French: A plus, but not mandatory
