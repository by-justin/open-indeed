Job Description:

Are you a hands-on engineer who thrives on turning complex aerospace designs into streamlined, scalable production reality? Airbus Helicopters is seeking a driven Manufacturing Engineer to lead the development, optimization, and scaling of our advanced manufacturing processes. Reporting directly to the Chief Engineer, this 100% on-site role places you at the heart of our production floor—driving manufacturing assembly, optimizing tool and workstation design, and managing process configurations for cutting-edge mechanical, electrical, and uncrewed aerial systems (UAS).

Meet the Team:

The Airbus Helicopters Flexrotor Program offers one of the most sophisticated small tactical unmanned aerial systems (STUAS) with vertical take-off and landing (VTOL). It can be used day or night by US and allied forces for a diverse range of intelligence, surveillance, target acquisition and reconnaissance (ISTAR) on land and at sea.

Your Working Environment:

Nestled in the heart of the spectacular Columbia River Gorge in Bingen, WA, our workplace offers innovation and the ability to contribute to game-changing solutions while enjoying the flexibility to spread your wings. Whether it's solving complex challenges, or celebrating victories, you’ll be part of a cohesive crew that thrives on innovation and the beauty that the Northwest has to offer!

How We Care for You:

  • Financial Rewards: Competitive base salary, incentive compensation which may include profit sharing schemes, retirement savings plan and the ability to participate in an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (“ESOP”)
  • Work/Life Balance: Paid time off including personal time, holidays and a generous paid parental leave program.
  • Health & Welfare: Comprehensive insurance coverage including medical (traditional and high-deductible health plans), prescription, dental, vision, life, disability, Employee Assistance Plan (“EAP”) and other supplemental benefit coverages.
  • As a leader in our field, Airbus Helicopters, Inc. provides relocation assistance for qualified positions and a comprehensive compensation and benefits package.

Your Challenges:

Manufacturing Process Development and Analysis:  80%

  • Develop manufacturing processes, procedures, and workflows for assembly of mechanical and electrical aerospace products.
  • Identifies and tracks risks and issues with manufacturing process quality, consistency, and efficiency; prioritizes leads and manages resolution of.
  • Create, release, version control, and maintain manufacturing work instructions.
  • Implement 5S in design of process, tool sets, part kits, and workstations
  • Maintains M-BOM, configuration control of, and compliance to engineering design.
  • Design tools, fixtures, jigs, and work space for efficient, accurate, and repeatable manufacturing assembly and test.
  • Determine in-process quality inspection steps required to achieve assembly requirements.
  • Conceptualize, design, develop, and fabricate in process and post-process static and dynamic test equipment for verification of critical to quality features and function.
  • Participate in design review with design engineering, providing input to design for manufacturability
  • Provide training to manufacturing staff on new procedures
  • Provides manufacturing decision-making information by calculating production, labor, and material costs; reviewing production schedules; estimating future requirements.
  • Support new production, and MRO.

Manufacturing Process Improvement:  10%

  • Evaluates manufacturing processes and identifies opportunities to improve quality, consistency, and efficiency; prioritized and leads implementation of.
  • Applies knowledge of product design, fabrication, assembly, tooling, and materials; conferring with equipment vendors; soliciting observations from operators to improve processes and environment.
  • Prepares product and process reports by collecting, analyzing, and summarizing information and trends.
  • Improves traceability of as-built product at all levels of child parts
  • Continuously improves processes with implementation of 5S methodologies.

Project Management:  10%

  • Ensure timely implementation of process improvement and general projects.
  • Assume the role of Project Manager for special projects, as requested.
  • Manage budgets, facilitation, timeline and change management for all assigned projects.

  • Knowledge and skills in use of and designing jigs and fixtures,
  • Knowledge in process inspection and traceability.
  • Knowledge of manufacturing ergonomics
  • Ability to read, analyze and interpret general business periodicals, professional journals, and technical procedures.

  • Experience working on UAS Composite Structure (secondary and primary).
  • Composite experience in other fields such as automotive, or boating will be considered.
  • Experience with 3D printing processes (ex: fused deposition modeling (FDM), selective laser sintering (SLS) or similar.
  • Engineering Processes for manufacturing and test work instructions.
  • Excellent computer skills, including Microsoft Office and OS.
  • One or more years of experience with mechanical design tools such as SolidWorks, CATIA, etc
