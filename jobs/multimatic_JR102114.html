Job Description

Champion the molding process flow design and development, equipment specification and cost assessment, create layout, analysis capacity etc主导包塑工艺流程设计与开发、设备规范确定及成本评估，制定布局规划、分析产能等. Develop and co-ordinate plant and equipment layout improvements, moulding process improvements, die change and first off improvements制定并协调工厂及设备布局改进、包塑工艺改进、换模及首件改进工作. Co-ordinate and follow up on the smooth introduction of new molding product to the company assist in selecting appropriate suppliers for subcontracted operations协调并跟进新产品顺利引入公司，协助为外包业务选择合适的供应商. Advise Management of any potential problems and opportunities for improvement就任何潜在问题及改进机会向管理层提供建议. Work in compliance with ISO 14001 / TS16949 standards遵守 ISO 14001/TS16949 标准开展工作. Participates in the engineering change (DPCRF) process by designing / updating Process Failure Mode Effects Analyses (PFMEAs) as needed, reviewing all operators instructions, ensuring assembly equipment readiness, packaging requirements are met, and all other Manufacturing Engineering tasks are completed on the ECN Checklist before the change effective date参与工程变更（DPCRF）流程，根据需要设计 / 更新过程失效模式及影响分析（PFMEA），审核操作指导书，确保装配设备就绪、包装要求得到满足，且在变更生效日期前，完成 ECN 清单上所有其他制造工程任务. Maintains regular daily presence on the production floor and provides engineering support to assembly in areas of safety, ergonomic evaluations, methods improvement, procurement and fabrication of necessary equipment to improve/complete any required process additions and/or alterations, and responding to their concerns/suggestions每日定期在生产现场值守，为装配环节提供工程支持，涉及安全、人体工程学评估、方法改进、必要设备的采购与制造（以完善 / 完成任何所需的工艺新增和 / 或变更），并回应相关关切与建议. Consider all aspects of industrial plant engineering when designing and/or implementing       process changes在设计和 / 或实施工艺变更时，考虑工业厂房工程的所有方面. Research, investigate and report manufacturing problems/concerns for improvement of         overall plant productivity including process cost reductions, monitoring and devising /            implementing corrective actions to minimize process downtimes and rejects调研、调查并报告制造问题 / 关切点，以提高工厂整体生产率，包括降低工艺成本、监控并制定 / 实施纠正措施以减少工艺停机时间和不合格品. Help provide new project equipment and production man-hour offer协助提供新项目的设备及生产工时报价. Choose qualified equipment suppliers, over-molding supplier, potential suppliers’ evaluation and position选择合格的设备供应商、包塑模具供应商，对潜在供应商进行评估及定位. Manages and follows up on all new program machine, over-molding testing (internal &

external) 管理并跟进所有新项目的机器、包塑模具测试（内部及外部）.

After equipment installation debugging, training operator and maintenance personnel设备安装调试后，培训操作员及维护人员. Control process change and relevant documents of update控制工艺变更及相关文件的更新.
