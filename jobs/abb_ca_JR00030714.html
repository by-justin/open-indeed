工作目标Primary Objectives of the Position

Responsible for mechanical design of MV switchgear.

负责中压开关设备的机械设计开发

主要工作职责Essential Duties & Responsibilities

1、  Select standard structural schemes and design non-standard schemes according to the specific requirements of the project.

根据项目的具体要求进行标准结构方案的选型及非标准方案的设计。

2、  Complete design tasks in a timely and accurate manner according to the project schedule requirements.

根据项目的进度要求，及时准确的完成设计任务。

3、  Cooperate in the production issue.

协调处理生产中产生的问题。

4、  Design new structural schemes to meet market requirements.

对市场要求设计新的结构方案。

Support pre-sales confirmation of design proposals

支持售前的设计方案确认。

5、  To write the relevant technical documents.

编写相关的技术文件。

岗位要求Qualifications & Requirements

Fresh graduates or with over 2 years of experience in medium-voltage switchgear design, familiar with medium-voltage switchgear standards and design specifications, familiar with the structure and primary principles of medium-voltage switchgear, and familiar with the manufacturing and process flow of medium-voltage switchgear. 应届毕业生或2年以上中压成套产品设计开发经验，熟悉中压成套产品标准和设计规范，熟悉中压成套产品结构、一次原理，熟悉中压成套产品生产制造、工艺流程。 Good command of power system knowledge and MV switchgear technology on manufacture process and standards. Be familiar with MS word, Excel, AutoCAD, Solidworks etc. 较全面、系统地掌握电力系统知识，了解中压开关柜的相关技术，流程及制造标准。熟练使用微软办公软件，AUTOCAD、SolidWorks等相关绘图软件。 Good communication skills. 良好的沟通技能。 Highly responsible, self-motivated and Willing to take on additional tasks. 工作主动负责，愿意承担额外工作任务。 Strong learning ability and team spirit 具备较强的学习能力和团队精神。 Being able to maintain a positive work attitude under pressure. 能在压力下保持积极的工作态度。 Ability to communicate in English, both written and oral. 英语流利。
