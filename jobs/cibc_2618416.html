Job Description

We’re building a relationship-oriented bank for the modern world. We need talented, passionate professionals who are dedicated to doing what’s right for our clients.

At CIBC, we embrace your strengths and your ambitions, so you are empowered at work. Our team members have what they need to make a meaningful impact and are truly valued for who they are and what they contribute.

To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com

What You’ll Be Doing The Analyst will be responsible for providing corporate credit analysis and transactional support within specific industries and product groups across Corporate Banking. The Analyst will work on credit analysis, financial modeling, conduct due diligence on new transactions and present proposed transactions to management and credit risk management.  In addition to conducting due diligence, the analyst will also be responsible for understanding the company, industry, regulatory issues, relevant economic/political trends, to help tailor coverage and financing solutions to meet client needs. The candidate will interface with internal product groups and assist in creating marketing materials.  The Analyst will also be responsible for on-going monitoring, including annual reviews, re-ratings of booked assets as well as portfolio reporting. Analysts work on a dedicated vertical within the Corporate Bank such as Consumer, Industrial and Telecom, Project Finance and Infrastructure, Power and Utilities, NBFI, Syndications, and Leveraged Finance Portfolio Management.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. Details on your work arrangement (proportion of on-site and remote work) will be discussed at the time of your interview.

How You’ll Succeed •    Draft and prepare pitch books with detailed industry materials and comprehensive corporate finance analyses •    Perform financial modeling, valuation, comparable and relative value analyses •    Conduct due diligence on new transactions •    Prepare internal screening and credit memos •    Present proposed transaction to management and credit group •    Assist in closing transactions, including managing external consultants, advisors and legal counsel •    Assist in the development and continued cultivation of client relationships •    Ability to effectively aggregate and synthesize data from a variety of platforms •    Support the ongoing monitoring of credit relationships by conducting credit analysis and preparing credit reviews •    Ensure that recommendation and opinions offered to clients are in compliance with legislation and at all times maintains the professional standards and policies of CIBC •    Interface with internal product groups, including rates, commodities, debt capital markets, and investment banking •    Assist in identifying new clients •    Collect, assemble and analyze market data

Who You Are •    Graduate with a degree in Business, Economics, Finance or related discipline (Graduation date of May 2026) •    Fundamental understanding of Corporate Finance theories and practices, well-developed financial analysis skills, including financial modeling •    Well-developed organizational skills in order to respond to shifting priorities on several simultaneous projects •    Strong ability to manage internal relationships within CIBC Capital Markets and across the broader organization •    Well-developed interpersonal, presentation, oral and written communication in order to convey factual and conceptual information to others and promote the interests of CIBC Capital Markets •    Ability to operate Microsoft Office software, particularly Word, Excel and PowerPoint •    Ability to work well and excel in a team environment •    Ability to think commercially about the needs of the client •    Demonstrated job commitment including a willingness to work extended hours and personal flexibility and adaptability in order to meet client requirements •    The successful candidate will be flexible to work hours as needed to help deliver a great client experience, which may include evenings and weekends. •    Values matter to you. You bring your real self to work and you live our values – trust, teamwork, and accountability.
