Job Description

Summary

Guidewire provides the industry-leading cloud platform for P&C insurers, trusted by 540+ insurers in 40 countries. We combine digital, core, analytics, and AI with an unmatched implementation track record and partner ecosystem.

Guidewire’s Go-to-Market team helps P&C insurers modernize on our cloud platform by combining deep industry expertise with AI-powered, data-driven solutions that transform the insurance lifecycle. We operate as one global, collaborative team focused on scaling innovation into new markets and product categories while ensuring every customer is successful and referenceable. You’ll join a culture grounded in curiosity, inclusion, and continuous improvement, where growth is shared and shaped by the people you work with. As a Senior Sales Executive for the Benelux region, you will own and grow a defined territory, driving a healthy mix of migration of existing business, expansion within current customers, and new logo acquisition. You’ll lead complex sales cycles with insurers, articulating the value of Guidewire’s cloud and AI capabilities, and aligning solutions to customer strategies. This is an opportunity to influence how the region’s insurers transform their businesses while contributing directly to Guidewire’s AI-powered growth and international expansion agenda.

Job Description

What you’ll do

  • Own territory management across the Benelux region, proactively generating a high-quality sales pipeline and delivering balanced growth across migration of existing business, expansion within current accounts, and new logo wins.
  • Build and deepen trusted relationships with IT and business decision-makers at insurers, positioning Guidewire as a strategic partner for cloud, data, and AI-enabled transformation.
  • Lead the full enterprise sales cycle from first contact to close, including discovery, value-based positioning, solution shaping with product experts, business case development, commercial negotiation, and seamless handover to post-sales teams.
  • Coordinate with Sales Operations, Marketing, Product, and Professional Services to execute territory and account plans that support Guidewire’s GTM priorities in AI-powered innovation, new product categories (such as pricing and underwriting), and international expansion into key EMEA markets.
  • Maintain accurate pipeline, forecasting, and account data in company systems, using insights to inform decisions, improve sales performance, and stay aligned with structured, data-driven GTM execution.
  • Manage post-sale executive relationships in collaboration with Customer Account Management and Customer Success, helping ensure adoption, measurable value realization, and strong customer references.
  • Deliver compelling technical and functional presentations and product demonstrations with specialists and advisors, clearly conveying how Guidewire’s cloud and AI capabilities improve operational efficiency, underwriting quality, and customer experience for P&C insurers.
  • Prepare persuasive proposals and quotations that align with client expectations and Guidewire guidelines, while monitoring competitor activity and sharing market and competitive insights with Marketing and Product to strengthen our positioning.
  • Represent Guidewire at customer meetings, onsite workshops, and insurance industry events across Belgium and the Netherlands, traveling regularly as needed to build presence and accelerate regional growth.

At Guidewire, we foster a culture of curiosity, innovation, and responsible use of AI—empowering our teams to continuously leverage emerging technologies and data-driven insights to enhance productivity and outcomes.

What you’ll bring

Required

  • Demonstrated ability to embrace AI and apply it to your current role as well as data-driven insights to drive innovation, productivity, and continuous improvement.
  • Significant experience in complex B2B software or SaaS sales, ideally within insurance, financial services, or closely related enterprise technology markets.
  • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets in a territory or account management role, including both new logo acquisition and expansion within existing customers.
  • Experience selling to both IT and business stakeholders, with the ability to navigate multi-level, multi-stakeholder decision processes and build long-term, trust-based executive relationships.
  • Strong consultative and value-based selling skills, including discovery, qualification, solution mapping, business case creation, and negotiation for high-value, multi-year software or platform deals.
  • Excellent collaboration skills, with a history of working cross-functionally with Marketing, Product, Services, and Operations to execute go-to-market and account strategies.
  • Clear, inclusive communication skills in written and spoken form, with the ability to adapt messaging for executive, technical, and business audiences.
  • Proficiency in English; additional fluency in French and/or Dutch is highly beneficial for success in the Benelux region.

Preferred

  • Experience selling cloud-based platforms, data/analytics, or AI-enabled solutions into P&C insurers or the wider insurance ecosystem.
  • Familiarity with enterprise sales methodologies and long-cycle, multi-million-euro software or platform transactions.
  • Knowledge of the Benelux P&C insurance landscape, including regulatory, competitive, and market dynamics.
  • Bachelor’s degree in business, technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Your Impact

We believe in clarity and setting you up for success. In your first six months, you will immerse yourself in Guidewire’s platform, GTM priorities, and Benelux territory, build relationships with key internal partners, and develop a focused territory plan that balances migration, expansion, and new logo growth.

You’ll qualify and advance strategic opportunities, refine value propositions around our cloud and AI capabilities, and help align internal teams around clear win strategies and measurable outcomes such as bookings, adoption milestones, and customer references.

As you grow in the role, your work will shape how insurers in Benelux modernize on Guidewire, influence our product and marketing direction through market feedback, and contribute directly to Guidewire’s mission to transform how the world’s P&C insurers do business.
