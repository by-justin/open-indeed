Context

The ITM Project aims to build a resilient and flexible ICT infrastructure that enhances business continuity and disaster recovery. It prioritizes replacing the legacy NS network with a modernized ITM Operational Network (ON) across the NATO Command Structure. The initiative transforms NATO’s IT operations from a decentralized model—characterized by distributed networks, server rooms, and local support—into a centralized approach with standardized services, centralized provisioning (IaaS), and a centralized ESOC managing infrastructure and service operations. This modernization enhances efficiency, security, and service consistency.

Job summary

The IVV ITM team role is to integrate and assure that the new features, requirements and bug fixes are implemented correctly (no new defects were introduced from one release to another). You will receive and analyze Dev team delivery (to accept or reject based on specific criteria), integrate the delivery following installation procedure on reference platform based in NATO facilities (Integration can be either remotely and/or in NATO facilities).
