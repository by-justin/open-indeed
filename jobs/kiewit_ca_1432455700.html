Responsibilities

Support and/or Lead Telecom Engineering & Design

Develop telecommunications and low-voltage system designs for industrial facilities, including fiber optic backbone, structured cabling, plant network architecture, wireless and radio coverage, paging/public address, CCTV, security, and access control systems.

Lead telecom scope development from concept through IFC design, construction support, commissioning, and turnover.

Apply NEC, NFPA, TIA/EIA, BICSI, ISA-95, IEC 61850, CSF, NERC / CIP, NIST and various other client standards to produce compliant, constructible designs.

Drive Technical Execution

Produce telecom deliverables such as one-lines, riser diagrams, block diagrams, interconnect diagrams, cable schedules, rack elevations, telecom room layouts, pathway plans, equipment data sheets, and installation details.

Prepare specifications and scopes of work for telecom equipment, cabling, and installation subcontracts.

Evaluate vendor bids, review submittals, and confirm technical compliance with project requirements.

Perform supporting analyses such as fiber optic link loss budgets, bandwidth and latency assessments, pathway fill, and wireless coverage or propagation studies.

Coordinate interfaces with electrical, instrumentation and controls, civil, structural, mechanical, and client IT / OT teams.

Support cybersecure network design through appropriate segmentation, access control, secure communication practices, and coordination with project cybersecurity requirements.

Support Construction & Commissioning

Respond to RFIs and field questions related to telecom systems, network infrastructure, security systems, and low-voltage installation.

Support FATs, site visits, field testing, troubleshooting, commissioning, and turnover activities as required.

Identify design constraints, propose practical solutions, and drive cost-effective engineering decisions.

Collaborate & Improve

Work closely with process, mechanical, electrical, instrumentation and controls, structural, civil, architectural, and construction teams.

Mentor designers and junior engineers, contributing to a culture of technical excellence.

Lead quality checks to ensure accuracy, consistency, constructability, and coordination across design packages.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related technical discipline.

  • 5+ years of relevant experience in telecommunications or low-voltage systems design, preferably for data centers or mission-critical facilities.

  • Working knowledge of fiber optic and copper structured cabling systems, network topologies, telecom rooms, pathways, and cabling infrastructure.

  • Experience producing telecom design documents in AutoCAD, Revit, or similar design tools.

  • Knowledge of applicable codes and standards, including NEC and TIA/EIA structured cabling standards.

  • Ability to manage multiple assignments, communicate clearly with project teams, clients, and vendors, and work effectively across disciplines.

  • Willingness to travel up to 20% of the time to project locations

Preferred Experience

  • Professional Engineer (PE) license.

  • BICSI RCDD Certification.

  • BICSI DCDC / OSP / RTPM credentials.

  • Experience with industrial control system networks, OT/IT convergence, and cybersecurity considerations for plant networks.

  • Network/Cybersecurity certifications – CompTIA Net+/Sec+/Pen+ or equivalent

  • Experience with licensed and unlicensed radio systems, DAS, Wi-Fi, or private LTE/5G deployments at industrial sites.

  • Understanding and practical application of NERC / CIP requirements for network architecture, access control, asset documentation, and cybersecurity compliance in utility or industrial environments.

  • EPC project support through design, construction, startup, and commissioning.

  • Familiarity with hazardous area classification as it applies to communication equipment installation.
