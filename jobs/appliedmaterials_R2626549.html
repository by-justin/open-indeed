Logistics Operations III Workday-Aligned Job Description Draft | Kalispell Outbound Logistics General Profile Logistics Operations III is an experienced, cross-functional operations role responsible for coordinating complex outbound shipments, maintaining shipment readiness visibility, supporting SAP WM execution, and helping implement new logistics functions that improve standardization, traceability, freight planning, shipment documentation, and operational handoffs across Kalispell outbound operations. The role operates with a limited degree of supervision, supports colleagues through practical process knowledge, and functions as a key connector between manufacturing, engineering, order management, freight partners, GIS, and logistics leadership. Position Summary This role owns and coordinates the operational flow required to move customer-facing systems, revenue NSOs, shipkits, LKPs, parcels, and freight from production readiness through shipment completion and post-shipment documentation. The position requires strong SAP and logistics process knowledge, proactive risk management, clear communication, and the ability to translate ambiguous operational inputs into executable shipment plans and sustainable logistics processes. Functional Knowledge • Applies developed proficiency in SAP WM and outbound logistics processes, including delivery notes, shipment numbers, packing documentation, proforma/commercial invoice support, PGI coordination, and shipment reversal or exception troubleshooting. • Maintains shipment readiness data using MPP, Smartsheet, revenue order trackers, shipping calendars, Q2C uploads, and other shared logistics tools. • Uses transportation management, freight quoting, and logistics planning processes for freight and parcels, including systems, NSOs, MMS, and other oversized or international shipments. • Understands crating, toting, NSSC, Ship Services, bill(s)-of-material, packing lists, and freight-forwarder dependencies well enough to identify gaps before shipment execution. • Supports weekly shipment reviews, scrum updates, and cross-functional readiness discussions by maintaining current shipment status, risk, and document visibility. Business Expertise • Understands how outbound logistics integrates with production control, manufacturing engineering, planning, order management, business management, GIS, freight forwarding, TransPak (crating), and customer-facing shipment commitments. • Recognizes the operational impact of late floor completions, missing components, unresolved bill-ofmaterial or packing-list gaps, incomplete freight planning inputs, and process disconnects between production, logistics, and transportation systems. • Uses operational knowledge to help Kalispell align with broader Applied Materials logistics standards through implementation of new logistics functions, standardized shipment documentation, improved system readiness, and stronger shipment traceability. • Balances customer commit dates, packing/crating readiness, freight carrier availability, cost, transit time, and escalation risk when coordinating shipments. Leadership • Acts as a resource for team members and cross-functional partners by explaining logistics requirements, SAP constraints, shipment priorities, and system/process expectations. • Leads or coordinates logistics improvement workstreams such as new logistics function implementation, transportation system readiness, shipment process mapping, hardware and label readiness, data validation, and outbound capacity/resource planning inputs. • Frames concerns and risks clearly for leadership so decisions can be made around staffing, space, workload, crating capacity, shipment prioritization, and implementation readiness. • Promotes ethical, professional, and quality-focused execution by documenting issues, escalating risks early, and encouraging process discipline across teams. Problem Solving • Provides solutions for atypical or infrequently occurring shipment problems by using SAP history, prior shipment records, freight-forwarder information, institutional knowledge, and cross-functional contacts. • Troubleshoots shipment blockers related to billing blocks, delivery notes, SAP WM inventory status, shipment consolidation, crating readiness, freight-forwarder notification, packing-list accuracy, label/document generation, and logistics process gaps. • Identifies process weaknesses where tribal knowledge, delayed production input, missing bill-of-material data, manual handoffs, or unclear ownership create risk, then proposes structured improvements or automation opportunities. • Converts vague shipment issues into actionable next steps, owners, data needs, and escalation paths. Impact • Impacts on-time shipment execution, shipping documentation accuracy, customer experience, freight readiness, and the ability of production and order-management teams to meet commit dates. • Improves the quality and reliability of outbound operations by maintaining accurate shipment visibility, supporting documentation controls, and helping reduce single points of failure in manual shipping processes. • Supports cost and schedule outcomes by comparing freight options, timing shipments to preserve delivery dates, consolidating opportunities when appropriate, and reducing avoidable expediting or rework. • Influences operational readiness for ramp activity by surfacing capacity constraints, staffing gaps, systemreadiness risks, process dependencies, and implementation needs. Interpersonal Skills • Uses clear, consistent communication to exchange shipment status, requirements, blockers, and escalation needs with production, planning, BMO, GIS, order management, freight forwarders, TransPak, and leadership. • Translates between different operational languages and identifiers, including opportunity numbers, sales orders, purchase orders, delivery notes, shipment numbers, slot numbers, bill-of-materials, packing lists, crates, totes, and work centers. • Builds trust with cross-functional partners by being responsive, factual, organized, and focused on helping others complete their part of the shipment process. • Communicates risks professionally even when issues are urgent, ambiguous, or cross-functional. Core Responsibilities • Coordinate outbound shipments for customer-facing systems, revenue NSOs, LKPs, MMS/AGS/eSR/return shipments, and other freight or parcel movements as assigned. • Develop packing lists for systems and NSOs, and work with TransPak to ensure crating accuracy and timeliness • Respond to crate orders and intercampus movement requests from multiple departments • Administer office and packaging materials through Ariba and various vendors and suppliers • Maintain revenue order tracking, shipping calendars, shipment status trackers, and Q2C documentation for required shipping records such as delivery notes, packing lists, AWBs/BOLs, PODs, and related shipment evidence. • Arrange freight-forwarder engagement and pickup readiness after appropriate crating, packing, documentation, weight, dimension, and shipment approval inputs are available. • Support SAP WM shipment execution and issue resolution, including delivery-note creation support, shipment number follow-up, label/paperwork printing, billing block or reversal coordination, inventory status checks, and escalation to SAP/GIS support when needed. • Lead or materially support implementation of new logistics functions for plant 4020, including requirements definition, data readiness, testing coordination, permissions, label/printer readiness, location or handlingunit considerations, handoff design, and cross-functional process alignment. • Lead or materially support transportation and logistics system testing and readiness for 4020, including script review, test-data validation, WM-specific scenario updates, label/document questions, and coordination with project SMEs. • Develop and improve logistics request processes, shipment prioritization practices, NSSC consolidation concepts, shipment visibility tools, and operational handoffs. • Escalate capacity, resource, space, staffing, or process-readiness concerns to leadership with practical impact statements and proposed path-forward options. Preferred Qualifications / Experience • Hands-on experience in logistics operations, SAP WM, shipment documentation, freight coordination, packing/crating workflows, and cross-functional manufacturing support. • Working knowledge of SAP delivery notes, shipment numbers, delivery flow, packing/PGI dependencies, proforma/commercial invoice timing, and freight-forwarder documentation requirements. • Experience with transportation/logistics platforms, Smartsheet, SharePoint, Teams, Excel, and operational data trackers. • Ability to work independently under pressure, prioritize competing shipments and projects, and drive clarity when ownership or process inputs are unclear. • Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to influence without direct authority. Success Measures • Customer-facing shipments are planned with sufficient lead time, accurate shipment data, and documented readiness status. • Shipment documents and Q2C records are complete, organized, and retrievable. • Freight-forwarder engagement, crating readiness, SAP dependencies, transportation-system dependencies, and logistics handoffs are identified early enough to prevent avoidable shipment misses. • Operational gaps are surfaced with clear business impact, owner recommendations, and next-step options. • New logistics function implementation activities are advanced with accurate site-specific inputs and practical validation scenarios.
