Our expectations:

We are seeking a highly motivated and dynamic and data-savvy intern to join P&WC AI Center of Excellence (AI COE) team as an Advanced Data Analytics Products & Change Management intern.

This position offers an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced and innovative environment. The successful candidate will be part of AI projects and work closely with cross-functional teams to support the implementation of Agile methodologies and drive effective change management strategies. This intern will also contribute to further develop and grow the AI COE.

What your day to day will look like:

  • The successful co-op candidate will be part of a cross-functional team focused on delivering PoCs and MVPs using data science and organizational change management skills.
  • You will be part of the delivery team applying agile framework and organizational change management skills, while also working closely with the data science and business teams, to apply your skills to real business problems.
  • Specializing in areas such as process optimization, predictive analytics and materials management science, you will play an important role in delivering a business transformation through advanced analytics. You will be key to the execution of the use case, acting as a bridge between the business and the analytics specialists, measuring and sharing insights with stakeholders and collecting inputs to verify analytics results. Your role will also include supporting the change management team with process documentation, building training packages and plans, and KPI measurement.
