Principal / Senior Principal Design Verification Engineer – CXL / PCIe / Ethernet:

Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced  Principal / Senior Principal Design Verification Engineer  to lead the verification of next-generation connectivity IPs and SoCs powering AI and cloud infrastructure. This is a highly visible technical leadership role where you will define verification strategies, drive methodology improvements, mentor engineering teams, and collaborate across architecture, RTL, firmware, validation, and silicon teams to ensure first-pass silicon success.

The ideal candidate is passionate about building world-class verification environments, has deep expertise in protocol verification, and enjoys solving complex architectural challenges.

Key Responsibilities

Technical Leadership

  • Lead the verification strategy for complex IPs and SoC subsystems from architecture through silicon.

  • Define verification methodologies, reusable verification frameworks, and best practices across multiple programs.

  • Drive verification planning, execution, coverage closure, and sign-off.

  • Identify verification risks early and proactively drive mitigation plans.

  • Lead technical reviews including architecture, RTL, verification, and tape-out readiness reviews.

Design Verification

  • Architect and develop scalable UVM/System Verilog verification environments.

  • Develop constrained-random, coverage-driven verification methodologies.

  • Design scoreboards, protocol monitors, checkers, assertions (SVA), and functional coverage models.

  • Debug complex RTL, protocol, timing, and integration issues.

  • Drive regression planning, automation, and continuous integration improvements.

  • Develop reusable VIP and verification infrastructure across multiple product lines.

Protocol Verification

Lead verification efforts involving one or more of:

  • Compute Express Link (CXL 2.x / 3.x)

  • PCI Express (Gen4/Gen5/Gen6)

  • Ethernet (10G–800G)

  • AXI, APB, AHB

  • High-speed SerDes interfaces

  • Memory interfaces including DDR/LPDDR/HBM (preferred)

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Work closely with Architecture teams to influence design for verification.

  • Partner with RTL designers during implementation and debug.

  • Collaborate with Firmware, Emulation, FPGA, Post-Silicon Validation, and System Engineering teams.

  • Support customer issue analysis and silicon bring-up when required.

Technical Mentorship

  • Mentor and coach verification engineers across multiple experience levels.

  • Establish verification standards and engineering excellence across the organization.

  • Drive innovation in automation, verification productivity, and quality metrics.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.

  • Minimum10+ years (Principal) or 15+ years (Senior Principal) of experience in Design Verification.

  • Strong expertise in System Verilog and UVM.

  • Extensive experience in verification planning, execution, and sign-off.

  • Deep understanding of constrained-random verification and coverage-driven methodologies.

  • Strong experience with assertions (SVA), functional coverage, and protocol compliance.

  • Excellent debugging skills involving RTL, simulation, protocol analysis, and waveform debugging.

  • Experience with industry-standard simulators such as Synopsys VCS, Cadence Xcelium, or Siemens Questa.

Preferred Technical Expertise

Strong experience in several of the following areas:

  • CXL Cache, Memory, and I/O protocols

  • PCIe Transaction, Data Link, and Physical Layers

  • Ethernet MAC/PCS/PMA architecture

  • Coherency protocols

  • High-speed interconnects

  • AI accelerator connectivity

  • Data center SoC architecture

  • Multi-chip and chiplet-based systems

  • Performance verification

  • Low-power verification

  • CDC/RDC verification

  • Formal Verification

  • Portable Stimulus (PSS)

  • Python, Perl, Tcl, or similar scripting languages

  • Emulation and FPGA prototyping

Leadership Expectations

  • Own technical direction for large verification programs.

  • Influence architecture and product quality through early engagement.

  • Drive cross-functional execution across geographically distributed teams.

  • Establish reusable verification IP and methodology.

  • Mentor engineers and help grow technical capability within the organization.

  • Champion engineering excellence, quality, and continuous improvement.

Preferred Experience

  • Experience leading verification for large SoCs or complex connectivity IPs.

  • Proven success delivering multiple first-pass silicon programs.

  • Experience with AI infrastructure, cloud networking, or hyperscale data center technologies.

  • Familiarity with customer-facing debug and post-silicon issue resolution.

  • Contributions to verification methodology, reusable frameworks, or industry standards are a strong plus.
