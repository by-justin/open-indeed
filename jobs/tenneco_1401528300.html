Job Description

Position Summary

The Head of Talent Acquisition will lead the end-to-end recruitment strategy and execution for India across all business units, plants, engineering centers, shared services, and corporate functions. The role is responsible for developing and implementing a robust talent acquisition strategy that supports business growth, strengthens employer branding, enhances candidate experience, and ensures the timely hiring of high-quality talent.

Key Responsibilities

Talent Acquisition Strategy

  • Develop and execute the India Talent Acquisition strategy aligned with business objectives and workforce planning.

  • Build proactive talent pipelines for critical and leadership positions.

  • Drive innovative sourcing strategies to attract top talent across manufacturing, engineering, digital, finance, and corporate functions.

  • Partner with business leaders to anticipate future talent requirements.

Recruitment Operations

  • Lead end-to-end recruitment delivery across all India locations.

  • Ensure achievement of hiring targets, time-to-fill, quality of hire, and cost-per-hire metrics.

  • Drive recruitment process standardization and continuous improvement.

  • Monitor recruitment effectiveness through dashboards and analytics.

Stakeholder Management

  • Act as a strategic talent advisor to business and HR leadership teams.

  • Collaborate with plant leadership, functional leaders, and global talent acquisition teams.

  • Ensure strong partnership with hiring managers to improve hiring outcomes and candidate experience.

Employer Branding & Talent Marketing

  • Strengthen Tenneco's employer brand in the market.

  • Lead campus hiring, apprenticeship, diversity hiring, and leadership hiring initiatives.

  • Build partnerships with universities, technical institutes, and industry forums.

Leadership Hiring & Succession Support

  • Lead recruitment for senior leadership and critical positions.

  • Support succession planning by creating external talent pipelines for key roles.

  • Ensure robust assessment and selection methodologies.

Team Leadership

  • Lead and develop the India Talent Acquisition team.

  • Establish performance standards and capability-building initiatives.

  • Foster a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Compliance & Governance

  • Ensure compliance with company policies, labor regulations, and recruitment governance standards.

  • Maintain recruitment data integrity and reporting accuracy.

  • Manage recruitment vendors, agencies, and outsourcing partners.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Time to Fill

  • Quality of Hire

  • Offer Acceptance Rate

  • Diversity Hiring Metrics

  • Recruitment Cost Efficiency

  • Hiring Manager Satisfaction

  • Candidate Experience Scores

  • Leadership Position Closure Rate

  • Campus Hiring Effectiveness

  • Talent Pipeline Readiness

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree required; MBA/PGDM in Human Resources preferred.

  • 12–18 years of progressive HR/Talent Acquisition experience.

  • Experience leading Talent Acquisition for a large manufacturing, automotive, industrial, or engineering organization.

  • Proven track record in leadership hiring, plant hiring, campus recruitment, and talent strategy.

  • Strong analytical, stakeholder management, and leadership capabilities.

Critical Competencies

  • Strategic Talent Planning

  • Leadership & Team Management

  • Business Acumen

  • Workforce Planning

  • Executive Hiring

  • Talent Market Intelligence

  • Data & Analytics

  • Change Management

  • Influencing & Stakeholder Management

  • Employer Branding
