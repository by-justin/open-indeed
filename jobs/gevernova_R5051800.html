Job Description

If you have a passion for technology and want a career in engineering, we want you! As a Co-op/Intern for the Critical Infrastructure Communications (CIC) team we will prepare you for an exciting career, tackling one of the world’s toughest challenges providing industrial communications equipment to help enable the grid of the future.

What you'll do

  • Read and fully understand documentation and engineering specifications that are public, private and/or provided by customers.
  • Create and configure test environments, including but not limited to Robot Framework, LabVIEW, Test STAND, Selenium, etc.
  • Report Product defects as discovered into Jira Database
  • Utilize Jira or other system to track work activities
  • Develop and maintain specific product automated test suites
  • Develop test scripts using Python
  • Develop test suites within Robot Framework
  • Maintain existing Automation developed previously in LabVIEW
  • Ability to develop new test automation including but not limited to LabVIEW
  • Assemble and maintain the hardware configuration and test setup to enable the test automation
  • Receive technical training, mentorship, and coaching from world-class technologists and technical managers.
  • Grow your technical expertise in software and test automation and expand your skills in new areas.
  • Gain exposure to industrialization at our onsite manufacturing plant in Rochester, NY.
  • Work on an assignment / project within engineering & technology to stretch your learning and skillset.
  • Report progress and status to project management. Perform rough estimates of time required to implement features and resolve issues, helping with task prioritization.
  • Use your academic knowledge to complete assigned project(s), deliver positive outcomes, and contribute to the success of our team.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
