  As a Mechanical Engineer, you’ll leverage your strong foundation in physics, mathematics, and materials science to process and resolve mechanical problems, design and implement custom mechanical tooling and associated processes, and enable the handling, assembly, and disassembly of parts, components, sub-assemblies, and final assemblies throughout the product life cycle. You'll be responsible for designing, developing, and modifying mechanical engineering layouts, schematics, and specifications. You will oversee manufacturing and installation processes, ensuring adherence to industry standards and regulations-- and you'll coordinate the procurement and assembly of mechanical components and equipment for design projects, identifying sources of critical parts and subsystems to resolve technical issues.

Role overview :

We are looking for an experienced  Senior R&D Mechanical Engineer with approximately  10+ years of industry experience  in high precision mechanical design,  advanced materials engineering , and design for manufacturability. This role is ideal for a senior professional who combines deep technical expertise with hands-on shopfloor understanding and the ability to influence design direction, manufacturing strategy, and engineering standards.

The primary emphasis of this position is on  materials selection, materials behavior in high-tech applications, and precision machine component design

Role Responsibilities:

  • Lead the mechanical design of complex, high precision components and assemblies for advanced equipment, ensuring robust functionality, manufacturability, and long term reliability.
  • Serve as the  materials authority  for mechanical components—evaluating metals, alloys, coatings, composites, polymers, and their performance under demanding environments (vacuum, thermal, dynamic loads). Own technical decisions related to materials, finishes, coatings, and critical tolerances.
  • Apply expert level knowledge of  GD&T, tolerance stack up analysis, and precision engineering principles  to develop designs that meet stringent performance and quality requirements.
  • Guide and mentor junior engineers on materials usage, DFM, machining practices, drawing standards, and engineering best practices.
  • Drive collaboration with manufacturing engineering, machining vendors, suppliers, metrology, and quality teams to optimize manufacturability, reduce costs, and improve design robustness.
  • Lead design reviews,  material selection workshops , and manufacturability assessments for new product introductions and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Provide technical leadership in resolving complex shop floor issues—leveraging hands-on experience from precision machining and assembly environments.
  • Oversee the creation of detailed 3D models, engineering drawings, and documentation using CAD tools (SolidWorks, Creo, or similar).
  • Support prototype builds, validation testing, and material performance evaluations.

Required Qualifications & Experience:

  • Bachelor’s or master’s degree in mechanical engineering, Materials Engineering, or related field.
  • 10+ years of experience  in mechanical design, with majority spent in high precision manufacturing industries (semiconductors, optical systems, aerospace, robotics, or similar).
  • Deep technical expertise in  materials behavior , mechanical properties and high precision applications.
  • Proven track record in  precision machining , including machining constraints, tooling considerations, tolerancing, and manufacturability optimization.
  • Mastery of  GD&T, tolerance analysis, design standards , and engineering documentation.
  • Strong shopfloor understanding—hands-on experience with machining, metrology, or assembly strongly preferred.
  • Experience collaborating with suppliers and manufacturing partners on high precision component development.
  • Engineering leadership experience: mentoring engineers, driving design decisions, and influencing cross functional teams.
  • Excellent communication and ability to represent the mechanical design function in technical and cross disciplinary discussions.

​ Preferred Skills:

  • Experience with high purity, high vacuum, thermal critical or contamination sensitive applications.
  • Familiarity with coating technologies, heat treatment processes, and advanced material performance testing.
  • Exposure to basic simulation methodologies FEA/thermal —as a complementary skill, not a core competency.
  • Experience in cleanroom or ultraprecision manufacturing environments.
