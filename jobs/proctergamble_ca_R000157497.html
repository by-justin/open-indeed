Job Description

Engineering & Manufacturing Internship

Plant: Milenio Start Date: as soon as possible

Location: Irapuato, Guanajuato Work Hours: Part-time / On-site. Profile: Current Engineering students (any discipline) expecting to graduate between December 2027 and December 2028. Salary: The gross salary for interns ranges from 9,000mxn to 18,000mxn depending on hours worked. Duration: 12-18 months.

Accommodations: At P&G, we value diversity and inclusion. If you have a disability and need any accommodations during the hiring process, please let us know when applying. We want to make sure you have the best experience possible.

Are you excited to kickstart your engineering career in a fast-paced, real-world environment? Do you want to learn, grow, and be part of a team that makes products millions of people use every day? Then this internship is for you!

About the role: As an intern, you’ll get hands-on experience in different areas of manufacturing and engineering. You can explore roles such as:

  • Digital & Automation: Participate in the analysis and improvement of industrial processes, and help define technical standards for new technologies.
  • Health, Safety & Environment: Support projects that protect the environment and keep everyone safe at work.
  • Quality: Learn how we keep our products safe and consistent by helping with quality checks and training.
  • Continuous Improvement: Be part of projects that make our operations better, faster, and smarter.
  • Production – Making: Help bring our products to life by supporting the manufacturing process and finding ways to improve it.
  • Production – Packing: Work on packaging products, setting up pallets, and helping install new equipment.
  • Warehouse: Assist in organizing and optimizing storage, making sure everything is tracked and ready to ship.
  • Project Management: Help plan and run projects that keep things moving smoothly.
  • Process Engineer: Analyze and improve how we make our products, helping us work more efficiently.

What you’ll do:

  • Work alongside experienced professionals on real projects
  • Learn how a world-class manufacturing plant operates
  • Develop skills like problem-solving, teamwork, and communication
  • Get hands-on with equipment, data analysis, and process improvements

What We Offer:

  • You will experience business responsibilities from Day 1  – You work every single day on key projects from the beginning.
  • You will be part of the P&G family since day one.
  • Attractive salary and benefits package  – for you to keep your studies as a priority,  salary is linked to the number of hours worked per day: 4 hours, 6.5 hours, or 8 hours.
  • You will receive continuous coaching & mentorship  – We are passionate about our work.
  • We will make sure you receive both formal training and as regular mentorship from your manager and others.
  • You'll get the chance to be a dynamic and encouraging work environment – We live our Purpose, Values, and Principles daily.
  • Flex arrangements  - We value every individual and encourage initiatives promoting agility and work/life balance.
  • We love hardworking people; you will have the opportunity to be promoted to full-time following successful completion of your Internship.

Job Qualifications

Important: Before applying, check out our Hiring Process and Assessment Information to get ready for the next steps.

Hard skills we’re looking for:

  • Technical proficiency relevant to manufacturing or engineering processes
  • Ability to analyze equipment performance and operational data
  • Proficiency in English (written and spoken)

Soft Skills:

  • Team orientation and collaborative mindset
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Positive “can-do” attitude and leadership potential

Bonus skills:

  • Experience or coursework in project management or process improvement
  • Knowledge of sustainability or environmental management principles
  • Multicultural work environment experience
