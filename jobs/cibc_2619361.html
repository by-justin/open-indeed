CIBC Capital Markets London – Corporate Banking Summer Analyst Programme

CIBC is a leading Canadian-based financial institution with a market capitalization of more than CA$100 billion. Through our three major business units - Retail and Business Banking, Wealth Management and Capital Markets – more than 44,000 employees provide a full range of financial products and services to 11 million individual, small business, commercial, corporate and institutional clients in Canada, the U.S. and around the world.

Through our Capital Markets Summer Analyst programme, you will get direct work experience and exposure to the Corporate Banking team at CIBC. CIBC offers investment banking advisory services, corporate lending and top-ranked research to corporate, government and institutional clients around the world. In Europe the Bank’s focus is on Infrastructure, Renewables and Energy Transition.

As a Capital Markets Summer Analyst you will have the opportunity to:

  • Participate in an orientation session to learn more about the bank and our Capital Markets business here in the region. Work within deal teams on any live transactions taking place, gaining experience across different products, industries and clients
  • Understand how the different businesses and markets operate by rotating around various product desks
  • Assist in idea generation, financial markets analysis, and risk monitoring
  • Work on specific projects across various industries throughout the origination and execution phase to the asset management
  • Attend and contribute to team meetings
