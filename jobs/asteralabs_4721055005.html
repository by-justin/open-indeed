Role Overview

Astera Labs is seeking a hands-on  Senior Sourcing Specialist – Germany Operations & Manufacturing  to support sourcing and procurement activities for our operations in Aachen, Germany. This role is responsible for supplier management, purchasing operations, commercial negotiations, and procurement support for manufacturing operations, engineering infrastructure, capital equipment, facilities, laboratories, and corporate services. The role will also provide support for materials management and logistics activities as needed to help ensure efficient operation of the Aachen site.

The ideal candidate will serve as a key sourcing partner for the Aachen site, working closely with Manufacturing, Engineering, Operations, Facilities, Finance, and Global Procurement teams to ensure the timely and cost-effective acquisition of equipment, materials, and services required to support manufacturing operations and site growth.

The ideal candidate will bring experience supporting  optical communications technologies , advanced manufacturing, engineering, laboratory, or other highly technical operating environments. Experience sourcing capital equipment, technical equipment, specialized services, or manufacturing-related products and services is highly desirable.

This position requires strong sourcing and procurement fundamentals, supplier relationship management skills, attention to detail, and cross-functional collaboration. The successful candidate will help execute sourcing initiatives, support supplier relationships, negotiate pricing and commercial terms, and contribute to procurement process improvements that support Astera Labs' continued growth.

A Day in the Life

In this role, you'll work closely with Manufacturing, Engineering, Facilities, Quality, Finance, and Operations teams to support a growing production and engineering environment. You will help ensure manufacturing and operational readiness by sourcing critical equipment, managing supplier relationships, obtaining quotations, supporting commercial discussions, and coordinating the procurement of materials and services needed to support day-to-day operations.

Your responsibilities will range from sourcing production capital equipment and manufacturing consumables to supporting facility expansion projects, supplier performance initiatives, and logistics coordination. While the primary focus of this role is sourcing and procurement, you will also assist with materials planning, inventory tracking, and supplier delivery management to help support operational continuity as the Aachen operation continues to scale.

This is a highly collaborative role requiring strong organization, responsiveness, customer service, and the ability to manage multiple sourcing activities across a diverse group of stakeholders.

  • Source specialized manufacturing equipment, laboratory equipment, automation systems, precision instrumentation, and technical services supporting production and engineering activities.

  • Support Facilities teams with site expansions, facility upgrades, equipment installations, contractor sourcing, and workplace infrastructure projects.

  • Assist with materials planning, inventory monitoring, and supplier delivery coordination to help ensure material availability and continuity of operations.

  • Support logistics coordination, including inbound shipments, freight management, import/export activities, shipment tracking, and supplier delivery performance.

  • Manage purchase requisitions, purchase orders, delivery tracking, and invoice resolution activities.

  • Coordinate supplier onboarding, compliance documentation, and supplier performance reviews.

  • Collaborate with Manufacturing, Finance, Operations, and Global Procurement teams to ensure purchasing activities align with production schedules, budgets, forecasts, and company policies.

  • Identify opportunities for cost savings, supplier improvements, process efficiencies, and operational enhancements.

  • Maintain procurement records and ensure compliance with company policies and applicable regulatory requirements.
