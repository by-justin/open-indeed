About the job

Our Hubs are a crucial part of how we innovate, improving performance across every Sanofi department and providing a springboard for the amazing work we do. Build a career and you can be part of transforming our business while helping to change millions of lives. Ready? As Candidate Care Partner within our Talent Acquisition Team.

Ready to push the limits of what’s possible? Join Sanofi’s talent team and you can play a vital role in the performance of our entire business while helping to make an impact on millions around the world.

This is an opportunity in our Global Talent Services function at Sanofi Hubs. Sanofi ‘Hubs’ are where our key strategic business operations are hosted providing centralised services across Global Medical, Finance, People, Procurement, Digital, R&D and more.

Our Vision : We deliver best-in-class enterprise solutions and are the catalyst for modernization and transformation, enabling Sanofi to chase the miracles of science. Hubs are synonym for GBS/GCC that is widely known in the industry.

Global Talent Services is one of our key service lines in Hubs part of Chief Talent Office that helps provide E-2-E Talent Acquisition services globally to businesses spread across General Medicines, Specialty care, Vaccines, R&D, Manufacturing from Hubs across regions in proximity. The Candidate Care Partner is a champion for delivering exceptional candidate experiences within their designated hub team. Leading and mentoring a team of Candidate Care Specialists, this role ensures consistent, positive, and timely interactions with candidates throughout the recruitment lifecycle. The Candidate Care Partner is passionate about continuous improvement, leveraging data insights and candidate and hiring manager feedback to enhance processes and elevate the candidate experience within their hub. This role will be responsible for leading a multicultural team of Candidate Care Specialists, who coordinate, schedule, and close administrative tasks to facilitate hiring across North America, LATAM & Canada, managing high-volume hiring needs. Across 2023/2024 our last few years average hiring volumes has been in the region of 3000+. This is subjected to growth as we expand our launches in the region. You will be responsible for assisting in hiring the best talent for Sanofi and do this while providing world class candidate experience. You will also be required to ensure service levels are met and hence will/may need to schedule as well.

We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. We’re also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started?

Main responsibilities:

  • Lead, motivate, and support a team of Candidate Care Specialists within the hub location, fostering a collaborative and high-performing team environment.​
  • Oversee the day-to-day delivery of candidate care services (eg: interview scheduling, post offer support) within the region, ensuring all candidate and hiring manager queries are addressed accurately, efficiently, and empathetically.​
  • Monitor candidate communication channels, ensuring timely and effective delivery of all candidate-facing messaging.​
  • Track and analyze key performance metrics related to candidate care, including candidate and hiring manager satisfaction feedback and response times.​
  • Continuously evaluate and identify opportunities to enhance candidate care processes within the hub. Conduct research and market analysis to stay informed about best practices in candidate care and identify opportunities to enhance Sanofi's approach.​
  • Leverage technology, data analysis, and industry best practices to streamline workflows, improve efficiency, and elevate the candidate experience.​
  • Partner with other Hub Team Leads and Local Activation teams to implement standardized and consistent candidate interactions that are tailored to the specific needs and expectations of the local region.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
