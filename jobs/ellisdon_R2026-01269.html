As a Project Administrator, you will:

  • Perform daily administrative tasks and support EllisDon office operations—including reception and mail services—by answering and routing incoming calls in a prompt, professional, and courteous manner.
  • Greet all company visitors, direct them to meeting rooms, and ensure the relevant person is notified of their arrival.
  • Prepare and document all outgoing internal and external mail and packages, and arrange for timely mail pickup and delivery.
  • Organize internal meetings as needed; this may include booking the appropriate meeting room, placing catering orders, coordinating with IT, setting up the room, and tidying up the meeting space afterward.
  • Manage incoming and outgoing mail, document room schedules, maintain various spreadsheets, and print training materials.
  • Assist managers with calendar management, client follow-ups, tracking industry events, planning or organizing internal events, compiling reports, monitoring emails, and handling urgent matters.

​

Is this the right role for you? ​

  • One to three years of experience in office administration or coordination.
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team, as well as under pressure and within tight deadlines.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to exercise good judgment by assessing the urgency of a situation, making decisions within the scope of authority, and involving the appropriate people when necessary.
  • Demonstrate a high level of professionalism and confidentiality when handling sensitive information, both internally and externally Proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google software, along with general technical knowledge Strong customer service orientation and a pleasant telephone manner
  • Bilingual (French and English) Proficiency in English is required to perform certain duties associated with the role, including communicating with stakeholders outside Quebec and consulting documentation available only in English.
  • Eligible for a "Secret" security clearance from the Government of Canada.

About Open Indeed

Open Indeed is a free, public job board created to help Canadian undergraduate students and new graduates discover verified tech internships, co-ops, and early-career opportunities.

Postings are indexed directly from official employer career portals via an autonomous scraping pipeline and updated on a recurring schedule—keeping listings timely, accurate, and completely free of spam or recruiter reposts.

The complete data collection and indexing pipeline is currently undergoing active reliability testing and will be fully open-sourced once thoroughly verified.
