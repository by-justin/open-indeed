KEY RESPONSIBILITIES / DUTIES 主要职责:

Perform all types of inspection per QS requirement to maintain high quality and to fulfill the production plans with close to general quality monitoring to all shop floor practices. 按照质量系统的要求完成所有类型的检验保持高质量，并且在完成计划的同时密切的监控整个生产活动。

  • Continuously fully comply with all GMP, company procedure document requirements.

持续的完全符合GMP,公司程序文件的要求。

  • Will perform, incoming, manufacturing inspections of products/component parts in accordance with the most current specifications, Ensure them meet company and QSR standards.

按照当前规范执行进料检验,过程检验和最终检验,包装检验保证确保产品符合公司质量系统的标准。

  • Responsible to stop further processing in the event of encountering non-conforming products condition.  了当遇到产品异常状态时，负责停止产品进入下一步流程。
  • Using gauges and other reliant inspection equipment in compliance to inspection equipment procedure requirement. Maintain the good condition of them and ensure all inspection records/reports and are compliant with GMP and QS requirements.

按照检验设备程序的要求使用量具和其他检验设备，维护检验工具和设备，保持它们良好状态，同时确保所有的检验记录/报告都符合GMP和QS的要求。

根据需要执行产品转移，FAI 和验证。

支持达成检验团队的关键绩效指标。
